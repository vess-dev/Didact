### Benny

6 HP, 13 STR, 14 DEX, 14 WIL, **Maria** (d8, unique 9mm — see Unique Weapons), switchblade (d6)

- Rat-pack casino boss. A talker first, a shooter last. Runs if it goes south.
- **Lucky:** Once per combat, re-roll a single failed save.
