### Vertibird Gunship

20 HP, 3 Armor, 16 STR, 14 DEX, 12 WIL, door-minigun (d10, _blast_), chin rockets (d10)

- Enclave VTOL. Strafes on a round-2 pattern (hover, then gun).
- **Airborne**: Ground melee attacks are Impaired. A Critical Damage result on the rotor brings it down.
