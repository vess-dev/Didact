The party reaches the bridge. Three endings:

- **Kill the captain.** The ship drifts, its crew leaderless. The saucer is free. Every abductee who lives returns to Earth.
- **Sabotage the reactor.** The ship falls into the ocean. The party escapes on the saucer; the other abductees do not. The party carries those ghosts.
- **Take the throne.** The party takes the bridge and *keeps* it. The aliens recognize a new chain of command. The ship becomes a permanent party relic — accessible only once per campaign, but from anywhere on Earth. Whatever this costs the Overseer's campaign balance is the point.
