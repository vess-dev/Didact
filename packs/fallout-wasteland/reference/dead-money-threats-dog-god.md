### Dog / God (Nightkin)

12 HP, 1 Armor, 16 STR, 12 DEX, 10 WIL, cleaver (d10, _bulky_), bite (d6)

- A super mutant nightkin with two minds sharing one body. Shifts mid-fight.
- **Two Minds**: At half HP the personality flips — Dog (biting, loyal) becomes God (calm, surgical) or vice versa; clear any mid-combat conditions.
