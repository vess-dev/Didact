*Vaults of Fallout: New Vegas*

**Premise.** The residents were told that one of them must be sacrificed each year, or the entire population would be terminated. They made it an election: whoever won became Overseer, and at the end of the term walked into the sacrificial chamber. The vault ran this election for sixteen years, until a rigged vote set off a civil war that left five survivors.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Murals painted by the residents list the name of every Overseer sacrificed, year by year; the last mural has an abrupt unfinished line. Against the wall by the door sit four skeletons with four guns — the last survivors, who could not live with what they had learned. A fifth gun lies on the floor, unfired.
- **The Bloc Halls.** Corridors papered with the propaganda of the vault's six voting blocs, each poster explaining why someone else should die.
- **Voting Hall.** A grand chamber with one podium and a sealed ballot box. Votes from the final election are still inside.
- **The Sacrifice Chamber.** A single chair with wrist restraints. A pre-war automated recording describes the honor and necessity of the sacrifice. Bloodstains on the floor are very old.
- **Overseer's Office.** The office of the last elected Overseer, who knew exactly what the title meant. A handwritten note is still on the desk.
- **The Terminal Room.** The vault's central computer. A pre-war recording plays if the computer is accessed: it reveals that the entire experiment was a bluff. Any population that refused to sacrifice anyone would have been praised and set free. Only the last five survivors ever refused, after the civil war had killed everyone else.

**Inhabitants.** SECURITY TURRETS in the voting hall (treat as two TURRET, MACHINEGUN). GIANT RADSCORPIONS in the lower maintenance levels.

**Reward.** The **Vault 11 Ballot Box** — evidence of pre-war psychological manipulation, worth 800 caps to a Followers researcher. The **Survivors' Holotape**, recorded at the door by the five who refused, so that others might learn from what they did.

**Hidden.** One of the murals has a small painted cross next to a Sacrifice's name — a resident who had been erased from every other record. A PC who pokes at the wall beneath the cross finds a **second, hidden ballot box** with the actual unrigged votes from the first election. Reading them reveals that the residents *had* tried to refuse the whole game once.

**Hook.** A pre-war historian among the Followers has been writing a book about the vault experiments. She needs the voting records to finish her chapter on Vault 11. She is willing to pay.
