- **Lincoln's Repeater** — from the Museum of History. See Unique Weapons.
- **The Shocker** — from the Flooded Metro, west of Arlington Library. See Unique Weapons.
- **A3-21's Rifle** — from Rivet City's security chief, if the party learns who he really is. See Unique Weapons.
- **Colonel Autumn's 10mm Pistol** (or his **Laser Pistol**) — looted from his corpse if the party kills him. See Unique Weapons.
- **James's Wedding Ring** (_petty_, relic). The wearer cannot become Panicked in the presence of a faction your father would have trusted (the Brotherhood of Steel and Project Purity's people certainly qualify; the Overseer rules on the rest).
- **Project Purity Flask** (relic, _petty_). One dose of purified water clears all Rads and clears Deprivation for one day. Refillable at a working purifier. One charge per session.
- **Pip-Boy 3000** (relic). Every Vault 101 PC already carries one; a second copy recovered from Vault 112 works as a spare.
- **Virtual Reality Goggles** (relic, from Vault 112). Once per session, enter a 20-minute lucid dream in which you can consult one pre-war expert of the Overseer's choosing.
- **Faction:** Brotherhood of Steel up to Idolized (+2), Enclave down to Vilified (−2), one step at a time as the party's deeds are reported. If the party accepted Eden's FEV vial: invert those.
