*Vaults of Fallout 4*

**Premise.** Vault-Tec built Vault 81 to develop a cure for every disease, using its residents as unknowing test subjects: nozzles hidden in every room were meant to release the pathogens, and to incinerate everyone if the vault were ever evacuated. The first Overseer, Dr. Olivette, refused. On the morning of the war she sabotaged the call list so most of the scientists never arrived, disabled the delivery system, and sealed the three who came into the secret wing. The residents were never infected. Two centuries later, Vault 81 is one of the few vaults that still works as a vault.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Indistinguishable from a standard vault. The community here is welcoming — they believe their vault is an honest one.
- **Residential Block.** A living community of about 50 people. They farm, they trade with nearby settlements, they are among the last intact vault populations in the region.
- **The Sealed Wing.** Vault 81-B, behind a locked door the residents never open: disease labs still stocked and still hot with pre-war pathogens, diseased test mole rats loose in the halls, and **Curie**, a Miss Nanny the scientists left running, who has spent two hundred years trying to finish their cure.
- **The Hidden Cure.** A small refrigerated cabinet contains **Vault 81 Cure** — a universal antiviral, stable in sealed dose form. See the Relics chapter.
- **Overseer's Office.** The current Overseer knows there is a sealed wing, and not what is in it. Her predecessors kept that secret for generations.

**Inhabitants.** VAULT COMMUNITY (friendly, trading), MOLE RATS in the sealed wing (diseased — any bite forces a STR save or the victim contracts mole rat fever, a slow wasting sickness: lose 1 STR per day until cured), and Curie, who would very much like to meet a human being.

**Reward.** The single dose of **Vault 81 Cure** (Relic, 1 use — see Relics chapter). **Pre-war Disease Samples** (1 slot, dangerous in the wrong hands, 600 caps to certain collectors).

**Hidden.** The former Overseer's terminal, forgotten in the generator room, records exactly what Dr. Olivette did and why every resident is alive. It also records that the nozzles in their bedrooms are still connected to the incinerator system, and that the evacuation protocol has never been disarmed. A PC who reads it and *doesn't* tell the community walks out with a secret that will weigh on them every time they pass through Vault 81 again.

**Hook.** A child in Vault 81 has fallen ill with a disease nothing in the settlement can identify. The current Overseer asks the party to look through the one wing she's never entered.
