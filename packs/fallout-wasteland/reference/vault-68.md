*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** Populated with 999 men and a single woman, under the cover story of a standard gender-diverse control vault. The purpose was never explained to the residents.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A pre-war welcome poster shows a generic family. The real resident roster is on a terminal behind the desk.
- **Residential Block.** Crowded. Nine hundred and ninety-nine men and one woman, who locked herself in an Overseer-assigned suite and stayed there for the rest of her life.
- **The Sealed Suite.** Locked from the inside. Every day, food was delivered to her door. Every day, she took it in. For her entire life.
- **Overseer's Office.** The last holotape on the Overseer's desk is hers — the woman in the suite, asking Vault-Tec to send her replacement. She is furious and unheard.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine.

**Inhabitants.**
- **Feral Residents** — detachment of Feral Ghouls.
- **The Overseer's Assistant** — 4 HP, 8 STR, 10 DEX, 13 WIL, no weapon. A lucid ghoul who still remembers the sealed occupant's name and is willing to say it.

**Reward.** **The Suite's Collected Diaries** (_bulky_ relic) — a Followers scholar will pay 800 caps for them. A PC who reads the entire set over a long rest gains a free **Bond** tied to the resident's story and, once per session, may automatically pass a WIL save against loneliness, despair, or imprisonment — they have read worse. A **Single Preserved Rose** the resident kept on her bedside table (_petty_).

**Hidden.** The sealed suite has a small sliding panel in the door used to pass food — and a stack of **paper slips** from the inside, addressed to a single man whose name isn't in any Vault 68 record. Notes passed daily for decades. The Overseer's assistant knows who he was. He will not say aloud.

**Hook.** A ghoul who has walked out of Vault 68 into the nearest settlement was the Overseer's assistant. He has a question he never got to ask. He will pay the party to go back down with him and find the answer in the diaries.
