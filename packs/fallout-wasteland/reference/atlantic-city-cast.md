- **Vin Russo** — the Rose Room's bartender and the Russos' only adult. Left a job he loved with the Munis to hold his family together. Tired, decent, underpaid by his own relatives.
- **Evelyn Russo** — once the finest lounge singer in Acee and a star of the Showmen's stages. Proud, prickly, and more loyal to her children than she knows how to show.
- **Abbie Russo** — her father's favorite until he pushed her out of the family business. Chose the one drug she knew would hurt him most. Smart, furious, and trying to want to get clean.
- **Antonio Russo** — former heir to the Lombardi Family, playing a senile old man upstairs. Invented Devil's Blood to get an edge on Concerta. Loves his daughter badly.
- **Concerta Lombardi** — ruler of the Lombardi Family in her ailing brother's name, and its real power for decades. Elegant, patient, and never forgets.
- **Stanley Soriano** — a small-time Lombardi with a big mouth and the right connections.
- **Gene** — the Lombardis' chemist, who helped Antonio turn devil blood into a drug.
- **Sloane Cao** — chief of the Munis' Pine Barrens Forestry Division, a monster hunter who keeps the Overgrown out of Acee. Vin's oldest friend.
- **Mayor Tim Lane** — the Munis' leader, more worried about the mob and the Showmen than the plants.
- **Mother Charlotte** and **Veracio Cruz** — the Showmen's matriarch and her favorite son, master of the Boardwalk's bloodiest game.
- **Lennox** and **Orlando** — the Whitespring Refuge's vertibird pilot and the resort's manager, who speaks for its mysterious "management" and helps the Responders run the refuge.
