### The Mechanist (Throned)

14 HP, 3 Armor, 11 STR, 13 DEX, 16 WIL, energy cannon (d12, 6/day)

- Isabel Cruz in her armored command throne; the Armor is the throne's plating. She speaks through a pre-war broadcast filter.
- **Throne Link**: While on the throne, she may command any robot in the lair without spending her action.
- **The Chair**: Reduced to 0 HP, or dragged or blasted out of the throne, she becomes **The Mechanist (Unseated)**.
