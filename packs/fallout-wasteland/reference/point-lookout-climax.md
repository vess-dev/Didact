Calvert and Lockheart both offer the party the same job: kill the other. A third path is the Punga Rite, which lets the party enter the Obelisk directly.

- **Kill Calvert.** Lockheart rewards you, steals the lab, vanishes east.
- **Kill Lockheart.** Calvert promises a cure for ghoulification. He is lying — the only gratitude he pays is murder, and the party is next in his Great Game.
- **Burn them both.** The Obelisk is the real monster. Shutting it down frees the swampfolk from their generational exposure. In the punga-haze, a deathclaw walks out of the mist and refuses to fight. It is not really there — no deathclaw has ever walked these swamps. Only the Overseer knows what the vision means.
