### Morris Stevens, Progenitor Wendigo

12 HP, 1 Armor, 16 STR, 16 DEX, 8 WIL, claws and bite (d10+d8)

- The Gourmands' leader, a cannibal chef who ate long enough to become the first wendigo. His lair lies deep in a Savage Divide cave, past mirelurk kings, in a room of stripped bones. Carries the Gourmands' key fragment.
- **Terror:** On first sight, PCs make a WIL save or flee for one round.
- **Hungry Shriek:** Once per combat, his scream calls 1d4 **Wendigos** from the side tunnels.
