### President John Henry Eden

— HP, — combat. Voice only.

- A ZAX-class pre-war AI. Not a combat threat. A **WIL-save encounter**: the party may talk him into self-destruction if they can pass a WIL save backed by evidence gathered in Raven Rock's terminals — no evidence, no save. Success: no final battle needed; Raven Rock self-destructs with Eden inside. Failure: the Enclave assault goes full force at Purity.
