- **Doc Mitchell** — Goodsprings physician; the one who dug the bullet out. Kind, retired, always has a pot of coffee on.
- **Sunny Smiles** — Goodsprings scout, lever-action rifle, trusts dogs more than people. Potential companion.
- **Victor** — a cheerful cowboy-themed Securitron who keeps being around. Mr. House's eyes. A PC with a Pip-Boy can hack his shell and get a peek at House's payroll.
- **Benny** — rat-pack casino boss at the Tops. Shot you in the head, stole the Chip. Charming, insecure, doomed.
- **Mister House** — the invisible hand of New Vegas. Speaks only through screens. Pre-war CEO in a sealed chamber, 261 years old.
- **Yes Man** — a Securitron reprogrammed to be helpful to the party specifically. Endlessly cheerful. Does not understand personal boundaries.
- **Caesar (Edward Sallow)** — general of the Legion. Former Follower of the Apocalypse, now a slaver emperor who quotes Gibbon. Brain tumor. Dying.
- **Vulpes Inculta** — Caesar's top frumentarius. The one holding the torch at Nipton. Smart and patient and coming for the party.
- **Legate Lanius** — Caesar's second and field general. A killer wearing an iron mask, swinging a blade the size of a door.
- **Colonel James Hsu** — NCR field commander at McCarran. Honest, overworked, worth an alliance.
- **General Lee Oliver** — NCR overall commander. An ass. Shows up at Hoover Dam to take credit.
- **Ambassador Dennis Crocker** — NCR diplomat on the Strip. Wants the party to play nice.
- **The King** — leader of the Kings in Freeside. Elvis impersonator with a golden heart and a straight razor. Potential ally.
- **Elder Nolan McNamara** — Hidden Valley Brotherhood elder. Paranoid and rightly so.
- **Pearl** — the Boomer elder at Nellis. Will parley if the party brings a surviving B-29 back up from Lake Mead.
- **Veronica Santangelo** — a Brotherhood scribe who has wandered from the bunker looking for a world worth staying in. Potential companion.
- **Arcade Gannon** — a Followers of the Apocalypse doctor with a secret Enclave past. Potential companion.
- **Craig Boone** — Novac's ex-NCR sniper, grieving. Potential companion. Will kill a Legionary on sight.
- **Lily Bowen** — Jacobstown's nightkin grandmother. Confused about the century, deadly with a sword ground from a vertibird blade. Potential companion.
- **Raul Tejada** — ghoul mechanic held prisoner atop Black Mountain. Old, tired, very dry. Potential companion.
- **ED-E** — an Eyebot from the Mojave Express office. Potential companion (see **Eyebot** background).
- **Rex** — a cyberdog belonging to the King. Loaner companion while the King is alive.
