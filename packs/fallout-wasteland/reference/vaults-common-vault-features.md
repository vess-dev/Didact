Every vault shares a general architecture. When describing a new vault, reach for these details first:

- A massive **rolling blast door** at the entrance, usually jammed half-open or sealed shut.
- A **greeting hall** with a pre-war Vault-Tec poster, a dead receptionist's desk, and a **Pip-Boy dispenser** that gave out the wrist computers on arrival day.
- **Residential blocks** of identical cabins, still furnished, sometimes still occupied.
- A **cafeteria** with a pre-war commercial kitchen and a vending machine.
- A **clinic** with an auto-doc and a stock of pre-war chems — usually rifled through.
- A **school** with pre-war educational posters and crayon drawings that become less cheerful the longer you look.
- An **Overseer's office**, sealed behind a keycode, with a terminal still logged in to the dead Overseer who ran the place.
- A **reactor room** deeper in, still producing power, usually the reason the door lights still work.
- A **water treatment plant** with a pre-war water chip, sometimes still functional, sometimes weeping radiation into the pipes.
- **Maintenance tunnels** behind the residential areas, full of the things the Overseer didn't want the residents to see.
