Broad strokes. Four to five sessions. The structure is a straight road and the party is walking down it together, but the Courier is the one Ulysses is addressing. Every other PC is a witness.

1. **The Eyebot at the Door.** ED-E arrives at the party's current settlement with a looping Ulysses transmission. The eyebot is damaged; fixing it is the first scene (a DEX save; a Tinker needs no save). When ED-E boots, it plays the *Courier's own voice* from a delivery the PC does not remember making. Something in the recording is not a greeting.
2. **The Canyon Wreckage.** The party enters the Divide. Ulysses speaks on the radio, not yet naming the PC, but addressing a "Courier" the other party members may not realize is one of theirs. The wreckage of a pre-war trade road stretches ahead. ED-E guides.
3. **Hopeville Missile Base.** The first sealed door. It asks for a Courier's name — the PC's. When it opens, Ulysses identifies the PC by name over the PA and asks the others to leave the Courier to him. The party can refuse. This is the first moment the campaign *accuses* a specific character.
4. **The Courier's Mile.** A radioactive corridor the Marked Men patrol. The crossing costs a Rad, shielded or not. Each party member makes the crossing in their own way; the Courier is the only one who cannot *not* go. Marked Men here include a named Centurion who speaks once through a broken helmet and asks why.
5. **Rawr's Cave.** A deathclaw lair. The fight is optional — a side tunnel bypasses it; each crawler takes a Fatigue. Ulysses will comment on whichever choice the party makes. He does not judge; he just remembers.
6. **The High Road and the Hive.** The ruined highway on stilts is held up by supports the tunnelers have all but chewed through. A tunneler queen waits beneath it. A clever party can collapse the road as they cross and destroy the hive behind them at the cost of cutting off a retreat.
7. **The Hanging Rock.** A silent scene. A vantage over the entire Divide. Ulysses speaks here even in absentia — his voice carries on broken radios across the canyon. Each party member describes what they see when they look out across the Divide from this point.
8. **The Silo Control Room.** The Courier meets Ulysses face to face. Two launch buttons on the wall — **NCR** and **Legion**. The PC is the one Ulysses is speaking to; the other players are the witnesses. Resolutions in the **Climax** below.
