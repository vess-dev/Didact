After the Battle of Project Purity, Enclave forces are broken but not destroyed. The Brotherhood deploys **Liberty Prime** to assault the Enclave's satellite relay station — and the Enclave destroys Prime with an orbital strike from space. **Adams Air Force Base** holds their last Vertibird wing, a mobile base crawler, and the uplink to that satellite. The Brotherhood needs a Tesla Cannon to punch through Enclave Hellfire armor, and they need someone expendable to ride the presidential metro into Adams AFB and end this.
