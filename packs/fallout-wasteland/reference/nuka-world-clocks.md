- **Crown Clock** (d4). Gage's patience with the party's indecision about the Overboss seat. Ticks when the party declines a gang's direct petition, refuses to hold court in Fizztop Grille, or pointedly helps a caged trader in view of a gang boss. At 4, one of the three gangs installs its *own* Overboss and treats the party as enemies.
- **Slave Clock** (d4). The traders caged in Nuka-Town USA. Ticks when the party walks past the cages without acting, when a gang boss orders a public execution for sport, and when the party takes on an Overboss task without negotiating for a prisoner's life first. Each tick is a death. The caged traders are a named roster — Chip Morse, Mackenzie Bridgeman, Aaron Corbett, and others. At 4, Mackenzie is among the dead, and the *Liberate the park* ending loses its ally.
- **Commonwealth Clock** (d6). If the party sides with the raiders. Ticks on each Commonwealth settlement raided, sacked, or surrendered to a Nuka-World gang. At 6, a **Minutemen relief force** arrives at the park gates.
