- **Launch Clock** (d4). Ulysses's ritual. Ticks when the party completes a Divide waypoint (the Hopeville Silo, the High Road, the Courier's Mile) — and once more if they turn back, or rest a full day inside the Divide. The party cannot reach him before it hits 3. At 4, missiles fly no matter what — Overseer's pick of target.
- **Rad Clock** (ambient). Each time the party enters a new Divide zone without a sealed suit, every exposed PC takes 1 Rad. A sealed **Hazmat Suit** negates it; a **Radiation Suit** halves it (ignore every second Rad). Passing through the same zone again does not tick it; it fires on first entry only.
