Flyers for the Rose Room's opening night are pinned up in every train station in Appalachia. The party arrives for the show. Evelyn Russo sings, a pair of hecklers get thrown out, and Vin, behind the bar, asks the party to keep the peace for the second set. Halfway through a tuba solo, hitmen open fire on the family. When the smoke clears, the dying ringleader admits his crew jumped the gun, and Abbie is on the floor, shaking, begging for Devil's Blood.
