- **Assault Clock** (d8). The Brotherhood timetable. Ticks when Sarah completes a briefing, when the Tesla Cannon gains a working component, and when Rothchild certifies a party contribution to the attack plan. At 8, Sarah attacks regardless of the party's readiness.
- **Orbital Clock** (d6). Enclave satellite prep. Ticks when the party retrieves an Enclave encryption log, destroys an Enclave relay without wiping it first, or is overheard discussing the assault plan in a public settlement. At 6, the Enclave fires the orbital weapon at the Citadel — Overseer's call whether it hits.
