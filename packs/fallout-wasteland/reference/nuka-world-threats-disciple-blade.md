### Disciple Blade

6 HP, 12 STR, 16 DEX, 10 WIL, Disciples blade (d8), surgical knife (d6)

- The quiet gang. Blood paint, ritual scars, a hundred blades between them.
- **Silent Cut**: Enhanced from stealth. On a Critical Damage result, the target also cannot cry out that round.
