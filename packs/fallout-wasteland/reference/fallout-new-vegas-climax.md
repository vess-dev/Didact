The Second Battle of Hoover Dam. Four endings:

- **For the NCR.** General Oliver gives the orders. The Dam holds; the flag flies. Vegas becomes an NCR client state. The Followers of the Apocalypse open two clinics. Caesar dies of his tumor — or under a PC's blade at the command center.
- **For the Legion.** The Dam falls to the Bull. General Oliver makes his last stand in the command compound, where he can be fought, shamed, or talked into marching his troopers home. The Mojave is free of NCR forever; so is every slave cuffed to a Legion camp.
- **For Mister House.** The upgraded Securitron army routs both NCR and Legion. House broadcasts a victory speech from the Lucky 38. The party delivered Vegas to a pre-war ghost; in ten years the Strip is bigger and colder than it ever was.
- **For an Independent Vegas (Yes Man).** The Securitron army answers to *the party* now. NCR and Legion both break on the Dam. Yes Man broadcasts a cheerful message from the 38. The party is, whether they wanted to be or not, the new government.

In every ending but the Legion's, the last obstacle is the Legate's camp. Lanius personally duels the party's strongest fighter — or withdraws, if a PC can show him that the East cannot hold the Mojave (a WIL save, and a real argument). Win and he signals retreat; lose and the water goes red.

The Courier walks away. **Lonesome Road** picks up the spare road the Courier left behind — the one that was waiting since the first package they delivered.
