- **Nuclear Keycard** (relic — see Relics). One per keycard convoy.
- **Overseer's Cache Key** (relic — see Relics). Pennington hands it over with the first holotape.
- **Taggerdy's ID** (relic, _petty_). Lieutenant Elizabeth Taggerdy's Army ID, taken from her body in the glassed cavern. Opens any Brotherhood door, terminal, or laser grid in Appalachia. When the Brotherhood returns from California (see **Steel Dawn**), bringing it home earns +1 with the Brotherhood.
- **Ultracite Rounds** — Hank Madigan's discovery: bullets tipped with depleted ultracite. Attacks with them against Scorched and scorchbeasts — the Queen included — are Enhanced. A box lasts one fight; Fort Defiance's ammunition press makes more from fissure-site ultracite.
- **Fire Breather Uniform** — issued on passing the Fire Breather exam. See Unique Armors.
- **Excavator Power Armor** — Garrahan Mining's black-titanium mining suit, found through Madigan's leads. See Unique Armors.
- **Ultracite Power Armor** — Scribe McNamara's unfinished design, waiting in Fort Defiance's terminals for someone to build it. See Unique Armors.
- **Grant's Saber** — Ulysses S. Grant's sword, on display in the Whitespring Resort. See Unique Weapons.
- **Blade of Bastet** and **Veil of Secrets** (relics — see Relics) — if the party takes up the Order of Mysteries' trials beneath Riverside Manor.
- **Mothman Feather** (relic — see Relics) — a gift from the Wise Mothman at Point Pleasant, for those who come in peace.
- **General's Commission** (relic, _petty_). MODUS's promotion to the Joint Chiefs, and command of the Kovac-Muldoon orbital platform: *3 charges*, **Recharge**: one per week the bunker is kept supplied and staffed. Mark a target under open sky, and next round an orbital strike hits it (d12, _blast_) or a supply drop lands beside it (2 Stimpaks, a box of ammunition, a fusion cell).
- **Faction:** Rose's goodwill carries over to the raiders who return to Appalachia; the Overseer's gratitude carries over to the settlers.
