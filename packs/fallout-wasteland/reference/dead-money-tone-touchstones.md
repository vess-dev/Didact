*A haunted-house heist. The central theme is **letting go** — every NPC in Dead Money is strangled by something they cannot release, and the Overseer's job is to make the party feel that pressure in every scene. The red cloud, the chimes of the hologram emitters, the wet click of a collar are your three instruments.*

**Touchstones:** *Dead Money* DLC itself (the central theme), *The Treasure of the Sierra Madre* (1948, the source text), *Ocean's Eleven* played as nightmare, *BioShock* (the city that refused to die), *Shutter Island*.

**Lean into:** silence between sentences. The Sierra Madre was a dream that never opened; every room should feel like somebody just stepped out of it. Keep the collars present — the party should hear the chime every time it gets too close to a speaker. The *reward* is not the gold; it is the choice to walk away from it.
