A shattered, wind-torn canyon. The sky is brown. Every step kicks up a memory.

- **The Canyon Wreckage.** Drop-in. The bones of a trade road. ED-E shorts out here in the first dust squall and must be coaxed back to life (as before) before it can open the path.
- **The Hopeville Missile Base.** A pre-war silo, its walls half-buried. The first locked door asks for a Courier's name.
- **The Courier's Mile.** A stretch of road saturated with Rads. Any PC crossing gains **1 Rad** regardless of shielding. **Marked Men** patrol here — ghoulified NCR and Legion fused with their own skin-searing armor.
- **Rawr's Cave.** A deathclaw lair — home of the **Divide deathclaw** called **Rawr** (mutated, stronger). The party can bypass it by crawling a side tunnel; each crawler takes a Fatigue.
- **The Hanging Rock.** A vantage over the whole Divide. Ulysses speaks here even when absent — his voice carries on broken radios.
- **The High Road.** A highway on stilts above the canyon. Tunnelers swarm the supports.
- **The Cave of the Abaddon.** The hive under the road. A tunneler queen (see Bestiary).
- **The Silo Control Room.** Climax. Two launch buttons. Ulysses is here.
