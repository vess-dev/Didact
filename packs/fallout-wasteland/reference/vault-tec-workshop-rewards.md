- **The Phoropter** (relic headgear, the wearer automatically detects hidden triggers, traps, and concealed programs in any pre-war terminal).
- **Lucky Slot Amulet** (relic, 3/day, re-roll the Die of Fate).
- **Bottoms-Up Tap** (relic, 1/session, pours a drink that restores 1 STR and adds 1 Rad).
- **Power Cycle 1000** (relic, _bulky_; no slots while installed in a settlement — pedaling it for a scene recharges one fusion cell weapon).
- **Vault-Tec Lab Coat** (relic armor, 0 Armor, the wearer is recognized as authorized staff by any Vault-Tec terminal and cannot be locked out of standard access).
- **Vault 88 as a settlement** — if the party builds it, they gain a *permanent base*: the vault's surviving pre-war works count as a built-in building granting **+1 Industry** while powered (requires a Power Generator or the reactor hookup). The price is what the party did to get there.
