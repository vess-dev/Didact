*A year after Reclamation Day, people come back to Appalachia: settlers out of Pittsburgh, raiders who fled the plague, treasure hunters chasing a radio rumor. The rumor is true. A secret vault in the Savage Divide holds the gold of Fort Knox, and everyone who wants it needs the one group of people who know these hills — the dwellers of Vault 76.*
