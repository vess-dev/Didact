**The Catalyst.** Blackburn is dead or dying on the test-chamber floor. Farha, Jain, and Nellie are behind a sealed security door, and Farha has already shut down the FEV production line. Rahmani wants them spared and put to work under Brotherhood guard. Shin wants them executed, as the Codex and the Elders would demand, so that what they know dies with them. Two resolutions and a coda:

- **Side with Rahmani.** The scientists live. Shin will not serve a Brotherhood that repeats the old world's mistakes, and he sets out for California to report Rahmani to the Elders in person. The party can let him go, talk him into staying, or stop him with force. Rahmani names the party a Knight-Errant, and her Brotherhood — merciful, independent, and on borrowed time — is Appalachia's.
- **Side with Shin.** The scientists die. Rahmani cannot watch, and she will not stay. Shin brands her a traitor; the party can let her walk away to build her own Brotherhood somewhere else, or end her there. Shin names the party a Knight-Errant, and the expedition returns to the Codex — disciplined, loyal, and feared.
- **Coda.** Either way, somebody is on the road west with a story to tell the Elders. The next expedition from Lost Hills will arrive knowing exactly what the Appalachian Brotherhood did, and who helped.
