### The Grim Reaper's Hallway

- Not a creature — a corridor of military laser turrets inside Vault 79. Each round in the Hallway, every PC makes a DEX save or takes d10 damage. Captain Fields' codes, Jen's grid trick, or a well-thrown pulse grenade shuts it down for a scene.
