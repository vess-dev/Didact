A radio signal from a pre-war **Nuka-Cola** family vacation resort invites the party to *Nuka-World, the world-famous amusement park — now open!* The broadcast omits that the park is run by the **Pack**, the **Disciples**, and the **Operators** — three raider gangs — whose lieutenants have rigged the Gauntlet to rid themselves of their own Overboss. The park's pre-war management AI, **Nuka-Cola Family Spokesbot "Nick Nuka,"** still runs rides and vending machines. The Overboss's seat is about to be vacant. Guess who's auditioning.
