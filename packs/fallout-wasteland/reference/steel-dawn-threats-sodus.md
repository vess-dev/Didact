### SODUS (XB-55 Sentry Bot)

12 HP, 3 Armor, 16 STR, 9 DEX, 16 WIL, twin automatic laser rifles (d10, _blast_)

- The Enclave facility's AI, uploaded into a legendary XB-55 sentry bot chassis when its visitors refused to die.
- **Queue:** Before combat, SODUS offers to help. Every request is added to a task list with a completion time measured in years.
- **Weak Point:** Like any robot, SODUS takes Enhanced damage from pulse weapons (see Marketplace).
- **Critical Damage to SODUS:** The sentry bot's fusion core vents; everyone within 10 feet makes a DEX save or takes d8 damage.
