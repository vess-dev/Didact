### DiMA

8 HP, 1 Armor, 10 STR, 13 DEX, 17 WIL, laser pistol (d8)

- Prototype synth, Acadia's leader. Peaceful by default, but capable in extremis.
- **Memory Den**: A PC who has spent a scene in DiMA's memory vault gains a free re-roll on one save during any confrontation with him.
