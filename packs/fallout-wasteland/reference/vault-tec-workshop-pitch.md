A radio broadcast on a pre-war Vault-Tec frequency advertises **Vault 88** — "the future of American living, opening soon." The vault was under construction when the bombs fell. Now **Overseer Valery Barstow**, a ghoul from the pre-war staff, is still at her post and ready to finish the job. She needs test subjects, researchers, and someone expendable to run experiments on the settlers she promises to invite. She is polite, brilliant, and monstrous in the old Vault-Tec way.
