A Commonwealth detective agency — **Valentine's Detective Agency** — hires the party to find a missing girl, **Kasumi Nakano**. The trail leads to **Far Harbor**, an island off the Maine coast where the Fog crawls off the sea and rewrites the land. Kasumi isn't missing; she ran *to* Far Harbor because she thinks she's a synth. Whether she is or isn't becomes the smallest of the island's problems.
