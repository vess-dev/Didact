Broad strokes. Four to five sessions. A straightforward investigation that keeps finding more empathy for its villain the deeper it goes.

1. **The Caravan Wreck.** The distress call leads to a line of destroyed brahmin, a scattered shipment of scrap, and a single surviving robot — Ada — standing over the bodies of her crew. She is shaken but lucid. She wants to find the Mechanist and put her down.
2. **Ada Joins.** The party meets Ada properly. She is the campaign's moral weight — a robot who watched her people die. Let her speak during quiet scenes; she does not say much, but she says the right things. She pushes the party toward the trail.
3. **Interrogating the Signal.** The party builds a robot workbench (or finds one) and interrogates a captured Robobrain to trace the Mechanist's command signal. The signal bounces through old military and pre-war infrastructure. A PC who cracks the Robobrain's encryption learns the source is routed through a pre-war satellite relay at **Fort Hagen**.
4. **Fort Hagen Satellite Array.** The relay station the Mechanist uses to broadcast commands. **Ahab**, the Rust Devils' deathclaw-skulled sentry bot, holds the hangar bay; Rust Devil scavengers under their leader **Ivey** lurk in the tunnels; and a Robobrain head on a shelf named **Jezebel** — immobile, charming, and cruel — offers to bargain. She will give up the Mechanist's lair coordinates if the party carries her head out with them. She is a risk either way.
5. **The Approach to the Lair.** A short journey to the Mechanist's headquarters at the **RobCo Sales & Service Center** — a pre-war showroom with a hidden underground complex. Rogue Mister Handies in the approach tunnels, each one convinced it is still running a pre-war errand. The Army Clock should be ticking hard by this point — if it hits 6, a wave of Isabel's robots assaults the party's nearest settlement off-camera.
6. **The Lair.** Isabel Cruz waits on a metal throne above a pit of failed prototypes beneath the RobCo building. Her mask distorts her voice into something imperial. The Isabel Clock determines how ready she is to listen when the party confronts her. The climax is a conversation as often as it is a fight. See **Climax** below.
