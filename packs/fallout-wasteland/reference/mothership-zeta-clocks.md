- **Alien Alert Clock** (d6). The ship's awareness. Ticks when the party fights loud (explosives, gunfire in a non-lab corridor), thaws a prisoner from stasis, or trips a Zetan security panel. At 6, a **Guardian patrol** sweeps every room.
- **Reactor Clock** (d4). Starts once the party has sabotaged the generator. Ticks when the party damages another ship system (gravity plating, coolant line, external hull), fires the main cannon, or detonates a Zetan power cell. At 4, the ship begins to *fall*.
