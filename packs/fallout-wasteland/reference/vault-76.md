*Vaults of Fallout 76*

**Premise.** Vault-Tec's "Official Vault of the Tricentennial" — one of the seventeen control vaults, stocked with some of America's finest and meant to open twenty years after the war so its people could rebuild. It opened after twenty-five, on **Reclamation Day**, October 23, 2102, and its residents walked out into Appalachia. The Overseer left a few hours ahead of them. See the **Fallout 76** main campaign frame.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Clean. Genuinely. The pre-war cleaning cycle is still running.
- **Residential Block.** Every cabin has been emptied. The residents left on the opening day and did not come back.
- **The Opening Day Hall.** A commemorative hall with portraits of every original resident, Reclamation Day banners, and Vault Boy cutouts still pointing the way out. The Reclamation Day cake, baked from the vault's pre-war stores, sits intact under a glass dome on a plinth.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's holotape contains no confession. She says goodbye to the residents, explains that she has gone ahead on a mission of her own, and walks out into the morning.
- **Reactor Room.** Untouched.

**Inhabitants.** The vault is nearly empty. A handful of MISTER HANDYS still running maintenance. A single feral RADROACH nest in the reactor cooling line.

**Reward.** **The Reclamation Day Cake** (_bulky_, 400 caps to a collector — or eat it for a single scene of nostalgia: you cannot be Panicked for the rest of the session as the taste of the Before hits like a freight train). A **Full Vault 76 Jumpsuit** in pristine condition (_petty_, 200 caps, confuses any faction that knows the vault program).

**Hidden.** Behind the commemorative cake's plinth, tucked into a gap in the glass dome's base, is a small folded **napkin** someone wrote on during the opening-day ceremony: a resident's handwritten confession of having loved another resident in silence for years. The cake was their last meal together. The name is one the party has seen before on a gravestone.

**Hook.** A historian wants to document Vault 76 before the Crater Raiders strip it for scrap and paint over the opening-day mural. He has a week's pay for a safe escort.
