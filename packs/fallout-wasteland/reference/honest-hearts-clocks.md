- **War Clock** (d6). Graham's offensive timeline. Ticks when the party accepts or trains with Graham, when a White Legs raid happens within the party's view, or when a named Dead Horse or Sorrows NPC is killed. At 6, the Three Marys ambush fires whether the party is there or not.
- **Evacuation Clock** (d4). Daniel's preparation. Ticks when the party completes a preparation (scouting the route east, making shoes for children, clearing a bloatfly nest, finding a signal beacon). At 4, Daniel can move the tribe.
