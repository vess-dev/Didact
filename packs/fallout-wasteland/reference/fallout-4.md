*You woke up two hundred years late. Your spouse is dead and your child is gone — taken by a stranger who walked into the vault while you slept. You step out of Vault 111 into a ruined Boston and start walking. Every road through the Commonwealth eventually leads to the Institute, to Shaun, and to a choice none of the four factions watching you will let you dodge.*
