*Vaults of Fallout 4*

**Premise.** Boston's elite — state officials, luminaries, and money — were promised a luxury vault beneath Park Street Station. They would have been packed several families to a room, sharing baths and meals, under an Overseer Vault-Tec picked for having no experience and a hatred of authority: a homeless man known only as "Soup Can Harry." The vault was never finished, and the experiment never ran. Now **Skinny Malone**'s Triggermen use it as a hideout.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Marble veneer over pre-war concrete. A receptionist's desk is still piled with registration forms.
- **The Unfinished Wing.** The residential block was never completed. Exposed rebar, unfinished drywall, concrete floors where carpet was promised.
- **Security Bunker.** The overseer staff's barracks, better appointed than the residents'.
- **The Lounge.** A pre-war lounge with a jukebox, a bar, and a half-full champagne bottle that has somehow not evaporated. Skinny Malone holds court here with his girl, Darla, and a crooner's idea of how a mobster talks.
- **Overseer's Office.** Never used. On the desk, a Vault-Tec personnel file: Soup Can Harry's interview transcript, and the note that he "ain't wearing no goddamn tie."
- **The Cell.** The Triggermen keep their prisoners in the vault's unfinished security block. Lately, a detective.

**Inhabitants.** TRIGGERMEN (treat as THUG detachments with a CREW BOSS — Skinny Malone). A lone MISTER HANDY working as a bartender at the lounge.

**Reward.** **A Pre-war Tuxedo / Cocktail Dress** (uses the rules for Dean's Tuxedo — see Unique Armors — though nobody famous ever wore this one). **The Champagne Bottle** (_bulky_, 300 caps, a curiosity).

**Hidden.** Behind the bar, under a floorboard the bartender Mr. Handy was specifically ordered never to reveal to anyone, is a **leather-bound ledger** listing every favor the Triggermen have called in across the Commonwealth — names, dates, amounts. Half the names belong to people in Diamond City and Goodneighbor who would pay to see it burn. Useful, dangerous, or both.

**Hook.** A detective went into Park Street Station a week ago looking for a runaway girl and never came out. His secretary will pay the party to get him back. A ghoul in Goodneighbor who ran with the Triggermen before the war wants something specific from the lounge, and will pay in caps not to say what.
