- **Daniel** — New Canaanite missionary. Pacifist. Wants the Sorrows taken east to the safety of the Grand Staircase, away from the coming war.
- **Joshua Graham** — the Burned Man. Former Legate of the Legion, now a devout New Canaanite. Speaks quietly. Kills very fast.
- **Follows-Chalk** — a young Dead Horse scout. Wants to see the Mojave. Potential companion.
- **Waking Cloud** — a Sorrows midwife and tracker. Potential companion.
- **Salt-Upon-Wounds** — White Legs war chief. Wants Legion recognition. He will not get it — Caesar has no use for tribes that fail.
