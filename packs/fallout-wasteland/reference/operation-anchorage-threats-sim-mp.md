### Sim-MP

8 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 14 DEX, 10 WIL, laser rifle (d10, _bulky_), baton (d6)

- Spawns if the Chase Clock maxes. Their laser rifle is Enhanced — the sim's targeting subroutine is flawless.
- **Invincible Mandate**: Flee on Critical Damage instead of dying; a Sim-MP killed simply respawns off-camera.
