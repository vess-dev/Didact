### Jersey Devil

16 HP, 2 Armor, 17 STR, 15 DEX, 10 WIL, claws and bite (d12+d8), wing-buffet (d8, _blast_)

- The adult: a dragon-like horror the size of a vertibird, the real thing behind two centuries of Pine Barrens folklore. Drawn by pheromones; enraged by fire.
- **Flight:** Ranged attacks against the Jersey Devil in flight are Impaired.
- **Shriek:** Once per combat, every PC who can hear it makes a WIL save or loses their next action.
- **Critical Damage:** The target is carried into the air and dropped into the floodwater.
