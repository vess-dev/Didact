*Vaults of Fallout: New Vegas*

**Premise.** A botanical experiment: the vault's scientists were working to end world hunger. A defense contractor donated a pest-control fungus, *Beauveria mordicana*, bred in Big MT's X-22 botanical garden. It turned out to infect people as easily as insects. The residents were gradually consumed by their own crop.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Vines have grown through the blast door and cover the walls.
- **Hydroponics Bay.** A jungle. Giant mutated plants grow along the ceiling, some still in rows from their planters.
- **Spore-Infested Residential Block.** Bodies of the original residents, covered in vines and spore pods. Some of them are still moving — SPORE CARRIERS.
- **Overseer's Office.** Sealed against the spores. Inside, the Overseer's final log, delivered as her office slowly filled with green.
- **The Seed Vault.** A locked cold-room at the lowest level containing every preserved pre-war plant seed for the region. Still viable, if someone can crack the lock.

**Inhabitants.**
- **SPORE CARRIER** — use the bestiary entry. The original residents, hollowed out by the crop.
- **GIANT MANTIS** — detachment in the upper tunnels; use the bestiary entry.

**Reward.** **Vault 22 Seed Packets** — a cache of pre-war plant seeds. Delivered to a settlement with at least one Farm Plot, they grant **+1 Industry** (to the usual cap of 5). A **Spore-Resistance Inoculant** (1 dose, grants immunity to spore attacks for one session).

**Hidden.** At the heart of the hydroponics bay, deep under the tangle of vines, is a single **Mother Plant** — older, slower, and aware enough to respond to voices. It does not attack. It is trying to communicate. A PC who spends a scene with it and passes a WIL save learns something no bestiary can tell them about the vault.

**Hook.** A farmer in a nearby settlement is losing crops to a blight. An NCR agriculture corps rep offers caps for any Vault 22 seed he can get.
