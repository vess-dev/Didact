### David Thorpe (Scorched)

8 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 8 WIL, handmade rifle (d8, _bulky_), machete (d6)

- The founder of Appalachia's raiders, the man who drowned Charleston, now a Scorched in his own raider armor at Big Fred's BBQ Shack. Carries the Cutthroats' key fragment.
- **Plague Carrier:** As **Scorched**.
- **Still Calling for Her:** Between shots he mutters Rosalynn's name. A PC who answers in her voice — or plays one of Rose's recordings — makes him falter: his next attack is Impaired.
