### Lost

5 HP, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 5 WIL, shock baton (d6) or laser pistol (d8)

- A Vault 63 ghoul remade by the weather machine: psychotic, crackling with static, still able to use a weapon. They babble about what their senses tell them — wounds that are loud, colors that smell. Builders swing hammers, welders carry rippers, engineers wear armor and throw plasma grenades.
- Groups of four or more are a _detachment_.
- **Storm-Charged:** During a lightning storm, a Lost's attacks step up one damage die (shock baton d8, laser pistol d10), and it takes 1 damage each round from the charge.
