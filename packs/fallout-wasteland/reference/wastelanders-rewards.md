- **Gold Bullion** — the Fort Knox reserve, cast in bars: the Overseer's new currency. Each bar trades for 20 caps with anyone who believes the currency will hold, and for scrap with anyone who doesn't. Ten bars fill a slot, and nobody in Appalachia can change more than ten bars a week into caps or goods. However the gold is divided, the party's finder's share is 50 bars off the top — or all 1,000, if they keep it and can find a way to carry it (see **Climax**).
- **Secret Service Armor** and **T-65 Power Armor** — the agents' own gear, traded for bullion. See Unique Armors.
- **Crane's Cache** — RobCo Auto-Cache 001: pre-war supplies, a crate of ammunition, and 2d6 × 10 caps' worth of trade goods.
- **Duchess's Tab** — the Wayward is the party's bar now. Free drinks, a safe room upstairs, and a bartender who hears every rumor in Appalachia first.
- **Faction:** up to Idolized (+2) with the crew the party sided with and down to Vilified (−2) with the other, one step at a time; +1 with both if the gold is split evenly; down one step with both if the party keeps it all.
