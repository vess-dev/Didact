*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** All entertainment tapes were removed from the vault on arrival day — the residents arrived to find every holotape of pre-war music, film, and recorded shows stripped from the shelves. The experiment measured the long-term effect of forced creative deprivation.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Bare. The Vault-Tec welcome poster was pried off the wall long ago.
- **Residential Block.** Each cabin has a carved wooden figure on the nightstand. The residents made them by hand.
- **The Gallery.** A large chamber the residents filled with their own art — murals, sculptures from pre-war scrap, a single long poem scratched into the concrete floor.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape is the Overseer reading her own original poem aloud.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine.

**Inhabitants.** An empty vault. The residents left years ago. One of them, an old ghoul, lives in a nearby settlement and still writes.

**Reward.** **The Long Poem** (_petty_ relic) — a PC who reads it may, once per session, automatically succeed on a single WIL save against despair, loneliness, or hopelessness. **The Gallery's Wooden Figures** — each worth 40 caps, but the Overseer may decide one of them is an original worth much more.

**Hidden.** Under the gallery's longest mural, the poem scratched into the floor continues beneath the wall — the residents kept writing *under* the concrete after they ran out of room. A PC who pries up the flooring finds **fourteen more stanzas** that were never seen, ending in the names of everyone who'd lived in the vault.

**Hook.** A ghoul poet in a nearby settlement wants to recover her own handwriting from the vault. She cannot go herself — the memories are too much.
