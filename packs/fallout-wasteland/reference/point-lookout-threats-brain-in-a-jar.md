### Brain in a Jar (Professor Calvert)

10 HP, 3 Armor, 3 STR, 8 DEX, 18 WIL, mesmerizer beam (d6, ranged)

- Professor Calvert, a pre-war brain preserved in a sealed tank.
- **Mesmerize**: As an action, any PC in line of sight makes a WIL save or spends their next action attacking an ally.
- **Vulnerable Shell**: A Critical Damage result on the tank glass ends the encounter immediately.
