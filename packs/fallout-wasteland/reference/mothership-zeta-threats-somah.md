### Somah (Abductee, Ally)

5 HP, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 11 WIL, pipe rifle (d6, _bulky_)

- A thawed wasteland survivor. Fights with the party; will not leave their side.
- **Grateful**: On the same round the party protects her from harm, her next attack is Enhanced.
