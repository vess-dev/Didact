### Father

— HP, — combat. A dying man on a leather chair.

- Shaun. Calm, articulate, sixty years old. Not a combat encounter. *Father is a WIL-save scene*. The party makes saves to keep their resolve when he offers them the Institute's directorship. Failing is not losing; it is *considering it*.
