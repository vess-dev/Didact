- **Knight-Errant** — the expedition's rank for the party, from whichever commander remains. Brotherhood Reactions start at **Kind**, and Fort Atlas's workshops are open.
- **Hellcat Power Armor** — the Hellcats' own design, recovered from Blackburn's hirelings. See Unique Armors.
- **Ultracite Power Armor** — if the party brings Scribe McNamara's old schematics from Fort Defiance to Valdez. See Unique Armors.
- **Taggerdy's Legacy** — handing Rahmani Paladin Taggerdy's ID from the **Fallout 76 main campaign** earns her personal gratitude and +1 with the Brotherhood.
- **Recovered Hellstorms** — each launcher returned to the Brotherhood earns 100 caps from the quartermaster; each one kept is a missile launcher (d12, _blast_, _bulky_) and a tick of the Hellstorm Clock.
- **Faction:** up to Idolized (+2) with the Brotherhood, one step at a time; −1 with the Crater if the Hellstorms were taken by force.
