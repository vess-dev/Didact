Four zones arranged around the central casino. The party may need to clear all three companion zones before the casino seals open.

- **The Villa.** Red-fogged streets, a fountain plaza, Ghost People hunting. A hologram of the starlet Vera Keyes flickers over the fountain, and Father Elijah speaks through it.
- **The Police Station.** **Dog/God's** territory. A nightkin with two minds in one body.
- **The Clinic.** **Christine's** territory. A mute Brotherhood Circle of Steel survivor with a voice-box scar.
- **The Residential District.** **Dean Domino's** hideout. A pre-war lounge singer turned ghoul, still holding a grudge about opening night.
- **The Casino.** Locked until all three others are free. A roulette wheel, a baccarat table, and a hologram dealer who won't take a bribe.
- **The Vault.** Below the casino. Thirty-seven gold bars and a sealed tomb behind them.
