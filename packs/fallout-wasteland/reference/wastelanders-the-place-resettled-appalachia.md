The main campaign's map, now with people on it. Pointcrawl nodes:

- **The Wayward.** A dram shop Duchess built from scratch where Routes 86 and 88 cross, below Vault 76 and across from the Overseer's old C.A.M.P. The closest thing Appalachia has to a town square.
- **WV Lumber Co.** The Free Radicals' fortified lumber yard on the Ohio River, taken from super mutants.
- **Gauley Mine.** Scorched, a wounded roboticist, and RobCo Auto-Cache 001 behind a keypad gate.
- **The Overseer's House.** A pre-war home on the hill east of Sutton, with a battered flag out front and Davenport in the basement.
- **AVR Medical Center.** Charleston's hospital, where the vaccine is made.
- **Foundation.** Log walls and a fusion generator on the site of the old Spruce Knob visitor attraction. A barracks, a boarding house, the Sunnys' shops, and Paige's Founder's Hall underground.
- **The Crater.** The crashed *Valiant-1* space station in the Toxic Valley, fortified with scrap. Its heart is the Core, where Meg holds court.
- **Vault-Tec University.** Morgantown. Professor-bot, an empty campus, a secret wing, and a vault simulator.
- **Hornwright Industrial.** Corporate headquarters in Charleston, with the Motherlode's egress pit beneath it, and the Hornwright Mega-Mansion where Penny's keycard codes are hidden.
- **Captain Fields' Bunker.** Across the river from the Valley Galleria. Signal from the dock by waving the flag.
- **Grafton Steel.** A poisoned steelworks in the Toxic Valley, full of security Protectrons and one small girl in the ducts.
- **The Watoga Civic Center.** An arena where slaves fight for raider crowds.
- **Vault 79.** Hidden in the Savage Divide behind an elevator in a "mysterious cave." Built from 2072 on, for 120 dwellers — its number chosen for the atomic number of gold.
