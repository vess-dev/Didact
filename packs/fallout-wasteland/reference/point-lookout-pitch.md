A paddlewheel ferry, the *Duchess Gambit*, at the Capital Wasteland docks advertises a weekend at **Point Lookout — the Last Resort!** The boatman, **Tobar**, charges in caps and doesn't ask names. The resort is a waterlogged horror show of punga farmers, Swampfolk, and a ghoulified British spy running a two-hundred-year feud with a psychic pre-war brain.
