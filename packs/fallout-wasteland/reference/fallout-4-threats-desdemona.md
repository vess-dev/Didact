### Desdemona

7 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 14 DEX, 16 WIL, silenced 10mm (d8), combat knife (d6)

- Railroad's head. Quiet, principled. Asks more than she orders.
- **Code Word:** Desdemona may, once per scene, speak a phrase and a hostile synth makes a WIL save or shuts down for one round. Works on Gen-2s reliably; on Gen-3s, rarely.
