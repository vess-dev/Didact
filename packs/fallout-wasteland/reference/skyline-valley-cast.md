- **Hugo Stolz** — Vault 63's overseer, a German-speaking magnate born blind, who now sees the world as pulses of electricity and calls it beautiful. Would do anything for Cassidy, and has. Charming, cultured, and never once sorry.
- **Cassidy "Cassie" Stolz** — Hugo's wife and partner in every deal, ghoul, comatose since 2095. She used to sit by the garden waterfall and pretend the war never happened.
- **Audrey Stolz** — their daughter, head of meteorology. Wanted to study the stars; was made to study weather instead. Loyal to her father until she isn't.
- **Hilda Stolz** — Hugo's niece, head of organics. A kind, prank-loving ghoul racing to cure the Lost, with her father on the table.
- **Chief James Oberlin** — head of vault security and the atrium's steady hand, still loyal to an Army that stopped answering long ago. Wants Hugo stopped but kept alive, because a living Hugo is the best chance of a cure.
- **Alex Guerra** — Audrey's girlfriend before the war, a communist Hugo tried to have killed. Leads a sham Mothman cult at Old Crimora Mines made of people with reasons to hate the Stolzes.
- **Alyssa** — a Foundation scavenger with bad theories and a good heart.
- **The Storm Goliaths** — **Decima**, **Morta**, and **Nona**, Vault 63's three guardian brain-bots.
