Three hundred rads in the Project Purity control chamber. Someone has to step into the radiation and enter the activation code. Resolutions:

- **Purify.** A PC enters the chamber and activates the purifier clean. The PC instantly fills all ten Rad slots (dropping to 0 HP) and resolves a Rad Scar save for each Rad as it lands; a PC already carrying Rad Scars almost certainly reaches a third. Any PC who does faces the ghoulification roll at the next long rest. The water runs clean. The Capital Wasteland is saved. The PC may return as a ghoul or as a face in a mural above the rotunda.
- **Send a ghoul or Super Mutant.** Fawkes, if recruited, walks into the chamber without harm. So can a ghoul PC — the Rads still fill their slots, but no Rad Scars follow. The purifier runs clean. The party stands in the rotunda and watches the water run blue for the first time in two centuries.
- **Poison.** If the party accepted Eden's vial and drops it into the purifier, the water kills every ghoul, super mutant, and "impure" being who drinks. The party's Karma slides toward −3 (Hated), one step per session as word spreads. The Enclave salutes. The Wasteland is cleaner, and much smaller.
- **Walk away.** The party refuses to activate. Autumn dies in the firefight regardless, but the dream dies with James. Brotherhood occupies the Memorial. The party is not welcome back to the Citadel. Leave the DC ruins behind.

Whichever ending the party picks, **Broken Steel** picks up the story two weeks later. A survivor crawls out of the rotunda with a limp and a half-remembered name, and the Enclave is not done.
