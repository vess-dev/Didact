*Slow Southern Gothic dread. Every character is someone's long-running grudge, every trail leads through a swamp that does not want you on it, and every drink is drugged. The party should feel like they've wandered into somebody else's generational argument and cannot leave until someone gets hurt.*

**Touchstones:** *True Detective* season 1, *Deliverance*, *Annihilation*, H.P. Lovecraft's *The Shadow Over Innsmouth*, *Point Lookout* DLC itself (the hallucinations, the obelisk, the punga).

**Lean into:** humidity, rot, the pollen that coats everything. No loud scenes. Combat should feel uncomfortable and improvised — boats, rope, waist-deep water. The supernatural reads are ambiguous; let the players decide whether Calvert's mesmerism is real or just chem-induced.
