In 2105, thunderheads settle over the southern mountains and do not leave. Lightning walks the ridges of **Skyline Valley**, a new region south of the Savage Divide, between the Ash Heap and the Cranberry Bog. Around the same time, a mangled vault door lands in the Forest, and an automated Vault-Tec broadcast begins calling for help. The door belongs to **Vault 63**.

Vault 63 was the private kingdom of **Hugo Stolz**, a Vault-Tec director who bought his way into an overseer's chair by promising the company research: weather control built from the military's shuttered **ATLAS Program**, and guardian robots built from stolen Pentagon plans. The vault was far from finished when the bombs fell. Radiation poured in, and everyone inside became ghouls. Then they began, one by one, to go feral. In 2095, desperate to save his wife **Cassidy**, Hugo ran the vault's weather machine at full power. It spared Cassidy — in a coma — and turned many of the dwellers around them into the **Lost**: crackling, psychotic ghouls whose senses have been scrambled into something else. It also gave Hugo, blind from birth, a way to see. In 2105 he ran the machine again, and made the Storm.

The last sane dwellers are sealed in the vault atrium: Hugo's niece **Hilda**, trying to cure the Lost; his daughter **Audrey**, trying to fix the machine; and **Chief James Oberlin**, head of vault security, trying to keep everyone alive. Hugo is helping them, graciously. He is also looking for a way to broadcast the signal inside his head through the machine and bring every Lost in the valley under his command.
