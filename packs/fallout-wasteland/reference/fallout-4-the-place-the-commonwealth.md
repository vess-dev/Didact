A big regional sandbox, crowded with factions. Pointcrawl nodes:

- **Sanctuary Hills.** Your pre-war home. Codsworth. A buildable settlement.
- **Concord.** The first fight. The Museum of Freedom. **Red Rocket** gas station on the outskirts, another buildable settlement.
- **Diamond City.** Boston's Fenway Park walled town. Piper, McDonough, Valentine's Detective Agency, the clinic, the marketplace, the gatekeeper Danny Sullivan at the entrance.
- **Goodneighbor.** The rough-side settlement. **Mayor Hancock**, a ghoul with a three-corner hat and a straight razor. **The Third Rail**, a bar. Drifters, chem dealers, **KL-E-O** the Assaultron weapons dealer.
- **Cambridge Police Station.** Paladin Danse's opening squad. Brotherhood recruitment.
- **Fort Hagen.** Kellogg's holdout. A pre-war military bunker full of Gen-1 synth patrols and one bad man.
- **The Glowing Sea.** Radioactive crater, deathclaws, Children of Atom faithful at the Crater of Atom, Virgil in a cave.
- **The Old North Church.** Railroad HQ, reached by following the **Freedom Trail** red stripe through a ruined Boston.
- **The Prydwen.** Brotherhood flying fortress. Anchored over the Boston airport ruins.
- **The Castle.** Minutemen HQ, a pre-war coastal fort in ruins, reclaimable midway through the campaign. Mirelurks in the sub-basement, a mirelurk queen in the courtyard.
- **The CIT Ruins / The Institute.** The final destination. The pre-war Commonwealth Institute of Technology, turned into the cover for a vast underground research facility. Spotless corridors, synths in uniform, children in school.
- **Spectacle Island.** A mirelurk-infested island that becomes a buildable hub if cleared.
- **Covenant.** A suspiciously clean settlement that tests visitors for synth-ness via the **SAFE test** — a polygraph by any other name. Cold War-era moral trap.
