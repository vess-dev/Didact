- **Devil's Blood** (chem, 1 use). The rarest high on the East Coast. For one scene, the user's attacks are Enhanced and they are immune to Critical Damage; when it wears off, they take 1d6 STR damage. **Addictive**; its addiction save counts as STR −4, and neither Addictol nor a clinic will break it — only the antidote. A single dose sells for 300 caps to Acee's high rollers.
- **Jersey Devil Blood** — each vial is either one dose of the antidote (cures a Devil's Blood addiction outright) or the raw stock for ten doses of the drug, worth 500 caps a vial, raw, to the Lombardis or the Russos. The adult Devil yields 1d4+2 vials.
- **The Russos' Gratitude** — a standing table at the Rose Room, free drinks, and Vin's word that the family owes the party one favor.
- **Faction:** +1 with the Munis for rescuing Sloane's crew; +1 or −1 with the Lombardi Family, depending on how the lab and the treaty fared.
