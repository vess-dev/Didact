*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** No resident over the age of fifteen. The vault was populated with children only, to see how a community of minors would self-organize.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Crayon drawings of families the residents remembered from before.
- **The Schoolhouse.** Somehow still mostly intact. The children ran it themselves after the doors sealed.
- **The Nursery.** The youngest residents lived here. Bottles, small beds, a stuffed bear with a Vault-Tec tag.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's role was filled by rotating fifteen-year-olds, each for exactly a week. The final holotape is from a child who knew her week would be her last, since there was no one left to replace her.
- **Reactor Room.** Maintained by a child prodigy who left a handwritten schematic on the wall.

**Inhabitants.**
- **Child Ghoul** — detachment. 2 HP, 6 STR, 12 DEX, 4 WIL, small teeth and nails (d4). Half the HP of a feral adult, still lethal in numbers. The Overseer should handle this encounter with care or skip it entirely.

**Reward.** **The Child Prodigy's Schematic** (Relic) — a technician studying it may install a Power Generator for half the usual cost. **A Stuffed Bear** (counts as Teddy Bear — see Relics).

**Hidden.** The nursery has a single crib with the name plate pried off. Under the mattress: a **hand-written election poster** — the children had apparently voted for a *permanent* Overseer at some point, and the winner's name matches the old ghoul the party may have spoken to before entering. She has been lying about leaving.

**Hook.** An old ghoul in a settlement was an original Vault 29 resident. She has asked the party not to disturb the vault. She will pay to make sure nobody else does either.
