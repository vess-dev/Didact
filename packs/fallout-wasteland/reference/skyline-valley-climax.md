**The Eye of the Storm.** Inside the Hawksbill weather station, past laser grids and Lost, Hugo waits beside the machine with Cassidy on the floor beside him, breathing hard. Audrey, on the intercom, begs the party to kill him — he is a master manipulator and he will escape. Oberlin, on the same channel, begs them to keep him alive for the cure. Hugo must be beaten three times before he kneels. Then he makes his own offer. Three resolutions:

- **Arrest Him.** Hugo surrenders. The party climbs the machine and powers it down, and the Storm begins to break. Oberlin becomes overseer; Hugo lives in a cell as the subject of the cure he never wanted to find. Audrey, betrayed again, stays on as a scientist — for now.
- **Kill Him.** Hugo dies. Oberlin wants the body recovered for research, and Audrey, listening on the intercom, shoots him for it. The party powers the machine down, and Audrey Stolz becomes the overseer of Vault 63, frightened of the job and determined to do it better than her father.
- **Walk Away.** Hugo promises the others will be safe and climbs the machine himself. The Storm swells. When the party returns to the atrium, Hilda, Audrey, Cassidy, and Oberlin sit on the floor around the overseer's desk, wailing, turning Lost, while Hugo smiles and calls them *found*. The Storm Clock fills.
