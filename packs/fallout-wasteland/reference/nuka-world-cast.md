- **Mags and William Black** — the **Operators**, the business gang. Efficient, greedy, reasonable.
- **Mason** — alpha of the **Pack**, dressed in deathclaw hide. Animal theatrics, genuine kindness to his own.
- **Nisha** — leader of the **Disciples**, a quiet blood cult. Terrifies even her own people.
- **Colter** — the **outgoing Overboss**. Dead in the Gauntlet by round three.
- **Gage** — Colter's former right hand. Offers himself as the party's guide. Loyal to whoever wears the crown.
- **Shank** — the gangs' logistics man. Hands out side contracts.
- **Mackenzie Bridgeman** — a *trader-doctor* the raiders have not yet killed. Contact for Commonwealth parties.
- **Nuka-Cola Family Spokesbot ("Nick Nuka")** — a pre-war Mr. Handy in a Nuka-Cola suit. Scripts, cheer, hidden violence.
- **Sierra Petrovita** — a Nuka-Cola superfan on pilgrimage from the Capital Wasteland. Already in the park. Already too far in.
