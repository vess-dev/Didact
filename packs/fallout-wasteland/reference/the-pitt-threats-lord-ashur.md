### Lord Ashur (in T-45d)

16 HP, 3 Armor, 15 STR, 13 DEX, 16 WIL, Gatling laser (d12, _blast_, _bulky_), power fist (d10, _bulky_)

- Ex-Brotherhood paladin, king of the Pitt, eerily calm under fire.
- **Visionary**: Automatically passes the first Morale save of any combat. Speaks during the fight.
- **Weak Points**: He wears no helmet — a called shot to his head (Impaired, as called shots are) ignores his Armor — and he will not fire the Gatling laser with Marie or Sandra in the room.
