*A nightclub opens in the Savage Divide, and on opening night hitmen from New Jersey shoot up the stage. The family that owns it came from a city the bombs never touched, a boardwalk town still running on casinos, dues, and a truce three factions are dying to break. The cure for their daughter is out in the Pine Barrens, and it has wings.*
