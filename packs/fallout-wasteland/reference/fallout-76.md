*Twenty-five years to the day after the bombs, the great cog of Vault 76 rolls aside on a green and empty West Virginia. The Overseer walked out hours ahead of you and left a trail of holotapes. Follow it far enough and you will learn why no one is left alive in Appalachia — and you will end the campaign holding the launch keys to three nuclear silos.*
