- **Shaun / Father** — your son. Taken as an infant. Sixty years old when you meet him. The director of the Institute. Dying of cancer. Asks you to inherit his life's work.
- **Codsworth** — the household Mister Handy. Two hundred years of grief and politeness. Potential companion (see **Mister Handy** background).
- **Preston Garvey** — last Minuteman. Holds the torch until he can hand it off. Sincerely, exhaustingly good. Potential companion.
- **Piper Wright** — Diamond City journalist. Chief writer of *Publick Occurrences*. Potential companion. Will interview the PC and publish the results.
- **Nick Valentine** — Gen-2 synth detective. Patterned his personality on a pre-war cop named Nick Valentine. Potential companion.
- **Mayor McDonough** — Diamond City. A Gen-3 synth replacement, if the party digs.
- **Paladin Danse** — Brotherhood squad leader. Honor-bound. Potential companion. *He is a Gen-3 synth*, though he does not know it. Finding out is his arc.
- **Elder Arthur Maxson** — Brotherhood elder, barely in his twenties, commanding the Prydwen. Hard, certain, young enough to grow up or young enough to be killed young.
- **Proctor Ingram** — Brotherhood scribe and engineer. Lost her legs in a power-armor fall; walks in a custom frame. Keeps the Prydwen flying — and rebuilds Liberty Prime.
- **Desdemona** — Railroad leader. Code name only. Heavy smoker, tired eyes.
- **Deacon** — Railroad spy with no confirmed face. Recruits the party. Lies about almost everything. Potential companion.
- **Glory** — Railroad heavy. A Gen-3 runaway synth. Potential companion.
- **Dr. Brian Virgil** — rogue Institute scientist turned super mutant, hiding in the Glowing Sea. Gave up his skin to escape. Will sell the Signal Interceptor specs for a favor — the return of a serum that restores his humanity.
- **Kellogg** — a century-old mercenary kept alive by cybernetic implants. Took Shaun. Flat affect, professional, kind to no one.
- **Hancock** — ghoul mayor of Goodneighbor. Robin-Hood demeanor, straight razor always on his belt. Potential companion.
- **Dogmeat** — a German Shepherd at the Red Rocket. Points. Potential companion.
- **John Hancock's brother (Mayor McDonough)** — a tie between Goodneighbor and Diamond City that the party can exploit or leave buried.
