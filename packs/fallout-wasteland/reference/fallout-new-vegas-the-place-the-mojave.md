The Mojave is a proper regional sandbox; treat it like the Capital Wasteland, big but knowable. Pointcrawl nodes:

- **Goodsprings.** Starter town. Doc Mitchell, Sunny Smiles, a single gas station, a cemetery with a shallow grave and your name on it.
- **Primm.** A broken casino town with a dead roller coaster looping over the interstate. Needs a sheriff.
- **Nipton.** Legion lesson. The crucified teacher on the front steps of the school is a picture to carry all session.
- **Novac.** Dinky the Dinosaur, a retired sniper in its mouth, a motel full of people who saw something.
- **Boulder City.** Ruined NCR checkpoint. Where the NCR proves it has rules of engagement and then breaks them.
- **New Vegas Strip.** **The Tops**, **Gomorrah**, **Ultra-Luxe**, and the **Lucky 38**. House's Securitrons stand motionless between games.
- **Freeside.** The seedy neighborhood outside the Strip. **The Kings** (an Elvis cult in the pre-war King's School of Impersonation) run it. The **Followers of the Apocalypse** run the local clinic from the Old Mormon Fort.
- **Camp McCarran.** NCR regional HQ. The airfield, the tents, the monorail to the Strip.
- **The Fort.** Caesar's Legion HQ on the far bank of the Colorado. Cross at the barge from Cottonwood Cove, after handing the legionaries your weapons.
- **Nellis AFB.** The **Boomers** — isolationist artillery clan with working howitzers and a museum of pre-war planes.
- **Jacobstown.** A settlement of lucid super mutants and nightkin at a pre-war ski resort, run by **Marcus**.
- **Hidden Valley.** A Brotherhood of Steel bunker, officially lost to history, not actually. Elder McNamara inside.
- **Red Rock Canyon.** **Great Khan** territory.
- **HELIOS One.** A pre-war solar array. Whoever reactivates it controls one of the Mojave's largest power sources. NCR wants it on; Legion doesn't care; House can redirect it.
- **Hoover Dam.** Still producing power. The NCR holds the western side, Legion the eastern. The climax lives here.
