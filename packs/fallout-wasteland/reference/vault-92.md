*Vaults of Fallout 3*

**Premise.** Advertised as a refuge for the country's finest musicians. Its real purpose: testing combat-priming audio broadcasts that induced aggressive behavior through white-noise signals.

**Layout.**
- **Recording Studios.** Still intact. Pre-war instruments hanging on the walls, a sound booth with a microphone labeled with a famous pre-war name — and, on a desk, the **Soil Stradivarius**, the most valuable violin in the world.
- **Sound Testing Wing.** Sealed soundproof rooms where the experiments ran. Dried blood on the soundproofing. The living-quarters terminals still offer a **Noise Flush** command that blasts the white noise at full power: it kills every bloatfly and mirelurk in earshot, and anyone else who hears it must make a WIL save or take d12 WIL damage.
- **Residential Block.** Picked over by raiders years ago, and flooded on the lowest level where an underground spring broke through the wall.
- **Overseer's Office.** A holotape of the final testing session. The Overseer begs the broadcast to stop. It does not stop.
- **Reactor Room.** The reactor is still running the broadcast faintly. Anyone in the vault for more than an hour must make a WIL save or attack a companion.

**Inhabitants.** BLOATFLY swarms in the upper levels and MIRELURKS in the flooded lower levels — up to a MIRELURK KING in the deepest water — all maddened by the lingering broadcast.

**Reward.** The **Soil Stradivarius** (Relic — see Relics), worth 400 caps to any pre-war music collector and far more to **Agatha**, the violinist whose radio station plays across the Capital Wasteland. A **Soundproofed Holotape** that plays a clean pre-war recording — once per session, the holder may use it to calm a single hostile crowd; each NPC in the group makes a WIL save or stands down for the scene.

**Hidden.** One of the soundproofed rooms has a scratched message on the inside of the door, below the hinge, in the Overseer's handwriting: *the broadcast is not from us*. The signal has an upstream source the experiment was only relaying. An Overseer may tie it to a separate pre-war installation if the campaign needs one.

**Hook.** A Followers of the Apocalypse medic has been tracking the broadcast signal. It's been getting stronger. She needs the party to find the source and shut it off before a nearby settlement snaps.
