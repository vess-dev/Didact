### Hijack

8 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 10 WIL, sledgehammer (d10, _bulky_), pipe revolver (d6)

- A Crater raider who wants to be one of Meg's top men. Holds Penny Hornwright hostage in the Cave beneath Hornwright Industrial.
- **All or Nothing:** Hijack can be talked down with a promised share of the gold and a clever argument, but he will not hear a second offer. If the first fails, he fights.
