The decision is not *whether* the party fights the White Legs. It is *what survives after*.

- **Graham's War.** The party helps Graham massacre the White Legs at the Three Marys. Salt-Upon-Wounds is executed. The Sorrows are armed and changed forever. The canyon is safe, and ruined.
- **Daniel's Exodus.** The party helps evacuate the Sorrows east to the Grand Staircase. The White Legs take the canyon. The Sorrows survive intact. Zion is lost but remembered.
- **The Third Path.** Kill Salt-Upon-Wounds *alone*, surgical, then lead the White Legs away without a general battle. The hardest. The Sorrows keep both the canyon and their innocence. Graham accepts it coldly. Daniel weeps with relief.
