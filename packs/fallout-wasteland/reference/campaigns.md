This book collects twenty-one short campaign frames — four large main arcs drawn from the big Fallout games, and seventeen shorter DLC and update frames that drop into any of them. Each is enough structure for four to ten sessions of play, stripped to a hook, a map, a cast, and a climax. They are not adventure paths. They assume the Overseer will improvise between the pointcrawl locations and reward the party for cleverness.
