You were shot, buried, and dug up alive by a **Securitron** named Victor. The man who shot you — **Benny**, a casino boss at the Tops — took the package you were carrying: a **Platinum Chip** commissioned by **Mr. House**, the reclusive pre-war CEO who secretly runs New Vegas from a penthouse atop the Lucky 38. The Chip is the key to House's endgame — an army of pre-war combat robots buried beneath the Fort, keyed to whoever plugs the Chip into the right terminal.

Three forces are converging on **Hoover Dam**: the **New California Republic**, a federal democracy stretched thin across a frontier it cannot quite hold; **Caesar's Legion**, a slave empire rolling west from Arizona; and **Mr. House**, who intends both of them to break on the Mojave's shore so Vegas can rise untouched. A fourth option — an independent Vegas piloted by a liberated Securitron AI named **Yes Man** — appears if the party kills Benny and takes his place.

Your first mission is simple: find the man who shot you. Every step after that builds toward the Second Battle of Hoover Dam.
