Broad strokes. Four to six sessions. Big MT is a sandbox — the labs can be tackled in any order. These are the scenes that matter.

1. **The Drive-In.** The party watches a reel and wakes at Big Mountain without their brains. Their bodies are fine. Their skills are fine. The loss is cosmetic and terrifying. Play the first scene as a cold shower of banter between the party, the Sink's talking appliances, and the five-panel Think Tank broadcasting from the dome.
2. **Meeting the Think Tank.** The party is introduced to Klein, Dala, 0, Borous, and 8 — the five floating brain-panels running Big MT. They are polite, petty, and completely disconnected from the war that ended the world. They want the party to fetch three pieces of tech from the crater's old labs. They also want to *keep* the party's brains — one of which Mobius has already stolen.
3. **The Sink Comes Online.** The party claims their home base. The toaster, the Book Chute, the Auto-Doc, Muggy, and the Central Intelligence all speak. Each wants one upgrade from somewhere in the crater. Turning the Sink into a real base is the B-plot of the campaign — and the stakes of the climax.
4. **X-13 Stealth Lab.** First mission. A pre-war stealth suit in a glass case that introduces itself as *Stealth Suit Mk II*. The lab's tests are the party's tutorial in the crater's strange tech. The suit joins the party as a semi-companion.
5. **X-8 Research Center.** Cyberdogs, nightstalkers, and the trail of the holotape that fixes the Sink's Auto-Doc — it ends in the **Y-17 medical facility**. A child's voice on the school PA. The lab was testing long-term conditioning on captives; the conditioning mostly took.
6. **X-42 Robo-Scorpion Warehouse.** The sonic emitter. A warehouse fight with smaller roboscorpions building up to a giant one — a proper boss. The emitter is the only weapon that will work on the climax.
7. **Little Yangtze.** The darkest detour. A pre-war internment camp where Chinese prisoners were held for Big MT's brain experiments — collared, abandoned when the bombs fell, and ghoulified by the fallout. Father Elijah used the camp as his base and learned to build his bomb collars from theirs; the party finds his workbench and sees what Dead Money's horror looked like when it was still an experiment. Trauma harnesses wait in a shed; the party may take one.
8. **The Forbidden Zone Dome.** Confronting Mobius. He is not the villain. He is the one sane member of the Think Tank who has been playing the villain to keep the other five from doing worse. His speech is the emotional center of the campaign.
9. **The Think Tank Confrontation.** The party returns to the dome with the truth. Klein refuses to hear it. The party can fight him, reason with him, or use the sonic emitter to shut the whole Think Tank down one panel at a time. Resolutions in the **Climax** below.
