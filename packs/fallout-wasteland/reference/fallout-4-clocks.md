- **Institute Clock** (d8). The Institute's readiness to act. Ticks when the party: visibly aids the Railroad or Brotherhood, kills a named synth without concealing it, acquires a piece of the Signal Interceptor, or is scanned by a Gen-3 infiltrator the party failed to identify. At 8, a **Courser** is dispatched (Bestiary).
- **Prydwen Clock** (d6). Brotherhood expansion. Ticks when the party accepts a Danse mission, refuses a Brotherhood requisition, or witnesses a Brotherhood purge of ghouls or super mutants without intervening. At 6, the Brotherhood sweeps a region the party cares about — a ghoul settlement, a synth safehouse, a lucid super mutant's home.
- **Railroad Clock** (d4, hidden). The Institute's net on the Railroad. Ticks when the party visits a Railroad safehouse in clothing or company that marks them (Brotherhood plating, an unscrubbed Pip-Boy, a known Institute synth companion), or when the Institute Clock ticks. At 4, a Railroad safehouse the party has visited is raided, and an NPC the party knows dies.
