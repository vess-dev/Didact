The park is a true open sandbox of themed zones, each with its own hook.

- **The Gauntlet.** The drop-in. A themed kill-corridor of traps, mutants, and one raider boss at the end, **Colter** (power-armored). Every PC takes d6 damage here regardless of skill.
- **Nuka-Town USA.** The hub. Market stalls, a slave pen (choose to free or ignore), the Overboss's residence.
- **Dry Rock Gulch.** Western theme. Bloatflies and bloodworms. A pre-war gunslinger protectron, **One-Eyed Ike**, challenges strangers to a duel.
- **Galactic Zone.** Sci-fi theme. Robots, a star-map terminal, a pre-war **Star Core** hunt for caps.
- **Kiddie Kingdom.** A fairytale theme. Ghouls. **Oswald the Outrageous**, a pre-war stage magician turned glowing ghoul, holds court over his feral troupe from the rides.
- **Safari Adventure.** Jungle theme. Gatorclaws, spawned without end by a runaway pre-war cloning replicator. **Cito**, a wild man raised by the park's ghoulrillas, protects his family from them.
- **Nuka-World Transit Center.** The far end of the Nuka-Express monorail, back in the Commonwealth: the way in, and the only fast way out.
- **Nuka-Cola Bottling Plant.** A *river of glowing Quantum*. Pre-war recipe terminals. A hidden **Cola-Nut** holotape carries the original formula for **Nuka-Dark** — the rum-strength **Nuka-Cola Dark** the wasteland still buys bottled, though no one alive can brew it fresh.
- **The Power Plant.** Beneath the park. Powering it on re-activates rides, vending machines, *and* the security grid — a dozen sentry bots and eyebots swarm the zones. A WIL save flips the grid to the party's side.
