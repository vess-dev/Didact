*Exhausted triumphalism. The Brotherhood just "won" but the win hasn't settled, the Enclave hasn't stopped, and Project Purity has a bullet hole in it nobody wants to look at. Water is sacred in this campaign — every character should be visibly drinking it, hoarding it, or withholding it.*

**Touchstones:** *Mad Max Fury Road* (water as politics), *Apocalypse Now* (the ride into the heart), *The Manchurian Candidate* (Enclave broadcasts), *Broken Steel* DLC itself, and the original *Fallout 3* ending the party gets to rewrite.

**Lean into:** the cost of victory. The Brotherhood is patching armor with duct tape. Every named NPC has lost someone. Let silence land when a character mentions Project Purity. A paladin who once quoted scripture now just drinks. And now their best weapon — their giant robot — is a smoking ruin in a crater.
