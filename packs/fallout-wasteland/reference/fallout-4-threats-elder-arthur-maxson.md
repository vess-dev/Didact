### Elder Arthur Maxson

10 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 14 DEX, 15 WIL, Final Judgment — Gatling laser (d12, _blast_, _bulky_)

- Brotherhood elder on the Prydwen's bridge. Young, certain, the only man who can call the Brotherhood's full strength on the party. See **Unique Weapons → Final Judgment**.
- **Mandate of Steel:** While he lives, Brotherhood units in his broadcast range automatically pass Morale saves.
