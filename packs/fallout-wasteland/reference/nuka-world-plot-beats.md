Broad strokes. Five to seven sessions. The opening is a violent railroad; after that, the park becomes a true sandbox.

1. **The Gauntlet.** The drop-in. A themed kill-corridor of traps, mutants, and raider hecklers on catwalks. Every PC takes d6 damage here no matter what. At the end, Colter waits in his power armor, announced as the Overboss. The fight is riggable — the Overseer should give the party a clever environmental cue to exploit (a loose cable, a water spout, a dangling cage).
2. **Meeting Gage.** The moment Colter falls, Porter Gage steps out of the shadows with a handshake and a pitch. *Gage rigged the Gauntlet on purpose.* He will serve whoever wears the crown next. He explains the three gangs, the open seat, and the short window before the gangs install their own Overboss. The Crown Clock starts ticking.
3. **Nuka-Town USA.** The hub. Market stalls run by gang quartermasters, a slave pen (choose to free or ignore — freeing is the first step of the *Liberate* climax), the Overboss's residence standing vacant. Sierra Petrovita, a Nuka-Cola superfan on pilgrimage, is here and will guide the party to the bottling plant if asked.
4. **Touring the Gangs.** The party visits Mags and William Black (Operators), Mason (Pack), and Nisha (Disciples). Each pitches their own vision for the park. Each offers the party a loyalty test — a job in one of the four themed zones that proves the party can work with them.
5. **The Zones.** Dry Rock Gulch, Galactic Zone, Kiddie Kingdom, Safari Adventure, and the Nuka-Cola Bottling Plant — five zones in all, the last one threaded by the World of Refreshment's river of glowing Quantum. Each zone is a pointcrawl dungeon with one iconic pre-war attraction and one iconic postwar inhabitant. The party should explore at least two; they will learn the park's pre-war weight only by walking through it.
6. **The Power Plant.** Beneath the park. Powering it on re-activates rides, vending machines, *and* the security grid. A dozen sentry bots and eyebots swarm the zones when the lights come up. A WIL save (or the right terminal password) flips the grid to the party's side — the single biggest lever in the campaign.
7. **The Gala Decision.** Gage presses the party for an answer: who wears the crown? The party can take the throne, play the gangs against each other in staged duels, ally with Mackenzie and liberate the park, or walk out and leave Nuka-World to fester. Each path reshapes the climactic fights.
8. **The Crown.** Whatever the party chose, a final scene resolves the park's rule. Taking the throne means dividing the park's five zones among the three gangs (see **Climax** below); liberating means a single bloody day. See the Climax section for all four resolutions.
