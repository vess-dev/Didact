You are a resident of **Vault 76**, one of Vault-Tec's seventeen control vaults: a showcase shelter in the hills north of Flatwoods, stocked with some of America's finest — Vault-Tec University graduates, soldiers, a former White House chief of staff. It was built to open twenty years after the war so that its people could walk out and rebuild. It waited twenty-five. On **Reclamation Day**, October 23, 2102, the door opens, the Reclamation Day banners flutter over the exit, and the Overseer is already gone.

Appalachia came through the bombs better than most places — few targets, deep hills, forests that went red and gold every autumn anyway. The people did not come through what came after. In twenty-five years, four factions rose here to save it: the **Responders**, volunteer firefighters and medics out of Charleston; the **Free States**, secessionist survivalists in their bunkers in the Mire; the **raiders**, rich tourists snowed into the ski resorts who turned on the world; and the **Brotherhood of Steel**'s Appalachian chapter, led by a former Army Ranger lieutenant named **Elizabeth Taggerdy**. Beneath a luxury resort, the **Enclave** plotted a war nobody else wanted. Every one of them is dead — the Enclave by its own hand, the rest to the **Scorched Plague**, spread by bat-winged **scorchbeasts** that boil out of fissures in the Cranberry Bog.

The Overseer left on a mission from Vault-Tec: find and secure the three nuclear silos of the Appalachian Automated Launch System. Her trail leads the party through every dead faction's last stand, and each one left a piece of the answer behind: the Responders' training, the raiders' uplink, the Free States' detectors, the Brotherhood's plan, the Enclave's keys. Put together, the pieces spell fire from the sky.
