### MODUS

— HP, — Armor, — STR, — DEX, 18 WIL, bunker turrets (d10), gas vents (STR save each round or d6 STR damage)

- The Whitespring bunker's artificial intelligence, spread through its walls. Cannot be attacked directly. Its memory banks behind the command wing can be destroyed (10 HP, 2 Armor), and every bunker service dies with it — doors, vendors, the orbital platform, the launch training.
- **Gracious Host:** MODUS never lies outright. It declines to answer instead.
