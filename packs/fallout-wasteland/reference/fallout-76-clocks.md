- **Chorus Clock** (d8). The scorchbeast hive mind learning the party's scent. Ticks when the party kills a scorchbeast, enters a fissure site, brings a Scorched Detector back online, or spends a night in the Cranberry Bog. At 8, the chorus turns on them: every Scorched anywhere in Appalachia is Hostile on sight — no Reaction roll — and a scorchbeast hunts the party's camp every third night, until the Queen dies.
- **DEFCON Clock** (d6). The launch system's automated threat rating — the machine Eckhart fooled. Ticks when a nuke is launched anywhere in Appalachia, when the party destroys a SAC cargobot, or when the party fights Liberators or Scorched inside a military installation. At 6, the system judges Appalachia a war zone: SAC dispatches a fresh keycard convoy (the next keycard is easy to find), and every silo's security robots are rearmed to double strength.
- **MODUS Clock** (d6, hidden). MODUS's suspicion. Ticks when the party lies to MODUS about an errand, damages bunker property, wears Brotherhood or Free States colors into the bunker, or presses it about the Enclave's civil war. At 6, MODUS seals the room the party stands in and begins pumping gas, as it did to Eckhart's dissenters. The party has three rounds to break out or to talk it down (a WIL save, which counts as WIL −4 unless someone holds a general's commission).
