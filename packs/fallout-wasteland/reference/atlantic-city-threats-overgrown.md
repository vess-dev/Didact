### Overgrown

5 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 10 DEX, 3 WIL, thorned lash (d8)

- Ambulatory mutant flora out of the Pine Barrens — "plantfolk" to the locals. Roots, thorns, and a blossom where a face should be.
- Groups of four or more are a _detachment_.
- **Rooted:** Fire damage against an Overgrown is Enhanced. On its first turn in any scene, it can hold still and pass for an ordinary thicket.
