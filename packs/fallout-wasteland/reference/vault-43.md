*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** Populated with twenty men, ten women, and one panther. The experiment's documented purpose was unclear even to the staff.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Scratch marks on the walls.
- **Residential Block.** Half of the rooms have been converted into a den.
- **The Hunting Grounds.** An open chamber, originally a gymnasium, where the surviving residents and the panther eventually formed something the Overseer will have to decide the shape of.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape asks the unseen observers what the point of the experiment could possibly have been.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine.

**Inhabitants.**
- **The Panther** — 12 HP, 1 Armor, 15 STR, 17 DEX, 8 WIL, claws (d10), bite (d8). Smaller-framed than a deathclaw but silent: it cannot be heard approaching by normal means.
- **Ghoulified Keeper** — 6 HP, 10 STR, 12 DEX, 12 WIL, pre-war hunting rifle (d10, _bulky_). Will not fight to the death if the party spares the panther.

**Reward.** **The Panther's Hide** — worth 500 caps; wearing it as a cloak counts as 1 Armor and the wearer cannot be detected by scent or sound while moving slowly. **A Resident's Final Letter** — a confession that the writer had learned to love the panther more than the other residents.

**Hidden.** In the hunting grounds' wall, behind a scratched-open panel, is a **Vault-Tec briefing memo** addressed to the observing staff. It explains the experiment's purpose: the panther was the control, not the subject. The men and women were being tested on whether they could form a society around a shared predator. They could.

**Hook.** A big-game hunter has offered a bounty for the Vault 43 panther. The ghoul keeper has offered a counter-bounty to anyone who protects it.
