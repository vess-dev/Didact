*Pulpy 1950s sci-fi by way of a ragtag mutiny movie. The aliens are cold and incomprehensible; the humans are from five centuries of history and don't understand each other either. The joy of this frame is the unlikely alliance of a samurai, an Army medic, a cowboy, and a wasteland wanderer yelling down a corridor together.*

**Touchstones:** *Mothership Zeta* DLC itself, *Flash Gordon*, *The X-Files*, *Alien* (the claustrophobic hallways, not the body horror), *Galaxy Quest*, 1950s drive-in flying-saucer serials.

**Lean into:** blinking consoles, unfamiliar metal underfoot, dialogue played for broad pulp rather than grim realism. Let NPC companions butt heads over things the players don't remotely care about (a samurai refusing a cowboy's hand) and play the aliens as silent until they aren't.
