Open wilderness. The party moves between camps. Each camp is a pointcrawl node.

- **Angel Cave.** Graham's base — a deep cave in the cliffs. The Dead Horses camp outside.
- **Dead Horse Camp.** Friendly tribals. Reaction roll Curious or better. Trade with them for jerky and healing poultice (_petty_, heals 1 HP).
- **Sorrows Camp.** The peaceful Sorrows tribe, hidden in the Narrows. Graham wants them armed. Daniel wants them evacuated.
- **White Legs Staging Ground.** Mountain pass. Their boss posts up here with scouts.
- **Zion's Overlook.** A view that makes people quiet. No combat allowed here.
- **The Three Marys.** A canyon kill-box on the river below the White Legs' main camp. Either side's final ambush plays out here.
- **A Pre-war Ranger Station.** A pre-war survivalist's bolt-hole. Cache of .45 pistols, survival gear, and a private journal that reads like scripture to the Sorrows.
