Broad strokes. Five to seven sessions. The first half is a frontier town finding its feet; the second half is a heist.

1. **The Wayward.** Duchess hires the party to stop the gang harassing her bar. The trick is to impersonate Crane: build a "Crane Treasure Hunting" beacon, broadcast it from relay tower EM-B1-27, and see who comes calling. The trail leads to the Free Radicals' lumber yard on the Ohio River and their leader, **Roper**, the former head guard of a Maryland prison who freed its convicts and made himself their boss. He can be killed, bargained with, or talked into leaving the Wayward alone.
2. **The Elusive Crane.** Duchess shows the party what is in her locked upstairs room: Crane himself, caught by the plague in Gauley Mine and Scorched on her front porch, kept alive behind a door she could not bring herself to open. Kill him or let him be — either way, his map and keycard lead to RobCo Auto-Cache 001 deep in **Gauley Mine**. Crane never got in: the cache only opens for a registered Pip-Boy, and hardly anyone outside Vault 76 wears one. Down there they also find Duchess's friend **Sol**, wounded among the Scorched, with his Assaultron **Polly** headless beside him.
3. **The Overseer Comes Home.** A new station crackles on the Pip-Boy: the Overseer, alive, calling her dwellers to her house on the hill above **Sutton**. She keeps a Mister Handy named **Davenport** and a year of secrets. She wants to know whether the party finished what she started, and she wants the newcomers inoculated against the plague with the vaccine the party can synthesize at the **AVR Medical Center**.
4. **Foundation and the Crater.** Paige will listen, but he wants his co-founder and doctor, **Aubrie Willem**, to confirm it. Aubrie's camp near Vault 96 has been overrun by Scorched, and he is hiding in a cave, terrified the party are Scorched themselves. The raiders are harder. **Rose** vouches for the party to Meg, and Meg lets them into the Crater's Core on sufferance.
5. **Overseer, Overseen.** The files on Vault 79 are in the secret wing of **Vault-Tec University**, and **Professor-bot**, the Protectron who runs the empty campus, will only open it for a graduate. The party is fast-tracked to the final exam: an overseer test in a vault simulation, refereeing a feud between Mister Handy engineers and medics over a sealed reactor and a missing assistant. Pass it, and the truth is in the wing: all the gold from the Fort Knox depository was moved into Vault 79 before the war.
6. **Choosing a Crew.** Both factions want in, and neither will share a plan with the other. The choice is final. **Foundation's job:** **Dr. Penelope "Penny" Hornwright**, a ghoul who once ran automation for Hornwright Industrial, can wake the **Motherlode**, her family's giant mining robot, to drill straight into the vault. Young **Jen** has an answer for the laser grids. **Captain Oliver Fields** and his Army team — the men who moved the gold in the first place — can walk the party through the turret gauntlet called the **Grim Reaper's Hallway**. Then a Crater raider named **Hijack** kidnaps Penny and wrecks the Motherlode's brain. **The Crater's job:** **Lucky Lou**, a hundred-year-old ghoul engineer, is the demolitions expert, and he has gone missing. **Gail**, a super mutant, needs help first: **Ra-Ra**, the child she has raised as her own, chased a thief into the Grafton Steel works to get back her stuffed rabbit, Bunnabun, and has not come out. **Gentleman Johnny "Two-Guns" Weston** will join only if the party frees his old partner, **Hal Gleeson**, from the slave arena at the Watoga Civic Center. Then a raider named **Lev** steals Lou's detonator and leaves him tied up for a yao guai.
7. **The Heist.** Vault 79: turrets, robots, laser grids, the Hallway. Behind the barracks door, the party finds survivors: Secret Service agents **AC**, **Slick**, **Regs**, and **Mercy**, sealed inside since the war. Their reactor tech, **Digger**, turned ghoul in the reactor accident that trapped them; he is the only one of the reactor crew who did not go feral. The agents will trade the gold for their freedom. Venting the reactor restarts the vault, sets its security booting back up, and lifts their lockdown. Then Regs and Mercy want Digger shot, and AC asks the party whether a ghoul can be trusted.
8. **Division of Wealth.** The gold processing room holds 1,000 bars of bullion — a suspiciously small share of a national reserve. The main vaults are still sealed. The party must decide who gets what. See **Climax**.
