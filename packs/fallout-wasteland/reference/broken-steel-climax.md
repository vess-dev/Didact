Sarah's assault hits the base exterior. The party is on the crawler's command deck, at the satellite targeting console.

- **Strike Adams AFB.** Target the orbital weapon at the base itself. The Enclave is annihilated. The crawler is destroyed. The party escapes on a Vertibird. The Brotherhood cheers.
- **Strike the Citadel.** Target the Brotherhood. The party has turned. The Enclave survives, the Brotherhood is crippled, and every NPC in the Capital Wasteland remembers who pushed the button.
- **Disable the satellite.** Shut the uplink down. The Enclave's last card is neutralized, but their troops escape on the last Vertibirds. They'll be back — as a problem for a future campaign.
- **Ride it out.** The party steals the presidential Vertibird and the targeting codes. They drop one step with the Brotherhood but gain a working Vertibird (one flight per campaign — treat it as a vehicle, see Vehicles) and a weapon no one else knows they have.
