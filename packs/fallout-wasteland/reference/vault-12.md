*Vaults of Fallout 1 & 2*

**Premise.** Vault 12's door was designed to not fully close, so Vault-Tec could observe how a community survived direct exposure to radiation. Everyone ghoulified within a year. The survivors became the ghoul city of **Necropolis**, built into the ruins of Bakersfield above.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** The door is fused half-open. A pre-war wind moves through it constantly. A Rad storm haunts the threshold — anyone entering takes 1 Rad immediately.
- **The Welcoming Chamber.** Skeletons in vault jumpsuits cluster around the door, their bones so old they crumble to ash when touched.
- **Residential Block.** Still inhabited by a small ghoul community. They remember the day the door wouldn't close.
- **Overseer's Office.** Sealed. Inside, a ghoul who was the Overseer's intern, still at his desk, still filing reports nobody will ever read.
- **Reactor Room.** The reactor is failing. A GLOWING ONE lives in the coolant pool.

**Inhabitants.** FERAL GHOUL PACK (outer levels), GHOUL COMMUNITY (residential), GLOWING ONE (reactor).

**Reward.** An **Overseer's Holotape** detailing the deliberate sabotage of the door. The intern-ghoul will hand it over if someone listens to his whole story first. A **Pre-war Medical Kit** (3 Stimpaks, 1 RadAway).

**Hidden.** Behind the reactor room's failing coolant pool, welded to the underside of the shielding, is a small lead-lined box containing the **original Vault 12 door schematic** with the sabotage marked in red. Worth 600 caps to a Brotherhood scribe; worth more to the ghouls of Necropolis, who always suspected and never had proof.

**Hook.** A caravaneer asks the party to escort a young scribe who wants to interview a ghoul who remembers the war. The ghoul lives in Vault 12.
