You step out of the elevator at Vault 111's entrance into morning sun over the Boston Basin. Sanctuary Hills is down the hill — the same street you lived on, the same red house, two centuries of weather. The household **Mister Handy** unit, **Codsworth**, is still tending the garden. He is the only being in the world who will recognize you. He tells you someone has taken your baby and walked into the world. He suggests you start in **Concord**, the nearest settlement, and ask about the trail.
