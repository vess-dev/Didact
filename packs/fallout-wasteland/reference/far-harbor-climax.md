The fog is rising. The three factions are at the tipping point. DiMA's secret — that **Avery is a synth replacement for the woman DiMA killed** — will unbind everything when it surfaces.

- **Peace.** The hardest ending. Tell Avery what she is, on her own terms. Hide the truth from Far Harbor at large. Convince Tektus to step down (an inside job via Sister Mai). All three communities remain. Far Harbor becomes a Commonwealth settlement with three permanent NPCs.
- **Wipe out Acadia.** The synths die. Far Harbor survives. Atom survives. The party carries a heavy bond.
- **Wipe out the Children of Atom.** Acadia survives. Far Harbor survives. A gentler ending, but Atom cells in the Commonwealth will remember.
- **Wipe out Far Harbor.** Only Acadia and the Nucleus remain. The fog takes the town. The party should not feel clean about this one.
- **Take Kasumi home or leave her.** Separate from the climax. Her family pays either way; the caps are dirty.
