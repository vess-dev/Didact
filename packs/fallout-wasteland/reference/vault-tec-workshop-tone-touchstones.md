*Cheerful complicity. Barstow is sweet, well-spoken, and absolutely committed to running human experiments until she dies of old age. Every scene should play this dissonance — she asks the party for their help like a neighbor asking them to water a plant.*

**Touchstones:** *Vault-Tec Workshop* DLC itself, *BioShock* (the dead utopia), *The Stanford Prison Experiment*, *Portal* (a cheerful voice asking you to do a monstrous thing), *Fallout 3*'s Vault 112 / Tranquility Lane, classic Vault-Tec recruitment posters.

**Lean into:** unfinished concrete, blueprints on walls, Barstow's polite voice over intercom. The vault is a *construction site* — no carpets, no furniture, just the promise of a future. The moral pressure is constant: Barstow keeps asking for *one more experiment*, and each one is individually reasonable until the party zooms out.
