A boardwalk city between the Pine Barrens and the Atlantic. Pointcrawl nodes:

- **The Rose Room.** The Russos' supper club in the old Ingram Mansion in the Savage Divide, recently a cultist shrine, now all velvet and brass.
- **The Whitespring Refuge.** The Responders' new home on the resort grounds, and the vertibird pad for Acee.
- **The Casino Quarter.** Dry, humid, vine-choked streets around the Lombardis' spotless establishments: **Quentino's Night Club** and the **Neapolitan Casino**.
- **The Atlantic City Boardwalk.** The Showmen's territory. Stage shows, arcades, rigged games, and **Showman's Pier**, which runs out to the **Aquarium of the Atlantic**, the Showmen's underwater arena.
- **The Flooded City Center.** Streets under water to the second floor, rowboats, rooftop walkways, Muni pump stations, City Hall, the community center, and the Lombardis' warehouse and hidden lab.
- **The Pine Barrens.** The forest on the city's edge, where the Overgrown grow and the Jersey Devil nests. Few who go in come back out.
