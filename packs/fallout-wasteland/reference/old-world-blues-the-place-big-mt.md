Big Mountain is a crater full of labs. The party can enter them in any order.

- **The Sink.** Home base. Talking appliances, a toaster with opinions about the pre-war West, a **Central Intelligence** AI that offers upgrades in exchange for tech.
- **The X-8 Research Center.** Cyberdogs and children-sized lobotomized subjects. The holotape that fixes the Sink's **Auto-Doc** waits in the **Y-17 medical facility**.
- **The X-13 Stealth Lab.** A pre-war stealth suit in a case, talking to itself.
- **The X-42 Giant Robo-Scorpion Warehouse.** A warehouse with a robo-scorpion *boss* built from the parts of smaller ones. Beating it unlocks the **sonic emitter**. The warehouse terminals can turn its own turrets on the giant, and pulse weapons are Enhanced against it.
- **Higgs Village.** A pre-war cul-de-sac preserved under a warehouse roof — the Think Tank's own houses, from back when they had bodies. The mailman is a lobotomite. One house has a body in the basement — an earlier Courier.
- **The Think Tank Dome.** The Think Tank's floating panels. Central HQ.
- **Forbidden Zone Dome.** Mobius's territory. Roboscorpions, traps, a screen that insults you.
- **Little Yangtze.** A pre-war internment camp for Chinese prisoners, now home to their ghoulified survivors — still collared, still behind the wire. Father Elijah's abandoned workbench is here. Trauma harnesses wait in a shed.
- **The Crater Road.** The outer ring. Lobotomites wander it like sheep.
