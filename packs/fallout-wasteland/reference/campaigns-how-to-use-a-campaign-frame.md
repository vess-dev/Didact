Each frame has the same skeleton:

- **Pitch** — one paragraph to read to the table.
- **Tone & Touchstones** — the mood, the touchstones, and the one thing an Overseer should reach for when describing the place. This is not a mechanic; it is the frame's thesis. Read it before the first session and once again before every subsequent one.
- **Arrival** — how the party is drawn in. Use it as-is or re-skin.
- **Plot Beats** — six to ten turning points that form the spine. Not scripted scenes — signposts.
- **The Place** — a handful of pointcrawl locations with their texture and danger.
- **Cast** — the NPCs who matter, with a one-line agenda each.
- **Clocks** — background timers the Overseer advances on specific triggers (party action, faction deed, a milestone reached). Clocks never tick on session count or scene count alone; each clock's entry names what ticks it.
- **Threats** — which Bestiary entries to use, plus full stat blocks for campaign-specific enemies.
- **Rewards** — relics, perks, caps, or faction rep the party may walk out with.
- **Climax** — the final confrontation or choice. Multiple resolutions are listed; none is "correct."

A campaign ends when the climax resolves. The party then returns to the main wasteland — richer, scarred, and possibly ghoulified.
