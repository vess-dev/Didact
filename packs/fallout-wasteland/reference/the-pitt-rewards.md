- **The Cure (Relic).** A vial of Marie's blood, 3 doses. Each dose, given over a full week's treatment, removes one Rad Scar or one other radiation-born mutation. Spoils after a season unless stabilized at an auto-doc.
- **The Mauler** (unique auto-axe, d10, _bulky_ — see Unique Weapons).
- **The Metal Blaster** (unique laser rifle, d10, _blast_, _bulky_ — see Unique Weapons).
- **Ashur's Power Armor** — if Ashur falls. See Unique Armors.
- **Union Power Armor** — the Pittsburgh Union's own suit, poured in these mills a century and a half before Ashur and left rusting in a sealed corner of the steelyard. See Unique Armors.
- **Steel Ingots** — the steelyard's prize; Everett pays out a piece of gear for every ten delivered.
- **Faction:** +1 Pitt Slaves or +1 Pitt Raiders depending on the climax.
