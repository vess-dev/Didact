- **The Tesla Cannon** (d12, _blast_, _bulky_ — see Unique Weapons) — the first off Vallincourt's bench.
- **Enclave Hellfire Armor** (unique armor — 3 Armor, _bulky_, fusion core; immune to fire and flamer damage, cannot catch fire). See Unique Armors.
- **Presidential Metro Access Key** (relic) — one free fast-travel per session between downtown D.C. and Adams AFB.
- **Faction:** up to Idolized (+2) with the Brotherhood of Steel, one step at a time, or, if the party turns the satellite on the Citadel, Vilified (−2) with the Brotherhood for good and a quiet Enclave contact who surfaces later.
