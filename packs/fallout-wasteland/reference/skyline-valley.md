*A storm that never ends has settled over Shenandoah National Park. A vault door came down in a field north of Sutton, and a Vault-Tec broadcast nobody has heard in years is inviting Vault Dwellers south. Beneath the storm, a blind billionaire has learned to see by lightning, and he means to make everyone else see the way he does.*
