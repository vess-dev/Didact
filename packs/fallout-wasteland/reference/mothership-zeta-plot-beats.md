Broad strokes. Four to six sessions, end-to-end, linear on purpose. The ship does not let them go back.

1. **Waking in the Cell.** The party comes to in stasis tubes. A Zetan worker is reading vitals from a glowing panel. Sally is already out of her tube, in the next cell, and hisses at the party to *play dead*. The escape window is short. The first alien they kill is the one that was tending them.
2. **Finding Somah (and the Others).** The first friendly abductee. Somah is bewildered but capable. She reveals there are *more* humans scattered across the ship and asks the party to find them. This scene sets the co-op tone: the party is not alone, but they are also not in charge.
3. **The Cryo Labs.** Rows of frozen humans from every era. Thawing them brings allies — Paulson the cowboy, Toshiro Kago the samurai, Elliott Tercorien the medic, and Sally's sister if the Overseer wants a heartbreak. Colonel Hartigan's pod is here too, but he has been dead inside it since the 1960s — his spacesuit is the prize. Each thaw gives the PC who opens the pod **1 Fatigue** from the coolant leak. The party picks its ensemble here.
4. **The Weapons Lab and the Relic Locker.** Alien weaponry on autopsy benches. Most of the party's original gear is in a secure locker near the bridge; getting it back is its own detour. A PC who can read an alien panel may reprogram one alien weapon to recognize them as *crew*.
5. **The Engineering Deck.** The reactor core, gravity generators, and the **Captain's Log** — a translated holotape that explains why humans are being collected. The party can sabotage the gravity here to turn off the ship's death ray, at a cost: the act locks the hangar behind them.
6. **The Observation Deck.** A silent scene. A full window on Earth, in orbit, at night. No save and no mechanical effect — the Overseer asks each player, *what does your character think when they see home from here?* Record the answer. It will matter at the end.
7. **The Bridge Assault.** The climax. The Captain, his Guardians, and the ship's consoles. The fight is winnable in a straight shootout but more interesting if the party has pulled strings in the earlier beats (sabotaged gravity, reprogrammed an alien weapon, rallied the abductees).
8. **The Hangar Escape.** However the bridge resolves, the party leaves by stealing an intact saucer from the hangar bay. The saucer becomes a relic for the rest of the campaign (see Rewards).
