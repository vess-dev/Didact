- **Tobar the Ferryman** — captain of the *Duchess Gambit*. Takes a hidden toll from sleeping passengers. Charming.
- **Desmond Lockheart** — ghoul, pre-war British intelligence, hates Calvert with the heat of two centuries.
- **Professor Calvert** — a brain in a tank. Charismatic, threatening, promises a cure for ghoulification.
- **Obadiah Blackhall** — patriarch of the Blackhall family. Wants the **Krivbeknih**, his family's stolen occult tome, back.
- **Plik** — a ghoul running a feral-ghoul safari for paying hunters. Charges a rich man's entry fee.
- **Marguerite** — a moonshiner in a stilt shack. Sells a unique brew (**Marguerite's moonshine** — heal d4 STR, take 1 Fatigue).
- **Jackson** — tribal leader of the Brethren, interpreter of the god on the dish, sane in his fashion.
