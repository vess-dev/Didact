It is 2103. The Vault 76 dwellers have beaten the Scorched back. The plague is not gone, but it is no longer the end of the world, and word has spread. The first living strangers in a quarter-century are walking into Appalachia, and they are all here for the same thing.

Two factions have planted flags. The **Settlers** of **Foundation**, a log-walled town on Spruce Knob, came south from the ruins of Pittsburgh under a builder named **Paige**. The **Crater Raiders**, led by **Meg Groberg**, are old Appalachian gangs come home. They have fortified the wreck of the pre-war space station *Valiant-1* where it fell in the Toxic Valley. Between them are treasure hunters, ex-convict **Free Radicals**, the vicious **Blood Eagles**, and a bar called **the Wayward** that is trying hard to stay neutral.

Then the Overseer comes home. She has two goals. The first is to inoculate every newcomer against the Scorched Plague before it starts over. The second is to open **Vault 79** — the rumor's vault, built in secret to hold America's gold reserve — and use that gold to found a currency, an economy, and a peace. She cannot do it without a crew, and she cannot have both crews. The party will have to choose.
