Broad strokes. Six to eight sessions. Reorder, skip, or reshape as the table pushes. Each beat is a turning point, not a scripted scene.

1. **The Escape.** Radroaches and vault security in the upper corridors. Amata begs the party to run, not fight — her father, the Overseer, will execute any vault dweller caught abetting you. Getting out without killing vault security sets up a return arc that rewards a peaceable player late. The last door opens onto a hillside and a horizon that keeps going.
2. **Megaton and the Bomb.** Descend into the crater. **Mister Burke**, a lean man in a suit at Moriarty's Saloon, offers the party 500 caps to arm the undetonated bomb at the town's center. **Sheriff Lucas Simms** asks the party to disarm it instead. Whatever the party does becomes their reputation on the radio, read aloud by a DJ who was not there.
3. **Following the Trail.** Moriarty remembers James. James asked about Galaxy News Radio, looking for **Three Dog**, looking for a working piece of pre-war comms hardware. The trail runs through a bombed-out DC, super mutants in the Metro tunnels, and the GNR plaza where a Brotherhood of Steel squad is pinned down by a **Super Mutant Behemoth**. Help them and Three Dog will hear about it before you reach the door.
4. **Three Dog's Request.** Three Dog will share what he knows about James if the party repairs the Galaxy News Radio dish — mounted atop the Washington Monument, guarded by a super mutant sniper. Completing the repair makes the party's deeds audible across the Wasteland. Karma from here on is amplified — Three Dog will either call them *saints* or *monsters*, and strangers will react accordingly.
5. **Rivet City and Dr. Li.** The trail leads to a pre-war aircraft carrier beached on the Potomac. **Dr. Madison Li**, James's old colleague, reluctantly confirms James returned to the Jefferson Memorial — the original Purity site. She thinks it's a mistake. She's about to be proven right.
6. **The Jefferson Memorial.** The party reaches the rotunda and finds James. They also find **Colonel Augustus Autumn** already on approach with an Enclave strike team. Autumn demands the activation codes. James refuses — and dies flooding the control chamber with radiation to deny the Enclave the purifier. The party escapes through a flooded maintenance tunnel, emerging, half-drowned, in the Taft Tunnel.
7. **The Citadel.** The Brotherhood of Steel has taken the Pentagon ruins. **Elder Owyn Lyons** offers an alliance: find a **Garden of Eden Creation Kit** — a pre-war terraforming device — and bring it back to finish what James started. The G.E.C.K. is in **Vault 87**, overrun by super mutants, accessible only through the long caverns behind **Little Lamplight** — the vault's own front door sits in the Glowing Zone's lethal radiation. The party can also rescue a captive **Fawkes** from Vault 87 — a lucid super mutant who can walk through the Rad field of the purifier chamber and help them.
8. **Raven Rock and Eden.** Captured, interrogated by **President John Henry Eden** — a pre-war ZAX AI that has convinced itself it is the last legitimate American government. Eden hands the party a vial of **modified FEV** and explains its purpose: released into the purifier, the water itself will kill every mutant, ghoul, and "impure" wastelander who drinks. The party can accept the vial, refuse it, or talk Eden into his own self-destruction — a WIL save the party can only attempt with transcripts mined from Raven Rock's terminals.
9. **Liberty Prime and the Final Push.** Brotherhood and Enclave meet on the broken tidal basin. **Liberty Prime** — a forty-foot pre-war combat robot — leads the charge. "Democracy is non-negotiable." The party fights their way toward the control room where Colonel Autumn has begun a final activation countdown.
10. **The Activation Chamber.** Three hundred rads in the rotunda. Someone has to step into the radiation and enter the code. See **Climax** below.
