### Rust Devil Scavenger

5 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 9 WIL, pipe rifle (d6, _bulky_), robotic arm-blade (d6)

- A raider grafted onto salvaged robot parts, picking over whatever the Mechanist's machines leave behind — and stealing the machines when she can.
- **Scavenged Frame**: On Critical Damage, she swaps in a salvaged limb and keeps fighting for one more round before dropping.
