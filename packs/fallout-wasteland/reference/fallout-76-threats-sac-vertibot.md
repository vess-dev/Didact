### SAC Vertibot

6 HP, 2 Armor, 6 STR, 15 DEX, 8 WIL, chin laser (d8)

- An unmanned vertibird drone, painted in Air Force grey, guarding the cargobots of the automated nuclear network.
- **Flight:** Ranged attacks against a vertibot at altitude are Impaired. On any round it drops low to fire its chin laser, they are not.
