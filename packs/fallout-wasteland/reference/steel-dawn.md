*The Brotherhood of Steel crossed a continent to find its lost Appalachian chapter and found only graves. Now the expedition has a fort, a mission, and two commanders who cannot agree on what the Brotherhood is for. Whoever the party stands beside when the argument ends decides what kind of knights Appalachia gets.*
