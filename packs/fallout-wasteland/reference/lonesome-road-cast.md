- **Ulysses** — the villain. A pre-war-loving former Legion frumentarius. Wears a long coat and a flag around his throat. Speaks in parables. Honest.
- **ED-E** — the eyebot. A holotape companion with recordings from Navarro and Hopeville — some of them *from* the PC. Think of it as a conscience in a can.
- **Waking Cloud's voice** (optional) — if the party did Honest Hearts, she speaks to the PC once over a dead radio, offering forgiveness in advance. If not, a stranger says her lines.
- **A Marked Centurion** — a named NPC, speaks once through a broken helmet, asking the party why.
