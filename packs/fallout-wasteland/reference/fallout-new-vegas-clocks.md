- **Dam Clock** (d12). The armies converge. Ticks when a PC publicly pledges to a faction, the party takes an outlying asset (Khans, Brotherhood, Boomers, HELIOS One), a McCarran or Fort mission is completed, or any FNV DLC reaches its climax. At 12, the Second Battle begins whether the party is ready or not.
- **Benny Clock** (d4). Benny's flight. Starts once the party has spoken to Manny Vargas. Ticks when the party finishes a major Strip-facing scene without confronting Benny. At 4, Benny flees the Strip for the Fort — where the Legion takes him prisoner, a gift kneeling in Caesar's tent.
- **Legion Frumentarii Clock** (d6). How many of Vulpes Inculta's spies the party has tripped. Ticks when a named Legion agent escapes, a Legion-sympathizer NPC is left alive after questioning, or Vulpes himself is seen and not killed. At 6, a four-man Legion hit team tries to assassinate the party during a scene of the Overseer's pick.
- **Courier Clock** (d8, hidden, Overseer-only). Ticks when the party first enters a major settlement (Primm, Novac, the Strip, the Fort, Camp McCarran, Nellis, Jacobstown, Hidden Valley). On each tick, the Overseer narrates a letter, a rumor, or a voice that recognizes the PC as *the Courier who survived*. At 8, the whole Mojave knows the story before the party walks in.
