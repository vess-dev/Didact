The Overboss seat is empty. Gage is asking who sits. Resolutions:

- **Take the throne.** The party becomes the Overboss. They must then divide the park's **five zones** among the three gangs — satisfying any two gangs and disappointing the third will trigger a civil war the party must suppress. Succeeding makes the park the party's permanent base.
- **Liberate the park.** Ally with Mackenzie, free the slaves, and use the power plant's security grid against the raider gangs. The three gangs die in a single bloody day. The park becomes a trade-hub settlement.
- **Play the gangs against each other.** Sequential duels with each gang leader, staged as park spectacles. The party walks out with the three bosses' own weapons and a reputation that follows them forever. The park dies behind them.
- **Walk out.** Monorail back to the Commonwealth and never look back. Gage leaves in disgust. The park becomes an enemy stronghold for a later campaign.
