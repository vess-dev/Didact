Ada (and any other modular robot the party recovers from the Mechanist's factory) uses a simple rebuild loop. The rebuild is a downtime activity, not a full background.

- **Station**: a Weapons Workbench *or* a Workshop / Scrap Bench. If neither is available, a field hack at 1d4 STR cost to the tinker is allowed at the Overseer's discretion.
- **Cost per rebuild**: 1 Downtime Action, **3 Scrap**, and **1d6 × 20 caps** in parts. Each rebuild swaps the module loadout completely.
- **Base chassis**: 5 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 10 WIL, and a built-in ram (d6). This is what the robot has with zero modules installed.
- **Pick two different modules** from the list below and install them. A third module may be installed only at a **Power Armor Station** and costs an additional 4 Scrap and 200 caps.

| Module | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Melee Arm** | Primary attack becomes a ripper/saw arm (d10). |
| **Ranged Arm** | Primary attack becomes a shoulder-mounted carbine (d8). |
| **Sensor Module** | The robot cannot be surprised, and detects hidden enemies within 40 ft in terrain it has previously traversed. |
| **Armor Plating** | +1 Armor (to a maximum of 3). |
| **Speaker Module** | Once per combat, the robot broadcasts a staged threat: anyone within 20 ft makes a WIL save or their attacks are Impaired for one round. |
| **Fusion Booster** | Once per session, the robot moves at double speed for one scene and may ignore terrain penalties. Burns one fusion cell. |
| **Medical Dispenser** | Once per scene, the robot administers a Stimpak (1d6 STR) from internal stores. Holds 3 doses; refilled at a Chemistry Station for the Stimpak's normal cost. |

A rebuilt robot that takes Critical Damage falls to pieces but is *not* destroyed — a Downtime Action and 2 Scrap at a Workshop restores it to the base chassis. Modules lost in the fall are gone.

If the party took the **Take the throne** climax and gained a robot production line, the same Robot Rebuild Procedure is used to build each new bot. Roll the 1d6 rogue-check *after* modules are installed, not before.
