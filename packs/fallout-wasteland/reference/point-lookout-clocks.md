- **Feud Clock** (d4). Lockheart vs. Calvert. Ticks when the party accepts a task from either faction, refuses a bribe from either, or is seen on the other side's ground. Each tick the two sides escalate; at 4, they'll try to use the party as the final weapon.
- **Swamp Clock** (d6). The bog gets *weirder*. Ticks when the party chews a punga fruit, kills a swampfolk elder, touches the Obelisk, or spends a watch unsheltered in the bog. Each tick, a new horror surfaces: 1 punga dreams shared, 2 swampfolk multiply, 3 a whale on the beach, 4 a flood, 5 a storm. At 6, the Obelisk sings.
