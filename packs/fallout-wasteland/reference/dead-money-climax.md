The vault is open. Elijah is on the radio telling the party to bring the gold. Four endings:

- **Trap him in the vault.** Lure Elijah down, close the door behind him. The collars deactivate. The gold is his. The party escapes with whatever fits in *one* slot.
- **Kill him on the catwalk.** He wears his tattered elder's robes and carries a Gauss rifle. **Father Elijah**: 15 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 16 WIL, Gauss rifle (d12, _bulky_). On Critical Damage, the party may loot his pack (3 Perk Magazines of the Overseer's choice).
- **Join him.** Take the gold together, raise an army, come back in a later campaign as the antagonists of someone else's game. The Overseer writes that down.
- **Begin again.** Leave the gold. Shut the vault. Walk out with the collar's disarm code and a lesson. The wasteland forgets nothing, but the party gets to.
