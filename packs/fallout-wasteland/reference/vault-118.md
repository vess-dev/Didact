*Vaults of Fallout 4*

**Premise.** An ultra-luxury vault hidden beneath the Cliff's Edge Hotel on the Island. Ten of the richest people in Maine were to live like royalty, above the law, while up to three hundred working-class locals were packed into a cramped second wing to serve and suffer under their judgment. The money ran out before the second wing was built. Instead, a General Atomics robotics designer, Dr. Bert Riggs, convinced his fellow guests to have their brains placed in Robobrain chassis to outlast the war. Their robot staff has kept them in luxury ever since.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A chandelier, carpet, a bellhop's abandoned podium.
- **Luxury Suites.** Ornate cabins, most still occupied — by Robobrains with very human voices, very old opinions, and nothing but time.
- **The Grand Dining Room.** Every evening at six, a pre-war meal is served by automated dumbwaiters to the nine surviving Robobrain residents. None of them can eat. None of them miss a meal.
- **The Second Wing.** Bare concrete and rebar where three hundred people were meant to be crammed. Construction stopped when the money did.
- **The Brain Lab.** The transplant chamber itself. One empty jar.
- **Overseer's Office.** Never used by an Overseer — the vault was never finished. The hotel's owner kept it for himself.

**Inhabitants.** NINE ROBOBRAINS with elegant manners, long grudges, and a murder mystery — one of them killed the hotel's owner, **Ezra Parker**, within the past week, and none of them will admit who. A Mister Handy butler named **Maxwell** runs the investigation's paperwork, a Miss Nanny named **Pearl** runs the gift shop, and PROTECTRONS keep order. FERAL GHOULS, fog-soaked, fill the hotel above. The Robobrains will parley first.

**Reward.** A **Robobrain Chassis Schematic** — a tinker with this plan can build a Robobrain from a corpse, given a brain and a Power Generator. A **Pre-war Luxury Watch** (400 caps).

**Hidden.** The Overseer's office holds Ezra Parker's real ledgers: the hotel's owner took every guest's fortune and quietly stopped paying for the vault the moment their brains were in jars. Someone found them the day before he died. A PC who raises the ledgers at the dinner table triggers a chain of accusations that crack the mystery open.

**Hook.** Pearl, the hotel's Miss Nanny, has been asking around Far Harbor for a police detective: there has been a terrible murder at Cliff's Edge, and the residents are offering significant caps to anyone who can solve it.
