The expedition's map of Appalachia. Pointcrawl nodes:

- **Fort Atlas.** The ATLAS Observatory in the Savage Divide: domes, laser arrays, a command center, and mines and caverns underneath. Petitioners at the gate, initiates on the walls.
- **The Retreat.** A tree-house village in the Mire, rebuilt by **Jennie Brown**'s people in the Free States' abandoned settlement.
- **Dagger's Den.** A Blood Eagle stronghold full of Brotherhood gear.
- **Foundation** and **the Crater.** The Settlers and Raiders from **Wastelanders**, both holding Hellstorms.
- **The Enclave Research Facility.** Under a transmission station in the Forest. SODUS, its endless queues, and its Scorched residents.
- **The Uncanny Caverns.** A pre-war tourist cave in the Savage Divide, sold on the legend of the Night Kid, a boy raised by bats. Now a super mutant highway.
- **Big Bend Tunnel.** The railway tunnel under the mountains between the Ash Heap and the Cranberry Bog, now the Blue Ridge Caravan Company's trade route.
- **Vault 96.** At the end of Route 103, south of Foundation. Advertised as an ark for Appalachia's plants and animals, secretly a genetics experiment whose residents died trying to escape. Now Blackburn's lab.
- **West Tek.** The research facility in the Savage Divide where Huntersville's super mutants were born, and where Blackburn means to finish what West Tek started.
