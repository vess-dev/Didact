### Storm Goliath

18 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 8 DEX, 14 WIL, lightning cannon (d12, _blast_), fists (d10)

- One of three brain-bot guardians — Decima, Morta, Nona — built by Vault 63's engineers from Liberty Prime's plans, with a two-hemisphere brain behind a glass eye. Quotes philosophers mid-battle to confuse its targets.
- **Charging:** The lightning cannon fires only every other round. On the rounds between, the glass eye flares and the air hums as it builds, and the Goliath fights with its fists.
- **Call the Storm:** Once per combat, the Goliath summons lightning onto a point it can see: everyone within 10 feet makes a DEX save or takes d10 damage.
- **Self-Destruct:** At 0 HP, the Goliath kneels, counts down aloud for one round, and explodes (d12, _blast_).
