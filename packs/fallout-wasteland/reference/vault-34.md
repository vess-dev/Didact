*Vaults of Fallout: New Vegas*

**Premise.** The armory was overstocked with guns and the living quarters were not built for the crowd. When overpopulation came, the Overseer locked the armory. One group of dissidents grabbed every gun they could carry and stormed out — their descendants are the **Boomers** of Nellis. Years later, a second revolt tried for the armory and wrecked the reactor's cooling vents instead. The radiation that followed trapped the rest inside and ghoulified most of them.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** The main door has not opened since the second revolt.
- **Residential Block.** Ghoulified residents still walk here — some of them are armed.
- **The Armory.** A massive pre-war arsenal. Locked behind a keyed door the original Overseer sealed during the riot.
- **Flooded Lower Levels.** Radioactive water has filled the bottom two floors. Any PC entering adds 1 Rad per watch.
- **Overseer's Office.** Drowned. The terminal still runs, but the holotape inside is water-damaged and contains only the final two minutes of the Overseer's last entry.

**Inhabitants.** FERAL GHOUL WITH ASSAULT RIFLE (multiple), GLOWING ONE in the flooded level. A handful of survivors are barricaded in a sealed wing, dying of the radiation and still hoping someone will open the door.

**Reward.** **The Armory** — on gaining access, roll twice on the Loot Roll Tables' *Random Weapon* and take both results. A **Pre-war Riot Shotgun** (d10, _blast_, _bulky_) pulled from an Overseer's desk. Two sealed crates at the back hold the **All-American** and the **YK42B Pulse Rifle** (see Unique Weapons), if the Overseer wants them found here.

**Hidden.** At the back of the armory, mounted in a display case behind a smashed glass pane, is a **pre-war ceremonial rifle** — engraved, polished, absurdly heavy. It was meant as a retirement gift for the Overseer, delivered the week before the bombs. Worth 1000 caps to the right collector. Worth far more as a symbol of what pre-war life almost looked like.

**Hook.** A scavver company has been trying to crack the Vault 34 armory for two years. They'll split the haul 50/50 with any crew willing to go in first.
