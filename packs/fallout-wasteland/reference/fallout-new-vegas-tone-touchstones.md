*A post-apocalyptic Western with a neon spine. The Mojave is big, dry, and full of people who have opinions about their neighbors. The Strip is a pre-war casino still lit up like the bombs never fell. Every faction thinks it's the reasonable one, and the Overseer should let each make a real case.*

**Touchstones:** *Fallout: New Vegas* itself, *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly*, *Unforgiven*, *No Country for Old Men*, *Deadwood*, *Casino*, *Seven Samurai* (the Boomers' refuge, the Kings' neighborhood). Classic Western and classic Mob, turned down and burned in the sun.

**Lean into:** the buzz of a working streetlamp at dawn, the clink of poker chips, a Legion eagle nailed to a fencepost. Mr. House speaks from a monitor without moving. Caesar speaks from a throne without standing. The NCR officer speaks from under a too-small helmet and a too-big contract.
