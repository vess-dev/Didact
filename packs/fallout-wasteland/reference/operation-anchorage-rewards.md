- **Sim Loadout (temporary):** T-51b Power Armor suit, Gauss Rifle, Stealth Suit, Missile Launcher — chosen at drop-in. All vanishes on ejection. Inside the simulation, every PC counts as power-armor-trained — the pods flash the drill straight into the motor cortex. A PC who plays the simulation through to the end keeps that training for good: it counts as the power-armor training Downtime Action, already paid.
- **Real loot from the cache:** T-51b Winterized Power Armor (see Unique Armors), Chinese Stealth Armor (cloaks for one scene, once per day — see Unique Armors), Jingwei's Shocksword (unique, d10, ignores 1 Armor — see Unique Weapons), Neural Interface Suit (relic — see Relics).
- **Faction:** +1 Brotherhood Outcasts rep, −1 Brotherhood proper rep (they'll hear).
