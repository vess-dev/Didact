### Dr. Klein (and the Think Tank)

*Non-combatant.* The Think Tank are floating brain-panels in a domed room. They have no weapons and no means to fight the party directly. They are an authority, not an encounter. If threatened, Klein opens every lab in Big MT against the party — the lab creatures are the threat, not the panels. If the party physically smashes a panel, it takes one solid hit to destroy (3 HP, no Armor) and the Think Tank collectively panics.
