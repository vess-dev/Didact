### Vault-88 Radscorpion Queen

20 HP, 3 Armor, 17 STR, 12 DEX, 7 WIL, stinger (d12), claws (d10)

- Nest-mother of the Deep Caverns. Armored above, soft beneath — attacks from below or behind ignore her Armor.
- **Burrow**: Once per combat, when first reduced to 0 HP, she retreats into the wall and returns the next round at half HP.
