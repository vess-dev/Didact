- **Paladin Leila Rahmani** — expedition commander from Lost Hills, a National Guard veteran who survived the bombs beside Alan Connors and joined the Brotherhood at its founding. Compassionate, pragmatic, willing to break the Codex to protect people, and hiding a disaster. Fights in T-51 power armor with a Gatling laser and a war glaive.
- **Elder Roger Maxson** — the Brotherhood's founder, offstage at Lost Hills. Rahmani's old friend, and the man who forbade Taggerdy her nukes.
- **Knight Daniel Shin** — her second-in-command. Rigid, honest, loyal to the Elders, and grieving his mentor. Believes the Brotherhood's first duty is to keep weapons like the Hellstorms out of the wrong hands, and that Rahmani's kindness got Connors killed.
- **Scribe Valdez** — brilliant, overworked, and the only one at the fort who never stops. Loyal to the mission more than to either commander.
- **Initiate Russell Dorsey** — a Maryland settler who found the dead Appalachian Brotherhood's traces and fell in love with the idea. The Brotherhood's biggest fan.
- **Marcia Leone** — a civilian member of the expedition who calls Shin a "militant asshat" to his face.
- **Initiates Hewsen**, **Ramirez**, and **Norland** — wastelanders in borrowed steel, some of whom will not survive the campaign.
- **Dagger** — a Blood Eagle boss who follows no one and never set out to lead a gang. People just kept following her.
- **SODUS** — an Enclave AI with a fourteen-thousand-hour task queue and a growing wish to hurt its visitors.
- **Dr. Edgar Blackburn** — a former West Tek scientist who wants to atone for the super mutants by perfecting the virus that made them. Courteous, patient, and monstrous.
- **Dr. Farha**, **Jain**, and **Nellie** — Blackburn's colleagues, frightened researchers who know too much.
