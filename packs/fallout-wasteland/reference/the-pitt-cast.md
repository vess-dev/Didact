- **Lord Ashur** — ex-paladin, genuinely believes his project is mercy. Scarred, radiation-resistant, calm.
- **Sandra Kundanika** — his wife, a doctor, holds the research notes.
- **Marie** — their infant daughter. Immune.
- **Wernher** — the man who brought you. Wants the baby for the resistance. Wants revenge more.
- **Midea** — inside woman, tired, pragmatic.
- **Mex** — sadistic Pitt Raider who guards the gate to Downtown. Rifle, mines, a short fuse.
- **Krenshaw** — Ashur's second-in-command. Returns the gear of arena champions; watches Midea for the first sign of revolt.
