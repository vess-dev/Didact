- **Glitch Clock** (d6). The sim's varnish cracks. Ticks when the party kills a character the sim flagged as a non-combatant, uses tactics not in the mission briefing, tries to leave the mapped play area, or damages a fixed set piece. Each tick reveals a layer: 1 enemy reskin, 2 weather lock, 3 invisible wall, 4 friendly fire enabled, 5 Chase goes off-script, 6 reality bleed.
- **Chase Clock** (d4). General Chase's patience with unorthodox tactics. Ticks when the party disobeys a direct order from Chase, questions the mission briefing openly, or steals rather than sabotages. At 4, he orders the party arrested by Sim-MPs.
