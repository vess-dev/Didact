The Vault 101 door opens at 0300 hours. Vault-Tec klaxons, Amata's scared voice over the PA begging you to leave, and the Overseer's guards closing from both corridors. You step out of the vault into the unblinking light of the Capital Wasteland. Down the hill is **Megaton** — a settlement built around an unexploded pre-war nuclear bomb. James passed through hours ago. Someone at Moriarty's Saloon remembers him.
