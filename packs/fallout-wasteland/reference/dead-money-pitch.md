A mysterious radio broadcast lures the party to an abandoned **Brotherhood bunker** in the Mojave. Inside: a knockout gas, a collar around their necks, and the voice of **Father Elijah** — a Brotherhood elder gone mad. He will detonate the collars unless the party helps him breach the Sierra Madre Casino and open its vault.
