### Overseer Valery Barstow

10 HP, 1 Armor, 9 STR, 12 DEX, 18 WIL, 10mm pistol (d8), lab scalpel (d6)

- Vault 88's ghoulified pre-war Overseer. Calm, brilliant, monstrous.
- **Terminal Override**: Once per combat, she activates every Protectron in the vault against a single target for one round.
- **Smiling Voice**: Automatically passes Morale saves.
