### Fiend

6 HP, 13 STR, 15 DEX, 7 WIL, varmint rifle (d6, _bulky_) *or* machete (d6)

- Chem-addled raider detachment from the West Vegas ruins. Individual or detachment. Each group carries a chem stash (1d4 random chems).
- **Jet-High:** On the first round, Fiends may take a free move action before their attack.
