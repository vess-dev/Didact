### Mister House

— HP, — combat. Voice on a monitor.

- Speaks only through the Lucky 38's PA or a Securitron proxy. Unkillable until the party breaches his life-support chamber and severs his feeds — a scene, not a combat.
- **Terms:** Mr. House's contract is literal. Breaking it activates every Securitron on the Strip against the party.
