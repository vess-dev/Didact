The Brotherhood's welcome broadcast plays on every radio in Appalachia: Fort Atlas is open, and the knights want to hear from the locals. At the gate, a Foundation settler named **Russell Dorsey**, newly made an initiate and ecstatic about it, explains that he helped the Vault 76 dwellers turn the observatory into a fort. Inside, the party walks in on Rahmani and Shin mid-argument about the line of civilian petitioners outside. Shin is ordered to deal with them. He hands the job to the party.
