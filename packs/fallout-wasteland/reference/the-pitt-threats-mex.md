### Mex (Gate Guard)

10 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 14 DEX, 10 WIL, assault rifle (d10, _bulky_), frag mines (d10, _blast_)

- Sadistic Pitt Raider who guards the gate to Downtown. Likes mines and watching slaves run.
- **Mine Layer**: The approach to his gate is mined; any PC who rushes in without scouting makes a DEX save or takes d10 _blast_ damage.
