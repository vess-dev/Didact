### ED-E (Companion)

4 HP, 1 Armor, 4 STR, 13 DEX, 10 WIL, built-in laser (d6)

- A battered eyebot. Potential companion, not a combat workhorse. Has a pre-war holotape library.
- **Memory Core**: Once per session, ED-E may play a holotape that reveals a Divide clue from the Overseer.
