- **Purity Clock** (d6). The Enclave's approach. Ticks when the party first reaches the Memorial, when James is named on open radio, when Dr. Li is recruited, and at each subsequent plot beat toward activation. At 1–3 the Enclave is distant; at 4–5 they have scouts in the Wasteland; at 6 they assault the Memorial.
- **Eden Clock** (d4). President Eden's awareness of the party. Ticks when the party passes through an Enclave checkpoint, when a named Enclave officer survives and returns a report, and when Three Dog broadcasts a deed Eden would care about. At 4, Enclave strike teams hunt them on sight.
- **Karma Clock** (Party Karma ladder; see Overseer's Guide → Party Karma). Ticks when the party makes a major public choice — the bomb, the slavers, Tenpenny, Paradise Falls — shifting karma ±1, at most one step per session. Three Dog's broadcasts amplify the effect for first-meeting reactions — for as long as he is talking about the party, treat their Karma as one step further from 0 (to the ladder's ends) on Reaction rolls in the Capital Wasteland.
