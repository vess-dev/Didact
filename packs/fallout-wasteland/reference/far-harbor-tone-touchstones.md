*A slow-burn noir in a fishing town the fog is eating. Three communities hate each other, and none of them are entirely wrong. The Overseer's job is to make every side sympathetic enough that the party genuinely struggles to pick one — and then to make the Fog roll in when they hesitate too long.*

**Touchstones:** *Far Harbor* DLC itself, *The Thing* (paranoia in an isolated settlement), *The Mist* (the fog as antagonist), Stephen King's Maine broadly, *Blade Runner* (who is synth, who isn't), *True Detective* season 3 (the long-form mystery).

**Lean into:** damp wood, salt, and a horizon that is never fully visible. DiMA speaks in measured pauses. Tektus preaches in verse. Avery hides a truth from herself. Let the fog eat the scenery when the players push — a point of interest should sometimes simply not be reachable this session.
