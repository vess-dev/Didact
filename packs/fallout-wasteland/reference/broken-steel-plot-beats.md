Broad strokes. Five to seven sessions, structured as a salvage-run into a suicide mission.

1. **Liberty Prime Falls.** The campaign opens with the aftermath — or, if the Overseer is bold, with Prime's last assault (see Bestiary → Liberty Prime). The Brotherhood deploys Prime against the Enclave's satellite relay station. An **orbital strike** from the Bradley-Hercules satellite drops from the sky and annihilates Prime in a single shot. The Brotherhood retreats in shock. Sarah Lyons is wounded. Both clocks start here — the Brotherhood is already planning its answer, and the Enclave knows it is reeling and is preparing to press the advantage.
2. **The Citadel Briefing.** Scribe Rothchild has a plan. The Brotherhood needs a **Tesla Cannon** — a weapon that can crack Hellfire power armor and Enclave shielding. To build one, they need a **Tesla coil** from the Olney Powerworks beneath Old Olney. And to reach Adams AFB, they need the **Presidential Metro** powered up. The party is handed both jobs.
3. **Old Olney and the Tesla Coil.** A deathclaw-infested town between the Citadel and the Powerworks. The party can blast through, sneak through, or find a sewer route scribes have marked on an old map. The **Olney Powerworks** lies beneath — a pre-war research facility holding a working Tesla coil. The facility is guarded by Enclave deathclaws in implant collars. Retrieving the coil lets Scribe Vallincourt build the Tesla Cannon.
4. **Waking the Metro.** The pre-war Presidential Metro is still parked in its station. **M.A.R.Go.T.**, the line's transit computer, greets the party in scripted pre-war courtesy. The train needs a working fusion core and a cleared line — a car full of ferals has to be dealt with before the train can move. The ride itself is one of the safest scenes in the campaign; let the party breathe.
5. **Under the Flight Deck.** The Metro drops the party into a service hatch beneath Adams's main runway. Above them: Vertibirds, fuel depots, Hellfire Troopers patrolling in pairs. The party's first job is *not* to be seen. This is the scene where stealth matters most; failure means the Assault Clock jumps two ticks.
6. **Boarding the Crawler.** Whether by ambush, stealth, or force, the party gets aboard the Enclave mobile base. The crawler is the Enclave's last major installation — command deck, barracks, reactor, holding cells. Enclave officers and Hellfire troopers man the halls. The **satellite uplink** is on the command deck.
7. **The Satellite Uplink.** The climax. The party reaches the command deck and the satellite targeting console. Sarah Lyons's frontal assault is hitting the base exterior. The party has control of the **Bradley-Hercules orbital weapon** — the same one that killed Liberty Prime. They choose the target. Resolutions in the **Climax** below.
