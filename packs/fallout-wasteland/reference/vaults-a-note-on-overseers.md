Every canonical vault had an **Overseer** — a Vault-Tec employee responsible for running the experiment and reporting back to a central database that no longer exists. Most Overseers are long dead, their terminals still running, their records still readable. The Overseer's final log is almost always the key to understanding what happened.

When a party breaches an Overseer's office, reward them with one of:
- A **holotape** revealing the truth of the experiment
- A **passcode or keycode** that unlocks a deeper area
- A **map fragment** pointing to a related vault or pre-war facility
- A **personal effect** (a photo, a letter, a signed confession) that turns the Overseer from a monster into a person
