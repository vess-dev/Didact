- **Pick a vault** that matches the current campaign's theme. Vault 11's democracy-by-execution fits a story about complicity; Vault 22's plant-gene experiment fits a story about nature reclaiming the world; Vault 112's virtual-reality torture fits a story about the pre-war past refusing to die.
- **Read the Premise** to understand what went wrong. This is the vault's thesis statement and the reason the party should care.
- **Use the Layout** as a room-by-room scaffold. Each room is one or two sentences of what's there, what the party notices, and what might happen. Expand them into full rooms at the table.
- **Fold in the Inhabitants** as encounters. Where a stat block is named in all caps (e.g., FERAL GHOUL PACK), use the matching bestiary entry. Where no entry matches the name exactly, use the nearest one: raiders of any stripe (Khans, Fiends, Blood Eagles) are THUGS led by a CREW BOSS; vault security and vault residents are SETTLER MILITIAMEN armed with a police baton (d6) or a 10mm pistol (d8); a "pack" or "crew" is a _detachment_.
- **Deliver the Reward** as relics, perk magazines, holotapes, or unique loot. Many vaults contain one specific thing worth the trip.
- **Hang the Hook** on an NPC in the party's home settlement who needs something from this vault, or on a rumor the party overhears on a caravan route.
- **Honor the Hidden.** Every vault entry has a **Hidden** note — a thing the party will probably miss. Do not hand it to them. Make them earn it.
