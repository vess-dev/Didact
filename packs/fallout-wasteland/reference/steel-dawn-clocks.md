- **Schism Clock** (d6). The rift between Rahmani and Shin. Ticks when the party openly backs one commander against the other's orders, when Brotherhood technology is lost to outsiders, when an initiate dies on a mission, or when the Codex is broken in front of the ranks. At 6, the expedition splits before the Climax: Shin's loyalists and Rahmani's followers stop taking each other's orders, and the party must declare for one of them on the spot.
- **Hellstorm Clock** (d4). Launchers loose in Appalachia. Ticks when the party lets a Hellstorm stay in raider hands, keeps one for itself, trades one away, or fails to recover it after a fight. At 4, a raider warband levels a settlement the party cares about with Brotherhood missiles, and everyone in Appalachia knows whose they were.
- **Mutagen Clock** (d8, hidden). Blackburn's progress. Ticks each week the party does not find him, each time another civilian disappears, and each time his Hellcats take a fight. At 8, Blackburn's refined virus is ready: he walks out of Vault 96 already transformed, and West Tek begins shipping FEV.
