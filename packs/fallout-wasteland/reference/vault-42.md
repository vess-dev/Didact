*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** All light bulbs in the vault were limited to 40 watts, creating a permanent low-light environment. The experiment measured the psychological effect of extended visual deprivation.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Dim. Every corner is in shadow regardless of how hard you look.
- **Residential Block.** The residents grew larger, paler eyes over generations. Their descendants, if any, will be sensitive to surface light.
- **The Darkroom Wing.** A sealed area where the experiment was most intense. Any PC entering must make a DEX save to navigate by feel; on a fail they blunder into something loud — roll on the Ruin Events table. Light sources are dimmed to one-third strength here by an unknown pre-war effect.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape is whispered — the Overseer herself could not stand loud sound after years of dim light.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine.

**Inhabitants.**
- **Pale Ghoul** — detachment. 5 HP, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 4 WIL, claws (d6). Attacks are **Enhanced** in darkness and **Impaired** in any strong light source. A bright flare or an open blast door shuts them down fast.

**Reward.** **A Pale Ghoul Eye Lens** (_petty_ relic) — a PC wearing it sees normally in darkness at a distance of 20 ft but is blinded by full daylight — ranged attacks in direct sun are Impaired. **A Pre-war Low-Light Lantern** — runs for twice as long as a normal lantern.

**Hidden.** The darkroom wing has a pre-war photographer's darkroom at its deepest point — a small red-lit lab the residents had built in secret. On the line: dozens of **developed photographs** taken with a hidden camera inside the vault itself. The subjects are the Overseer and her private visitors, who should not have been there.

**Hook.** A settler's child has gone missing into Vault 42. She can see in the dark now, and she doesn't want to come home.
