*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** All entertainment holotapes were removed from the vault except those of a single, particularly bad comic actor — and the man himself was given a berth. The experiment measured the residents' tolerance for poorly crafted humor over an extended period — sociologists predicted Vault 56 would fail before Vault 55.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A pre-war stage with a single spotlight.
- **The Theater.** Rows of uncomfortable chairs facing the stage. The audience is still there.
- **The Comedian's Dressing Room.** A cracked mirror, a pre-war joke book with every joke crossed out, a note that reads *I know it isn't working*.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape is her, weeping, describing the comedian's seventh year of the same routine.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine.

**Inhabitants.**
- **The Comedian** — 5 HP, 8 STR, 10 DEX, 14 WIL, no weapon. A lucid ghoul who has told the same routine for a century. He will not harm the party.
- **Audience Ghouls** — detachment, treat as Feral Ghouls. They will **not** attack while a joke is being told from the stage. Any PC who can hold the mic for a full scene holds the room.

**Reward.** **The Pre-war Joke Book** (_petty_ relic) — reading it in downtime is genuinely unpleasant, but a PC who endures the full read may, once per session, automatically succeed on a WIL save against boredom, tedium, or sustained psychological pressure (from sheer endurance). A single functional **Stand-Up Spotlight** (_bulky_) — at a settlement with power, it installs as a **Lit Signs & Streetlamps** building at no cost.

**Hidden.** Under the comedian's dressing room vanity, taped to the bottom of a drawer, is a **single handwritten joke** that *is* funny. The comedian wrote it the week before the vault sealed and never told it on stage. It's written in his own hand. He does not remember writing it. If the party reads it to him, he laughs once — and then weeps.

**Hook.** The Comedian knows he is not funny. He has written a new routine. He wants a real audience one last time — people who came in from outside. He is willing to trade anything in the vault for the chance.
