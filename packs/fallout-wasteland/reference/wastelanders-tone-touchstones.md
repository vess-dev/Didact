*A heist western about coming home. After a campaign of holotapes and dead voices, Appalachia is suddenly full of loud, living, lying people, and every one of them has a reason to want the gold. The Overseer's job is to make the party feel the weight of being the locals now — the ones who know where the Scorched nest, which mines are safe, and which stranger will shoot first.*

**Touchstones:** *Fallout 76: Wastelanders* itself, *Ocean's Eleven* (assembling the crew is the campaign), *The Treasure of the Sierra Madre* (gold makes everyone worse), *Deadwood* (a saloon as the heart of a lawless town), *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly* (three parties, one grave full of gold), *Hell or High Water*. Bluegrass on the Wayward's jukebox.

**Lean into:** the first living human faces; Duchess polishing glasses with a shotgun under the bar; Foundation's smell of sawdust and cookfires; the Crater's space-station hull welded into walls, with raider graffiti across a NASA-style flag; the Stars and Stripes waved from a riverside dock to call in an Army team that never stood down; Secret Service agents who have been locked in a vault barracks since 2077, still wearing their earpieces.
