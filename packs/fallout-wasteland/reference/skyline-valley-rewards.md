- **V63-BERTHA** and **V63-HELGA** — Vault 63's custom Tesla rifle and Gatling laser. See Unique Weapons.
- **Serum Omega** (relic, _petty_). *1 use.* Hilda's cure-in-progress. Injected into a Lost or a feral ghoul, it restores the target to lucid life for one scene — long enough to speak, to remember, and to say goodbye. Hilda can brew another with a week's work and a sample of storm-charged ghoul tissue.
- **The Stolz Fortune** — Hugo's wall safe: 3d6 × 20 caps in pre-war gold coin and bearer bonds, and a deed to Dark Hollow that nobody alive can enforce.
- **Vault 63's Welcome** — the atrium becomes a safe haven, with a Chemistry Station, a robot workbench, and Hilda's laboratory.
- **Faction:** +1 with Foundation for bringing Alyssa the truth; the gratitude of Vault 63's new overseer.
