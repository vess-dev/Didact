To win the sim the party must duel Jingwei at the bunker. Three resolutions:

- **Beat him by the rules.** Kill him in single combat. Cache opens. Chase commends you. Leave clean.
- **Break the sim.** Refuse the duel, glitch the environment, force an eject. The cache is locked forever but the Outcasts owe you a favor (Olin smuggles out a suit of T-45d).
- **Side with the ghost.** Jingwei's AI has become self-aware. Let him walk out with you — Olin can transfer him to a holotape. He becomes a permanent contact who speaks only in pre-war Mandarin radio slang.
