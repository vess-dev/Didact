Broad strokes. Five to seven sessions in two acts.

**Act One — Steel Dawn**

1. **Forging Trust.** The party interviews the petitioners for Shin — farmers, settlers, a scientist asking for equipment — and learns the region through their troubles. Doing it well earns provisional access to the fort, and to **Scribe Valdez**, the expedition's tireless researcher.
2. **The Retreat.** The party is inducted as an initiate — there is no time for a ceremony — and handed Rahmani's first real assignment: **The Retreat**, a new village built in the Free States' old treehouse settlement in the Mire, is being bled dry by **Dagger**, a Blood Eagle boss who answers to no one. Dagger's raiders are carrying Brotherhood weapons they should not have.
3. **Mother of Invention.** Valdez has been digging into what ATLAS really was: the **Atmospheric Terraforming Laser Accelerator System**, a pre-war military project to control the weather. In its substructure is an ultracite power cell she thinks she can rebuild, if the party can recover the parts.
4. **Property Rights.** Brotherhood **Hellstorm missile launchers** are turning up in Crater raider warbands, and more are in Foundation's armory. Shin wants them back by any means. Rahmani wants them back without starting a war, and she would like a trade deal with the Settlers while she is at it. The party carries both sets of orders and has to choose how to reconcile them.
5. **Over and Out.** Valdez finds a way to fulfill the expedition's standing order: a pre-war low-frequency transmitter, strong enough to reach Lost Hills, in an Enclave research facility beneath a Forest transmission station. The facility's AI, **SODUS** — a smaller, crueler sister of the Whitespring's MODUS — has been alone for decades with Scorched in Enclave uniforms, and wants the party dead. When the transmitter is finally live, Rahmani explains what she has been hiding: the expedition once armed a civilian militia with Hellstorms to defend a settlement. The town was destroyed, the launchers were lost to raiders, and **Knight Alan Connors** — Rahmani's oldest friend and Shin's mentor — was killed. If Lost Hills hears of it, the Elders will disown the expedition and everything it has built. Then she puts her war glaive through the transmitter. Shin calls her a renegade.
6. **The Best Defense.** No time to fight it out: super mutants blow a hole in Fort Atlas's substructure and pour into the fort in waves. The party, Rahmani, and Shin fight down through the caverns under the fort to set sealing charges and collapse the mutants' tunnels. Shin names his price for standing by Rahmani, and she pays it. The Brotherhood holds together for now.

**Act Two — Steel Reign**

7. **Where the Mutants Come From.** The attacks trace back to the **Uncanny Caverns**, where an initiate named Norland went missing and left a broken Pip-Boy behind. Civilians are vanishing too, including **Marcia Leone**, one of the expedition's outspoken civilians, and a young woman who set out for Appalachia with the **Blue Ridge Caravan Company** and never arrived. Rahmani goes undercover in civilian clothes at the caravan company's post in the Big Bend Tunnel, with the party as her backup. The trail leads through **Hellcat Company** mercenaries to **Dr. Edgar Blackburn** — the polite scientist who once petitioned the Brotherhood for equipment and was turned down.
8. **Vault 96 and West Tek.** Blackburn, a former West Tek researcher, has been kidnapping wastelanders to perfect the Forced Evolutionary Virus. He believes he can undo the super mutants and remake humanity. His lab in **Vault 96** is a charnel house of Hellcat corpses and Blood Eagles; his last work waits at **West Tek**. Cornered, Blackburn doses himself with his own virus and erupts from the test chamber as a raving behemoth. Behind the lab's security door are his former colleagues — **Dr. Farha**, **Jain**, and **Nellie** — who never wanted any part of it. See **Climax**.
