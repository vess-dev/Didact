*Vaults of Fallout 3*

**Premise.** Cloning research. A single resident, Gary, was selected for genetic duplication. Dozens of Garys were produced before the vault ate itself.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A single hand-lettered sign reads *GARY*.
- **Cloning Lab.** The vats are empty but still running. A pre-war terminal logs the batches — Gary 12, Gary 17, Gary 23, Gary 43 — for pages, and notes that every Gary turned on the staff the moment he was decanted.
- **Residential Block.** Every cabin's name tag reads GARY. Every diary entry reads GARY. Every photograph is of the same face.
- **Overseer's Office.** Overseer Brody Jones is gone. He was chosen because the scientists knew a cancer would kill him within forty months, and the vault's main power was rigged to fail after twenty; his last logs describe the cloning experiment spiraling out of control — the scientists' duplicates of a single resident, every one of them named Gary, multiplying until the identical clones overran the staff.
- **Reactor Room.** The oldest Gary lives here now, grey and enormous and angrier than any of his brothers.

**Inhabitants.** GARY CLONES — identical, hostile to anyone who is not a Gary, and able to say exactly one word (treat as a WILD FOLK detachment). Nobody else: the original Gary is long dead, and his diary is the only sympathetic voice left in the vault.

**Reward.** A **Genetic Sample Cylinder** (Relic, 1 use) — plugged into an auto-doc, it restores a dying PC to full HP and STR one final time. **The Original Gary's Diary** — a holotape, the only sympathetic voice in the vault.

**Hidden.** One of the vats — the only one with no number on its label — is still sealed, still running, and still cycling. Inside: a clone that never decanted, now the size of a full-grown man, still breathing through the cable. A PC who touches the glass hears a heartbeat. A PC who breaks the glass faces an Overseer-decided consequence the party will remember for the rest of the campaign.

**Hook.** A medic in a nearby settlement has a patient dying of a blood disorder. The auto-doc needs genetic material it cannot synthesize. A clean sample from Vault 108 would save her.
