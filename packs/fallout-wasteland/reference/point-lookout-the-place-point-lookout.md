- **Point Lookout Pier & Boardwalk.** The resort. Ghoul shopkeepers, a shooting gallery, a tilt-a-whirl still running.
- **Calvert Mansion.** The Calvert family estate, now the ghoulified **Desmond Lockheart's** stronghold. He pays for damage done to his enemy.
- **Professor Calvert's Lighthouse.** A pre-war brain in a jar. Broadcasts a mind-beam on certain frequencies.
- **The Swamp.** Cypress islands, punga fields, a sunken church, **Plik's Safari** (a feral-ghoul hunt for caps).
- **The Ark & Dove Cathedral.** The **Brethren of the Punga** — Point Lookout's punga-farming tribals. They huff pollen and speak with god. God is a pre-war anti-aircraft radar — and the voice on it is Calvert's.
- **The Sacred Bog.** A drowned grove where the **Brethren of the Punga** perform the **Punga Rite** around **Mother Punga**, a punga plant grown vast and old. A PC who breathes her pollen gains a vision: the PC may immediately take back one perk they once swapped out, without a fresh copy of its magazine, once this session, and takes **1d4 Fatigue**.
- **The Chinese Intelligence Bunker.** A pre-war Chinese spy cell hidden beneath the aviary next to Calvert Mansion. The underground lab beneath the lighthouse is a separate pre-war medical facility.
