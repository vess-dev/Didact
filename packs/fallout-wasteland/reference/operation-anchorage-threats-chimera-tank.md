### Chimera Tank

15 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 8 DEX, 3 WIL, laser cannon (d10, _blast_), ram (d8)

- A co-opted pre-war mining rig converted into a Chinese armored vehicle — bigger and tougher than any American tank. Mounted laser cannon on the turret. Never tests Morale.
- **Armored**: A called shot to the cannon or radar antenna (Impaired, as called shots are) knocks that system out for the rest of the combat.
