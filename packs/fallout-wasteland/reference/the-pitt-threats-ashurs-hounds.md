### Ashur's Hounds

4 HP, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 5 WIL, bite (d6)

- Attack dog detachment. Pit-trained on Pitt slaves.
- **Pack Sense**: On a Critical Damage result against a PC, the rest of the pack is Enhanced until that PC moves.
