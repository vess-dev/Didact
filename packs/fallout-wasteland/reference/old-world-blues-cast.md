- **Dr. Klein** — Think Tank chair. Mean, loud, sure he's right.
- **Dr. Mobius** — the villain. Mostly a mask over the group's shared guilt.
- **Dr. Dala** — Think Tank biologist. Flirts with her specimens. Polite.
- **Dr. 0** — Think Tank engineer. Still nursing a grudge against a pre-war rival named House.
- **Dr. 8** — Think Tank communications. Speaks in whistles and static. Kindest of the five.
- **Dr. Borous** — Think Tank geneticist. Mad for cyberdogs and high school football.
- **Muggy** — a tiny Securitron obsessed with coffee mugs.
- **Roxie** — a cyberdog spliced together at X-8. Thinks she's a person.
- **The Sink's Auto-Doc** — speaks to the party only after repair. Wants to cure them. Wants to upgrade them.
