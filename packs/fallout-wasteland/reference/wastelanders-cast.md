- **The Overseer** — home at last, older, hopeful. Her vision: a vaccinated Appalachia and a currency backed by real gold. She will ask the party to trust her after a year of silence.
- **Davenport** — the Overseer's Mister Handy, her scout and confidant for the lost year.
- **Duchess** — owner of the Wayward. A former ER nurse and pre-war chem kingpin out of Welch. Hard, funny, and the only person in Appalachia who pays on time.
- **Mort** — a ghoul regular at the Wayward who shoots first.
- **Sol** and **Polly** — a roboticist and his Assaultron "bot-sis."
- **Paige** — Foundation's builder and reluctant leader. Rode the last train out of Union Station in Washington after the bombs, then led his people out of Pittsburgh.
- **Aubrie Willem** — Foundation's co-founder and doctor. Frightened, brilliant, the key to the Settlers' trust.
- **Penelope "Penny" Hornwright** — a ghoul scientist who spent her pre-war career replacing miners with robots, and knows it.
- **Captain Oliver Fields** — a U.S. Army officer who never accepted that America died. With **Sergeant Fred Radcliff**, **Sergeant Thompson**, and **Private Lucky**, a dog.
- **Meg Groberg** — Crater's leader, a former Diehard who led her people out of Appalachia when the plague came, and back in when they heard it was being resettled.
- **Rose** — still at the Top of the World, still Meg's old headache, still the party's way in.
- **Lucky Lou** — a nearly hundred-year-old ghoul engineer from Pittsburgh. The Crater swears a dud nuke once fell through his living room.
- **Gail** and **Ra-Ra** — a gentle super mutant and the abandoned child she took in. Meg accepted them both when no one else would.
- **Gentleman Johnny "Two-Guns" Weston** — a failed pre-war actor turned raider. Charming. Wants a bigger cut.
- **Hijack** and **Lev** — raiders who want the gold without the party.
- **Roper** — the Free Radicals' leader.
- **AC**, **Slick**, **Regs**, **Mercy**, and **Digger** — the Secret Service of Vault 79, a quarter-century late for their shift change.
