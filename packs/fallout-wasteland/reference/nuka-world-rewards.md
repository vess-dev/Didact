- **Thirst Zapper** (unique, d8 with the **Project Cobalt** schematics installed — see Unique Weapons).
- **Splattercannon** (unique handmade rifle, d10, _bulky_, 7.62 — see Unique Weapons).
- **Western Revolver** (d10, .44 — the prize for beating One-Eyed Ike's duel).
- **Nuka-Cola Quantum** (relic, _petty_ — see Relics). Heals to full HP, the drinker automatically passes DEX saves involving speed or reflexes for one scene, adds 1 Rad, and leaves the drinker Deprived the next day.
- **Nuka-Dark Rum Recipe** (relic — the original **Nuka-Cola Dark** formula; allows brewing it at a Chemistry Station. Bottles sell for 15 caps).
- **Gatorclaw Gauntlet** (unique unarmed, d10, _bulky_ — see Unique Weapons).
- **Nuka-Girl Rocketsuit Costume** and a **Sealed Nuka-Cola Factory Crate** (relics — see Relics).
- **The Star Cores** (35 of them, hidden. 20 bring the Galactic Zone's Star Control mainframe online; all 35 unlock the **Quantum X-01** power armor display. Getting a full suit out takes a whole party on a dedicated session).
- **Faction:** +1 Pack / +1 Disciples / +1 Operators depending on climax (max two gangs); possibly down to −2 (Vilified) with the Minutemen, one step at a time, or −1 with any Commonwealth settlement the party raids.
