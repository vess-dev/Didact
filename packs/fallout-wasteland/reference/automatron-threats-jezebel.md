### Jezebel (Robobrain Head)

4 HP, 2 Armor, — STR, — DEX, 18 WIL, mesmerizer pulse (d6; WIL save or the target loses their next action)

- A disembodied pre-war robobrain on a shelf; her HP is the brain case. Immobile. Brilliant. Malicious.
- **Negotiator**: A party that agrees to install her in a chassis gains a powerful hireling — and a liability.
