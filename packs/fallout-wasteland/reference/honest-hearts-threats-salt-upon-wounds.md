### Salt-Upon-Wounds

12 HP, 2 Armor, 15 STR, 14 DEX, 13 WIL, tribal power fist (d10, _bulky_), throwing axe (d6)

- White Legs war chief, the man who sacked New Canaan. Ruthless, cunning, canny in a retreat.
- **War Chief's Honor**: While he lives and they can see him, his warband fights Enhanced. On his death, the warband routs.
