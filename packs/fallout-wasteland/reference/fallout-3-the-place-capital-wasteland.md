A sprawling ruin, as big as the party lets it be. Key pointcrawl nodes:

- **Vault 101.** Sealed until it isn't. The Overseer's office terminal holds James's goodbye holotape.
- **Megaton.** Settlement built around a dud bomb. **Moriarty's Saloon**, Sheriff Simms, the Children of Atom's small congregation, Doc Church's clinic.
- **Galaxy News Radio.** Three Dog's broadcast tower. The only good news in the Wasteland.
- **The Mall.** Super mutants and Brotherhood patrols fight over the museums. The Museum of History holds Lincoln's repeater and the ghoul city of **Underworld** in its lower halls; escaped slaves dream of resettling the **Lincoln Memorial** nearby.
- **Rivet City.** A beached aircraft carrier. **Dr. Madison Li**, Father Clifford's congregation, a science lab running hydroponics.
- **Tenpenny Tower.** A pre-war luxury tower run by a senile plutocrat. Ghouls from Warrington Station want a share; the tenants want them gone. Mr. Burke lives on an upper floor.
- **Paradise Falls.** Slaver encampment. **Eulogy Jones** runs the roster. Burning the place down or being recruited into the crew — karma consequences in both directions.
- **Big Town.** A settlement of former Little Lamplight children trying not to get eaten by super mutants from Germantown.
- **Little Lamplight.** A cave settlement run entirely by children. Adults are not welcome past the gate, and the children will back that up.
- **The Pitt entrance.** A rail tunnel to Pittsburgh (links to **The Pitt** campaign — one-way and trog-infested).
- **The Jefferson Memorial.** Project Purity's home. Super mutants patrol the exterior. The rotunda hides James's work.
- **The Citadel.** Brotherhood of Steel HQ in the Pentagon ruins. Elder Lyons, Sarah Lyons, Scribe Rothchild, Liberty Prime in a pit.
- **Raven Rock.** Enclave stronghold — miles of pre-war mountain bunker. President Eden's home terminal lives here.
- **Vault 87.** Super mutant origin site. The G.E.C.K. is on the lowest level; Fawkes is in a cell.
- **Vault 112.** Under Smith Casey's garage, a Tranquility Lounger rig full of pre-war elderly who never woke up. **Dr. Stanislaus Braun** runs it as a simulated neighborhood where he has been the god for 200 years.
- **The Glowing Zone.** A crater where a pre-war bomb went critical. A hot zone: 1 Rad every scene without sealed armor.
