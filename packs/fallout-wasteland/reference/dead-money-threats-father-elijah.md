### Father Elijah (via bomb collar)

The collars themselves are the ambient threat: any PC within arm's reach of a working radio speaker makes a DEX save at the top of the next round or takes d6 damage from the collar — any Scar that results is **Concussed**. The party does not fight Elijah on equal terms unless they reach the vault.
