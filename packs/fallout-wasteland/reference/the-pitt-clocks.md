- **Heat Clock** (d6). Raider suspicion of the party. Ticks when the party commits open violence in Downtown, speaks to Midea where a raider can see, or wins in the Hole. At 6, Krenshaw locks down the Pitt — a raider detachment confronts the party on sight and the Steelyard becomes hostile territory.
- **Cure Clock** (d4). How close the resistance is to forcing the issue. Ticks when the party accepts a package from Midea, smuggles a slave out of the Mill, or openly speaks against Ashur at his audience. At 4, Wernher strikes whether the party is ready or not.
