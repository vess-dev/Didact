### Paladin Rahmani

12 HP, 3 Armor, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 16 WIL, Gatling laser (d12, _blast_, _bulky_), war glaive (d10, _bulky_)

- Only a combat encounter if the party sides with Shin and she will not stand down. In T-51 power armor.
- **Voice of the Brotherhood:** Initiates who can hear her must pass a WIL save to keep fighting against her.
- **Fusion Core:** A called shot to her Gatling laser's core (Impaired, as called shots are) knocks the gun out for the rest of the combat and leaves her the glaive.
