- **Old Olney.** A ruined town. Deathclaw territory. The Olney Powerworks — and the Tesla coil — lie beneath.
- **The Presidential Metro.** A pristine pre-war train, still climate-controlled. **M.A.R.Go.T.**, the transit computer, greets you. Feral ghouls have broken into one car.
- **Adams AFB Exterior.** Cratered runway, Vertibird pads, a mobile base crawler. Enclave patrols and Hellfire troopers.
- **The Crawler.** The mobile base. Command deck, barracks, reactor, holding cells. The **satellite uplink** is on the command deck.
