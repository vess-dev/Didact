You are a **pre-war survivor** — one half of a small nuclear family from the Boston suburb of **Sanctuary Hills** — who stepped into Vault 111 on October 23, 2077 and was frozen inside a cryogenic pod for two centuries. You woke once, years into your freeze, to watch a stranger shoot your spouse and take your infant son, **Shaun**. You woke again, alone, in the post-war Commonwealth. Shaun is gone. Your spouse is dead. You are the only one left in the vault.

The Commonwealth is four factions fighting over scraps: the **Minutemen**, rebuilding a settler militia from a handful of refugees; the **Brotherhood of Steel**, descending from the skies in a flying fortress; the **Railroad**, smuggling runaway synthetic humans out of the region; and the **Institute** — a hidden research facility beneath the pre-war CIT ruins, producing perfect android infiltrators and the reason your son was taken.

Your child is at the end of every thread. What he has grown into by the time you find him is the campaign's real question.
