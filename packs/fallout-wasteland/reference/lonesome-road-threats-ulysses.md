### Ulysses

16 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 16 DEX, 17 WIL, anti-materiel rifle (d12, _bulky_, .50 BMG), **Old Glory** flagpole (d10, _bulky_), war knife (d6)

- A former frumentarius of the Legion. Long coat, flag, pre-war weapon, older grief.
- **Speaks While Firing**: The first shot each round is preceded by a line of dialogue; a PC who answers the line may make a WIL save to avoid the shot entirely.
- **Launch Keys**: Carries the silo launch codes on a holotape; surviving his defeat means deciding what happens to them.
