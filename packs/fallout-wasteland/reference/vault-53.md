*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** Most of the equipment in the vault was engineered to break down every few months. The experiment measured how long a community could tolerate constantly repairing its own infrastructure.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A pre-war poster has fallen off the wall, picked up, been re-glued, and fallen again. The residents left it on the floor.
- **Maintenance Office.** A wall covered in schematics, none of them complete.
- **Residential Block.** Every room has a tool kit. Every kit is missing one tool, a different one each time.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape is a calm, weary instruction to the next Overseer to burn the place down and walk out. It is unclear whether she was joking.
- **Reactor Room.** Still running. The reactor has been hand-patched hundreds of times.

**Inhabitants.**
- **The Tinker Ghoul** — 7 HP, 9 STR, 14 DEX, 15 WIL, wrench (d6); she will not swing first. She knows every pre-war system in the vault by feel. Treats the party as a long-delayed shift change.

**Reward.** **The Tinker's Tool Kit** (Relic, _bulky_) — a PC using it to install a weapon mod (a **Craft** Downtime Action) pays half the mod's caps cost. **A Hand-Patched Reactor** — the knowledge that it is possible to keep any pre-war machine running forever if you are willing to stop living.

**Hidden.** One of the maintenance office's wall schematics, the only one ever completed, was drawn in invisible pre-war developer ink — a PC holding it under a Pip-Boy's radiation lamp reveals a **full map of the vault's hidden subsystems**, including a secondary reactor core nobody in the vault ever knew existed. It is still sealed, still full, and can keep a settlement running for a decade.

**Hook.** The Tinker ghoul has agreed to leave the vault if a single person promises to take over the repairs forever. She is serious.
