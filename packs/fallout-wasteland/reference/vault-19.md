*Vaults of Fallout: New Vegas*

**Premise.** The vault was split into two color-coded halves — a Red Sector and a Blue Sector — each with its own Overseer, and the experiment was paranoia. Strange noises in the vents, drafts from nowhere, lights blinking in patterns: each sector blamed the other, and some blamed the doctor in the middle. None of them guessed that the two Overseers were staging every incident on Vault-Tec's orders. What finally became of the residents is a mystery. By the time escaped convicts found the vault, it was empty.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Faded Red and Blue paint divides the walls.
- **Red Residential.** Now the flophouse of **Samuel Cooke**'s Powder Gangers — escaped NCR convicts who split from the main gang after the prison breakout.
- **Blue Residential.** Collapsed in a fire decades ago, and cut off from the rest of the vault. A lone ghoul still lives here, nursing a grudge older than the NCR — the only witness to what happened, if anyone can get him to tell it.
- **The Sulfur Caves.** A second way in, through caves northwest of the Whittaker farmstead that reek of rotten eggs.
- **Overseer's Office.** Two offices, one per sector, mirror each other across the vault. A holotape recovered from the Red Sector office explains the experiment, including that Overseer's private suspicion that the two of them were also being manipulated.
- **The Shared Cafeteria.** The one room in the vault where residents from both sectors were forced to eat together. The walls are scratched to the concrete.

**Inhabitants.** POWDER GANGER detachment under Samuel Cooke (Red), LONE GHOUL with a grudge (Blue).

**Reward.** **Dynamite Charges** — 2d6 sticks (each d10, _blast_, 1 use), a holdover from the Powder Ganger occupation. The Overseer's **Final Report** — a holotape confessing the psychological experiment in detail.

**Hidden.** The shared cafeteria has a single long table with names carved into its underside — a list of residents from *both* sectors who had secretly been meeting under it for years, passing notes and sharing food. The final name carved is the Overseer's own.

**Hook.** Two strangers claim descent from the vault's vanished residents — one from Red Sector, one from Blue — and want to know what happened to their families. Samuel Cooke's convicts hold the vault. The pair need escorts past the Powder Gangers to the shared cafeteria, where the answer is carved into a table.
