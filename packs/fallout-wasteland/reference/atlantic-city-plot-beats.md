Broad strokes. Four to six sessions. A family drama wrapped in a crime story wrapped in a monster hunt.

1. **Opening Night.** The Rose Room, the hitmen, Abbie's breakdown. Evelyn storms off, Antonio babbles, and Vin asks the party to help his sister, for pay. The only dose of Devil's Blood is in Atlantic City.
2. **The Flight to Acee.** The only aircraft in Appalachia with the range is **Lennox**'s vertibird at the **Whitespring Refuge**, the rebuilt Responders' new headquarters on the resort grounds. Lennox, a pre-war Air Force pilot who swears she once shot at a UFO, needs clearance from **Orlando**, the resort's manager. Evelyn talks; the party's reputation seals it. A refugee warns them the Jersey Devil is real. Evelyn laughs.
3. **The Casino Quarter.** Evelyn's old contact, **Stanley Soriano**, a low-level Lombardi, can get Devil's Blood — for a favor, and only in the high rollers' lounge of the **Neapolitan Casino**. Getting in takes money, luck, or nerve. The treaty means no one draws a weapon on the casino floor, which makes every conversation more dangerous.
4. **Showman's Pier.** Stanley's price is a Showman named **Zayde**, humiliated in front of his own crowd. The party slips backstage on the Boardwalk's pier, performs in the show, and makes Zayde a laughingstock. Back in Appalachia, Abbie is dosed and steady, for now.
5. **Honor Bound.** Vin remembers his father's trips into the flooded city center. He calls on his old Muni friend **Sloane Cao**, chief of the Pine Barrens Forestry Division, who kills Overgrown for a living. Once the party has pulled her scattered crew out from among the Overgrown, the Munis lead the party to the Lombardis' old warehouse, where the treaty forbids violence: talk in, bluff in, or knock four times at the back door. Behind it, a sealed chem lab. Reversing the city's flood pumps drowns it out.
6. **Sins of the Father.** Confronted, Antonio drops the act. He faked his senility to keep Concerta from finishing him, and he is the one who invented Devil's Blood. He killed a **Lesser Devil** that wandered in from the Pine Barrens, and his chemist, **Gene**, found what its blood could do. The synthetic drug cannot cure itself. An antidote needs blood from an adult **Jersey Devil**.
7. **The Devil.** Five pheromone lures across the flooded city center, a long night on the rooftops, and then the Jersey Devil comes down out of the dark. Kill it, and decide how much of its blood to take: enough for Abbie's cure, or enough for a fortune. See **Climax**.
