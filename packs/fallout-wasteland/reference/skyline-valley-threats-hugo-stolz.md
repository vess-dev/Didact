### Hugo Stolz

10 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 14 DEX, 18 WIL, Tesla rifle (d10, _blast_, _bulky_), lightning (d8, ranged, once per round)

- The overseer, floating on the current of his own machine, Cassidy at his feet.
- **Signal:** While Hugo is airborne he cannot be harmed, and he summons 1d4 **Lost** each time he rises. He rises when first reduced to 0 HP, and again the second time; each time he hangs in the air for a round, then comes down with half his HP restored. Only the third defeat ends it.
- **Sight:** Hugo sees by electricity, not light. Darkness, smoke, and disguises mean nothing to him.
