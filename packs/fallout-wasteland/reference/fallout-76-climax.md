**Scorched Earth.** The party has the keycard, the code, and a silo. The Overseer's last log waits at the silo's door. Four resolutions:

- **Scorched Earth.** The missile falls on **Fissure Site Prime**. The blast seals the fissure in rubble and wakes the **Scorchbeast Queen** (Bestiary), who claws her way out with waves of Scorched at her back. The party fights her in the blast zone, taking 1 Rad each round — halved in power armor, none in a sealed hazmat suit. If she falls, the chorus breaks. The Scorched left in Appalachia are leaderless husks, and the region can be resettled. If she escapes into the sky, the Chorus Clock fills.
- **Taggerdy's Ultimate Solution.** Three silos, three keycards, three launches: every major fissure in the Cranberry Bog burns at once, as the Brotherhood planned. The scorchbeasts die in their nests. So does the Bog for a generation. Fallout drifts over the Mire and the Savage Divide, and every future settlement in Appalachia will be built in the shadow of that choice.
- **The Free States' Snare.** No nukes. Abbie Singh's scorchbeast lures and the restored detection network draw the beasts down one at a time, and the party kills them on the ground — then climbs down into Fissure Site Prime to face the Queen in her own nest. The slowest, bloodiest, cleanest ending.
- **The Directive.** The party hands the silos to the Overseer, as Vault-Tec ordered, and she must decide what Vault-Tec will get. Or the party accepts MODUS's offer of the presidency, and a new Enclave holds three mountains full of missiles over everyone who ever comes home to Appalachia. What either does with them is the next campaign's problem — see **Wastelanders**.

A year later, strangers begin to arrive over the mountains, following rumors of treasure in a sealed vault. The door of Vault 76 is still open. Someone has to tell them what happened here.
