Shenandoah National Park under a sky that never clears. Pointcrawl nodes:

- **The Door Crash Site.** A field north of Sutton, a scorched vault door, Alyssa's salvage camp.
- **Vault 63 Transit Hub.** The Ash Heap entrance: a vault door leading to an underground railway, not a vault.
- **Skyline Drive.** The park's scenic highway along the ridgeline, its overlooks full of Lost and wrecked tour cars.
- **Dark Hollow Manor.** The Stolz Manor on its hill: a gazebo, a fountain, a garden wall, and an overseer's office directly under the front hall. The Storm is worst here.
- **Vault 63.** A vault so large it runs beneath the whole region, split into sectors — atrium, organics, meteorology, engineering, a garden with a waterfall. Only the atrium was finished, and only the atrium is safe.
- **Shenandoah Visitor Center.** Park maps, a gift shop, and weather equipment.
- **Old Crimora Mines.** A mine full of cultist robes, fake disciples, and one very real grudge.
- **High Knob Lookout.** A fire tower turned Blood Eagle camp, with a caged body in the cellar.
- **Research Sites Saxony, Bavaria, and Rhineland.** Vault 63's storm-harvesting stations, named for Hugo's homeland.
- **The Hawksbill Weather Station.** The heart of the meteorology sector, on the park's highest peak. The machine that made the Lost.
