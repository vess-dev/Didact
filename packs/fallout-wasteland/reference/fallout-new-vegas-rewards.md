- **Maria** — Benny's engraved 9mm pistol. See Unique Weapons.
- **That Gun** — sold out of the back room of Novac's Dino Bite gift shop. See Unique Weapons.
- **Pew Pew** — a novelty-etched laser pistol from a dead mad tinker. See Unique Weapons.
- **Mercy** — a dead Brotherhood Paladin's grenade machine gun. See Unique Weapons.
- **Ranger Sequoia** — awarded for NCR service or taken from a fallen Veteran Ranger. See Unique Weapons.
- **NCR Ranger Combat Armor (Black)** and/or **Desert Ranger Combat Armor** — see Unique Armors.
- **Elite Riot Gear** — full Divide-issue Ranger kit. See Unique Armors.
- **Platinum Chip** (relic, _petty_ — see Relics). Unique item that, when delivered to the Securitron upgrade terminal beneath the Fort, upgrades an entire Securitron detachment to Mk II.
- **A Casino Line of Credit.** Any Strip casino will front the party caps on demand once per session — up to 500 caps outstanding in all — owed in full by campaign's end.
- **Faction:** up to Idolized (+2) with whichever side wins Hoover Dam, one step at a time; ±1 with outlying powers per what the party brokered.
