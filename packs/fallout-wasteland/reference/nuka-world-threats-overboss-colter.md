### Overboss Colter (in Power Armor)

15 HP, 3 Armor, 16 STR, 12 DEX, 13 WIL, electrified gauntlet (d10)

- The outgoing Overboss. Power-armored, overconfident, already ashamed.
- **Shock Field**: On a hit, the target makes a STR save or loses their next action.
- **Chair Above the Gauntlet**: The fight happens in the arena — the Overseer may give the party environmental aid (a water spout, a loose cable) for a clever DEX save; on a success the suit shorts out, and attacks against him ignore his Armor until the end of the next round.
