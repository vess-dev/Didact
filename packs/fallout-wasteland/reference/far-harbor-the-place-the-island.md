The island is a proper regional sandbox. Three factions, several pointcrawl locations.

- **Far Harbor town.** Fishermen, a working tavern, a church turned warehouse. Protected by **the Hull** and the **fog condensers** DiMA built, scattered around the island.
- **Acadia.** The synth refuge, hidden in a pre-war observatory on the highest peak. **DiMA**, a prototype synth, leads the community. A dozen or so runaway synths live here in secret.
- **The Nucleus.** A pre-war nuclear submarine base. The **Children of Atom** worship here. Their high confessor, **Tektus**, is new and zealous.
- **Echo Lake Lumber Mill.** Abandoned. Trapper ambushes and a Fog Crawler nest.
- **The Vim! Pop Factory.** Pre-war soft drink plant. A unique **Vim! Captain's Blend** recipe lives here — consumed, the drinker smells faintly of the sea, and sea creatures will not attack them unprovoked for one scene.
- **The Cranberry Island Bog.** Hunters, gulpers in the pools, a ghoul trapper who sells bait.
- **The National Park Visitor Center.** A mirelurk queen in the basement.
- **The Fog Condensers.** Five of them, scattered, keeping the town breathable. Sabotaging them is a viable Atom play.
