### Blood Eagle

6 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 13 DEX, 8 WIL, handmade rifle (d8, _bulky_), serrated machete (d6)

- A raider from the gang too cruel for Meg's coalition. Mixed scrap armor, trophies of the last people they robbed, and a pecking order enforced by murder.
- Groups of four or more are a _detachment_.
- **No Quarter:** Blood Eagles never surrender, and attacks against a downed or fleeing target are Enhanced.
