Vault 101 has been sealed since the bombs fell. You grew up inside, ran the G.O.A.T., stood in the schoolroom under fluorescents that have never been off in your lifetime. One night your father **James** slipped out the front door. The Overseer's guards hunt for him, then for you, and by dawn you are standing on a hillside under a sky you have never seen, blinking.

James has gone to finish something he started before you were born: **Project Purity**, an attempt to scrub the Potomac basin clean of radiation and pour that water across the ruined Capital Wasteland. He cannot do it alone. Neither can you. And the **Enclave** — a pre-war remnant whose hidden bunker has watched the Wastes since 2077 — will move to seize the purifier the moment it comes online. What you do inside the Jefferson Memorial rotunda decides whether the Capital Wasteland drinks clean water or coughs up FEV-poisoned blood.
