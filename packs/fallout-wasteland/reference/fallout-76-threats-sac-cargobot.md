### SAC Cargobot

8 HP, 2 Armor, 8 STR, 12 DEX, 6 WIL, no attack

- A Strategic Air Command cargo drone, flying a fixed route with a secure container slung beneath it. Some carry supplies; the ones the DEFCON rating dispatches carry a **Nuclear Keycard**.
- **Escort:** Always flies with three **SAC Vertibots**.
- **Secure Container:** Drops to the ground when the cargobot dies. Opening it without the right codes calls the surviving vertibots back.
