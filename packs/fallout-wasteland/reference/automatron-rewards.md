- **Ada** — a modular robot companion; she follows a **Directive** in place of Loyalty (see Companions). See the **Robot Rebuild Procedure** below for full rules. On recruitment she starts with a melee arm (d10) and a sensor module (cannot be surprised).
- **The Mechanist's Helmet** (unique headgear, +1 Armor — see Unique Armors).
- **Salvaged Assaultron Head** (relic, d12 beam, 3 shots, recharged by plugging into a fusion core).
- **Robobrain Chassis Plans** (relic, allows the party to refit a felled Robobrain into a mount or a companion at a Workshop).
- **Faction:** A named settlement (Overseer's pick) owes the party one free favor — a one-time missile strike from Isabel's reclaimed arsenal, or the use of her workshop.
