*Vaults of Fallout 4*

**Premise.** A treatment program for chem addicts. The vault offered counseling, clean accommodations, a yearly elected Overseer to run the support meetings, and abstinence support — and a large hidden stash of every chem the residents had ever used. On the fifth anniversary of the sealing, a Vault-Tec agent posing as a resident opened it while everyone slept, and took notes on what happened next. The Gunners hold the vault now, at the edge of the Glowing Sea.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A pre-war rehabilitation clinic façade. Encouraging posters everywhere.
- **Counseling Wing.** Still running. Terminals contain pre-war addiction profiles of every original resident.
- **Group Therapy Room.** A circle of chairs, a pre-war coffee urn, a chalkboard with the twelve-step pre-war program written out in chalk.
- **The Hidden Stash.** The chamber the agent opened on October 23, 2082. Thousands of doses of Med-X, Psycho, Buffout, Jet, and Mentats, spilled across the floor.
- **The Detox Chamber.** The program's one honest promise: a clean room and an extractor chair built to purge a patient's system of chem dependency. Restored to power, it clears every addiction a PC carries in a single treatment, at the cost of a fusion core and a day of agony (1 Fatigue).
- **Overseer's Office.** The final holotape is the last elected Overseer describing her own relapse.

**Inhabitants.** GUNNER squad in the upper levels, with a GUNNER, HEAVY in salvaged power armor at the door (3 Armor; if he dies in it the suit is broken — see **Crafting → Repairs** — and its core is nearly spent) and a GUNNER SERGEANT guarding the clean room. GHOUL JUNKIES (treat as FERAL GHOULS; each still clutches one dose of a random chem) deeper down. A lone lucid ghoul who has been clean for forty years.

**Reward.** **Up to 12 doses of mixed chems** (Overseer's call — roll on the Chem Stash table). **A Pre-war Addiction Recovery Manual** (_petty_, 1 use — the pages do not survive a second reading) — the PC who works through it automatically passes the WIL save on their next **Cure an Addiction** attempt.

**Hidden.** The group therapy room has a large ring of chairs around a small circular rug. Under the rug, stuck to the underside of the concrete with old chewing gum, is a single **pre-war photograph** of the Vault-Tec intake director standing with the chief counselor, smiling, each holding a matching keycard. The director was in on the trap the counselor wasn't told about.

**Hook.** A settlement's most valuable medic has fallen off the wagon. Their apprentice will pay the party to find them a clean source of chems — or a reason to stop.
