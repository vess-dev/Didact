### The Motherlode

16 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 6 DEX, 8 WIL, drill head (d12), rock crusher (d10, _blast_)

- Hornwright Industrial's giant autonomous mining robot, built to chew through mountains. Its intelligence core is damaged. Until it is replaced, the Motherlode treats anything in its tunnel as ore.
- **Burrow:** The Motherlode moves through rock as easily as air. It cannot be engaged in melee while tunneling.
- **Open Core Bay:** Seating Penny's replacement core in the open brain housing (an action within reach of the drill head, and a DEX save) shuts the Motherlode down at once.
- **Critical Damage:** The target is buried in rubble and takes d6 STR each round until dug out.
