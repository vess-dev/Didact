### Enclave Sigma Squad Leader

12 HP, 3 Armor, 14 STR, 14 DEX, 14 WIL, heavy incinerator (d10, _blast_, _bulky_), frag grenades (d10, _blast_)

- The Enclave's last field commander on the crawler, in flame-scorched Hellfire armor. A true believer with nowhere left to retreat.
- **Rally**: While he lives, nearby Enclave Troopers automatically pass Morale saves.
