*A western parable with scripture running through it. Two good men, each holding a different answer to the same question, each willing to kill to prove he's right. The canyon itself is one of the most beautiful places in the wasteland — the party should not want to fire a single bullet, but they will.*

**Touchstones:** *Honest Hearts* DLC itself, *Blood Meridian* (the Legion's judgment), Cormac McCarthy broadly, *There Will Be Blood* (two hard men at a crossroads), *The New World* (Malick's wilderness), Mormon frontier histories.

**Lean into:** sky, silence, red rock. The Overseer should over-describe the landscape and under-describe the combat. Graham speaks in measured Old Testament sentences; Daniel in gentler New Testament ones; let the party feel the gap. Every death in this campaign should feel like a cost somebody tallies.
