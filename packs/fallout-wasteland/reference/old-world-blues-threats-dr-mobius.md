### Dr. Mobius

6 HP, 2 Armor, — STR, 10 DEX, 18 WIL, roboscorpion handlers (summons a detachment once per scene)

- A Think Tank panel; the Armor is his throne's plating. Invulnerable in his chamber until the throne's emitter is destroyed. Off-throne: no HP, only dialogue.
- **Delusion**: Cannot be killed through combat alone. The party must shut him off at the Forbidden Zone terminal.
