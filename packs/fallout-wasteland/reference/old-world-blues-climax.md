Mobius is found in the Forbidden Zone. He is also a Think Tank panel, the sixth — the one who "left."

- **Kill Mobius.** The Think Tank cheers. The brain is returned. The party leaves. The Sink stays — they can come back between campaigns.
- **Spare Mobius, expose the Think Tank.** The five realize what they are. Some hold it together. Dr. 8 weeps in static. The Sink becomes a neutral home the party can use *and* rent out.
- **Join the Think Tank.** Upload one PC's mind to a floating panel. They become a recurring NPC ally. The player rolls a new character.
