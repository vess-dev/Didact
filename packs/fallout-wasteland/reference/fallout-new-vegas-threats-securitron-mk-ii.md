### Securitron (Mk II) *(post-Platinum-Chip upgrade)*

12 HP, 3 Armor, 14 STR, 10 DEX, 13 WIL, twin Gatling lasers (d12, _blast_, _bulky_), missile rack (d10, _blast_, reloadable)

- What the Chip upgrades House's or Yes Man's force into. Devastating at the Dam.
- **Weak Point:** Like any robot, Mk I or Mk II, it takes Enhanced damage from pulse weapons (see Marketplace).
