*Vaults of Fallout 76*

**Premise.** A research vault beneath Shenandoah National Park, in the Skyline Valley south of the Savage Divide, built to the specifications of **Hugo Stolz** — a blind, German-speaking magnate on Vault-Tec's board of directors, and the vault's Overseer. Stolz bought his chair with research: weather control built from the military's shuttered ATLAS Program, and guardian robots built from stolen Pentagon plans. The vault's only door in the north is in the Ash Heap, the head of an underground railway that runs south under the mountains. The vault was far from finished when the bombs fell. Radiation poured in and ghoulified nearly everyone, and the ghouls began to go feral. In 2095, Hugo ran the weather machine at full power to save his wife and created the **Lost**, lightning-charged ghouls driven psychotic by the energy trapped inside them. In 2105 he ran it again and created the Storm. See the **Skyline Valley** campaign frame.

**Layout.**
- **Transit Hub.** The Ash Heap entrance. Its blast door was torn loose by the Storm's first surge and came down in a field north of Sutton. Beyond the empty frame, a railway platform leads south.
- **Dark Hollow Manor.** The Stolz mansion above the vault. The real entrance lies under its front hall, behind a hand scanner that opens only for a vault dweller's hand — living, dead, or Lost.
- **The Atrium.** The only finished sector, sealed off by the survivors. A clinic, a mess, and Hugo Stolz's quarters — austere, organized by touch, with a terminal that logs every experiment, every death, every compromise.
- **Organics Sector.** Hilda Stolz's laboratory: Serum Omega, and three Lost test subjects, one of them her own father.
- **Meteorology Sector.** The weather machine, a pre-war device capable of manipulating regional weather, under the Hawksbill weather station on the park's highest peak.
- **Engineering Sector.** Where the vault's three Storm Goliaths were built, and where the machines still build a new one whenever one is destroyed.
- **The Garden Sector.** A waterfall under a painted sky, where Cassidy Stolz used to sit and pretend the war never happened.

**Inhabitants.**
- **The Lost** — 5 HP, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 5 WIL, shock baton (d6) or laser pistol (d8). Ghouls charged with lightning from the weather machine, driven psychotic. During a lightning storm their attacks step up one damage die (shock baton d8, laser pistol d10) and the charge burns them for 1 damage a round. They perceive the world as pulses and wavelengths.
- **The Atrium Survivors** — Hilda Stolz, her cousin Audrey, security chief James Oberlin, and a handful of lucid ghouls. Friendly but guarded. Many remember the day the vault sealed unfinished.
- **Storm Goliath** — see the Skyline Valley campaign frame.

**Reward.** **The Weather Machine Schematic** (Relic) — a blueprint for a settlement-scale weather condenser. Grants +1 Industry to a settlement at the cost of occasional electrical storms. **Hugo's Cane** (_petty_) — a blind man's walking stick, weighted with lead. d6 melee, Enhanced on a surprise attack.

**Hidden.** Deep in the engineering sector, behind a bulkhead the survivors never opened, one cryo pod is labeled differently from anything else in the vault — not a ghoul resident, but a **pre-war Vault-Tec observer** placed here before the war to monitor Stolz. The observer is still alive, still frozen, and still employed. Waking them changes the vault's politics overnight.

**Hook.** A Vault-Tec broadcast is calling Vault Dwellers south: Hilda Stolz needs outsiders — people who aren't charged with lightning — to reach the meteorology sector and shut the machine down. Hugo Stolz, courteous as ever, says the same thing. Only one of them means it.
