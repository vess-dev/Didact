*Forge-smoke, rust, and the taste of iron in the back of the throat. The Pitt is a place with a stable government that is also hell — run by a man who thinks he's rebuilding civilization while standing on people. Every scene should carry the weight of a decision the party does not get to avoid.*

**Touchstones:** *Mad Max Beyond Thunderdome*, *Gangs of New York*, *Snowpiercer*, *Children of Men*, *The Pitt* DLC itself (the slave revolt, the baby, the auto-axe arena).

**Lean into:** grime, noise, and class. The slaves outnumber the raiders ten to one but know exactly how many broken legs a rebellion costs. Describe every rivet and every cough. The party cannot "win" without choosing whose side a child belongs to.
