*Vaults of Fallout 1 & 2*

**Premise.** A standard control vault — sealed on schedule, opened on schedule. Its residents emerged to found **Vault City**, a high-tech settlement in northern Nevada known for its advanced medical technology. Vault-Tec's records list it as "nominal."

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** The blast door stands open. A worn footpath leads away from it, uphill.
- **Residential Block.** Cabins picked clean, walls lined with old family photos never taken down.
- **Overseer's Office.** A pristine terminal still boots. The last log entry thanks the residents "for their service to the future."
- **Hydroponics.** Still running. A second, hidden hydroponics bay lies behind a maintenance panel — this one grows something with eyes.
- **The Garden of Observers.** A deeper chamber the Overseer never documented. Rows of glass cylinders, each with a pre-war volunteer suspended in brown preservative. They are labeled by job title.

**Inhabitants.** The surface settlement descended from Vault 8 — friendly, proud, and wary of outsiders. The thing in the hidden hydroponics, which watches with every eye it has grown (build it with **Creating Monsters**, or use a SPORE CARRIER ELITE that never leaves its bed).

**Reward.** A **Pre-war Medical Ledger** (Relic, _petty_) — reading it in downtime yields one true name of a person now alive in the wasteland. A single intact **Stealth Boy Mk II**.

**Hidden.** The Overseer's terminal has a buried maintenance log — a single line about *the Garden of Observers* reference-numbered to an unfinished Vault-Tec project called **ARBOR-4**. The name is not mentioned anywhere else in this book. An Overseer who wants a thread that leads out of this vault should follow it.

**Hook.** A settler from Vault 8's descendant town dies at dinner, leaving a single sentence to the party: *"The Garden is still feeding."*
