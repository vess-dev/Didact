| Origin | Main Campaign | DLC & Update Campaigns |
|---|---|---|
| Fallout 3 | Fallout 3 (Capital Wasteland) | Operation: Anchorage · The Pitt · Broken Steel · Point Lookout · Mothership Zeta |
| Fallout: New Vegas | Fallout: New Vegas (Mojave) | Dead Money · Honest Hearts · Old World Blues · Lonesome Road |
| Fallout 4 | Fallout 4 (Commonwealth) | Automatron · Far Harbor · Vault-Tec Workshop · Nuka-World |
| Fallout 76 | Fallout 76 (Appalachia) | Wastelanders · Steel Dawn · Skyline Valley · Atlantic City |

Run one DLC standalone, fold it into a main campaign as a side arc, or string several into a grand tour. A party that runs a main campaign plus its DLCs has played a full game's worth of story; a party that survives all twenty-one has seen more of the old world than most ghouls.
