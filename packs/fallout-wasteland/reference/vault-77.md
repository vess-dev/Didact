*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** A single resident was placed in the vault, accompanied only by an enormous crate of government-issue hand puppets. The experiment's purpose was not recorded.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Empty. One chair facing the door.
- **Residential Block.** Empty. A single cabin has been lived in.
- **The Puppet Workshop.** A pre-war workshop, still functional. Hand puppets of every description — among them a little old woman, a king, a dog, and a Vault Boy.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer never arrived. A holotape in the office was prerecorded and played on a loop for the single resident's entire stay.
- **The Stage.** The single resident built a stage and put on shows for the prerecorded Overseer. The shows are still scripted, still filed, in a pre-war script format.

**Inhabitants.** Nobody. The resident left long ago. Some of the puppets seem to move when you aren't looking directly at them. This may or may not be a trick.

**Reward.** **A Hand Puppet** (Overseer's choice which one) — _petty_, but a PC who produces the puppet at the right moment during a tense social encounter shifts the Reaction roll two steps better, once per session. **The Resident's Script Collection** — 6 holotapes of his puppet shows, which are unexpectedly brilliant.

**Hidden.** At the back of the stage, inside the prompter's box, is a **sealed envelope** addressed to Vault-Tec corporate. The resident had been writing them weekly for a year — fan letters, pitching new scripts, asking if anyone was watching. The letters were never collected. The last one ends mid-sentence.

**Hook.** An old caravaneer remembers watching the Vault 77 resident walk out of the vault years ago, carrying one puppet. He has never forgotten the look on that puppet's face. He wants to know if the rest of them are still there.
