**Everyone enters Dead Money stripped of all inventory — nothing comes through the bunker door except perks, which are skill memory.** The collars are explosive and pair-linked to others in the party; a PC's collar detonates if they stray too far from their teammates *or* if any radio speaker is within arm's reach. The red cloud damages anything exposed.
