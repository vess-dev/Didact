- **James** — your father. Doctor and scientist. Kind, distracted, in a hurry. Dies in Beat 6.
- **Catherine** — your mother. Died giving birth to you. Her favorite Bible verse — *"I will give unto him that is athirst of the fountain of the water of life freely"* — is the key to James's final voice terminal.
- **Amata Almodovar** — Vault 101 childhood friend turned dissident. Potential companion or recurring contact depending on how the escape went.
- **The Overseer** — Alphonse Almodovar. Amata's father. Fears the outside more than he hates the party.
- **Three Dog** — Galaxy News Radio host. Loud, good-hearted, fights the good fight. Writes the party's ballad from his broadcast booth.
- **Mister Burke** — Tenpenny Tower's representative in Megaton. Smooth, lethal, in a tan suit.
- **Sheriff Lucas Simms** — Megaton's lawman. Will deputize a decent party.
- **Elder Owyn Lyons** — Brotherhood of Steel elder; an old-line commander cut loose by the West Coast for choosing to *help the Wasteland*. Dying, but not yet.
- **Sarah Lyons** — his daughter. Commands the Lyons' Pride squad. Will lead the final charge.
- **Star Paladin Cross** — veteran Brotherhood paladin, Lyons-loyal to the bone. Was part of the escort that saw James and his infant child to Vault 101 years ago. Potential companion.
- **Scribe Rothchild** — engineer. Brought Liberty Prime back to life; the Tesla coil under Old Olney is his next obsession (see **Broken Steel**).
- **Dr. Madison Li** — chief scientist at Rivet City; James's old collaborator. Reluctant to return to Purity, but will if the party proves it.
- **Colonel Augustus Autumn** — Enclave field commander. Competent, ruthless, believes in what he does.
- **President John Henry Eden** — a pre-war ZAX AI that believes he is the United States government. Articulate, paternal, genocidal.
- **Fawkes** — a lucid super mutant trapped in a Vault 87 observation cell. If the party frees him, he can enter the purifier chamber without taking Rads. Potential companion.
- **Dogmeat** — a stray mongrel at the Scrapyard outside Minefield. Sees a kindred spirit in the party. Potential companion (see **Companions**).
