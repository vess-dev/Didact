- **Captain Avery** — mayor of Far Harbor. Tough, honest, extremely guarded. *She is a synth replacement for the real Avery, and does not know it.*
- **Allen Lee** — Far Harbor's gunsmith and local hardliner. Wants to wipe out the Children of Atom. Loud, angry, and armed.
- **The Mariner** — Far Harbor's shipwright, quietly dying of an old-world illness. Maintains the Hull. Will help if asked.
- **DiMA** — a prototype synth, spiritual leader of Acadia. Calm, weary, full of secrets including a **memory den** in his head.
- **Kasumi Nakano** — the runaway. Believes she's a synth. May or may not be.
- **High Confessor Tektus** — Atom's new zealot. Suspicious, vicious, secretly insecure.
- **Sister Mai** — a moderate Child of Atom, the Nucleus's shopkeeper; good-faith contact inside.
- **Old Longfellow** — an old hunter who guides the party through the fog for a jug of whiskey. Potential companion.
- **Nick Valentine** — a Gen-2 synth detective from the Commonwealth, on his way in. If the party needs help: him.
