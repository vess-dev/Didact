A green, mountainous sandbox in six regions: the Forest, the Toxic Valley, the Savage Divide, the Ash Heap, the Mire, and the Cranberry Bog. Pointcrawl nodes:

- **Vault 76.** The Forest, north of Flatwoods. The door stays open. A Mister Handy keeps the lights on.
- **Flatwoods.** The first empty town. The Responders' volunteer program, a church, a dog bowl by a door.
- **Morgantown.** **Vault-Tec University**, where the Overseer studied, and the **Morgantown Airport**, where the Responders died.
- **Charleston.** A drowned capital. The Capitol Building and its jail, the *Charleston Herald*, the fire department, the AVR Medical Center. Mud lines on the second floors.
- **Grafton.** A steel town in the Toxic Valley whose computerized mayor elected himself in a 1–0 vote and has not noticed the war. He wants help with the tourist season. The **Grafton Monster** walks the dam.
- **Top of the World & Pleasant Valley.** The Savage Divide's ski country: Rose's thumbtack above the clouds, mines beneath its legs, the raiders' cache below the old resort.
- **Camp McClintock.** An automated Army base where Master Sergeant Gutsy still drills the empty parade ground.
- **Huntersville.** A farm town that took a West Tek development grant in 2075 and got FEV in its water. The birthplace of Appalachia's super mutants.
- **The Ash Heap.** Strip-mined, smoldering, ash falling like snow. Mount Blair and Hornwright's giant excavator, the **Rockhound**; the Garrahan Mining headquarters beneath Bramwell, where the Excavator power armor was built; mole miners in every shaft.
- **Watoga.** The automated "City of the Future," still run by its robots for a population that fled a virus attack before the bombs fell. A human can be mayor for a day. Atomic Mining Services' headquarters is where Madigan found the ultracite research.
- **The Mire.** Swamp, strangler vines, giant flowers that bite. Harpers Ferry and its Free States armory, Abbie's bunker, the waste dump over Blackwell's bunker.
- **The Cranberry Bog.** Red grass and alien crystal under a sky full of wings. **Fort Defiance**, **Drop Site V9**, the **glassed cavern**, and **Fissure Site Prime**.
- **The Whitespring.** A grand old sulphur-spring resort in the Savage Divide, still staffed by polite robots, a golf course full of ghouls — and the Enclave's bunker under it all.
- **Point Pleasant.** The Mothman Museum, the cult's old church in its basement, and a thing on the rooftops that watches.
- **Helvetia.** A Swiss heritage town whose parade robots still march for Fasnacht, masks and all.
- **The Silos.** Sites **Alpha**, **Bravo**, and **Charlie**, sunk deep into three mountains, fully automated, fully armed.
