The PC meets Ulysses in the control room. Two launch buttons: **NCR** and **Legion**. Also: Ulysses himself. Resolutions:

- **Kill him.** The most common answer. The buttons go dark as the launch codes leave his hand. The Divide remains broken but doesn't take anything else with it.
- **Talk him down.** A series of Bond-based appeals — can he accept that the Courier didn't know what was in the package? If yes, he walks away. He may return in a later campaign as a difficult ally.
- **Fire one missile.** If the party picks a target, Ulysses will honor the choice and leave on his own terms. The wasteland feels the blast. That faction's Reputation falls to **Vilified**, one step at a time as word spreads, permanently. The Overseer describes a crater that will outlast everyone at the table.
- **Fire neither.** Walk out and *tell nobody*. The party gains a silent bond no one else will understand.
