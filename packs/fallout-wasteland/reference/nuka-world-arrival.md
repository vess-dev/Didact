A traveling **Preston Garvey** type (or any do-gooder NPC from the party's network) cannot warn the party in time: the Gauntlet has already been rebuilt. The party arrives at the front gate, the monorail pulls up, and the Gauntlet — a deadly pre-war attraction converted into a deathrun — flings them at the throne.
