In 2105, the **Russo family** opens **the Rose Room**, a supper club in an old Savage Divide mansion, and invites all of Appalachia. They are refugees from **Atlantic City**, "Acee," the boardwalk town on the Jersey shore that the Great War spared from a direct hit. The floods that followed drowned its center to the second floor, but the **Casino Quarter** stayed dry, and Acee has been running ever since on three powers held apart by a treaty:

- the **Lombardi Family**, the mob that owns the Casino Quarter, ruled in the name of the ailing Don Quentino by his sister **Concerta**;
- the **Munis**, the **Atlantic City Municipal Government** under **Mayor Tim Lane**, who keep the water pumped, the lights on, and the plant monsters out;
- the **Showmen**, the carnival clan that owns the Boardwalk under **Mother Charlotte** and turns every entertainment into blood sport.

The Russos left because **Antonio Russo**, Quentino's favored heir, lost the succession to Concerta and ran before she could finish him. Now Antonio sits upstairs pretending to be senile. His son **Vin** keeps the bar, his wife **Evelyn** sings, and his daughter **Abbie** is going through withdrawal from **Devil's Blood**, the rarest and most addictive chem on the East Coast. When the hitmen come on opening night, the party is the only muscle in the room. Following them home means following the Russos' debts all the way back to the Jersey shore.
