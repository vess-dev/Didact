- **Fog Clock** (d6). Condenser failure. Ticks when the party returns to Far Harbor without having tended a condenser since their last visit (max one tick per return). Tending a condenser walks the clock back one. At 6, the town evacuates.
- **Atom Clock** (d4). Tektus's sermon count. Ticks when the party challenges Atom doctrine inside the Nucleus, interferes with a Cleanser's mission in Far Harbor, or is revealed to have accepted Far Harbor's hospitality while inside the Nucleus. At 4, he declares holy war.
- **DiMA Clock** (d6). DiMA's secret works its way loose. Ticks when the party asks Avery about her past, speaks DiMA's name inside Far Harbor, recovers a memory segment in the Memory Den, or is seen leaving Acadia by a Harbor native. At 6, Acadia is exposed to both Far Harbor *and* Atom, whether the party wants it or not.

**The Fog itself.** Beyond the condensers' reach the Fog is an ambient rad zone — 1 Rad per watch (see **Hazards → Rad Zones**) — and thick enough that ranged attacks beyond close range are Impaired. A working condenser clears both for the ground around it.
