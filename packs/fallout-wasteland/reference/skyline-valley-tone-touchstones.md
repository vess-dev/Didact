*Southern Gothic in a thunderstorm. A great family's manor on a hill, a feud older than the war, a patriarch whose love for his wife excuses everything, and a daughter who has only now learned what her father is. The Overseer's job is to let Hugo be charming for as long as possible. Every kindness he shows the party is real. So is everything else.*

**Touchstones:** *Fallout 76: Skyline Valley* itself, *The Fall of the House of Usher* (a family and its house going down together), *Frankenstein* (lightning and a man playing god), *Rebecca* (a manor built on a buried wrong), *Deliverance* (the hills remember who was driven off them), *Twin Peaks* (a pleasant national park hiding something). Skyline Drive overlooks. Blue Ridge fog.

**Lean into:** the smell of ozone; lightning striking the same ridge twice; the Lost muttering that their wounds are "so loud"; the front hall of Dark Hollow Manor with a vault beneath its floor; Hugo's courtesy, his accent, his perfect manners; Cassidy's waterfall in the garden sector where she used to pretend she was outside; the three Storm Goliaths, brain-bots built from Liberty Prime's plans, quoting philosophers as they fire.
