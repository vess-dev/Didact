*Silver Age robot serial with a sleep-deprived engineer at the center. The Mechanist is a villain the party is going to feel sorry for. Every scene should carry the weight of *her* exhaustion — she's been doing this alone for months, and it shows in everything her robots have stopped doing right.*

**Touchstones:** *Automatron* DLC itself, *The Iron Giant*, *Metropolis* (the Maschinenmensch), golden-age *Astonishing Tales* comics, *The Incredibles'* Syndrome, *Ex Machina* (a mind building a mind).

**Lean into:** blinking lights, humming servos, and the specific guilt of someone who thought they could fix things. Ada is the moral weight of this campaign — let her watch the party. When Isabel finally takes the mask off, the Overseer should pause for a full beat before describing her face.
