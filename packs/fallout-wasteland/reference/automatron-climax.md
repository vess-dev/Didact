Confronting the Mechanist on her throne. Three outcomes:

- **Unmask her.** A peaceful resolution if the party has been ticking the **Isabel Clock** toward 4. She takes off the mask and weeps. Ada forgives her — or doesn't. Isabel becomes a Workshop NPC.
- **Take the throne.** Kill Isabel and take the factory. The party now has a *robot production line* — one custom bot per Downtime Action, but every robot rolls 1d6 on build; a 1 means it went rogue and will return later.
- **Blow the factory.** Ada gets her revenge. Everything collapses. The party walks out with the head of Jezebel clipped to their belt and a story told at every settlement for months.
