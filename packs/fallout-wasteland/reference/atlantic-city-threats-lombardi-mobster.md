### Lombardi Mobster

6 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 11 WIL, .45 submachine gun (d8, _bulky_), switchblade (d6)

- A made man of the Lombardi Family in a pinstripe suit with plating sewn into the lining. Polite on treaty ground, lethal off it.
- Groups of four or more are a _detachment_.
- **Omertà:** A captured mobster will not talk about Family business unless the party has something to trade.
