Vault 88 is a literal *construction site*. Big empty caverns, scattered pre-war equipment, half-built section walls. The party can choose *how* to build it — or refuse.

- **Main Entry.** A huge vault door, half-installed. Functional enough to lock someone in.
- **The Overseer's Suite.** Barstow's command room. Monitors, a terminal that orders experiments, two centuries of tidy Vault-Tec paperwork.
- **The Dormitories.** Bare rooms, mattresses, graffiti from construction workers.
- **The Cafeteria.** Ancient Vault-Tec mess hall, still stocked.
- **Experimental Pod A — The Phoropter.** A pre-war eye-test rig wired to a mind-alteration experiment. Finishing it creates a relic helmet.
- **Experimental Pod B — The Slot Machine.** A gambling-reward rig designed to reinforce compliance via dopamine spikes. Finishing it creates a relic amulet.
- **Experimental Pod C — The Soda Fountain.** A cola dispenser that mixes subject-specific chems into drinks. Finishing it creates a relic drink tap.
- **Experimental Pod D — The Power Cycle 1000.** A stationary bike that powers the vault when pedaled and doses the rider with addictive stimulants. Finishing it creates a relic bicycle.
- **The Deep Caverns.** The unfinished excavation zones. A **Radscorpion Queen** nests here. One pre-war construction foreman, ghoulified, still roams the tunnels with his crew.
- **The Settler Intake Bay.** Empty, as far as anyone has told the party (see **Ezra Caldwell**). Where arriving settlers are processed.
