Barstow wants to run the full experimental program on a full settler population. The party has to choose what Vault 88 becomes.

- **Stop her.** Confront Barstow in her suite. She fights like a ghoul who remembers her pre-war commission. Kill her, and two centuries of Vault-Tec know-how die with her. Cut her loose, and she walks out a changed woman.
- **Take over.** Kill Barstow, finish the experiments ethically (each experiment relic can be "cleaned up" with a DEX or WIL save to retain the benefit without the cruelty). Open the vault as a party-run settlement. A surprising number of settlers show up anyway.
- **Run the experiments her way.** The vault fills. The party gains caps, relics, and a reputation that closes a lot of doors in the Commonwealth. Karma slides to −2 (Infamous), one step per session as word spreads.
- **Collapse the vault.** Blow the cave mouth. Seal Barstow inside with her ghouls. She outlives the party by a century. She remembers the party's names.
