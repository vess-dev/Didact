An old drive-in theater, the **Midnight Science Fiction Feature**, plays a reel that hypnotizes the audience. The party wakes at **Big Mountain**, a pre-war science crater full of robotic horrors and experimental weapons. Their brains are missing. The Think Tank's old nemesis — **Dr. Mobius** — stole one of them. To get it back, the party must fetch three pieces of the crater's forgotten tech. Everyone in this place is already insane.
