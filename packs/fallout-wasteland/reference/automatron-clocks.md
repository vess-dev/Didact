- **Army Clock** (d6). The Mechanist's production line. Ticks when the party defeats a Mechanist patrol without destroying the factory relay, leaves an intact Robobrain behind, or broadcasts on an open channel. At 6, she sends an **attack wave** at the nearest settlement — party's home base, if they have one.
- **Isabel Clock** (d4). Her grip on reality. Ticks when the party spares one of her robots or contacts her by radio without naming her. Each tick is a new reveal: 1 doubts herself, 2 sets aside the megaphone, 3 asks for help, 4 ready to surrender *if* the party has not attacked her lair yet.
