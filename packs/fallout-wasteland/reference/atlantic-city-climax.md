**Sins of the Father.** The Jersey Devil lies dead in the floodwater, and the party holds its blood. Abbie and Antonio face each other in the ruins of the city they both grew up in. Three resolutions:

- **The Cure.** The party harvests enough blood for the antidote and nothing more. Abbie takes it. Antonio has to decide whether to tell her he pushed her away to keep her out of the Family, and Abbie has to decide whether that is an excuse. The Russos go home to the Rose Room as a family, or at least as something like one.
- **The Fortune.** The party takes all the blood. Antonio sees his edge on Concerta come back to life; Gene sees a new lab; Abbie sees her father choose the drug again. With a supply of Devil's Blood in the party's hands, every faction in Acee comes looking. The Treaty Clock ticks twice.
- **Abbie's Choice.** Abbie refuses the cure, or takes it and walks away from all of them — to Sloane and the Munis, or back to Appalachia on her own. Antonio is left with a sealed vial and a dead monster. The Overseer decides whether Concerta has heard Antonio is alive yet.
