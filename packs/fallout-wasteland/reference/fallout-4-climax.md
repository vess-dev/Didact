**The Nuclear Option.** However the Signal Interceptor was built, the party is in the Institute when the end comes. Four resolutions:

- **Minutemen Ending.** Preston Garvey and the Commonwealth militia breach the Institute through an old service tunnel and detonate its reactor; artillery from **The Castle** covers the retreat. The party leads the ground assault. Father dies in his chair, asking the party to take his watch. Synths scatter; some are hunted, some melt into settlements. The Minutemen are the Commonwealth's new peacekeepers.
- **Brotherhood Ending.** Elder Maxson unleashes a rebuilt **Liberty Prime** (see Bestiary → Liberty Prime) on the CIT ruins. Prime marches into the Institute. The party fights alongside paladins. Father dies; so do the synths, the scientists, and the children in the schoolroom. The Brotherhood rules the Commonwealth.
- **Railroad Ending.** Desdemona's agents surgically liberate every synth in the Institute while the party covers. A self-destruct protocol takes the facility. Father dies knowing his own parent betrayed him. Freed synths disperse across the Commonwealth. The Railroad quietly folds into memory; its work is done.
- **Institute Ending.** The party accepts Father's directorship. Father dies in his sleep. The party broadcasts from within the Institute, declaring a new order. The Brotherhood's Prydwen is brought down by an Institute strike team; the Railroad is rooted out by Coursers the party now commands. The Commonwealth enters a quiet, clean, surveilled peace.

The party walks out. Some of them walk out as something *very* different than what they came in as. Sanctuary Hills is still there. The vault is still open. The cryopod is still cold.
