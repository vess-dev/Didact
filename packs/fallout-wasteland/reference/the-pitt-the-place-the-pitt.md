- **The Yard.** The slave pens. Filthy, loud, no weapons allowed. **Midea** runs the resistance here.
- **The Steelyard.** The irradiated scrapyard where slaves collect steel ingots. Trogs and wildmen prowl. **Everett**, the mill foreman, trades ingots for gear.
- **Downtown Pitt.** Ruined skyscrapers. Trogs nest in them. **Mex** guards the gate — sadistic, mine-happy, and armed with a rifle. Scavenging runs here supply Midea's resistance.
- **The Arena.** Ashur's gladiatorial pit. A slave who wins the **Hole** earns freedom — or is supposed to.
- **Uptown & Haven.** Ashur's gated keep. Luxury. The only clean air in the city.
- **The Pitt Underground.** A hot-zone network of tunnels beneath the mill where the trogs breed.
