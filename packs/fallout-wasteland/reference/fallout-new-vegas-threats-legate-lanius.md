### Legate Lanius

14 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 14 DEX, 12 WIL, **Blade of the East** (d10, _bulky_), spiked gauntlets (d8)

- Caesar's field general. Only seen at the climax. Huge, masked, the only enemy in the Mojave who moves like a deathclaw but talks like a man.
- **Monster of the East:** Automatically passes Morale saves. On Critical Damage, spits a speech and fights for one more round before dying.
