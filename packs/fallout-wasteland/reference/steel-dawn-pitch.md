In 2104, a handful of knights in power armor walk out of the west: the **Brotherhood First Expeditionary Force**, sent from Lost Hills in California by the Council of Elders to re-establish contact with Paladin Elizabeth Taggerdy's Appalachian chapter. They are years too late. Taggerdy's people died fighting the scorchbeasts, and the expedition has had to start over. It has made a home in the old **ATLAS Observatory** in the Savage Divide, renamed it **Fort Atlas**, and opened its gates to anyone who wants to petition the knights for help.

The expedition's commander, **Paladin Leila Rahmani**, has known Taggerdy since Taggerdy was a young Army lieutenant before the war, and she believes the Brotherhood exists to protect people, not to hoard their weapons. Her second, **Knight Daniel Shin**, believes in the Chain That Binds: the Codex, the Elders, and the discipline that keeps dangerous technology out of dangerous hands. Both are good soldiers. Both are carrying the same old failure. The party is recruited as a probationary initiate and quickly becomes the knight both commanders trust, which is the most dangerous place to stand.

The first act is about what the Brotherhood owes Appalachia. The second is about what it owes itself, and it ends in a West Tek laboratory with a room full of scientists and a choice about whether they live.
