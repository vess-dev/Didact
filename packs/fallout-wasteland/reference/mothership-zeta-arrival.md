The party investigates the crash site (Point 1 below). A **cold blue beam** takes them. They wake in stasis tubes in a cell, gearless. Their equipment is in the ship's **Relic Locker**, somewhere forward near the bridge. A little girl named **Sally** in the next cell knows the way out — she's been here for *two hundred years* and she remembers the hallways.
