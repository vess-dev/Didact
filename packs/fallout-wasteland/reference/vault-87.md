*Vaults of Fallout 3*

**Premise.** A bioweapon testing site. The residents were exposed to a forced-mutation agent developed before the war. The survivors became the first super mutants of the region.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Radiation pours from the door. Anyone entering by it adds 2 Rads immediately, and 1 more for every round they linger on the threshold. The quieter way in is through the caverns under Little Lamplight, if the children there can be talked into opening their gate.
- **Containment Chambers.** Glass cylinders, shattered outward, each labeled with a vault resident's name and a date.
- **Holding Pens.** Where the super mutants are now born. The FEV vats still run, and the mutants still haul in captives from across the Capital Wasteland to be dipped.
- **The Isolation Cell.** Behind a force field sits a lucid super mutant who reads everything the vault's library holds and remembers being a man. The mutants call him Fawkes; he would like very much to leave.
- **The G.E.C.K. Chamber.** So irradiated that no human can walk in and walk out — anyone inside takes 1 Rad every round. The vault's Garden of Eden Creation Kit waited here for two centuries.
- **Overseer's Office.** Caved in. A holotape in the rubble names the scientists who signed the order.
- **Reactor Room.** Hot. A GLOWING ONE and several CENTAURS patrol it.

**Inhabitants.** SUPER MUTANT GRUNTS, SUPER MUTANT MASTER (leader), CENTAURS and a GLOWING ONE (reactor level).

**Reward.** A **Sealed Sample of the FEV** (Relic, 1 use — see the FEV Sample entry in the Relics chapter). An **Overseer's Terminal Dump** revealing the sister sites to Vault 87.

**Hidden.** The oldest containment chamber has a single cylinder whose label reads **SUBJECT 00** — a Vault-Tec scientist who volunteered for the first dose. His name is still on a lanyard. The cylinder is empty. If the party is paying attention, they can match the name to one of the original super mutant Masters still alive in the wasteland, and the encounter changes.

**Hook.** A super mutant who remembers being human is living in a nearby ruin. She wants someone to end the cycle by destroying the vats.
