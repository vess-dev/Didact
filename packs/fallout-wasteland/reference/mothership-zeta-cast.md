- **Sally** — a little girl abducted shortly after the bombs fell in 2077. Knows the ship better than anyone. Keeps a teddy bear in her cell.
- **Somah** — a wasteland survivor taken last week. Wants to get home.
- **Paulson** — a cowboy from the 1890s. Revolver in his boot.
- **Toshiro Kago** — a samurai from feudal Japan (late 1500s). Speaks no English. Cuts like a laser.
- **Colonel Hartigan** — a 1960s astronaut abducted mid-spaceflight. Found dead in his cryo pod — his spacesuit is salvageable, but he is not.
- **The Zetan Captain** — does not speak to lesser beings. Will fight to the last.
- **Elliott Tercorien** — a pre-war Army medic from the 108th Infantry Battalion, stationed at Anchorage before abduction. Has one dose of a cure in his kit that will remove a single Rad Scar.
