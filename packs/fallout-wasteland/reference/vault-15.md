*Vaults of Fallout 1 & 2*

**Premise.** An experiment in forced diversity — Vault 15 packed a thousand people of wildly different cultures, languages, and values into a single vault and sealed it. When overcrowding forced the door open, four groups walked out in different directions. Three became raider gangs — the **Khans**, the **Vipers**, and the **Jackals**. The fourth, under a man named Aradesh, founded **Shady Sands**, the village that became the capital of the New California Republic.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A collapse has cut the vault into two halves. The upper half is raider-held. The lower half has been sealed off for most of two hundred years.
- **Upper Residential (Raider-held).** Khan raiders use the old cabins as flophouses.
- **Lower Residential.** Untouched. Diaries in four languages. A child's drawing showing four different houses under four different flags.
- **Overseer's Office.** Collapsed ceiling, but the terminal on the desk still has one unread message from 2077.
- **Deep Armory.** A sealed vault-door at the bottom. Pre-war riot gear, ten pipe rifles, and a single working .308 hunting rifle.

**Inhabitants.** KHAN RAIDERS (upper — treat as THUGS with a CREW BOSS), MOLE RAT DETACHMENT (lower, around the armory), a lone half-feral ghoul who mutters in four languages (a survivor from the original sealing).

**Reward.** The **Overseer's Message** — a holotape in four languages simultaneously, none of them quite translating the others. A **cached .308 hunting rifle**.

**Hidden.** The deep armory has a false back. Pry the rear wall's metal plating and find a **Vault-Tec surveyor's map** showing the original four-faction sealing plan — including a fifth, unmarked exit tunnel that none of the factions ever found. It leads to the surface a mile east of the raider camp.

**Hook.** A Khan defector wants revenge on the raider chief in the upper vault. She knows a back way in, through the lower levels, and needs someone who isn't a Khan to walk point.
