The ship is a single-direction dungeon. No going back. Doors seal behind the party.

- **The Crash Site (Earth).** A shallow crater northwest of the Greener Pastures disposal site. A half-melted probe. The beacon still pulses.
- **Holding Cells.** The drop-in. Cells, stasis tubes, an alien **Abduction Log** holotape (tells the party they've been selected at random).
- **Cryo Labs.** Abductees frozen in ice. Thaw any or all. Each thawed prisoner is a potential ally. Each thaw gives the PC who opens the pod **1 Fatigue** from the coolant leak.
- **Weapons Lab.** Alien atomizers and other alien energy weapons on autopsy benches. Party gear is *not* here.
- **Hangar Bay.** Flying saucers. One is intact. The party may steal it at the climax.
- **Engineering.** The **Captain's Log**, the reactor core, and the ship's gravity generator. Blowing the generator kills half the crew and locks the hangar — a desperate play.
- **The Observation Deck.** A window on Earth. No combat, no save — but each player must describe what their character is thinking.
- **The Bridge.** The alien **Captain** and his three **Guardians**. The climax.
