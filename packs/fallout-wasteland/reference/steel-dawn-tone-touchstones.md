*A military drama about a family fighting over its inheritance. The Brotherhood here is small, far from home, and short of people — initiates are wastelanders who signed up last month. Rahmani and Shin should both be right often enough that the choice between them hurts. The Overseer's job is to keep the argument human: two officers who have buried the same friend.*

**Touchstones:** *Fallout 76: Steel Dawn* and *Steel Reign* themselves, *Band of Brothers* (small unit, long war), *A Few Good Men* (the code vs. the conscience), *Apocalypse Now* (a mission upriver to a man who went too far), *The Island of Doctor Moreau* (Blackburn's lab), *Master and Commander* (an officer's duty to a crew far from home).

**Lean into:** the hiss of power armor on a mountain road; Rahmani's war glaive leaning against her command table; Shin's perfect posture and polished armor at the mess table; initiates who were farmers a month ago; the whine of a Hellstorm launcher in raider hands; the quiet of a transmitter that could reach California, and the sound it makes when a glaive goes through it.
