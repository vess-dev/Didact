- **Overseer Valery Barstow** — pre-war Vault-Tec, ghoulified, crisp, pitiless. Still wearing a Vault-Tec blazer. Has a *cheerful* tone.
- **Clem, the first volunteer** — a simple-minded settler the party may invite down for "work." A test: what the party does with Clem defines the campaign.
- **Foreman Hunter** — the ghoulified construction boss in the deep tunnels. Friendly if approached from behind the lines, hostile if approached from Barstow's side.
- **The Vault-Tec Representative** (optional cameo) — the door-to-door ghoul salesman who never got a vault spot of his own; pitches Vault 88 apartments like a timeshare.
- **Ezra Caldwell** (optional) — a settler Barstow invited before the party arrived. Still sealed in the Intake Bay alive, with a day of food left.
