### Securitron (Mk I)

10 HP, 3 Armor, 14 STR, 10 DEX, 13 WIL, 9mm SMG arm (d8, _bulky_), missile (d10, _blast_, 1 use)

- Mister House's automated enforcers. Cheerful cartoon face, rolling on one tread. Stationed on the Strip and inside the Lucky 38.
- **Loyal to House:** Ignores the party entirely unless they take hostile action on the Strip. Upgradeable to Mk II via the Chip.
