- **Sonic Emitter** (relic, d8 to living targets, 3 charges; disables holograms and force fields; a robot hit by it makes a WIL save or loses its next action; recharge via a Downtime Action).
- **Stealth Suit Mk II** (unique armor — see Unique Armors — and a chatty synthesized voice that narrates your vitals).
- **Protonic Inversal Axe** (unique, d10, _bulky_, fusion cell — see Unique Weapons).
- **LAER** (unique energy rifle, d10, _bulky_, microfusion cell — see Unique Weapons).
- **K9000 Cyberdog Gun** (unique, d8, _bulky_, .357 Magnum — see Unique Weapons).
- **X-2 Antenna**, **Compliance Regulator**, **Dr. Klein's Glove**, and **Dr. Mobius' Glove** — see Unique Weapons.
- **The Sink's Auto-Doc Upgrades** — each PC who lies down in it picks one permanent upgrade: +1 max STR, +1 max DEX, +1 max WIL, +1 max HP, or a re-grown nervous system that re-rolls the first failed Rad Scar save each session. Only one each. Ever.
- **Faction:** there is no Big MT faction in the wider wasteland. The Think Tank can be summoned once as a one-scene cameo later.
