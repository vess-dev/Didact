Broad strokes. Six to eight sessions, long enough for the three communities to feel like a place. The missing-girl case is the doorway; Avery's secret is the room.

1. **Docking at Far Harbor.** The party steps off the Nakanos' boat into the town. Fog on the wharf, condensers humming, Captain Avery running the militia. Kasumi Nakano's family letter gets the party through the gate. The Fog Clock starts ticking at 1.
2. **Finding Kasumi at Acadia.** A short trail through the Cranberry Island Bog leads the party to the pre-war observatory on the highest peak. The synth community of Acadia welcomes them warily. Kasumi is here, and she is fine — she ran *to* the refuge, not from it. DiMA asks to speak privately.
3. **DiMA's Memories.** DiMA confesses he has lost memories — pieces of his own past he cannot access on his own. He asks the party to walk into his **memory den**, a pre-war simulation rig, and recover them. The memories reveal things about Far Harbor the party will wish they didn't know.
4. **The Nucleus.** A pre-war nuclear submarine base run by the Children of Atom. High Confessor Tektus preaches a new, hungrier gospel. Sister Mai, a moderate, offers the party a way to speak to the faithful without igniting them. The party learns Atom has been planning to sabotage the fog condensers.
5. **Tending the Condensers.** The five condensers keep Far Harbor breathable. A side trail that stretches across three of the island's dangerous locations — the lumber mill, the Vim! factory, the National Park Visitor Center. Each repair buys the town time; each one the party skips ticks the Fog Clock up.
6. **Avery's Secret.** The turning point. DiMA's memories reveal that Captain Avery is a synth replacement for the real Avery, whom DiMA killed decades ago — and *Avery herself does not know*. DiMA made the decision alone, in desperation, in good faith, years ago. The party now holds a truth that will end the town if spoken aloud.
7. **The Three Plans.** Each faction makes its final move. Tektus wants to attack the town. DiMA wants his secrets kept. Far Harbor wants its enemies dead and its truth protected. The party is the only group with a complete picture.
8. **The Climax.** See **Climax** below — Peace, Wipe Out Acadia, Wipe Out the Children of Atom, Wipe Out Far Harbor, or walk away with Kasumi. The fog will keep coming either way; the question is who's still standing under it.
