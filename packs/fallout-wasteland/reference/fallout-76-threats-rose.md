### Rose

6 HP, 2 Armor, 10 STR, 13 DEX, 15 WIL, buzzsaw (d8), flamer (d8, _blast_)

- A Miss Nanny with a scrap-metal mohawk, wrapped chains, and painted tattoos, voiced by a dead woman's recordings. Rarely a combat encounter; more often the party's most dangerous friend.
- **Rosalynn's Habits:** Rose begs for chems she cannot use. Giving her one shifts her Reaction a step better. Refusing her twice shifts it a step worse.
- **The Legs:** The Cutthroats mined the ground beneath the Top of the World. Anyone who crosses it without Rose's directions makes a DEX save or takes d12 damage (_blast_).
