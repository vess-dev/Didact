*A lonely western that ends at a confessional. This is the most **personal** campaign in the book — one PC is its protagonist, the others are witnesses. Ulysses never shouts. The Divide never stops howling. Every scene should feel like a hand on a revolver under a table.*

**Touchstones:** *Lonesome Road* DLC itself (Ulysses's monologue cadence), *The Road* (McCarthy — grey sky, ash, small mercies), *Unforgiven* (the consequences of a reputation), *High Noon*, *No Country for Old Men*.

**Lean into:** wind, dust, and a radio that never stops broadcasting. Let Ulysses speak in full paragraphs and quote the PC back to themselves. The eyebot ED-E is a conscience the party carries on a strap. Every other NPC in this campaign is a ghost. The party has no allies here, only witnesses.
