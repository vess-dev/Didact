*Vaults of Fallout 4*

**Premise.** Built beneath Malden Middle School and sold as a safe shelter for its students, Vault 75 was a genetics program. The children were separated from their families on arrival; the parents and teachers were led off to "orientation" and shot by security. The children were raised to fear "Uptopland" and told that graduation would make them strong enough to save it. At eighteen, the finest were "harvested" for their genes, the clever and obedient joined the science staff, and the rest were executed after a graduation ceremony.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A pre-war elementary school façade. Posters on the walls.
- **Classrooms.** A pre-war curriculum still loops on the terminals, to rows of empty desks. The chalkboards count down the days to graduation.
- **Residential Block (Children's Wing).** Small cabins arranged like a summer camp.
- **The Testing Labs.** The dark heart of the vault. Dissection tables, gene sequencers, a wall of cryogenic samples labeled by student number.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer left a holotape confessing the experiment and asking for forgiveness. The Gunners who took the vault use the office as a mess hall and have never played it.

**Inhabitants.** GUNNER squad with a GUNNER SERGEANT (upper floors and classrooms), SENTRY BOT in the testing labs.

**Reward.** A **Genetic Sequencer Chip** (Relic) — installed in a friendly auto-doc, it identifies any PC's hidden bloodline (Overseer's choice). **Classroom Holotapes** containing real pre-war educational material — worth 300 caps to a Followers scholar.

**Hidden.** Deep in the testing labs, behind a wall of cryogenic samples, is a single small sample drawer that the Gunners never found — marked **TEACHER 01**. A genetic record of one of the original adults who was "eliminated." Delivered to an Institute scientist, it is worth 1000 caps. The Gunners will know if the party takes it.

**Hook.** A Gunner deserter wants to destroy the testing labs before his former crew gets the chance to weaponize them. He has a map but not the nerve.
