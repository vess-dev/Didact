*Jingoistic pre-war propaganda, slowly coming apart at the seams. The sim is supposed to be a clean action movie — cheerful orders, grateful soldiers, an enemy who speaks villain-lines. Every Glitch tick is a crack in the varnish.*

**Touchstones:** *Black Hawk Down*, *Starship Troopers* (the satire, not the straight reading), *The Manchurian Candidate*, old Cold War recruitment reels, Anchorage-era *Liberty Prime* broadcasts.

**Lean into:** crunchy snow, red banners, the *wrongness* of a sky that never clouds and a radio that never cuts out. The sim is trying very hard to convince the party it's real. Let the Overseer's voice shift when Chase speaks — one register too cheerful.
