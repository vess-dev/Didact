*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** Deliberately overpopulated. The vault was designed for 1,000 residents and its Overseer was instructed to admit an additional 1,000 on opening day — double the sustainable population.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Graffiti from the original occupation covers the walls, most of it angry.
- **Residential Block.** Every cabin is a triple-bunk room. Shared closets. Shared bathrooms. A single cabin marked *OVERSEER* stands alone.
- **The Collapsed Wing.** An entire residential block caved in under its own weight years ago. A mass grave is still identifiable underneath.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape is a sustained, weeping apology.
- **Reactor Room.** The reactor is undersized for the population. Still running, but barely.

**Inhabitants.**
- **Vault 27 Ghouls** — small, resentful community. Treat as lucid ghoul (3 HP, 9 STR, 10 DEX, 11 WIL, pipe pistol (d6)). They will parley but remember every slight.
- **Radroach Swarm** — detachment in the collapsed wing. See Bestiary.

**Reward.** A **Mass Grave Ledger** — a pre-war notebook listing every resident who died in the collapse. Worth 400 caps to a pre-war historian. An **Intact Reactor Regulator** (Relic component for a settlement Power Generator).

**Hidden.** Under one of the collapsed wing's crushed bunks, sealed in a waterproof tin, is a **child's drawing** of the collapse — made *before* it happened. The child had drawn it a week prior and given it to her mother. Nobody had understood it at the time.

**Hook.** A descendant of a Vault 27 survivor has asked the party to recover a specific name from the ledger — her grandmother.
