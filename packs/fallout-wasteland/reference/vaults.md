This chapter is a playable catalog of the known Vault-Tec vaults, drawn from the canonical Fallout settings. Each entry is a short dungeon-crawl guide the Overseer can drop into a campaign: the vault's original experimental premise, a few distinctive locations inside, the current inhabitants and threats, and a hook or two for pulling the party in.

The vaults are not a complete map. They are starting points — an Overseer should add rooms, rearrange floors, and adjust the threat level to fit the party. Each vault entry is deliberately smaller than a full adventure module. Think of it as the outline a seasoned Overseer would scribble into a session notebook to run an evening's play.
