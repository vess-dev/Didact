A genius wasteland engineer — **Isabel Cruz** — has put on a mask, taken up a megaphone, and declared herself the **Mechanist**, using a cache of modular robot parts to "protect" the Commonwealth by force. Her creations are rewriting themselves faster than she can command. The party is hired to find her lair, shut down the production line, and confront whatever she has become.
