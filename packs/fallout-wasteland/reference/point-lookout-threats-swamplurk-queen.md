### Swamplurk Queen

18 HP, 2 Armor, 16 STR, 12 DEX, 8 WIL, claws (d12), acid spit (d8, ranged)

- A monstrous swamp-bred mirelurk queen, grown enormous on punga and the cypress mire's background radiation. Lurks half-submerged in the shallows.
- **Cypress Ambush**: Enhanced the first round of any combat that began in dense growth or standing water.
