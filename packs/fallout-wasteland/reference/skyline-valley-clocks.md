- **Storm Clock** (d8). The Storm spreading. Ticks each time a storm harvester is lost, each week the party leaves the weather machine running, and each time Hugo's plans advance unopposed. At 8, the Storm spills past Skyline Valley into the Ash Heap and the Cranberry Bog: lightning strikes (d8, DEX save) on any exposed ridge, and Lost appearing wherever it rains.
- **Family Clock** (d6, hidden). How much Audrey and Hilda know about Hugo. Ticks when the party finds evidence of the Dark Hollow betrayal, reads Cassidy's journal, meets Alex, or recovers Oberlin's report. At 6, Audrey confronts her father alone, and the Overseer decides whether she survives it.
- **Goliath Clock** (d4). Vault 63's guardian protocol. Ticks each time the party damages vault systems or fights in the engineering sector. At 4, a Storm Goliath is dispatched against them — and if one is destroyed, the engineering sector begins building its replacement.
