*Vaults of Fallout 76*

**Premise.** A secure depository for the pre-war United States gold reserve, built in secret from 2072 in the Savage Divide — its number is the atomic number of gold. Army teams moved the entire Fort Knox reserve inside. Not technically an experiment; this was the real thing. When the bombs fell, the Secret Service detail guarding it was sealed in, and a reactor accident trapped the survivors in their own barracks. See the **Wastelanders** campaign frame.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** The heaviest blast door in the Vault-Tec catalog.
- **Security Wing.** Automated pre-war turrets, still functional.
- **The Gold Processing Room.** A thousand bars of bullion on pallets — a suspiciously small share of a national reserve.
- **The Main Vaults.** Sealed behind doors nobody has opened since 2077. The rest of the gold is in there.
- **The Barracks.** Where the surviving Secret Service agents — **AC**, **Slick**, **Regs**, and **Mercy** — have been locked down since the reactor failed, and where their ghoulified reactor tech, **Digger**, waits outside the door.
- **Overseer's Office.** Tiny. The watch officer's desk still holds the standing orders: treat all intruders as hostile agents; stay out of sight.
- **Reactor Room.** Fusion-powered, sealed.

**Inhabitants.**
- **Vault 79 Turret Network** — detachment. 8 HP, 2 Armor, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 3 WIL, heavy laser (d10, _blast_). Locked to the vault's IFF grid; the right keycard shuts them off.
- **Sentry Bot** — see Bestiary. Guards the main chamber.

**Reward.** **Gold bullion** — the Fort Knox reserve, cast in bars: each trades for 20 caps with anyone who believes the Overseer's new currency will hold, and ten fill a slot; nobody in Appalachia can change more than ten bars a week into caps or goods. The vault holds more than any party can carry or spend in a lifetime; the real reward is choosing what to do about it. A **Pre-war Bank Seal** — a heavy brass-and-glass token stamped with the old U.S. Treasury crest. Presenting it to any still-running pre-war automated bank, vending cluster, or corporate credit kiosk unlocks one transaction of the Overseer's choosing (a withdrawal, a safe deposit, a forgotten pre-war account). The first faction to hear a party is carrying it will try to take it.

**Hidden.** One of the gold pallets near the back of the chamber has a single bar that is *not* gold — painted lead, carved with a glyph. It was a smuggler's flag, left here by one of the vault's original guards to mark the location of a **pre-war courier drop** outside the vault. Following the coordinates leads to a buried footlocker in the Wastes.

**Hook.** Every major faction in the region wants Vault 79's gold. A caravaneer knows the code. She needs someone to walk her in, and she needs the vault's existence kept quiet afterwards — though she knows it won't be.
