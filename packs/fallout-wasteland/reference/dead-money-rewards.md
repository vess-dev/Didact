- **Holorifle** (unique, d10, _bulky_, microfusion cell — see Unique Weapons). Elijah's own design.
- **Bear Trap Fist** (unique unarmed, d8 — see Unique Weapons).
- **Automatic Rifle** (unique, d10, _bulky_, .308 — see Unique Weapons).
- **Christine's CoS Silencer Rifle**, **Dean's Tuxedo**, and **Sierra Madre Reinforced Armor** — see Unique Weapons and Unique Armors.
- **Sierra Madre Gold Bars** (37 of them, _bulky_ — two slots each; see Relics). Walking out with more than one is an act of will.
- **Sierra Madre Chips** (casino scrip, worthless outside the Madre). The casino vending machines still honor them — food, chems, even ammo.
- **Faction:** +1 Brotherhood (for taking Elijah down) or −1 (for succeeding at his plan).
