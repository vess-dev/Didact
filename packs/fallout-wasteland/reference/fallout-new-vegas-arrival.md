You wake in **Doc Mitchell's** house in **Goodsprings**, southern Mojave, with a headache and a story you don't remember. Mitchell patches you up, runs the **Vigor Tester**, asks a few questions, and sets you loose. The town is in trouble: **Joe Cobb** and a crew of Powder Gangers plan to raid in a day or two. Whether you defend Goodsprings or slip out the back sets your reputation with the NCR, who will hear about it either way.
