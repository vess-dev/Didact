### Atom Cleanser

10 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 13 WIL, flamer (d8, _blast_, _bulky_), ritual bat (d8)

- Children of Atom zealot in blessed radiation gear. Rad-resistant to a fault.
- **Cleansed**: Ignores Rads entirely. A Critical Damage result from a Cleanser adds 1 Rad to the target.
