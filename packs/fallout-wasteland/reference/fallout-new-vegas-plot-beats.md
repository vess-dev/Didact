Broad strokes. Six to ten sessions — the Mojave is big, and the party will wander. The chase of Benny is the spine; the Hoover Dam decision is the throat.

1. **Goodsprings.** Doc Mitchell, **Sunny Smiles**, **Trudy** behind the bar at the Prospector Saloon. Joe Cobb and his Powder Gangers coming tomorrow morning. The party defends or flees. Sunny gives a PC a ranch-dog companion if they help. Chet at the General Store reluctantly trades supplies for favors. Victor the Securitron is also in town and keeps being around.
2. **Primm and Nipton.** South on the I-15. Primm is a broken casino town looking for a sheriff (Primm Slim's automated warning, Deputy Beagle, the convict boss holed up in the Bison Steve hotel). Nipton is worse — the Legion rolled through a day before the party, raffled the town, and crucified half the population. A lone Legion **frumentarius** stands on the school steps and explains the lesson.
3. **Novac and Boulder City.** The party picks up the Benny trail at **Novac** — a roadside motel with a giant dinosaur statue called *Dinky*. **Manny Vargas**, a sniper at Dinky's mouth, remembers Benny heading north for the Strip. **Boulder City**, a ruined NCR checkpoint town, offers the party an NCR faction contact — **Lieutenant Monroe**, mid-standoff with the same Great Khans who helped Benny dig the Courier's grave.
4. **The Strip.** New Vegas is still lit. The NCR has the gate, House's Securitrons have the inside. Credit check before you walk in. Benny is at **The Tops**, a rat-pack-themed casino. The party confronts him. Three outcomes: kill him in his suite and take **Maria** off his body, arrest him and haul him to Mr. House, or slip into bed with him and take the Chip in the morning.
5. **Mister House's Offer.** With the Chip, the party may accept an audience at the **Lucky 38**. Mr. House reveals himself as a pre-war CEO sealed in a life-support chamber for over two centuries. He offers a contract: deliver the Chip to the terminal beneath the Fort, secure two outlying assets, and win the Mojave for Vegas.
6. **The NCR's Offer.** Contact with **Colonel James Hsu** at **Camp McCarran** makes the case for the Republic. Hsu needs the party to investigate a missing caravan, which uncovers a Legion spy network (**Frumentarii**) inside the NCR itself. A patriot run with a brittle hierarchy.
7. **Caesar's Offer.** The party is summoned to **The Fort**, on the far side of the Colorado. They meet **Caesar** — Edward Sallow — face to face. He offers them the favor of Rome. He also wants something done about the Brotherhood of Steel hiding in **Hidden Valley** (a bunker he cannot afford to leave at his back). Agreeing is crossing a line; disagreeing means killing your way out of camp.
8. **The Independent Road.** If the party killed Benny or let him escape, they left with a gift: **Yes Man**, a hackable Securitron who now answers to the party directly. Yes Man makes a fourth path: a **free Vegas** under no flag but the party's, won by turning every other faction's assets to the party's use.
9. **The Outlying Assets.** The Mojave's three outlying powers — the **Great Khans**, the **Brotherhood of Steel**, and the **Boomers** — can be recruited for or against the Dam. Each requires a side arc: defusing the Khans' alliance with the Legion, brokering with Elder McNamara at Hidden Valley, and earning the Boomers' trust at Nellis Air Force Base by recovering a crashed B-29 from the bottom of Lake Mead.
10. **The Second Battle of Hoover Dam.** The party, their allies, and a hundred miles of broken highway meet at the Dam. The Legion pushes from the east. The NCR holds the west. Mr. House or Yes Man sends the Securitron army, or not. The Dam holds, or it doesn't. See **Climax**.
