### Secret Service Agent

7 HP, 2 Armor, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 14 WIL, 10mm pistol (d8), police baton (d6)

- One of the agents sealed in Vault 79's barracks since 2077. Disciplined, suspicious of everyone, and still under orders to treat intruders as hostile.
- **Protective Detail:** When an agent is attacked, another agent within reach may take the hit instead.
