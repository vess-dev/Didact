### Kellogg

10 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 15 DEX, 14 WIL, Kellogg's pistol — .44 revolver (d10, first shot each combat is Enhanced), combat knife (d6)

- Mercenary kept alive over a century by cybernetic implants. Took Shaun from the party's arms. Does not speak about it.
- **Neural Implants:** Cannot be intimidated. Automatically passes Morale saves. Takes one extra round to die on Critical Damage, and uses that round to shoot the closest living thing.
