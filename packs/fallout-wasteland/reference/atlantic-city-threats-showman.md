### Showman

6 HP, 13 STR, 14 DEX, 9 WIL, baseball bat (d8), throwing knives (d4, ranged)

- A performer of the Boardwalk cult in greasepaint and sequins. Every fight is a show, and every show needs an audience.
- **Play to the Crowd:** A Showman who has an audience makes its first attack each combat Enhanced.
