### General Jingwei

10 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 15 DEX, 14 WIL, shocksword (d10, ignores 1 Armor), sidearm (d8)

- The simulation's antagonist general. Honorable; accepts a duel.
- **Duelist**: While he is in single combat with one PC, attacks against him from bystanders are Impaired — his pre-war honor discipline reads the room.
