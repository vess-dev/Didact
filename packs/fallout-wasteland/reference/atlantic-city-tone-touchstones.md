*Mob noir on a drowned boardwalk. Sequins over rot, a lounge singer with steel in her voice, a family more dangerous to each other than any rival. Everyone in Acee is civil because the treaty says so. Under the table, everyone is armed. The Overseer's job is to keep the glamour up while the water rises.*

**Touchstones:** *Fallout 76: Atlantic City* itself, *Boardwalk Empire*, *The Godfather Part II* (the son who wanted out, the father who never left), *Miller's Crossing*, *Nightmare Alley* (the Showmen's sideshow), *The Little Shop of Horrors* (the Overgrown), the Jersey Devil of Pine Barrens folklore. Big-band ballads. Neon reflected in floodwater.

**Lean into:** a mansion lit up like a casino in the middle of the mountains; a tuba solo interrupted by gunfire; the Casino Quarter's humid streets overgrown with mutant vines around a spotless casino door; a Muni forestry ranger with a flamer and a hard hat; the Showmen's painted faces and cheering crowds; floodwater to the second floor and rowboats tied to street signs; the leathery flap of something big over the Pine Barrens.
