- **Kiloton Radium Rifle** (unique radium rifle, d10, _blast_, _bulky_ — see Unique Weapons).
- **Admiral's Friend** (unique harpoon gun, d10, _bulky_ — see Unique Weapons). The Mariner's parting gift.
- **Marine Tactical Armor** (unique, 2 Armor, _bulky_ — see Unique Armors).
- **Fog Condenser Part** (relic, deployable in a settlement to ward off rad storms one session per installation).
- **Memory Holotape** (relic, one read, grants **one free Bond** on any PC it's played for).
- **Faction:** +1 Far Harbor, +1 Acadia, or +1 Children of Atom depending on the climax. Two can be kept if the party is careful.
