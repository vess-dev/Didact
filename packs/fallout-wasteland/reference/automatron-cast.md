- **Isabel Cruz / The Mechanist** — a genuine engineer who thought she could fix the wasteland with machines. The voice-filter on her mask makes her sound like a god. She's been sleeping five hours a week.
- **Ada** — a custom robot, sole survivor of the caravan the Mechanist's robots destroyed. Wants revenge. A potential companion — see Rewards.
- **Jezebel (a Robobrain head)** — a disembodied Robobrain on a shelf at Fort Hagen. Charming, cruel. Will bargain. The head itself is portable, _bulky_, 2 slots.
- **KL-E-O** — an Assaultron shopkeep in Goodneighbor, if the party needs parts. Will also buy robot heads for a premium.
- **Rogue Mister Handies** — Isabel's former assistants, now malfunctioning and in denial that she's in charge.
