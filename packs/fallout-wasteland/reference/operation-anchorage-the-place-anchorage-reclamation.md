All scenes happen *inside the sim*. HP and gear in the real world are paused; the party receives **sim loadouts** (see Rewards). Wounds in the sim are not real: HP, STR, and Scars lost inside are restored on ejection. A PC who takes Critical Damage in the sim ejects with 1 Fatigue and a migraine.

- **Listening Post Bravo.** The drop-in. Chinese perimeter patrol. A signal tower must be lit to call artillery.
- **The Chimera Depot.** A yard of pre-war Chinese armored rigs — co-opted mining vehicles fitted with weapons and heavy armor. Rigging one to self-destruct blows the gate to the next zone.
- **The Ice Camp.** A Crimson Dragoon sniper nest, three stealth suits in the snow. Taking the camp quiet clears the pass. Taking it loud summons a Chimera.
- **The Pulse Field.** An EMP minefield. A WIL save (or any tech perk) shuts down a lane.
- **General Jingwei's Bunker.** The climax. The sim was never meant to be *lost*.
