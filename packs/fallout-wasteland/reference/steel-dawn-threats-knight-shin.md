### Knight Shin

10 HP, 3 Armor, 15 STR, 13 DEX, 14 WIL, automatic laser rifle (d10, _bulky_), combat knife (d6)

- Only a combat encounter if the party sides with Rahmani and refuses to let him leave for California. In T-51 power armor.
- **The Chain That Binds:** Shin automatically passes Morale saves.
