- **Experiment Clock** (d4). Barstow's patience with the party's progress. Starts at 0; ticks when the party refuses an experimental pod, frees a subject mid-experiment, or spends a Downtime Action on anything other than her work. Completing an experimental pod walks it back one. At 4, she starts issuing threats to the first Bond-NPC the party knows from outside the vault.
- **Tunnel Clock** (d6). Radscorpion intrusion from the deep caverns. Ticks when the party detonates explosives in the caverns, breaches an unmapped wall, or leaves a scouting radscorpion alive to skitter back to the nest. At 6, a queen breaks through a wall of the dormitories.
- **Mutiny Clock** (d4). Settler patience, once any settler has been strapped into a pod. Ticks when a settler sees the party participate in an experiment, when a pod subject visibly deteriorates in front of the others, or when Barstow publicly overrules the party on a settler's behalf. At 4, the settlers revolt against Barstow *and* the party.
