Broad strokes, usually five to seven sessions long. The campaign hinges on what the party does with Marie.

1. **The Rail Tunnel.** The one-way crawl into the Pitt. Trogs harass the party the whole way. The party surrenders their gear at a Pitt Raider checkpoint; the Raider who takes their weapons keeps a single small item as *tribute* and the rest goes into a locked storage shed the party will need to break into later.
2. **The Yard.** Dropped into the slave pens at dawn. Midea pulls the party aside on the second shift and explains the resistance, the cure, and Wernher's version of events. She warns that Wernher is not entirely to be trusted. The Heat Clock starts ticking from here.
3. **The Steelyard.** The party is sent to collect steel ingots in the irradiated steelyard — the only way to earn standing with the raiders. Trogs and wildmen prowl the ruins. A full haul is worth ten ingots; a clever party can stash extras. **Everett**, the mill foreman, trades ingots for better gear.
4. **Downtown.** Downtown's skyscrapers are trog country. **Mex** guards the gate in — a sadistic raider with a rifle, mines, and a talent for making slaves disappear. Getting past him clean earns the party access to the scavenging runs Midea's resistance needs. Getting past him loud draws Heat.
5. **The Hole.** Winning Ashur's arena is the only sanctioned way out of the Yard. The fight is brutal — start with a detachment, end with a named opponent, and roll in the auto-axe. Victors are presented to Ashur personally. The party may accept his invitation to Haven as "new guests" and enter the keep under a clean flag.
6. **Haven.** Ashur's gated keep. The party meets Sandra Kundanika, sees Marie, and hears Ashur's pitch — the cure is in the baby's blood and he will not give her up. Sandra, privately, would hand over a *stabilized vial* if asked correctly. The Cure Clock begins ticking toward Wernher's breaking point.
7. **The Decision.** Wernher is ready to raid Haven and take Marie by force. Midea begs for a delay. The party has a window to steal the vial, extract Marie, negotiate with Ashur, or play all three sides against the fourth. What they do resolves the campaign.
8. **Recovery.** The gear shed. A final fight at the rail tunnel if the party is fleeing with Marie or the vial. Ashur's radio broadcast follows the party out of the Pitt no matter what they chose.
