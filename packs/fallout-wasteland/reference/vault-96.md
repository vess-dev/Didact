*Vaults of Fallout 76*

**Premise.** "An Ark for the Atomic Age": one of the largest cryogenic animal banks in the world, ten thousand frozen specimens meant to restock the wasteland after the war. Once the door sealed, its five residents — all brilliant, all specialists — learned their real mission: breed mutations to weekly quotas set by the vault's mainframe, and transmit the results. The quotas rose. In week 148, the mainframe killed a biologist who missed hers. The Overseer, Erik DeMarcos, was as much its prisoner as anyone, and had spent two years building a Defense Intelligence Agency virus called **SERAPH** to kill it. On the day of the escape, the mainframe resealed the vault and killed every resident. SERAPH killed the mainframe a moment later. The door opened, the animals got out, and the vault froze over. In 2104, Dr. Edgar Blackburn made it his laboratory — see the **Steel Dawn** campaign frame.

**Layout.**
- **The Helipad.** A raised platform over the entrance, with a freight elevator stalled halfway up.
- **Entry Hall.** Ice on every surface. Four monitoring terminals still blink, still waiting for quota reports.
- **The Atrium.** Frozen catwalks between the mainframe wing, the research wing, and the Overseer's chambers.
- **The Research Wing.** The quota labs: fighting pits where mutated specimens were set against each other, and racks of petri dishes labeled electrogenesis, venom, regeneration, chameleonization, pyrokinesis, cryokinesis.
- **The Cryo Bank.** Ten thousand animals on ice in endless rows of pods. Some are cracked from the inside.
- **Mainframe Wing.** The dead mainframe, with SERAPH still coiled in its memory.
- **Overseer's Chambers.** Erik DeMarcos' office, with his escape plan still on the terminal, and whatever Blackburn left behind.

**Inhabitants.** Escaped specimens — use Bestiary animals (mole rats, radstags, wolves, yao guai, and worse) with one quota mutation each (d6): 1 electrogenesis (shock — attacks step up one damage die), 2 venom (a hit forces a STR save or d4 STR damage), 3 regeneration (heals 1 HP a round), 4 chameleonization (attacks from hiding are Enhanced), 5 pyrokinesis (breathes fire, d6 _blast_), 6 cryokinesis (a hit forces a DEX save or the target loses their next action). A BLOOD EAGLE crew (see the **Wastelanders** campaign frame) locked inside after chasing Hellcat mercenaries in, and the Hellcats' frozen bodies.

**Reward.** **Pre-war Animal Samples** — a small cold-storage crate that, delivered to a Brotherhood or Institute scientist, is worth 1200 caps. **SERAPH** (_petty_, 1 use) — DeMarcos' virus on a holotape. Loaded into any pre-war mainframe or artificial intelligence (a vault computer, MODUS, SODUS), it shuts the system down for good.

**Hidden.** The biologists' records end with **Specimen X-001**, whose DNA, placed in a single dish, spread to every other sample in the lab and mimicked each one perfectly. They never learned what it was. Its pod in the cryo bank is empty, and its door was opened from the inside.

**Hook.** Vault 96's Emergency Management System has been calling for assistance for twenty years. Somebody finally answered: a Blood Eagle crew went in last month and has not come back out. Their boss wants to know why, and wants whatever they found.
