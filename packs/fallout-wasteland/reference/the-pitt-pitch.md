The ruins of Pittsburgh are a walled industrial hell. **Lord Ashur**, an ex-Brotherhood paladin, rules from **Haven**, an intact tower above the mills. His slaves work the forges in a rad-saturated haze, slowly becoming **Trogs**. His infant daughter is immune — her blood may be a cure, or a lie Ashur tells himself.
