The party walks out of Vault 76 into October sun. At the foot of the ramp, beneath an arch of Reclamation Day balloons, a Mister Handy named **Pennington** waits with a holotape. The Overseer's recorded voice explains that she has gone ahead on a mission of her own, that she has left a C.A.M.P. — a portable campsite kit — at the crossroads north of Flatwoods, and that she will be leaving supply caches for them along the way. She sounds proud of them. She sounds like she is hiding something.
