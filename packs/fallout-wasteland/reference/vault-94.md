*Vaults of Fallout 76*

**Premise.** An experiment testing how a non-violent ideological group would fare when confronted with post-apocalyptic realities. The vault was populated by an agrarian religious community led by **Pastor Gabriella Salavar**. After a mandatory one-year shelter period, the vault opened and its residents sent ambassadors into Appalachia as missionaries, offering food and aid. Outsiders from Harpers Ferry, mistaking the vault dwellers' hospitality for a trap, massacred the Community Council — then, sure the vault's **G.E.C.K.** was a mind-control machine, shot it with a minigun. Its fusion generator melted down in a nuclear blast, and the fog that poured from the vault mutated the surrounding swamp into the worst of **the Mire**.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Decorated with pre-war pacifist art and posters about reconciliation. The cog door answers only to an ambassador's access code, and none of the ambassadors' bodies scattered across Appalachia still carry one. It stayed sealed until 2103; mirelurks have nested in the flooded halls since.
- **Residential Commune.** Open-plan dormitories with no personal spaces. A communal kitchen. A single shared toolkit. Everything designed for collective living.
- **The G.E.C.K. Chamber.** The heart of the vault. The Garden of Eden Creation Kit is here — damaged, leaking mutagenic material into the groundwater. The surrounding walls are choked with strangler vines that knot into a single pulsing mass the Vault 76 raiders called the **Strangler Heart**. They killed it once. It is growing back.
- **Meditation Garden.** A pre-war Zen-style garden with a dry fountain, somehow mostly intact despite the Mire creeping in from outside.
- **The Council Room.** Vault 94 had no Overseer, only a Community Council, and this is where the outsiders killed it. Pastor Salavar's terminal holds her final entries: the day the outsiders came, and the moment she realized the G.E.C.K. had broken. In a room off the atrium, a second terminal holds the reports of **Tyrone Hayes**, the maintenance engineer who was secretly Vault-Tec's observer.

**Inhabitants.**
- **Mirelurks** — see Bestiary. They came in through the flooded entrance.
- **Strangler Vines** — the Mire is alive in here. Treat vines as an environmental hazard (not a combatant): any PC moving through a vine-choked room makes a DEX save or takes d4 direct STR damage from constriction. This bypasses HP — the vines squeeze, not strike.
- **Commune Ghoul** — 5 HP, 8 STR, 9 DEX, 14 WIL, open palms (no damage). The last of the original residents. They will beg the party to leave before the vines get worse.

**Reward.** **The Meditation Garden Stone** (_petty_ relic) — a PC carrying it may, once per session, automatically pass a single WIL save. **A G.E.C.K. Component** — damaged but recoverable. Worth 1500 caps to a Brotherhood scribe or an Institute scientist. Using it on a settlement is an Overseer decision with permanent consequences.

**Hidden.** The G.E.C.K. chamber's control terminal has a buried log — the **final transmission from the outsiders** who attacked the vault. They didn't know what a G.E.C.K. was. They thought it was a mind-control machine. The log ends mid-sentence. An Overseer who wants to tie Vault 94 to the Mire's ongoing ecology should follow this thread.

**Hook.** A ghoul resident has walked out of Vault 94 and is living in a nearby settlement, preaching a pacifism so old nobody knows what to do with it. He wants someone to go back with him for a reason he won't explain until they get there.
