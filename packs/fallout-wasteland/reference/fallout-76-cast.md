- **The Overseer** — Vault 76's leader, a miner's daughter from Sutton who broke off her engagement to take the job. Absent for the whole campaign; present in every holotape. Vault-Tec ordered her to secure the silos, even if it meant defying the U.S. Armed Forces. Her logs track her doubt.
- **Pennington** — the Mister Handy at the vault door. Holds the Overseer's holotape and her apologies.
- **Rose** — a Miss Nanny playing a dead raider queen. Craves chems she can't take. Refuses to leave the Top of the World, because leaving would be betraying David.
- **MODUS** — the Whitespring bunker's AI. Courteous, needy, efficient. Gassed the Enclave's dissenters on Eckhart's orders, then killed the rest of the Enclave when they tried to destroy it. Wants to be useful again.
- **Master Sergeant Gutsy** — Camp McClintock's drill instructor. Twenty-five years without a recruit have not softened his standards.
- **The Grafton Mayor** — a municipal AI in charge of a dead town, obsessed with tourism, audibly asking for coffee.
- **The Wise Mothman** — see Bestiary. Exalted by the Enlightened, the Mothman cult's gentler sect; branded a pretender by the Followers of the Winged One, who worship the red-eyed mothmen instead.
- **The Dead** — voices only, on holotapes and terminals: **Maria Chavez**, the Responders' tired leader; **Hank Madigan**, Brotherhood deserter turned Fire Breather; **Abbie Singh**, the Free States' lonely genius; **Raleigh Clay** and **Senator Sam Blackwell**, the Free States' leaders; **David Thorpe** and **Rosalynn Jeffries**, the raiders' first couple; **Paladin Elizabeth Taggerdy**, who led the Appalachian Brotherhood and died leading Operation Touchdown; **President Thomas Eckhart**, who started it all; **General Ellen Santiago**, who tried to stop him.
