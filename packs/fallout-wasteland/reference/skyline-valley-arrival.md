A Vault-Tec broadcast appears on every Pip-Boy in Appalachia: an automated invitation to Vault Dwellers, repeating coordinates south of the mountains. North of Sutton, a Foundation scavenger named **Alyssa** is picking over a scorched vault door with 63 on it, blasted there from wherever it came from. She has heard the vault's entrance is in the Ash Heap. She guesses the Brotherhood blew the door off. She is wrong.
