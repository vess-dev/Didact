Broad strokes. Six to ten sessions; Appalachia is a sandbox with a spine. The first half should feel like wandering a haunted forest; the second half should feel like assembling a bomb.

1. **Reclamation Day.** The vault, Pennington, the Overseer's C.A.M.P. at the crossroads. Her first logs describe the empty Forest and her dread. They point south to **Flatwoods**.
2. **Flatwoods and the Responders.** An empty town, a church, a school bus — and the party's first Scorched, a townsperson still wearing an apron. The Responders' automated volunteer program is still running: terminals and holotapes teach the party to test river water for radiation and to cook meat safe enough to eat. Passing the course certifies the party as Responders volunteers. The dead are still teaching. The trail points to the Responders' last base at **Morgantown Airport**.
3. **Morgantown Airport.** The Responders' last stand, overrun on November 7, 2096. Scorched in the terminal, a barricaded second floor, last messages on the tower radio. The Responders' elite **Fire Breathers** trained out of the old Charleston fire station; their lieutenant, **Hank Madigan**, went into the raider country of the Savage Divide carrying the uplink that would tie the Responders into the Free States' Scorched detectors. The raiders took him, and he never came back.
4. **Charleston and the Fire Breathers.** Charleston was the Responders' capital until Christmas morning, 2082, when the raider king **David Thorpe** blew the Summersville Dam with a stolen mini nuke and drowned the city. At the Charleston Fire Department, the Fire Breathers' automated exam still waits — a written quiz, an obstacle course through the *Charleston Herald* building, and a final exam among the Scorched in the Belching Betty mineshaft in the Ash Heap. Passing it earns the uniform. The **AVR Medical Center** holds Dr. Hudson's Scorched vaccine research, one step from finished when the Responders fell. Madigan's trail runs up the mountain to the **Top of the World**.
5. **Top of the World.** The raiders are gone. Only **Rose** is left — a heavily modified Miss Nanny robot running on the recorded personality of **Rosalynn Jeffries**, Thorpe's lover and second-in-command, dead since 2082. She will help the party find Madigan's uplink, but first they must prove themselves: repair her radio station, craft raider tricks, walk up to a deathclaw and live. Rose is charming, profane, and heartbroken.
6. **The Five Keys.** The raiders locked their treasure beneath the **Pleasant Valley Ski Resort** and split the key between five gang leaders. The party must collect every fragment from a corpse or something worse: **Freddie Lang** of the Blackwater Bandits, now a glowing one deep in Blackwater Mine; **Walter Griswold** of the Trappers, a booby-trapped corpse in Huntersville; **Margie McClintock** of the Diehards, whose copy survives at the Palace of the Winding Path; **Morris Stevens** of the Gourmands, now the progenitor wendigo in a cave full of bones; and **David Thorpe** himself, Scorched, still wearing his raider armor at Big Fred's BBQ Shack. Behind the cache door is Madigan's uplink. In the Charleston Capitol's jail is the truth about Rosalynn: the Responders took her alive on Christmas Eve and let David believe she was dead, and the flood he loosed the next morning drowned her in her cell.
7. **The Mire and the Free States.** Madigan's uplink was meant for **Abbie Singh**, the Free States' last genius, who built the **Scorched Detection System** from her bunker in the Mire. She is dead; her terminals are not. The party restores her detectors across the Mire, carries her master holotape to Raleigh Clay's terminal in the **Harpers Ferry** armory and on to the Charleston Capitol, and watches every detector point the same way: the Cranberry Bog. Abbie's last files name the one faction that knew how to fight back.
8. **Defiance Has Fallen.** Years ago, the Brotherhood broadcast a single message — *Defiance has fallen* — and went silent. Fort Defiance, the old Allegheny Asylum, is full of Scorched and dead knights. Its secure floor is sealed to anyone who is not a U.S. soldier, so the party enlists at **Camp McClintock**, where **Master Sergeant Gutsy** has been running basic training for twenty-five years with no recruits. Then the party follows five Brotherhood transponders to the **glassed cavern**, where Paladin Taggerdy died in Operation Touchdown, and takes her ID. Her terminal holds the Brotherhood's **Ultimate Solution**: nuke the fissures. Elder Maxson forbade it. Taggerdy never got the chance to disobey.
9. **Uncle Sam and the Whitespring.** A nuclear launch requires what only the Enclave had. The trail runs through a pre-war *Charleston Herald* interview with **Senator Sam Blackwell** to his bunker under an abandoned waste dump in the Mire — deathclaws in the pit, a dead Enclave agent in the dark — and from there to the **Whitespring Resort**, where a bunker beneath the resort opens its doors to the party with a voice like a gracious host. **MODUS**, the Enclave's AI, is delighted to have staff again. It will make the party officers, then generals, if they do its errands. It is also the last witness to the truth: **President Thomas Eckhart** loosed the scorchbeasts on Appalachia on purpose, to fool the automated launch system into raising the DEFCON rating and handing him the keys to a war with China.
10. **I Am Become Death.** A launch needs three things: a **Nuclear Keycard** from a Strategic Air Command cargobot and its vertibot escort, a launch code assembled from eight pieces carried by Scorched and ghoul officers and decrypted in the Whitespring, and a silo — **Site Alpha**, **Bravo**, or **Charlie** — fought through room by room against its own security robots, with a leaking reactor to repair before the doors will open. The Overseer's caches sit at all three silos. Her last logs are there too. See **Climax**.
