- **The Dismemberer** (unique fire axe, d10, _bulky_ — Plik's prize; see Unique Weapons).
- **Backwater Rifle** (unique lever-action rifle, d10, _bulky_ — first shot of any combat is Enhanced).
- **Microwave Emitter** (unique, d8, microfusion cell, disintegrates on Critical Damage — no salvage; see Unique Weapons).
- **Punga-Ash War Paint** (relic, 3 uses; the wearer cannot be Panicked for a week after application).
- **Faction:** +1 to whichever of Lockheart, Calvert, or the Brethren of the Punga the party sides with at the climax.
