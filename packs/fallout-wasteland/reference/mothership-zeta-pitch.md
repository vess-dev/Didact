An **alien recon saucer** crashed in the Capital Wasteland and began transmitting a homing beacon on a hidden band. Anyone within range gets **teleported aboard** the Mothership Zeta, an alien cruiser in low orbit, stripped of their gear and dropped in a containment cell. The rest of the campaign is an escape, then a mutiny, then a takeover.
