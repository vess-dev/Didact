### Hellcat Mercenary

7 HP, 3 Armor, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 10 WIL, combat rifle (d10, _bulky_) or missile launcher (d12, _blast_, _bulky_)

- A soldier of the Hellcat Company, a mercenary outfit out of Pittsburgh, in its own make of power armor. The Hellcats bled for the Fanatics in the Pitt, and now they take any contract that pays.
- **Power Armor:** Cannot sneak, sprint, or swim. Brotherhood knights hate the paint job.
