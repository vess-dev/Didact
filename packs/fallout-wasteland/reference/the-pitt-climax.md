Wernher wants the baby. Ashur wants the work to continue. Sandra wants both her daughter *and* the cure. Three endings:

- **Take Marie.** The slaves rise. The city burns for days. The party walks out with the cure and the baby. Ashur dies in his keep. Marie grows up in a safer place — the wasteland owes the party a debt they will remember.
- **Leave Marie, free the slaves.** Sandra hands over a stabilized vial under the table. Ashur lives but loses his workforce; the Pitt becomes a ghost town within a year.
- **Side with Ashur.** The resistance is crushed. Ashur rewards the party lavishly and names them citizens of his new nation. Wernher dies cursing their name. The cure is real, eventually, but years away.
