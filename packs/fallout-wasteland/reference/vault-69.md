*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** The inverse of Vault 68 — 999 women and a single man. As with its counterpart, the stated purpose was never explained.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** Intact.
- **Residential Block.** Organized into a functional community that survived for generations. The sole male resident became a minor folkloric figure within the vault.
- **The Archive.** A room filled with hand-written records, diaries, and community minutes — the vault's residents organized into a functional democracy.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape thanks her community for keeping faith with each other against the experiment.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine.

**Inhabitants.** A small, proud ghoul matriarchy still living in the vault. They are peaceful but distrustful of newcomers.

**Reward.** **The Vault 69 Archive** — a working pre-war democratic model in miniature, worth 800 caps to an NCR historian. A **Hand-Written Constitution** (Relic, _petty_) — a settlement that adopts it counts its Defense as 1 higher against its next raid.

**Hidden.** In the archive's deepest drawer, a sealed envelope marked in the handwriting of the original Overseer: a **confession** that the sole male resident had been her brother, and her private notes on how the community had chosen to protect him anyway. The matriarchy's current leader knows. She has kept the secret her whole life.

**Hook.** The matriarchy's current leader is dying and wants a single outside witness to her final address before she is laid to rest.
