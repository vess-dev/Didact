### Desmond Lockheart

9 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 17 WIL, silenced 10mm (d8), cane-sword (d6)

- Pre-war ghoul spy. Hates Calvert. Carries two hundred years of grudge and a working pistol.
- **Century of Patience**: Once per combat, re-rolls a failed save.
