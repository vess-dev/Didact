- **Collar Clock** (d4). Ticks when the party triggers an interference — entering a zone with an active broadcast speaker, detonating explosives near the collars, crossing a radio dead zone. At 4, a PC of the Overseer's choice takes d6 damage from the collar — any Scar that results is **Concussed**. Reset to 0 when the party silences the nearest speaker or leaves the interference.
- **Elijah Clock** (d6). Elijah's patience. Ticks when the party mouths off on the radio. At 6, he cuts the party loose: unless someone talks him back from it (a WIL save, and a reason he still needs them), every collar fires in earnest — d12 damage to each PC, ignoring Armor — and the clock resets to 3.
- **Cloud Clock** (ambient). At each new zone, roll 1d6: on a 1–2, the fog surges and the whole zone deals 1d4 STR damage to any exposed PC.
