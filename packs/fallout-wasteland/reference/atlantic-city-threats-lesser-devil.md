### Lesser Devil

7 HP, 14 STR, 15 DEX, 6 WIL, claws and bite (d8+d6)

- A juvenile Jersey Devil: a goat-headed, bat-winged mutant that wanders out of the Pine Barrens into Acee's streets. Its blood is the raw stuff of Devil's Blood.
- **Flight:** Ranged attacks against a Lesser Devil in flight are Impaired.
