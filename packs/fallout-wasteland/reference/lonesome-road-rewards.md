- **Old Glory** (unique melee flagpole weapon, d10, _bulky_, Enhanced against targets wearing faction colors).
- **Anti-Materiel Rifle** (d12, _bulky_, brace required, .50 BMG — Ulysses' own gun).
- **The Courier's Duster** (relic armor, 1 Armor, re-roll one failed STR, DEX, or WIL save, once per session, earned by finishing the campaign).
- **The Red Glare** (unique rocket launcher, d12, _blast_, _bulky_ — see Unique Weapons).
- **Blood-Nap**, **Fist of the North Rawr**, **Elite Riot Gear**, **Ulysses' Mask**, and the **Scorched Sierra Power Armor** — see Unique Weapons and Unique Armors.
- **A Light Shining in Darkness** (returns as a prize if Graham was honored in Honest Hearts).
- **ED-E Upgrade** — the eyebot becomes a permanent companion with +1 DEX and the ability to carry 2 slots.
- **The Launch Codes.** A holotape relic. Whoever holds it can *attempt* one missile strike — but the silo needs a working launch sequence (a separate session-length problem), the codes degrade after use, and the target's Faction Reputation falls to **Vilified**, one step at a time as word spreads, permanently. The codes attract every intelligence agency in the wasteland. Holding them is a burden, not a gift.
