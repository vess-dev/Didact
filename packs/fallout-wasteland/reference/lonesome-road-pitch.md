An eyebot — **ED-E** — arrives at the party's feet with a looping signal from the Divide. The voice is **Ulysses**, a former frumentarius turned courier, calling one of the PCs out by name. He blames them for the Divide's destruction — a load the PC dropped there years ago triggered a chain reaction of dormant nukes. Ulysses now holds the codes for two *more* missiles, aimed at the NCR and Caesar's Legion. He wants the party to come to him. He wants them to choose what the missiles hit. He wants the called PC to *understand*.
