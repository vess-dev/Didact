- **Science Clock** (d6). The Think Tank's attention span. Ticks when the party refuses an experimental offer, disagrees with a Think Tank member in audience, or returns to the Sink without a new specimen. At 6, they try to "upgrade" the party without consent.
- **Mobius Clock** (d4). Mobius's opposition. Ticks when the party clears a lab, recovers a pre-war technology the Think Tank will want, or is broadcast-at from the Forbidden Zone. Each tick, a new roboscorpion patrol is loosed into a zone of the Overseer's pick. At 4, the patrols converge on the Sink itself.
