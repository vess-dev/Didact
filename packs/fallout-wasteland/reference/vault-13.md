*Vaults of Fallout 1 & 2*

**Premise.** The most famous vault in the West: a control vault meant to stay sealed for two centuries, until its water chip failed in 2161 and the Overseer sent a single volunteer out to find another. The Vault Dweller brought back a chip from Vault 12, killed the Master, and was exiled at the blast door for his trouble. The dwellers who were disgusted by that left to follow him north and founded Arroyo; the ones who stayed tried the Overseer, sentenced him to death, and abolished his office. In 2241, the Enclave broadcast a false all-clear, the door opened, and vertibird assault squads shot three dwellers on the threshold and carried the rest off for their experiments. They left behind a pack of intelligent deathclaws, who made the vault a home — until Frank Horrigan's squad came back and killed them all. Arroyo's descendants still call it the Vault of the Holy 13.

**Layout.**
- **Blast Door.** Open since 2241. Three skeletons in blue-and-gold jumpsuits lie where the Enclave shot them, the day the door finally opened.
- **Greeting Hall.** A projector still loops the pre-war *Leaving the Vault* training film the vault's spokesman played for the dwellers that morning, to an audience of dust.
- **Residential Block.** Deathclaw nests built out of vault mattresses. On the walls, children's crayon drawings of deathclaws and people holding hands — the refugees who lived here with the pack.
- **The Mainframe.** A computer the Brotherhood installed to run the vault after its last Overseer was executed. It answered to voice commands, and it still waits to hear the pack leader's. It will talk to anyone who fixes its damaged boards.
- **Overseer's Office.** Stripped. The last Overseer's trial transcript is still in the desk.
- **Water Treatment.** The chip the Vault Dweller carried home from Vault 12 still sits in the purifier, long dead.
- **The Kill Room.** The atrium where Horrigan's squad trapped the pack. Plasma scoring on every wall, and the great bones of the pack leader, Gruthar.

**Inhabitants.** A DEATHCLAW, YOUNG nest in the residential block — ordinary animals, drawn by the smell of their own kind. A **Brotherhood scribe** team cataloguing the mainframe, who would prefer no witnesses. **Arroyo pilgrims** at the door, some years, come to see where their tribe began.

**Reward.** The **Vault 13 Archive** (Relic, _petty_) — a holotape copy of the mainframe's records: the Vault Dweller's reports, the pack's lessons in human speech, the Enclave's all-clear code. Worth 800 caps to the Followers of the Apocalypse or the Brotherhood, and the reason both want the party dead once they learn someone else has it. A **Vault 13 jumpsuit**, which every NCR citizen recognizes.

**Hidden.** Behind the mainframe, a deathclaw-sized reading nook: stacks of books whose margins are annotated in careful scratches of claw. It was the library of **Goris**, the pack leader's scholarly son, who was out of the vault the day Horrigan came. Someone has been back here recently, and put the books in order.

**Hook.** An NCR historian hires the party to recover the Vault Dweller's original report from the mainframe before the Brotherhood's scribes wipe it. On the road, a traveler swears a deathclaw asked him, very politely, for directions.
