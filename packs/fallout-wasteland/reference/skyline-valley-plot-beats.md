Broad strokes. Five to seven sessions. The first half is an expedition into a storm; the second half is a family's secrets coming up out of the ground.

1. **An Unlikely Invitation.** The door, Alyssa, and the Ash Heap entrance to Vault 63 — not the vault itself but a transit hub for an underground railway that runs south beneath the mountains. Inside, the Lost come in waves. Over the intercom, a calm ghoul voice introduces herself: **Hilda Stolz**, head of the organics sector. She asks the party to come to Skyline Valley.
2. **Into Lands Unknown.** Up Skyline Drive into the Storm. At the park entrance, a survivor named **Craig** needs personal effects gathered and signal flares set at three points in the storm. The valley is gorgeous and wrong — lightning everywhere, Lost on the overlooks.
3. **The Calm Before.** **Dark Hollow Manor**, the Stolz family's pre-war home, stands on a hill wreathed in fissures and toxic purple pools, the Storm strongest overhead. The vault's entrance is beneath it, behind a hand scanner that only a vault dweller's hand will open — any vault dweller's, dead or Lost. Inside the atrium the party meets the survivors, and the overseer himself: Hugo Stolz, gracious host.
4. **Housekeeping.** The atrium needs everything: **Oberlin** wants the Lost cleared from the lower levels and the storerooms emptied of radrats; Hugo wants his wife's journal found, the key to his wall safe recovered, and the weather machine's blueprints brought home. Along the way, the party learns what the vault was built on: the **Dark Hollow Falls** families, talked off their land with a handshake promise of places in the vault, and met by Hugo's guards at the park's edge when the bombs came.
5. **The Powerhouse of the Cell.** Hilda has a serum, **Serum Omega**, and three Lost test subjects. The last is her own father, **August**, one of the first dwellers to turn. The party brews the serum in the lab, doses the subjects, and learns what it can and cannot give back.
6. **The Oldest Trick in the Book.** Audrey needs the Shenandoah Visitor Center's weather-monitoring gear. Then Hugo bursts in: **Cassidy** has been kidnapped by Mothman cultists. In his panic, Hugo lets slip that the cult's leader is someone he once paid an assassin to kill, and Audrey walks out on him. The party infiltrates **Old Crimora Mines** in cultist robes and finds a congregation of fakes who do not worship the Mothman at all. They want revenge on Hugo, and they follow **Alex Guerra**, Audrey's pre-war love, the communist the assassin never reached.
7. **Double-Crossed Wires.** Oberlin is still loyal to the U.S. Army, and he still files reports to his superiors. His last courier, security officer **Daniel Smith**, never came back. The party finds Daniel dead in a cage beneath High Knob Lookout, now a Blood Eagle camp, with Oberlin's report and his own holotape: every report Oberlin ever sent went straight to Hugo. There is no Army. Hugo admits it pleasantly, and suggests the party keep it to themselves.
8. **Gathering Clouds.** Audrey and Hilda are one experiment from understanding why Hugo kept his mind when the others became Lost. It takes a sample of his tissue and three lightning harvesters at **Research Sites Saxony**, **Bavaria**, and **Rhineland**, each activated and held against super mutant raiders while it drinks the storm. The answer comes back, and Hugo is already gone — to the **Hawksbill weather station** on the valley's highest peak, with Cassidy, to finish what he started. See **Climax**.
