- **Elder Lyons** — the Brotherhood's conscience. Wants the Enclave broken, not annihilated. Exhausted by Prime's loss.
- **Sarah Lyons** — Sentinel, wounded in the relay station assault but still leading. Will lead the frontal assault on Adams.
- **Scribe Rothchild** — mission handler. Knows the metro. His assistant, **Scribe Vallincourt**, designs the Tesla Cannon.
- **M.A.R.Go.T.** — the metro's pre-war transit computer; remembers when the line ran a president's train. Useful, polite, delusional.
