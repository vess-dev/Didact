### Liberty Prime

60 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 8 DEX, 10 WIL, twin-laser (d12, _blast_), stomp (d10, _blast_)

- A pre-war combat robot forty feet tall. "**Democracy is non-negotiable.**" Allied asset, not a combat encounter for the party — a moving battlefield advantage. All enemies within thirty feet of Prime take _blast_ damage (d12) from his cover fire at the start of each combat round.
- **Siege Engine:** Prime's attacks ignore up to 2 Armor. He is not intended as a survivable target — the Enclave's orbital countermeasure eventually downs him, but not until **Broken Steel** opens.
