The Outcasts (Protector McGraw) broadcast on an open channel: pre-war weapons, armor, and Pulse Grenades to anyone who can beat the General Chase simulation. No ghouls, no mutants, no cyborgs — the pods scan and reject. A cover story is fine: say you're human, sign the waiver, step in.
