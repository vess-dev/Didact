- **Father Elijah** — Brotherhood elder, obsessed with Old World tech. Sees the Sierra Madre as the weapon that cracks the Mojave open.
- **Dean Domino** — pre-war lounge ghoul. Thinks the heist is still *his*. Will betray anyone.
- **Dog / God** — a nightkin with dissociative identity. Dog is gentle, hungry, loyal. God is cold and sharp.
- **Christine** — mute Brotherhood survivor. Communicates by gesture — the machines that took her voice scrambled her letters too. Christine hates Elijah more than she hates the fog.
- **Vera Keyes (voice)** — dead two centuries. Her recordings still run the casino's speakers.
