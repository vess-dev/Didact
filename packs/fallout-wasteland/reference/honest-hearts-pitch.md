The **Happy Trails Caravan** hires the party to escort a shipment to the New Canaan mission. New Canaan is already burning by the time they arrive — destroyed by the **White Legs**, a raider tribe courting Caesar's favor, under **Salt-Upon-Wounds**, their war chief. Only **Daniel**, a New Canaanite missionary, and **Joshua Graham** — the **Burned Man**, former Legion Legate, wrapped in bandages and righteous fury — made it to Zion. They are fighting different wars in the same canyon.
