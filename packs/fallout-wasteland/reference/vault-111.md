*Vaults of Fallout 4*

**Premise.** A cryogenic suspension experiment. Residents lured from Sanctuary Hills and Concord were told the pods were for decontamination, then frozen without their knowledge or consent. The staff were hired short-term. When the mandatory 180 days passed with no all-clear, the Overseer refused to open the door, and the security and support staff mutinied; the logs end there. The pods' life support failed one by one. In 2227, the Institute found the vault, opened a single pod, shot one parent, and took an infant named Shaun. In 2287 the last working pod thawed, and its occupant walked out alone.

**Layout.**
- **The Elevator.** The vault's front door: a great round platform on the hill above Sanctuary Hills. It still rises and falls on command. The only other way in is the mole rats' — their tunnels behind the reactor surface somewhere on the hillside.
- **Entry Hall.** Racks of blue jumpsuits with 111 on the back, handed out that last morning. A sign cheerfully explains decontamination. By the vault door, a scientist's skeleton still wears a Pip-Boy — the one piece of Vault-Tec kit anyone ever carried out.
- **The Pod Chamber.** Rows of frosted cryo pods, every occupant dead in place when the life support failed. One pod's glass is starred by a single bullet hole. One pod is open and empty.
- **Staff Quarters & Cafeteria.** Skeletons in lab coats and security armor, where the mutiny ended. The last meals are still on the trays.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's skeleton and a terminal whose logs stop mid-lockdown. A locked display case holds a pre-war **Cryolator**.
- **Cryo Control.** The terminal that ran the pods, and the reactor room that still powers them.

**Inhabitants.** RADROACHES in every room and a MOLE RAT DETACHMENT in the tunnels behind the reactor; their MOLE RAT BROOD MOTHER has denned in the staff quarters. Nothing else lives here. The vault's only voices are its terminals.

**Reward.** A **Pip-Boy 3000** from the skeleton by the door — unless the last survivor already took it. The **Cryolator** from the Overseer's case (see Marketplace). A working **Cryo Storage Cylinder** (Relic). A **Photograph** of a pre-war family taken on the day they entered the vault.

**Hidden.** Cryo Control's automated log recorded everything the staff never lived to see: the door cycling open in 2227, a pod's seal broken, a single infant's vital signs leaving the building — and, sixty years later, the last pod reporting *occupant revived*. Anyone who reads it knows exactly how old Shaun would be now.

**Hook.** A Diamond City journalist wants the staff's last logs for a story on what Vault-Tec did to Sanctuary Hills. The elevator still works. Something has been sleeping in the staff quarters since the last person left.
