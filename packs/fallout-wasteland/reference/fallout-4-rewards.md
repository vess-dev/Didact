- **Deliverer** — a silenced 10mm pistol (d8). See Unique Weapons. Recovered from the Railroad's old Switchboard HQ if allied.
- **Righteous Authority** — a laser rifle (d10, _bulky_). See Unique Weapons. Danse's gift on the Brotherhood path.
- **Pickman's Blade** — a hand-ground combat knife. See Unique Weapons.
- **Shishkebab** — a lawnmower-blade flaming sword. See Unique Weapons.
- **Kellogg's Cybernetic Brain Augmenter** (relic) — cut from Kellogg's skull. See Relics.
- **Power Armor Frame** — T-45 from the Concord museum, upgradeable to T-51, T-60, or X-01 given the right scavving.
- **Codsworth's Service Ring** (relic, _petty_; once per session, give a single polite command to any pre-war domestic robot capable of obeying).
- **Institute Directorship** (only on the Institute ending — see Climax).
- **Faction:** up to Idolized (+2) with whichever faction the party sided with; down to Vilified (−2) with the opposing faction — one step at a time.
