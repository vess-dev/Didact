*Vaults of Fallout: New Vegas*

**Premise.** A control vault. A water leak forced the residents to open the door to find help, and for a short while they traded peacefully with local caravans — until a chem-addled raider gang conned their way in, slaughtered the dwellers, and claimed the vault for themselves.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** The blast door stands open. Fresh bullet holes in the walls.
- **Residential Block.** The original fixtures are still here, but every room has been converted into a drug den, a weapons stash, or a squalid bunkroom.
- **Water Treatment.** Still functional. The raiders charge passing caravans a hundred caps a gallon.
- **Overseer's Office.** Now a raider chief's den. Four bodies hung from the ceiling as warnings.
- **Reactor Room.** Unchanged. The raiders don't know what it is, only that the lights still work.

**Inhabitants.** FIEND RAIDER CREW (CHEM FIENDS and THUGS) with a chem-addled CREW BOSS — **Motor-Runner** himself, if he still lives — and a CHAINED CAPTIVE somewhere in the back. An NCR Ranger sent in alone to kill Motor-Runner is holed up near the residential block with a bullet in his leg.

**Reward.** The **Vault 3 Water Valve** — controlling it gives the party leverage over every caravan on the route. A stash of **Psycho**, **Jet**, and **Mentats** in the raider chief's footlocker.

**Hidden.** A PC who searches the water treatment's filter housing finds a **pre-war wedding ring** wedged deep in the intake grate. The inscription matches one of the original residents' names on the Overseer's terminal. The captive chained in the back room is their descendant and does not know.

**Hook.** A caravan master will pay 500 caps to anyone who frees the water supply from the raiders' control, *without* killing the chained captive they use as a hostage.
