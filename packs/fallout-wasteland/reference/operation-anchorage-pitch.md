The Brotherhood Outcasts have uncovered an intact **VSS pod network** — a military virtual simulation rig. Inside is *Operation: Anchorage*, a propaganda reconstruction of the liberation of Alaska. The Outcasts can't crack the loadout cache locked behind the sim's victory condition. They'll open the armory for anyone who plays it through.
