### Mechanical Gunslinger

10 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 15 DEX, 10 WIL, western revolver (d10), spin-kick (d6)

- Pre-war Dry Rock Gulch lawman protectron — **One-Eyed Ike** to the locals. Challenges strangers to a duel.
- **High Noon**: In the first round only, its revolver shot is Enhanced. The targeted PC makes a DEX save; on a success they act before the shot, on a failure the Gunslinger fires first.
