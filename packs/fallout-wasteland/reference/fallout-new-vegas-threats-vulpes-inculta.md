### Vulpes Inculta

8 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 16 DEX, 13 WIL, machete gladius (d8), hunting revolver (d10, _bulky_)

- Caesar's spymaster. Not a straight fight — he retreats when bloodied, to arrange a worse encounter later.
- **Frumentarius:** Vulpes is always somewhere the party is not. Every scene the party meets him face-to-face and does *not* kill him, the Legion Frumentarii Clock ticks one.
