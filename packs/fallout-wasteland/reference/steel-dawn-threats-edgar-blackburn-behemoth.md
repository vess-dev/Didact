### Edgar Blackburn, Behemoth

16 HP, 2 Armor, 18 STR, 9 DEX, 10 WIL, test-chamber club (d12, _bulky_), acid glob (d8, _blast_, ranged)

- The scientist, transformed by his own virus in the middle of the experiment. Still arguing with the mutant in his head.
- **Lucid Moments:** Once per combat, Blackburn's mind surfaces and he begs to be finished. He loses his next action.
- **Critical Damage:** The target is doused in acid; their armor is destroyed.
