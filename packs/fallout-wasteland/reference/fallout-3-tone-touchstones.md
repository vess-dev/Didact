*A vault kid's first month above ground. Earnest, a little naive, and the Wasteland keeps showing you how small you are. The Capital Wasteland is ruins and radio — bombed-out suburbs, dead monuments, a Washington Monument visible from twenty miles off. Every ruin contains a skeleton who lived before the world ended and an empty bottle of Nuka-Cola beside it.*

**Touchstones:** *Fallout 3* itself, *The Road* (a father and child through a poisoned country), *A Boy and His Dog*, *Beneath the Planet of the Apes* (the vault-dweller coming up to the ruined Capitol), *Mad Max Beyond Thunderdome*, old Cold War civil-defense films, Arthur C. Clarke's *The Nine Billion Names of God* (pre-war science that doesn't know the world has ended).

**Lean into:** Galaxy News Radio's broadcasts — "Fight the good fight, children, and don't forget to drink your Nuka-Cola!" Rusted traffic signals swinging in the wind, ghouls in business suits, super mutants bellowing from the Mall. The Washington Monument still standing, missing half its capstone. Everything smells like iron and dust.
