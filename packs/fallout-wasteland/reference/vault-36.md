*Vaults of Fallout: New Vegas*

**Premise.** The residents were given food processors rigged to produce thin, flavorless gruel with barely enough nutrition. The experiment was to measure how long a community would tolerate a diet with no joy. Vault-Tec's records never gave its location; this book puts it in the Mojave.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** The blast door stands ajar. A small wooden sign in the greeting hall reads, in crude letters, *NO MORE GRUEL*.
- **Cafeteria.** The food processors are still running, still producing their beige slurry. A pre-war cookbook, untouched, sits next to them.
- **Residential Block.** Empty. The residents left years ago.
- **Overseer's Office.** A holotape — the Overseer's final entry — describes the day her community finally walked out, together, without a word.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine. The reactor is in perfect shape.

**Inhabitants.** A lone MISTER HANDY (treat as friendly unless provoked) still running the cafeteria kitchen.

**Reward.** **The Pre-war Cookbook** (Relic, _petty_) — a PC at a Cooking Station who consults it may produce a day's rations that let the eaters automatically pass the next WIL save against fear or despair. **The Food Processors** can be stripped for a Cooking Station building at a settlement at half the usual cost.

**Hidden.** One of the cafeteria tables has a **resident's journal** welded to the underside with a salvaged magnet — a secret diary of every meal the writer *imagined* eating. Four hundred entries. The final one is a full Thanksgiving dinner the writer describes down to the gravy. The Mr. Handy knows the writer's name.

**Hook.** A settler has heard rumors of a vault full of working kitchen equipment. Her community is desperate for a functional cooker. She has 300 caps and a good bottle of whiskey.
