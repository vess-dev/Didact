*Classified Vault Experiments*

**Premise.** Located in Salt Lake City, Utah, Vault 70 was populated largely by Mormon congregations. The experiment: all jumpsuit extruders were rigged to fail after six months, leaving the entire population without clothing. The vault opened in 2190; its residents used three G.E.C.K.s to found the city of **New Jerusalem** in the Salt Lake City ruins. After New Jerusalem was sacked, the survivors went north to Ogden and founded **New Canaan**.

**Layout.**
- **Entry Hall.** A wall of empty suit dispensers. The extruders are visibly broken — panels torn off, motors burnt out. Someone tried to fix them two hundred years ago and gave up.
- **Residential Block.** Intact. The residents adapted — leather, scrap cloth, hand-sewn blankets. A micro-culture of improvised clothing grew from nothing.
- **The Tailoring Room.** A pre-war fabrication bay the residents converted into a hand-sewing workshop after the extruders died. The bench is still functional.
- **Overseer's Office.** The Overseer's final holotape describes the community's decision to open the door and build something on the surface.
- **Reactor Room.** Fine. Three empty G.E.C.K. bays — all used.

**Inhabitants.** The vault is empty. Its descendants lived on the surface in New Canaan and the settlements it spawned — until the White Legs burned New Canaan in 2281 and the survivors scattered to Zion and beyond. A lone Mr. Handy still runs the tailoring room.

**Reward.** **A Pristine Vault 70 Jumpsuit** — one of the last vacuum-sealed originals, never worn (_petty_, 200 caps to a collector). **The G.E.C.K. Bay Schematic** — a technical drawing worth 500 caps to a Brotherhood scribe.

**Hidden.** One of the empty G.E.C.K. bays has a false floor. Beneath it: a **sealed Vault-Tec memo** documenting a bureaucratic G.E.C.K. mix-up. Most vaults were issued two kits; a shipping clerk's error sent Vault 70 a third, and the same clerk's paperwork shows Vault 13 getting a third kit in place of its spare water chips — which went to Vault 8 instead. The memo was never sent. An Overseer who wants to tie Vault 70 to Vault 13 should follow the thread.

**Hook.** A New Canaanite missionary has been asking about the old vault. She wants to recover a family Bible her great-grandmother left behind when the vault opened. She has faith and 400 caps.
