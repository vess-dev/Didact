- **Gold Fever Clock** (d8). How far the rumor spreads. Ticks when the party talks about Vault 79 in the Wayward, travels with a treasure hunter, lets a Free Radical or Blood Eagle escape a fight, or approaches the vault's cave openly. At 8, a Blood Eagle war band reaches the vault the same day the party does.
- **Rivalry Clock** (d6). Foundation and the Crater edging toward war. Ticks when the party works openly for one faction in the other's territory, when either side's caravan is hit, or when Hijack or Lev acts. At 6, the feud goes hot: Crater raiders burn a Foundation outpost, the Settlers answer in kind, and the faction the party has not chosen treats them as enemies.
- **Lockdown Clock** (d4, hidden; ticks only inside Vault 79). Ticks when a laser grid is tripped, a power line fluctuates, or a turret alarm sounds. At 4, the vault seals every bulkhead and the Grim Reaper's Hallway goes live behind the party.
