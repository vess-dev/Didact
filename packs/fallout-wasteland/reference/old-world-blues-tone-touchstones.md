*Absurdist retrofuturist horror played for laughs until it isn't. The Think Tank is a sitcom cast of pre-war scientists who have forgotten that they are floating brains in jars; the Overseer should play them like a comedy ensemble that keeps tripping over the body-horror truth of their situation.*

**Touchstones:** *Old World Blues* DLC itself (the comedic voice acting is the model), *Rocky Horror*, *Futurama*, *The Fly*, *Dr. Strangelove*, 1950s mad-scientist serials, *Archer* in the quips.

**Lean into:** banter. Every scene at the Sink is a comedy routine between appliances. Every scene at the Think Tank is five competing PhDs interrupting each other. Let combat be brief and weird — plasma turrets, lobotomites, a giant roboscorpion. The horror creeps in when the party remembers *why* they're there.
