### Dagger

9 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 15 DEX, 13 WIL, Brotherhood laser rifle (d10, _bulky_), combat knife (d6)

- A Blood Eagle chieftain who refused to kneel to Meg Groberg, avoided Foundation, and took her gang into the Mire to squeeze The Retreat. Her raiders are armed with stolen Brotherhood weapons.
- **Nothing to Lose:** Dagger never checks Morale. Kill her raiders and more will come; she tells the party so.
