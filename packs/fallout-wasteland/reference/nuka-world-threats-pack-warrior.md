### Pack Warrior

8 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 9 WIL, deathclaw gauntlet (d10, _bulky_), bite-chain (d6)

- Face-painted raider of the Pack. Dressed in hides and teeth. Howls during combat.
- **Ferocious**: Enhanced on any turn the Pack Warrior took damage the previous round.
