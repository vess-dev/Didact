*Vaults of Fallout 3*

**Premise.** A control vault that was also, secretly, a permanent sealing experiment: the Overseer was instructed to never allow residents to leave. The door was meant to stay shut forever.

**Layout.**
- **Blast Door.** Fully sealed. Requires a Pip-Boy override, an inside ally, or a long siege to open.
- **Greeting Hall.** Intact. A pre-war welcome recording plays every morning at 0600.
- **Residential Block.** A living community of about a hundred people. They believe the outside world is radioactive ash and that their Overseer is their shepherd.
- **The Garden.** A secret hydroponics bay the Overseer keeps locked. Growing here: clean pre-war apple trees from seeds sealed in 2077 — fruit nobody outside has tasted in two hundred years.
- **Overseer's Office.** The current Overseer is a direct descendant of the original, still receiving sealed messages from a dead pre-war chain of command. They are not an evil person. They believe every order they follow.

**Inhabitants.** VAULT COMMUNITY with a small security detail (treat as SETTLER MILITIAMEN with police batons, d6, and 10mm pistols, d8). A single SECURITY OFFICER (treat as SETTLEMENT SHERIFF) with a baton at the door.

**Reward.** A packet of the garden's seeds (Relic, _petty_ — see **Pre-war Mutfruit Seeds** in Relics). One functional **Pip-Boy 3000**.

**Hidden.** In the Overseer's private quarters, behind a false panel in the wardrobe, is a small stack of **outside-world letters** the original Overseer received from Vault-Tec *after* the war. The dates go up to 2079. Somebody was still writing to Vault 101 for two years after the bombs fell. The return address is redacted on all of them.

**Hook.** The Overseer of Vault 101 has sent a messenger to the nearest settlement with a sealed letter and a bag of caps. The letter offers a contract: retrieve a stolen data module taken by a rogue resident who fled into the Wastes. The Overseer does not mention that the "rogue resident" is their own child, or that the data module contains evidence of the vault's true purpose.
