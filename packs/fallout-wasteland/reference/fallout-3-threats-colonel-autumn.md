### Colonel Autumn

10 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 15 DEX, 14 WIL, **Colonel Autumn's 10mm Pistol** (d8), combat knife (d6)

- Enclave field commander. Confident, principled in his own way, does not bluster. See **Unique Weapons → Colonel Autumn's 10mm Pistol**.
- **Officer's Calm**: Morale saves for Enclave troops in his line of sight auto-pass, until he falls.
