**Pennington** reports "non-Vault types" in the courtyard below Vault 76. Two travelers, **Lacey Drummond** and **Isela Mejia**, have come up the hill on a rumor from a new tavern called the Wayward. The party walks down to see it and finds the owner, **Duchess**, held at gunpoint by a Free Radical named **Batter**, who wants to know where a treasure hunter called **Crane** went. Her regular, a ghoul named **Mort**, settles the question with a 10mm pistol. Duchess needs help keeping her bar alive, and she is willing to pay.
