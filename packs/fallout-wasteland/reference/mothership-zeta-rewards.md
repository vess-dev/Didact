- **Captain's Sidearm** (unique alien blaster variant, d10, ignores Armor — see Unique Weapons). The Captain's body also carries a box of alien power cells (3 uses).
- **Atomic Pulverizer** and **Drone Cannon Ex-B** (unique Zetan weapons — see Unique Weapons).
- **Destabilizer** (unique alien disintegrator, d10, _bulky_, alien power cell — the target's body disintegrates on Critical Damage, no salvage).
- **Samurai's Sword** (unique melee, d8, Enhanced against unarmored targets) and **Samurai Armor** — Toshiro Kago's own, if he falls or gifts them. See Unique Weapons and Unique Armors.
- **Alien Captain's Key** (relic) — unlocks pre-war military terminals once per session.
- **The Zeta Saucer** (unique vehicle) — one orbital hop per campaign. Re-enters anywhere the party names. Take 1 Rad on use.
- **Abductees as companions.** Any of the named NPCs may leave with the party as a companion with a campaign-specific trick.
