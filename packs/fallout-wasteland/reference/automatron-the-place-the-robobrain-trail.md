A three-step trail of robot nests, then the lair.

- **The Caravan Wreck.** Drop-in. A head-swiveling **Robobrain Scout** is still watching the bodies. Interrogating it is a WIL save; a Tinker, or anyone with the *Robotics Expert* perk, needs no save.
- **Fort Hagen Satellite Array.** The pre-war military relay station the Mechanist uses to broadcast commands. Sentry Bots, Rust Devils, and **Jezebel** (a Robobrain head on a shelf) are here.
- **The RobCo Sales & Service Center.** A derelict pre-war robotics showroom hiding a massive underground complex. Ancient protectrons, a **Mr. Handy** receptionist who still greets visitors cheerfully.
- **The Mechanist's Lair.** Beneath the RobCo building. Workshop, barracks, a control room. **The Mechanist** waits in a chair above a pit full of failed prototypes.
