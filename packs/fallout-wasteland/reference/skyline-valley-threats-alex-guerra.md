### Alex Guerra

7 HP, 13 STR, 13 DEX, 15 WIL, revolver (d8), ritual knife (d6)

- A ghoul, a communist, and the leader of a cult that only pretends to worship the Mothman. Kidnapped Cassidy so that Hugo would know what losing someone feels like.
- **Congregation:** While she lives, the cultists at Old Crimora Mines fight to the death for her. If the party talks her down, most of them simply go home.
