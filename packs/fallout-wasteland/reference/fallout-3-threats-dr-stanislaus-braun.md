### Dr. Stanislaus Braun (Vault 112, optional side arc)

— HP, — combat. An old man in a Tranquility Lounger — unremovable without killing him.

- A pre-war monster who has been god of a simulated Tranquility Lane for 200 years. Running his simulation "correctly" to earn his aid — or murdering his neighbors to force him out — is a side arc that nets the party *Virtual Reality Goggles* and one of the strongest karma swings in the campaign.
