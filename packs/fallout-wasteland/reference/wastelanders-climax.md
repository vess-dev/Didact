**Division of Wealth.** The vault is open, the bullion is stacked on the processing-room floor, and Paige or Meg is walking in to collect. Three resolutions:

- **Keep the Deal.** The allied faction gets its promised half. It becomes the power in Appalachia, and the other side does not forget. If the party rode with the Crater and Johnny Weston still lives, he makes a play for all of it; reminding him of the Watoga arena, or of what Hal would say, can talk him down.
- **Split It.** Half to Foundation, half to the Crater. Neither faction is satisfied, and both have something to lose. For the first time, the Settlers and Raiders share an interest in Appalachia's peace — and in the Overseer's currency working.
- **Keep It.** The party walks out with all of it. The Overseer's economy starts in their pockets. Their allies call them traitors, and every gold bar they spend is proof.

Whatever the party decides, the Secret Service agents settle into the vault's Gold Operations Center and open for business. Digger keeps the reactor running from its control room — if the party vouched for him. And the main vaults of Vault 79 are still sealed. Somewhere below the processing room is the rest of America's gold.
