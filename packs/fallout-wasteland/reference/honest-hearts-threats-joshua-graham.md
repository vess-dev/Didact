### Joshua Graham (optional, as hazard or ally)

14 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 16 DEX, 17 WIL, **A Light Shining in Darkness** (d8), combat knife (d6)

- The Burned Man. Bandaged, unmoving eyes, the best gun in Zion.
- **Deliberate**: Graham's first attack on any named target in a combat deals Enhanced damage.
- **Righteous**: Will not fight the party unless they attack him first or side openly with Salt-Upon-Wounds.
