*Neon and gore. A place that was built to sell sugar water to children is now run by three flavors of cruelty, and it still plays the jingles. The party should feel the tonal whiplash in every zone — a roller coaster followed by a slave pen followed by a vending machine that thanks them for the purchase.*

**Touchstones:** *Nuka-World* DLC itself, *Westworld* (the park that runs on people), *Zombieland's* amusement park sequences, *Mad Max Beyond Thunderdome*, *The Warriors* (three gangs carving up a territory), 1950s Disneyland recruitment films played upside down.

**Lean into:** bright primary colors over rust. Nick Nuka announces every scene in the voice of a cheerful dad. The three gangs each have a distinct texture — the Pack is loud and animal, the Disciples are quiet and surgical, the Operators are efficient and bored. Let combat be *loud* and messy, not tactical.
