Broad strokes. Four to six sessions. Each pod is a moral ratchet; the party thinks they can say no and mostly cannot.

1. **The Descent.** The party follows the signal into a cave mouth and down a pre-war construction shaft. The vault is an unfinished concrete cathedral — sconces hanging loose, blueprints fluttering on the walls, a half-built blast door waiting for someone to cut the final weld.
2. **Meeting Barstow.** Overseer Valery Barstow greets them from a monitor in her cheerful, polite voice. She explains the situation, thanks them for coming, and asks them to help her finish the vault. Her first favor sounds small: clear a nest of radscorpions from the Deep Caverns. She frames it as settler safety. It is.
3. **Clearing the Deep Caverns.** The excavation zones are Thermal Radscorpion country. Foreman Hunter and his ghoulified work crew live down there too. If the party approaches from the right angle, Hunter will talk first — he is friendly from behind and hostile from the front, which is a nice metaphor for the whole campaign.
4. **The First Experimental Pod.** Barstow hands the party a blueprint for one of the four pods — Phoropter, Slot Machine, Soda Fountain, or Power Cycle 1000. Each is a pre-war compliance-conditioning experiment with a deliverable relic at the end. Building the first pod is presented as structural work. The experiment is something they will have to reckon with later.
5. **Clem Arrives.** The first real volunteer settler. A simple, friendly man named Clem shows up at the Intake Bay, attracted by Barstow's broadcast. Barstow asks the party to "help orient him." What the party does with Clem is the campaign's first hard moral test — the Overseer should draw the scene out and let each player speak.
6. **The Second and Third Pods.** As pods come online, more settlers arrive, drawn by the radio. The Mutiny Clock starts ticking. Each subsequent pod ratchets the ethical weight. The fourth pod is the breaking point for any party that still has a conscience.
7. **Ezra Caldwell in the Intake Bay.** A hidden settler Barstow invited before the party arrived, sealed inside the Intake Bay. A day of food left. Finding him is a discovery; freeing him without Barstow's knowledge sets up the climactic confrontation.
8. **Confronting Barstow.** The party must decide what the vault becomes. Stop her, take over, run the experiments her way, or collapse the cave mouth and seal her in. See **Climax** below.
