**Making the map.** When the GM calls to **expand the map** — at the start, at the edge of the known, or whenever the story opens new ground — everyone at the table, GM included, takes **3 exploration tokens** and spends them going around: **settlements**, **sites**, **dangers**, **curiosities**, **areas**, and **barriers**, joined by **paths** — major roads, minor trails, rough scrambles — with a **waypoint** for each day of hard travel. Everything gets a two-word label (*Drowned Shrine, Giant's Pasture, Smugglers' Stair*). Heroes also earn 1–3 tokens for a real discovery, and can spend thread as a token when a wise or story arc explains how they knew. Town maps work the same at street scale; barrows and ruins become 5-point site maps (Chapter 9). The GM draws the first road in; nobody knows the whole province, and the map grows the way rumor does.

*Didact:* `didact do tokens who=<hero> take=3`; `didact gen run grimscrolls.region`.
