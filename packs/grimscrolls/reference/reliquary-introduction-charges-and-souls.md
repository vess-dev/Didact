**Charges and souls.** A relic's pool refreshes each session, unless its card says **soul-fed**: then the pool comes back only when a filled soul gem is spent on it. A common soul — a troll, a bear, an atronach — restores **2d**; a grand soul, from anything elite or greater, fills it. Soul-fed is the usual limit on the hungriest enchanted weapons, and it makes every filled gem a small decision. (Black soul gems hold the souls of people. They work exactly as well, and everyone knows what using one makes you.)
