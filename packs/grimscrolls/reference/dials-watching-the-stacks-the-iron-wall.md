- **The iron wall.** *Plate* takes the sting out of every messy defense, *Shield Wall* adds a die, and *Juggernaut* deepens the pool: a hero built this way is genuinely hard to hurt with steel. So don't use steel. Fear, fire, deep water, a long fall, a locked door, and anything that goes around armor still work — and the weight is a real cost: enforce it.
