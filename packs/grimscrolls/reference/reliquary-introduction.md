These are **arcana** — the singular things of Tamriel, rated by tier and sorted into three halls: **Relics & Wonders**, **Arms of Renown**, and **Armor, Raiment & Rings**, each broken down into **Minor**, **Major**, and **Mythic**. The name and description are always the touchstones; where a card lists no pool, its permission simply holds. Single-use finds are **expendable** — marked **×1** — and punch one tier above their weight.

*Didact:* A haul can be a relic of its tier, with its first pool: `didact gen run grimscrolls.treasure`; a new one by the book's recipe: `grimscrolls.arcana`.
