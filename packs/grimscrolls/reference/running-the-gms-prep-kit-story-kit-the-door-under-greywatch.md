*A first session for the Powderkeg. The seal under the Moot Hall is failing, the dead in the old barrow are restless, and the only claw that opens the door is in the wrong hands.*

- **The hook, three ways.** The Moot hires you to find out why the hall's cellar is freezing in high summer · the Legion's centurion wants to know what's down there before the Bear's Friends do · a Khajiit from the caravan sells you a golden claw and a bad feeling.
- **Pressure.** *6d The Seal Wears Thin* — roll it each midnight, and whenever anyone digs, prays, or bleeds in the cellar. *4d The Split-Tusks Come for the Toll* — roll it whenever the town looks distracted.
- **Faces.** **Speaker Hrefna Stone-Tally** wants the town to outlive the war; she counts on her fingers when she lies. **Centurion Varo Callidus** wants one quiet posting before he retires; he quotes the regulation he's about to break. **Justiciar Ancalime** wants the shrine of Talos she is sure is down there; she never raises her voice. **J'zarro,** the caravan's youngest, wants out of the sugar trade — and knows where the barrow's air shaft comes up.
- **The site.** *The Door:* **6d Open the Sealed Barrow** — trait: three rings and a claw-shaped lock; moves: Wrong Combination, Wake the Niches. *The Riddle:* the barrow was built to keep something *in*; every ward faces the wrong way for a tomb. *The Twist:* the townsfolk's grandparents knew, and have been feeding the seal ever since — with what, nobody will say. *The Reckoning:* a dragon priest's lieutenant (elite · blaster) and a *4d Honor Guard* that has stood watch since before the Empire. *The Hoard:* a word wall, a major relic — and a seal that needs feeding again by winter.
- **Fail state.** The seal hits 0d: the cold comes up through the floor, the barrow empties into the street, and the Legion declares the town lost.
- **The question.** What is a town allowed to do to stay safe?
