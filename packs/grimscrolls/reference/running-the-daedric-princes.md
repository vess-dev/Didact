Sooner or later a Prince takes an interest. A Prince is never a stat block. It is a **voice**, a **bargain**, and a campaign pool: when a hero accepts a Prince's task, start an *8d [Prince]'s Patience* pool and roll it whenever they stall, bargain, or disappoint. At 0d the Prince clears the matter up in person, and nobody enjoys it. Finish the task and the relic is yours (Chapter 8) — along with the Prince's attention, which doesn't end when the task does.

| Prince | Sphere | Wants from mortals | Pays in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azura** | dusk and dawn, prophecy | devotion, and her prophecies kept | Azura's Star |
| **Boethiah** | plots, treachery, the strong arm | a worthy betrayal, a champion who wins | Goldbrand, the Ebony Mail |
| **Clavicus Vile** | wishes and bargains | a deal you'll regret | the Masque |
| **Hermaeus Mora** | forbidden knowledge, fate | secrets nobody else has | the Oghma Infinium, the Black Books |
| **Hircine** | the hunt, were-beasts | a good chase — you needn't be the hunter | the Savior's Hide, his Ring |
| **Malacath** | the spurned, the sworn oath | grudges honored, weakness culled | Volendrung |
| **Mehrunes Dagon** | destruction, change, ambition | the world broken open | his Razor |
| **Mephala** | secrets, murder, the web | a friendship cut at the right moment | the Ebony Blade |
| **Meridia** | life's energy, hatred of undeath | her temples cleansed, loudly | Dawnbreaker |
| **Molag Bal** | domination, the first vampire | a will broken, a soul owned | his Mace |
| **Namira** | the ancient dark, all things revolting | a seat filled at her table | her Ring |
| **Nocturnal** | night and luck, thieves | what was stolen from her returned | the Skeleton Key, the Gray Cowl |
| **Peryite** | pestilence, the natural order | his plague tended like a garden | Spellbreaker |
| **Sanguine** | revelry and dark indulgence | one more round | the Sanguine Rose |
| **Sheogorath** | madness | to be amused; cheese | Wabbajack |
| **Vaermina** | dreams and nightmares | sleepers, and what they see | the Skull of Corruption |

*Didact:* `didact do prince-task who=<hero> prince=Azura` makes the 8d Patience pool.
