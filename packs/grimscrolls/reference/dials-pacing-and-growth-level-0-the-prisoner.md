- **Level 0: the prisoner.** Start in the cart, the cell, or the hold of a prison ship: a race, a birthsign, and a background, but no Core Talent, and 3 stat points instead of 4. The Core Talent and the last point arrive after the first session — once you've seen what your hero reaches for when it counts.
