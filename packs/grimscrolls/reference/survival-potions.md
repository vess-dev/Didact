Every hero carries a *3d Potions* resource pool: the red bottles, the healer's poultice, the flask a priest pressed on you at the gate. Given a breather — a moment out of reach to catch your breath — drink one or hand it to an ally: clear **one harm**, then roll the pool. It refills after a night somewhere safe or in downtime — or sooner, if you buy a restock (a Coin roll) or loot a stash. Mid-fight there's no breather: getting a potion into a friend under fire is a risky action roll. Want a meaner or kinder Tamriel? Turn the dial in Chapter 10.

*Didact:* `didact do potion who=<hero>` (`giver=<hero>` when an ally pours it; `free` with Mara's blessing).
