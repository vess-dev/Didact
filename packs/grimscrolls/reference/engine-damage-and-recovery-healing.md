**Healing.** Treatment takes time, tools, and know-how — a healer's hands, clean linen, a quiet room. It nearly always works; the drama is what it costs. Marks are too light to bother treating — they clear themselves. **Harm** clears with treatment or a potent spell, and conditions clear when the fiction says so (a shrine, a cure, time, or a task pool). Any real rest — a night's camp, a quiet week on the road — clears all marks and harm, and a night **somewhere safe** (an inn, a guild hall, a warded camp) also refills potions. In the field, **potions** are the shortcut: every hero carries a *3d Potions* pool that clears one harm with a breather (Chapter 6).

**Downtime** is a long break — a season in a city, a winter at the guild hall. Per-session talents refresh, and each hero picks a downtime action — only those who choose to **Rest** shake their lingering conditions (Chapter 6). Meanwhile the GM rolls the faction pools, then picks one and runs it dry. Tamriel moves while you sleep.
