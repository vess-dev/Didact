**The eight moves:**

- **Build Up** *(suspense move)* — a vignette before the storm; every PC adds a detail and takes spark, you take suspense. *The dragon's shadow crosses the valley and goes on. How do you spend the hour before it comes back?*

- **Clue In** *(suspense move)* — cut away to show the players what their characters can't see; take suspense. *We see the justiciar fold the letter, smile, and send for her soldiers.*

- **Foreshadow** *(suspense move)* — telegraph danger for free; if it goes unaddressed, it lands. *The flagstones here are a different color. Something in the wall niche has blue eyes.*

- **Resolve** *(suspense move)* — hand the party a way out and take suspense. *The guard squints, decides you must be the new recruits, and waves you through.*

- **Complicate Things** *(impact move)* — add or worsen a threat. *The horn starts a 4d Reinforcements pool. The oil on the floor finds the torch.*

- **Counter** *(impact move)* — stop a success or a permission cold; save it for exceptional foes (this is what beats an "always"), and never use it on a defense roll or a critical. A countered talent usually refunds its cost (your call), but it can't be used again right away. *The dragon priest's ward drinks your perfect spell.*

- **Hit Hard** *(impact move)* — marks, harm, vex, conditions. *The rune goes off — bloodied, and your ears ring.*

- **Lock Down** *(impact move)* — close a door forever; force a stark choice. *The portcullis drops. The ship sails. Him or the Scroll — pick.*
