**Suspense** is your currency of dramatic timing. Earn it by skipping a prompted impact move or by making suspense moves; spend it to strike at any moment. Impact moves aimed at a PC who didn't invite them grant a defense roll. Every challenge you frame brings **2 bonus suspense** of its own — a linked challenge brings it once, not per pool.

*Didact:* `didact do suspense take=1`, `didact do suspense-move move=build-up`; `didact table roll gm-crucible`.
