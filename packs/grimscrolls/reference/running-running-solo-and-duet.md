One hero and a GM (or none): make the party's missing muscle into the world's help. Give a lone PC a **follower** (build one with tricks and flaws, per Speechcraft's *Allure*), lean harder on montage rolls, and let story rolls answer what a second player would have. Playing truly GM-less, use the crucibles as your oracle: frame the scene, roll the GM crucible for what pushes back, roll story dice for everything uncertain, and spend suspense against yourself honestly — Tamriel doesn't pull punches just because nobody's holding them. Two guardrails keep it honest: on a messy, make an impact move only if you're in a challenge, in a quest's last task pool, or holding 3+ suspense (otherwise take suspense); on a disaster or at dire stakes, take the usual consequences and roll your quest's peril pool too (see Quests, below).
