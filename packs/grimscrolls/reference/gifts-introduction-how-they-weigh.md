**How they weigh.** These talents are deliberately lighter than the ones in Chapter 4 — a narrow edge and a gift you can call on once a session — because every hero carries two of them from the start. They sit outside your levels: you never spend a level on one, and you can't pick up a second race or a second sign later. Where one of them hands you a die, it's a talent die like any other, and doesn't stack with the rest.
