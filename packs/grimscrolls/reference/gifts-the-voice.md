*Optional. Ask your table before you take this one: it tends to become what the campaign is about.*

Shouting is not a school of magic. It is the dragons' own speech, in which saying a thing and doing it are the same act. The Greybeards spend lifetimes on a single word; a Dragonborn takes one from a dying dragon in a breath. Either way, this is an ordinary talent, taken with a level like those in Chapter 4.

- **The Voice.** *Optional.* You have learned to Shout — as one of the Dragonborn, or through years of silence on the Throat of the World. You know **1 Shout**: choose it from the Words below, or name another the table agrees on. Its name is its touchstone, and it does what a spell of that name would do: no roll when there's no risk, **Presence** when there is. You can Shout **once per scene**, and everyone for a mile hears it. Once per session, speak all three of its words: that Shout is **potent**. You learn a new Shout by finding its word wall and giving it a dragon's soul — or a season with a master.
