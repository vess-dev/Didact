**The curses.** Vampire and Werewolf are classes like the rest: if the curse is your Core Talent, it's what your story is about. Their seven talents **need the blood** — only the afflicted can take them. A hero of another class who is *turned in play* (Chapter 6) can take the curse as an ordinary talent — at their next level, or at once by trading away the last talent they gained — at pocket size, and that opens the curse's list to them:

- **Sanguinare Vampiris.** *Turned in play only.* You are a vampire. You have *The Blood* with all its rules and its price, but your Blood pool is **3d** and never grows.
- **Lycanthropy.** *Turned in play only.* You are a werewolf. You have *Beast Blood* with all its rules and its price, but your Bloodlust timer starts at **3d**, and you never gain more changes per session.

Cures exist for both. They are adventures, not purchases.

*Didact:* `didact do turn who=<hero> curse=vampire`; the curse's talent at the next level (`didact char advance`), or `didact do switch-talent`.
