**Consequence craft.** One impact move is flexible: split it into smaller cuts (a mark *and* the bow skitters away), spread it across several PCs (defense rolls for the bystanders), or land it **off-screen** — the ritual ticks forward, an innocent takes the blame, the caravan leaves without you. Pressure and timer pools are always a legal target; sometimes the scariest move is starting a new 4d pool and rolling it immediately. When a messy has no natural on-screen bite, bank suspense and hit later with better timing.
