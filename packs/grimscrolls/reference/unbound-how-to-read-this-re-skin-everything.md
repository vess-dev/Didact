- **Re-skin everything.** A toughness talent might be orcish plate, a Nord's thick blood, or pure stubbornness. A stealth talent might be an Illusion cantrip, a Khajiit's tread, or boots with the nails pulled. Pick the fiction that fits *your* hero and let the mechanics ride along.
