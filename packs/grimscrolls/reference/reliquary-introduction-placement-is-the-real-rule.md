**Placement is the real rule.** A relic is a story the party can hold — hand out one per session at most, give the strong ones a drawback or an old owner, and remember that a named relic is **one of a kind** (expendables are finds; a chest of them is a haul). When a named blade changes hands, the province hears about it within the week.
