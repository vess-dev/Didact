Build a barrow — or any delve — as a **5-point site**: the GM places the Hoard first, then everyone spends exploration tokens on the rest (Chapter 6):

- **The Door** *(Entrance)* — getting in is its own story: the claw, the collapsed stair, the thing nesting in the antechamber.
- **The Riddle** *(Puzzle)* — whoever built it wanted something kept in, or out; meet that as a problem that can't just be hit.
- **The Twist** *(Hitch)* — what the centuries did to the builders' plan. The rival expedition, the second seal, the tenant.
- **The Reckoning** *(Showdown)* — the fight or the bargain this place has been waiting to have.
- **The Hoard** *(Reward)* — the haul, the truth, and usually a hook to the next site.

> **RUIN CRUCIBLE.** *Roll a thorn (d8) for who built it and what holds it now — or choose.* **Built by:** *Nords, for their dead — the Dwemer, for their work — the Ayleids, for their stars — the Dunmer, for their ancestors — the Legion, and then abandoned — a Daedric cult — Akaviri invaders — nobody; it's a cave, and the cave was enough.* **Now it holds:** *a tomb that isn't quiet — a cult — a bandit clan with a problem of its own — a machine still running — a necromancer's school — something that only wants to be left alone — a rival expedition, two rooms ahead — exactly what the map promised, guarded.*
