Frame anything worth screen time as a challenge: a task pool (2d–8d), one or two **traits**, two or three **moves**, and — when failure has a shape — a **fail state**. *6d Cross the Pass Before the Storm — trait: white-blind — moves: Wall of Snow, The Cornice Goes — fail: 4d The Pilgrims Are Lost.* Tiers and roles for enemies are in Chapter 7.

*Didact:* `didact gen run grimscrolls.challenge`, `grimscrolls.linked-challenge`, `grimscrolls.battleground`; a Bestiary threat with its pools: `grimscrolls.foe`.
