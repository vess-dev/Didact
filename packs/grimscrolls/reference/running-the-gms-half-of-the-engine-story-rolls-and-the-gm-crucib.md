**Story rolls & the GM crucible.** Roll to disclaim any decision you'd rather the dice make — 1d bad odds, 2d even, 3d good. When you need the *seed* of an answer, roll a thorn (d8) for each column, take a word from each row you land on, and smash them together:

| d8 | First word | Second word |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | drowned · collapsing | shrine · shortcut |
| 2 | glowing · warded | debt · prophecy |
| 3 | buried · stolen | caravan · crown |
| 4 | patient · sworn | machine · sentinel |
| 5 | hungry · forgotten | bargain · barrow |
| 6 | Dwemer · desperate | grudge · trade |
| 7 | cursed · marked | well · door |
| 8 | smiling · singing | uniform · mask |

*What's in the ruin?* — patient + machine: something the Dwemer left running. *Why is the village silent?* — smiling + uniform: someone official arrived first.
