The things a campaign builds toward, and the names everyone already knows.

Kinds of thing, not individuals: challenges to hang the end of an arc on. Most are linked — beat the parts in the order the card allows, and mind the fail state.

The provinces keep stranger things than these chapters can hold. Statted light — a tier, a role, and the one fact that matters. Give any of them a full card the night they matter.

The people everyone has heard of, statted light — a tier, a role, and the one fact. Most are conversations that can become fights; a few are fights pretending to be conversations.

Drop these in whole, or strip them for parts — a trait, a move, a voice. They're as much objectives, factions, and arguments as they are fights, and the strangest of them are beaten with something other than steel.
