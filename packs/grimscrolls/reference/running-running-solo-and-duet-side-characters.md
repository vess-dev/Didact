**Side characters.** Want a whole company without the bookkeeping? Run two or three followers as side characters: no class and no Core Talent, just one talent; stats as a hero's; backgrounds, but no tracked wises; one trait they are and one they're not, one thing they want and one they don't; and a bond with your hero only. They don't earn XP, spark, or thread, and they never take marks — anything that would mark them, a push included, is harm instead.
