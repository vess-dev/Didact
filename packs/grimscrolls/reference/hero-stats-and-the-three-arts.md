Heroes have **four stats** — Brawn, Agility, Wits, and Presence — and they drive every roll and the whole damage track. The guild registers talk about three **arts** and twenty-one skills; those are what your class and talents are made of. They aren't a second stat line: each simply leans on the four stats in its own way.

| Art | Its classes | Mostly rolls |
|---|---|---|
| **Combat** | Armorer, Athletics, Blade, Block, Blunt, Hand to Hand, Heavy Armor | Brawn — Agility for the quicker blade, Wits at the forge |
| **Magic** | Alchemy, Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Illusion, Mysticism, Restoration | Wits for Alteration and Mysticism · Presence for Destruction and Illusion · their own pools for the rest |
| **Stealth** | Acrobatics, Light Armor, Marksman, Mercantile, Security, Sneak, Speechcraft | Agility — Presence for the talkers and traders, Wits for a hard lock |
| **The Curses** | Vampire, Werewolf | the Blood, and the beast's Brawn and Agility |
