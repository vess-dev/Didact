In Tamriel you are what you've practiced until it stopped being practice. The guilds count **twenty-one skills** — seven of Combat, seven of Magic, seven of Stealth — and the world adds two **curses** that work like a calling whether you wanted one or not. Your class is the one you've given your life to. Each is a **Core Talent** — an engine that grows as you level — with seven talents gathered around it.

The Core Talent is yours alone. **Every other talent in this chapter is open to every hero:** a Blade can learn *Quiet Casting*, and an Illusionist can learn to take a punch. The lists are neighborhoods, not walls.

**The Arts of Combat**

| Class | Core Talent | You're the one who… | First talent to try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Armorer** | *Tempering* — 5 Temper a session | made the gear, and says so at the perfect moment | *Steel Smithing* |
| **Athletics** | *Wind* — 6 Wind a session | never tires, never slows, and pushes for free | *Fleet-Footed* |
| **Blade** | *Bladework* — +1d, and 2 Edge a scene | wins the exchange, then improves on it | *Bladesman* |
| **Block** | *The Guard* — 3 Guard a scene | takes the hit meant for someone else | *Shield Wall* |
| **Blunt** | *The Heavy Hand* — 3 Might a session | ends fights by subtraction | *Sweep* |
| **Hand to Hand** | *The Open Hand* — 4 Focus a scene | needs nothing but hands and an opening | *Brawler* |
| **Heavy Armor** | *Plate* — a 4d pool a session | wades in where nobody else can stand | *Tower of Strength* |

**The Arts of Magic**

| Class | Core Talent | You're the one who… | First talent to try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alchemy** | *The Satchel* — 4 recipes, 5 draughts a session | has a bottle for this | *Physician* |
| **Alteration** | *Formulae* — 4 spells and 2 potent a session | argues with the rules of the world | *Mage Armor* |
| **Conjuration** | *The Binding* — a thrall, as a 4d pool | never fights alone | *Mystic Binding* |
| **Destruction** | *The Elements* — at will, never safe | is the fire, the frost, and the storm | *Impact* |

**The Curses**

| Class | Core Talent | You're the one who… | First talent to try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vampire** | *The Blood* — a 4d pool, refilled by feeding | outdoes the living, and pays for it at dawn | *Blood Healing* |
| **Werewolf** | *Beast Blood* — the change, once a session | has a monster to let out | *Totem of the Moon* |

Pick the class whose engine you want to run every session; pick talents for the hero you want to be. If you want one of the old guild-register professions — the *battlemage*, the *nightblade*, the *witchhunter* — Chapter 3's backgrounds list the seven skills each one trains: take your Core Talent from one of them and shop for talents among the rest.
