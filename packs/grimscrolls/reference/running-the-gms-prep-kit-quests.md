- **Quests.** Frame goals at the weight they deserve: an errand is a montage, a job is a session with a task pool, and a *quest* — end the plague, take the fort, close the gate — is a chain of linked task pools (4d + 4d + 6d for a daunting one) racing linked **peril** pools — one fewer than the quest has, 4d + 4d for a daunting one — that the whole table can see. Reward tiers follow Chapter 6's hauls.
