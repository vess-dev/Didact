Grimscrolls assumes the chronicles' own mix: high adventure on the surface, a dry sense of humor about itself, and something old and sad under the snow. Turn it grimmer or softer with the lethality dial in Chapter 10, or stranger with **Sheogorath's dial** in Chapter 9. Set it at session zero, like any flavor of the Third Era.
