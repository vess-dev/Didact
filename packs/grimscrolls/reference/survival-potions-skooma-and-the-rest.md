**Skooma and the rest.** Ride a drug through a **grim or disaster**, or lean on it session after session, and the GM may hand you a long-term condition — ***Hooked***, +1t when you're craving and dry. Clear it like any long-term condition: real treatment, a stretch of clean living, or a story beat that costs you something. In most holds skooma is contraband, and so is the moon sugar it's cooked from.
