- **Conditions** — specific maladies: **short-term** (*winded — clears after the scene*), **urgent** (a timer to worse: *4d Frostbite Venom → dropped*), **long-term** (*Rockjoint — until cured*), **permanent** (*lost eye*). +1t when one specifically hinders you; some close doors entirely.
