**Vigilance.** Nobody rolls to notice. When a hidden danger is in play, the GM spotlights the hero it matters to and, by their vantage, gives a **reveal** (they see it plainly), a **hint** (one shot at intuition), or a **strike** (it lands — the GM spends suspense, and you get a defense roll). More in Chapter 9.

**Potency** stretches vantage: a talent that grants a **potent feat** lets you attempt what's normally beyond mortal limits — group-effort results, single-handed wonders, one-in-a-million shots — and moves an action one or more steps toward certain. At its simplest, a potent action is a normal action with a critical bonus built in.
