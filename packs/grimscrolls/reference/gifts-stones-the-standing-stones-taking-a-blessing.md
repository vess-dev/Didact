**Taking a blessing.** Lay a hand on the stone and accept. You hold **one stone's blessing at a time**: take another, and the first fades. While you hold it, the blessing is a talent like any other, and nothing but another stone — or the story — takes it away. It need not match your birthsign; the stars are generous to people who walk that far.

> **[Restriction]** Found in play, never chosen at creation. One stone's blessing at a time.

*Didact:* `didact do take-stone who=<hero> stone=the-lady-stone`.
