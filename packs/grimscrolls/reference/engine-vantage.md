Your frame of reference: who you are, what's on your sheet, and the scene around you. It decides what's plausible — and you're assumed to carry the tools of your trade, so nobody counts lockpicks or arrows.

- **Certain** — stated on your sheet or obvious from the fiction. No roll; it just works. *A healer setting a bone in a quiet chapel.*
- **Reasonable** — fits you and the moment. Roll it, 0–1 thorns. *A scout finding the pass before the snow closes it.*
- **Doubtful** — a stretch. Roll with 2+ thorns or higher stakes. *A pilgrim staring down a bandit chief.*
- **Impossible** — beyond you, no roll. Propose something smaller. *Out-flying a dragon on foot.*
