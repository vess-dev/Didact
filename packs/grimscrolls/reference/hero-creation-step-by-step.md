1. **Race:** choose one of the ten in Chapter 5. It is your first **background** — three wises — and it gives you your **racial talent**.
2. **Background:** choose one of the Twenty-One below — the trade you followed before the adventure found you. Three more wises.
3. **Birthsign:** choose one of the thirteen signs in Chapter 5, or roll a month, and take its talent.
4. **Class:** pick one of the twenty-three in Chapter 4 (its opening tables sort them at a glance) and take its **Core Talent**. A core, a racial talent, and a sign: that's everything you start with. Each new level brings one more talent, from any class's list.
5. **Stats:** start with 1 in each of Brawn, Agility, Wits, and Presence, then assign 4 more points (max 3 in any one).
6. **Traits (2 you are, 1 you're not):** brave, caring, confident, curious, gentle, honest, honorable, persistent, protective, quiet, rash, stubborn.
7. **Desires (2 you want, 1 you don't):** belonging, glory, harmony, honor, justice, knowledge, love, power, renown, thrills, wealth, wisdom.
8. **Features (3):** what people notice — *a Legion brand on the forearm, ash-grey skin and a soft voice, a tail that gives every lie away, a hammer too big for the doorway.*
9. **Arcs:** choose one or two character arcs — *earn my stronghold's forgiveness — find the mage who took my name.*
10. **Bonds:** as the others introduce themselves, set one bond toward each PC — an intensity and a nature (*growing curiosity, complicated camaraderie, tense rivalry*).
11. **Kit:** you carry the tools of your trade, a *3d Potions* pool, and a *4d Coin* pool (Chapter 6).
12. **Montage:** check the party concept, the era, and the group arc still fit; then run the last job you pulled together as a group montage roll (2d each), and narrate how it went.

> **A HERO IN TEN LINES — "KAZARI."** *The party: Sellswords and Hunters of Monsters, definitely not Outlaws. The era: Skyrim, 4E 201. The group arc: make the Pale Pass road safe to walk.*
> **Race:** Khajiit *(the caravan roads · the moons and their sugar · what the night hides)* — talent, *Night Eye*. **Background:** Scout *(lay of the land · tracks and sign · weather coming)*. **Birthsign:** born in Mid Year, under the Steed.
> **Class:** Marksman — *The Hunt*. **Stats:** Brawn 1, Agility 3, Wits 2, Presence 2. **Traits:** curious and persistent, not gentle. **Desires:** thrills and belonging, not power. **Features:** a notched left ear · a bone bow older than she is · never sits with her back to a door.
> **Arc:** find the caravan that left her behind. **Bond:** *grudging respect* toward the Orc who keeps walking in front of her arrows.
