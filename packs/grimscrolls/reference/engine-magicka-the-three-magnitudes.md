**The three magnitudes.** A **spell** does what one skilled person could do, a new way. A **potent spell** bends the rules — group-scale effects, lasting effects, the impossible made merely difficult. A **ritual** changes the map: seal an Oblivion gate, raise a tower in a night, bind a Daedra to a bargain, break the curse in the blood. Rituals take three things — a **source** (a caster of the right school, a scroll, a relic, a Prince's favor), **anchors** (two or three concrete requirements the GM and table set: a black soul gem, the full of Secunda, the blood of the one who swore the oath), and the **invocation**: a task pool worked down over time, usually while something tries to stop you. Missing anchors make the pool bigger or the whole thing impossible.
