**How a fight works.** Enemies never roll. A dangerous foe is a **task pool** (Chapter 7): hurt it and the GM rolls its pool; at 0d it's beaten, and one good roll can clear a handful of mooks outright. The world hits back through **impact moves** — on your messy and grim rolls, and whenever the GM spends suspense — and a move you didn't invite gets a **defense roll**.
