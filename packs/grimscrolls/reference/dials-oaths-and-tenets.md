Half of Tamriel has sworn to something. At creation, each hero may take **one oath**: an edge and a flaw, welded together. The edge is always on — a permission or a thorn ignored, never a die. The flaw is a standing tangle: the first time each session it truly bites, take spark. Break the oath for good and you lose the edge; the people you swore to will have opinions.

| Oath | The edge | The flaw |
|---|---|---|
| **The Green Pact** | The wild knows its own: forest and jungle never thorn you, and its beasts don't strike you first. | You harm no living plant, eat only meat, and leave nothing you kill to waste. |
| **The Code of Malacath** | Among Orcs your word is a contract, and a blood price settles anything you do against them. | Every insult is answered and every debt is paid — in blood, if nothing else will do. |
| **The Five Tenets** | Any Dark Brother or Sister shelters you without a question, and you know the Brotherhood's signs on sight. | Disobey, betray, steal from, or kill one of your own, and the Wrath of Sithis comes looking. |
| **The Guild's Rule** | Fences, safehouses, and shadowmarks are open to you in every city. | No killing on the job — and never rob the poor, or a guildmate. |
| **The Way of the Voice** | Your stillness unsettles the violent: you always get to speak before steel is drawn. | You raise your hand, and your voice, only in true need; needless force is a mark on you. |
| **The Legion's Oath** | Any Imperial fort feeds, arms, and quarters you, and legionnaires take you at your word. | Orders are orders, and they always find you. |
| **The Vigilant's Vow** | You always know Daedric taint when you stand near it. | You strike no bargain with a Daedra, and you can't walk past those who do. |
| **The Pilgrim's Vow** | Any chapel or temple gives you bread, a bed, and a hearing. | You give to whoever asks in the god's name, and the road isn't finished. |
| **Honor of the Hall** | A hall you've feasted in will stand beside you in a fair fight. | You refuse no challenge and take no low advantage: no ambush, no poison, no blow at a fallen foe. |
| **Guest-Law of the Ashlands** | Once you've shared a fire, the tribe treats you as kin, and no door of theirs is shut to you. | Whoever eats at *your* fire is under your protection, whatever they turn out to be. |
