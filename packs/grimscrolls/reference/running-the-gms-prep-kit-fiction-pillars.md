- **Fiction pillars.** Before a session, sketch up to seven elements that might come up — a face, a place, a relic, an event — each with three sensory details, its drive (what it wants or does), and one way to embody it at the table: a voice, a gesture, a sound. Hang the night's improv on them, and keep the unused ones for later. Above them, set two or three load-bearing truths for the region and never contradict them: *the Legion can't be everywhere — the dead here don't rest — somebody has been selling Dwemer metal.*
