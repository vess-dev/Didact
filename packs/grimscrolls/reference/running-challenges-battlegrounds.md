When the place itself fights, list its **features** (they bend vantage and thorns) and **threats** (each carries 2 suspense of its own, or a timer):

**The Barrow Hall** — *Features:* burial niches along both walls (some occupied, some merely waiting) · a floor of pressure plates and swinging blades · a word wall at the far end, chanting faintly. *Threats:* *4d The Niches Open* · *6d The Oil Lamps* (one spark from a hall on fire) · the thing in the sarcophagus.

**The Dwemer Pump Room** — *Features:* catwalks over boiling runoff · valves that reroute the steam (and the cover) · brass doors on a rhythm nobody's timed in an age. *Threats:* *4d Spiders in the Pipes* · *6d Pressure Builds* (a timer to the whole gallery venting) · a centurion in its cradle, not yet awake.
