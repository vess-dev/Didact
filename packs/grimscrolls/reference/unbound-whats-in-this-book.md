- **Chapter 2, The Core Engine** — the rules for nightly play: rolls, pools, magic, harm, and healing.
- **Chapter 3, Building a Hero** — the party, the ten races, the twenty-one backgrounds, and creation step by step.
- **Chapter 4, The Classes** — twenty-three classes: the twenty-one skills of Combat, Magic, and Stealth, plus the Vampire and the Werewolf. Each is a Core Talent with seven talents around it.
- **Chapter 5, Blood, Stars & Stones** — the racial talents, the thirteen birthsigns, the thirteen standing stones, and the Voice.
- **Chapter 6, Surviving Tamriel** — potions, disease, the two curses, coin, crime, shrines, the long road, and downtime.
- **Chapter 7, The Bestiary** — the continent's threats, ready to run.
- **Chapter 8, The Reliquary** — relics, arms of renown, and things worth wearing.
- **Chapter 9, Running Tamriel** — the GM's kit.
- **Chapter 10, Options & Dials** — optional rules to tune the game to your table, and tools for building your own.
- **The Talent Index** — every talent in the book, alphabetical, with its page.
