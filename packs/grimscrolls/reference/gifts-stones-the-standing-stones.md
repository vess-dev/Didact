Thirteen stones stand in the wilds of Skyrim, one for each constellation, older than the Nords and humming faintly when the stars are out. Other provinces have their own — the doomstones of Cyrodiil, the menhirs of High Rock, things in the ashlands the Temple would rather you left alone — and the GM decides where they are. A stone is a find, like a relic: something the road gives a company that has walked a little of it. The three Guardian Stones, which stand together by a road, are the traditional first.
