- **Lethality.** *Deadlier:* high and dire stakes often; a desperate hero also carries an urgent condition (*4d Bleeding Out*); dropped = a 1d story roll; no coming back; potions at 2d. *Softer:* low stakes often, even in fights against weak foes; dropped = a 3d story roll; permanent conditions are rare; potions flow.
