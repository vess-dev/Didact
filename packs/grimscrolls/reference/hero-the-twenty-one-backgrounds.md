These are the professions of the old Imperial registers — what you'd have written on the census form before things got interesting. Each gives **three wises**: phrases that widen your vantage — what you know, notice, and get away with. Swap any wise for one that fits better, and rename freely (*you're not a Scout, you're a Pathfinder of the Jerall Passes*). A background doesn't limit your class or your talents. But the registers also list the **seven skills** each profession trains, and if you want to play one as the old books describe it, take your Core Talent from one of its seven and choose your later talents among the rest. (In the table, a dot separates the arts: Combat · Magic · Stealth.)

**A register of your own.** The old registers also allowed a custom entry, and so does this one: name your profession, give it three wises, and — if you care to — list the seven skills it trains.
