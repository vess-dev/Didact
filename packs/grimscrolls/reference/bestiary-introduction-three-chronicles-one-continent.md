**Three chronicles, one continent.** These cards come from Vvardenfell, Cyrodiil, and Skyrim alike. Where a creature or a faction belongs to one province or one era, its card says so in a clause; move it anyway if your story wants it.

Each card's **role** says how the threat fights, and what makes it falter:

| Role | Fights by | Falters when |
|---|---|---|
| **Blaster** | raining power from range | the fight gets close |
| **Brute** | sudden, overwhelming force | it's baited or taunted |
| **Lurker** | striking from concealment | it's exposed, or the fight drags on |
| **Marksman** | picking targets off at a distance | caught in melee or forced to move |
| **Predator** | running its prey down | the prey won't break |
| **Protector** | shielding allies and soaking blows | cut off from those it guards |
| **Skirmisher** | hit-and-run from odd angles | pinned down |
| **Swarmer** | numbers, from every side | its numbers thin |
| **Tactician** | preparation and command | the plan fails |
| **Trickster** | confusion and deceit | its tricks are exposed |
