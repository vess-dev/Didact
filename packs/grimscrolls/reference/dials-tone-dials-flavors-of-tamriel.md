- **Flavors of Tamriel.** Point the dials at a subgenre: *the grim north* (deadlier + supplies + hard country), *pulp heroics* (softer + larger scale), *Great House politics* (smaller scale + factions front and center), *Daedric weird* (wild magic + Sheogorath's dial on).
