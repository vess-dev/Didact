A class says **what you're best at**, never **who you are**. A Blade can be a Redguard sword-saint, a Legion deserter, or a Telvanni wizard's bored daughter; a Destruction mage can be an Orc. Guilds, Great Houses, faiths, and loyalties live in your **backgrounds**, your **features**, and the story — none of them is baked into a class. The two curses are the exception that proves it: a Vampire or a Werewolf is still a smith, a thief, or a scholar in everything but the blood.
