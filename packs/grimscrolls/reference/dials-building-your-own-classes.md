- **Classes.** The registers have changed over the eras — Spear, Enchant, Unarmored, and Medium Armor were all skills once. Build a twenty-fourth class as a Core Talent with Growth (at levels 2, 4, and 6, or 3 and 6) plus seven talents, measured against Chapter 4. A **per-scene well** of 2–4 buys maneuvers: a critical effect, a thorn ignored, a narrow potent feat. A **per-session well** of 3 buys story beats — a potent feat, an interrupt — and one that only buys off damage or small edges can run to 6. A **pool** of 4d–6d a session, or 2d–3d a scene, can stand in for a well. Add at most one always-on rider, and make sure the engine hands its player a decision every scene.
