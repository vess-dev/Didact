The constellation in the sky on the night you were born is your **birthsign**. Three are the **Guardians** — the Warrior, the Mage, and the Thief — and each watches over three **charges**. The thirteenth, the Serpent, belongs to no month and no one, and threatens all the others. Choose your sign, or roll a month and take what the calendar gives you. You get one, and it never changes.

> **[Restriction]** One birthsign, chosen at creation. It never changes, and you can't pick up a second.
| Month | Sign | Month | Sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning Star | The Ritual | Sun's Height | The Apprentice |
| Sun's Dawn | The Lover | Last Seed | The Warrior |
| First Seed | The Lord | Hearthfire | The Lady |
| Rain's Hand | The Mage | Frostfall | The Tower |
| Second Seed | The Shadow | Sun's Dusk | The Atronach |
| Mid Year | The Steed | Evening Star | The Thief |
