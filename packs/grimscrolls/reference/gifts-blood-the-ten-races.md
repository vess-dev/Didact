Your race is one of your two backgrounds. It comes with **three wises** — swap out any that don't fit how your hero was raised; a Nord brought up on the Imperial City waterfront knows different things — and one **racial talent**, which is in the blood and stays whatever your upbringing.

> **[Restriction]** One race, and its talent alone. You can't pick up a second race later.
