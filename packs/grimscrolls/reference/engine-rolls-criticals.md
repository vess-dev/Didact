- **Criticals:** more than one 6. Thorns can't cut a critical. Pick a bonus: *greater effect* (drop 1d from the related task pool before rolling it) — *secondary effect* (the effects of a related perfect, too) — *setup* (a benefit to a follow-up roll — usually an assist without risk). If nothing fits, take spark and keep moving.
