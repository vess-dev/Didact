**Story rolls.** For luck and off-screen fact — *is the ferryman still at the dock? did the fence keep the map?* — roll 1d (bad odds), 2d (even), or 3d (good). **Perfect:** ideal. **Messy:** yes, with a catch. **Grim:** it's a problem now. When a story roll stands in for an action, a messy or grim can prompt an impact move; thorns are rare, but allowed.

**Montage rolls.** To cross a stretch of time or country in one breath, everyone makes a 2d action roll and the table keeps the highest result. Narrate the journey together, and slow down wherever it gets interesting.

*Didact:* `didact do story-roll dice=2` (or `didact ask '…' --odds even`); a montage: `didact do montage who=<hero>` for each hero.
