**Armor.** Anyone can wear armor, and the fiction says what it stops. Talents make it count, and they answer to what you actually have on: *Plate* and *Juggernaut* want heavy armor, *Evasion* and *Unhindered* light, *Mage Armor* none at all. One suit at a time.
