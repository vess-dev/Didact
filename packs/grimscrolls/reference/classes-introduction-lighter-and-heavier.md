**Lighter and heavier.** **One step lighter** turns harm into a mark and a mark into nothing; **one step heavier** turns a mark into harm, and harm into harm and a mark. A step applies to whatever would land after the defense roll, and anything that makes harm *land as a mark* is a step. Steps never stack — one at most, and a lighter cancels a heavier. If a sign, a stone, or a curse would charge you a price you already pay, the GM names another of equal weight.
