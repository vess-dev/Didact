PCs are as alert as their vantage says — nobody rolls to not-walk into the obvious. Against a hidden danger, spotlight the most relevant PC and pick: **reveal** (they see it plainly — the pressure plate sits proud of the floor), **hint** (one shot at intuition — *the birds have stopped*), or **strike** (spend suspense: the danger lands as an impact move, and the target gets a defense roll). A thief gets a reveal on a trapped chest; a pilgrim gets a hint; a scholar reading the wall aloud takes the strike.
