- **Talents.** A talent never grants standing potency outside a narrow domain and never raises a stat. Its pools run 2d–3d, its "always" covers only knowledge and vantage, and every strong trick is once per session, push-priced, or given time. A racial or birthsign talent is lighter still: one narrow edge, one gift a session.
