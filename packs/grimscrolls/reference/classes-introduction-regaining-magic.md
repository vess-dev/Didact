**Regaining magic.** Wherever a rule lets you **regain magic**, choose one: a spent spell, a spent illusion, 2d of a magic pool, one step of strain, or a potent Destruction spell without the push. A spent *potent* spell comes back only when the rule says so. A **magic pool** is one a school's talent gives you — a rite, *Thrall, Familiar, Risen, Unbinding, Reflection, Ward, Resistance, Flesh* — never an alchemist's draughts, *Poisons*, *Potions*, *Blood*, or a relic's charge.

*Didact:* `didact do regain-magic who=<hero> what=spell why=Highborn`.
