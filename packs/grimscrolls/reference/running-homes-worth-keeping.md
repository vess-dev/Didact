When the party plants a flag — a homestead, a guild hall, a fort they cleared and kept — it becomes a **holding**, and it grows on a **Stores** pool. Trade hauls into it (a minor haul adds +2d, a major +4d, a mythic +8d); when time passes, roll it, and every die that drops pays toward the next **asset**: *minor, 4* (a wall, a forge, an alchemy garden, a tavern with a name) or *major, 8* (a mine that pays, a shrine with a priest, a trade road that comes to you). Give every asset a face — the steward, the smith, the bard who won't leave — and the bandits something to regret. When trouble comes, play it out, but don't erase the players' work: damage to an asset costs a die or two of stores to fix.

*Didact:* `didact gen run grimscrolls.holding`; `didact do stores pool=<stores> haul=minor`, `roll`.
