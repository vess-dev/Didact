*"You're finally awake."*

Grimscrolls is a tabletop roleplaying game about heroes in Tamriel, the continent of *The Elder Scrolls*: nine provinces, one cracked Empire, a pantheon that can't agree on its own headcount, and a ruin under every hill. The dungeons are barrows and Dwemer halls. The miracles are spells, potions, and stubbornness. Play is fast and conversational — say what you do, roll a handful of six-sided dice, keep the highest, and let d8 **thorns** complicate things — and every hero is built from **talents** you can re-skin to fit anyone you want to play. The Elder Scrolls foretold a hero. They never said it would be you.
