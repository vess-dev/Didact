- **Relics.** Pick a **function** (*attack — enhance — hinder — influence — protect — restore — reveal — summon — transform — traverse*) and a form, name it, and give it **three touchstones** and **one Limit that bites** — a pool, a price, a Prince's attention, or a person who wants it back. Set its tier by Chapter 8's gate; expendables punch one tier up.
