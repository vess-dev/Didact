- **Supplies.** For survival-forward games, each hero has a *4d Supplies* resource pool — arrows, rations, torches, the little things. Roll it after heavy use, and as time passes away from towns; at 0d, hunger and cold start writing the thorns.

*Didact:* `didact do supplies` rolls every hero's Supplies at once (`who=` one hero's, after heavy use).
