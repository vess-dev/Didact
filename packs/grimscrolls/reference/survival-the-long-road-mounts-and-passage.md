**Mounts & passage.** A horse is a haul first and vantage after: give it **two words** (*steady, greedy · fast, skittish · ugly, fearless*) and let the fiction do the rest. Riding hard is an action roll — Agility to ride, Wits to find the way — and what goes wrong lands on the animal as a condition. A chase is opposed task pools, or one pressure pool closing in. Between the big towns, somebody will always carry you for coin: a carriage, a riverboat, a silt strider, a Mages Guild guide who steps you from one hall to the next. That's set dressing — until the GM complicates things, and then the road is why.
