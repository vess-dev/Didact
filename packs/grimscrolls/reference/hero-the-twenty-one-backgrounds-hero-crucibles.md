> **[Optional] HERO CRUCIBLES.** *Stuck? Roll a thorn (d8) on any line — or just pick.*
> **What you're wearing:** fur and a wolf's-head cloak — Legion leathers with the insignia cut off — a scholar's robe, singed — netch leather, far from home — a noble's cast-offs — bonemold with a house mark filed away — a pilgrim's grey — whatever fit, off a bandit.
> **What you'd never trade:** an amulet of one of the Nine — a letter you can't read — a tooth on a cord — a ring that isn't yours to wear — your first lockpick — a pressed mountain flower — a map with one place circled — a sweetroll recipe.
> **What the road already took:** a home — a name — a finger or two — a guild's trust — the one who taught you — your faith — a horse you still talk about — the idea that the Empire is coming to help.
> **A name, if you need one:** *Altmer* Naerwen, Sorondil · *Argonian* Wakes-the-Water, Okeeh · *Bosmer* Maelin, Dirnor · *Breton* Elise, Gaston · *Dunmer* Varona, Drelas · *Imperial* Cassia, Marcellus · *Khajiit* Kazari, Dro'Zahr · *Nord* Hjalda, Torvald · *Orc* Shura gra-Bol, Uzgash gro-Morg · *Redguard* Samira, Kazim.
