- **Story kits.** Prep an adventure as parts, not plot: a **hook** heard three ways, a **pressure pool** or two to keep it moving, four or five **useful pieces** (a site map, two faces with wants, a challenge, a relic), a **fail state**, and the question the night is actually about. Let the party pick the order; the kit doesn't care. *(For the pattern worked, see The Door Under Greywatch, below.)*
