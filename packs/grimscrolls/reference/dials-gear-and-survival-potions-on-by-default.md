- **Potions** *(on by default)*. A *3d Potions* pool per hero clears one harm with a breather, on yourself or an ally (Chapter 6). *Grimmer:* *2d*, only yourself, or a single pool the whole party shares. *Softer:* *4d*, refilled by any real rest, and a draught poured into a dropped ally adds +1d to their story roll.
