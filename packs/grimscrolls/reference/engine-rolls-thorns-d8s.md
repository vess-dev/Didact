**Thorns (d8s).** Each significant difficulty adds a thorn — darkness, a blizzard off the Sea of Ghosts, you're outnumbered, you're picking a Dwemer lock with a fork. A thorn landing 7–8 **cuts** the result one step (perfect → messy → grim → disaster), and every such thorn cuts again. Typical rolls carry 0–2; at 3 or more, the odds fall off a cliff.
