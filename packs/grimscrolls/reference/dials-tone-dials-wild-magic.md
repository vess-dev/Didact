- **Wild magic.** Magic bites back for everyone, not just Destruction: any spellcasting roll with two or more 1s, thorns included, goes wrong — *a 1d story roll for the collateral — a dark omen (the GM takes suspense) — a roll of a 6d Corruption pool* (at 0d, a trait, desire, bond, or look turns for the worse).
