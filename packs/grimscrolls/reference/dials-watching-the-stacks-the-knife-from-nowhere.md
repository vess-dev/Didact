- **The knife from nowhere.** *Shadow* adds a critical effect, *Backstab* makes it potent, *Shadow Warrior* buys the next one. It's a strong loop, and it's meant to be — but each turn of it costs a mark and a roll of Shadow, and greater foes roll their pools rather than falling over.
