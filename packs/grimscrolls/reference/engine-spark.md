Pure protagonist energy. Spend it **before any roll you make** for **+1d** each; hold up to **2**, and it carries between sessions. Take spark when you: introduce a **tangle** — roll a **disaster** — resolve a **story arc** — settle a **quarrel** — or an ally changes their **bond** with you — and whenever a rule says so.
