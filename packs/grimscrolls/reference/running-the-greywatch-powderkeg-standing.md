Tamriel keeps score. For each faction that matters, write one word for how it sees the party — or one hero, if they've earned a name of their own. Standing is vantage: it sets the thorns on every dealing with that faction, and what's certain, doubtful, or impossible to ask of them.

| Standing | What it buys |
|---|---|
| **Honored** | Doors open and reasonable favors are certain — a thane's welcome. When they hear you're in trouble, they send help. |
| **Trusted** | Fair dealing and fair prices; you're let inside the walls after dark. |
| **Stranger** | The default. They don't know you from a mudcrab. |
| **Watched** | Prices double and eyes follow you: +1t on every dealing. |
| **Outlawed** | Arrested or attacked on sight, and somebody's paying: start a *6d They Find You* campaign pool. |

Standing moves a step when you do something that faction will tell stories about — and the same deed usually moves two factions in opposite directions. Out of every faction's earshot, it's just **fame**: what the bards sing about you decides whether the next stranger buys you a drink or sends for the guard.

*Didact:* `didact do standing who=<faction> shift=1`.
