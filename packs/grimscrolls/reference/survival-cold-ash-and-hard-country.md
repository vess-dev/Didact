Weather is **set dressing** until the fiction says otherwise. When exposure matters, it lands with the usual tools:

- **Hard country** — a blizzard in the Pale, an ash storm off Red Mountain, the Alik'r at noon, the air of the Deadlands — inflicts **+1t** while you work in it, or a **mark** as you push through (GM's call). A mark from exposure **lingers** like a condition: +1t on every roll with that stat until you're properly warm, fed, or clear of it. Mark that stat again and you take harm as usual.
- **A bad dose** is a condition: *frostbitten*, *ash-lunged*, *sun-struck* (short-term), or something that needs a healer (long-term).
- **The killing kind** — the Sea of Ghosts in winter, a river of lava a hand's breadth away — is an **urgent condition**: a *4d* timer (*2d* in the water), rolled as time passes. At 0d you're dropped.
- A fire, a cloak, a *Resist* draught, or a Nord's blood lightens all of this a step. Blight storms carry disease as well as ash.

*Didact:* `didact do exposure who=<hero> stat=brawn`; `didact do shelter who=<hero>`.
