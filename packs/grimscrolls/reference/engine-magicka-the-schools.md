**The schools.** Six of the classes in Chapter 4 are schools of magic — Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Illusion, Mysticism, Restoration — and each casts in its own way. Any hero can dabble: every school has a *Novice* talent, a scroll lets anyone cast its one spell once, and a staff carries its own.
