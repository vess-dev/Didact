**The passage of time.** When the GM declares that time passes, the game jumps ahead — usually a month or a season. Give each stretch three sensory details, and let one be strange — *snowberries out of season, both moons dark, a road shrine with fresh flowers and no village.* Campaign pools roll, factions move, and downtime opens.
