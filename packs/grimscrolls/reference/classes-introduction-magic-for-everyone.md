**Magic for everyone.** Six classes are schools of magic, and each casts in its own way. A hero of any class can dabble by taking a school's ***Novice*** talent — a small version of its Core Talent — and a few talents that deepen a school say *needs its spellcasting*: the class, or its Novice talent. The rest of each school's list works for anyone: a scroll, a staff, or an enchanted blade is magic enough.
