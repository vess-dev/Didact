**Reading a talent.** *Italic words split by dashes* are a menu — pick one. Anything that triggers **on a perfect** also triggers on a critical; anything that triggers **on a grim** also triggers on a disaster. A **critical effect** added by a talent is only the bonus — the roll isn't a critical — and a roll takes one such bonus at most. **Given time** means at least a beat of undisturbed work; talents that say so usually let you **push yourself** to do it on the spot instead — mark a stat after it resolves. **Always** holds about 95% of the time: it takes a Counter from a truly exceptional threat, or fiction that plainly rules it out, to stop it. A **quick action** is a second action taken at once: the world's answer to the first, impact move included, waits until both are done. One per beat, however many talents offer it. **Never potent** limits the talent that says it; potency from another source still applies.
