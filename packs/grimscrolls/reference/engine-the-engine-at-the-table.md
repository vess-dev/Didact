*Dusk on the Pale Pass road. A frost troll has a band of pilgrims pinned inside a broken watchtower, and Kazari wants the signal brazier on the roof lit.*

- **Goal and approach.** *"I go up the outside wall, fast and quiet, and get a flame into that brazier."* Risky, so it's an action roll. The GM calls **Agility** and adds **1 thorn** — the stones are rimed with ice. Agility 3, plus 1 spark spent: she rolls **4d + 1t**.
- **The dice: 6, 4, 2, 2 — thorn: 7.** The 6 would be a perfect, but the thorn **cuts** it to a **messy**: she's on the roof with the oil catching — *and* the GM makes an impact move. The troll's three eyes swing upward: a *4d The Troll Climbs* timer hits the table and rolls at once. Two dice drop. Not long now.
- **Uzgash steps up.** *"I put my hammer into its knee and knock it off that wall."* Blunt is his class, so the troll's hide costs him no thorn. Brawn 3 comes up 5, 3, 1 — **messy**. The hammer lands and the troll comes off the wall; the GM rolls its *4d* pool and one die drops. Uzgash spends **1 Might** to drop another: 2d left. For the messy, instead of striking back now, the GM quietly **banks suspense**.
- **The brazier.** Flame takes — a **2d story roll** for whether the Legion fort down the valley is watching (even odds): a 6, **perfect**. A horn answers from the dark.
- **The spend.** The GM spends the banked suspense: the troll, already knitting, tears a block from the wall and hurls it at the roof — an impact move Kazari didn't invite, so she gets a **defense roll**, Agility again. Uzgash bellows a warning to **assist** (1d, sharing the risk): her 3, 3, and 2, his 4 — **messy**, so the GM lessens it and lets it land on him: flying stone **marks Brawn**. He's laughing about it. He won't be next time.
*That's the whole engine: goal and approach, a stat, thorns for the ugly parts, pools for everything with a fuse, and suspense deciding when the world swings back.*
