Everyone feeds the shared picture — say what it looks like, before and after the dice. Use the camera: zoom in on the hands working the lock, cut away to the draugr opening its eyes behind you. Do what your hero would do and let the GM map it to the rules; don't lawyer your way into better rolls. There's no turn order, only the **spotlight** — grab it when you have something, pass it when you've held it, open doors for each other and step through the ones opened for you. Failure isn't losing; it's the next scene. Keep secrets from characters, never from players — the table watching the Thalmor's trap close around your friend *is* the fun.
