A disease is a **long-term condition**: +1t whenever it specifically gets in your way. The Bestiary's cards say which creatures carry what. When a carrier's blow lands as harm — or the GM spends suspense — the disease can come with it; you'll notice at your next rest. A *Cure Disease* draught, a shrine's blessing, a healer's purging, or a downtime Rest ends an ordinary one.

| Disease | Carried by | It costs you +1t… |
|---|---|---|
| **Ataxia** | skeevers, alit, old traps | on locks and pockets |
| **Rockjoint** | wolves, the risen dead | to swing a weapon |
| **Bone Break Fever** | bears | on feats of endurance |
| **Brain Rot** | hagravens | to work magic |
| **Witbane** | sabre cats, ice wraiths | to work magic |
| **Swamp Fever** | mudcrabs | on feats of strength |
| **Greenspore** | slaughterfish | to charm or haggle |
| **Droops** | shalk and ash hoppers | to swing a weapon |
| **Rattles** | chaurus | on quick work |
| **The Blight** (*Ash Woe, Chanthrax, Black-Heart*) | anything Red Mountain's storms have touched | as named on its card — and no ordinary cure touches it: only a Temple shrine, a cure-blight draught, or a potent purging |
| **Corprus** | the Sixth House and its beasts | on thinking clearly, more each season; it is a permanent condition, and curing it is a quest |

*Didact:* `didact do disease who=<hero> disease=rockjoint`; `didact do cure who=<hero> condition=Rockjoint`.
