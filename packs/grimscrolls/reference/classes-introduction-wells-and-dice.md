**Wells and dice.** Per-session wells and pools refill when a new session starts; per-scene ones refill with every scene. **Talent dice don't stack:** however many talents apply to a roll — Core Talents included — they add +1d in total, and the same goes for setups, for potions, and for relics: one die of each kind. Spark, assists, and special dice like the Hunter's die always add on top.

*Didact:* `didact do spend who=<hero> well=edge`; wells refill by themselves each session or scene.
