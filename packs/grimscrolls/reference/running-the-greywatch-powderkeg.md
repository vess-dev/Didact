A drop-in sandbox. Greywatch is a toll town on the Pale Pass road, the one way between Cyrodiil and Skyrim that stays open in winter — which is why everyone wants it and no one can hold it. It's written for the Skyrim civil war; for another era, swap the uniforms and keep the grudges. Track each faction's goals with **faction pools**, rolled between sessions or whenever the story triggers them — and in downtime, roll them all, then pick one and run it dry. When one empties, the goal lands on screen — or the faction moves against a rival, with a story roll in its favor deciding how it goes. Every other faction reacts. Keep four to six in play, and replace any faction the story uses up.
