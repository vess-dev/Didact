An optional dial, set at session zero like any other flavor: Tamriel gets *strange*. When things tip — a critical on a story roll, a disaster in the middle of nowhere — roll a thorn (d8) or choose from the crucible, and play it absolutely straight.

> **THE MADGOD'S CRUCIBLE.** *a wizard falls out of the clear sky, clutching three scrolls — a mudcrab with a merchant's manners and a great deal of gold — a talking dog who needs a favor and won't say for whom — a Khajiit by the roadside who knows much, and tells some — a giant's club, a guard, and a very long way up — a headless horseman, in no hurry at all — a chicken, an accusation, and the whole village — a stranger in purple and gold who would like to discuss cheese.*

*Didact:* `didact do madness` rolls the Madgod's crucible.
