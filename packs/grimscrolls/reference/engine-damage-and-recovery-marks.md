- **Marks** — light damage to a stat: +1t on your next roll with it, then it clears (a few marks linger — the cold, Chapter 6). Marking an already-marked stat inflicts harm instead, and pushing yourself costs a mark. A mark that gets lessened (by a messy defense, say) becomes set dressing.

*Didact:* `didact do mark who=<hero> stat=wits`.
