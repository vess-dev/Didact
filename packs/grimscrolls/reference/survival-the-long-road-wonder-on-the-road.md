**Wonder on the road.** Take spark when you let the world in: pause for an aurora over the Throat of the World and show us what it means to your hero — ask a question about the Dwemer nobody at the table can answer, and let the table run with it — travel somewhere far off just to see what's there (everyone who goes takes spark) — or make a scene about a place itself, one you'll never forget.
