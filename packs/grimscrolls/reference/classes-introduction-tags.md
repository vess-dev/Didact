**Tags.** A tag after a talent's name is a restriction, and where a whole list shares one, its bullets are locks. No tag, no strings: anyone can take it.
**Foes and ranks.** A **lesser** foe is a mook; a **greater** one is an elite or a boss (tiers, Chapter 7). Whatever its source — a talent, a spell, a draught, a poison, a Shout — an effect that would take a creature out of the fight, control it, or calm it does so outright only to a **tough or lesser** foe. A greater foe **loses its next move** instead — the next impact move the GM would have made with it — and rolls its pool; nothing holds one longer than a beat or two. A foe put **out of the fight** is done for the scene: if it has a pool to itself, the pool empties; if it shares one with a pack, the pool loses dice instead, no roll — 1d for a mook, 2d for a tough.
