**The token menu:**

- **Points** — a settlement, site, or danger: **1** token minor, **2** major.

- **Specials** — a curiosity, barrier, or area: **3** tokens (the GM adds them free).

- **Paths** — rough **free** · minor **1** · major **2**. Passes (bridges, tunnels) ride along free; waypoints are the GM's.

- **Street scale** — roads **1**; notable buildings **2** (minor, lower-case on the map) or **3** (major, CAPITALS).

- **Site scale** — rooms **1** (minor) or **2** (major).
