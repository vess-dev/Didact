**Action rolls.** When you try something risky, say your **goal** (why you're doing it) and your **approach** (how). The GM picks the stat; roll that many d6 and read the **highest**:

| Result | Highest die | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Perfect** | 6 | You get it, clean. |
| **Messy** | 4–5 | You get it, and the GM makes an impact move. |
| **Grim** | 1–3 | You don't get it, and the GM makes an impact move. |
| **Disaster** | grim + cut | The worst case. You take spark as consolation. |

*Didact:* `didact do action who=<hero> stat=agility thorns=1` (`spark=`, `talent`, `setup`, `relic`, `draught`, `assist=`, `stakes=`, `interrupt`).
