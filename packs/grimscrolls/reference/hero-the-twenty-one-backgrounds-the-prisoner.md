> **[Optional] THE PRISONER.** *Every hero in these stories starts in somebody's chains. Why were you a prisoner, and how did it end? Choose or roll a thorn:* 1 crossing a border at the wrong moment — 2 a crime you did — 3 a crime you didn't — 4 a debt — 5 somebody took a prophecy seriously — 6 you knew a name you shouldn't — 7 it was a favor to someone, and it went wrong — 8 you honestly don't remember. **And then:** *the cell door was left open — an emperor walked through your cell — a dragon interrupted the headsman — a boat put you ashore with release papers and a name — you paid — you're still wanted.*
