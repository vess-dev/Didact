- **Active vigilance.** Prefer dice to GM judgment on hidden dangers? The spotlighted PC makes a story roll, shaped by their vantage: perfect = reveal, messy = hint, grim = the strike lands. (A talent that guarantees a hint turns a grim into a hint; one that guarantees a reveal turns a messy or a grim into one.)
