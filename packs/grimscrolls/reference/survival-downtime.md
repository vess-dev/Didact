When time passes — a month in a city, a winter at the guild hall — per-session talents refresh, and each hero picks **one** downtime action — or **two** different ones if they sold off a haul:

- **Rest** — shake every non-permanent condition (marks and harm already clear with any real rest); the GM may rule that a long-term one lingers.
- **Venture** — odd jobs, a guild contract, a solo errand. Make a story roll: on a perfect, a minor haul, a rumor, or a new ally; on a messy or worse, it's complicated.
- **Carouse** — give a vignette of how you unwind — the mead hall, the Arena, the chapel, a drinking contest with a stranger named Sam — and take spark.
- **Pursue a goal** — chase a project with a task pool, rolled each time you pick this: rise in a guild, build the house, forge the blade, retrain a wise or a talent with a master who charges for it.
- **Plan ahead** — write a few words about how you prepare and take a special thread, on top of your usual two, usable only on details from that preparation. It's lost when time passes again.

*Didact:* `didact do downtime who=<hero> act=venture` (`also=` for a second action after selling a haul; `act=pursue project='Forge the Blade'` makes and rolls a project's pool).
