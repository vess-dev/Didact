**Set dressing is free.** Minor, common-sense details never cost anything — you have a tinderbox, the inn has a hearth, your knife is where knives go. Anything *certain* in your vantage can just be established. Thread is for the details that change the situation.

**Thread** is authorship. You get **2 per session**. Spend 1 to add a story detail beyond set dressing — gear you'd plausibly have, a contact in this hold, a rumor that pans out. It must fit your vantage (at least doubtfully) or tie to a story arc; the GM can call for a story roll on the bold ones, add nuance, or veto what breaks the story. A detail can't override a roll or contradict what's already established, and details that touch another hero need their okay.

**Tangles** are complications you volunteer — your character's flaws steering the story. *You can't leave the Daedric shrine unexamined. You tell the Thalmor justiciar exactly what you think of the Concordat. You take the sweetroll.* A tangle lands with the weight of an impact move, and you take **spark** for it.
