**Other draughts.** Anything stranger than a healing potion is a *trivial* expendable — one spell-sized effect, no roll, good for a scene — and it's a find, a purchase, or an Alchemist's work (Chapter 4), never a bottomless pocket. A stock is as deep as the fiction says, or a *2d* resource pool if your table would rather count.

| Draught | While it lasts | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Cure Disease** | ends an ordinary disease on the spot | the Blight and the two curses need more than a bottle |
| **Cure Poison** | ends a poison's timer | — |
| **Magicka** | **regain magic** (Chapter 4) | one a scene, or it comes back up |
| **Resist** (fire, frost, or shock) | that element lands one step lighter | name the element when you buy it |
| **Stamina** | ignore thorns from exhaustion; your next push is free | you'll sleep like the dead tonight |
| **Water Breathing** | a scene under the surface | the scene ends when it does |
| **Night-Eye** | darkness never thorns you | daylight dazzles: +1t for a beat when it hits |
| **Invisibility** | unseen until you strike, cast, or speak | rare, expensive, and everyone assumes the worst of the buyer |
| **Mead & ale** | shrug off one vex | +1t on anything delicate |
| **Skooma** | **+1d** on anything that wants speed or strength | mark Wits when it fades; see below |
| **Sleeping tree sap** | the first harm you take lands as a mark | slow and happy: +1t on Agility |
