This book draws on three chronicles: Vvardenfell in the last years of the Third Era, Cyrodiil during the Oblivion Crisis, and Skyrim two centuries on, with dragons in the sky. Its cards and relics come from all three, and a clause tells you when something belongs to only one. Set your game in any of them, between them, or somewhere the chronicles never looked. Lore is a toolbox, not a test: when the book and the table's memory disagree, the table wins.
