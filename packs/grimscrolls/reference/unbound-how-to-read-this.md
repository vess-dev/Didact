- **Talents** are the special advantages that set your hero apart. Each one expands your *vantage* — what you can plausibly attempt.

- **Classes** are skills: the one art you've practiced until it became who you are, gathered with seven talents around a signature engine. The class name adds nothing to your vantage on its own; your talents and backgrounds do. Every talent except the Core Talents is open to every hero; the class you pick just decides your Core Talent and where your story starts.

- **The Core Talent** is your class's engine, and it has **Growth** — it gets stronger as you level: "at levels 2, 4, and 6" or "at levels 3 and 6." Level 7 is the cap.

- **At creation,** you have three talents: your class's Core Talent, your race's talent, and your birthsign's. You gain a new talent at each level, from any class's list.

- **Push yourself** always costs: after the talent resolves (and any roll it calls for), mark a stat of your choice. Spend those moments wisely.
