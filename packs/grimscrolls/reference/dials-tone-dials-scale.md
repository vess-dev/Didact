- **Scale.** *Smaller:* more rolls are risky or impossible, mooks are rare and every foe is a threat, and levels cap at 3 (swap talents at story milestones instead). *Larger:* fewer rolls are risky, mooks are waded through and toughs fall a few at a time, heroes start at level 3, and bosses have a *secret* to overcome, not just a bigger pool.
