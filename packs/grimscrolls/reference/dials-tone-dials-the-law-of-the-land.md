- **The law of the land.** Pick the era's hard rules and enforce them: *the Blight closes Red Mountain and the Temple hunts heretics* (Vvardenfell, 3E 427) — *necromancy is banned by the Mages Guild, and gates to Oblivion open outside every city* (Cyrodiil, 3E 433) — *Talos worship is a crime, the Thalmor carry the warrants, and dragons are real again* (Skyrim, 4E 201).
