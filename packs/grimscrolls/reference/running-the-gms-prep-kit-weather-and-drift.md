- **Weather & drift.** Track drifting conditions (weather, an occupying army's mood, an ashfall) as a wandering step: each downtime or dawn, roll a d6 — 1–2, a step toward calm; 3–4, hold; 5–6, a step toward the worst — along clear ↔ wind ↔ snow ↔ blizzard ↔ the storm has a voice. It wanders back the way weather does, and everyone plans around it.

*Didact:* `didact do drift` (the Weather counter, made at 1 the first time; `name=` another drift, `track=` a counter of your own).
