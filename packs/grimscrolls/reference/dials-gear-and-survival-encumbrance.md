- **Encumbrance.** Normally common sense. If you want teeth: each hero carries **2 + Brawn** slots of gear beyond armor and main weapons. Most gear takes a slot, small items half, and large or bulky ones two or more. *Beast of Burden* and the Steed Stone laugh at this rule.
