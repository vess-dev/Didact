Before anyone writes a name, decide together what your company *is* — and *when*. Tamriel's stories cluster around a few bad years: Vvardenfell under the Blight (3E 427), Cyrodiil with the gates of Oblivion opening (3E 433), Skyrim with dragons in the sky and a civil war on the ground (4E 201). Pick one, or a quiet decade between them, and say where you are when the story starts.

Then choose **2 concepts** that fit your party and **1 that definitely doesn't**:

*Caravan — Chartered Guild — Delvers of Ruins — Exiles — Hunters of Monsters — Legion Detachment — Outlaws — Pilgrims — Sellswords — Sworn to a Jarl — Thieves' Crew — Vigil against the Dark*

And pick a **group arc** — the next stretch of road, not the destination: *get the relic to the Greybeards before the Thalmor do — find out who put the bounty on us — make this town too hard to raid — close the gate before it opens for good.*
