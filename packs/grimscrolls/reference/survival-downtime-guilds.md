**Guilds.** Joining one — Fighters, Mages, Thieves, the Companions, the College, a Great House, the Legion — is a story detail and a set of duties. Track your rise with a task pool (*6d Rise in the Guild*), rolled when you finish its contracts or pursue it in downtime. Each time it empties you gain a rank and something real: a bed and a fence, a key to the library, a title that opens doors — and a guild-master who now knows your name.

Meanwhile the GM rolls the **faction pools** (Chapter 9), then picks one and runs it dry. Tamriel moves too.

*Didact:* `didact do guild who=<hero> guild='Mages Guild'` makes its 6d Rise, then `pool=<it>` rolls it as contracts finish.
