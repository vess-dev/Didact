Magic is real, taught, and everywhere a little — a healer's hands, a guard's torch-spell, a scroll in a pawnshop. Any single working of it is a **spell**, and a **potent spell** is a potent version of the same. **Touchstones** rule everything: the school and the name of the spell bound what it can do. *Flame Cloak* burns what comes close; it doesn't mend a wound — and if a use feels like a reach, it's past the touchstone's edge (the GM can allow it at +1t, or veto it). Spells that carry no risk just work, and a riskless spell doesn't count against a caster's session — unless it's potent, or it mends someone. A power pool, though, is rolled for every use, risky or not.

*Didact:* `didact do cast who=<hero> school=alteration spell='Lock & Open'` (`potent`, `riskless`, `mends`).
