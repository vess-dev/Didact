**Quarrels.** When two heroes are at genuine cross-purposes and both players agree to settle it, name the stakes — dramatic, not deadly (bruises, not bloodied) — then both roll 2d; highest wins. No talents apply; others can assist or pile in at 2d with their own agenda, and a tie means compromise or another round. The winner narrates how it goes their way; the loser narrates how they take it. The result is final — and everyone involved takes spark.

**Bonds.** Toward each other hero, hold one bond — an intensity plus a nature (*lowkey doubts, tense rivalry, deep affection*). Change it when something lands; give a short vignette, and the other player takes spark.
