- **Armor for everyone.** For crunchier plate without a talent: light armor gives a *1d Armor* pool each session, heavy armor *2d*. After a physical consequence lands, roll it to lessen the consequence, as a messy defense would. Light armor carries the *Lightly Armored* condition (it hinders delicate movement); heavy carries *Heavily Armored* (loud, slow, and no swimming). The armor talents in Chapter 4 still *avoid* harm outright; this rule only lessens it, and a hero with an armor talent uses that instead.

*Didact:* `didact do armor who=<hero>` rolls the sheet's own Armor pool (`pool=` another).
