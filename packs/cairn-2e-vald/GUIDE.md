# Vald (Cairn's implied setting)

The implied setting of Cairn 2e: small towns a day's walk apart, the one Wood that is every forest,
the Roots below, the Neighbors beyond, and the faiths and factions of the realm.

- **Enable it** for a world: `didact pack enable cairn-2e-vald --scope world`. Its calendar (6-day
  weeks, 12 months of 24 days, from 7728) replaces the default; `didact time show` names the
  date, the season (Dead, Dry, Wet, Harvest) and any holiday, and `time advance` reports the
  holidays it passes.
- **Factions and faiths:** `didact compendium import cairn-2e-vald` brings the House of Burgesses,
  the Order of Nine, the Marchguard, the Tideborn, the White Raven and the Five-Pointed Star
  into the world as faction records.
- **Weather:** each new day rolls it by itself (`time advance`): Vald's own for the season (what
  it's like) and Cairn's for travel (what it does to a journey). By hand: `didact do vald-weather
  season=Wet`. Vald's seasons match Cairn's weather tables by the solstices and equinoxes the
  calendar names: **Dead is winter** (the Dead Solstice is the shortest day), **Dry is spring**
  (an equinox), **Wet is summer** (the Wet Solstice is the longest day), **Harvest is fall** (an
  equinox). Extreme weather twice in a row still turns Catastrophic.
- **Reclamation, the leap week:** every 10 years a six-day week (Recognize, Remember, Reward,
  Rejoice, Relinquish, Renew) belongs to no month. Didact's calendar has no days outside the
  months, so it doesn't count it: when a decade turns in play, narrate the six days and don't
  advance time through them (advancing would move the months on), or treat them as the new
  year's first days if the date matters less than the story.
- **Months are 24 days** here, and Cairn's Stone Heart (bond 15) grows a slot heavier as each of
  Vald's months begins (`burden`).
- **Lore:** the Wood, the Roots and Gates, the Neighbors and Vald's settlements come up when play
  mentions them; the Vald principles are always in the brief.
