| d20 | Omen |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | Dragons — long thought a myth of the elder days — are seen again in the skies. Where one passes, it is said, the dead claw their way out of their barrows.                                   |
| **2**   | The aurora burns an unnatural green every night over Winterhold, and the College's ruined tower hums like a struck bell.                                                                     |
| **3**   | A Hold's Jarl has not been seen in weeks. His steward speaks for him now — and the steward's eyes are the wrong color.                                                                       |
| **4**   | Wolves and bears come down from the peaks in unnatural numbers, fleeing something deeper in the Wilds. Hunters whisper of a great Hunt, and of antlered shapes moving among the pines.       |
| **5**   | Whole caravans vanish on the road to Markarth. The only thing left behind: heart-shaped stones carved from briar.                                                                            |
| **6**   | The mead in an entire town soured to blood overnight. The priest of Mara blames the Daedra; the elders blame the new Jarl.                                                                   |
| **7**   | Gray ash falls like snow on the eastern coast, and those who breathe it too long mutter the same name in their sleep: _Miraak_.                                                              |
| **8**   | The Civil War is turning bloody near here. Both Stormcloak and Legion banners have been sighted, and the village must choose a side before the armies arrive to choose for them.             |
| **9**   | A star has gone out. The astronomers at the College say it is a wound in Aetherius closing — and that magicka has felt _thin_ ever since.                                                    |
| **10**  | Children across the Hold dream the same dream: a black door, a whispered question, and a woman who smells of nightshade.                                                                     |
| **11**  | The Vigil of Stendarr has gone silent. Their hall stands open and empty, the hearth cold, a Daedric sigil scorched black into the floor.                                                     |
| **12**  | An early frost has come and will not lift; crops freeze in a single night. The Greybeards do not answer the village's prayers, and the high passes are buried.                              |
| **13**  | The dead of a nearby battlefield will not stay buried. By night they form ranks and drill, commanded by a wight wearing a dead king's crown.                                                 |
| **14**  | A Dwemer ruin has begun to hum and glow after an age of silence. Its automatons march out at dusk, gathering the living and carrying them down into the dark.                                |
| **15**  | Every lock in the city sprang open on the same night, and every key turned to lead. The Thieves Guild swears it was not them — and, for once, they sound afraid.                            |
| **16**  | A white stag has been seen, a streak of frost down its flank. Those who hunt it are found days later, far from home, with no memory and no shadow.                                           |
| **17**  | A shrine of Talos was thrown down by Thalmor decree. Now the soldiers who did it are dying one by one, each with a Word carved into their chest.                                             |
| **18**  | Skooma flows through the Hold cheaper than mead. Those who drink deeply do not wake — and do not die — but lie smiling and cold as the grave.                                                |
| **19**  | A spriggan-haunted grove swallowed a logging camp whole overnight. The trees bleed sap that smells of memory, and the loggers wander among them, rooted and weeping.                         |
| **20**  | An Elder Scroll has surfaced in the local market, and every power in Skyrim — Thalmor, College, Brotherhood, the vampire courts — is converging to claim it. They say reading it brings blindness. Or worse: clarity. |
