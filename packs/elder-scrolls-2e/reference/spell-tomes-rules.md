- A Spell Tome holds **one spell** and fills **one inventory slot**. It is not _petty_.
- Tomes cannot be cheaply copied or scribed. They are **found**: in Nordic barrows, Dwemer ruins, a hagraven's nest, a dead mage's study, the locked cases of the College of Winterhold, or bought at ruinous prices from the few who deal in them.
- Carrying a tome openly invites trouble. Hedge-mages, necromancers, and especially the **Thalmor** take a keen, unfriendly interest in who reads what.
- A Tome is not used up by casting. It can, however, be lost, burned, stolen, or destroyed — sometimes by the very spell within it (see **Casting**).
- Every tome has a **rank** — Novice, Apprentice, Adept, Expert, or Master. Rank says how rare and how potent a tome is, not who may read it: anyone can cast from any tome. It tells the Warden where a tome belongs — a Novice tome on a hedge-witch's shelf, a Master tome at the end of a quest.
