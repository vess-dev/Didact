A party that wants to *build* rather than inherit can purchase land from a Hold's steward — three plots are commonly offered across Skyrim, and a Warden may site more:

- **Lakeview Manor** — wooded land in Falkreath Hold, south of Lake Ilinalta. Timber and game close at hand.
- **Windstad Manor** — cold marshland in Hjaalmarch, north of Morthal. Bleak and fog-bound, but defensible and far from prying eyes.
- **Heljarchen Hall** — high tundra in the Pale, south of Dawnstar. Bitter winters; a mine and trade road within reach.

Buying the plot grants a parcel of land and a small starting steading — call it the 1/1/1 homestead, one building slot already filled by the main hall. From there, **building each wing or structure is a Downtime Action** (plus Septims and gathered materials), exactly as filling a building slot above. A homesteader hauls or quarries the stone and timber, raises the frame between sessions, and watches the place grow plot by plot: a wing here, a smithy there, a palisade when the raids start coming. The grand house in the fiction is, on the page, simply a homestead with its eight slots steadily filling — Pop, Defense, and Prosperity climbing as the family, the guards, and the trade arrive.
