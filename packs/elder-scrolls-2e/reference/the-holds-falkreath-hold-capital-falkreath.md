A quiet, mournful land of dense pine forest on the southern border, where Skyrim meets Cyrodiil and Hammerfell. Its economy is timber, and **Falkreath** is best known for one thing: its enormous graveyard, the largest in Skyrim, where heroes and commoners alike are laid to rest beneath the pines.

- **Jarl:** **Siddgeir**, young, idle, and pro-Imperial — a venal lordling who took the seat from his uncle and keeps low company. The Hold can fall to the Stormcloaks in the war, which restores his uncle, **Dengeir of Stuhn**.
- **The barrow:** the Hold's defining ground is the *Hall of the Dead* and its vast **cemetery**; deeper still, on the western marches, lies the *Twilight Sepulcher*, Nocturnal's shadow-tomb. Many a Nordic crypt hides among the trees.
- **The notable site:** the *Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary*, hidden in the pine woods — the assassins' last active chapter in Skyrim, sealed behind a door that answers only the right black words.
- **Other corners:** the *Jarl's Longhouse* in Falkreath; *Helgen*, the walled town near the Cyrodiil road (and, in the campaign's opening, the site of a catastrophe that lights the whole story); *Lake Ilinalta* with its sunken ruins; and the wooded plot of *Lakeview Manor*, land a party may buy to raise a home.
- **Troubles:** a grieving family seeks justice — or a grieving family seeks something far darker, and a child has begun the Black Sacrament. A creature out of the lake or the deep woods is taking woodsmen. The graveyard is being robbed, and the dead are not staying put.
- **Faces:** *Astrid*, the cool, commanding mistress of the Dark Brotherhood's last Sanctuary, who recruits with a knife to the throat. *Siddgeir*, the spoiled young Jarl, ever ready to farm out his dirty work to anyone who will take the coin.
