title: "Dexterity"
tags: [creating, monsters]
source: wardens-guide/creating-monsters#dexterity
---
Start from **10** as a baseline. Raise it for the quick, the agile, the nimble (a sabre cat, a frostbite spider); lower it for the slow, the bulky, the lumbering (a giant, a Centurion).
