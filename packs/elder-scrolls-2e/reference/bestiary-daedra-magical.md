title: "Daedra & Magical"
tags: [bestiary]
source: wardens-guide/bestiary#daedra--magical
---
The Daedra of the planes of Oblivion come to Nirn by summoning, by a Prince's command, or through a thinning of the barrier between worlds. They are immune to disease, and a Daedra "slain" in Mundus is only banished home. A **Banish** or **Expel** spell hurls a summoned one back to Oblivion.
