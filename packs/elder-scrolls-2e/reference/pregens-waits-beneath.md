4 HP, 1 Armor, 10 STR, 14 DEX, 12 WIL, Shadowscale dagger (d6)

- **Argonian Shadowscale**, age 29. **Hist-Blooded:** you breathe water as easily as air, and ordinary disease never takes hold in you.
- **Background:** you learned the marsh's poisons: a blade coated in your deathbell leaves the next creature it wounds _Slowed_ until the fight ends unless it passes a STR save. You also know every way water moves through a city: by cistern, sewer, or harbor, a guarded place's walls and gates count for nothing to you.
- **Carries:** Shadowscale dagger, garrote wire (_petty_), dark wrappings (1 Armor), **deathbell poison** (3 uses), Rations (3 uses), shuttered lantern with an Oil flask (6 uses), 13 Septims. _(6 slots)_
- **Bond:** you carry the **knucklebones of a murdered friend** (_petty_). The Dark Brotherhood believes a death is owed for it. They have not yet decided whether it is yours.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Shadow.
- **Who you are:** a cold, patient killer from the last class trained at Archon, who hears the Void in the silence between heartbeats and wonders whether it still has any use for them.
