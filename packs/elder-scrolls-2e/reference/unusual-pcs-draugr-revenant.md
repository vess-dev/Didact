**What it is.** An **awakened Nordic dead** — one of the honored or accursed corpses laid in Skyrim's barrows, risen and *aware*, retaining its mind where most draugr keep only a guttering hatred. You are dry, cold, and tireless, sustained by the old Nordic death-magic rather than by blood or breath. (See the Draugr Revenant background.)

**How you become one.** You were buried — a Nord warrior, a tomb-warden, perhaps one sworn to a Dragon Priest in the Merethic dark — and something woke you with your wits intact: a disturbed ward, an old oath unfulfilled, the pull of a word-wall. You begin play already risen.

### Edges
- **The grave needs nothing.** You require **no food, no water, no air, and no sleep**. You cannot be Deprived by hunger, thirst, exhaustion, or drowning, and you are **immune to inhaled poison and gas**.
- **Cold and tireless.** You are **immune to all mundane disease** and to **mind-affecting effects** — fear, charm, sleep, and illusions that sway the living mind find nothing living to sway. You never save to resist being swayed, frightened, or charmed; you simply are not.
- **Barrow-hardened.** The dry, preserved flesh of the honored dead turns blades: you have **1 Armor** even when unarmored. Worn body armor replaces it rather than adding to it; a shield or helmet still adds +1 each (to the cap of 3).
- **Old steel.** You rise knowing the weapons buried with you; centuries have not dulled the hand. (Flavor — though a Warden may let a revenant's one ancient Nordic weapon never break or rust.)

### Costs / Drawbacks
- **No healer mends the dead.** **No Restoration, potion, temple, or healer can knit you** — you are already dead. You recover lost STR only by **resting a full week within a Nordic barrow** (the death-magic that sustains you pools there; the week restores your other lost Attributes too), or by **absorbing the life** of a creature you slay (a kill restores 1d6 STR, once per day). HP still recovers from a short rest as normal — that is luck and poise, not flesh.
- **A walking corpse.** Mortals' **Reactions toward you start one step worse** — the living do not abide the walking dead. Most folk will flee or call for a priest; the Vigil of Stendarr and many Nords would see you returned to your tomb (**Vigil of Stendarr −1**).
- **Fire and the sacred.** Fire, and Restoration magic *turned against the undead* (a sun-spell, a priest's rite of banishing), bite at you: any such attack against you is _Enhanced_.
- **Bound to the barrow-ways.** You are a creature of the old Nordic dead. Hallowed ground, the wards of a word-wall, and certain Dragon Priest rites may compel, bar, or unmake you where they would only unsettle a mortal — the Warden decides when the old magic has a claim.
