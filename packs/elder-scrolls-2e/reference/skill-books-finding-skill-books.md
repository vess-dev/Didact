Skill Books are uncommon. They turn up on a scholar's lectern, a barrow's burial shelf, a bandit chief's footlocker, a Dwemer reading-room, a wizard's study, a temple library, the saddlebag of a dead adventurer who was trying to better themselves. A Skill Book is loot that tells a story — a copy of *Mace Etiquette* on a brawler's table says something about the brawler. The Warden should:

- Place at least one Skill Book in every second or third significant location the party clears.
- Hide the titles marked _rare_ in locked rooms, named NPCs' possessions, and the deep ends of dungeons — give them as the reward for real risk.
- Let players find duplicates sometimes — a copy the party already trained one PC on is still worth trading, selling, or handing to another character.
