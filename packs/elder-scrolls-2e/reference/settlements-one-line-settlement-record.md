A settlement's entire state fits on one line of the notebook:

> *Heljarchen Hall (the Pale) — Pop 3 / Def 2 / Prosperity 2 — slots: longhouse, palisade, smithy, livestock pen, cottages, (three free) — Jarl: Skald (favorable)*

That is the whole game. When the party raises a wing or weathers a raid, erase and rewrite.
