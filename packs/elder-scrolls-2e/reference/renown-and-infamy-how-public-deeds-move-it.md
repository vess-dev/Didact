- **One step per session, maximum, and only for public deeds.** A deed moves the needle if it was witnessed, sung, or seen in the aftermath — a dragon slain over a village, a Jarl's hall saved, a child returned, a massacre with survivors, a sacred shrine desecrated in daylight. What no one witnessed and no one can trace does not touch Renown at all; that is the province of Faction Reputation and Bounty.
- **The scale of the deed sets the direction, the cap sets the pace.** Even a world-shaking act only moves Renown one rung in a session — fame builds and tarnishes by accumulation, arc over arc, not in a single afternoon.
- **Some things never lower Renown.** Killing bandits, Forsworn raiders, draugr, vampires, or the plainly monstrous is a public good, not a public crime — it never drags the party down the ladder, however bloody. (It may *raise* it.) Renown falls for crimes against ordinary folk and the innocent, not for cutting down what everyone agrees should be cut down.
