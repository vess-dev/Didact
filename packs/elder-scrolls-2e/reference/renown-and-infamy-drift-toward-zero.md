Fame fades. Skyrim is wide, the roads are long, and yesterday's hero is tomorrow's stranger two Holds over. **Between arcs, Renown drifts one step toward 0** unless the party keeps feeding it — a Hero who vanishes into the wilds for a season comes back merely Renowned; an Infamous band that lies low and harms no one slowly becomes only Suspect, then Unknown again. Notoriety is easier to outrun than to earn, which is exactly why a guilty party so often just *leaves*.

Drift only ever moves *toward* 0; it never carries the party past Unknown into the opposite column.
