A temperate southeastern Hold of golden autumn forests and still lakes — and the most corrupt corner of Skyrim. **Riften** is a canal-laced town of creaking wooden walkways on the shore of Lake Honrich, ruled in name by its Jarl and in truth by the Black-Briar family and the Thieves Guild hidden in the sewers beneath it.

- **Jarl:** **Laila Law-Giver**, a Stormcloak sympathizer with little real grip on her own city, quietly managed by the Black-Briars. If the Imperials take the Rift, the cold matriarch **Maven Black-Briar** — with her Guild and Brotherhood ties — is installed openly as Jarl.
- **The barrow:** *Shroud Hearth Barrow*, above Ivarstead, which the villagers swear is haunted — and they are not wrong. More crypts dot the Velothi foothills, and in the hills near the Shadow Stone the Nightingales keep a hidden hall of their own.
- **The Dwemer ruin:** *Avanchnzel*, a Dwarven ruin high in the Jerall Mountains at the Hold's southwestern edge, whose sealed halls hold a secret the Dwemer meant to keep sleeping.
- **The notable site:** the *Ragged Flagon* and its **Cistern**, the Thieves Guild's lair beneath the city, reached through the rat-warren of the **Ratway**; and the *Temple of Mara*, Skyrim's center for the goddess of love, where pilgrims come to be wed.
- **Other corners:** *Mistveil Keep* (the Jarl's seat), the *Black-Briar Meadery*, and the inn called *The Bee and Barb*; out in the Hold, *Ivarstead* (the village at the foot of the seven thousand steps to High Hrothgar), *Shor's Stone* (a mining hamlet plagued by spiders), and *Fort Greenwall*. In the Velothi Mountains hides **Fort Dawnguard**, the vampire-hunters' stronghold.
- **Troubles:** a newcomer is approached in the marketplace with an offer to do "a little job." A mining village begs for help clearing the spiders — or worse — from its only shaft. Fort Dawnguard sends word of an Elder Scroll and a sleeping woman sealed in a crypt far off in the Pale — and a vampire court wants them first.
- **Faces:** *Maven Black-Briar*, the venomous matriarch who owns Riften in all but name and forgets no slight. *Brynjolf*, the silver-tongued thief who works the market crowd and recruits likely talent into the Guild.
