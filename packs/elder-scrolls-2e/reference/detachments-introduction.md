A detachment is a large pack of similar foes run as one creature. It is harder to hit and strikes several opponents at once. It stays a single unit right up until it takes Critical Damage, at which point it shatters or is wiped out. Now and then a lone enormous creature — a dragon, a giant, a Dwarven Centurion — is run as a detachment too.
