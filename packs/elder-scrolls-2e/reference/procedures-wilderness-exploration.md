> The Wilds of Skyrim are the snowbound tundra, the pine forests, the crags of the Reach, the ash wastes of Solstheim, and the high ice fields and passes between the Holds.

### Watches

- A day is divided into three **watches**, called _morning_, _afternoon_, and _night_.
- Each character can choose _one_ **Wilderness Action** per **watch**.
- If the characters split up, each group is treated as an independent entity.

### Points

- Potential destinations on a map are called **points**: a Hold capital, a wayshrine, a roadside inn, a barrow, a standing stone, a bridge over an icy river.
- One or more **watches** may be required to journey between two **points**, depending on the path, terrain, weather, and party status.
- The party has a rough idea of the challenges ahead, but rarely the specifics.

### Travel Duration

Travel time is counted in watches, divided into three eight-hour segments per day. Because most parties elect to spend the third watch resting, one can use "days" as shorthand for travel time.

To determine the distance between two points, combine all penalties from the path, terrain, and weather difficulty tables, accounting for any changes to those elements along the route. For travel via rivers and the coast, refer to the surrounding terrain difficulty. For especially vast stretches (the great glaciers, the endless tundra north of the White River), assign a penalty of up to +2 watches to the journey.

The weather, terrain, darkness, injured party members, and other obstacles can slow travel or even make it impossible. In some cases, the party may need to add **Fatigue** or expend resources to sustain their pace. Mounts, guides, and maps can increase the party's travel speed or even negate certain penalties.

### Path Difficulty

| Path | Penalty | Odds of Getting Lost |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------------------------ |
| Roads      | None        | None                     |
| Trails     | +1 Watch    | 2-in-6                   |
| Wilds      | +2 Watches  | 3-in-6                   |

| Path Distance | Penalty |
| ----------------- | ----------- |
| Short             | +1 Watch    |
| Medium            | +2 Watches  |
| Long              | +3 Watches  |
