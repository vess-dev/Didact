No homestead in Skyrim stands outside the Holds. A settlement sits within one of the nine Holds (or on Solstheim under Raven Rock), and it answers — in taxes, in militia levies, in law — to that Hold's **Jarl**. This ties the settlement to two larger dials in the game:

- **Standing with the Jarl.** A settlement in the Jarl's favor enjoys real protection: the Hold's guards patrol the road, bounties posted against raiders get answered, the market thaws, and a Prosperity check is easier (the Jarl directs trade and taxes lightly). A settlement out of favor — built without leave, sheltering outlaws, slow with the levy — draws audits, billeted soldiers, and a blind eye when raiders come. Treat the Jarl as a faction under **Faction Reputation** at the Warden's option, or simply judge it from the fiction.
- **The Civil War's outcome.** When a Hold changes hands — Whiterun to Vignar Gray-Mane, Windhelm to Brunwulf Free-Winter, the Reach or the Rift flipped by the war — the new Jarl inherits the settlement's ledger, and old favor does not always carry over. A homestead that flew the Stormcloak banner may find an Imperial-installed Jarl cold, and the reverse. War also raises the **raid** stakes: a contested Hold sees more war-parties on the roads, and a settlement astride a strategic route can be seized, garrisoned, or burned by whichever army is losing it. A wise party keeps one eye on which way their Hold is leaning.
