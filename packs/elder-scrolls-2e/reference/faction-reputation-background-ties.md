A character whose background binds them to a faction (a Stormcloak soldier, a College apprentice, a Thieves Guild burglar, a Vigilant of Stendarr, a Dawnguard hunter) **starts that faction at +1** — already Trusted, already known by name and history. The cost of that head start is loyalty: a character who betrays the faction they came from **drops a step faster** — two rungs on the first real treachery, where a stranger would drop one. Their own people take it personally.

A tie cuts both ways across the opposed pairs, too: a sworn Stormcloak's background starts the Imperial Legion at **−1** (Distrusted) before they have done a thing, and the reverse.

These are the backgrounds whose own text places them in a faction, and where each starts. A tie is that character's own: keep their standing on its own line of the ledger.

| Background | Starts at +1 | Starts at −1 |
|---|---|---|
| Companion | The Companions | — |
| Imperial Legionnaire | The Imperial Legion | The Stormcloaks |
| Stormcloak Soldier | The Stormcloaks | The Imperial Legion |
| Vigilant of Stendarr | The Vigil of Stendarr | The Daedric cults |
| Dawnguard Hunter | The Dawnguard | The Volkihar |
| Blades Agent | The Blades | — |
| Forsworn | The Forsworn | — |
| Thieves Guild Burglar | The Thieves Guild | The city guards |
| Dark Brotherhood Assassin | The Dark Brotherhood | — |
| Bard | The Bards College | — |
| College Apprentice | The College of Winterhold | — |
| Skaal Hunter | The Skaal | — |

The unusual backgrounds carry their nature's standing instead: a vampire counts as Volkihar +1 and Dawnguard and Vigil of Stendarr −1, the beast-blooded as Companions +1, and a Dremora, a draugr revenant or a Briarheart as Vigil of Stendarr −1 (see Playing Unusual PCs).
