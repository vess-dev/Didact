Skyrim is carved into nine **Holds**, each ruled from its capital by a **Jarl**. The Civil War has nearly every Jarl leaning toward the Empire or the rebellion; one has already been eased from his seat for it, and more will fall before the war is done.

- **Whiterun** — capital of Whiterun Hold, the windswept tundra plain at Skyrim's heart and its breadbasket. The trade-city of Whiterun is the Jewel of the North, watched over from the great hall of Dragonsreach. Jarl **Balgruuf the Greater** tries to keep his Hold neutral while the war closes in.
- **Solitude** — capital of Haafingar, a coastal Hold of sea-cliffs in the northwest. Perched on a vast stone arch above the water, Solitude is the largest city in Skyrim and the seat of Imperial power. Ruled by **Elisif the Fair**, young widow of the slain High King.
- **Windhelm** — capital of Eastmarch in the northeast, a land of sulfur springs and snow. The ancient City of Kings is the oldest settlement in Skyrim and the heart of the rebellion, with a poor, resentful quarter of unwelcome Dunmer refugees. Ruled by **Ulfric Stormcloak**, who leads the uprising.
- **Riften** — capital of the Rift, a temperate land of autumn forests in the southeast. A canal-laced wooden town on a lake, Riften reeks of corruption, ruled in name by Jarl **Laila Law-Giver** and in truth by the Black-Briar family and the Thieves Guild hidden beneath its streets.
- **Markarth** — capital of the Reach, a harsh mountain country in the southwest and the native land of the Forsworn. The City of Stone is built into a cliff atop ancient Dwemer ruins, run by the silver-rich Silver-Blood clan under Jarl **Igmund**.
- **Falkreath** — capital of Falkreath Hold, a quiet pine-forest land on the southern border with Cyrodiil and Hammerfell. A lumber town best known for its vast graveyard, ruled by the young and idle Jarl **Siddgeir**.
- **Morthal** — capital of Hjaalmarch, a poor, fog-shrouded marsh in the north. A small, eerie lumber village of little strategic worth, governed by the riddling mystic Jarl **Idgrod Ravencrone**, who rules by her visions.
- **Dawnstar** — capital of the Pale, a frigid tundra Hold on the northern coast. A small mining and fishing port plagued by relentless nightmares, held by the old and fiercely pro-rebel Jarl **Skald the Elder**.
- **Winterhold** — capital of Winterhold Hold, the northernmost and bleakest of all, a coast of ice and fisherfolk. Once a great city, it was mostly swept into the sea by the Great Collapse, leaving little but the still-standing **College of Winterhold**. Ruled by the bitter Jarl **Korir**, who blames the College for the ruin.

A fuller account of each Hold and its corners is the **Warden's** to keep.
