**Sanguinare Vampiris** is the seed of vampirism, contracted from the draining bite or spell of a vampire (save STR to avoid, as any disease). For three days it behaves like the affliction in the table above — a minor wasting that any of the cures above will still wash away.

If those three days pass uncured (at the third dawn after it took hold), the disease blossoms into full **Vampirism** and can no longer be cured by ordinary means; only a specific rite (sought from Falion in Morthal) — or becoming a werewolf — can lift it thereafter. A character who turns is no longer a victim of an affliction but the bearer of a curse, with its own dark strengths and costs; any mundane disease they carried is gone, for the curse leaves them immune.

> Playing a vampire — feeding, sunlight, the Vampire Lord form, and how the curse is finally cured — is covered in Unusual PCs.
