The rule is the Cairn magic rule, unchanged — see the Player's Guide. In brief:

- To cast, hold the Tome in **both hands** (it cannot share a hand with a weapon, shield, or torch), **read the spell aloud**, then add **one Fatigue** to your inventory.
- Given **time and safety**, you may freely _enhance_ the casting — strike more targets, reach farther, hit harder — at no extra cost. A patient mage in a quiet room is a powerful one.
- If you are **Deprived** or **in danger** (combat, a collapsing ruin, a duel of wills), the Warden may call for a **WIL save** to avoid the spell going wrong. Failure costs something on par with the effect: extra Fatigue, a destroyed Tome, injury, or death. Magicka is not a tame thing.
- The numbers below are already converted to Cairn. Damage is a die (never above d12); protection is Armor (never above 3 in all); mind-magic and undead-warding are **saves**, not point totals. A spell that lasts "for the scene" ends with the scene, or sooner if its caster is knocked senseless.

A few words recur:

- **Slowed.** Frost magic numbs its victims. On its next turn a slowed creature can move *or* act, not both.
- **Ablaze.** A creature set ablaze burns as the Hazards chapter describes: 1d4 STR each round until someone spends an action putting it out.
- **Healing has limits.** Magic can mend a given creature's STR only **once per day** — whether by spell, potion, Relic, or blessing; any further magical healing that day closes cuts and bruises (restoring HP) but no more. A surgeon's or herbalist's craft is not magic, and is not counted.
- **Servants.** A summoned or raised servant obeys its caster without question, acts on the caster's turn, and is gone when the scene ends unless its spell says otherwise. Its short stat line gives only what matters in a fight, and it is a lesser thing than the same creature met in the wild — use the spell's numbers, not the Bestiary's. A servant reduced to 0 HP is destroyed.
- **Bound weapons** are magic, not steel. They strike ghosts and other spirits at full force.
