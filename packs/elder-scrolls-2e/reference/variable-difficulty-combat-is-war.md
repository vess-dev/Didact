> **Hjolgeir**, **Qa'zirr**, and **Elsinde** are fleeing a _detachment_ of twelve Hold guards [4 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 12 DEX, 10 WIL, sword (d8), bow (d6), _detachment_] who want them for a Bounty. They have holed up in a burnt-out watchtower at the forest's edge, reachable only by a single ladder. The guards are led by a captain with an officer's sash. They know the party has gone to ground in the tower and are conferring at a safe distance to the north. The _detachment_ is run as a single creature until it takes Critical Damage.

**Warden**: _"The tower's still partly walled, and through the gaps you watch the guards split: six peel off toward the southwest along the treeline, while the rest — the captain among them — move southeast, where the walls are most broken. A pincer. You've less than a minute before they reach the open side of the ruin. What do you do?"_

**Hjolgeir**: _"The Companions have hired out to enough Jarls' wars — I know this game. The one with the sash is their captain. Drop him and the rest may scatter. I'll hold my bow until he's as close as he'll get, then put an arrow in him. If that leaves me exposed, so be it."_

**Elsinde**: _"And what stops them loosing arrows into our hole through those great gaps in the south wall? At best they wait us out until our food runs dry. I'll lean out over the broken wall and read Flames into the southeast group as they come through the gap. They'll be bunched up."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Always so fiery. Qa'zirr will borrow the lamp oil from Elsinde's pack and pour it down the ladder. Effective — and entertaining."_

**Warden**: _"That's fine. But be aware — combat starts the instant Hjolgeir stands to shoot. And since you're all braced and ready, no DEX save: you go first."_

**Warden**: _"The guards keep coming, until at last the captain sweeps his sword overhead and roars, 'Charge!' They surge in. Everything happens at once. Hjolgeir rises and looses at the captain. Since the captain is part of a detachment, Hjolgeir rolls only a d4. Elsinde, your Flames is a blast, so it strikes the detachment at full force: add a Fatigue and roll your d6."_

> Hjolgeir's arrow rolls a 3 and Elsinde's Flames a 4. Both strike the same detachment, so only the higher die counts: 4, less 1 for the guards' Armor. The detachment drops from 4 HP to 1.

**Warden**: _"The arrow skims the captain's shoulder, tearing his bright sash — and at the same moment a sheet of fire rolls through the gap in the south wall. The guards there fall back with their cloaks alight. Qa'zirr, you slop the oil down the ladder, and the first men to reach it slither straight back down into a heap at the bottom. You've bought yourselves a breath — but through the coughing and shouting, you hear the captain: 'Burn it down around them!'"_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Qa'zirr — how well does that oil burn?"_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Very. And Qa'zirr fears we are running short of clever tricks."_

**Elsinde**: _"Well — it's been a pleasure, but I'd sooner die fighting down there than roast up here with you two. No offense."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"None taken. Go for their captain while we make a noise. Every guard in Skyrim has heard the stories about the Companions — so I'm going over the wall howling like one of Hircine's own, and I'll run circles in the smoke."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"And Qa'zirr will be right behind you, roaring like a sabre cat. They will never know which way to run. Qa'zirr prays this works. If not — well, at least we go down howling."_

**Warden**: _"Elsinde, your target and weapon?"_

**Elsinde**: _"The captain — and my dagger."_

**Warden**: _"All right. Elsinde drops down into the smoke and the oil and makes straight for the captain. But she's striking at a detachment, so it's only a d4."_

> Elsinde rolls a 4. The guards' Armor soaks 1, and the remaining 3 takes the detachment's last HP and 2 STR besides, forcing a Critical Damage save on its reduced STR. The Warden rolls a 15: a fail. With their captain cut down, the detachment is broken and routed.

**Warden**: _"Elsinde lunges, and her dagger drives into the captain's side. He folds, and the guards nearest him cry out — some already backing away. At that very moment an unearthly howl goes up: Hjolgeir vaults down from the tower and tears through the smoke on all fours, snarling, with something roaring like a sabre cat right behind him. The guards haul their fallen captain up and flee for the hilltop, shouting 'Werewolves!' and 'They never paid us enough for this!'"_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Told you it would work."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr is fairly certain this was all his doing. But never mind."_

**Elsinde**: _"Who cares? Let's be gone while the going's good."_

**Warden**: _"You've a clear chance to vanish into the forest, or to chance the open fields if you fancy your speed. What do you do?"_
