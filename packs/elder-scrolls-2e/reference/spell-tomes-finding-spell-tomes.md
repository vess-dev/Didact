Treat Spell Tomes as treasure, not equipment to be bought off a list. When a delve or a quest should reward magic, roll or choose a tome that fits the place:

- A **Nordic barrow or Dwemer ruin** yields old, dangerous tomes — Conjuration and Destruction, often warded or trapped.
- A **mage's study, a Court Wizard's chambers, or the College of Winterhold** holds the broadest range, including the Restoration and Alteration arts.
- A **necromancer's lair, a hagraven's nest, or a Daedric shrine** hides the reanimation, soul-trap, and summoning tomes — the kind the Vigil of Stendarr burns on sight.
- A **ruined Imperial fort, a Third-Era scholar's estate, or a Telvanni library** holds the old arts: Mysticism, Open Lock, Levitate, the Oblivion-summons of the Crisis years.
- The strongest spells — the **Master** tomes: Fire Storm, Blizzard, Lightning Storm, Bane of the Undead, Guardian Circle, Dragonhide, Mass Paralysis, Harmony, Hysteria, Mayhem, Call to Arms, the three Thralls, and Dead Thrall — should be **earned**: the prize at the end of a quest, never shelf-stock.

A single tome is a fine reward. A scattered library is a campaign.
