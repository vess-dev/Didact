- **Spell Tome:** hold in both hands, read aloud → cast, then add **1 Fatigue**. Under duress or while Deprived, a WIL save or suffer ill effects. Takes one slot.
- **Scroll:** _petty_, no Fatigue, one use, then gone.
- **Relic:** charged item with a **Recharge** condition; no Fatigue. (Includes the Daedric Artifacts.)
