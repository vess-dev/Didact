title: "Hit Protection"
tags: [creating, monsters]
source: wardens-guide/creating-monsters#hit-protection
---
A creature's knack for avoiding harm — through toughness, speed, or skill. Give an ordinary person 2–4 HP, a soldier or a beast 3–6, a serious threat 7–12, and a legend — an elder dragon, a Dragon Priest — 12 to 18. Nothing in Skyrim needs more than 18.
