Use this procedure for any expanse of wilderness in a region. If a contradiction or a tempting pattern turns up, feel free to restate or bend any earlier result.

### Materials Required

- A sheet of paper and a notebook. Letter or A4 is fine.
- Pencils and erasers. Colored pencils or crayons help.
- At least one d6 and one d20; 6d6 and 2d20 are ideal.

> From here on, the sheet of paper is the **map**.
> As with every rule in this book, if a table result does not suit you, roll again or change it.

### Overview

1. Describe the wild.
2. Describe the spirit of the wild.
3. Create the wild's POIs.
4. Create the wild's trails.
5. Create wilderness encounters.
6. Name the wild.
