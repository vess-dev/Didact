**What it is.** A **Forsworn warrior remade** by the deepest rite of the Reach. A hagraven cut the living heart from your breast and pressed a thorned, poisoned briar into the wet hollow, binding it there by her craft. You are not undead and you still bleed — ordinary healing mends you as it mends any mortal — but where your heart should beat there is a knot of briar that lends you tireless strength, at the price of unending pain and a leash held by the crone who made you. (See the Briarheart background.)

**How you become one.** You do not contract this — it is done *to* you. A hagraven of the Reach performs the rite, tearing out your heart and seating the briar in its place; you rise stronger than any mortal and bound to her will. You begin play already remade, the briar lodged behind your ribs. Your **WIL is your own**, undimmed.

### Edges
- **Tireless vigor.** You need **no sleep** and are **never Deprived by exhaustion**; a short rest still restores HP as the briar floods you with stolen strength.
- **The heart's strength.** You never need a save to **lift, hold, or shove** anything a strong mortal could manage with real effort, and a single blow never staggers you or drives you back.

### Costs / Drawbacks
- **The thorn behind the ribs.** Pull the briar free — from a helpless or willing Briarheart, or by a knowing hand during Downtime — and you **die at once**: no Critical Damage save, no second chance. This is the only sure way to end you, and your enemies in the Reach know it.
- **The matron's leash.** The hagraven who made you can feel where you are and may **compel, summon, or curse** you; her death does not free the briar, only the leash. The Warden may pull this leash as the terms of your binding.
- **A thing of the briar.** Mortals who know your nature treat you **one step worse** on the Reaction ladder; the Vigil of Stendarr counts you a thing to be put down (**Vigil of Stendarr −1**). The Forsworn, by contrast, may shelter you as kin.
- **No rest from the ache.** Ordinary Restoration mends your wounds, but **nothing lifts the constant pain** of the thorn; the Warden may call for a **WIL save** when the agony spikes at the worst moment.
