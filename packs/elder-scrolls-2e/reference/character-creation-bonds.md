| d20 | Bond |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | You carry a **soul gem** (_petty_) holding the trapped soul of someone you loved. A necromancer in the Reach claims he can free them — for a price you do not want to pay.                  |
| **2**   | A dying Imperial courier pressed a sealed, ciphered **dispatch** (_petty_) into your hands, meant for General Tullius. You cannot read it, but men have already died for it.                |
| **3**   | You owe a blood-debt to the Companions of Jorrvaskr. Take a shield-brother's **iron ring** (_petty_); any Companion will know you by it, for good or ill.                                    |
| **4**   | You found a warm **dragon's scale** (_petty_) in the ash of a burned village. It grows warmer the farther north you carry it.                                                                |
| **5**   | You escaped a Thalmor cell with a dead Justiciar's **signet** (_petty_). It opens doors among the Aldmeri Dominion — and marks you for death if the wrong elf recognizes it.                 |
| **6**   | A Khajiit caravan master once saved your life. Take a pouch of **Moon Sugar** (_petty_) and a debt of hospitality: any caravan will shelter you once, no questions asked.                    |
| **7**   | You wear a **bear-claw amulet** of the Old Gods (_petty_). Kyne's hunters treat you as kin; the priests of the Divines eye you with suspicion.                                               |
| **8**   | You woke in a Nordic barrow with no memory of how you came there, clutching an **ancient iron key** (_petty_) that fits no lock you have ever found.                                          |
| **9**   | You were once a slave in Morrowind. You still bear the brand, and a hidden **shiv** (d6), and a hatred of chains that has never cooled.                                                       |
| **10**  | A College mage entrusted you with a **warded journal** (_petty_) before she vanished into Blackreach. It writes back, in a hand that is not your own.                                        |
| **11**  | You carry the **knucklebones of a murdered friend** (_petty_). The Dark Brotherhood believes a death is owed for it. They have not yet decided whether it is yours.                          |
| **12**  | A Daedric Prince marked you in a dream. Once per session, you may ask the Warden one yes-or-no question about what a Daedra desires. The answer is always true — and the Prince always wants something back.|
| **13**  | A Greybeard once breathed a single Word into your ear. You do not understand it — but animals fall silent and still when you draw near.                                                       |
| **14**  | You hold the deed to a ruined **homestead** in a Hold of your choice, burned in the war. Rebuilding it would cost a fortune and stir up old enemies.                                         |
| **15**  | You pried a **frostbitten ring** (_petty_) from a frozen corpse high on the Throat of the World. It will not come off, and your fingers no longer feel the cold.                            |
| **16**  | A Volkihar vampire spared you and called you "useful." Take a **blood-red phial** (_petty_); shatter it and one of Harkon's court will come, once, to collect what you owe.                  |
| **17**  | You carry a volume of the **Mythic Dawn Commentaries** (_petty_), Mankar Camoran's scripture from the Oblivion Crisis. The Vigil of Stendarr would burn you with it, and something in the margins keeps rewriting itself. |
| **18**  | You served a Jarl who is now dead. Take their housecarl's **axe** (d8) and a duty: their heir is hidden somewhere in Skyrim, and you swore on your life to find them.                         |
| **19**  | A Forsworn hagraven cursed your bloodline: mirrors crack at your reflection. But in the shards, you can see the briar-hearts hidden in the breasts of men.                                   |
| **20**  | You traded something to a Daedra and cannot recall what. You sleep without dreams, cast no shadow at dusk, and the **black soul gem** (_petty_) in your pack refuses to stay empty.|
