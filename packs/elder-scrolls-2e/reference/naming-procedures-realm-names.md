For a Hold, a petty kingdom, or a contested domain in the Civil War:

1. Roll on the Realm Names Formula.
2. Roll on the Adjectives, Nouns, Terrain Synonyms, and Realm Types, adding "of" or "the" where it reads well.

> Optionally replace any value with the dominant terrain synonym, e.g. *The Frostbitten Reach.*

### Realm Names Formula

| d6 | Formula |
|---|---|
| 1 | (The) **[Noun]** **[Realm]** |
| 2 | (The) **[Adjective]** **[Realm]** |
| 3 | **[Realm]** of (the) **[Noun]** |
| 4 | **[Adjective]** **[Realm]** of (the) **[Noun]** |
| 5 | (The) **[Noun]** **[Adjective]** **[Realm]** |
| 6 | **[Realm]** of (the) **[Adjective]** **[Noun]** |
