Skyrim is a sick land as often as a savage one. A skeever's bite, a draught of foul tundra water, the filth on a chaurus' mandibles — any of these can leave a lingering ailment in the blood. A **disease** is not a wound that heals with rest and a drink. It is a named, minor affliction that clings to you, dragging at your body or your magic until it is properly **cured**.
