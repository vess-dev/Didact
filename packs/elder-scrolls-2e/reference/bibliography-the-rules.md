The Elder Scrolls 2e is built on the chassis of **Cairn Second Edition** by **Yochai Gal**, released under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** license (CC BY-SA 4.0). Every good idea in the mechanics — the three-stat saves, the slot inventory, automatic-hit combat, HP-as-luck, Scars, Fatigue, detachments, the casting rule — comes from there. The game text at **cairnrpg.com** is the reference. Cairn in turn stands on *Into the Odd* and *Knave*; their lineage of light, fiction-first design is felt on every page.
