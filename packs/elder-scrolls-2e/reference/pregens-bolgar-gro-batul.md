6 HP, 2 Armor, 15 STR, 10 DEX, 8 WIL, masterwork Orcish war axe (d10), forge hammer (d6)

- **Orsimer Stronghold Warrior**, age 35. **Berserker Rage:** once per day, make one attack _Enhanced_.
- **Background:** you held the stronghold's gate alone while the clan fled the giants; you need not save against fear while you stand in a doorway, a gate, or a narrow pass. Your mother forged your war axe herself (**masterwork**, d10), and every Orc smith knows her mark.
- **Carries:** masterwork Orcish war axe, forge hammer, Orcish mail (2 Armor, _bulky_), ancient iron key (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), Torch (3 uses), 8 Septims. _(6 slots)_
- **Bond:** you woke in a Nordic barrow with no memory of how you came there, clutching an **ancient iron key** that fits no lock you have ever found.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Steed — your Orcish mail is not _bulky_ for you.
- **Who you are:** a mountain of a smith-warrior from Dushnikh Yal, slow to anger and unstoppable once roused, who left the stronghold to find the lock his key was made for.
