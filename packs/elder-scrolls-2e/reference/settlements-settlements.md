Sooner or later a party puts down roots. It starts as a barrow they cleared and a stretch of tundra the Jarl will sell, a crossroads farm they defended, a homestead willed to them by a dying woodsman. The settlement system lets a Warden track what a place *is* — and how it fares as the Civil War rolls over the Holds — without turning the game into a ledger of nails and firewood.

A settlement has three attributes, each on a 1–5 scale: **Population**, **Defense**, **Prosperity**. It has up to **eight building slots** by default, with room to grow. _petty_ additions — the small comforts of a steading, a well, a garden patch, a roadside shrine — do not consume a slot. That is the whole model.
