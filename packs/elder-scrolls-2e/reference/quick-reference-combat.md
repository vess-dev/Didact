- **Side initiative.** Each side acts together; results are simultaneous.
- **First round:** each PC makes a **DEX save** to act, or loses the turn.
- On your turn: **move ~40 ft and take one action.**
- **Attacks auto-hit.** Roll weapon die − Armor = damage to HP. Multiple attackers on one foe: roll all, **keep the highest**.
- **Impaired** = roll **d4** (cover, bound, long range; unarmed is d4). **Enhanced** = roll **d12** (helpless foe, daring move; never stacks). Both at once cancel: roll the weapon's own die. **_blast_** = roll vs. each target in the area. **Two weapons** = roll both, keep higher (e.g. d6+d6).
- **Armor** caps at **3**. Body armor: light 1, mail 2 (_bulky_), plate 3 (_bulky_). Shield and helmet +1 each, while held or worn.
