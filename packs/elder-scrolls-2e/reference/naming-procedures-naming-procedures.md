Two problems land on a Warden mid-session: a stranger speaks and needs a name *right now*, and the party crests a hill and asks what the ruin below is called. This chapter solves both. The first half names **people, by race** — because a Nord, a Dunmer, and a Khajiit are named on completely different principles. The second half is a fast generator for **places, factions, and realms** across Skyrim.
