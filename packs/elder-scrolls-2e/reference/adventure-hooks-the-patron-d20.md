| d20 | Who's Asking |
|---|---|
| 1 | A **Jarl's steward**, hiring quietly so the court need not know the Jarl is worried. |
| 2 | A **Hold guard captain** with a bounty notice and not enough swords to serve it. |
| 3 | An **innkeeper** passing on a regular's trouble for the price of a room. |
| 4 | A **frightened farmer or miller** who has scraped together what coin they can. |
| 5 | A **merchant or caravan-master** who has lost goods, a guard, or a debtor. |
| 6 | A **College mage** who needs something fetched, cleared, or quietly studied. |
| 7 | A **priest of the Divines** (or a lone shrine-keeper) with a holy errand. |
| 8 | A **Companion** at Jorrvaskr handing down a contract too small for the Circle. |
| 9 | A **Thieves Guild fixer** (Delvin or Vex's sort) with a job and no questions. |
| 10 | A **Vigilant of Stendarr** hunting a Daedra, a vampire, or a witch. |
| 11 | A **Stormcloak officer** who needs a deniable hand in the war. |
| 12 | An **Imperial legate** who needs the same, for the other side. |
| 13 | A **mysterious hooded stranger** — a Daedric servant, or a Thalmor cut-out. |
| 14 | A **grieving relative** seeking a body, a killer, or a stolen keepsake. |
| 15 | A **court wizard** out of a Jarl's earshot, needing a delicate thing done. |
| 16 | A **stronghold chief** (Orc) calling on outsiders for a clan matter. |
| 17 | A **bard or the Bards College** chasing a lost verse, relic, or scandal. |
| 18 | A **fellow adventurer**, dying or maimed, passing on their unfinished job. |
| 19 | A **courier** with a sealed letter and no idea who sent it. |
| 20 | **Someone from the party's past.** Warden's pick. The debt is real. |
