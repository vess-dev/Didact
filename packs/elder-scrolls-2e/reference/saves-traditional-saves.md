### Strength

> Hjolgeir charges down a flooded passage of an old Nordic barrow, a bundled-up survivor clutched to his chest. Qa'zirr and Elsinde are at his heels, holding off a hulking bandit chief and his snarling war-hound.

**Hjolgeir**: _"These are innocents we're carrying — they don't have to die!"_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr can hear nothing over this dog!"_

**Warden**: _"The chief suddenly stops, whistles his hound back, and yanks a lever in the wall. A grinding roar starts up from the east end of the hall — and you see it: an old iron portcullis grinding down from the ceiling. If it shuts, you're sealed in this stretch of the barrow with him. Hjolgeir, you can clear it easily. Qa'zirr and Elsinde each need to save DEX to slip through in time."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"I'll stop and hold the gate as long as I can. What's the risk?"_

**Warden**: _"That iron is heavy. It could crush your arms, or pin you beneath it."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"I shove the bundle through to the far side and brace under the gate. Qa'zirr, Elsinde — go. I've got this."_

> The others agree, and Hjolgeir rolls a 13: a success.

**Warden**: _"You roar as the bars grind down onto your shoulders. Just when it feels like the gate will fold you in half, Qa'zirr and Elsinde scramble past to safety. You heave back, and the portcullis slams to the stone so hard the mechanism shatters. No going back now — but the chief's not getting through either."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"There's iron in a Nord's back for a reason. Now — wait for me!"_

### Dexterity

> Qa'zirr is crouched in the rafters of a run-down Riften tavern, watching a knot of armed thugs drinking below.

**Warden**: _"After twenty cramped minutes, your patience pays off: one of the men below says aloud where their hideout lies. That's what you came for. Now what?"_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr must vanish before these fools know a cat was ever here. Do they look near to leaving?"_

**Warden**: _"One of them is shuffling a deck of cards, and the others are settling in. They'll be here a good while yet."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Then no sense waiting. Qa'zirr creeps back the way he came."_

**Warden**: _"Fine — but the room you crept through earlier is empty no longer. There's a serving-girl below now. Save DEX to slip out unseen."_

> Qa'zirr rolls a 16: a fail.

**Warden**: _"As you ease across the last beam, it groans. All six men snap their heads up in time to see you dart through the crawlspace toward the kitchens. They didn't get a clean look at your face — but they know someone's up there. They shout and reach for their blades."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr does not stay to be introduced. He drops into the kitchen, finds the back door, and is gone before they reach the stairs."_

### Willpower

> Elsinde has been taken prisoner after helping a client try to break into the sealed Dwemer vault beneath Understone Keep in Markarth. In a sudden fit of nerve, she let the guards seize her so the others could escape.

**Hjolgeir**: _"Shame about Elsinde. But the client has to reach that vault tonight. We free her after."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr does not abandon his own. Go, both of you — but Qa'zirr will get her out. A burglar of the Guild has friends in every gutter, no? Or some thieves' cant to open the right doors here."_

**Warden**: _"He does. You can spend the night working your contacts among the Guild's people in Markarth, if you like."_

> The party splits. Qa'zirr learns what he needs from a local fence — it costs him his dagger, and he is sour about it. Elsinde, meanwhile, sits in a holding cell beneath Understone Keep awaiting sentence.

**Warden**: _"In the gray of early morning, a folded scrap is slid through the cell grate. It reads: 'Speak these words and you go free — "The Pale Man Longs To Die." You have one chance.'"_

**Elsinde**: _"...What? Someone left me this. And I'm to say it aloud — but when? To whom?"_

**Warden**: _"Footsteps. The jailer is coming, keys jingling, as if to open your cell. What do you do?"_

**Elsinde**: _"This isn't the right person, I think. I'll try to be passed up the chain."_

**Warden**: _"He opens the cell and waves you out."_

**Elsinde**: _"Good sir! Might I have a word with your superior? I carry news they'd find very much worth hearing..."_

**Warden**: _"The jailer smirks and lifts a pair of manacles."_

**Elsinde**: _"'THE PALE MAN LONGS TO DIE!'"_

**Warden**: _"He stops dead. The manacles drop to his side. One nod, and he gestures for you to follow."_

**Elsinde**: _"Lucky. Some watchword among jailers? Whatever it is — I follow."_

**Warden**: _"He leads you deep into the dark of the prison, far from any torch. At the far wall he raps three times on a stone above his head. After a moment the wall swings inward, and he strides through into a small chamber where a dark-haired woman waits at a desk. She smiles and waves you to the chair across from her."_

**Elsinde**: _"May I return to my cell? — I jest. I sit."_

**Warden**: _"The jailer withdraws as you take the seat. One door behind the woman; a very large man stands beyond it, his face framed in a little window. She smiles again. 'Welcome, Mistress... well, the name hardly matters. You've spoken a phrase known only to thieves and worse. So which are you, I wonder?'"_

**Elsinde**: _"What now? I've nothing to trade... except the truth, perhaps. Which means a WIL save to seem convincing, yes?"_

**Warden**: _"You'd need the WIL save even speaking plain truth — you're plainly the one most at risk here, and for all your College polish, you sense she reads liars for a living. You two aren't so unalike."_

**Elsinde**: _"Very well. I'll be straight with you, madam. My companions and I came to slip a client into the Dwemer vault below the Keep. I learned that phrase minutes ago — a friend of mine, a Khajiit burglar named Qa'zirr, surely left me the note. Set me loose and I'll be sure to put in a good word for... your name, again?"_

**Warden**: _"Endrith. And please — tell me everything."_

> Elsinde lays out the party's plan and how far they've gotten, then makes a WIL save. She rolls a 5: a success. Endrith is convinced.

**Warden**: _"As she listens, Endrith frowns, then breaks into a smile. 'All right — I believe you. Let's strike a bargain. Introduce me to this client you've so kindly walked into my city, and I'll make certain no one is looking when you slip away. But we move now, before this whole scheme caves in. And if it does — even I can't promise your hide.'"_

> An hour on, Elsinde finds Qa'zirr — but not the others. He explains that Hjolgeir and the client have already left for the vault. He'll guide them there, but says he needs to make one quick stop along the way. Something about a certain dagger he means to get back...
