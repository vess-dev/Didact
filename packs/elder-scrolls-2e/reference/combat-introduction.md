A fight in The Elder Scrolls 2e is quick, chaotic, and frequently fatal. A handful of principles set its combat apart from most other games:

- **Side Initiative**: Apart from the opening round — where each PC must first save DEX to seize their turn — the party and their foes alternate as whole sides. Everything one side does resolves at the same moment.
- **Attacks Automatically Hit**: There is no roll to see whether a blow lands; you simply roll for damage. When several attackers gang up on the same target, only the single highest damage die is applied.
- **Hit Protection and Scars**: HP stands in for skill, grit, endurance, and plain luck, and it returns on its own once a PC is safe and rested. Damage that drops a PC to exactly 0 HP earns them a **Scar**, which can cut either way.
- **Critical Damage**: Damage carrying a target below zero is taken out of their STR by the leftover amount. They then immediately save STR — using the reduced score — to stave off Critical Damage. Failure means they are out of the fight.
