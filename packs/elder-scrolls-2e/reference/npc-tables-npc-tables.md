A nameless face the party did not expect to talk to suddenly matters. These tables build one in a few seconds: roll a **name**, a **quirk**, a **background**, a **goal**, and a **virtue** and a **vice**. That is enough to play almost anyone in Skyrim.

**First, pick a race.** Roll on the table below or choose to fit the place — a court in Solitude skews Nord and Imperial; the Gray Quarter of Windhelm is Dunmer; a caravan camped outside the gates is Khajiit; a smuggler's hold runs to Argonians and Bosmer. Then take the name from that race's column. The naming conventions — surnames, bynames, clan prefixes — live in Naming Procedures; the given names here are gathered for a fast draw.
