Each of the three **Attributes** is used in different circumstances. (See **Saves**, below.)

- **Strength (STR)**: Used for saves requiring physical power, like lifting a portcullis, bending bars, resisting poison or disease, shrugging off the cold, etc.
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: Used for saves requiring poise, speed, reflexes, dodging, climbing, sneaking, balancing, etc.
- **Willpower (WIL)**: Used for saves to persuade, deceive, interrogate, intimidate, charm, provoke, resist fear, and channel a Spell Tome under pressure, etc.
