5 HP, 2 Armor, 13 STR, 12 DEX, 10 WIL, Imperial sword (d8), Forsworn axe (d8)

- **Imperial Legionnaire**, age 38. **Voice of the Emperor:** once per day, re-roll a failed WIL save made to calm, persuade, or bargain.
- **Background:** you spent years in the hills of the Reach putting down the Forsworn, and you never walk blind into their snares or rockfall ambushes. The Legion's shield-wall drill is in your bones: once per combat, brace your shield with an ally beside you, and you both gain +1 Armor (to the cap of 3) until your next turn.
- **Carries:** Imperial sword, captured Forsworn axe, Imperial shield (+1 Armor), Imperial light armor (1 Armor), Legion field manual (_petty_), sealed dispatch (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), Torch (3 uses), 14 Septims. _(6 slots)_
- **Bond:** a dying courier pressed a ciphered **dispatch** into your hands, meant for General Tullius. You cannot read it, but men have already died for it.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Lover.
- **Who you are:** a tired, dutiful soldier who believes in the Empire more than the Empire believes in itself, and would like one war to end before the next begins.
