1. Roll on the Faction Names Formula.
2. Roll on the Adjectives, Nouns, and Group Types where the formula calls for them.

For a more Skyrim ring, swap a rolled noun for a guild touchstone — *Silver, Shadow, Dawn, Dusk, Talos, Frost, Snow, Ash, the Hand, the Voice* — or lean on a Nordic group name (*Sons of Skyrim, Sworn, the Reachfolk*).

### Faction Names Formula

| d6 | Formula |
|---|---|
| 1 | (The) **[Noun]** **[Group]** |
| 2 | (The) **[Adjective]** **[Group]** |
| 3 | **[Group]** of (the) **[Noun]** |
| 4 | **[Adjective]** **[Group]** of (the) **[Noun]** |
| 5 | (The) **[Noun]** **[Adjective]** **[Group]** |
| 6 | **[Group]** of (the) **[Adjective]** **[Noun]** |

*Examples: the Silver Hand · the Frostbitten Vigil · Order of the Hollow Crown · Sons of the Broken Stone.*
