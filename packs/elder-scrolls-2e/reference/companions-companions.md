A **companion** is someone who travels with you for reasons beyond coin — a housecarl sworn to your name, a mercenary who decided the road with you beat the road alone, a guild ally who owes you, a beast you tamed, a servant you raised or conjured. A **hireling** works for pay, takes Morale saves, and walks off when the Septims stop (see **Hirelings** in the Core Rules). A companion stays because the story made them stay.

Each PC may have **one active companion** at a time. A second willing follower will still walk, fight, camp, and share loot, but they will not develop — they drift off by the next week's rest unless the first companion leaves first. (In the fiction, the road is hard and two sworn shadows is one too many.) A party may keep any number of hirelings *alongside* their companions, but only companions have **Loyalty** and the **Command** action.
