Roll a race, a name, a quirk, a background, a goal, and one virtue and one vice. Read the results together for a breath and a person usually steps out of them — *a gaunt Dunmer scribe (background) who wants forbidden knowledge (goal), learned but selfish; she squints over a candle in the Arcanaeum and will trade a secret for a secret.* When you need a quick stat block to go with the face, see Creating Monsters for ability-score ranges; most ordinary folk are 10s across the board, **3 HP**, no Armor, and one improvised weapon.

A few Skyrim notes:

- **Reactions and race.** Nords are wary of mages and openly cold toward Dunmer, Argonians, and "the elves" generally; the Gray Quarter and the docks are where the beastfolk and ash-folk are pushed. Let a rolled vice of **Bigoted** shift the Reaction one step worse toward the wrong PC — and let an unprejudiced NPC stand out for it.
- **The war is everywhere.** Most NPCs have a quiet lean toward the Stormcloaks or the Empire, even the ones who say they don't. A merchant overcharges a Legion soldier; a guard mutters about Talos. The party's own faction standing rides on who is listening.
- **Names are not surnames.** Many Skyrim folk go by an earned byname — *Crag-Walker, Half-Hand, the Quiet, Two-Winters.* Hang one on a memorable NPC and the table will remember them forever.
