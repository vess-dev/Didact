- Resting for a few moments and having a drink of water (or mead) restores lost HP but may leave the party exposed. Bandages can stabilize a character that has taken critical damage.
- Attribute loss (see **Critical Damage**) can usually be restored with a week's rest, facilitated by a healer, a temple of the Divines, or another appropriate source of expertise.
- Some healing services are free, while restoration magic or more expedient means of recovery may come at a cost.
