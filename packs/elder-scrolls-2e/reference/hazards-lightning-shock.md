A storm-struck crag, a Dwemer arc-pylon, a shock rune, a charged Destruction bolt: **1d6 STR** damage and a **DEX save**, **dropping whatever is held** in hand on a fail.

**Metal armor does not help** — it conducts the charge. A Resist Shock enchantment, a grounded stance, or insulating gear does.

> Picking through a flooded Dwemer hall, a thief brushes a humming pylon and takes the discharge: **1d6 STR**. She fails the DEX save and her lockpicks fly from her grip into the dark water. Her Dwarven mail did nothing — it only carried the jolt.
