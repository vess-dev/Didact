**What it is.** A second strain of Hircine's curse, thick-furred and brown, native to the cold reaches of **Solstheim**. Werebears are larger and hardier than their wolf-cousins — slower, but able to shrug off blows that would fell a werewolf. The two beasts loathe each other and attack on sight. (In Skyrim canon the gift is rarely offered to outsiders; a Warden who allows a werebear PC should treat the change as a hard-won Solstheim secret. Use the Werewolf background as the template.)

**How you become one.** As Lycanthropy above — a willing rite, taken on through werebear blood among the Skaal or Solstheim's beast-cults rather than the Companions. It is permanent, immune to disease, and cured or displaced by the same means.

### Edges, Costs, and Form
A werebear shares **every edge and every cost of the Werewolf** above — Beast Blood, the once-per-day Change, silver as its bane, no gear or speech while Transformed, the fresh kill afterward, Reactions a step worse where known — with these differences:

- **The bear's vigor.** You gain a **permanent +1 max HP**, even in mortal form.

> **Werebear, Beast Form** — *Transformed; lasts the scene or until you revert.* Claws **d8+d8**, or a bite **d8**; **+1 Armor** from heavy hide (to the cap of 3). You are mighty but lumbering: you do **not** gain the werewolf's terrifying speed. Once per transformation you may give a **bellowing roar**: every enemy who hears it makes a **WIL save** or flees for the rest of the round.

- As the werewolf, you revert at scene's end, or earlier on a **WIL save**.
