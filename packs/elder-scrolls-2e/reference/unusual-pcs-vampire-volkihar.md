**What it is.** A mortal whose blood has been replaced by the curse of Molag Bal. The vampires of Skyrim trace to the Volkihar — an ancient, aristocratic clan dwelling in a frozen castle off the Sea of Ghosts. A vampire does not age and does not sicken, but the sun is poison to them and they hunger always for the blood of the living. (See the Vampire Fledgling background.)

**How you become one.** You contract **Sanguinare Vampiris** from a vampire's draining bite or spell (a STR save to resist, like any disease). If it goes uncured for three days, it blossoms into full Vampirism — see Diseases & Afflictions. Thereafter ordinary cures no longer work: only the rite sought from Falion in Morthal — or taking on Lycanthropy, since the two curses cannot share one body — will lift it.

### Edges
- **The Blood Sustains.** Once per day, when you **feed** — biting a sleeping, willing, or helpless victim — you **clear all Fatigue** and shed the **Deprived** condition, as a full night's rest would. A fresh kill is not required: the living victim survives, drained and weak, having lost **1d4 STR** (which returns with rest, like any Attribute loss).
- **Undying.** You do not age and are **immune to all mundane disease**; you never save against the afflictions in the disease table. Sanguinare Vampiris cannot take root in one already cursed.
- **Drain.** Your bite or draining touch is an unarmed attack that deals **d6** and *ignores Armor*. Once per combat — or whenever the victim is willing or helpless — the HP it deals is **restored to you** (never above your maximum). On a willing or helpless victim the drain is _Enhanced_.
- **Vampire's Sight.** You see in the dark as if by daylight.
- **The Long Cold.** Cold and snow alone never leave you **Deprived**; the chill of the grave is already in you.

### Costs / Drawbacks
- **Sunlight withers you.** While standing in direct sunlight you are **Deprived** (you cannot recover HP, Attributes, or clear Fatigue), and your **drain** restores no HP. Heavy cloth and shade hold it off; an open noon sky does not.
- **Fire is your bane.** Any **fire attack against you is _Enhanced_** (roll d12 damage).
- **The hunger.** If you go a **full day without feeding**, you become **Deprived** until you do. Feeding is the only thing that lifts this particular Deprivation.
- **A thing of the night.** If your nature is known or revealed, mortals' **Reactions toward you shift one step worse** on the Reaction ladder. The Dawnguard and the Vigil of Stendarr are your enemies by nature: you count as **Volkihar +1** and **Dawnguard / Vigil of Stendarr −1**, and either drops to **Hunted** the moment it learns what you are.
- **No healer mends what blood must.** Restoration meant for the living still works on you, but a Cure Disease shrine or potion does **not** lift the curse itself — only Falion's rite does.

### Vampire Lord (optional form)
A vampire who accepts Molag Bal's deeper gift — from Lord Harkon, or from Serana — can take on the **Vampire Lord**: a towering, winged, clawed blood-mage. Taking the form is **once per day** and costs **one Fatigue**. While it lasts you are **Transformed**: your worn gear and Spell Tomes fall away (only enchanted rings and amulets still function), and you fight with the form's own powers.

> **Vampire Lord** — *Transformed; lasts the scene.* Claws **d8+d8**, or a drain touch **d8** (*ignores Armor*, HP dealt is restored to you). **+1 Armor** from unnatural hide (to the cap of 3). You **hover** above the ground, untroubled by difficult footing, and out of reach of grounded foes who cannot reach up. Once per transformation, **raise a nearby corpse** as a servant (3 HP, its own weapon or d4) that crumbles at the scene's end. Fire attacks against the Lord remain _Enhanced_, and the form is just as helpless in sunlight as the vampire who wears it.
