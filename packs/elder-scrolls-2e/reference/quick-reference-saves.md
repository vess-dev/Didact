---
- Roll **1d20, equal-or-under** the Attribute to succeed. Natural **1** always succeeds; natural **20** always fails.
- The one **most at risk** rolls. For a group effort, the **worst** relevant Attribute rolls.
- No modifiers. An ability may let you **skip** a save, **re-roll** a failed one, or **pass** it automatically — never add to the roll.
- STR: force, endurance, resist poison/disease/**cold**. DEX: speed, stealth, balance, aim. WIL: persuade, resist fear/charm, channel a Spell Tome under pressure.
