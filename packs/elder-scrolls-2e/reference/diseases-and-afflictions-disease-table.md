When a source in the **Source** column wounds or exposes you, save STR or be afflicted. The **Effect** persists until cured.

| Disease | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Rockjoint** | Foxes, wolves | Your joints stiffen and ache: your **attacks in the first round of any combat are _Impaired_** (roll 1d4 damage) while your limbs loosen. |
| **Ataxia** | Skeevers | Your hands tremble: **picking locks, picking pockets, and other fine handwork always require a DEX save**, even at leisure. |
| **Witbane** | Sabre cats (also dogs) | Your mind clouds: you **cannot channel a Spell Tome without first passing a WIL save**, even in safety. |
| **Brain Rot** | Hagravens (also risen corpses) | Magicka festers in you: whenever you channel a Spell Tome, **make a WIL save or take an extra Fatigue** from the effort. |
| **Bone Break Fever** | Bears | A wracking ache settles in: you **carry one Fatigue that rest cannot clear** (drop an item if you have no free slot). |
| **Rattles** | Chaurus (Hunters & Reapers) | Your limbs shudder and tire: a **short rest no longer restores your HP** — only a full night's rest does. |
| **Brown Rot** | Draugr | Your skin blotches and your gear seems to rot with you: your **body armor gives 1 less Armor** (to a minimum of 0). |
| **Droops** | Ash hoppers | Your arms hang heavy: **attacks with a _bulky_ weapon are _Impaired_**. |
| **Greenspore** | Slaughterfish | A green bloom creeps over your skin: **strangers' Reactions toward you shift one step worse**. |
| **Sanguinare Vampiris** | Vampires (their draining touch) | The vampire's curse takes root. Your **maximum HP is reduced by 1** while it festers, and if left uncured it **becomes Vampirism after three days** (see below). |
