Each slot is a single named structure or function the settlement runs. The party decides what fills a slot, and a slot can be upgraded, torn down, or repurposed. Filling a slot costs **a Downtime Action and a sum in Septims** (the Warden sets the figure from the fiction — a watchtower is dearer than a chicken coop), or the equivalent in traded goods, hauled timber, and honest labor. _petty_ comforts cost coin and effort but no slot.

The menu below is what a Nord steading might plausibly pile onto a cleared plot. The Warden may invent others.

### Defense

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Palisade & Gate** | Defense +1. Required for any Defense above 2. |
| **Watchtower** | +1 Defense if paired with a Palisade. Lookouts stand rotating watches; raids are spotted one watch early. |
| **Guard Post** | Defense +1. A named, paid warrior on a shift schedule. The militia's weapons step up one tier. |
| **Barracks (housecarls)** | +1 Defense. Quarters a small standing guard. The settlement may field a detachment in its own defense without the party present. |
| **Armory & Forge-rack** | +1 Defense. The militia's gear steps up one tier, and a PC in good standing may draw one common weapon from the marketplace list per visit (limit one per PC per season). |

### Production & Prosperity

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Farm Plots** (wheat, cabbage, leeks, gourds) | +1 Prosperity. Rations are free for the party in good standing. Crops fail in an **Extreme** weather week or a hard winter. |
| **Livestock Pen** (cattle, goats, chickens) | +1 Population. The settlement feeds itself; caravans and herders stop here freely. |
| **Mill** (lumber or grain) | +1 Prosperity. Produces trade-grade timber or flour as a standing resource; a river or steady wind helps. |
| **Mine** (iron, silver, corundum, quicksilver) | +1 Prosperity. A passive trickle: a Downtime Action here yields ore and the odd raw gem. Stalls if the vein runs dry (Warden's call). |
| **Apothecary's Garden** | _petty_ building. A plot of alchemy reagents. A PC may gather a handful of common ingredients here each visit without foraging. |
| **Trading Post** | +1 Prosperity. Caravans route through automatically; the resident merchant restocks when the party visits, and a PC may sell one unused item at full market price once per session. |

### Craft & Hearth

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Smithy** (forge, anvil, grindstone, workbench) | +1 Prosperity. Unlocks the **Smithing** Downtime Action on-site — see Player's Guide → Crafting. A resident smith may temper one piece of the party's gear per visit. |
| **Alchemy Lab** | Unlocks the **Alchemy** Downtime Action on-site. A potion or poison brewed here costs half the usual reagents per batch. |
| **Arcane Enchanter** | Unlocks the **Enchanting** Downtime Action on-site, given a filled soul gem (see Crafting). Often built only where a College mage or hedge-wizard has settled. |
| **Cookfire & Smokehouse** | Raw meat from the bestiary becomes lasting rations here; attached to Farm Plots or a Pen, it keeps every settler fed. |

### Hearth & Hall

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Longhouse / Mead Hall** | The settlement's heart. Reaction rolls on a first visit shift one step better, and the party may always hear a fresh rumor here, Warden's pick. A PC may make a Downtime *Strengthening Ties* action with the whole settlement here. |
| **Temple / Shrine to the Divines** | A priest tends the sick: settlers recover Attribute loss in three days instead of a week, and a visiting PC may receive a free Cure Disease blessing once per visit. The town automatically passes WIL saves against panic during a raid. |
| **Inn** | A bed for travelers and a hearth for gossip. Reaction rolls on a first visit shift one step better; caravans and wayfarers carry the settlement's name abroad (a slow trickle toward local renown). |
| **Bard's Hearth** | _petty_ building. A resident skald or a visiting bard. The settlement's defending detachment automatically passes its first Morale save per raid; spirits hold and stories spread. |

### Folk & Land

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Cottages (beds)** | +1 Population. Without beds, new settlers do not stay. May be built more than once; each fills a slot. |
| **Stable & Paddock** | _petty_ building. Quarters mounts and remounts; the party may keep horses here between journeys and a fresh mount is available when they set out. |
| **Steward's House** | The settlement runs itself in the party's absence: the steward keeps up repairs and hires hands, and once per season a new building can be raised without a PC spending a Downtime Action (the Septims must still be paid). |
| **Standing Watch-beacon** | Raids are spotted a full day early. With a day's warning, a settlement of Defense 3 or more can turn back a raid on its own (see **Raids**), the defenders having dug in and barred the doors. |
