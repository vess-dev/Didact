Loyalty runs from **1 (Wary)** to **5 (Sworn)**. It shifts when the party's choices match — or clash with — what the companion holds dear.

| Loyalty | Name | What It Means |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | Wary | Follows for now. Leaves at the next clean chance. |
| 2 | Tested | Stays, but grumbles. Won't charge into obvious death for you. |
| 3 | Trusting | The default. A reliable ally. |
| 4 | Devoted | Takes the first hit for a PC. Won't leave without a direct order. |
| 5 | Sworn | Will die for the party and mean it. Once per day, may take a hit meant for their PC — the companion suffers that damage instead. |

The Warden raises or lowers Loyalty, **one step at a time**, usually after a meaningful choice.

**Loyalty tends to rise when** you keep a promise you made them; you fight for a cause they believe in; you risk yourself to save them; you honor their dead, their kin, their gods, or their oath; you treat their advice as worth hearing.

**Loyalty tends to fall when** you break a sworn word; you act against their faith or their people (a Vigilant ordered to spare a Daedra-worshipper; a Stormcloak made to serve the Legion); you abandon allies, rob the helpless, or take the murderer's path when they did not sign on for it; you leave them idle and unspoken-to for a long stretch (a season's silence costs a step).

A background tie cuts both ways: a companion sworn to a faction starts more forgiving of that faction's work and **falls a step faster** when betrayed — two steps on the first real betrayal, where another would fall one.
