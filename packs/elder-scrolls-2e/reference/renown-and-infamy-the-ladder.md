Renown runs on a seven-rung ladder from −3 to +3. The party starts at **Unknown** — just another band of armed strangers on the road.

| Score | Name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| +3 | Hero of Skyrim | Legendary; doors open everywhere. |
| +2 | Renowned | Strangers default Kind. |
| +1 | Known | Small favors offered. |
| 0 | Unknown | Default. |
| −1 | Suspect | Strangers default Wary. |
| −2 | Infamous | Strangers default Hostile. |
| −3 | Reviled | Flee or attack on sight. |

The ladder clamps at ±3. It is shared by the whole party — a band travels on its collective name. (If one character earns fame entirely alone, you may let them carry a personal score, but the default is one dial for the group.)
