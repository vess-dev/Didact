A settlement with more buildings than its people can run begins to fail. **If filled slots ever exceed Population × 2, one building falls into disrepair each season** until the balance is restored — boards rot, a roof caves, a watchtower stands empty. Raise Population, or tear down what you cannot keep, and the decay stops. _petty_ comforts never count against this; only true slots do.
