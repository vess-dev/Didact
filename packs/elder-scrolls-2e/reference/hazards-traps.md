Old places are full of teeth: pressure plates, swinging blade-gates, oil slicks and flame-spouts, dart-walls, the toppling spiked gates of a Nordic barrow. Resolve a trap as a Cairn dungeon trap.

- A sprung trap deals **Attribute damage** (usually STR for crushing, burning, or piercing harm; DEX for a pit or a sudden fall). **Armor applies only if it realistically would** — plate turns a dart; nothing turns a pit.
- The right save (usually **DEX** to dodge or yank a hand back) avoids or lessens it where the fiction allows. Spotting a trap is not a roll — see **Knowledge & Perception**; a careful description of how a PC searches is what finds it.

> Reaching for an offering on a barrow altar, a treasure-hunter triggers a **frost trap**: a Nordic mechanism that vents a gout of enchanted cold from carved vents in the wall. A failed **DEX save** to recoil means a blast of freezing air for **1d6 STR**, and the sudden chill leaves her **Deprived** until she can warm up — barrows guard their dead jealously.
