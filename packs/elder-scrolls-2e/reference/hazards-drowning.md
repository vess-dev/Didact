A PC holds their breath for **three rounds**. Every round after that, make a **STR save** — on a fail, take **1d6 STR** damage. Repeat each round until the PC reaches air or is dragged out. STR restores with rest like any Attribute.

Heavy armor, a full pack, or being knocked senseless cuts the three-round grace short, at the Warden's call. Note that submersion in **icy water** is drowning *and* cold at once — see **Frost & Cold**.

> A Stormcloak in steel plate goes through the thin ice of a mountain tarn chasing a fleeing thief. The freezing shock costs him his grace rounds; the weight of his armor drags him down. He saves against drowning each round, and he has counted as **Deprived** from the cold since the moment he went under — assuming his companions haul him out at all.
