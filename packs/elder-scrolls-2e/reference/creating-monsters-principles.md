- Lean on **Critical Damage** to bring out the threat or the strangeness of any aggressive NPC.
- Use flavor and specificity to make a creature stick. A frost troll that hoards the boots of those it has eaten lingers in memory; "a troll" does not.
- Always bend a creature's stats toward whatever is fun and interesting.
- Four or more similar foes fighting together are a **detachment** (see **Detachments**), as are a few truly enormous creatures.
