A harsh, vertical country of gorges and crags in the southwest — the ancestral land of the Reachfolk, and the native ground of the Forsworn. **Markarth**, the City of Stone, is built *into* a cliff atop the bones of a vast Dwemer city, all carved stone and rushing falls, dominated by the silver-rich Silver-Blood clan and the mine that feeds them.

- **Jarl:** **Igmund**, an Imperial loyalist whose father hired Ulfric's militia to win the city back from the Reachmen, then gave Ulfric up to the Empire when the Thalmor objected to the Talos worship he had been promised. The Reach is bitterly contested and can fall to the Stormcloaks in the war, who seat **Thongvor Silver-Blood**.
- **The barrow:** *Valthume*, in the mountains on the Hold's southeastern edge, where a ghost keeps watch over the dragon priest **Hevnoraak** and begs help to stop him rising.
- **The Dwemer ruin:** Markarth itself sits over **Nchuand-Zel**, the Dwarven ruin beneath the city; deeper in the Hold lie **Bthardamz** (a vast Dwemer complex now a den of the diseased and the Daedric) and **Arkngthamz** (a collapsed ruin holding a legendary Dwarven secret). The Reach is the richest Dwemer country in Skyrim.
- **The notable site:** *Cidhna Mine*, the brutal silver mine that doubles as Markarth's prison — and the secret cell of **Madanach, the King in Rags**, leader of the Forsworn, who runs his rebellion from inside the rock; and the *Temple of Dibella*, center of the goddess of beauty's worship.
- **Other corners:** *Understone Keep* (the Jarl's seat, built among the Dwarven halls), the *Silver-Blood Inn*, and the slum called *The Warrens*; out in the Hold, *Karthwasten* (a contested silver village), *Old Hroldan*, *Fort Sungard*, and the Forsworn redoubts scattered through the crags.
- **Troubles:** a public murder in the Markarth market drags the party into a conspiracy that runs from the Silver-Bloods to the imprisoned Forsworn king. A Forsworn redoubt is raiding the silver road and the Jarl wants it cleared. A Dwemer ruin has gone silent — its automatons stirred, or worse.
- **Faces:** *Madanach, the King in Rags*, the imprisoned Forsworn leader, equal parts freedom-fighter and butcher, who will test any newcomer thrown into his mine. *Ondolemar*, the haughty Thalmor Justiciar stationed in Markarth, forever sniffing out Talos-worship.
