Reagents are bought by the bunch from apothecaries, gathered in the wild, or grown. A brewed potion is _petty_, single-use, and causes no Fatigue (it behaves like a Scroll). A healing draught is magical healing: a creature's STR can be mended by magic only once per day (see **Crafting**). Soul gems power **Enchanting**: a filled gem binds an effect into a weapon, armor, or jewelry, making a Relic, and a filled gem also restores a spent Relic's charges. See **Crafting** for brewing and enchanting at a lab or arcane enchanter.

A larger soul gives a Relic more charges (see **Crafting**). **White (creature) souls** fill any standard gem of sufficient size; **black souls** — those of the sapient — fit only a **black soul gem**, are always grand-sized, and are not stocked by honest merchants (seek a College mage or a Morthal conjurer). *Azura's Star* and *the Black Star* are reusable gems and are unique — see the catalogs, not this list.

---

*Unique and enchanted items — named weapons, named armor, Dragon Priest masks, Relics, and the Daedric artifacts — are never ordinary stock. They are recovered from barrows, ruins, and quests, and are detailed in the Warden's catalogs.*
