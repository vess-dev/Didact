The lore-books of Tamriel that carry a real and lasting lesson, gathered the way a Skyrim adventurer thinks of them. The books of **Steel** teach the warrior's arts: One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Block, Heavy Armor, and Smithing. The books of **the Arcane** teach the mage's: Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Enchanting, Illusion, and Restoration. The books of **Shadow** teach the thief's: Sneak, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Speech, Light Armor, and Alchemy. Every title is a single card granting a single perk.
