The Elder Scrolls 2e assumes most of the party are mortals — Nords and Bretons, Dunmer and Khajiit, folk who eat, sleep, bleed, and bury their dead. Most backgrounds are. But Skyrim is a haunted province. Molag Bal's blood runs in the lords of Castle Volkihar; Hircine's beast-blood howls under the moons; conjurers drag lesser Daedra screaming into Mundus and chain them to a name; and the old Nordic dead do not always stay in their barrows. A player may roll or choose a background that is **not a living mortal**, and the rules for that PC live here.

Every unusual PC keeps Cairn's core chassis untouched — three Attributes, Hit Protection, Scars, saves, ten inventory slots, fiction-first resolution. What changes is what the body is made of, what it needs (or no longer needs), how it is mended, and how the rest of Tamriel reacts to it. Each type below grants real **Edges** — powers a mortal cannot match — paid for with equally real **Costs**. The two always travel together.

These curses and bindings tie back to five of the monstrous backgrounds — the Vampire Fledgling, the Werewolf, the Briarheart, the Dremora, and the Draugr Revenant — and to Diseases & Afflictions, which covers how Vampirism and Lycanthropy are first contracted and finally cured.
