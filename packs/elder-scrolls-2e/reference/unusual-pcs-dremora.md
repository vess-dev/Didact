**What it is.** A **lesser Daedra** — one of the warrior-caste clan of Mehrunes Dagon and other Princes — bound out of Oblivion and into Mundus, given leave (or compelled) to walk Nirn in a mortal shape. A Dremora PC is proud, martial, and bound by oaths; it needs nothing the living need, but it is a foreigner to the mortal world and never truly at home in it. (See the Dremora background.)

**How you become one.** You do not *become* a Dremora — you are one, dragged across the Liminal Barrier by a conjurer's pact or your own ambition, and anchored to Mundus by a name, a debt, or a ruined binding. You begin play already bound.

### Edges
- **No mortal needs.** You require **no food, no water, no sleep, and no breath**. You cannot be Deprived by hunger, thirst, exhaustion, or drowning, and you are **immune to inhaled poison and gas**.
- **Daedric flesh.** You are **immune to all mundane disease** and never save against the afflictions in the disease table, nor against Vampirism or Lycanthropy — the curses of Mundus find no purchase in Oblivion-stuff.
- **Banished, not slain.** When your STR is reduced to 0 you are **not killed** — your vestige is cast back to Oblivion. You re-form there and may return to play after a **season** (a new arc), at the Warden's leave. Death holds no permanent terror for you, only exile.
- **Born to war.** You take to arms and armor as your birthright: you **never make the DEX save to act in the first round of combat** — you always act.

### Costs / Drawbacks
- **A stranger to Mundus.** Mortals' **Reactions toward you start one step worse** — your aura, your eyes, your bearing all mark you as no child of Nirn. The Vigil of Stendarr and most temples treat you as a thing to be banished (**Vigil of Stendarr −1**).
- **No healing for the living.** **Restoration meant for mortals does not mend you** — no temple, no healing potion, no healer's spell. Your wounds and lost STR are repaired only by **Conjuration, Daedric rites, or a binding-master's craft** (a Downtime Action with a willing conjurer, restoring 1d6 STR), or by a full season's reconstitution in Oblivion. A short rest still restores your HP — that is poise and luck, not flesh.
- **Bound by the pact.** You walk Mundus on sufferance. The terms of your binding — a master, an oath, an unfinished task — are a leash the Warden may pull: broken bindings can drag you home, and a rival conjurer may try to seize your name.
- **The long memory.** You do not forget a debt or a slight. This is flavor, but the Warden may rule that a Dremora cannot lie about its given oaths, nor break a freely sworn bargain without consequence.
