A worked example, built table by table.

### People

- **Culture** — _Stoic_ / _Independence_. A flinty, close-mouthed folk who want only to be left to govern themselves; they suffer the Civil War's recruiters and the Jarl's tax-collectors alike with the same gray patience.
- **Resources** — abundant in _Ore_, scarce in _Food_. The vale lives off its silver and iron mines and buys its grain from down-river, so a blocked pass means a hungry winter.

### Factions

Two factions contest the vale.

- **The Silver-Vein Company** (_Merchants_; traits _Shrewd_ and _Corrupt_; advantages _Wealth_, _Position_). A mining concern that owns the largest mine and half the town with it, and quietly pays the Jarl's steward to look the other way. Their agenda: _Dominate Others_ — buy or break every rival claim in the vale — obstructed because _commoners stand openly in opposition_ (the miners are near to revolt). Their agent is **Steward Halvdan**, WIL 13, who skims from both the Company and the Jarl.
- **The Ashen Circle** (the Forsworn coven from the worked example above), lairing in the crags to the west, who mean to drive every Nord from the vale and reclaim it for the Reach.

### Topography

A roll of 4 on the d6 gives four terrain dice. The map comes up: **Tough — Pine Forest** along the river, with a _Leaning Dwemer Tower_ on a ridge; **Easy — River Valley** at the heart, with a _Great Standing Stone_; **Perilous — Sheer Crags** to the west, with a _Looming Word Wall_; and **Tough — Sulfur Springs** to the south, with a _Fissure of Steam_ that vents day and night. The Greywater runs down from the western crags through the valley and off the southern edge of the map.

**Seasonal weather**: mild springs, short close summers, early blizzards in fall, and howling blizzards in winter — the southern pass closes for months. Around the sulfur springs I roll once on Unusual Weather and get _the hot springs steam far thicker than usual, smelling of sulfur and old blood_ — fitting, near the Forsworn's crags.

### Points of Interest

Four d6 land on the map; with the Heart, that makes five POIs.

1. **Heart — Greywater** (Settlement: _Mining Town_, feature _Ruled by a Powerful Family_). The Silver-Bloods' lesser cousins run the town from a stone longhouse above the mine-head.
2. **Karth's Rest** (Waypoint: _Inn_, feature _Front for Smugglers_) — the last roof before the southern pass, where stolen silver leaves the vale.
3. **The Steaming Hollow** (Curiosity: _Field of Hot Springs_, feature _Cult Ritual Site_) — the Ashen Circle bathe and bind their Briarhearts here.
4. **Mournhal Barrow** (Dungeon: _Nordic Barrow_, feature _Sealed_) — a draugr-haunted tomb under the standing stone, its door shut by an iron claw lost generations ago.
5. **The Leaning Tower** (Lair: _Falmer Warren_, feature _Humming Machinery_) — the Dwemer ruin under the tower has been colonized by the Falmer, who creep up at night into the mines.

### Paths

- 1 <-> 2: a **road** down the river valley, _Constant Patrols_ but _Choked with Ash_ in summer. A medium distance: two Watches.
- 1 <-> 4: a **trail** to the barrow, _Mammoth Trails_, _Bitterly Cold_. A short distance: two Watches (one, plus one for the trail).
- 1 <-> 3: a **wilderness route** into the crags toward the springs, _Disappears in Snow_, _Night Predators_ — the Forsworn hunt it. A short distance: three Watches (one, plus two for the wilds).
- 2 <-> 5: a **hidden** trail the smugglers use, skirting the Dwemer tower, _Strewn with Bones_ and _Confusing to Navigate_; finding it costs a favor to the innkeeper. A medium distance: three Watches.

### Three Hooks

- **A Job**: Steward Halvdan will pay 300 Septims to anyone who recovers the iron claw and clears Mournhal Barrow, so the Company can mine the silver vein beneath it. He does not mention that the Jarl believes the barrow should stay sealed.
- **A Rumor**: The miners say the night shift has begun to vanish, and that something climbs up out of the deep workings near the old Dwemer wall.
- **A Danger**: The Ashen Circle have been seen on the valley road in daylight for the first time in years — and the hot springs have begun to steam and stink as if something below them had woken.
