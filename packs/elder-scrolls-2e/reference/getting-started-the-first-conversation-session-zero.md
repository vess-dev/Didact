Before the first session, the Warden and the players spend half an hour — no dice, no characters yet — talking about the game they're about to play. This is the single most useful hour of prep you will ever do.

**Cover these five things:**

1. **Tone.** Heroic Dragonborn saga, or grim survival in a frozen, war-torn country? Pick a place on the dial below.
2. **Safety.** Name the topics the table does not want in play. Slavery, torture, child harm, religious persecution, sexual content — whatever is on the list, it stays off the screen. Agree on a signal (a card, a gesture, a word) any player can use when a scene is getting to them. Honor it instantly and without argument.
3. **Stakes.** Death is real in The Elder Scrolls 2e — a single bad fight can end a character. Make sure the table wants that. If someone doesn't, lean on **Scars**, **Bounty**, and lost reputation as the cost of failure instead of dead PCs.
4. **Pacing.** One-shots, an arc of a few sessions, or a long campaign? How long is a session? How often will you meet?
5. **Scope.** Is this a focused quest (clear one barrow, end one feud), a Hold-spanning sandbox (wander Skyrim and follow what's interesting), or a guild/faction campaign (rise through the Companions, the College, the Guild)? Pick one to start; it shapes the first session.
