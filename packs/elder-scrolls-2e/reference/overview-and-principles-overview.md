### Neutrality

The **Warden** speaks for the world without taking a side. They referee the rules, voice every Jarl and bandit and shopkeep, paint the scene plainly, and call for saves only when the outcome is honestly in doubt. They are neither your enemy nor your champion.

### Classless

No class hems in what a character can be. What you carry and what you have lived through define you. A farmhand who takes up a recovered blade and learns its weight becomes a warrior; a thief who pores over a Spell Tome becomes a mage. Your background is where your story began, not the limit of where it can go.

### Death

Adventurers can grow mighty, yet they remain vulnerable to harm of every kind — steel, frost, claw, and curse. Death is never far off in Skyrim, but it is never a cheap trick either. It comes as the plain consequence of a choice made with eyes open, and the world warns you before it arrives.

### Fiction First

The dice do not decide how hard a thing is or how it turns out. What matters is the situation as it stands in the world — and that is settled by the Warden in conversation with the players. Roll only when a sensible answer could genuinely fall either way.

### Growth

Characters change by living, not by tallying numbers. You sharpen your skill at arms because a shield-brother drilled you; you grow hardier because you survived a blizzard or a draugr's barrow. Every gain carries a tale of how it was earned.

### Player Choice

You should always grasp why you are choosing what you choose. The Warden hands out the stakes and the risks freely and often, so that no decision is made blind.

### Principles

The Warden and the players each follow a short set of guidelines. Together they steer play toward sharp thinking, real exploration, and a story that grows out of what actually happens at the table.

### Shared Objectives

The players trust one another to take the shared world, their characters' aims, and the party's troubles seriously. The party is, as a rule, a band pulling toward a common goal — they get further as a company than any of them would alone.
