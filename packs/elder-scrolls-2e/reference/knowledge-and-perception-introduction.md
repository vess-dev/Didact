The Elder Scrolls 2e parts ways with many modern games by carrying no rules for knowledge, lore, or expertise. A character is simply assumed to know whatever the fiction can justify. In the same way, there is no stat for perception and no "notice" roll. Instead, the Warden should hand the players every relevant detail their characters could pick up with their ordinary senses.
