The Elder Scrolls lore in this book — the province of Skyrim, its Holds and peoples, the Civil War, the Dragon Crisis, the factions, the creatures, the spells, the artifacts, and the Daedric Princes — is **paraphrased from the community UESP wiki** (*The Unofficial Elder Scrolls Pages*, en.uesp.net) for non-commercial home use. No copyrighted game text, dialogue, art, logos, maps, or proprietary content is reproduced here; every entry was rewritten into original rules language and balanced to Cairn's dice. The fan editors of the UESP did the work of compiling that lore, and this book would not exist without it.
