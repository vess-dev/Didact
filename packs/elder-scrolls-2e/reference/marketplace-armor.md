You wear **one suit of body armor** at a time: light armor, mail, or plate. A worn helmet and a held shield each add +1 Armor, only while worn or held, up to the cap of 3. Light armor is normal weight; mail and plate are _bulky_.

Armor is rated by its **type**, never its make. Elven and glass are light armor; Dwarven and steel come as mail or plate; Orcish, ebony, dragonplate, and Daedric are plate. A cuirass of finer material is lighter to wear, harder to ruin, feared on sight, and takes an enchantment well — and it costs many times the price above (see **Crafting**) — but a suit of Daedric plate is still **3 Armor**. Its superiority is in the fiction, and in the fear it provokes.
