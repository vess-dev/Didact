The breadbasket at Skyrim's heart: a windswept tundra plain ringed by mountains, its farms feeding much of the province. **Whiterun**, the trade-city they call the Jewel of the North, rises in stepped districts to the great hall of **Dragonsreach**, and it is here — in the skies over the Hold — that a dragon is first fought and felled, days after Helgen burns.

- **Jarl:** **Balgruuf the Greater**, who labors to keep his Hold neutral while both armies court him and the war closes in. He is a Greybeard-admiring descendant of old Nord kings and a longtime rival of Ulfric. Should the Stormcloaks take the city, the aged ex-Companion **Vignar Gray-Mane** is installed in his place.
- **The barrow:** *Bleak Falls Barrow* looms above Riverwood — a classic Nordic tomb of draugr, traps, and a sealed inner chamber, the sort of first delve that makes or buries an adventuring band. (It sits on the Hold's southern marches, shared with Falkreath country.)
- **The notable site:** *Jorrvaskr*, the overturned-longship mead hall of the Companions in the Wind District, with the **Skyforge** above it and the secret **Underforge** below; nearby stand the **Temple of Kynareth** and the great withered tree, the **Gildergreen**.
- **Other corners:** *Riverwood* (a sleepy lumber village under Bleak Falls), *Rorikstead* (a too-prosperous farm hamlet with old secrets), *Fort Greymoor* and the *Valtheim Towers* (held by whoever the war favors, or by bandits between times). High above the Hold's southeastern corner rise the *Throat of the World* and **High Hrothgar**, monastery of the Greybeards.
- **Troubles:** a dragon attacks a watchtower and the Jarl needs proof of what is loose in his skies. A bandit lord has seized Valtheim Towers and taxes the river road. The Gray-Manes and the Battle-Borns, two great Whiterun families split by the war, edge toward bloodshed in the streets.
- **Faces:** *Kodlak Whitemane*, the wise and weary Harbinger of the Companions, who carries a curse he longs to be free of. *Aela the Huntress*, fierce huntress of the Companions' inner Circle, who runs with the wolf and hunts the Silver Hand.
