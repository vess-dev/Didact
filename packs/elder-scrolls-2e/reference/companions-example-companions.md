Six ready companions. Roll their HP when recruited if you prefer, or take the listed value. Each carries a weapon or two, wears **1 Armor** unless noted, and has one trick. Their listed Loyalty is a *starting* suggestion for how they're usually met.

### Sworn Housecarl (à la Lydia) — Nord

5 HP, 3 Armor, 13 STR, 9 DEX, 8 WIL, steel sword (d8) or greatsword (d10, _bulky_, _two-handed_)

- Wears steel mail and carries a shield (**3 Armor**; 2 when she swaps the shield for the greatsword).
- A grim, dutiful warrior granted to a new Thane to guard them and their hall. Loyal to the death, blunt to a fault, and not above muttering that she is *sworn to carry your burdens*.
- **Trick — Shield-Sworn:** once per combat, she steps between a PC and an incoming attack; that attack strikes her instead. (This is in addition to anything Command grants.)
- Starts at **Loyalty 4** (the housecarl's oath). She will not leave without your word; she falls fast if you turn outlaw against her Hold.

### College Conjurer — Breton

3 HP, 7 STR, 10 DEX, 13 WIL, iron dagger (d6)

- Wears College robes (no Armor) and carries the **Spell Tome: Conjure Familiar** (casting it costs a Fatigue as normal, and a WIL save under duress).
- A robed apprentice of the College of Winterhold, more comfortable among soul gems and summoning circles than people. Curious, careless of danger, and quietly arrogant.
- **Trick — Call the Bound Blade:** once per day, they call a bound sword (d8) into their hand without a Tome and without adding Fatigue; it lasts the scene.
- Starts at **Loyalty 3**. Rises when you defend the arcane arts; falls if you side with those who'd burn mages (Thalmor zealots, torch-bearing mobs).

### Khajiit Sellsword — Khajiit

4 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 13 DEX, 8 WIL, scimitar (d8), hunting bow (d6, _bulky_)

- Wears leather and furs (1 Armor). A wandering caravan-guard and blade-for-hire, all wit and watchfulness, used to being turned away at city gates. Will fight for coin first and friendship later.
- **Trick — Night-Eye:** sees in the dark by the faintest starlight or the last ember of a torch, and warns the party of what waits there.
- **Hired**, not sworn — runs as a *hireling* (no Loyalty, takes Morale) until the party earns him, then starts at **Loyalty 3**. Pay him fairly and he stays; cheat him and he's gone by morning.

### War-Dog (à la Meeko)

3 HP, 1 Armor, 8 STR, 14 DEX, 4 WIL, bite (d6)

- A shaggy, half-wild hound that adopted you on the road — a stray won over with food, a hunter's dog, a Death Hound's gentler cousin. Fearless, devoted, quicker than it looks, and matted thick enough to turn a blade (1 Armor). It follows the animal-companion rules above.
- **Trick — Keen Nose:** scents hidden foes, a hidden trap's oil and iron, or an ambush a room away, and warns with a low growl before the party walks into it.
- Starts at **Loyalty 4**. A dog does not betray.

### Dark Brotherhood Initiate — Imperial

4 HP, 1 Armor, 9 STR, 13 DEX, 11 WIL, poisoned dagger (d6), hunting bow (d6, _bulky_)

- Wears shrouded leathers (1 Armor). The dagger's venom forces a STR save on each hit: on a failure the target also loses **1 STR**.
- A soft-spoken killer newly sworn to the Dread Father Sithis, all manners and menace. Treats murder as a sacrament and finds the squeamish tiresome.
- **Trick — From the Shadows:** the first strike on an unaware foe is _Enhanced_; if it drops them, no one nearby hears.
- Starts at **Loyalty 3**. Rises when you embrace the contract and the kill; falls hard if you spare a sworn mark or break a given word — the Brotherhood does not forgive a broken oath.

### Stronghold Hunter — Orc (Orsimer)

5 HP, 2 Armor, 13 STR, 11 DEX, 7 WIL, war axe (d8), hunting bow (d6, _bulky_)

- Wears Orcish mail (2 Armor). A stern hunter and smith from an Orc stronghold, out to prove themselves worthy of Malacath in the wider world. Honest, unbending, and contemptuous of cowards and oath-breakers.
- **Trick — Stronghold Fury:** once per combat, when first reduced to 0 HP or when an ally falls, their next attack is _Enhanced_.
- Starts at **Loyalty 3**. Rises when you keep your word and fight honestly; falls when you lie, flee a fair fight, or shame their kin.
