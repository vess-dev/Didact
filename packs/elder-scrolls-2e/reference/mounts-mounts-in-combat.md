A common horse is no warhorse. It is a prey animal that would rather be elsewhere.

### A Startled Mount

When fighting erupts, a battle is also fought for the animal's nerve.

- At the **start of combat**, a mounted rider makes a **WIL save** to keep an untrained mount steady, as well as the usual first-round DEX save to act. On a failure the mount **shies, bucks, or bolts**: the rider loses the turn wrestling it, or is thrown (a short fall — see *Hazards*) at the Warden's call.
- Loud, fire, undead, and the roar of a dragon are all good reasons for the Warden to call a fresh save. A trained warhorse, a Skyrim-bred mount used to wolves, or one of the special steeds below may **ignore this save entirely**.

### Fighting from Horseback

- A rider trained to the saddle who strikes from a moving mount — bearing down on a foe, a lance-charge, a sword-sweep from above — is making a **daring maneuver**: the attack is _Enhanced_ (roll d12). This is a deliberate, declared charge, not every swing.
- A rider who is **not** trained, or who tries to fight while also fighting to control a panicking animal, attacks from a position of weakness: the attack is _Impaired_ (roll d4). Loosing a bow from a bolting horse is _Impaired_ the same way.
- Striking a mounted foe is resolved normally. Bringing the **mount** down (where the fiction allows targeting it) drops the rider — a short fall, then both fight on foot.
