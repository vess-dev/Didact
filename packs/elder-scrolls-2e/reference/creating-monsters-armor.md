title: "Armor"
tags: [creating, monsters]
source: wardens-guide/creating-monsters#armor
---
For some creatures this is natural hide or chitin; for others it is worn or carried plate. If a creature can shrug off a hit without giving ground, raise this number. Armor never exceeds **3**, no matter how dread the beast — a Dwarven Centurion's bronze and a frost troll's hide both cap there.
