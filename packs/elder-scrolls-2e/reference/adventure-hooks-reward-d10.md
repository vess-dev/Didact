*Base pay (a few hundred Septims) plus a bonus. Roll one bonus.*

| d10 | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Septims**: 2d6 × 50, straight coin. |
| 2 | **Gear**: a Marketplace item worth up to ~200 Septims the patron has spare (a suit of plate, a Skyrim horse, a wagon). |
| 3 | **A Scroll or potions**: a single-use Scroll, or 1d4 useful potions. |
| 4 | **A Skill Book** (Warden's pick, scene-appropriate). |
| 5 | **A Relic** (Warden's pick from **Relics**). |
| 6 | **Faction Reputation** up one step with a friendly faction (toward Trusted/Sworn). |
| 7 | **Renown**: a public deed worth one step of Renown (toward Known/Renowned), or a **Bounty cleared** in this Hold. |
| 8 | **Standing or favor**: a settlement, Thane, or Jarl now owes the party a real debt. |
| 9 | **A contact and a map** — a new ally who owes the party their life, and a lead on a further adventure. |
| 10 | **A unique item and a string attached**: generous pay (a named weapon, armor, or artifact) *and* a secondary hook comes with it. |
