A PC who catches fire takes **1d4 STR** damage per round until the flames are out. Smothering them takes an action — drop and roll, plunge into snow or water, beat them out with a cloak.

A PC standing in a sustained blaze (a Destruction mage's wall of flame, a burning longhouse, a dragon's breath that has set the ground alight) makes a **DEX save** each round they remain — on a fail, they catch fire.

**Armor does not apply to fire damage** unless it is specifically rated against it (a Resist Fire enchantment, a warding ring, certain Daedric wards).

> A College apprentice fumbles a fire spell and sets his own robes alight: **1d4 STR** each round. He spends his next action throwing himself into a snowbank to put it out, losing his place in the fight but keeping his skin.
