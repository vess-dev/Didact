Skyrim is a land of sworn brotherhoods, secret guilds, warring armies, and zealous orders. Few of them care what a stranger thinks of the world at large — but every one of them keeps a ledger on the people who keep turning up at their door. **Faction Reputation** is that ledger. It is a small, separate dial for each organized group the party deals with, tracking how a single faction regards these particular adventurers — distinct from the wider fame measured by Renown & Infamy.

Keep it light. Do not roll for it, do not tally points behind the screen. When the party does something a faction would hear about and care about, you nudge their standing one rung up or down. That is the whole engine.
