What a character knows is bounded only by what the fiction can support. If a PC grew up scraping by in the warrens of Riften, their player can fairly argue that the character knows how to run down a hiding fence in the slums — which palms to grease, which back rooms to ask in, and what it'll cost to learn where their mark is holed up.

If nothing in a character's background can justify knowing something, then they simply don't. Even so, the Warden may roll the **Die of Fate** to see whether the character happens to know _anything_ at all — the higher the result, the more they might reasonably have picked up.

### Relics

When a character first lays hands on an unfamiliar Relic, they can either study it during a Downtime action or simply tinker with it. Tinkering carries risk and may demand a WIL save to avoid an unpleasant surprise. More often than not, though, it's better to let the player ask all the questions they like and then — eventually — just _tell them how it works_.
