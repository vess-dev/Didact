A tamed or befriended beast follows the companion rules with a few changes: it has no special trick beyond its nature, its only Armor is its own hide's (as the Bestiary gives its kind, or none), it cannot wield gear or read a Tome, and it is commanded by tone and gesture. It still has **Loyalty** and can be the target of the **Command** action.

(A ready-made hound — the **War-Dog** — appears among the Example Companions below.)

### Horse or steed

6 HP, 14 STR, 10 DEX, 4 WIL, hooves (d8)

- A Skyrim horse — sure-footed, stubborn, and unbothered by mountain trails. It adds **+4 slots** and lets the party ignore one watch of Path or Terrain penalty on a journey (see **Mounts**), but it is no warhorse: it bolts from dragons and bandits unless trained.
- **Trick (nature):** carries a rider and gear at a steady pace where folk on foot would tire; finds its own way home if loosed near familiar ground.
- A named or undying steed (a black horse that cannot die, a skeletal mount called from the Soul Cairn) is written as a **companion mount** or a **Relic** — see **Mounts**.

Larger tamed beasts — a frostbite spider, a sabre cat, an armored troll — are possible at the Warden's discretion, statted from the Bestiary, but they unsettle townsfolk (reactions one step worse) and cannot enter most settlements.
