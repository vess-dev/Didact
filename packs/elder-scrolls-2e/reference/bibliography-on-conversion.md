The Elder Scrolls 2e is not the only hack of Cairn's chassis, and it shouldn't be. Cairn 2e converts cleanly into any genre where the fiction is king, death is close, and characters are made by their bags and their scars. Pick a world you love; run the same engine; change the tables; find what you find.
