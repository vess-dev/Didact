The full procedure — the cycle, the events table, light, doors, traps, and the rest — lives in the Player's Guide under Procedures. This chapter is the Warden's companion to it: a short set of running principles, then a worked example of a delve, table-talk and all, so you can see the cycle turning in practice.

Remember that "dungeon" is shorthand. It covers any sealed, dangerous place the party must pick through a piece at a time: a draugr-haunted Nordic barrow, a buried Dwemer hall full of dormant machines, a Falmer warren, a frost-rimed cave, a bandit fort, a Forsworn redoubt, a flooded shipwreck.
