The Nords say Kyne breathed their people to life on the Throat of the World, and the old land remembers her. In some sense each wild is a single living thing — its will spread through root and rock and running water, its limbs the beasts and plants that answer its needs, its blood the spriggans, giants, and stranger things that rise to drive off intruders or shelter the worthy. The Wilds protect themselves and their own. Roll on the Traits and Wilderness Agenda tables and note the results. Together they set the temper of the creatures found here and what the country _wants_ as a whole.
