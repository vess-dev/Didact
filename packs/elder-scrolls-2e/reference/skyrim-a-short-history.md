- **The Merethic Era.** Men came to Skyrim from frozen Atmora under Ysgramor and his Five Hundred Companions. Dragons ruled them then, worshipped through the masked Dragon Priests whose barrows still dot the mountains — until men learned the Voice and rose against them, and three heroes cast Alduin the World-Eater out of time atop the Throat of the World.
- **The First Era.** Nord kings ruled from Windhelm and Winterhold. The Snow Elves were driven underground, enslaved by the Dwemer, and twisted into the blind Falmer; then the Dwemer themselves vanished from the world in a single moment, leaving their brass cities empty and humming.
- **The Third Era.** Tiber Septim, whom the Nords call Talos, united Tamriel into an Empire that lasted four hundred years, and was raised to godhood as the Ninth Divine.
- **The Oblivion Crisis (3E 433).** The Mythic Dawn cult murdered Emperor Uriel Septim VII, and Oblivion Gates tore open across Cyrodiil until Mehrunes Dagon himself walked the Imperial City. Martin Septim, the last of the line, gave his life as the avatar of Akatosh to banish him, and the Third Era ended with him.
- **The Red Year (4E 5).** Red Mountain erupted and buried much of Morrowind in fire and ash. Argonians invaded from the south, and Dunmer refugees fled to Skyrim and to Solstheim.
- **The Great Collapse (4E 122).** Most of the city of Winterhold slid into the Sea of Ghosts. The townsfolk still blame the College.
- **The Great War (4E 171–175).** The Thalmor of the Aldmeri Dominion made war on the Empire of Titus Mede II. The Empire bought peace with the **White-Gold Concordat**, which outlawed the worship of Talos.
- **The Markarth Incident (4E 174–176).** The Reachmen seized Markarth; Ulfric Stormcloak's militia took it back on a promise of free Talos worship, and were arrested when the Thalmor learned of it. The rebellion was born in that broken promise.
- **4E 201.** Ulfric has killed High King Torygg with the Voice, the Holds are at war, and the first dragon in an age has burned the town of Helgen.
