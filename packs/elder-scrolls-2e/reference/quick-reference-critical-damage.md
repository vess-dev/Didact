---
- Damage past 0 HP spills into **STR**. Then make a **STR save at the new STR** — fail = **Critical Damage** (crawling; dead in an hour without aid).
- **STR 0 = dead. DEX 0 = paralyzed. WIL 0 = delirious.** Out-of-combat harm hits an Attribute directly (usually STR).
- Restore lost Attributes with a week's rest and proper care.
