A short stumble is scraped knees. A real fall hurts.

- Every full **10 ft** you fall deals **1d6 STR** damage. A drop of less than 10 ft costs nothing but pride.
- Make a **DEX save** to land well. On a pass, **halve** the total damage (round down).
- A soft landing (deep snow, water, a heap of rotted sacking) removes **1d6** from the roll. A hard landing (jagged rock, an iron spike, a Dwemer floor) adds **1d6**.

> A delver misjudges a gap on a corroded Dwemer walkway and drops two storeys — about 20 ft — onto the brass plating below: **2d6 STR**, and the unforgiving metal floor adds **1d6** more. She makes her DEX save, twisting to roll as she lands, and halves the total. Falling in a Dwemer ruin is rarely clean.
