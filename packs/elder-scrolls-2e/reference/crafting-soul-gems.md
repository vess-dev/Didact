A **soul gem** is a crystal that traps the soul of a dying creature. Filled gems power **Enchanting** (binding a new effect) and **Recharge** (refilling a depleted Relic); empty gems wait to be filled by a Soul Trap effect or a suitable Spell Tome. A gem is **consumed** when its soul is spent. Empty gems are bought from court wizards and College mages, looted from barrows and ruins, or mined from the glittering geode veins of Blackreach.

A gem holds a soul only up to its size, and the bigger the soul, the mightier the enchantment or the fuller the Recharge. The ladder, smallest to largest:

| Gem | Holds | Charges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Petty** | the soul of the smallest vermin | 1 — the apprentice's gem |
| **Lesser** | a small beast | 1 |
| **Common** | an ordinary animal | 2 — the workaday gem of most enchanters |
| **Greater** | a large or fierce creature | 2, or a constant enchantment |
| **Grand** | the mightiest beasts | 3, or a constant enchantment — the fullest a white soul goes |
| **Black** | the soul of a thinking being | 3, or a constant enchantment — grand-sized always, and forbidden |

### White souls and black souls

Most creatures yield **white souls** — beasts, vermin, monsters — and a white soul fits any standard gem of sufficient size. The souls of **thinking beings** (people, and the like) are **black souls**, and they can be caught **only** in a **black soul gem**. A black soul always counts as grand-sized, powering the strongest enchantments and the deepest Recharges.

Trapping a person's soul is **taboo** across Skyrim — necromancer's work, abhorred by the temples and the Vigil of Stendarr alike. Black soul gems are never sold openly; they are bought in dark corners (a College fixer, a Morthal conjurer) or made by darker rites still. Carrying one marks you; using one marks you worse. The power is real. So is the price.
