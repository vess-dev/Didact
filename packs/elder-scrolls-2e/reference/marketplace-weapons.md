| Crossbow (d8 damage, _bulky_) | 30 |
| Quiver of arrows or bolts (_petty_) | 5 |

A finer weapon — Dwarven, elven, glass, ebony, Stalhrim, dragonbone, Daedric — costs many times these prices but keeps its die: a Daedric sword is still d8, a dragonbone greatsword still d10. The difference is lightness, a keen edge that holds, how well it takes an enchantment, and the dread of the thing. Only tempering steps a weapon's die up (see **Crafting**).
