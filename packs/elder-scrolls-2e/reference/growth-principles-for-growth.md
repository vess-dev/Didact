title: "Principles for Growth"
tags: [growth]

---
- Growth is never arbitrary. It is tied to a specific experience in the fiction.
- It matters more for a character to become *more interesting* than to become more skilled or capable.
- Characters grow not merely because something new happened, but because that something affected them in a significant way.
- Seed opportunities for growth in as many places as you can.
- Characters grow as often during a session as they do during Downtime.
- Growth should pull a character deeper into the world and its dangers, not let them coast above it.
- Growth is not a prize. It is the logical consequence of what a character did.
- Growth happens most often when a character meddles with something they do not understand — or something they were never *meant* to understand. A black soul gem, a humming word-wall, a Daedric Prince's bargain.
- Characters may change in ways they dislike, or gain power at a cost. Lycanthropy is a gift and a chain at once.
- Relics, Daedric Artifacts, factions, monsters, and important NPCs are all excellent triggers for growth.
- Characters can change slowly over time, or by piling up many related experiences around a single kind of growth.
