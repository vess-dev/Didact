The undead servants of Molag Bal stalk Skyrim's nights. Vampires are immune to poison and disease; fire damage against them is _Enhanced_, and sunlight leaves them Deprived (unable to regain HP). The Volkihar of the frozen northwest command the deadliest of their kind.
