Ten peoples walk Tamriel, and all of them can be found somewhere in Skyrim, though some far more than others. (Which to play, and how it shapes your character, is covered in Character Creation.)

- **Nords** — the tall, fair, hardy folk of Skyrim itself, born to the cold and shrugging off frost that would kill another. Fierce warriors and bards, proud of their kin and their old gods, and often wary of elves and outsiders alike. This is their land.
- **Imperials** — the cosmopolitan folk of Cyrodiil to the south: shrewd traders, soldiers, and administrators who keep the Empire's law. In Skyrim they hold the cities of the Imperial cause and draw cold looks in rebel country.
- **Bretons** — the part-elven people of High Rock to the west, born with a knack for magic and a head for intrigue. Many serve as court wizards, merchants, and mercenaries throughout the Holds.
- **Redguards** — the dark-skinned, sword-skilled folk of Hammerfell, reckoned the finest natural warriors in Tamriel. Travelers, sailors, and sellswords, a fair few have drifted into Skyrim seeking work or fleeing trouble at home.
- **Dunmer** — the ash-gray Dark Elves of Morrowind, grim and clannish, resistant to fire and quick with Destruction magic. Many fled to Skyrim after Red Mountain erupted and live crowded and unwelcome in Windhelm's Gray Quarter.
- **Altmer** — the golden, long-lived High Elves of the Summerset Isles, the most gifted mages of all and the proudest. In Skyrim the name mostly conjures the hated Thalmor, and a lone High Elf will feel the suspicion.
- **Bosmer** — the slight, nimble Wood Elves of Valenwood, the keenest archers and woodsmen in the world. They turn up in Skyrim as hunters, scouts, and thieves, at home in the deep pine forests.
- **Orcs** — the tusked, fierce Orsimer, peerless smiths and unbreakable heavy infantry who live by a hard code of honor in fortified strongholds scattered across the wilds. Long scorned, now grudgingly accepted, many serve in the Legion.
- **Khajiit** — the cat-folk of Elsweyr, clever and agile, born thieves, acrobats, and traders. Barred from Skyrim's cities, their caravans camp outside the gates, hawking wares and moon sugar at the edge of the law.
- **Argonians** — the reptilian Saxhleel of Black Marsh, scaled and cold-eyed, who breathe water and shrug off disease. Slow to trust and long enslaved by others, in Skyrim they take the work no one else will, often on the docks.
