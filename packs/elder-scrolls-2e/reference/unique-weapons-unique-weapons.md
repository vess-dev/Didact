These are the named blades, bows, and staves of Skyrim — heirlooms drawn from barrows, prizes torn from the hands of the powerful, and the dread **Daedric Artifacts** loaned to mortals by the Princes of Oblivion. They are found, won, or quested for; none of them is stocked for sale. (Named armor, shields, and the Dragon Priest masks live in **Unique Armors**.)

Most unique weapons are simply a fine weapon with **one** special property. A few of the greatest — and every staff — burn through **charges** and must be recharged; where a weapon is charged, its **Recharge** line tells you how. Damage dice never exceed d12, and no weapon grants Armor. Their superiority is in their single trick and in the dread of the thing.
