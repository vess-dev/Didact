- **Dungeon:** a turn = move within your light (~40 ft) + one action. Lingering, haste, noise, or entering a new area risks a **Dungeon Events** roll (d6: 1 Encounter, 2 Sign, 3 Environment, 4 Loss, 5 Exhaustion, 6 Quiet).
- **Wilderness:** the day has three **watches**; each PC takes one **Wilderness Action** (Travel, Explore, Supply, Make Camp). Roll for getting lost, then the **Wilderness Events** table (d6: 1 Encounter, 2 Sign, 3 Environment, 4 Loss, 5 Exhaustion, 6 Discovery).
- **Cold:** a watch in the snow without warm clothing, shelter, or fire → Deprived. A blizzard without shelter → Deprived at once.
