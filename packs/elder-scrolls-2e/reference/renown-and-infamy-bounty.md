**Bounty is not Renown.** It is a separate thing, and it is **per-Hold**: a number of **Septims** the local Jarl's guards will demand from anyone known to have broken the Hold's laws where someone saw it. Each of the nine Holds (and Solstheim's Raven Rock) keeps its own bounty book. A crime in the Reach earns a price on your head in Markarth and nowhere else; ride into Whiterun and the guards there neither know nor care, until word travels or you re-offend.

**How a bounty is incurred.** A witnessed crime against the Hold's peace adds Septims to your bounty in that Hold — theft, assault, trespass in a forbidden place, murder, and the like, scaled to the offense (a lifted apple is a pittance; a murder in the street is a fortune). The crucial word is *witnessed*: a guard who sees it, a victim who lives to report it, or evidence that points back to you. A crime with no witness and no trail adds no bounty, however grave.

**What a bounty does.** While you carry a bounty in a Hold, that Hold's guards will confront you on sight — demanding payment, ordering you to surrender, and turning Hostile (a fight) if you refuse or run. A large enough bounty means they come to arrest or kill rather than to talk. Carrying a bounty effectively bars you from the Hold's lawful comforts: the market, the inn, the Jarl's hall.

**Clearing a bounty.** There are three ordinary ways to wipe the slate clean in a Hold:

- **Pay it.** Hand the full sum to a guard or the steward. The bounty is cleared in that Hold; the Septims are gone.
- **Serve the time.** Surrender to the guards and sit in the Hold's jail (Cidhna Mine, the Chill, Mistveil's cells, and so on). Jail clears the bounty in that Hold at the cost of time — a stretch of days that passes like a Downtime block, with the attendant indignities and the chance of a desperate escape.
- **Leave the Hold.** Get out and stay out. A bounty cools while you are gone; given enough time away — and no fresh offenses — that Hold's guards stop hunting and the slate clears. (The most cynical, most reliable method in Skyrim.)

**Bounty does not move Renown by itself.** A price on your head in one Hold is a local legal problem, not province-wide fame or shame. Skulking thieves can run up enormous bounties and remain Unknown to the common folk; conversely a Hero of Skyrim with no bounty anywhere is still arrested the moment they pick a guard's pocket and get caught. The two only meet when a crime is *both* public enough to be sung about *and* witnessed enough to be charged — and even then you, the Warden, decide each independently. As a rule of thumb: petty witnessed crime is pure Bounty; an open atrocity is Bounty *and* a step toward Infamy; a quiet murder no one can pin on the party is neither.
