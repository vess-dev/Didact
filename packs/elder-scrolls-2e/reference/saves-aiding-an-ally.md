> The party reaches the entrance to the second level of a long-forgotten mountain barrow. Elsinde leads, Qa'zirr behind her, Hjolgeir bringing up the rear with a lit torch; the other two have their hands free.

**Warden**: _"To the north, the path runs out over a narrow stone arch above a black chasm. To the east, a broken stair drops down into the murk. What do you do?"_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Our prize is at the top of this place, the legends say. North, over the arch."_

**Warden**: _"The rest of you agree? Then, keeping the same marching order..."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Wait — let me take point. A Companion learns to feel a trap before it springs, and something ahead in the dark sets my teeth on edge. I'll lead, slow and careful, sword ready."_

**Warden**: _"Hjolgeir, you start across the arch. It moans and creaks underfoot but holds firm. As the party reaches the middle, the stone begins to shudder, then splits with cracks — it's collapsing from the near end!"_

**Hjolgeir**: _"If we sprint for the far side, can we make it clean?"_

**Warden**: _"You and Qa'zirr are farthest along and least at risk. But Elsinde's last — she needs a DEX save to reach the other side safely."_

**Elsinde**: _"You simply had to lead, didn't you. Qa'zirr — your rope! Is there time?"_

**Warden**: _"Yes. Hjolgeir has his hands full with the torch and his sword, so it's Qa'zirr. Tossing you an end of his climbing rope is easy enough, but holding fast as the floor goes will take a STR save — and since you're the one most at risk, you make it."_

**Elsinde**: _"A friend and a rope still beat a long fall. Do it."_

> Elsinde rolls a 5: a success.

**Warden**: _"As the arch crumbles, Hjolgeir and Qa'zirr bolt to the far ledge with Elsinde right behind. The moment the stone drops away beneath her, Qa'zirr flings her the rope and braces, and she slams into the cliff face, clinging on for dear life. Hand over hand she hauls herself up the last few feet and rolls onto the dusty floor above."_

**Elsinde**: _"Bracing. What's next?"_
