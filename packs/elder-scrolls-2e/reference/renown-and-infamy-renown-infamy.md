Faction Reputation tracks what an organization thinks of the party. **Renown & Infamy** tracks what *everyone else* does — the songs in the taverns, the warnings whispered at the gate, the way a stranger's hand drifts toward a sword or a coin purse when the party walks in. It is the party's reputation with the common folk of Skyrim at large: one shared dial, moved only by deeds done in public, that colors how strangers meet them.

This is fame and dread, not law. A Hero of Skyrim can still owe a fortune in **Bounty** to a Hold whose laws they broke; an Infamous killer can still be a Sworn brother of the Thieves Guild. Keep the three tracks — Renown, Faction Reputation, and Bounty — separate in your head, because they pull in different directions all the time.
