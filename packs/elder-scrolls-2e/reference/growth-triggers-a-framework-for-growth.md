As characters engage with Skyrim, they must keep adapting to new realities in the fiction. Some adaptations are minor and never revisited. Others are cumulative, a collection of related experiences. And some experiences are so powerful their impact is immediate and unmatched. For each, it helps to think in terms of **Triggers** — opportunities for growth. A Trigger marks a turning point in a character's life and should be met with a permanent, related change. Good Triggers depend on a character doing at least two of the following:

- A focused, consistent pattern of behavior around a single objective or course of action.
- Taking an obvious risk with potentially serious consequences, especially when the outcome is unknown.
- Interacting with a unique item, creature, or entity.
