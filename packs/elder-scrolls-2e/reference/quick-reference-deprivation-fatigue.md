title: "Deprivation & Fatigue"
tags: [quick, reference]
source: players-guide/quick-reference#deprivation--fatigue
---
- **Deprived** (no food, rest, or warmth): cannot recover HP, Attributes, or clear Fatigue.
- **Fatigue** fills a slot each; cleared by a full night's safe sleep.
