A riding horse adds carry slots and lets the party ignore one watch of Path or Terrain penalty on a journey; a Skyrim horse also treats snow, ice, and mountain terrain as one step easier; a mule, cart, or wagon only carries. Named, undying, or summonable steeds are not for sale — see **Mounts** for stabling and for the storied horses you must earn.
