A Spell Tome is a single spell bound into a book. Anyone literate can cast from one — no class, no training, no Magicka score. The cost is paid in **Fatigue** and, in the heat of the moment, in nerve. The tomes below are the working magic of Skyrim: the College's curriculum, the Court Wizards' arts, the necromancer's whispered pacts, the half-forgotten arts of the Third Era, and worse things pried from barrows and Dwemer vaults.

Each entry is one spell. The first line is what it does. The italic line names its school and rank, then the quirk of *that particular book* — for a Spell Tome is never a tame object, and no two copies open the same way.
