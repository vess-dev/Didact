3 HP, 0 Armor, 9 STR, 10 DEX, 15 WIL, dagger (d6)

- **Breton College Apprentice**, age 24. **Dragonskin:** once per day, a spell or magical effect that targets you fizzles harmlessly.
- **Background:** Faralda took you on in Destruction; her one rule is never to cast into the wind. You summoned a mote of light you could not send back, and it trails you still, lighting close quarters.
- **Carries:** **Spell Tome: Flames**, dagger, College novice robes (_petty_), charcoal and notebook (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), Lantern with an Oil flask (6 uses), 12 Septims. _(5 slots)_
- **Bond:** a **warded journal** (_petty_) a College mage entrusted to you before she vanished into Blackreach. It writes back, in a hand that is not your own.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Mage.
- **Who you are:** a sharp, anxious scholar who has read everything and done almost nothing, chasing the teacher who walked into the dark.
