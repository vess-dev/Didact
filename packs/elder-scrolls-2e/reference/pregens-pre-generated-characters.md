Ten ready-made adventurers, one of each race, for a quick start or a one-shot. Each was built by the rules in Character Creation: Attributes from 3d6 (two swapped), Hit Protection from 1d6, a background with both of its tables rolled, and a Bond. The stat line gives HP, Armor, Attributes, and weapons; the slot count after each inventory is out of ten. Standing Stones are listed for tables that use them. Hand one to a player and they can be in the snow within minutes — and change names, faces, and details freely.
