---
- First, roll or choose from the Backgrounds table below and note the page. You will refer to that page for the next few steps.
- Choose a **Race** (see Races) and a **name** from the lists in your background or the Naming Procedures. Note your race's single trait.
- Record your starting items from your background. See Inventory for how to record items.
- Roll on each of the included tables in your background. Record your character's items, skills, or abilities from each result. Some backgrounds may ask you to refer to the Marketplace as well.
