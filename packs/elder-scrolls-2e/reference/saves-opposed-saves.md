> The party has driven their foes — two bandits and their hulking chief — back against a sheer drop in the Reach. Hjolgeir and Elsinde took their turns at once, but Qa'zirr failed his opening DEX save and acts after the enemies. As his friends each take a bandit, the chief's club very nearly caves his skull in.

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr cannot survive another trade of blows with this brute. So — he shall put the brute over the edge instead."_

**Warden**: _"You're the one most at risk here, so if you try to shove him off, you make the save."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"And if Qa'zirr fails, Qa'zirr goes over. So be it — but he twists his enchanted ring onto his claw first, just in case."_

> Qa'zirr rolls a STR save to grapple the chief and hurl him from the cliff. He rolls a 17: a fail. His ring is enchanted to fortify his reflexes (1 charge): he may spend its charge to re-roll a failed DEX save — but not this STR save.

**Warden**: _"The chief catches you mid-lunge, lifts you, and flings you backward off the edge. Save DEX to catch yourself."_

> Qa'zirr rolls a 16: a fail. He spends the ring's charge to re-roll, and gets a 9: a success.

**Warden**: _"The ring flares, and time seems to slow: you snag a jutting pine root ten feet down and hang there, light as a cat. It'll take two turns to climb back up, or a DEX save to do it fast."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Cursed luck. Qa'zirr only hopes his friends last until he claws his way back..."_
