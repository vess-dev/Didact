Reputation runs on a five-rung ladder from −2 to +2. Everyone starts at **Unknown** with everyone, unless a background says otherwise.

| Step | Name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| −2 | **Hunted** | Attacked on sight by the faction. |
| −1 | **Distrusted** | Watched, overcharged, barred from the inner circle. |
| 0 | **Unknown** | Default. |
| +1 | **Trusted** | Discounts, tolerated armed, given minor work. |
| +2 | **Sworn** | Treated as one of their own; one meaningful favor per session. |

The ladder clamps at its ends. A faction already at Hunted cannot sink further, and a faction at Sworn cannot rise higher — though pushing against the top or bottom may shift the *flavor* (a Sworn member who keeps over-delivering might be groomed for leadership; a Hunted enemy who keeps killing their people earns a named bounty).
