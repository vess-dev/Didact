A **Realm** is a loose name for a related cluster of regions — potentially a whole Hold, or the better part of one, holding dozens of places for the party to find and meddle with. Skyrim's nine Holds (and the island of Solstheim) each make a fine Realm; a single region is a valley, a stretch of coast, or the country around one capital.

A few results on the tables below name a single place — the Skaal, Red Mountain's ash, the Drajkmyr, the White River, the Throat of the World, the Reach. They belong only where that place is; anywhere else, reroll or reskin them.

### Materials Required

- A sheet of paper and a notebook. Letter or A4 is fine, but bigger is better. From here on, we call the sheet of paper the **map**.
- Pencils and erasers. Colored pencils help if you want to mark terrain by color, or just make the map handsome.
- At least one d6 and one d20, though 6d6 and 2d20 are ideal.
