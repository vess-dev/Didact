4 HP, 1 Armor, 10 STR, 15 DEX, 10 WIL, hunting bow (d6, _bulky_), skinning knife (d6)

- **Bosmer Hunter**, age 58. **Command Animal:** once per day, a natural beast that can see you makes a WIL save or treats you as a friend for the rest of the scene.
- **Background:** a Greenspeaker taught you to hunt with a hawk; it is your companion (talons d6, no Armor), and it scouts ahead and returns with what it saw. You never lose your way in forest, fen, or thicket, and can lead others by game-trails no map shows.
- **Carries:** hunting bow, skinning knife, leaf-dyed leathers (1 Armor), Rations of dried meat (3 uses), Torch (3 uses), 7 Septims — and a hunting hawk on your shoulder. _(6 slots)_
- **Bond:** a Greybeard once breathed a single Word into your ear. You do not understand it — but animals fall silent and still when you draw near.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Thief.
- **Who you are:** a quiet, meat-eating wanderer of the Green Pact, as out of place among the Thalmor as among the Nords, and happiest a day's walk from anyone.
