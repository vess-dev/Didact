A frigid north-central Hold of windswept tundra along the icy shore of the Sea of Ghosts. **Dawnstar** is a small mining-and-fishing port, and lately a town that cannot sleep, for every soul in it is gripped by relentless nightmares.

- **Jarl:** **Skald the Elder**, an aged, fervent Stormcloak loyalist so overzealous that his own court thinks him a fool. The Pale can fall to the Imperials in the war, who seat the retired Legion officer **Brina Merilis**.
- **The barrow:** the Hold is studded with Nordic crypts in the snow, *Volunruud* on its western marches the oldest of them; its standout holy ruin is *Nightcaller Temple* above the town — once a temple of Vaermina, Daedric Prince of nightmares, now the dark source of the curse that plagues Dawnstar's dreams.
- **The Dwemer ruin:** *Raldbthar*, a Dwarven city-state high in the southern mountains — one of the three ways down into **Blackreach**, the vast cavern-realm beneath Skyrim (*Mzinchaleft*, another, lies just over the line in Hjaalmarch). Close by, on the shore of Lake Yorgrim, *Irkngthand* hides a great statue of a Snow Elf, carved in secret by the Falmer's ancestors when they were slaves.
- **The notable site:** the *Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary* beneath Dawnstar (the order's fallback refuge, opened late in their story), and the town's *lighthouse* on the freezing shore.
- **Other corners:** *The White Hall* (the Jarl's seat), the *Iron-Breaker* and *Quicksilver* mines that keep the town alive, the inn; out in the Hold, *Fort Dunstad* on the tundra road and *Lake Yorgrim*.
- **Troubles:** the whole town is wracked by nightmares and an old priest of Mara offers a desperate remedy at Nightcaller Temple — where the cure is worse than the curse. Bandits or worse have taken Fort Dunstad astride the Pale's main road. A miner has broken through into something Dwarven, and into the endless night of Blackreach below.
- **Faces:** *Skald the Elder*, the cantankerous old Jarl who would sooner lose the Pale than bend a knee to the Empire. *Erandur*, a gentle, haunted priest of Mara who knows the secret of the nightmares and the price of ending them.
