title: "Principles"
tags: [creating, backgrounds]
source: wardens-guide/creating-backgrounds#principles
---
- Players should be *eager* to roll on a Background's tables.
- Backgrounds can be archetypal and familiar (the bandit, the court wizard, the hunter) — but leave room for the unexpected reading.
- Every Background should tell the player something about the implied setting.
- Every table result should be unique, so two characters of the same Background still come out distinct.
