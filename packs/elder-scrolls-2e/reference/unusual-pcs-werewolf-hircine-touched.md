**What it is.** A mortal who carries the **Beast Blood** of Hircine, Daedric Prince of the Hunt. Under the curse you can loose a monstrous wolf-shape that runs down prey no mortal could catch. In Skyrim the gift is held by the inner Circle of the Companions. (See the Werewolf background.)

**How you become one.** Lycanthropy is **not** caught like a disease — it is taken on willingly, by drinking the blood of a werewolf in ritual (most often the Companions' Circle, who keep faith with Hircine through the Glenmoril witches). The change is permanent once taken. It ends only by the deliberate rite at Ysgramor's Tomb — or by contracting Vampirism, since the two curses cannot share a body. See Diseases & Afflictions.

### Edges (always, in mortal form)
- **Beast Blood.** You are **immune to all mundane disease** and never save against the afflictions in the disease table.
- **The Change.** You may transform into **Beast Form once per day**, at will (see below). The **Ring of Hircine**, should you find it, lets you change without limit.
- **Hunter's senses.** You can follow a scent-trail a mortal would lose, and smell fresh blood and fear nearby.

### Beast Form
> **Werewolf, Beast Form** — *Transformed; lasts the scene or until you revert.* Claws **d6+d6**, or a bite **d8**; **+1 Armor** from thick hide (to the cap of 3). You move with terrifying speed (cover ground as though every path were Easy) and are not staggered or knocked back by mortal blows. Once per transformation you may loose a **Howl of Terror**: every enemy who hears it makes a **WIL save** or flees for the rest of the round.

- You revert at the **end of the scene**; to revert early, pass a **WIL save**.

### Costs / Drawbacks
- **Silver bites deep.** Any attack against you with a _silver_ weapon is _Enhanced_ (roll d12 damage), in beast form or out.
- **A beast carries nothing.** While Transformed you **cannot use gear, channel a Spell Tome, wield weapons, loot, pick locks, open containers, or speak** — only run, rend, and feed.
- **The hunger after the hunt.** After you revert, you must **feed on a fresh kill** before the next watch ends. If you do not, you are **Deprived** until you do.
- **No rest as a beast.** You cannot recover HP or clear Fatigue while Transformed — the change is for the hunt, not for healing.
- **The Hunt is known.** Where your curse is recognized, mortals' **Reactions shift one step worse**; the Companions count you as kin (**Companions +1**), but the Silver Hand and most steadings would see you put down.
