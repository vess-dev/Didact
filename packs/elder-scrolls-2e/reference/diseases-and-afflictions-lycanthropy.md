Lycanthropy is **not** caught like a disease. The beast-blood of Hircine is taken on willingly — most often by drinking the blood of a werewolf during the rites of the Companions' inner Circle. There is no save to resist a wound here; one becomes a werewolf by choice and ritual, not by infection.

The change is permanent once taken. It can be ended only by a deliberate cure (the rite at Ysgramor's Tomb) — or by contracting vampirism, since the two curses cannot share a single body. A werewolf carries Beast Blood and is immune to all mundane disease: any it carried is gone with the change.

> Playing a werewolf or werebear — Beast Form, feeding, the costs of the change, and the cure — is covered in Unusual PCs.
