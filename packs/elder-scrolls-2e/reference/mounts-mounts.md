A horse will not win your battles, but it will carry your loot, outrun a pack of wolves, and shorten the long, cold roads between the Holds. In Skyrim a sound mount is a working animal, not a war machine — it adds carrying space and eats away at the misery of travel. For an ordinary mount nothing more is tracked: no health pool, no fodder ledger, no rules for it falling apart. A mount is gear that breathes. (Only the storied steeds at the end of this chapter, who fight at your side, get a stat line.)
