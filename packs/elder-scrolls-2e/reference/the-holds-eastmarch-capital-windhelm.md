A northeastern Hold where snowfields steam over sulfur hot springs. **Windhelm**, the ancient City of Kings, is the oldest settlement in Skyrim — black stone and bitter cold — and the beating heart of the rebellion. It also shelters a crowded, resentful quarter of Dunmer refugees who are given little welcome.

- **Jarl:** **Ulfric Stormcloak**, leader of the uprising, who slew the High King and now rules from the *Palace of the Kings*. Should the Legion win the Hold, the fair-minded **Brunwulf Free-Winter** — a friend to the city's outcast Dunmer — takes his seat.
- **The barrow:** *Ansilvund*, an excavated Nordic crypt in the Velothi Mountains at the Hold's far southeastern edge, where a widowed conjurer who could not raise her husband raises the tomb's draugr instead.
- **The Dwemer ruin:** *Mzulft*, in the mountains southeast of Windhelm — a Dwarven observatory the Synod's mages went into and did not come out of.
- **The notable site:** the *Palace of the Kings* itself, seat of the rebellion and the oldest hall in Skyrim; and the *Gray Quarter*, where Dunmer refugees live cramped and unwelcome — a tinderbox of grievance the war keeps stoking.
- **Other corners:** the *Temple of Talos* (where the banned god is still openly honored), the *Stone Quarter* and the noble street of *Valunstrad*, the city docks (departure point for the boat to Solstheim); out in the Hold, *Kynesgrove* (where a dragon is seen rising from its mound), *Darkwater Crossing* (a mining camp), *Fort Amol*, and *Mistwatch* (a ruined keep turned bandit lair with a bitter story inside).
- **Troubles:** a string of murders stalks the Gray Quarter, and the guard will not trouble themselves over dead Dunmer. A dragon is raised from the burial mound at Kynesgrove before the party's eyes. A man hires the party to bring his wife home from the bandits of Mistwatch — and the truth is uglier than a kidnapping.
- **Faces:** *Ulfric Stormcloak*, charismatic and uncompromising, a hero to his own and a tyrant-in-waiting to his foes. *Galmar Stone-Fist*, Ulfric's gruff housecarl and war-chief, who runs the rebellion's musters and tests every would-be recruit.
