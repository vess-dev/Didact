Skyrim is the cold roof of the world — a northern province of tundra and pine, of glacier and geyser, where the wind never quite stops and the dead do not always stay buried. It is the ancestral home of the **Nords**, hardy folk descended from the lost continent of Atmora, who prize strength, plain speaking, mead, and a good death over a long one. This is the year **4E 201**. A civil war has split the nine Holds between those loyal to the failing Empire and the rebel Stormcloaks, and into that strife something older has woken: the **dragons**, gone from the sky for an age, are wheeling over the mountains once more. What follows is the lay of the land — enough to walk into a Skyrim tavern and know where you stand.
