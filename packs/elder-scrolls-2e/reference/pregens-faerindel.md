3 HP, 0 Armor, 8 STR, 10 DEX, 15 WIL, iron mace (d8)

- **Altmer Restoration Healer**, age 112. **Highborn:** once per day, channel a Spell Tome without adding Fatigue.
- **Background:** you learned to lay hands on the sick at the Temple of Mara in Riften, and the hurt and frightened calm at your touch. Your surgeon's kit mends by craft, not magic: an hour's work restores 1d4 STR to someone.
- **Carries:** **Spell Tome: Healing Hands**, iron mace, healer's robes (_petty_), bandages and simples (3 uses), surgeon's kit (_bulky_, 3 uses), soul gem (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), Lantern with an Oil flask (6 uses), 12 Septims. _(8 slots)_
- **Bond:** you carry a **soul gem** holding the trapped soul of someone you loved. A necromancer in the Reach claims he can free them — for a price you do not want to pay.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Apprentice.
- **Who you are:** a Summerset noble who walked out of the Dominion's service after the Great War, and who tends Nords that spit at your pointed ears — because Mara asks it, and because no one else will.
