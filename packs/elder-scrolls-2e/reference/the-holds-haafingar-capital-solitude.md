A small, sheer coastal Hold of sea-cliffs and salt wind in the far northwest, and the seat of Imperial power in Skyrim. **Solitude** straddles a colossal natural arch of stone above the Karth delta — the largest city in the province and the home of the High King's court.

- **Jarl:** **Elisif the Fair**, young widow of the slain High King Torygg and figurehead of the Imperial cause. As the seat of the Legion, Haafingar does not change hands unless the rebellion wins the entire war.
- **The barrow:** the Hold's Nordic dead lie in the highlands and along the coast, *Volskygge* in the far western mountains the greatest of their tombs; the great holy site here is *Mount Kilkreath*, where the shrine and statue of the Daedric Prince Meridia crown a Nordic ruin — light against the necromancer Malkoran, who has fouled her temple with the war's dead.
- **The notable site:** the *Thalmor Embassy*, a fortified manor in the mountains above the city where First Emissary Elenwen hosts her glittering, poisonous parties; and *Castle Dour*, the grim fortress that is the Imperial Legion's headquarters under General Tullius.
- **Other corners:** the *Blue Palace* (Elisif's court), the *Bards College* and the *Temple of the Divines* within the walls, the *East Empire Company* warehouse on the docks; *Dragon Bridge* (a village over the Karth, with the Penitus Oculatus' lone Skyrim outpost), *Fort Hraggstad*, and the Thalmor prison of *Northwatch Keep* on the coast.
- **Troubles:** a captive languishes in Northwatch Keep and someone wants them freed. The Bards College needs a lost verse recovered to reinstate a banned festival. A masked ball at the Thalmor Embassy is the perfect cover for a theft, a rescue, or a murder — if the party can talk their way past Elenwen's wards.
- **Faces:** *Elisif the Fair*, grieving and uncertain, leaning on Imperial advisors she does not entirely trust. *Elenwen*, the smiling, ruthless First Emissary of the Thalmor, who keeps a dossier on everyone who matters.
