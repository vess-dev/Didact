Many factions are sworn enemies. Climbing with one shoves you down the other's ladder. When the party rises a step with a faction, drop them a step with its opposite if word would plausibly reach it.

- **Stormcloaks ↔ Imperial Legion** — you cannot ride for both armies; the Civil War admits no fence-sitting once you take up arms.
- **Dawnguard ↔ Volkihar** — the vampire hunters and the vampire court are at open war over the sun itself.
- **Vigil of Stendarr ↔ Daedric cults** — the Vigil exists to burn out Daedra-worship, witches, and the undead; pleasing a Daedric Prince earns their hatred.
- **Thieves Guild ↔ city guards** — every Hold's guard is the Guild's natural foe; a thief in good standing is a marked figure to the law.
- **Greybeards ↔ Blades** — the Blades want the Greybeards' master, the dragon Paarthurnax, dead; once that demand is spoken, serving one order closes the other's door.
- **The Thalmor distrust nearly everyone** — Talos-worshippers, Stormcloaks, the Blades, and the Forsworn are all enemies of the Dominion. Pleasing the Thalmor almost always costs you somewhere else.

The Companions and the Bards College keep mostly to themselves and have no sworn rival worth tracking — they sit outside the opposed pairs.
