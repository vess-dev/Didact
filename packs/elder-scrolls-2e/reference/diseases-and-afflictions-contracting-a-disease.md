- When you are bitten, scratched, or otherwise exposed by a source listed below, the Warden calls for a **STR save**. On a failure, you contract that disease and are **Diseased** until cured. On a success, you shrug it off.
- A diseased character notes the affliction and applies its single ongoing effect. The penalty is not a wound and does not heal with a short rest, a week's rest, or a healing potion — only a proper cure removes it.
- Multiple diseases stack: each is tracked separately, though any single cure clears them all (see below).
- **Argonians** never catch ordinary disease (see their race trait in Character Creation).
- **Vampires, werewolves and werebears, Dremora, and draugr revenants** are **immune to mundane disease** and never save against the afflictions in the table below. (Sanguinare Vampiris cannot take hold in one already cursed.)
