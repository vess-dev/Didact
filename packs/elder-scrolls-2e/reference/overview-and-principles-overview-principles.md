It is the year 4E 201, and the province of Skyrim is coming apart. A war between those loyal to the Empire and the rebel Stormcloaks burns through the nine Holds, and into that strife something far older has returned: the dragons, long thought to be only legend, are once more in the cold northern sky. You play adventurers loosed upon this land — sellswords and spellcasters, thieves and pilgrims — picking your way through barrows and ruins, through tundra and ash, in search of coin, glory, or simply the next sunrise. This chapter lays out how the game is played and the handful of ideas that keep it running well at the table.
