### Agency

- Your Attributes and the saves tied to them are not who your character is. They are merely tools.
- Don't ask only what your character would do — ask what *you* would do, here, with this inventory and this predicament.
- Lean on your wits, your gear, and the bonds you have forged.

### Teamwork

- Find agreement with the others before charging ahead.
- Keep a shared sense of the goals and the limits, respect one another, and you will accomplish far more as a band than apart.

### Exploration

- Asking good questions and listening closely will serve you better than any statistic, item, or knack you possess.
- Take the Warden's description at face value, but never be shy about pressing for more.
- There is no single right path through a Nordic tomb or a Dwemer ruin.

### Talking

- Treat the folk you meet as real people, and let your curiosity be the safe way to gather what you need and untangle your problems.
- You will find most people interesting, and most would rather talk a matter through than draw steel.

### Caution

- A fight is a choice, and seldom a wise one. Weigh whether violence truly serves your ends.
- Tilt the odds your way before you commit, and fall back when the moment turns against you.

### Planning

- Look for ways around your obstacles — scout ahead, move unseen, dig up what you can.
- Do your asking. Trade a few Septims for a drunkard's rumor; learn the lay of the land before you walk into it.

### Ambition

- Set yourself a goal and use what little you have to take the next step toward it.
- Expect nothing handed to you. Earn your name.
- Keep things moving, and play to find out what happens.
