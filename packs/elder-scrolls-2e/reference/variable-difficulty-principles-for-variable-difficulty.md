title: "Principles For Variable Difficulty"
tags: [variable, difficulty]

---
- Reward thinking sideways. The payoff might be a smaller risk, a useful new fact, a bit of character growth, or something else entirely.
- Scale the result to the effort. If the party worked hard to pull something off despite real danger, let the reward match the struggle.
- Lean on fictional consequences over mechanical bonuses. A flat numeric reward tends to ring hollow next to a real change in the world.
- Many dangers can be reduced or removed given enough time, effort, and skill. Once a threat is gone, skip the roll.
- Build situations that invite non-mechanical answers. A problem is far more satisfying when its solution lives entirely in the fiction.
