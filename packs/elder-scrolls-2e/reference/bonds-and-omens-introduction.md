Bonds and Omens hand a new character adventure hooks, a scrap of starting gear, or a small ability — and they hand the Warden a setting half-built for free. They are distinct from a character's **Background**, but they do the same heavy lifting: tying a person to Skyrim and giving the table something to chase. A single well-rolled Bond can seed a whole arc; a single Omen can be the spine of an entire campaign.

This chapter is the *design guidance* for writing your own. The ready-to-use tables already live in the Player's Guide — see the **Bonds** and **Omens** tables in the **Character Creation** chapter. Use those at the table; use this chapter when you want to build Bonds and Omens of your own, tuned to your Holds, your factions, and the troubles brewing in your Skyrim.
