### Map

_(Sketch the map by hand: nine numbered POIs strung along five trails — the Old Imperial Road, the Ridgeline Heights, the Foraging Path, the Dry Riverbed, and the hunters' Game Trail. Entrance at 1, exit at 9.)_

### The Wild

**Description**: Diseased, Frozen

**Name**: the Oak-Warden's Wood

A black-pine wood in southern Falkreath Hold, sickening with rot. In places the trees and the very air have frozen hard as glass.

### Denizens

**Traits**: Stable, Territorial

The wood's creatures long for the old, settled order, but the land's ancient boundaries hem them in and turn them against trespassers.

**Wilderness Agenda**: Purification, Infestation

The spirit of the wood wants only to burn out the sickness in its roots. A nest of Frostbite Spiders (POI **8**) has strung silk through the trees, killing far more than they eat and unraveling the whole web of life here.

### The Theme

For a generation, peace held in the wood through a pact between the wise woman **Halvdis** and the **Oak-Warden**, a hunter who long ago gave himself to the grove's spriggans and became Kyne's keeper of the wood. The wise woman's magic kept the trees and beasts hale; the Oak-Warden saw to it that hunters never took any creature marked as essential to the wood — those bearing a streak of white in their pelts. The pact was binding and unbending, and it split the wood in two, each keeper barred from crossing into the other's ground.

When old Halvdis died, one of her wardings slipped loose and soured. A creeping magical blight now spreads through the pines and the beasts, threatening to swallow the whole wood. The hunters of Falkreath call it the **Hoarfrost Rot**, for it drains the warmth and life from everything it touches and leaves it gray and brittle.

### Trails

#### Old Imperial Road, Worn Cart Ruts

The path between **1** and **5** is broken by a sheer crag and demands climbing irons to scale. Deep ruts worn by old Imperial supply wagons still scar the road.

#### Ridgeline Heights, Stone Piles

The pilgrims who tended **7** raised a small cairn beside the path for each of their dead.

#### Foraging Path, Faint Chanting

Halvdis lived chiefly on the berries and roots that grow thick along this trail. Her magic ran so deep that an echo of her lingers: those who walk it still catch her low, tuneless humming.

#### Dry Riverbed, Fresh Spiderwebs

A stream once ran down the wood's west side until a dry season killed it. Frostbite Spiders now travel the old bed toward **8**, trailing silk as they go.

#### Game Trail, Claw Marks

The Oak-Warden suffers outsiders to take their quarry on this trail and no other — so long as they never kill a beast with white in its coat.

### POIs

### 1 - Frozen Camp [Entrance]

**Ruin**: _Ash-Buried Camp_, _Overrun with Strange Flora_

At the wood's edge sits a dead **campfire** with heaps of frost-gray **bedding** strewn about. Pale **flowers** and creepers grow thickly over the whole site. A **trail** north leads into the trees.

- **Campfire**: The leftover wood is stained deep gray, ice-cold to the touch, and hard as iron.
- **Bedding**: Splinters of gray wood are caught in the cloth.
  - Cold as a glacier to the touch. Beneath the cloth the ground shows patches of blue and red, but gray rings spread out from each fleck of wood.
- **Flowers**: Rare blooms, not native to this part of Falkreath.
  - Careful pruning and a few minutes' work yields a **Healing Salve** (restore 1d4 STR, 1 use).
- **Trail**: Long furrows roughly the shape of a tree trunk are gouged into the ground.
  - Fine spiderwebs curtain the way into the wood, as though no one has passed in a long while.

### 2 - The Amber Tarn

**Hazard**: _Sap Trap_, _Ghosts Trapped in Crystal_

A ring of deep-red **pines** encloses a wide **pool** of bubbling sap. A pointed green **shape** is just visible at its center.

- **Pines**: Golden sap weeps from the base of each tree and runs down into the pool. They show no sign of the Rot.
- **Pool**: Sticky sap, twelve feet across.
  - Ankle-deep, fiercely sticky, and an irritant on bare skin.
- **Shape**: Crystalline in appearance.
  - Very slippery, and slow, difficult work to free without the right tools (gloves, a pole, and so on).
  - Trapped inside the crystal is the spirit of a familiar named **Vethri**.
  - If the crystal is shattered, the shadow of a great hawk rises over the party, then is gone. Any party member who is Deprived has the condition lifted.

#### Vethri

- Can speak mind to mind with whoever holds the crystal.
  - Will beg to be let out. (A solid blow with something hard does it.)
  - In return for freedom, Vethri offers the party a black feather, saying it will let them pass into Halvdis's Hut at **4**.

### 3 - The Oak-Warden's Lodge

**Shelter**: _Hunter's Lodge_, _A Meal Already Laid Out_

On a low hill stands a timber **lodge**. Short **logs** are laid out in rows along the west wall, and a tall drying **rack** is fixed to the east wall.

- **Lodge**: There is a 3-in-6 chance the Oak-Warden is here; otherwise he is out walking the wood. A ring of white dust is scattered across the threshold.
  - If the dust is disturbed, the Oak-Warden knows at once and comes to investigate.
- **Logs**: Stacked four by four.
  - Each bears the stained outline of an animal (bear, rabbit, beaver). A fresh drop of white marks the rabbit.
- **Rack**: Various hides are stretched tight to dry.
  - Sewn together, the hides make two suits of hide armor (light armor, 1 Armor each), highly resistant to cold and the elements.

#### Inside the Lodge

Spare and bare, holding only a simple **cot** and a small **table**.

- **Cot**: Speckled with green moss and twigs. It smells faintly of rain.
  - The floor beneath is thick with moss. If the Oak-Warden lays his hands on it, the moss parts to reveal a tunnel down to **4**. Only he can open this passage.
- **Table**: A full meal of roast venison and root vegetables is set out, enough to feed two.
  - A gift from Halvdis's lingering magic. If eaten, it reforms in d12 hours.

#### The Oak-Warden

12 HP, 2 Armor, 13 STR, 15 DEX, 17 WIL, hunting bow (d6), spore burst (d6, _blast_)

- A surly, moss-bearded man who is more spriggan than not. When he is hurt, the wood shields and even heals him. Each time he falls, he returns greener than before.
- He longs to end the Hoarfrost Rot but cannot enter **6**, barred by his pact with Halvdis. As a test of the party's honor and skill, he asks them to clear the Frostbite Spiders from **8**.
  - If they succeed at this first task, he gives them a **Pendant of Kyne** and promises greater reward if they can break the magic ravaging the wood at **6**.
  - If they manage to disrupt the Rot, he opens the hidden tunnel to **4**, telling them the hut there is theirs to enter.

#### Pendant of Kyne (_petty_)

- A carved wooden charm of the Hawk-Mother. While in the Wilds, add a Fatigue to borrow the senses of any one beast nearby.

### 4 - Halvdis's Hut

**Shelter**: _Wise Woman's Hut_, _Hunting Is Forbidden Here_

A round, chimneyed **hut** smothered in thick **vines** stands at the edge of a small clearing. A wooden **sign** is driven into the ground at the west door. A low wattle **fence** runs along the north side.

- **Hut**: The front windows are dark; no light or smoke shows within.
  - The door has no keyhole and opens only to friends of Halvdis, the Oak-Warden, or Vethri.
  - If the door is forced, Halvdis's last ward wakes: whoever forced it must pass a WIL save or wander the wood lost until dawn, always circling back to this clearing.
- **Vines**: Thick, leafy growth blankets the roof and the windows.
  - Nightshade and deathbell grow among them, planted on purpose; a careful harvester can gather enough for 1d4 doses of poison.
- **Sign**: Vine-wrapped. In red letters it reads: "No Hunting. Harsh penalties."
  - Halvdis warded this place. If any creature is killed here by something capable of speech, the killer turns to stone instantly (no save).
  - Nothing dangerous will follow the party into this corner of the wood.
- **Fence**: Rings a small, withered garden. A vine-grown stone statue of a young hunter stands at its center.
  - The horrified hunter (a victim of the clearing's ward) holds a limp rabbit upside down, a knife at its throat.

#### Inside the Hut

Richly furnished. A tall wooden **barrel** stands by the north hearth, a long broom jutting from it. A large **stove** is set against the east wall. A **string** of herbs and roots hangs from the ceiling.

- **Barrel**: Tightly coopered and free of dust. Inside, packed in straw, are Halvdis's last brews: two **Potions of Healing** and a **Potion of Cure Disease**.
- **Stove**: Warm, and broad enough to sleep on. A red scarf is knotted around one iron handle.
  - The scarf was the Oak-Warden's gift to Halvdis, otherwise plain. He would dearly love it back.
- **String**: Herbs and roots of rare, near-extinct quality. Worth 500 Septims to the right buyer.
  - A **nirnroot** hangs among them, still faintly chiming. Any alchemist would pay 100 Septims for it, and the old wives say it rings louder when a lie is told nearby.

### 5 - The Fallen Pine at the Crossroads

**Monster**: _Cave Bear_, _Guarding_

A long fallen **pine** lies across a small glade where two **trails** meet. A sharp **cliff** to the south drops steeply to the foot of the wood.

- **Pine**: Vast and partly hollowed out. Bear tracks mark the east opening.
  - A Cave Bear and her three cubs den inside. They sleep through the deep winter; any other season they are awake. The mother attacks anyone who enters the hollow (skip the reaction roll).
- **Trails**: Broken needles and fresh rubbings make the bear's presence plain.
- **Cliff**: Iron rings are driven into the rock face.
  - Hammered in when the wood was still being logged, but still sound. Ideal for ropes.

#### Cave Bear

10 HP, 15 STR, 12 DEX, 5 WIL, claws (d8+d8), bite (d8)

- A huge, dark-furred bear of Skyrim's caves and pine woods, and a mother defending her cubs.
- It roars before it charges, giving fair warning, and will not follow far from the den.
- **Critical Damage**: A swat breaks the target's ribs; they cannot run or carry a _bulky_ load until they mend.

### 6 - The Gray Grove

**Hazard**: _Frozen Troll_, _A Spell Gone Wrong_

A washed-out forest **grove** lies where four trails cross. At its center, two enormous **statues** seem locked in a wrestling hold.

- **Grove**: Every plant nearby is gray and lifeless, as though its color has been bled away.
  - The air shimmers with magic, strongest of all above the two statues.
  - The Hoarfrost Rot is visible even before one enters the clearing — it has begun creeping outward in every direction.
  - Any living plant carried into the grove starts to petrify, even untouched.
- **Statues**: Two frost trolls, frozen mid-struggle over a shiny object the larger holds high.
  - The object is a **Webspinner's Glass**, a hand-mirror sacred to Mephala, whose lies have unbalanced the warding that once kept this place a green haven — and drawn her spiders to the wood.
  - Halvdis's warding can only be undone by taking the mirror out of the clearing or destroying it outright. The statue grips it tightly, but it can be freed with grease, or by breaking the troll's arm.
  - Break the spell and the magic lifts; the trolls slowly thaw and wake. They will at once try to take the mirror back.

#### Webspinner's Glass (_petty_, 1 charge)

- A Daedric Relic of the Webspinner. Spend its charge while a person's reflection is caught in it, and you learn the secret they most want kept. Using it becomes a craving: a day without looking into it leaves the owner Deprived.
- **Recharge:** tell a lie that costs someone dearly.

#### Frost Troll

16 HP, 1 Armor, 16 STR, 12 DEX, 4 WIL, claws (d8+d8), bite (d8)

- A hulking, three-eyed, white-furred troll of the high cold places, fiercely territorial. These two are still stiff from their long freeze: in the first round after they wake, they may move or attack, not both.
- **Regeneration**: it regains 1d4 HP each round unless fire has touched it since its last turn. Fire damage against it is _Enhanced_; frost damage is _Impaired_.
- **Critical Damage**: it tears off one of the target's limbs (the Warden decides which).

### 7 - The Drowned Shrine

**Ruin**: _Flooded Shrine_, _A Hidden Cache_

Three flooded **buildings** rise from the middle of a small pond, their peaked **roofs** just breaking the surface. On the north side, a dry riverbed runs straight into the pond. The east path is lined with dozens of piled **stones**.

- **Buildings**: Old and weathered, carved with the quiet, faded iconography of Kyne. Plainly a wayside shrine.
  - Flooded after the dam at **8** came down.
- **Roofs**: The tallest building has a glass window high above the water.
  - Rusted shut, but it can be pried or broken open to reach the shrine's upper hall.
- **Stones**: Heaped along the south side of the east path. Most are carved with names.
  - The pilgrims raised a cairn beside the path for each of their dead.

#### The Upper Hall

Faded **banners** hang on the walls of this round room, its plank **floor** all but rotted through from the damp. A broken rail on the southwest side is all that is left of the flooded **stairway** down into the rest of the shrine.

- **Banners**: Moldered past saving.
  - They once showed the shrine in its prime and the pilgrims who kept it.
- **Floor**: One board is less rotted than the rest. It smells faintly of lavender.
  - Beneath it lies a hidden **Spell Tome: Clairvoyance**.
- **Stairway**: Wholly underwater. The rooms below hold nothing but floating corpses and ruined books.

#### Spell Tome: Clairvoyance

- A glowing path unspools from the reader's feet toward a place or thing they name and know of (see **Spell Tomes**). _This copy smells of lavender, and its path always passes one flower more than it needs to._

### 8 - The Spider Mass

**Hazard**: _Spider Nest_, _An Injured Creature_

A white-streaked **elk** limps and bellows near a great **tangle** of silk and fallen trees. Fat, shriveled **carcasses** hang in spun silk from the treetops. A dry **riverbed** to the south is choked with piles of timber.

- **Elk**: Half-wrapped in thick silk, it bellows pitifully.
  - Poisoned by a spider's bite in its hindquarters. If the party helps it, the wood will remember.
- **Tangle**: Threads of heavy black silk bind a 10-foot heap of timber.
  - The nest holds three **Frostbite Spiders**. They attack anyone who meddles with their food or comes too near.
  - 200 Septims' worth of silk can be carefully gathered from the nest. It makes a fine rope (about 60 feet), uncommonly strong and prized for shedding water.
- **Carcasses**: Mostly animals. A human shape shows through the black webbing.
  - The man was a hunter who ignored the Oak-Warden's warning and took game north of the Game Trail. On the body are Rations (2 uses left), 33 Septims, and a hip-knife (d6).
- **Riverbed**: Timber is strewn everywhere. Black silk is tied around the longer pieces.
  - It was once a beaver dam that kept the water to a trickle. The spiders tore it down to build their nest. The flooding drowned the shrine at **7**, though the river itself only dried up in a drought this past year.

#### Elk

4 HP, 12 STR, 12 DEX, 6 WIL, antlers (d6)

- One of the beasts the Oak-Warden guards: note the streak of white in its coat.

#### Frostbite Spider

6 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 12 DEX, 4 WIL, venomous bite (d8), poison spit (d6, _ranged_)

- A hairy, dog-sized arachnid of cave and wood; it drops from the branches and spits even when facing away.
- Its venom lingers: the first time it hits a creature in a fight, that creature must pass a STR save or lose 1d4 STR, and the venom keeps working for up to three rounds more (see **Hazards**).
- **Critical Damage**: The venom locks the victim's limbs; they are wrapped in silk and hauled up into the nest.

### 9 - The Fishing Shack

**Shelter**: _Fishing Shack_, _Held by Army Deserters_ [Exit]

A ramshackle **shack** stands beside a small **tarn** to the north. **Smoke** drifts from a cooking rack on the west side, and loud **voices** carry through the south window.

- **Shack**: Built of plain logs lashed with rope that looks fresh. Fishing line runs out the north side and into the tarn.
- **Tarn**: Still, but thick with insects. A path on the west side leads out of the wood.
  - It has not been fished in some time and is badly overstocked. Easy pickings.
- **Smoke**: Rises from a wooden cooking rack beside a pile of fried fish (4 × Rations, 3 uses each).
- **Voices**: Laughter and muffled talk. At least two people are inside.
  - Three deserters from the Civil War squat here — conscripts, sick of the killing, who fled rather than fight kin against kin. They are jovial and kind but utter cowards. They mean to shelter in the shrine at **7** but have not gone that far.
  - They know little of the wood, only that a river once ran south from the tarn and ended somewhere near a shrine.
