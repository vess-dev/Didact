Any one of the following clears **all** mundane diseases you carry:

- A night's **rest at a temple of the Divines**.
- Drinking a **Potion of Cure Disease**.
- A **shrine blessing** (kneeling at any shrine of the Nine).
- The attentions of a **Vigilant of Stendarr**.
- A rare **Restoration rite or Relic** made to cleanse disease.

No common Spell Tome cures disease; cleansing is the work of the faithful and the alchemist.
