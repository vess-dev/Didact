| Room & board at an inn (per night) | 10 |
| Private room & board (fits 4) | 35 |
| Stable & feed (per night) | 5 |
| Temple healing (restoration of an Attribute) | 50 |
| Rations (3 uses) | 10 |
| Animal feed (3 uses, _bulky_) | 5 |

A night's rest at an inn restores HP and clears Fatigue as a safe sleep does. A temple of the Divines (Kynareth, Mara, Stendarr, and the rest) will restore Attribute loss and, for the faithful or the paying, lift a disease or tend a Scar. Some temple tending is free to the devout; restoration on demand costs.
