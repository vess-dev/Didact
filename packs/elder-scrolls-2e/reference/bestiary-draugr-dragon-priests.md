The dragon cult buried its honored dead in the deep barrows of Skyrim, bound to wake and slay anything that disturbs their rest. Draugr are immune to poison and disease, and frost damage against them is _Impaired_ — most wield it themselves. None can attack in the round it breaks free of its sarcophagus.
