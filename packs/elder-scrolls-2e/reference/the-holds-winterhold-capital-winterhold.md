The northernmost and bleakest Hold of all: a thin coast of ice, snow, and hardy fisherfolk facing the Sea of Ghosts. **Winterhold** was once a great city and a seat of kings — until the **Great Collapse** swept most of it into the sea, leaving a windblown handful of buildings, a bitter Jarl, and the one thing that did not fall: the **College of Winterhold**, perched alone on its sea-cliff pillar.

- **Jarl:** **Korir**, embittered by the ruin of his city, who blames the College for the Collapse and wishes the mages gone. Stormcloak-aligned, the Hold can fall to the Imperials, who seat the College-friendly **Kraldar**.
- **The barrow:** *Saarthal*, one of the oldest Nordic sites in all Tamriel — an ancient excavated city beneath the snow where the College digs, and where something the First Men buried was never meant to be unearthed. To the north lies *Ysgramor's Tomb*, resting place of the legendary founder of the Companions.
- **The Dwemer ruin:** *Alftand*, a great spiraling Dwarven ruin half-swallowed by a glacier, another of the descents into **Blackreach** and the deep dark beneath the world.
- **The notable site:** the *College of Winterhold* itself — the Hall of the Elements with its **Arcanaeum** library, the Arch-Mage's quarters, the mage-halls, and the strange **Midden** beneath — Skyrim's only refuge for the practice of magic, and an island of cold tolerance in a hostile Hold.
- **Other corners:** the *Jarl's Longhouse*, the inn called *The Frozen Hearth*, and the prison called *The Chill*; out in the Hold, *Mount Anthor* (a dragon's roost in the peaks).
- **Troubles:** the College's dig at Saarthal turns up a buried power, and a Thalmor agent within the walls means to seize it. A dragon has taken Mount Anthor and harries the coast. The townsfolk's hatred of the College threatens to boil over into violence against the mages.
- **Faces:** *Tolfdir*, the kindly, absent-minded master mage who teaches the College's newcomers and stands by them when crisis comes. *Korir*, the resentful Jarl of a drowned city, who sees enemies in every robe.
