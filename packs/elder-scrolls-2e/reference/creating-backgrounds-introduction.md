In The Elder Scrolls 2e, as in Cairn, a character's **Background** is the single most important element of who they are — their past and their present both. A Background shapes what a character knows, what they are good at, and how they fit into Skyrim. It is also a *filter* for reading the dice, or a reason not to roll at all: a Forsworn need not save to find her way through the crags of the Reach, and a College Apprentice can read the old Atmoran script without a roll. Most of all, a Background tells a story through a character's starting gear and personal history, binding them to the setting and its themes.

The Player's Guide ships fifty Backgrounds (see the **d50 Backgrounds** table in **Character Creation**). This chapter is for building your own — a Skaal shaman, a Riften dock-thug, a disgraced Penitus Oculatus agent, whatever your Skyrim needs.
