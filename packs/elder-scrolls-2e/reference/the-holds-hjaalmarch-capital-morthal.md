A poor, isolated Hold of fog-bound marshland — the Drajkmyr — in the north, of little strategic worth and less comfort. **Morthal** is a small, eerie lumber village wrapped in mist, governed by a Jarl who rules less by decree than by the visions that come to her.

- **Jarl:** **Idgrod Ravencrone**, a riddling mystic and seer who governs by prophecy and keeps Hjaalmarch nominally with the Empire while caring little for the war. The Hold can fall to the Stormcloaks, who seat **Sorli the Builder** of Stonehills.
- **The barrow:** *Ustengrav*, a great Nordic tomb sunk in the marsh, where Jurgen Windcaller, founder of the Greybeards, was laid to rest with his horn — draugr-haunted, flooded, and far older than the village above it.
- **The Dwemer ruin:** *Mzinchaleft*, in the snows east of Morthal — bandits camp in its upper halls, and its lowest gate opens on **Blackreach**.
- **The great ruin:** *Labyrinthian*, on the Hold's southern edge — a sprawling, fallen Nordic city and dragon-priest stronghold, one of the deadliest ruins in Skyrim, layered with traps, draugr, and an ancient masked lord.
- **The notable site:** *Movarth's Lair*, a cave near Morthal where a master vampire and his thralls prey on the isolated village from the dark.
- **Other corners:** the Morthal lumber mill, apothecary, and inn; *Stonehills* (a mining settlement at Rockwallow); *Fort Snowhawk* on the marsh road; and the cold, defensible plot of *Windstad Manor*, land a party may buy to build upon.
- **Troubles:** a house burns with a family inside, the townsfolk whisper of a ghost-child, and the Jarl wants the truth teased out of a frightened village. The vampire Movarth schemes to make all of Morthal his cattle. Idgrod has seen something coming in a dream and needs a stranger to go where her own people fear to.
- **Faces:** *Idgrod Ravencrone*, the unsettling seer-Jarl who speaks in portents and is rarely wrong. *Movarth Piquine*, the ancient master vampire in the marsh, patiently weaving a plan to devour a whole town.
