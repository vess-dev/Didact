To build a creature of your own — a Falmer mutation, a haunting from a barrow no map names, a thing dredged up from the Deep — follow these steps:

- Roll on the Monster Appearance table.
- Roll on the Monster Traits table.
- Roll on the Monster Attacks table.
- Roll on the Monster Abilities table.
- Describe the creature's look, powers, and attacks in plain language.
- Write up its stats using the principles, definitions, and stat block below.
