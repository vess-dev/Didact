Skyrim's place names follow plain instincts:

- **Settlements** are named for a feature — a river's bend, a watchtower, a windmill — and dressed with a noun or adjective: *Riverwood, Dragon Bridge, Windhelm, Shor's Stone.*
- **Important places get important names** — heroes, gods, kings, events: *Ysgramor's Tomb, the Throat of the World, Talos's Stand.*
- **Proper nouns attach or replace** part of a name: *Maven's Folly, Olaf's Verse.*
- **Dangerous places are named for their hazard** — *Bleak Falls, Frostmere, Cragslane.*
- **Some names tell a story** — *Lost Echo Cave, Halldir's Cairn, the Sundered Towers.*
- Lean on the **local terrain** and a place's details. Add **Nordic flavor** by tacking on a Nordic suffix or root: *-grave, -reach, -fell, -watch, -gard, -mire, -stead, -crag, -hall*, or *Dwemer* roots like *Bthar-, Nchuand-, Mzin-, -zel, -dum* for Dwarven ruins.

### Generating a Name

- Roll on the Names Formula for each point of interest (POI).
- Roll on the Adjectives and Nouns tables where the formula calls for them.
- To roll **1d100**, roll **2d10**: one die for the tens place, one for the ones.
- To name a **region** by its land, swap "POI" for the terrain type and consult Terrain Synonyms.

### Names Formula

| d6 | Formula |
|---|---|
| 1 | (The) **[Noun]** **[POI]** |
| 2 | (The) **[Adjective]** **[POI]** |
| 3 | **[POI]** of (the) **[Noun]** |
| 4 | **[Adjective]** **[POI]** of (the) **[Noun]** |
| 5 | (The) **[Noun]** **[Adjective]** **[POI]** |
| 6 | **[POI]** of (the) **[Adjective]** **[Noun]** |

*POI examples for Skyrim: Barrow, Cairn, Crypt, Tomb, Ruin, Hall, Watch, Tower, Fort, Keep, Hold, Mine, Pass, Bridge, Camp, Den, Cave, Falls, Stead, Mire, Stone, Shrine.*
