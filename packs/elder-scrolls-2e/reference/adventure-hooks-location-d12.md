*Where the job sends them. Roll one, or pick to match the task.*

| d12 | Where |
|---|---|
| 1 | A **Nordic barrow** — draugr, traps, claw-doors, a word wall in the deep. |
| 2 | A **Dwemer ruin** — bronze automatons, steam, Falmer in the dark below. |
| 3 | A **bandit-held fort or watchtower** astride a road. |
| 4 | A **frozen cave or ice cavern** — trolls, frostbite spiders, a buried thing. |
| 5 | A **Forsworn redoubt** in the crags of the Reach. |
| 6 | A **wilderness expanse** — open tundra, a pine wood, a glacier, a mountain pass. |
| 7 | A **walled Hold city** and its under-streets, docks, or sewers. |
| 8 | A **farmstead, mine, or logging camp** out in the country. |
| 9 | A **Daedric shrine** or a cursed grove where a Prince's hand lies heavy. |
| 10 | A **ruined or working temple** of the Divines or the old Nordic dead. |
| 11 | A **noble manor or Jarl's hall** — politics, guards, and locked rooms. |
| 12 | **Solstheim** — ash, the Skaal, Telvanni towers, the reach of Miraak (DLC). |
