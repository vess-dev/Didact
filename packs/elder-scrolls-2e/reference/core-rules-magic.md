Magic in Skyrim is real, common enough to be feared, and dangerous enough to be respected. The spells of the five schools the College teaches — Destruction, Restoration, Alteration, Illusion, and Conjuration — and of old Mysticism, which it no longer does, are bound into **Spell Tomes**. The lingering charges of long-dead enchanters and Daedric pacts persist in **Relics**. And the careful work of a Court Wizard or a College mage can be captured, once, on a **Scroll**.

### Spell Tomes

- **Spell Tomes** contain a single spell and take up one slot. They cannot be easily transcribed or created; instead they are recovered from places like barrows, Dwemer ruins, mages' studies, and the vaults of the College of Winterhold.
- Spell Tomes sometimes display unusual properties or limitations, such as producing a foul or unearthly smell when opened, possessing an innate intelligence, or being legible only when held in moonlight.
- Spell Tomes will attract the attention of those who seek the arcane power within, and it is considered dangerous to display them openly. The Thalmor, in particular, take an unhealthy interest in who reads what.

### Casting Spells

- Anyone can cast a spell by holding a Spell Tome in both hands and reading its contents aloud. They must then add a **Fatigue** to inventory.
- Given time and safety, PCs can _enhance_ a spell's impact (e.g., affecting multiple targets, increasing its power, etc.) without any additional cost.
- If the PC is Deprived or in danger (such as during combat), the Warden may require a PC to make a WIL save to avoid any ill-effects from casting the spell. Consequences of failure are on par with the intended effect and may result in added **Fatigue**, the destruction of the Spell Tome, injury, and even death. (Magicka is not a tame thing.)

### Scrolls

**Scrolls** are similar to Spell Tomes, however:

- They are _petty_.
- They do not cause **Fatigue**.
- They disappear after one use.

### Relics

**Relics** are items imbued with a magical spell or power — an enchanted blade, a Daedric artifact, a charged staff. They do not cause Fatigue. Relics usually have limited use, as well as a **Recharge** condition. Their charges are most often replenished with a filled soul gem or by satisfying some stranger, costlier rite.
