Skyrim is but one province of the continent of **Tamriel**, and the wider world presses in at every border: the Empire's heartland of Cyrodiil to the south, the deserts of Hammerfell and the kingdoms of High Rock to the west, ash-choked Morrowind to the east, and beyond it the swamps of Black Marsh. Closest of all is **Solstheim**, a windswept island in the Sea of Ghosts between Skyrim and Morrowind, reached by boat from Windhelm — a place of ash and snow where Dunmer colonists, isolationist Telvanni wizards, and the All-Maker-worshipping Skaal all dwell beneath the shadow of distant Red Mountain. A traveler's road need not end at Skyrim's edge, but for now, the cold north is trouble enough.
