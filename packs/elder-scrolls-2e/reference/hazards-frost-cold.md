Skyrim is a land of snow, and the cold is patient.

- A PC who spends a **watch** in snow or bitter cold **without warm clothing, shelter, or fire** becomes **Deprived**. While Deprived they cannot recover HP, Attributes, or clear Fatigue (see **Core Rules → Deprivation & Fatigue**). Each further watch so exposed adds **1 Fatigue**. Furs, a fur-lined cloak, or wilderness clothes count as warm clothing.
- **Extreme** weather — a full blizzard or a Reach ice-storm — leaves anyone without shelter **Deprived at once**, warm clothes or not (see **Procedures → Weather**).
- Plunging into **icy water** (a frozen river, a glacial lake, the seas off Solstheim) is **drowning *and* cold**: resolve drowning normally, and count the PC as Deprived from the cold the moment they go under.

Frostbite and the slow numbing of the limbs are narrative. If exposure turns severe, the Warden may apply a result from the **Scars** table rather than tracking it further. A warm hearth, a Resist Frost effect, or a Nord's hardy blood all stave it off.

> The party is caught on the high tundra above Winterhold when the sky closes in. With no shelter raised before the blizzard hits, every one of them is **Deprived** at once — no short rest, no clearing the day's Fatigue — until they reach a cave, a wayshrine, or a steading and get a fire going.
