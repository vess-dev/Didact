The Elder Scrolls runs at very different temperatures. Show the dial to your players and let them put a pin in it.

| Setting | What It Feels Like |
|---|---|
| **Heroic** | The party are the heroes the songs will name. Dragons fall. Jarls owe favors. Violence is cinematic, evil is clear, and the right people win in the end. Lean light on Bounty and disease; let the party be larger than life. |
| **Adventurous** | The default. Capable but mortal adventurers earning coin and a name one job at a time — barrows, bandits, bounties, the odd dragon. Wins are real but partial; the world keeps turning whether or not the party shows up. |
| **Gritty** | Skyrim is poor, cold, and at war. Coin is tight, the dead are many, and every faction wants something. Heroes get Scars and make enemies. Sympathy is rationed; the right choice usually costs. |
| **Grim** | A hard land in a hard year. Hypothermia, plague, vampires, and a war that grinds up everyone it touches. Survival is the story. A barrow is a tomb you might not leave. Violence is ugly and consequences last. |

A campaign can shift along the dial over its length — a heroic saga can have a grim chapter. Commit to one setting *per session* so the table knows what kind of evening it is.
