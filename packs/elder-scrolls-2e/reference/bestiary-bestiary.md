The wilds and ruins of Skyrim are crowded with things that bite, freeze, drain, and devour. This bestiary collects the creatures a Warden is most likely to throw at a party of adventurers: the restless draugr of the Nordic barrows, the dragons of the returning World-Eater, the clockwork sentinels of the Dwemer, the blind Falmer of the deep, the Daedra of the planes of Oblivion, and the beasts, vampires, and bandits of the surface world.

Each entry opens with a stat line and a short description. Stats follow the Cairn frame exactly: HP measures the knack for avoiding a killing blow (ordinary folk 2–4, soldiers and beasts 3–6, serious threats 7–12, legends 12–18), STR, DEX, and WIL run 0 (mindless) to 18 (legendary), Armor never exceeds 3, and damage dice run d4 to d12. Groups of four or more similar foes fight as a single _detachment_, and so do a few creatures big enough to fight like an army. Resistances and weaknesses use the ordinary Effect vocabulary: "fire damage against it is _Enhanced_" steps an attack up to d12; "mundane attacks against it are _Impaired_" drops them to d4; where both apply to one attack, they cancel and it rolls its ordinary die. **Critical Damage** lines say what happens to a victim who fails the save against this creature's blow.

Many entries list **variants** — tougher or stranger tiers of the same creature. Run a variant by swapping in its noted stats; they are not separate cards unless they behave differently enough to warrant one.

Reskin freely. A "Bandit Chief" is a fine Forsworn Ravager, a vampire master, or a Stormcloak deserter; a Draugr Overlord serves equally as any barrow's final guardian.
