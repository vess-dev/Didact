### Trail Types

- **Standard** trails are obvious, well-trodden routes between two points. _Drawn as a thick line._
- **Hidden** trails appear on no known map. Finding one always costs something. _Drawn as a dashed line._
- **Conditional** trails may be blocked, or demand particular gear (a boat, climbing irons, snowshoes) to cross. Some cannot be followed at all without a map or a guide. _Drawn as a crossed line._

### Draw Trails

- Beginning at the entrance, draw 1–4 paths between each point.
  - Make loops, branches, dead ends, and shortcuts between points.
  - Some paths are Conditional, drawn as a crossed line.
  - Some paths are Hidden, drawn as a dashed line.
  - Where it fits, make intersections (crossed lines) with no points between them.
- Form trails by tracing over paths that link 3–4 points in an obvious, consistent, or interesting way.
  - Hidden paths and short, isolated routes are not usually part of a trail.
  - Use colored pencils or crayons to keep different trails apart.
- Roll on the Trail Features table for each trail and note the results.
