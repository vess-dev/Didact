This procedure helps you grow the _seed_ of a setting: the topography, natural features, peoples, and paths a party meets as it crosses a stretch of Skyrim. Start with the folk who live there — their temper, their ambitions, and the resources that bind them to the land. Then raise the terrain of the region, scatter its points of interest (its landmarks, settlements, barrows, and Dwemer ruins), and lace it with the roads and trails between. As with every rule in this book: if a result does not please you, roll again or change it on the spot.
