The Elder Scrolls 2e rewards light prep. Before a session, have these ready — a few lines each is plenty:

- **One settlement** the party might visit — its name, one building, one named local (race + a quirk + what they want), one rumor going around.
- **One delve** they might enter — a barrow, Dwemer ruin, cave, or fort: its name, one reason to go in, one memorable room, one danger, one thing worth carrying out.
- **One faction** leaning on the party this session — what it wants, and who is carrying that want to the party's table.
- **One clock** ticking toward something the party would want to stop (a dragon circling closer, a Civil War muster, a sickness spreading, a Jarl's patience running out).
- **One card up your sleeve** — a named NPC, a strange relic, a turn of weather, a courier with a letter — to drop in if the evening goes quiet.

Do not script the beats. The party's choices fill in everything between these pieces.
