- **Appearance**: _Hunched_ + _Carapace_.
- **Traits**: _Moves in a straight line_ + _Weak Spot_.
- **Attack**: _Smashes_ + _Grapples_.
- **Abilities**: _Disables_ + _Hearing_.

_A heavy, shelled thing the size of an ox, hunched low to the cavern floor — kin to the chaurus that breed in the Deep beneath the Reach. It moves only in straight lines, dragging its armored bulk, and the seam where its plates do not quite meet leaves its belly soft. It charges in a dead-straight line, bowling foes over and dragging them into its grinding orbit, and the booming scrape of its shell on stone drowns out every other sound._

This creature is physically tough — higher than average **STR**, strong natural Armor. It is probably fast in a straight line but cannot turn well, and it does not lean on skill or agility to stay alive. It is brutish, but only as willful as any beast hunting its next meal. Its attack is a straight-line charge that ought to catch more than one target at once.

### Chaurus Behemoth

6 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 6 DEX, 4 WIL, charge & grind (d10, _blast_)

- A massive armored insect of the Deep that attacks by barreling over its prey in a straight line. The grinding scrape of its shell on stone is deafening.
- Its plated back is near-impenetrable, but the seam along its underbelly is a clear **Weak Spot** — strike there and the attack is _Enhanced_.
- **Critical Damage**: The target is caught in the behemoth's grinding momentum, losing an additional 1 STR each round until they break free (STR save to escape).
