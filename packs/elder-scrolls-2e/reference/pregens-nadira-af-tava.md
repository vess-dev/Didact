5 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 14 DEX, 10 WIL, scimitar (d8)

- **Redguard Alik'r**, age 26. **Adrenaline Rush:** once per day, clear a Fatigue in the heat of action.
- **Background:** you rode into Skyrim with Kematu's band, hunting a traitor named Iman — who calls herself Saadia now, in Whiterun. Your sash is blessed in the name of Leki: once per day, when a foe would strike you, make a DEX save, and on a success the blow misses.
- **Carries:** scimitar, desert armor and sun-veil (1 Armor), whetstone (_petty_), bounty writ (_petty_), strong rope (30 ft), Sash of Leki (_petty_), Thalmor signet (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), Lantern with an Oil flask (6 uses), 15 Septims. _(6 slots)_
- **Bond:** you escaped a Thalmor cell with a dead Justiciar's **signet**. It opens doors among the Dominion — and marks you for death if the wrong elf recognizes it.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Lady.
- **Who you are:** a sword-dancer of Sentinel with a bounty in one pocket and a doubt in the other, beginning to wonder whether Saadia is the traitor after all.
