> The party makes camp in the mouth of a cave in the Rift, beside a trickle of meltwater. Deeper in, a colony of skeevers [3 HP, 8 STR, 12 DEX, 3 WIL, bite (d6), _detachment_] has caught the smell of their supper.

**Warden**: _"As the fire catches, you hear it from the dark behind you: claws on stone, a great many of them, and a thin, wet chittering. Dozens of mangy, rat-like shapes with red eyes spill out of a crack at the back of the cave. We'll run them as a detachment."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Must we save DEX to strike before these vermin?"_

**Warden**: _"No — they're still pouring out of the crack. You have time to make the first move."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Good. Then Qa'zirr shall see how many he can spit at once."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Every nest of these things has a big one. I'll find it and put an arrow in it."_

**Elsinde**: _"They're packed tight. I'm reading Flames into the thick of them — that's a blast, isn't it?"_

**Warden**: _"It is, so it isn't Impaired against the swarm. Add a Fatigue and roll. Qa'zirr, you're one fighter against a detachment, so your attacks are Impaired: roll d4s."_

> Elsinde adds a Fatigue and rolls a d6 for Flames: a 2. Qa'zirr rolls d4+d4 for dagger and claws: a 2 and a 3. Only the single highest die counts against the same target, so the detachment takes 3 — exactly its HP.

**Warden**: _"A gout of fire rolls across the cave floor and the front rank goes up shrieking. Qa'zirr, you skewer a pair with your dagger and rake another with your claws — but for every one that falls, two more flood the gap. Hjolgeir, you push into the colony in search of their leader. The whole turn goes to the hunt, but in the last of the firelight you spot it: a skeever twice the size of the rest, a pale stripe down its belly, hanging well back."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"That's the one. One clean shot, that's all I need."_

> Now the skeevers act. A detachment's attacks against individuals are _Enhanced_ and deal _blast_ damage, so the swarm boils over everyone in the thick of it. The Warden rolls a d12 for each: 3 against Qa'zirr, whose leather turns 1, and 5 against Hjolgeir, whose shield and hide turn 2. Both have been bitten, so both must save STR against the skeevers' disease.

**Warden**: _"They're all over you both — up your legs, in your sleeves, at your throats. Qa'zirr, you lose 2 HP; Hjolgeir, 3. And you've both been bitten. STR saves."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"A 9. Fine."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"A 12. That is not fine."_

**Warden**: _"Qa'zirr, you've caught Ataxia. Until it's cured, picking a lock, lifting a purse, or any fine handwork needs a DEX save, even at your leisure."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"A thief with shaking paws. Qa'zirr will be praying at the Temple of Mara."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"First things first. I draw on the big one and loose. I know it's only a d4 with the swarm all around — but it's the shot we've got."_

> The detachment is already at 0 HP, so Hjolgeir's 2 goes straight to its STR. The detachment then saves against Critical Damage on its reduced STR. The Warden rolls a 9 against STR 6: a fail. A detachment that takes Critical Damage is routed.

**Warden**: _"The arrow streaks through the dark and takes the pale-bellied leader in the heart. It shrieks and topples — and at once the whole colony breaks, a gray tide pouring back into the crack in the rock. The cave is suddenly very quiet."_

**Elsinde**: _"Please tell me you're not going back for that."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Only for the tail. Alchemists pay for those."_

> The party rests the night. Qa'zirr's paws are still shaking in the morning.
