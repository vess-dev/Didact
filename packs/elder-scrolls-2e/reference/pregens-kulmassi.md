4 HP, 2 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 11 WIL, chitin spear (d8)

- **Dunmer Ashlander**, age 221. **Ancestor's Blood:** fire attacks against you are _Impaired_, and heat, smoke, and ash alone never leave you Deprived.
- **Background:** you came north with the refugees who raised Azura's statue above Winterhold; once per day, at dusk or dawn, re-roll a failed WIL save. An amulet of ghost-worn iron lets you call an ancestral shade to fight beside you once per day, at the cost of a Fatigue (3 HP, spectral blade d6).
- **Carries:** chitin spear, chitin shield (+1 Armor), chitin armor (1 Armor), ancestor's amulet (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), guar-hide lamp with an Oil flask (6 uses), 9 Septims. _(6 slots)_
- **Bond:** Azura marked you in a dream. Once per session, ask the Warden one yes-or-no question about what a Daedra desires. The answer is always true — and the Prince always wants something back.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Lord.
- **Who you are:** a proud, weathered wanderer who owes nothing to the Great Houses, and who walks through the Gray Quarter's squalor with her chin up.
