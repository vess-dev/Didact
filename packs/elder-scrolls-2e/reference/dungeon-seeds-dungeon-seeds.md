The deep places of Skyrim are rarely empty. A Nordic barrow may hold sleeping draugr and the dragon-cult priest who set them to their watch; a Dwemer ruin may still grind through its endless brass routines while Falmer creep up from below; an ice cave may shelter bandits, a hagraven, or worse. This chapter is a procedure for generating such a delve — its history, its denizens, its factions, its map, and the contents of its rooms — and a worked example you can read or run as written.
