Most folk in Skyrim honor the **Nine Divines**, the gods of the Empire: Akatosh the Dragon-God of Time, Mara of love, Dibella of beauty, Kynareth of the sky, Arkay of life and death, Stendarr of mercy, Julianos of wisdom, Zenithar of honest labor — and **Talos**, the ninth, the mortal hero who became a god. Since the Thalmor's treaty banned Talos worship, his shrines are tended in secret, and a stubborn devotion to him runs through the rebel cause.

Older still are the **Nordic gods** of the Old Ways — many of them the same Divines under harsher northern names, alongside hero-spirits and totem-beasts the Nords have revered since before the Empire came. Apart from these stand the **Daedric Princes**, the seventeen dread lords of Oblivion — Molag Bal, Hircine, Sheogorath, Azura, Mephala, and the rest. They are not gods of mercy but of bargains: they answer the desperate and the ambitious, and the price is always steep. Their worship is shunned, and the Vigil of Stendarr hunts it wherever it hides, but their shrines, cults, and artifacts persist. Off to the northeast, on the island of Solstheim, the **Skaal** keep to none of these, honoring instead a single nature-spirit they call the **All-Maker** and living in quiet harmony with the land.
