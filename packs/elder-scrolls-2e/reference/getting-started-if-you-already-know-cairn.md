The Elder Scrolls 2e is Cairn Second Edition with Skyrim's snow on it. Saves, Hit Protection, Armor, Fatigue, Scars, inventory slots, Morale, and the dungeon and wilderness procedures all work exactly as they do in Cairn. What is new:

- **Races.** Every PC picks one of Tamriel's ten races, which grants one small trait (Character Creation).
- **Spell Tomes** are Cairn's Spellbooks, sorted into the schools of magic; **Scrolls** and **Relics** work as in Cairn. Staves are Relics.
- **Skyrim's dangers.** Cold, falling, venom, and disease have their own short rules (Hazards; Diseases & Afflictions).
- **Crafting.** Smithing, alchemy, and enchanting are Downtime Actions (Crafting).
- **Standing Stones** are an optional blessing, off unless you turn them on.
- **Unusual PCs** — vampires, werewolves, Dremora, draugr, and Briarhearts — have their own chapter.
- **Standing in the world** is tracked with Faction Reputation, Renown & Infamy, and Bounty.
