Skyrim is home to all the peoples of Tamriel. Choose one of the ten races below, or roll 1d10 and count down the list (1 Nord … 10 Argonian), and note its single **trait** — a minor, lasting gift of blood and birth. A trait used "once per day" comes back at the next dawn. A character's race shapes how others treat them but never their calling; what you _do_ defines you, not what you are.

A background that names its people fixes your race: a Nord Reaver or a Skaal Hunter is a Nord, a Redguard Alik'r a Redguard, an Orc Stronghold Warrior an Orsimer, a Khajiit Smuggler a Khajiit, an Argonian Shadowscale an Argonian, a Dunmer Ashlander or a Telvanni Mage a Dunmer, and a Bosmer Hunter a Bosmer.

(Playing a vampire, werewolf, or other monstrous character uses one of these races _plus_ an unusual background — see Playing Unusual PCs.)

- **Nord** — _Children of the Sky._ **Frost-Hardy:** frost attacks against you are _Impaired_, and cold and snow alone never leave you Deprived.
- **Imperial** — _Citizens of Cyrodiil._ **Voice of the Emperor:** once per day, re-roll a failed WIL save made to calm, persuade, or bargain.
- **Breton** — _the Manmer of High Rock._ **Dragonskin:** once per day, a spell or magical effect that targets you fizzles harmlessly.
- **Redguard** — _the Warriors of Hammerfell._ **Adrenaline Rush:** once per day, clear a Fatigue in the heat of action.
- **Altmer (High Elf)** — _the Cultured People of Summerset._ **Highborn:** once per day, channel a Spell Tome without adding Fatigue.
- **Bosmer (Wood Elf)** — _the Tree-Sap People of Valenwood._ **Command Animal:** once per day, a natural beast that can see you makes a WIL save or treats you as a friend for the rest of the scene.
- **Dunmer (Dark Elf)** — _the Ash-Folk of Morrowind._ **Ancestor's Blood:** fire attacks against you are _Impaired_, and heat, smoke, and ash alone never leave you Deprived.
- **Orsimer (Orc)** — _the Pariah Folk of the strongholds._ **Berserker Rage:** once per day, make one attack _Enhanced_.
- **Khajiit** — _the Cat-Folk of Elsweyr._ **Claws and Night-Eye:** your unarmed claws deal d6 damage, and you see in dim light as others do by day (utter darkness still needs a flame).
- **Argonian** — _the Saxhleel of Black Marsh._ **Hist-Blooded:** you breathe water as easily as air, and ordinary disease never takes hold in you.
