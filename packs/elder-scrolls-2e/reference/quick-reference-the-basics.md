- **Attributes:** Strength (STR), Dexterity (DEX), Willpower (WIL). Rolled 3d6 in order, swap two.
- **Hit Protection (HP):** 1d6 to start. Luck and skill, not meat. Restored by a short rest.
- **Currency:** Septims. A pouch of fewer than 100 is _petty_.
- **Inventory:** 10 slots. _Petty_ = 0 slots, _bulky_ = 2. Backpack holds 6 of the 10. Fatigue fills slots. All 10 full → 0 HP.
