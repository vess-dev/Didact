For years a war has smoldered and now blazes across the Holds. On one side stand the **Stormcloaks** — the Sons and Daughters of Skyrim — led by Ulfric Stormcloak from Windhelm. They fight to throw off the Empire, to win Skyrim its independence, and above all to restore the open worship of **Talos**, the Nord hero-emperor risen to godhood. Against them stands the **Imperial Legion** under General Tullius, sent from Cyrodiil to crush the rebellion and hold the province together by law and the sword. Many a household and family is split down the middle between the two.

Behind it all looms the shadow of the **Thalmor**, the High Elf supremacists of the Aldmeri Dominion. Their last war with the Empire ended in the humiliating **White-Gold Concordat**, the treaty that outlawed the worship of Talos throughout the Empire. To devout Nords this ban is intolerable, and it is the spark beneath the rebellion. The Thalmor publicly back the Empire while their agents quietly work to keep the war burning, the better to bleed both sides dry. Whichever side a traveler favors, the elves in their high embassy profit from the strife.
