Roll **1d6** for the kind of job, then **1d20** on that table for the specific.

| d6 | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Clear** — a place full of foes needs emptying. |
| 2 | **Retrieve** — a specific object must be recovered. |
| 3 | **Hunt** — a named creature or person must be killed or captured. |
| 4 | **Escort / Deliver** — a person, beast, or cargo must reach somewhere alive. |
| 5 | **Settle** — a dispute (often Civil War) needs resolving, by words or otherwise. |
| 6 | **Investigate** — something is wrong, and the patron wants to know what. |

### Clear (d20)

| d20 | The Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | A **Nordic barrow** has begun to stir; the draugr are wandering past its doors. |
| 2 | **Bandits** have seized an old fort and are choking a trade road. |
| 3 | A **Falmer warren** has spread up from a cave into a town's lower mine. |
| 4 | A **vampire nest** is draining a hamlet one sleeper at a time. |
| 5 | A **Forsworn redoubt** is raiding caravans out of the crags. |
| 6 | A **necromancer's coven** has taken over a ruin and is raising the local dead. |
| 7 | A **giant** (or a mammoth herd) has wandered onto grazing land. |
| 8 | **Skeevers and a chaurus nest** have overrun a settlement's cellars and well. |
| 9 | A **Dwemer ruin's automatons** have reactivated and seal off a needed passage. |
| 10 | A **frostbite-spider** brood has webbed shut a mountain pass. |
| 11 | A **hagraven** and her Forsworn have cursed a shrine the locals depend on. |
| 12 | A **warlock** holed up in a tower is loosing summoned atronachs on travelers. |
| 13 | A pack of **wolves or a sabre cat** is taking livestock and now children's dogs. |
| 14 | **Deserters** of the war (either side) have turned bandit in a ruined mill. |
| 15 | A **Silver Hand** band is squatting in a barrow the patron wants emptied. |
| 16 | A **troll** (or two) has denned in a cave above a crucial mine shaft. |
| 17 | **Skooma smugglers** have fortified a sea cave and corrupted the local guard. |
| 18 | A **spriggan grove** has turned hostile and is strangling a logging camp. |
| 19 | A **dragon** has taken a peak crowned by a word wall; the road below is now ash. |
| 20 | A **dragon priest** stirs in his sealed temple, and his cultists gather. |

### Retrieve (d20)

| d20 | The Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | A **Dwemer artifact** — a cog, a lexicon, an animunculus part — from a sealed ruin. |
| 2 | A family's **stolen heirloom** — a sword, an amulet, a wedding band — taken by bandits. |
| 3 | A **stolen Spell Tome** the College wants back before the wrong hands read it. |
| 4 | A Jarl's **looted gold or tax chest**, hidden in a thieves' bolt-hole. |
| 5 | An **ancient claw-key** needed to open a barrow's inner door. |
| 6 | A dead adventurer's **journal and map**, lost with them in a crypt. |
| 7 | A **Daedric relic** a cult is about to use; recover it first. |
| 8 | A specific **soul gem** (a black one) a mage needs and will not explain why. |
| 9 | A **dragon priest's mask**, sealed with its owner in his temple. |
| 10 | A **stolen meadery recipe / ledger** worth a fortune to the right buyer. |
| 11 | A holy **relic of a Divine**, looted from a sacked temple. |
| 12 | A **Nordic word-wall rubbing** a scholar will pay handsomely to copy. |
| 13 | A noble's **incriminating letter**, to be lifted from a locked study. |
| 14 | A rare **alchemical ingredient** (a Glenmoril witch's head, nirnroot, a heart stone) from a deadly place. |
| 15 | A **smuggled crate** a fence wants quietly pulled off a watched dock. |
| 16 | An **Elder Scroll fragment** rumor — almost certainly a fake, but the patron is sure. |
| 17 | A **unique weapon** the patron knows by name and history (see Unique Weapons). |
| 18 | A **frozen corpse** high on a mountain, who carried something the patron needs. |
| 19 | A **caged or enslaved person** a reformer is paying to free, papers and all. |
| 20 | Something **sealed in a warded box**, from a ruin the patron will not name. |

### Hunt (d20)

| d20 | The Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | A **named bandit chief** with a price on his head, dead or alive. |
| 2 | A **vampire** preying on a town under a false, friendly face. |
| 3 | A **werewolf** killing by night; the patron does not know it is a neighbor. |
| 4 | A **rogue mage or necromancer** the College has disavowed. |
| 5 | A **dragon** newly landed near a settlement, terrorizing the roads. |
| 6 | A **Forsworn Briarheart** leading raids out of the Reach. |
| 7 | An **escaped prisoner** the Hold wants returned to its mine or its noose. |
| 8 | A **giant or sabre cat** that has killed a hunter and must be put down. |
| 9 | A **Thalmor agent** someone wants quietly gone (and someone else, kept alive). |
| 10 | A **traitor in the war**, marked for death by the side they sold out. |
| 11 | A **Dark Brotherhood contract** — a name, a fee, and no questions about why. |
| 12 | A **hagraven** that has cursed a family line for three generations. |
| 13 | A **frost troll** denning on a pilgrim road to High Hrothgar. |
| 14 | A **conjurer** binding atronachs and selling them to raiders. |
| 15 | A **Silver Hand captain** the Companions want answered for in blood. |
| 16 | A **skooma kingpin** poisoning a Hold from a hidden den. |
| 17 | A **revenant draugr lord** that walks beyond its barrow on the dark of the moon. |
| 18 | A **wanted thief** the Guild itself wants silenced for breaking the code. |
| 19 | A **spriggan matron** or a **Daedra** loosed by a broken seal. |
| 20 | A **legend** — a beast or outlaw the locals half-believe is a ghost. |

### Escort / Deliver (d20)

| d20 | The Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | A **merchant caravan** crossing a Hold known for bandits and worse. |
| 2 | A **priest or pilgrim** bound for a far shrine through dangerous country. |
| 3 | A **noble's heir** traveling between courts in a contested Hold. |
| 4 | A **prisoner** to be walked to a Jarl's justice without being sprung. |
| 5 | A **bard** going to a feast in a town that hates their last patron. |
| 6 | A **sealed dispatch** the war's other side would kill to read. |
| 7 | A **wagon of supplies** to a fort, a College, or a snowed-in village. |
| 8 | A **terrified witness** who must reach a Hold's court alive to testify. |
| 9 | A **College courier** carrying something that must not be jostled or opened. |
| 10 | A **defector** from one war faction crossing to the other under a flag. |
| 11 | A **Khajiit caravan** barred from the city gates, needing safe passage outside. |
| 12 | A **body** to be carried home for proper burial through the wilds. |
| 13 | A **healer** going to a plague-struck steading no one else will enter. |
| 14 | A **bride or groom** (a marriage of two feuding clans) and their dowry. |
| 15 | A **tame or rare beast** — a horse, a sabre cat cub, a mammoth calf — to a buyer. |
| 16 | A **child** orphaned by the war, to be delivered to kin in another Hold. |
| 17 | A **Dwemer device** to a mage, kept intact and unsprung the whole way. |
| 18 | A **condemned innocent** the patron believes is being framed. |
| 19 | A **tax collector** on a circuit through hostile, half-rebel country. |
| 20 | A **mysterious passenger** whose name the patron will not give. |

### Settle (d20)

| d20 | The Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Two **farmsteads** are about to come to blows over a moved boundary stone. |
| 2 | A **Stormcloak and an Imperial family** in one village are tearing it apart. |
| 3 | A **Jarl and a rival claimant** both want the party to back their right to the seat. |
| 4 | A **mine** is struck — owner and miners each want the party's muscle. |
| 5 | A **guild and the city guard** are escalating; someone wants a quiet truce. |
| 6 | A **clan blood-feud** (wergild) could be paid off — or settled with steel. |
| 7 | Two **caravan companies** both claim the same road and the same toll. |
| 8 | A **Dunmer quarter** and the **Nords** of a city are one insult from a riot. |
| 9 | A **debt** binds three parties; an honest arbitrator could untangle it. |
| 10 | A **Forsworn cell** offers a truce to one steading the others want refused. |
| 11 | A **bandit chief** wants to surrender; the Hold wants him hanged; a fair trial is possible. |
| 12 | A **war muster** is bullying a neutral village for men and grain. |
| 13 | A **shrine** is claimed by both the Divines' priests and a Daedric cult. |
| 14 | Two **heirs** dispute a homestead a dead relative left to "the worthy." |
| 15 | A **Thalmor demand** (hand over a Talos-worshipper) splits a town that protects him. |
| 16 | A **dragon's lair** sits between two Holds that each want the other to deal with it. |
| 17 | A **water source or warm spring** is being dammed by an upstream rival. |
| 18 | A **marriage alliance** between feuding houses needs a neutral hand to broker it. |
| 19 | A **deserters' camp** wants amnesty; the war faction wants them made an example. |
| 20 | A **conflict the party themselves started**, come back around. Warden's pick. |

### Investigate (d20)

| d20 | The Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | A **caravan vanished** on a safe stretch of road — no bodies, no wreckage. |
| 2 | A whole **village fell silent** overnight; the last letter out spoke of nightmares. |
| 3 | A **barrow's seal** was found broken from the *inside*. |
| 4 | **Livestock and then a hunter** turned up bloodless near a "friendly" manor. |
| 5 | A **word wall** on a peak has begun to hum, and dragons circle it at dusk. |
| 6 | A **shrine's blessing** has soured; pilgrims who pray there sicken. |
| 7 | A **noble** has not been seen in a week and his steward grows evasive. |
| 8 | A **mine collapse** uncovered a sealed Dwemer door — and the diggers won't go near it. |
| 9 | **Strange lights and chanting** rise nightly from an abandoned tower. |
| 10 | A **string of disappearances** all touched the same inn, the same night of the week. |
| 11 | A **plague** is spreading faster than any natural sickness should. |
| 12 | A **trusted guard captain** is suspected of taking Forsworn (or Thalmor) coin. |
| 13 | A **dead adventurer's map** points to a ruin no records name. |
| 14 | A **child** keeps describing, in detail, a crypt no child could have entered. |
| 15 | A **meadery's rivals** are dying of "accidents"; the patron fears the Black-Briars. |
| 16 | A **College apprentice** vanished into the Midden, leaving only scorched notes. |
| 17 | **Two Jarls' couriers** carry the same forged seal; someone is playing both Holds. |
| 18 | A **frozen body** on a glacier wears clothing centuries out of date — and isn't decayed. |
| 19 | A **dragon burial mound** has gone empty, the earth thrown outward. |
| 20 | Something the elders **will not speak of**. Only the children talk, and only in whispers. |
