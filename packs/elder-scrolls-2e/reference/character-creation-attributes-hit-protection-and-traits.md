- Roll for your character's **Attributes** and **Hit Protection**.
- Roll for the rest of your character's Traits, and then on the Bonds table.
- If your Warden uses them, roll or choose a Standing Stone.
- Finally, roll for your character's **Age** (2d20+10). The Mer are long-lived: an Altmer, Bosmer, or Dunmer may be far older than the dice say — a Dunmer who remembers the Red Year has seen two centuries. If you are the _youngest_ character, roll on the Omens table. The result should be read aloud to the other players. The Warden should incorporate Omens into the setting as they see fit.
