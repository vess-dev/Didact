mechanics: [command]
---
Once per combat, the PC whose companion it is may spend their **own action** to direct that companion. The companion takes a single clean action — strike a foe, drag a fallen ally clear, hold a doorway, read a Spell Tome, loose an arrow — with _Enhanced_ damage *or* a **re-rolled save**, as the fiction fits. (Enhanced and re-rolls do not stack with each other; pick one.)

Without a Command action, companions act on their own under the **Warden's** control. They defend themselves and the party, but they will not carry out a clever plan unless told to.
