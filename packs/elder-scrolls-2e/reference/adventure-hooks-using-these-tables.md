Roll the dice and sit with them for a minute. Often the whole job appears — *a frightened farmer (patron) wants the party to retrieve a stolen heirloom (task) from a bandit-held fort (location), but the "bandits" are starving war-deserters with a sympathetic reason (complication), and the pay is a Relic the farmer doesn't know is cursed (reward).* When the rolls don't spark, re-roll one or pick.

A rolled adventure is a starting point, not a script. If the party resolves the hook in the first scene in a way you didn't expect, let them — the tables exist to restart the engine, not to fence in the story. And remember the standing rules of Skyrim's economy: killing bandits, Forsworn, draugr, and the clearly monstrous never costs Renown, while a witnessed crime against the innocent earns **Bounty** in that Hold whatever the patron promised.
