When the party is between arcs, or a session needs a shape fast, roll here. This is the **"job of the week"** generator — Skyrim's bounties, errands, and radiant quests, the kind tacked to an inn board or pressed on the party by a Jarl's steward. Each job is five steps (six dice):

1. **Roll 1d20 — the Patron** (who is asking, and who they are).
2. **Roll 1d6 — the Task** (what kind of job it is), then **1d20** on that task's table (the specific job).
3. **Roll 1d10 — the Complication** (what's wrong with it).
4. **Roll 1d12 — the Location** (where it sends the party).
5. **Roll 1d10 — the Reward** (what's in it for them, beyond base pay).

Combine and run. Sit with the five results for a minute and the job usually assembles itself: who really wants this done, what the catch is, where the danger lies, and what walking back out is worth.
