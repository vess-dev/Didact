At an **arcane enchanter** a PC binds a known magical **effect** into a weapon, a piece of armor, or apparel, spending a filled **soul gem** to power it. The result is a **Relic** — a charged wondrous item with a **Recharge** condition — and it behaves exactly as Relics do everywhere in these rules: it costs no Fatigue to use, and when its charges run out it is inert until recharged.

### What you need

Three things, and a **Downtime Action** at the enchanter:

1. **An item** to bind the effect into — a weapon, armor, or worn item you own.
2. **A learned effect** — an enchantment you know (see *Learning effects*).
3. **A filled soul gem** — its size sets the number of charges (see *Soul Gems*).

The enchanter draws the captured soul out of the gem (consuming it) and seals the effect into the item. The finished Relic holds **charges** set by the soul that powered it: a **petty or lesser** soul gives **1 charge**, a **common or greater** soul **2**, a **grand or black** soul **3**. A weapon's enchantment spends a charge when you call on it. A worn enchantment spends a charge each time it is used — or, if it was powered by a **greater, grand, or black** soul, it may instead be made **constant**: always working while worn, with no charges to spend.

Each item holds **one** enchantment, and a person can draw on at most **one enchanted ring and one enchanted amulet** at a time; the magics of a second ring sour against the first.

### Learning effects

You can only bind an effect you understand. A PC **learns an effect by destroying a magic item that already carries it** — unbinding the enchantment at the enchanter to study how it was made. This consumes the source item permanently (the most storied Relics and Daedric Artifacts resist being unmade and cannot be learned from). Once learned, an effect is yours to bind again and again, soul gems permitting.

### Recharge

Every Relic made at the enchanter spends charges as it is used and falls inert when they run out. **Recharge** one by feeding it another **filled soul gem** at any time — restoring as many charges as that soul would grant (1, 2, or 3), up to the Relic's maximum. A *found* Relic — anything from the Warden's catalogs — is recharged only as its own **Recharge** line says; a soul gem serves only where that line names one. Recapturing a soul is the everyday way: trap a creature's soul in a gem (a Soul Trap effect, a fitting Spell Tome, or a black gem for the right prey), then pour it into the blade.

### Sample enchantments

Effects in the project's vocabulary, ready to bind. The Warden sets each Relic's charge count from the soul gem used.

| Bound effect | What the Relic does |
| --- | --- |
| **Fire / Frost / Shock damage** | When you attack, spend a charge: the attack is _Enhanced_ and counts as fire, frost, or shock. |
| **Soul Trap** | When you attack, spend a charge: if the blow kills, the creature's soul fills a fitting empty soul gem you carry. |
| **Absorb Health** | When you attack, spend a charge: the attack is _Enhanced_ and you restore **1d4 STR**. |
| **Turn Undead** | Spend a charge: undead in sight make a **WIL save** or flee for the round. |
| **Resist an element** (worn, constant) | While worn, that element's attacks against you are _Impaired_. |
| **Fortify a save** (worn) | Spend a charge: re-roll a failed save of one chosen type (STR, DEX, or WIL). |
| **Muffle** (worn, constant) | While worn, you need not save to move silently in ordinary conditions. |
| **Waterbreathing** (worn, constant) | While worn, you breathe water and never make drowning saves. |
