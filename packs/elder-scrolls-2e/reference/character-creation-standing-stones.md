The Standing Stones rise throughout Skyrim, each tied to a constellation. The Stones are **optional** and off by default: with your Warden's permission, your character may be marked by one — a minor, lasting blessing. Roll 1d12 or choose. The thirteenth, **the Serpent**, wanders the sky and is never rolled: a character must seek it out and choose it. Only one Stone marks you at a time, and you may change it only by visiting another Stone in play.

| d12 | Stone | Blessing |
| ------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | The Warrior    | Once per day, one of your attacks is _Enhanced_.                                                                 |
| **2**   | The Mage       | Once per day, channel a Spell Tome without adding Fatigue.                                                       |
| **3**   | The Thief      | Once per day, re-roll a failed DEX save.                                                                         |
| **4**   | The Lord       | Once per day, one attack against you is _Impaired_.                                                              |
| **5**   | The Lady       | Once per day, a short rest also clears one Fatigue.                                                              |
| **6**   | The Steed      | Ignore the _bulky_ tag on one worn suit of armor.                                                                |
| **7**   | The Apprentice | Twice per day, channel a Spell Tome without adding Fatigue — but magical attacks against you are _Enhanced_.     |
| **8**   | The Atronach   | Once per day, a spell that targets you is absorbed: it fizzles, and you clear one Fatigue. But healing spells cast on you fizzle too. |
| **9**   | The Ritual     | Once per day, raise a nearby corpse as a mindless servant (3 HP, its own weapon or d4). It crumbles at the scene's end. |
| **10**  | The Lover      | Once per day, shift one person's Reaction toward you one step better.                                            |
| **11**  | The Shadow     | Once per day, become invisible until the end of your next turn, or until you attack or cast.                     |
| **12**  | The Tower      | Once per day, open one mundane lock without tools or a save.                                                     |
| —       | The Serpent    | _Seek it out; never rolled._ Once per day, spit venom at a foe you can see: it takes d6 damage and must pass a STR save or lose its next turn, paralyzed. |
