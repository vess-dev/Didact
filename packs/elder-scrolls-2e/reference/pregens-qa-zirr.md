4 HP, 1 Armor, 9 STR, 15 DEX, 11 WIL, dagger (d6), claws (d6)

- **Khajiit Thieves Guild Burglar**, age 27. **Claws and Night-Eye:** your unarmed claws deal d6, and you see in dim light as others do by day.
- **Background:** you ran stolen silver through Riften's canals for the Guild's fence; once per day, re-roll a failed DEX save to climb or to squeeze through somewhere you shouldn't fit. Your grappling hook has opened many high windows.
- **Carries:** dagger, leather armor (1 Armor), lockpicks, charcoal for shadowmarks (_petty_), knotted climbing rope (30 ft), grappling hook, pouch of Moon Sugar (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), Torch (3 uses), 10 Septims. _(7 slots)_
- **Bond:** a Khajiit caravan master once saved your life. Any caravan will shelter you once, no questions asked.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Thief.
- **Who you are:** a soft-spoken cat with light fingers and lighter loyalties, lately of the Ratway, who has noticed that the Guild's luck turned bad the same year its Guild Master started smiling.
