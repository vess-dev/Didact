A new party needs a reason to be standing in the same place willing to face the same danger. Hand the table one of these, or roll, and build the first scene around it:

| d6 | Why You're Together |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Caravan guards.** You hired on to escort a merchant's wagons across a Hold. The pay is fair, the road is long, and something is following the train. |
| 2 | **Barrow expedition.** A scholar, a Jarl's steward, or a treasure-hunter pulled you together to crack open a sealed Nordic tomb. You split what you find — if you walk back out. |
| 3 | **Bound for the block.** You were all caught up in the same Imperial sweep — rebels, bandits, and the simply unlucky, chained in the same cart. When the chance to run comes, you take it together. |
| 4 | **Guild hopefuls.** You each came to the same hall — Jorrvaskr, the College, the Ragged Flagon — to be let in. They won't take you until you prove yourselves on one job, together. |
| 5 | **Conscripts and recruits.** A muster (Stormcloak or Legion), a Hold militia, or a Vigil patrol threw you into the same unit. Brothers- and sisters-in-arms before you ever chose to be. |
| 6 | **A debt or a vow.** You all owe the same person, or swore the same oath, and they have called it in. The job is not optional; the company is. |

Whatever the table picks, write one shared stake — *"the merchant's coin," "a fair split of the tomb," "earning your place," "getting out of these chains alive"* — at the top of the page. That sentence is the engine of session one.
