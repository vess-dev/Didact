Dragons are the children of Akatosh, ancient and proud, returned to the world with the World-Eater. Every dragon is a _detachment_: one great foe that shrugs off lone attackers (their attacks are _Impaired_ unless they are _blast_) and savages whatever it strikes. All are immune to paralysis and cannot be soul-trapped; each breathes either fire or frost, and frost-breathers shrug off frost (fire-breathers, fire). A flying dragon lands to fight once it has lost half its HP — or when something forces it down.
