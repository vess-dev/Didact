A settlement on the edge of the wild *will* be attacked — by bandits, a Forsworn band, a giant down from the foothills, a vampire hunting pack, a war-party in the Civil War, or something worse. **Each season, roll a d6: if the result is higher than the settlement's Defense, a raid comes.** A Defense 1 steading is raided almost every season; a Defense 5 village perhaps once in a year and a half. A raid is a combat scene the Warden designs around what stalks that corner of Skyrim. Its outcome:

- **Party wins (or a forewarned, well-defended town wins alone):** no loss. A hard-won victory may raise Defense by one, at the Warden's call, as the settlers learn to fight.
- **Party loses or is absent:** **Population −1 and one building destroyed** (Warden's pick, usually whatever stood nearest the breach). A catastrophic loss can put the whole settlement to the torch.

A settlement of Defense 3 or more with a Barracks or a Watch-beacon can turn back an ordinary raid *without* the party present, if the Warden judges it has the numbers and the warning.
