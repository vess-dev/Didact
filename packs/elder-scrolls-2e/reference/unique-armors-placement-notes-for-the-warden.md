- A Daedric artifact is a leash as much as a gift. The Prince who lent it is watching, and the Vigil of Stendarr wants it destroyed. Let possession draw consequences — a Daedric cult's favor, a Vigilant's blade, a quiet madness.
- The Dragon Priest masks reward a campaign of barrow-delving: scatter the eight across Skyrim and let the Wooden Mask and Konahrik be the prize for collecting them all.
- Do not stack unique armors on one PC. One body armor, one helmet, one shield, up to the cap of 3 — and one of these named pieces in each slot per campaign, not a wardrobe of them.
- Remember the cap is fictional above 3. When a player wants their Daedric cuirass to "be better" than steel, it is — it is dreadful, unbreakable, and worth a fortune. The number stays 3.
- When an artifact changes hands, note why. The Princes notice who carries their gifts.
