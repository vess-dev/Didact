A **Relic** is a wondrous item that holds a magical effect without being a Spell Tome. Unlike Spell Tomes, Relics do not cause **Fatigue**. Most carry a few **charges** (or single-use **uses**) and a **Recharge** condition — a filled soul gem for some, a stranger and costlier rite for most. A few simply *are* what they are, always at work while worn or carried; those need no charges.

The relics gathered here are the enchanted *miscellaneous* curios of Tamriel: amulets, rings, circlets, staves and rods, and assorted oddments recovered from barrows, Dwemer ruins, mages' studies, and the hoards of the dead. Enchanted **weapons** and **armor** — including the Daedric Artifacts Dawnbreaker, the Ebony Mail, Spellbreaker, the rings of Hircine and Namira, the staves Wabbajack, Sanguine Rose, and Skull of Corruption, and the Dragon Priest masks — are catalogued in *Unique Weapons* and *Unique Armors*.

Four of the most storied curios below are **Daedric Artifacts**, the personal toys and tools of the Princes. They obey the same grammar as any relic, but they cannot be created, copied, or improved, and the Prince who made each may one day come to collect.

A few rules of thumb:

- A relic's effect is written in plain rule terms, and no relic ever adds to a save.
- A relic is recharged only as its entry says. A filled soul gem recharges the Relics a character makes at an arcane enchanter (see **Crafting**) and any relic whose **Recharge** line names one — nothing else.
- A person can draw on at most **one enchanted ring and one enchanted amulet** at a time (see **Crafting**); a circlet or helm counts as neither.
- An amulet that grants Armor is a ward, not a plate: it counts as a worn **helmet** (+1 Armor), and does nothing more if you also wear one.
- Worn rings and amulets keep working while their wearer is Transformed — a Vampire Lord or a werewolf in Beast Form still benefits.
