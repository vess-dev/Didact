- A unique weapon keeps the base die of its kind — dagger d6, sword, mace, or war axe d8, greatsword, battleaxe, or warhammer d10 (_bulky_, _two-handed_), bow d6 (_bulky_), crossbow d8 (_bulky_), staff d6 — or, at most, one step more, as a masterwork already tempered. Nothing raises a die past **d12**, and a unique weapon cannot be tempered again.
- A property that is always on is narrow: a single kind of foe, a single circumstance, a small drain. The strongest powers cost **charges**. Staves behave as Relics — no Fatigue — and are ordinary weapons (d6) when swung.
- No unique weapon adds to a save or forces a save at a penalty. Where a foe must save, it simply saves.
- These items are rare and load-bearing. Hand them out as the reward of a quest, the prize at the bottom of a barrow, or the spoils of a dangerous foe — never as shop stock. When one changes hands, it leaves a mark: an old owner who wants it back, a debt, a curse that follows the blade.
