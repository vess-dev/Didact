> After days slogging through snow and tundra, the party reaches the barrow of the Cutlass King, a sea-raider warlord laid to rest in the age before the Empire.

**Warden**: _"At last the cairn on your map resolves out of the morning fog: a barrow door set into the hillside, ringed with old standing stones. As the mist thins, you catch a flash of movement and the glint of armor near the entrance."_

**Elsinde**: _"Of course. Nothing in Skyrim is ever simple. Bandits, I'd wager — come to rob the same tomb we have."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr sees no drawn steel yet. Perhaps they only wish to talk?"_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Doesn't matter. Hands off your weapons, the both of you — I'll hail them first, the way a Companion should. Maybe we settle this without blood."_

**Warden**: _"Hjolgeir, you call out a Nord's greeting toward the door. Silence — then a snort of laughter. They're laughing at you. Three figures stride out of the fog, weapons bared. The biggest grins behind a two-handed battleaxe; the other two, a man and a woman clearly kin, carry matching swords. Everyone roll a DEX save to see whether you act before they do."_

> Hjolgeir, Elsinde, and Qa'zirr each save DEX to act in the first round. Hjolgeir and Qa'zirr succeed; Elsinde does not.

**Elsinde**: _"Wonderful. I fumble my Spell Tome open to the wrong page, no doubt."_

**Warden**: _"So you do. Hjolgeir, Qa'zirr — each of you may move and take one action. If the bandits live through it, they strike back. Then it comes around to the party again, Elsinde included."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"My bow's slung, so it's the sword. I'll go for the big one — drop the leader and the others may break."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Ha! Qa'zirr had the very same thought. Let us both fall upon him."_

> Hjolgeir and Qa'zirr each roll to attack, but only the highest result counts as damage against the big bandit [8 HP, 15 STR, 7 DEX, 9 WIL, battleaxe (d10)]. Hjolgeir rolls a 4 on his d10; Qa'zirr fights with dagger and claws together, rolls d6+d6 for a 5 and a 1, and keeps the 5.

**Warden**: _"You both rush the axeman. He turns Hjolgeir's swing aside with the haft, but Qa'zirr nearly guts him — dagger and claws part the air a hair's breadth from his belly. Close."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Close is not dead. Qa'zirr is displeased."_

> Now the bandits act. Though the Warden narrates them one after another, the results all happen at roughly the same instant. The Warden rolls an attack die for each foe, each striking a single PC. The big bandit rolls his d10 for a 3 against Qa'zirr, whose leather armor turns 1 of it. The siblings each roll a d8: a 3 against Hjolgeir, whose shield and hide stop 2 of it, and a 3 against Elsinde, who wears no armor at all. The players note their lost HP.

**Warden**: _"The axeman hammers down at Qa'zirr — the blow glances off your shoulder, and it stings. The younger man stabs at Hjolgeir, the nearest target, but the sword skids off his shield. And the woman lunges at Elsinde and nearly takes her head off. No blood, somehow — but the flat of the blade cracks against her skull and throws her into the snow."_

**Elsinde**: _"That takes me to exactly 0 HP. The Scars table, yes?"_

**Warden**: _"It does, and we look up how much HP you lost in the blow. You had 3 HP and took 3 — so you're **Walloped**."_

**Elsinde**: _"Sent flying, flat on my face, winded. I'm Deprived until I rest a few hours — and then I roll 1d6 and add it to my maximum HP. A 4! Seven HP. Perhaps being thrown about suits me."_

**Warden**: _"Perhaps. One point less and you'd only be rattled; one more and it would have been a broken limb. Write it down. You've a charmed life, scholar. Now it swings back to the party."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"I cut at the younger one."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr returns to the big one. This time, his belly."_

**Elsinde**: _"No time to read. I draw my dagger and stab at the woman."_

> Hjolgeir rolls a d10 for 2 damage against the younger man, who has the HP to soak it. Qa'zirr rolls a 1 and a 3, keeping the 3; the big bandit absorbs it, down to exactly 0 HP (only PCs gain Scars). Elsinde rolls a 6 on her dagger's d6 against the woman [5 HP, 11 STR, 8 DEX, 14 WIL, sword (d8)]. The woman loses her 5 HP and 1 STR besides, and must then save against Critical Damage using her _new_ STR.

**Warden**: _"Hjolgeir, your swing rings off the younger man's guard. Qa'zirr, your blades work the axeman over — sweat runs down his face; he's tiring fast. Elsinde, your dagger sinks into the woman's side. She saves STR to stay standing — and rolls a 13. A fail. She drops."_

> This is the fight's first casualty, so the woman's allies must save WIL to keep fighting or flee. The Warden rolls against the leader's WIL — and fails.

**Warden**: _"Seeing his sister fall, the younger man screams. He flings down his sword and shouts for the axeman to drop his too. 'Mercy,' he begs. 'Keep the tomb, keep it all — only help her, if you've the means.'"_

**Hjolgeir**: _"The Companions don't slaughter folk who yield. Put up your blade and stand aside, and we'll do what we can for her. But cross us and it's done."_

**Warden**: _"The younger man nods and rushes to his sister. The axeman only grins. 'I ain't swearin' to nothin'. But I do as he says, long as he pays. Just let me keep my axe.'"_

> The party binds the woman's wound and stabilizes her — left untended, she would have bled out within the hour. With the danger past, the PCs catch their breath with a swig from a waterskin and recover their lost HP. Elsinde, still Deprived, shows off the lump rising over her brow; no one is impressed.

**Hjolgeir**: _"Settled, then. You lot get back to the road. We've still got a dead sea-king to dig up, and I've heard far too many songs about him to turn back now."_
