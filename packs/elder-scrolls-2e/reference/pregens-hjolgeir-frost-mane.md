5 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 11 DEX, 9 WIL, masterwork steel sword (d10), hunting bow (d6, _bulky_)

- **Nord Companion**, age 31. **Frost-Hardy:** frost attacks against you are _Impaired_, and cold and snow alone never leave you Deprived.
- **Background:** Eorlund Gray-Mane judged you true and tempered your sword on the Skyforge itself (**masterwork**, d10). Your old hunting bow's draw is silent: a shot loosed from hiding never gives your position away.
- **Carries:** masterwork steel sword, round shield with the Companions' crest (+1 Armor), hide armor (1 Armor), hunting bow, mead horn of Jorrvaskr (_petty_), Rations (3 uses), Torch (3 uses), 11 Septims. _(7 slots)_
- **Bond:** a warm **dragon's scale** (_petty_) found in the ash of a burned village. It grows warmer the farther north you carry it.
- **Standing Stone** (if used): the Warrior.
- **Who you are:** a hot-blooded Whiterun warrior, quick to laugh and quicker to swing, who wants a name worth singing in the hall — and has begun to suspect the dragons are real.
