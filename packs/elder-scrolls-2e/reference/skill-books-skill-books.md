Most books in Skyrim are just books — histories, prayers, smutty serials, the diaries of the dead. A **Skill Book** is the rare volume whose lessons actually *stick*: a fencing manual that drills a single guard into your wrists, a poacher's confession that teaches you to read the wind, a battlemage's working notes that show you one trick of the Synod. Read one in a quiet hour and you carry that small mastery for the rest of your life.

A Skill Book is the found-in-the-world cousin of training-based Growth: it introduces no levels, no experience points, and no classes — only a rare volume that, once read, makes one character a little better at one thing forever.
