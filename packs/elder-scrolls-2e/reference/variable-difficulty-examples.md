### Evening The Odds

> Qa'zirr is cornered by a frost troll [16 HP, 1 Armor, 16 STR, 12 DEX, 4 WIL, claws (d8+d8), bite (d8)] after being cut off from the rest of the party. Trapped between the beast and an ice-cave wall, he has no choice but to fight. He has already passed his opening DEX save, and so acts first.

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr cannot best this thing fairly. But Qa'zirr learned to fight ugly in the gutters of Senchal. Is there anything close at hand to blind it — without getting clubbed for the trouble?"_

**Warden**: _"Let me roll the Die of Fate for how much loose grit is around. A 4. There's plenty of frost-rimed gravel between you and the troll, but you'll have to close in to scoop up a handful. Make a DEX save."_

> Qa'zirr rolls a 13: a success.

**Warden**: _"You snatch up a fistful of icy grit and fling it square into the troll's three eyes. It reels back, pawing at its face, wide open and unable to defend itself."_

> The frost troll loses a turn while it claws the grit from its eyes. It cannot rely on HP in this state: damage goes straight to STR.

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr knows he ought to run... but he cannot resist. Allow him to introduce this brute to his dagger and his claws."_

> Qa'zirr rolls d6+d6 for dagger and claws, a 3 and a 5, and keeps the 5. The troll's hide turns 1, so it loses 4 STR. The Warden then makes a Critical Damage save for the troll, rolling a 19 against its reduced STR of 12: a fail.

**Warden**: _"Both strike home and the troll roars, blood steaming where it spatters the frozen floor. Let's see if it weathers that — no. It fails its STR save and takes **Critical Damage**. It's down!"_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr takes the hint. Time to leave."_

> Qa'zirr flees the scene (no DEX save needed), leaving the troll where it lies. Had he known that trolls knit their wounds before your eyes, he might have stayed to finish it...

### Teamwork

> Hjolgeir, Qa'zirr, and Elsinde are chasing rumors of something that has been dragging folk off near the marshes of Hjaalmarch. They have tracked it to a stand of drowned willows on an islet some 40 feet out in a stagnant, poison-misted bog. Thick mudflats ring the water, cutting the islet off from the firmer ground of the marsh.

**Warden**: _"The trail of broken reeds ends at the edge of a bubbling, stinking pool. You know this stretch of Hjaalmarch by reputation: if the water doesn't drown you, the fumes rising off it will sicken you."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"Whatever it is lives on that islet. We'll never cross the mud — so we lure it out. Elsinde, no offense, but of the three of us you look the most... edible. You're the bait. We'll make it look like your cart's mired at the water's edge. Qa'zirr and I will lie under the tarp in the back and spring the moment the thing's close."_

**Elsinde**: _"I knew I should have left the College robes at home. Fine. Let's get on with it."_

**Warden**: _"The party sets the trap as described — a fine lure for an unsuspecting quarry. Let me roll the Die of Fate to see whether it even takes the bait... a 5. It definitely wants a closer look — but there's a complication: the light is failing, and the mist is thickening."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"So Hjolgeir and Qa'zirr can watch the islet from under the tarp, yes? While Elsinde keeps her back turned, fussing over a 'stuck' wheel?"_

**Warden**: _"Just so. Through a gap in the cart-boards, you both watch the willows on the islet shiver — and one of them unfolds, taller than a man, and steps down into the water. In this murk you can barely make out its shape. Then the ripples turn straight toward the cart."_

**Hjolgeir**: _"I can't see a thing in this fog. I'll have my bow drawn, but I need a target."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr's eyes were made for dusk. He will lie beside you and whisper where to aim."_

**Warden**: _"That works — but spotting for Hjolgeir is all you'll do until the creature has had a chance to react."_

**Elsinde**: _"And me? I won't see it coming at all. I'd like to ready an action for the instant Hjolgeir looses: I'll turn and read Flames into whatever is in front of me. It's a wood-thing, isn't it? Wood burns."_

**Warden**: _"That works too. So you each set yourselves. The ripples crawl nearer. Then they stop. Qa'zirr breathes 'there — two paces left of the reeds' as Hjolgeir draws. Hjolgeir, you see it only as he names it: a shape of bark and moss hauling itself out of the water, glowing faintly green at its heart. A breathless pause, then the hum of the bowstring."_

> Hjolgeir rolls a d6: a 6. Since the creature — a spriggan [8 HP, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 14 WIL, thorned arms (d8), spore burst (d6, _blast_)] — had no idea it was under attack, the damage goes straight to STR. It makes a Critical Damage save on its reduced STR and rolls a 3: a success.

**Warden**: _"The arrow punches through it, and sap sprays where it lands. It wheels toward the cart — but before it can strike, Elsinde turns and reads, and fire rolls over it."_

> Spriggans burn: fire against one is _Enhanced_, so Elsinde rolls a d12 for her Flames and adds a Fatigue. She rolls a 10. The spriggan's 8 HP burn away, and the last 2 points come off its already wounded STR. It fails its next Critical Damage save and collapses into smoldering bark. Working together, the party heads back toward Morthal with a sack of spriggan sap to prove the deed — and an alchemist who will pay well for it.

### Character History

> The party is escorting a client deep into hostile country. Their target sits in a village ringed by a tall timber palisade: the job is to slip the client past the gate guards and down into the Dwemer vault beneath the village. As they near it the sun is setting, and Qa'zirr has gone ahead, playing a Khajiit caravan trader come to peddle his wares.

**Warden**: _"You follow the rutted track up to the village's shut front gate. A guard stands to either side, near-identical but for the thin, sorry moustache on one of them."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Good evening, fine sirs! Might one such as Qa'zirr be permitted within your village this lovely night? The road has been long, but Qa'zirr's purse is heavy, and he would dearly love to share a little of his good fortune."_

**Warden**: _"The two trade a glance. The moustached one answers: 'No Khajiit inside the walls. And no entry after dark. Those are the rules.'"_

> Here Qa'zirr is laying on all his charm, which is to say he is trying to bribe his way in. He is also the one most at risk: the guards are well-armed for a lone traveler and have help within call. Were a roll required, Qa'zirr would make the WIL save, not the guards.

**Qa'zirr**: _"Of course, of course. But it is not quite dark yet, is it? The sun is only now slipping behind the wall. And Qa'zirr would be glad to begin sharing his good fortune with you two first — a well-earned drink, perhaps, to show his gratitude?"_

**Warden**: _"The two exchange another look — but this time the other guard answers with a smirk. 'Well... just this once. The sun's not truly down yet, is it. And we are awful thirsty.'"_

> The Warden made the ruling on what was known about these men: they are plain village guards at the end of a long day, and they would love a drink. With another character it might have fallen flat — but Qa'zirr is a Guild burglar whose gift for winning a mark's confidence is well established, and every guard in Skyrim knows the caravans pay. They wave him in.

### Greater Risk For Greater Reward

> The party has sent Qa'zirr out under cover of dark to scout a bandit camp in an old Imperial fort. He is meant to learn their numbers and whatever dangers wait there.

**Warden**: _"Halfway to the fort, you spot a torch bobbing along the trail. Someone's coming your way. What do you do?"_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Qa'zirr slips into the brush at the trailside, still as stone. Does that need a save?"_

**Warden**: _"No — there's plenty of distance between you, and you carry no light of your own. You can hide without any risk of being spotted."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"If this one wears the bandits' furs, Qa'zirr would ambush them and take their clothes. He wants no alarm raised, so he must be quick and savage."_

**Warden**: _"All right. They'll get a DEX save to react in time and fend you off, then — it may not go as smoothly as you'd like."_

**Qa'zirr**: _"Or — what if Qa'zirr simply drops a tree limb on their head and knocks them cold?"_

**Warden**: _"That could work. You'll need to dash back a way, climb a pine, and save STR to saw the branch loose with your dagger at exactly the right moment, without tipping off your target. Fail, and you come down with the branch — taking a fall and leaving yourself wide open."_

> Qa'zirr doubles back down the trail, climbs a pine, and saws at a thick limb. He rolls a STR save: a 7, a success. He drops the branch at just the right instant, and it crashes down on the lone traveler below, laying them out cold. Qa'zirr takes their furs and their torch, then heads on toward the fort.
