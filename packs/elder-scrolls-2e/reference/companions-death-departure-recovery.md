title: "Death, Departure, Recovery"
tags: [companions]
source: players-guide/companions#death-departure-recovery
---
- A companion whose HP runs out takes damage to STR and makes a STR save like a PC. On a failure they suffer **Critical Damage** and die within the hour without aid — bandages, a healing potion, or a restoration spell will stabilize them, if someone can reach them through the fight.
- A companion at **Loyalty 1** who watches the party do something they cannot stomach leaves at the next settlement. They may turn up again later, in a story.
- A dead companion cannot be replaced by another until their PC has taken one week's rest and had a scene to mourn them.
