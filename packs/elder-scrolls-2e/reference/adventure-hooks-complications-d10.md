*Every job has something wrong with it. Roll one.*

| d10 | Complication |
|---|---|
| 1 | The patron is **lying** about the target or the reason. |
| 2 | A **rival party** (or a faction) is after the same thing. |
| 3 | The patron **can't pay in full** up front, or at all. |
| 4 | The place is **deeper, older, or more haunted** than advertised. |
| 5 | A **faction the party has history with** is tangled up in it. |
| 6 | The "target" has a **sympathetic reason** for being where they are. |
| 7 | A **clock is ticking** — the party has far less time than they were told. |
| 8 | The job **can't be done as described** — the place is sealed, the target has moved, or the road is closed. |
| 9 | Succeeding will **compromise a companion, a friend, or the party's standing**. |
| 10 | A **relic or Daedric interest** is involved, and *someone else* wants it badly. |
