The dragons were thought a thing of old songs — until they returned. At their head flies **Alduin**, the great black World-Eater, eldest of his kind, who in the dawn of history ruled over Men until heroes wielding the **Voice** cast him from time itself. Now he has come back, raising the long-dead dragons from their burial mounds, and the wise fear he means to devour the world as the old prophecies foretold.

High on the Throat of the World, Skyrim's tallest mountain, dwell the **Greybeards** — silent monks who have mastered the **Thu'um**, the dragon-tongue Shout that bends the world to the speaker's will. They keep to their monastery of High Hrothgar and meditate on the Way of the Voice, breaking their peace only in true need. Hunting the dragons from the shadows are the **Blades**, a dwindled order of dragonslayers who once served the emperors and now seek out the one mortal said to be able to slay a dragon for good and steal its very soul. (The Voice and the dragon-souls of legend are matters of story here, not powers a traveler is likely to wield.)
