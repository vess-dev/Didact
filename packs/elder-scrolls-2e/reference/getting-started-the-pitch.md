You are running Skyrim. It is the year **4E 201**, the two-hundred-and-first year of the Fourth Era, and the cold northern province is coming apart at three seams at once.

- **The Dragon Crisis.** Dragons — extinct for an age, remembered only in carvings and bad dreams — have returned to the sky. The first one burned Helgen to the ground in the middle of an execution. Nobody knows why they are back, and the few who suspect are not telling. Out in the world, a barrow that has slept for four thousand years is stirring; the dead are walking; word-walls hum on the mountainsides.
- **The Civil War.** Skyrim is tearing itself in half. The **Stormcloaks**, led by Ulfric, Jarl of Windhelm, want the Empire and its laws gone, free worship of Talos, and an independent Skyrim ruled by Nords. The **Imperial Legion**, under General Tullius out of Solitude, wants the rebellion crushed and the province held together. Behind both, the **Thalmor** — high-elf enforcers of a treaty that outlawed Talos — quietly profit from every Nord who dies fighting another Nord. Every Hold has a Jarl, and every Jarl has picked a side or is about to be forced to.
- **The little disasters.** Bandits hold the old forts. The Forsworn raid the Reach. A vampire court schemes off the Haafingar coast. The Thieves Guild is in decline and looking for blood. Most people, most days, just want the roads safe, the dead to stay buried, and the price of mead to hold.

Into all of this walk the players: sellswords, mages, thieves, hunters, and stranger things, picking their way through Nordic barrows and Dwemer ruins for **Septims**, relics, and a place in the songs.
