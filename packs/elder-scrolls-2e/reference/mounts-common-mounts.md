Full slots, speed tags, and prices are in the **Marketplace** (*Transport*); this is the quick reference.

|                       |                                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Riding Horse**      | +4 slots. The standard saddle horse. Ignores one watch of Path or Terrain penalty. Startles in a fight, and its rider's attacks are _Impaired_, unless trained. |
| **Skyrim Horse**      | +4 slots. A shaggy, sure-footed northern breed. As a riding horse, and additionally treats snow, ice, and mountain (**Perilous**) terrain as one step easier. |
| **Mule / Pack Donkey**| +6 slots, *slow*. Cargo only — no travel relief, no riding into a fight. Stubborn, hardy, cheap.         |
| **Cart**              | +4 slots, *bulky*. Pulled by hand or hitched to a mount (which then loses its penalty-easing).           |
| **Wagon**             | +8 slots, *slow*. A homestead's hauler; needs a horse or two to draw it.                                 |
