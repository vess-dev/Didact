The following represent some of the most common **Downtime Actions** a player can choose. The **Warden** can also create custom actions based on the needs of play.

### Research

A PC investigates a question about lost or forgotten lore, the location of a missing item, the whereabouts of an important NPC, and so on. To take this action, the player must have a clearly formulated question and a **Source** of knowledge in the game world their character can reach. If the PC lacks a **Source**, they can spend a **Downtime Action** trying to find one — with no guarantee of success. Once a question is posed and an appropriate **Source** has been identified, the **Warden** provides any **Milestones** and associated **Costs**.

#### Questions

As always, the question must arise from an experience in play.

**Examples:**

- "Where is the lost barrow of **Ysgramor's** shield-thane?"
- "Who in **Riften** might know how to crack a Dwemer lockbox?"
- "Where can I find the cure for **Sanguinare Vampiris**?"

#### Sources

A **Source** is a person, place, faction, or entity that holds part or all of the answer the character seeks. Sources can be NPCs, factions, spirits, or even other PCs.

##### Examples:

- **Brynjolf**, a frequent contact for the **Thieves Guild**. Despite a good standing with them, asking for help with illicit work may come at a high cost.
- A **hagraven** who keeps her nest deep in the **Reach**. The party crossed her once on an earlier delve, and the meeting did not end well. Still, she is said to know the nature of every herb and its uses.
- The **Greybeards** of High Hrothgar, who have watched the world from the throat of the mountain for an age. The party once carried word up the seven thousand steps. If anyone has heard of forgotten places, it is them.

### Training

A character can improve their skill with an item or ability, with clear narrative or mechanical results. They might want to deal greater damage with a particular weapon, lower their chance of getting lost in a blizzard, or learn to read the language of the ancient Nords. A PC might spend several **Downtime Actions** sparring week by week, or travel to a distant master for short but intense study.

The player must describe precisely what they want to improve and a **Master** to train with. As always, the inspiration to improve should come from an experience in play. The **Warden** provides any **Milestones** and associated **Costs**.

**Examples:**

- **The Two-Handed Parry**: When fighting with one hand free, a PC's HP temporarily increases by 1d4. The party took on a Companion of Jorrvaskr, who has agreed to train anyone who can best her in the training yard.
- **Herbalism**: Given the proper ingredients, a PC can brew a **Healing Draught** (restores 4 STR) as a **Downtime Action**. After being nursed back to health by an old alchemist in Falkreath, the wounded PC asked to learn the craft. The **Master** agreed, but only once they gather three rare mountain flowers.
- **Ice-Fisher**: When taking the Supply **Action**, **Rations** gathered near cold freshwater (a frozen lake, a glacial stream) increase by one step (1d4 becomes 1d6). The party guided a stranded Skaal hunter safely home across the Solstheim ice. In thanks, he offered to teach a PC to find and take the fish that gather under the ice.

### Strengthening Ties

A character fosters a connection with an NPC or faction. First, they identify the entity with whom they wish to strengthen ties, as well as a specific **intent** (building trust, mending a friendship, seeking membership in a faction, forming an alliance, and so on). The **Warden** then provides concrete measures (as **Milestones** and **Costs**) the PC can take to advance the relationship. With each completed **Milestone**, the **Warden** describes how the relationship has grown or changed.

**Examples:**

- After an unlucky delve into a flooded barrow, a PC discovers they unwittingly carried out a stowaway: a frostbite spider, barely hatched. They decide to keep the creature and raise it in secret.
- During a session, a PC grows friendly with an agent of the **Companions**. Impressed by the band's code, the PC asks what it would take to be counted among the Circle.
- An emissary for a powerful faction dies during a job while under the party's protection. Now those responsible wish to make amends, so the party may once again take work from that faction.
