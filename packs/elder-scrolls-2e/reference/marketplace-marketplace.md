> All prices are in **Septims**. This list covers the everyday arms, armor, gear, and services a traveler finds in a Hold market, at a general goods shop, or with a road-side trader. Enchanted curios, named blades, and Daedric artifacts are not stocked for ordinary sale — those are found, won, or quested for, and the Warden handles them through the catalogs.

The dice and prices below are the common standard across Skyrim, for plain iron and steel. Finer materials — Dwarven, Elven, Orcish, glass, ebony, Stalhrim, dragonbone, Daedric — cost many times more and are lighter, more durable, or more dreadful, but they never change a weapon's die or an armor's value. Only **tempering** at a forge steps a piece up, once, and never past **d12** or **3 Armor**. See **Crafting** for the material tiers and tempering.
