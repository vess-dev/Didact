- **Encounters.** Write a short d6 table of what the party might meet between points: two or three of the wild's denizens, a hazard, a sign of the Spirit's agenda, and one oddity. (The Example Wild below ends with one.)
- **Name the wild.** Do it last, and take the name from what the tables gave you — its description, its Spirit, its best-known point, or whatever the locals fear in it.
