A companion is simpler than a full PC:

- **HP** 1d6 (rolled once when recruited)
- **STR / DEX / WIL**: 3d6 each, may swap any two
- **1 Armor** unless their background says otherwise (max 3, as ever)
- **One weapon** appropriate to who they were
- **One special trick** — the one thing they reliably do that a PC cannot, drawn from who they are (a housecarl plants herself between you and the blow; a College conjurer calls a bound blade from nothing; a Khajiit sees in pitch dark; a war-dog smells an ambush a room away).
- **Loyalty** — starts at **3** on a 1-to-5 scale.
