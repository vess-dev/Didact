A windswept island in the Sea of Ghosts between Skyrim and Morrowind, reached by the boat *Northern Maiden* out of Windhelm. Solstheim is a land divided against itself: ash-choked and gray in the south beneath the shadow of distant Red Mountain, snow-bound and forested in the north, peopled by Dunmer colonists, isolationist Telvanni wizards, and the All-Maker-worshipping Skaal. Lately a dread power stirs at its center, and the very stones of the island bend to a long-dead will.

- **Ruler (Raven Rock):** **Councilor Lleril Morvayn** of House Redoran, who governs the Dunmer colony of **Raven Rock** — once an Imperial ebony-mining outpost, now a forgotten Redoran town on the south coast, shielded from Red Mountain's ash by the great wall called the **Bulwark**. There is no Civil War here; Solstheim answers to Morrowind and to House Redoran, not to a Skyrim Jarl.
- **The Skaal:** **Skaal Village** on the northern Felsaad Coast — the northernmost settlement in all Tamriel — home to the Skaal tribe under **Chief Fanari Strong-Voice** and the shaman **Storn Crag-Strider**. They worship the All-Maker, live in harmony with the land, and alone know the secret of forging **Stalhrim**, the enchanted ice.
- **The Telvanni:** **Tel Mithryn**, a cluster of titanic living mushroom-towers on the southeastern river, grown from Vvardenfell cuttings by the brilliant, insufferable Master **Neloth** of House Telvanni to study Red Mountain — reached by a levitation shaft and steeped in arcane research.
- **The notable site:** the *Temple of Miraak* at the island's heart, rising over a corrupted All-Maker Stone — the stronghold of a cult enthralled to a will reaching out from beyond the world, drawing Solstheim's folk to build and to forget.
- **Other corners:** the *Bulwark* and the Redoran garrison at Raven Rock, *Raven Rock Mine* (its ebony long thought spent), *Severin Manor*, and the scattered *All-Maker Stones* the cult has begun to foul.
- **Troubles:** the islanders fall under a strange compulsion, building at the Temple of Miraak with no memory of why, and the Skaal shaman begs a free mind to break the spell. Raven Rock Mine may hold ebony yet — and a deadly secret with it. A conspiracy threatens Councilor Morvayn's life behind the Bulwark's safety.
- **Faces:** *Neloth*, the ancient, gloriously self-absorbed Telvanni wizard of Tel Mithryn, who treats every visitor as a potential apprentice or test subject. *Storn Crag-Strider*, the steadfast Skaal shaman who guards the knowledge his people need to be free, and will pay any price to keep them so.

> *Solstheim belongs to the Dragonborn DLC; drop it in only when the party takes ship for the ash-island. The nine Holds are Skyrim enough for most campaigns.*
