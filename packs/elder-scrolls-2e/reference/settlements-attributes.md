- **Population** — how many souls live there. 1 is a homesteader and a hired hand; 5 is a thriving village that can raise a militia and feed travelers through a hard winter.
- **Defense** — palisade, watch, fighting folk, warning, the lay of the land. 1 is "we have a gate"; 5 is "we can turn back a war-band."
- **Prosperity** — what the place produces that others want. 1 is "we keep ourselves fed"; 5 is "our name is on the trade routes, and the Jarl counts our taxes."

**A new homestead starts at 1/1/1.** Each attribute is its starting value plus 1 for every building that raises it, to a maximum of 5; losing a building loses its bonus. A raid or a hard season can also move an attribute directly (see **Raids**); none falls below 1 — a steading that would is abandoned or burned, at the Warden's call.
