Use this move when things are too easy or stable, adding elements that raise the stakes or add thorns to relevant rolls.

Add a new obstacle, twist, or threat to a scene, or develop an existing one. When used, introduce a new aspect to the scene working against the characters, or make one worse. Mechanically, this may be an aspect that adds thorns to some rolls (more difficulty), raises the stakes (more danger), introduces or rolls a pool, or allows other opportunities to spend suspense.

*The guard catches sight of you and rings the alarm bell (start 4d reinforcements arrive). | The ruffians in the tavern all draw their weapons. | The fire spreads to the furniture. | You see skeletal hands burst from the ground as the Lich summons more undead!*
