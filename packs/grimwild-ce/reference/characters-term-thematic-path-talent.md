tags: [characters]
page: 53
---
Some core talents allow you to select a temporary or changeable path talent to go along with with, such as those granted by the warlock’s pact, or to the summoner’s vassal. The trappings for these talents should fit with the character and their source. Because of the fleeting nature of these talents, PCs (and vassals) can’t use “One time only…” features. Work with your GM to see what talents fit.
