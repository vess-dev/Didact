There is a vast difference in the scale of character impact between small village common folk and mythic heroes. Use these options to change the scope of the game.
- **Smaller scale:** More rolls have risk, and are judged as impossible. Mooks should be rare, with even common enemies posing a threat. The max level for PCs is 3, though the GM should allow talents to be switched out after in-game milestones.
- **Larger scale:** Fewer rolls have risk and are judged as impossible. Mooks should be waded through freely, as pure set dressing, while toughs can be taken a few at a time. Bosses should be epic in their own right, not just being linked challenges more often, but having an interesting 'catch' or 'secret' to overcoming them that provides the real test. PCs start at level 3.

*Didact:* the smaller scale caps levels at 3; the larger starts PCs at level 3, gained at creation as leveling up gains them.
