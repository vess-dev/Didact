### Backgrounds

Your heritage, upbringing, profession—the core of your vantage. You choose any two that most influences who you are now. This book contains 18 backgrounds, use these to make a character quickly, or mix and match wises to create your own. The ragamuffin background means you learned a lot from the streets; without it, your upbringing is just set dressing.

### Wises

Each background has a set of three wises, though you can also create your own for a more personalized backstory. These are evocative key phrases that clearly expand your vantage. They can cover niche knowledge, key insights you’ve picked up, or neat tricks you’ve grown accustomed to using. Wises are most often used when adding details to the story and determining thorns.
