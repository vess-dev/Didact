Detail your 3 most distinctive, outward features that others quickly notice. This may include clothes, hair, markings, or how they hold themself. Consider other features that some may notice after spending time traveling with you. Check out the character crucibles on page 51 if you need inspiration.

### Character creation (p. 46)

Features: Write down three distinctive features, words or short phrases, that others soon notice about you, like strong hands, kind eyes, or a gravelly voice.

### Character crucibles (p. 51)

Choosing 2-3 distinctive features helps cement your character not only in your mind, but also in the shared imagination space at the table. You can come back to your features again and again during scenes to add them in to paint the scene. Be open to change over time as well.

- Personality · Clothing · Voice · Body · Eyes
