Use this rule if you want to expand the types of details your players can add.

Story details added with both tangles and thread don't need to be connected to PC vantage. The PCs can freely offer their own impact moves to gain spark (as a tangle), or another benefit and can expand the world with their own details. Only details that benefit the party cost thread.
