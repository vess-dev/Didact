Not all scenes need to be played out in detail. If you want to zoom out on the fiction and accordion in a few beats while still leaving the outcome up to dice, make a montage roll. Each participating player makes a 2d action roll, collaborating on the results. Take the highest result as the final outcome for the scene. Zoom back in to key moments on unlikely or interesting results.

*Example: it's a six-hour climb to the cultist sanctum at the top of the mountain. The Ranger finds the safest path (5, messy), the Fighter looks for shortcuts with his climbing gear (3, grim), and the Wizard wards away the cold (6, perfect). Overall it's a perfect, but the GM zooms in on the Fighter: a piton breaks and he's about to fall. Who saves him? The Wizard adds a little set dressing, making him light as a cloud so he drifts safely to the ground—"I could have handled it!" The party reaches the top tired and a little sore, but ready for a fight.*
