Your group of adventurers already know each other and have adventured together, at least for some time. Answer the prompts below together to get a sense of your party's concept to ensure that you build characters that fit well within that type of party.

Concepts: Choose 2 that you see your party as and 1 that you definitely aren't.

- Conquerors · Disciples · Explorers · Heroes · Mercenaries · Merchants · Mystics · Outsiders · Renegades · Scholars · Scoundrels · Wardens

To help whittle down choices, each player and the GM can individually write down one group concept they want to play as and one they won't play as, then reveal them.

*Didact:* `didact gen run grimwild-ce.party` picks the concepts and the group arc, rolls group-building questions, and keeps the party as a constant lore entry.
