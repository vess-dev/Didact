Crucibles for labelling what you place on a map (p. 133) and describing a site's points (p. 136). For each: roll 2 on each table, smash them together, and interpret.
- **Settlements:** roll `settlements-crucible`.
- **Sites:** roll `sites-crucible`.
- **Dangers:** roll `dangers-crucible`.
- **Curiosities:** roll `curiosities-crucible`.
- **Barriers:** roll `barriers-crucible`.
- **Factions** (for factions on the map and in the campaign, p. 114): roll `factions-crucible`.
