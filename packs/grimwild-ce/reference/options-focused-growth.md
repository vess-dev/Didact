Use this rule if you want to narrow the focus of your game. Tying experience to the story can help incentivize more passive players to engage with the themes. Make use of slow progression rules, slashing XP boxes before filling them (effectively doubling the amount needed to level), but at the end of each session, give a chance for an additional XP based on what happened in the game. This can often be connected to the adventuring party concept.
- *Themes about social relationships → bonus XP to those who changed their bond.*
- *Party concept: Disciples → bonus XP to those who convert others.*
- *Campaign focus: Treasure Hunting → bonus XP when carousing.*

*Didact:* Didact asks about the themed XP when each session ends: `didact do experience who=<pc> why=theme`.
