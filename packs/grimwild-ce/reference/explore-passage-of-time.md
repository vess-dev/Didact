The GM can declare that time passes, which moves the game forward, usually to the next month or season. Try creating custom months to add a fantastic touch to your world. Stick to a four-season cycle with three months per season, rolled on the season tables (spring → summer → fall → winter; each month's name joins a word from each of the season's two columns): roll `season-months --with season=spring`. Include three simple details like atmospherics, weather, or flora—don't forget to mix in fantastic elements! When you reach another month, add three more for it. For details, roll `atmospherics --with season=spring` (the example atmospherics).

If you want to keep it simple, you can track time passing with a 3d campaign timer.

If tracking specific months isn't appealing, try just using parts of the season: "The beginning of…", "The height of…", and "The end of…"

When time passes, the PCs choose downtime actions (p. 141).

*Didact:* `didact track new 'Time passes' --kind pool --size 3 --pool campaign`.
