title: "Expendable Arcana"
tags: [arcana, magic, potions]
page: 40
---
Limited use items such as potions and scrolls can only be used once, and because of that limitation, have an effect one tier higher. Scrolls are highly sought-after arcana, used to learn theorems with the wizard's Spellcraft talent (p. 86).

Trivial arcana is a special tier exclusive to expendable arcana, allowing a single minor effect (as an action or spell). Vantage and other passive effects last for the scene. Minor expendable arcana bestows a single potent effect, while Major allows the PC to produce the effects of a ritual. Mythic expendable arcana are plot devices only, changing the world as the GM sees fit.
