The left side of the character sheet is the same for all paths, with: basic stats (p. 22), damage (p. 27), spark (p. 12), backgrounds (p. 20), thread (p. 25), and ally bonds (p. 22). The right side has a core talent for each path, a collection of thematic path talents available to everyone, as well as your current story arcs, and experience.

What the example sheet shows:
- **Stats and damage:** Brawn, Agility, Wits and Presence, each with a mark box below it. The Brawn and Agility marks lead to one **bloodied** box, the Wits and Presence marks to one **rattled** box, and both of those lead to **desperate**. Its reminders: *Mark: +1t to stat, then clear. | Harm: +1t connected stats. | Critical: greater effect—secondary effect—setup—spark.*
- **Conditions** (+1t related), with examples: *daunted, winded, strained, battered, unsteady, confused*. Reminder: *Vex: fight—flight—freeze—freakout.*
- **Spark (+1d)**, two boxes. *Take spark: roll a disaster (grim cut by thorn); introduce a tangle; resolve or move on from story arc; quarrel with an ally; ally changes bond with you.*
- **Thread**, two boxes. *Spend thread: establish a character, setting, or scene details within your vantage. Doubtful details may require a story roll.*
- **Name and distinctive features, backgrounds & wises** (two backgrounds, three wises each), and **bonds** (each ally's name with an intensity and a nature: *lowkey affection, playful doubts, deep rivalry*).
