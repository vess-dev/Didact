Any significant stretch of time between action (*a night's camp, a week's travel*) clears all marks and harm. This natural healing is paced for drama, not realism. If you prefer per-session talents to reset during rest instead of with a new session, use the Rest Resets optional rule on p. 174.

*Didact:* `didact do rest who=<pc>`.
