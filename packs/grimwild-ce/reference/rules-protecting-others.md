title: "Protecting Others"
tags: [core, rolls, teamwork, defense]
page: 14
mechanics: [defense]
---
When an ally is targeted by an impact move from their own action roll, you can choose to put yourself in harm's way to protect them. Make a 1d defense roll and share the risk. With a perfect you both avoid the trouble. On a grim, you both get hit with it. On a messy, the GM can lighten the consequences for you both, or hit only you with the impact move.
