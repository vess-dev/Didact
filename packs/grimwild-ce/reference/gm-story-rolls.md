Roll to disclaim decision-making, letting the dice decide story questions when you don't want to, like an NPC's reaction, off-screen action, or how good or bad something that just happened is. You can also collapse any rule back to a story roll when there's no specific mechanic to map it to.

Roll dice based on the odds of a favorable outcome for the chosen perspective. *Are you rolling for the fish or the fisherman?* **1d** bad odds · **2d** even odds · **3d** good odds.
- **Perfect (6):** It's the ideal situation.
- **Messy (4-5):** It's okay, but there's a catch.
- **Grim (1-3):** It's not good, and now it's a problem.

*How does the weather look as the party reaches the cliffside? Perfect: the clouds start to part as the rain stops, and it appears you have a clear day ahead of you. Messy: the light drizzle holds steady; it'll be a little slick moving up the side of the mountain. Grim: the wind begins to blow harder and the rain gets heavier; it's going to be tough to get to the top.*
