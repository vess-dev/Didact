This tracks the effort to overcome complicated tasks. In combat, this is used to track the progress towards defeating enemies, with larger pools used for higher tier monsters (p. 109). Roll this pool when action rolls bring the task closer to completion, or it makes sense in the fiction. *4d Barroom Brawl | 6d Tracking Quarry | 8d Escape the Guards | 6d Wind Elemental.*

If no dice drop from a task pool, push (take a mark to drop 1d anyway) or pivot (take a secondary effect instead), p. 18. Greater effect and potency drop 1d before rolling.

*Didact:* `didact track new 'Barroom Brawl' --kind pool --size 4 --pool task`, then `didact track roll <pool>` (`--drop 1` for greater effect).
