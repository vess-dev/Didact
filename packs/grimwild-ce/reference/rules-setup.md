When a previous action gives you an upper hand in your task, increase your vantage and take +1d. Setups handle more impactful but temporary fiction changes, lasting between a single follow up action, and the rest of the scene depending on the fiction. *The city watch is distracted. | The queen is caught off-guard. | The wizard is invisible.*

Setup is also a critical bonus (p. 10): a benefit to a follow-up roll, usually as an assist without risk.
