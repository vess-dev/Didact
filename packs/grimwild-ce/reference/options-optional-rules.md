The optional rules, by kind:
- **Gameplay:** token initiative, rest resets, stat progression, vigor pool (p. 174).
- **Gear:** armor, supplies, encumbrance (p. 175), wealth, weapon styles (p. 176).
- **Advancement:** level 0 (p. 176), milestone levels, focused growth (p. 177).
- **GM tools:** active vigilance, tick pools, writers' room (p. 177).

Each is its own section here (`didact rules lookup <name>`). Setting dials (pp. 172-173) and flavors of fantasy (pp. 181-187) change the game's tone.
