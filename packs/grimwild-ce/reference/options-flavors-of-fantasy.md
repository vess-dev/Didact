Each flavor gives guidance, rules tweaks, and setting dials to shift the base assumptions of Grimwild over to other popular sub-genres of fantasy. Pick and choose what you want to use for your own setting, altering it as needed.

The flavors: Heroic Fantasy (p. 181, the default), Grimdark (p. 182), Low Fantasy (p. 183), Sword & Sorcery (p. 184), Noblebright (p. 185), High Fantasy (p. 186) and Cozy Fantasy (p. 187). Each rates its scale, lethality and magic, and lists media touchstones.
