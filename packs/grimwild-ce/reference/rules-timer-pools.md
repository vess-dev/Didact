This tracks events unfolding in the background. Roll this pool as the occasional impact move, for key moments, time passing, or after a few PCs take action. *8d Sunrise | 6d Corruption Grows | 4d Patrol Returns | 8d Magical Wings.*

*Didact:* `didact track new 'Sunrise' --kind pool --size 8 --pool timer`.
