- **Armor:** Heavy armor and shields can be represented by talents like Bulwark (p. 65). Other armors remain as vantage and set dressing—impacting rolls and thorns. *Splintmail may let a berserker make a defense roll with one less thorn, but it may also add +1t when they're climbing a cliff or fleeing from an angry owlbear.* If you want armor choice to stand out a bit more, the armor optional rule (p. 175) gives PCs an armor pool to help absorb hits.
- **Attacking multiple:** PCs with the vantage to do so can target multiple dangerous foes with their weapons at once, though this often depends on the foe's tier (p. 109) and comes with great risk, adding thorns or higher stakes with the attempt. Vantage often comes from sweeping weapons, time on the battlefield, or incredible martial skill.
