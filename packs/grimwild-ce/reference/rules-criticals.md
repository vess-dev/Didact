A critical happens when you roll more than one 6. These ignore thorns (difficulty, p. 11), and let you choose a critical bonus:
- **Greater Effect:** The action is more impactful. If a task pool (p. 18) would be rolled because of the action, remove 1d from it before rolling. *The knife gets plunged between the dragon's scales. | Your quick-witted remark infuriates him.*
- **Secondary Effect:** Gain the effects of another related perfect action. *With a flick, your attack also disarms the pirate. | The baroness is charmed, and she also lets slip a secret.*
- **Setup:** Give a benefit to a follow-up roll, usually as an assist without risk (p. 14). *Your rain of arrows gives your ally a chance to rush through their lines. | You draw the orc's full attention.*

If nothing comes to mind, take spark and keep moving.

*Didact:* action, defense, montage and power pool rolls read two or more 6s as a critical before any thorn.
