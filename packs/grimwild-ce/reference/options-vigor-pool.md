This rule is intended for games without healing magic.

**Each PC has a 3d Vigor resource pool.** When the PCs have a moment to catch their breath, they can roll the pool to clear 1 harm. This pool refreshes after an extended period of rest or downtime. Increase or lower the pool to change the lethality.

*Didact:* character creation makes each PC's 3d Vigor pool; `didact do vigor who=<pc> pool=<vigor>` rolls it to clear 1 harm, and `didact do refresh pool=<vigor>` after an extended rest or downtime.
