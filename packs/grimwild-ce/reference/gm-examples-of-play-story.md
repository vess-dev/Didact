- **Upholding Honor (p. 125).** Swapping your desire for belonging for honor, you tangle yourself into holding the bridge alone while your friends escape. *Traits and desires give vantage for tangles; when they change, let the group see it play out.*
- **Chieftain's Temper (p. 126).** A 4d Temper pool, triggered by a grim, drops to 1d at once. *Pools are swingy by design, and shape scenes even when not rolled.*
- **Nemesis Tangle (p. 126).** Spotting your nemesis mid-battle is a tangle (spark); a 2d story roll, its outcomes announced first, decides who sees whom first. *A tangle can add story details instead of thread.*
- **Dwarf vs. Cave Troll (p. 126).** Thread on an underground-navigation wise finds a tunnel where the troll can't maneuver: +2t becomes +1t. *Thread can buy mechanical advantage.*
- **Story Arcs (p. 127).** A runaway heir plays the arcs Enjoy Life and Keep a Secret: the player brings them into play, the GM challenges them. *Arcs are an optional player tool that expands vantage and signals themes.*
- **Settling Debts (p. 127).** Thread puts your estranged father in town; he's a benevolent guild leader, so you let it go and take spark for resolving the arc. *Tangles are always bad, while story just makes things happen; however an arc ends, you take spark.*
- **Archmage Secrets (p. 127).** A damning secret: Presence +1t, a grim, and vex—fight, flight, freeze or freakout. *Vex is a big open door to roleplaying.*
