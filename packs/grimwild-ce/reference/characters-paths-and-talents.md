Below, you'll find explanations of common options and terms used throughout the paths. These clarifications help you better understand how to customize your character and make use of talents. Not every path is available in every game. Some only fit certain settings and themes. Make sure to ask your GM ahead of time what is available.

Some paths include additional character-building features and advice on roleplaying. Both are entirely optional, but if they resonate with you, lean into them to make those aspects important in the game.

Reader Lens - D&D: Grimwild works off flavor and descriptions in the moment. If you’re trying to remake a character in this system, don’t get caught on capturing each small mechanical bit. Make the character with broad strokes, and fill in the rest with flavor.
