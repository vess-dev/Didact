Paths are collections of talents grouped by common adventuring themes. Each has a core talent and core growth—your core talent gets stronger as you gain levels. Remember, paths cannot be used to establish story details—backgrounds and talents can. Each path is covered in detail later in this chapter.

### Core Paths

- Bard: Stirring the soul with song or deed. Spend inspiration to bolster your allies, and influence foes to turn the tide.
- Berserker: Blood, mettle, and the sounds of battle. Fly into a frenzy to wade through foes and lay waste to your enemies.
- Cleric: Faith is stronger than any blade. Use your rites pools to call upon your god, wielding their divine magic.
- Druid: The fury of nature—untamed and relentless. Use your wild shape pool to take bestial forms and learn their abilities.
- Fighter: Resolve honed with every strike. Spend mastery to fight with unparalleled expertise in your chosen style.
- Monk: Perfection is a journey, not a destination. Spend flow to act with mystical grace and stop foes in their tracks.
- Paladin: An Oath taken, a burden willingly carried. Spend smite to overcome challenges with the power of your conviction.
- Ranger: No escape, no mercy, only the chase. Tag a foe as your quarry to learn about, track, and slay your chosen enemy.
- Rogue: A whisper in the dark, then a quiet end. Use your competence pool to grasp victory from the jaws of defeat.
- Sorcerer: Magic flows like blood, powerful and wild. Use your magic in near infinite ways, but be careful of magical twists.
- Warlock: Power always demands a hefty price. Bargain with your patron for Eldritch Talents, but beware their fickle nature.
- Wizard: Arcane knowledge, wielded with precision. Use the spell theorems you've learned to call precise magic to your hands.

### Extra Options

Also included are the Artificer, Psion, Summoner, Swashbuckler, and Witch. These alternatives were not a part of the original free version of Grimwild. Legacy options—those from the original version of the game—are also included on page 178, alongside path talents cut from previous versions of the Community Edition.
