---
id: rules-forward-momentum
title: "Forward Momentum"
tags: [core, rolls, gm]
page: 17
mechanics: [action]
---
If a grim threatens to grind the story to a halt, the GM can keep things moving—you get what you want, but in a way that makes things worse, or has an unintended effect. If a roll somehow turns out to have no effect (*the guard was already dead*), you learn new info or setup a follow-up.
