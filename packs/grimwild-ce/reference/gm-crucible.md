A forge for inspiration, encouraging emergent, low-prep gameplay. Turn to the GM crucible, often along with a story roll, when you want the seed of an idea to expand upon to help answer questions about the story. Roll once on each table. Each roll gives two results (*3, 1 = 31: precarious or 13: broken*). Let your mind drift towards the most evocative, then drill down on it and interpret what it might mean: roll 2, smash them together, and interpret.

Roll `gm-crucible` (reprinted with the solo rules, p. 192).

*What's inside the temple? aged + beacon → a forgotten artifact; perilous + maze → a dangerous trial. | Why is the village so quiet? tense + scheme → they're plotting rebellion; bleak + debris → it was ransacked. | What caused the havoc? flickering + rift → demonic portal opened; immense + betrayal → a coup took place. | What is the farmer hiding? tough + scar → a wartorn past; lost + jackpot → secret rumored trove.*
