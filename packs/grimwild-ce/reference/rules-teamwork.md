When a larger group of characters work together, only one PC rolls for the action while others can assist, as long as it makes narrative sense. The GM decides who rolls—sometimes the most skilled when success is more important, and sometimes the least when failure has greater impact. The entire group shares the consequences, but the scale of the action increases as well.

*The group works together to lift a boulder (strongest rolls). | Group sneaks around a guard patrol (least sneaky rolls). | Group finds information on criminal local faction (most street savvy rolls).*
