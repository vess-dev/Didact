This is an extended break, such as a month or season. All PCs fully heal, including relevant conditions, and reset per-session talents. The GM rolls faction pools, then picks one and depletes it completely to move the story forward.

For downtime actions (rest, plan ahead, pursue a goal, unwind, venture), see Expanded Downtime (p. 141).

*Didact:* `didact do downtime who=<pc>` for each PC; the GM then runs `didact faction turn` and depletes one pool: `didact do faction-goal who=<faction> pool=<pool>`.
