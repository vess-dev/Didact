Every path talent is available to every other path. Those under each specific path are collected around their loose theme, but by no means fit every character in that path, nor do they only fit that path. You are encouraged to check out all the talents and pick which ones fit your character best.

To avoid redundancy, similar talents across paths have been avoided. Instead, you're encouraged to reflavor talents to fit your character's theme. For example, a wizard could take the fighter's Bulwark and flavor it as a magical shield.
