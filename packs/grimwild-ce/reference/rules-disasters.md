A disaster means you don't get what you want, and things take a sharp turn for the worst. The GM can turn whatever would have happened into the worst case scenario. When a PC rolls a disaster, they take spark.

A disaster is a grim cut by a thorn (perfect → messy → grim → disaster). On an action roll: failure, with a worse impact move, and the player takes spark. On a defense roll: take a worse impact move, then take spark (p. 17).
