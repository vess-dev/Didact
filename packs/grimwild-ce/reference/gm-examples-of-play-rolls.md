- **Kobold Trials (p. 122).** A perfect that drops nothing from the 4d Trial by Ordeal pivots to a secondary effect (the chieftain is won over); later thorns cut perfects to messies that still drop dice (4d→1d→0d), with a marked Wits and quarrelling kobolds as the cost.
- **Distracted Guard (p. 122).** Thread gives the rogue a makeshift lockpick, but it's still +2t: [3, 4, 2 | 7, 8] cuts a messy twice, to a disaster—the lock opens, but two impact moves follow (bloodied, and the door slammed and locked). *Without thread it was impossible; with the guard watching, +3t.*
- **Cliff Escape (p. 123).** Bloodied and climbing under arrows (+1t), a cut grim leaves you desperate; you try again with spark (2d, now 3t): a messy—you make it, and the GM makes you rattled. *You can roll again if the situation has changed; a messy is a success, just not pretty.*
- **Undead Hordes (p. 123).** Flee (an 8d | Escape) or fight three linked 4d-6d-8d | Undead Horde challenges; a quarrel settles it, and a Build Up follows. *The rogue would lose a spark to the cap of 2 (tangle, quarrel, Build Up): give them a pass to have three, just this once.*
- **Rogue Healing (p. 124).** A risky healing spell with two assists: [2, 4, 1] plus 1 and 6, a perfect. *Assist dice combine into one outcome, each rolled by its owner; whoever matters most leads the roll.*
- **Tunnel Collapse (p. 124).** A montage out of a collapsing dungeon: a disaster, a grim and a messy, so the group gets a messy. Its Agility and Presence marks land on your already-marked Agility, which becomes harm—and you were already bloodied and desperate, so you're dropped; a 2d roll gives a messy (knocked out, carried to safety). *2d is a good default for dropped (3d would be fine after a hit that was only a mark).*
- **Assassin Attack (p. 125).** A surprise attack at dire stakes: an Agility defense roll (a physical reaction), +1t—a messy, so no bloodied, just a Brawn mark. *The GM picks the defense stat; the player has only a soft veto.*
- **Blessed Incense (p. 125).** A 4d resource pool rolled on each use: 4d→3d, then nothing drops, then 3d→0d. *If an action's pool drops nothing, push or pivot.*
