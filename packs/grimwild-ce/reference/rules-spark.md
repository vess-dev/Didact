Pure protagonist energy, useful when there's a pivotal task you want to succeed. You can spend spark before any type of roll you make to add +1d per spark spent. Characters can have up to 2 spark at a time, and it carries over across sessions. Take spark when you:
- introduce your own complication (a tangle, p. 25),
- roll a disaster,
- resolve a story arc (p. 26),
- quarrel with an ally (p. 23), or
- an ally's bond with you changes (p. 22).

Other rules give spark too: pre-session (p. 31), the Build Up suspense move (p. 98), carousing (p. 39), the joy of exploration (p. 131) and the Unwind downtime action (p. 141).
