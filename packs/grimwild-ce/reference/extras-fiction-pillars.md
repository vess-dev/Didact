GM tools are extensions of the GM Toolkit—optional extras to enhance your game. They might not be for every GM, but you may find them useful. Experiment with these ideas and see if they stick in your repertoire!

Fiction pillars is a method of preparation that lets you create ready-made blocks of fiction, giving you a sturdier foundation than pure off-the-cuff improvisation. These "pillars" serve as anchors for the improv storytelling that unfolds during a session, helping you maintain consistency and confidence in your game.

Before a session, imagine and detail 7 elements that might come up in that session. These can be key NPCs, important locations, items, encounters, or events. Anything works. Add sensory details—sights, sounds, and smells. Define the element's pull on the story, the drive. This may be an NPC's goal, an object's purpose, or impact of the element as a whole. Note one way to embody it at the table as the GM, like a behavior or sound effect. Keep everything short—just a few words per detail. Skip any steps you draw a blank on and make final notes if you have anything left to add.

During play, we can sprinkle in these pillars to act as reinforced parts of the fiction to hang the rest of our fiction off of. They reside more vividly in our mind. You can keep any unused ones and repurpose them for later sessions. The key is that you activate your imagination beforehand so the pillars feel more vivid and real.

*Examples:*
- **Harvest Festival** — *Leave your troubles and arguments at the door. Enjoy the food, shows, and games.* Sights: *vibrant banners, overflowing carts, bonfires at dusk.* Sounds: *laughter and music, creak of carts, rustle of leaves.* Smells: *roasted corn, spiced cider, damp post-harvest soil.* Embody: *bite an apple, play a flute, raise a mug.*
- **Raddigan, blind goblin** — *Trade wisdom for gold. Live plainly, and meditate for visions of the dire futures to come.* Sights: *gray-green wrinkles, clouded eyes, sparse hair.* Sounds: *prophetic rhymes, whispered goblin curses.* Smells: *warm tea, freshly picked herbs.* Embody: *turns head to think, taps cane on floor.*
