title: "Milestone Levels (Optional Rule)"
tags: [options, optional-rules, advancement]
page: 177
mechanics: [experience, level-up]
---
Use this rule to eschew keeping track of experience altogether. Players don't add XP after each session. Instead, the party members level up after meaningful completion of a major group story arc. The group should talk about what counts as meaningful, but ultimately the choice is up to the GM.

*Didact:* with milestone levels, no XP is given; `didact char advance <pc>` levels up without it.
