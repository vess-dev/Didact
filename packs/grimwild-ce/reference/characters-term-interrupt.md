tags: [characters]
page: 53
mechanics: [action]
---
Make an action roll to try to stop an impact move. This requires a specific talent. If not already involved, you now share the risk. On a messy, the GM keeps (or takes) their suspense instead of finishing the impact move. On a grim, the GM makes the impact move as normal (while you share the risk).

*Didact:* `didact do action who=<pc> stat=… interrupt`.
