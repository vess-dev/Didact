**Reading a perk.** *Italic words split by dashes* are a menu — pick one. Anything that triggers **on a perfect** also triggers on a critical; anything that triggers **on a grim** also triggers on a disaster. **Given time** means at least a beat of undisturbed work; perks that say so usually let you **push yourself** to do it on the spot instead — mark a stat after it resolves. **Always** holds about 95% of the time: it takes a Counter from a truly exceptional threat, or fiction that plainly rules it out, to stop it. A **lesser** foe is a mook; a **greater** one is an elite or a boss (tiers, Chapter 7). A **quick action** is a second action taken at once: the world's answer to the first, impact move included, waits until both are done. One per beat, however many perks offer it. **Your own dice** are the ones you roll yourself — your stat's, plus any spark, perk, setup, chem, or relic die — never an ally's assist, outside help, a pool's dice, or a special die like the Weak Point die.
