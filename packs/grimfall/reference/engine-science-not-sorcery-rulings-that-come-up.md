**Rulings that come up.** Working tech is visible and loud — hiding it is extremely difficult at best, and usually impossible. Anyone who knows tech is at work can try to **counter** it, no training needed: cut its power, smash the emitter, out-logic the program — creativity beats wattage. Helping with your tech is just an assist (say how, roll 1d, share the risk). A device's effect lasts a beat or two; a potent one, a scene — after that the wasteland reasserts itself. Buffs ride the usual shapes — a 2–4d timer or a small bonus-dice pool — and stretching one across a whole scene or a whole group takes a potent device. Healing follows suit: a device can clear marks, vex, or a minor condition; it takes a potent one to mend harm.
