*the wasteland kills everything but you*

**ALSO:** Diehard, Hardcase, Stray, Survivor

You've eaten radiation, walked off wounds that should've buried you, and crossed deserts that swallowed whole caravans. People keep waiting for you to drop. You keep disappointing them.

> **Core Perk — TOUGHNESS**
> *Growth: At levels 2, 4, and 6, gain +1 Grit per session.*
>
> There is simply more of you to spend — scar tissue, a ghoul's hide, a metabolism that laughs at poison, or sheer refusal. *(Your call — re-skin it freely.)* Each session you have **6 Grit**:
> - **Dig deep.** Whenever you would **mark a stat** — from damage, *or from pushing yourself* — you can spend 1 Grit instead, even when that mark would have stacked into harm.
> - **Walk it off.** Whenever you would take **harm** — even harm that would drop you — you can spend 2 Grit and shrug it off entirely.
> - ***Is that all?*** When Grit turns aside something a foe did to you, you're **set up** against that foe: take +1d on your next roll against them.

> **[Optional] HOW THE WASTELAND MARKED YOU.** *Choose 3:* skin bleached or peeling from rads — a cough that never fully clears — eyes that don't dilate right — a limb that's mostly scar — fingers lost to frostbite or worse — a gut that digests anything — a heartbeat you can't kill — too stubborn to show any of it. **WHAT KEEPS YOU WALKING.** *Choose 1:* a promise — a grudge — a grave to reach — someone who can't make it alone — habit — you honestly never thought to stop.
