**Suspense** is your currency of dramatic timing. Earn it — **1** at a time — by skipping a prompted impact move or by making suspense moves; spend **1** to strike at any moment. Impact moves aimed at a PC who didn't invite them grant a defense roll. Every challenge you frame brings **2 bonus suspense** of its own.
