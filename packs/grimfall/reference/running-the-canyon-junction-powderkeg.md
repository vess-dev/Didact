A drop-in sandbox. Canyon Junction is a water-rich crossroads town that everyone wants and no one can hold. Track each faction's goals with **faction pools**, rolled between sessions or whenever the story triggers them — and in downtime, roll them all, then pick one and run it dry. When one empties, the goal lands on screen — or the faction moves against a rival, with a story roll in its favor deciding how it goes. Every other faction reacts. Keep four to six in play, and replace any faction the story uses up.

| Faction | Resources | Goals (faction pools) |
|---|---|---|
| **Junction Town Council** | the only clean well for miles · a militia of shopkeeps | *4d Hire Guns* · *6d The Well Runs Dry* |
| **NCR Garrison** | disciplined troopers · a supply line (thin) | *4d Fortify the Crossing* · *6d Crack Down on Smuggling* |
| **Legion Frumentarii** | hidden agents · fear and coin | *6d Turn the Tribes* · *8d Mark the Town for Caesar* |
| **Brotherhood Recon Lance** | power armor · a Vertibird (barely) | *4d Seize the Reactor Tech* · *6d Vanish Back East* |
| **The Jackal Boys** | a chem lab in the hills · desperate numbers | *4d Raid the Caravans* · *6d Buy Real Guns* |
| **Vault 113 (sealed)** | unknown · the town is built on its door | *8d The Door Opens* |
