PCs are as alert as their vantage says — nobody rolls to not-walk into the obvious. Against a hidden danger, spotlight the most relevant PC and pick: **reveal** (they see it plainly — the tripwire glints), **hint** (one shot at intuition — *the street is too quiet*), or **strike** (spend suspense: the danger lands as an impact move, and the target gets a defense roll). A raider gets a reveal on an ambush; a vault kid gets a hint; a Mister Handy mid-monologue takes the strike.
