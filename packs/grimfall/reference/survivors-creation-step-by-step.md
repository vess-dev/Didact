1. **Backgrounds (2, three wises each):** your origin and trade — the pillars of your vantage. Pick from the tables below, or build your own: a name and three wises. A heritage — ghoul, synth, super mutant, tribal, robot — can be one of your backgrounds if you want it to matter mechanically; otherwise it's set dressing you narrate freely.
2. **Traits (2 you are, 1 you're not):** brave, caring, confident, curious, gentle, honest, honorable, persistent, protective, quiet, rash, stubborn.
3. **Desires (2 you want, 1 you don't):** belonging, glory, harmony, honor, justice, knowledge, love, power, renown, thrills, wealth, wisdom.
4. **Features (3):** what people notice — *a ghoul's rasp, a glowing scar, a Pip-Boy that never comes off, a thousand-caps smile, dust-caked NCR fatigues.*
5. **Class:** pick one of the seven in Chapter 4 (its opening table sorts them at a glance). Take its Core Perk, plus one more perk — from your class's seven, another class's (never its Core Perk), or the Perk Compendium in Chapter 5.
6. **Stats:** start with 1 in each of Brawn, Agility, Wits, Presence, then assign 4 more points (max 3 in any one).
7. **Arcs:** choose one or two character arcs — *settle my debt to the Followers, find out what the Institute left in my head.*
8. **Bonds:** as the others introduce themselves, set one bond toward each PC — an intensity + a nature (*growing curiosity, complex camaraderie, tense rivalry*).
9. **Review:** check the party concept, group arc, and setting still fit. Adjust.
10. **Montage:** run the last job you pulled together — a group montage roll (2d each) — and narrate how it went, working in your bonds.
