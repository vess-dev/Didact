A class describes **what you can do**, never **who you are**. Any class can be a vault dweller, a ghoul, a super mutant, a synth, a tribal, an NCR trooper, a Brotherhood initiate, a raider, a courier, or a wandering robot — none of that is baked in. Being a ghoul lives in your **backgrounds** and **features** (and maybe one perk, if you want it to carry mechanical weight).

**Opting in is fine, too.** A few perks capture iconic wasteland transformations — the Sledge's *Power Armor Training*, the Everlast's *Ghoulish*. These are never required by a class and never define one; they're choices, and they re-skin like anything else. Like any perk, they can be taken by anyone — a Pathfinder who scavenged a working frame, an Inventor slowly turning ghoul. The class is just where the perk happens to live on the page.
