- **Rest resets.** Per-session perks reset on a real rest instead of each session. When the group rests: everyone takes spark, per-session perks and thread reset, some conditions fade, and the GM takes 2 suspense. Better for marathon sessions and West-Marches play.
- **Milestone levels.** Skip XP; the crew levels when a group arc resolves meaningfully.
- **Focused growth.** Slash XP boxes before filling them (double the road), but award a bonus XP when the session hits the party concept dead-on.
- **Level 0.** Start as vault kids and dirt farmers: no Core Perk, 3 stat points instead of 4. The Core Perk and the last point arrive after the first session — the wasteland's real character creation.
- **Stat progression.** For long campaigns: when you level up, you can forgo the new perk to raise a stat by 1 (max 3). It counts as the survivor's one lifetime bump (Chapter 2). Keep it out of small-scale games.
