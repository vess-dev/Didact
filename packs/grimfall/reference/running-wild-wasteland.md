An optional dial, set at session zero like any flavor of the apocalypse: the wasteland gets *weird*. When things go strange — a critical on a story roll, a disaster in the middle of nowhere — roll a thorn (d8) or choose from the crucible, and play it absolutely straight.

> **WILD WASTELAND CRUCIBLE.** *a fridge holding a skeleton in a fedora — a downed scout saucer, still humming — a phone ringing in a dead booth, for you — a robot convinced it's your mother — holy hand grenades in a cracked shrine — a deathclaw that just watches you pass — a vending machine that haggles — two ghouls re-enacting a pre-war sitcom, forever.*
