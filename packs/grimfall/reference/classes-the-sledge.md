*the world is soft, and you are not*

**ALSO:** Bruiser, Reaver, Slab, Warboss

You solve problems by being bigger, meaner, and harder to stop than whatever's in front of you. Raider, deathclaw, or a rusted blast door — it all bends the same way when you put your back into it.

> **Core Perk — UNSTOPPABLE FORCE**
> *Growth: At levels 3 and 6, gain +1 Might per scene.*
>
> Anything heavy enough is a weapon in your hands: you never take thorns for an improvised, oversized, or outmatched weapon. Each scene you have **4 Might**. Spend 1 to:
> - **Heave** — pull off a **potent feat of raw force** against the world: tear a door off its track, flip a car, hold up a sagging ceiling, carry two friends out at a run. Turning that force on a foe — hurling them across the room, dropping the car on them — costs **2 Might**.
> - **Follow through** — before a melee, thrown, or charging roll, declare you also: *send them flying — pin them down — wreck their gear — drive them back.* On a perfect or messy, it happens too.
> - **Bull through** — ignore all thorns on one roll from being outnumbered, outsized, or grabbed.
>
> *Pain is fuel:* Whenever a blow leaves you bloodied or worse, gain 1 Might — even past your limit.

> **[Optional] THE SOURCE OF YOUR STRENGTH.** *Choose 2 to combine, and 1 you have disdain for:* hard labor in the wastes — a body remade by FEV or rads — relentless training — chem-fueled muscle — born big and mean — you genuinely don't know. **MARKS OF VIOLENCE.** *Choose 3:* a flattened nose — knuckles like gravel — a web of old knife scars — burn-slick forearms — a jaw that clicks — not a scratch on you.
