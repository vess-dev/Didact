Frame anything worth screen time as a challenge: a task pool (2d–8d), one or two **traits**, two or three **moves**, and — when failure has a shape — a **fail state**. *6d Outrun the Storm — trait: static-blind — moves: Wall of Dust, Buried Alive — fail: 4d The Caravan Is Lost.* Tiers and roles for enemies are in Chapter 7.
