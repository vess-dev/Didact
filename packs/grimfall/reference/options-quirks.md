Fallout's old double-edged "traits," renamed so they don't trip over Chapter 3's. At creation, each survivor may take **one quirk**: an edge and a flaw, welded together. The edge is always on — a permission or a thorn ignored, never a die. The flaw is a standing tangle: the first time each session it truly bites, take spark.

| Quirk | The edge | The flaw |
|---|---|---|
| **Built to Destroy** | Your criticals with a weapon always add collateral damage, free. | Your gear wears out fast — breaking it is always a fair impact move. |
| **Fast Shot** | You never lose a beat drawing, reloading, or snapping off a shot. | You can't slow down: nothing with a trigger is ever *given time*. |
| **Four Eyes** | Glasses on, fine detail at any distance never thorns you. | Glasses off — and they will come off — +1t on anything that needs your eyes. |
| **Good Natured** | Strangers read you as harmless: first impressions start a step warmer. | You're no killer: +1t on your first aggressive roll of any scene you didn't start. |
| **Heavy Handed** | What you hit moves — back, down, or through something. | You can't do subtle: no pulled punches, no quiet takedowns. |
| **Hoarder** | You always have the odd bolt, cord, or tin: small set dressing is never in doubt. | Walking away from loot takes a push. |
| **Jinxed** | Disasters near you — anyone's — splash onto the other side, too. | They land on your friends exactly as often as on your foes. |
| **Kamikaze** | You always act first when you rush in. | The first impact move of any fight finds you, if it can. |
| **Small Frame** | You fit where others don't, and slip a grab like smoke. | Knockdowns and shoves always work on you. |
| **Trigger Discipline** | You never hit a bystander or a friend, whatever the dice say. | You never act first — you're still making sure. |
