A machine or creature background is a body as much as a past — its wises cover what the chassis knows and does. Re-skin marks and harm as dents, breaches, and stripped servos; treatment is a wrench and a welder, and your *3d Stimpaks* are a *3d Repair Kit*. You're still a survivor like any other: pick a class, and let the perks ride the frame.
