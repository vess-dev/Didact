**Arcana** (Chapter 8) are the relics: **minor**, **major**, and **mythic**, most balanced by a pool or a price. Single-use finds — a syringe, a grenade, one glowing bottle — are **expendable**, and punch one tier above their weight. The GM picks the stat an arcana rolls (Wits when nothing fits, or a story roll when it's pure luck), and mythics answer to no cap but the table's judgment.
