- **Lethality.** *Deadlier:* high and dire stakes often; a desperate survivor also carries an urgent condition (*4d Bleeding Out*); dropped = a 1d story roll; no coming back; stimpaks at 2d. *Softer:* low stakes often, even in fights against weak foes; dropped = a 3d story roll; permanent conditions are rare; stimpaks flow (the *Softer* stimpak dial).
- **Scale.** *Smaller:* more rolls are risky or impossible, mooks are rare and every foe is a threat, and levels cap at 3 (swap perks at story milestones instead). *Larger:* fewer rolls are risky, mooks are waded through and toughs fall a few at a time, survivors start at level 3, and bosses have a *secret* to overcome, not just a bigger pool.
- **Dangerous tech.** Weird tech bites back: any device roll with two or more 1s, thorns included, misfires — *a 1d story roll for the collateral — a dark omen (the GM takes suspense) — a roll of a 6d Corruption pool* (at 0d, a trait, desire, bond, or look turns for the worse).
- **Flavors of the wasteland.** Point the dials at a subgenre: *grim survival* (deadlier + supplies + focused growth), *pulp heroics* (softer + larger scale), *frontier western* (smaller scale + factions front and center), *weird science* (dangerous tech + Wild Wasteland on).
