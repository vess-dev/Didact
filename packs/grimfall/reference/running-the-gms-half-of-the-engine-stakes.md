**Stakes.** Say them out loud when they change: **low** (a mark at most — a barfight), **regular** (one harm — the default), **high** (moves land double, and a disaster can drop you), **dire** (any impact move drops you). Nobody gets dropped by surprise math.

**Consequence craft.** One impact move is flexible: split it into smaller cuts (a mark *and* the rifle skitters away), spread it across several PCs (defense rolls for the bystanders), or land it **off-screen** — the ritual ticks forward, an innocent takes the blame, the caravan runs dry. Pressure and timer pools are always a legal target; sometimes the scariest move is starting a new 4d pool and rolling it immediately. When a messy has no natural on-screen bite, bank suspense and hit later with better timing.
