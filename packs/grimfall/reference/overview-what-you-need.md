- **People:** A GM and some players — three or four is ideal. Solo and duet play are covered in Chapter 9.
- **Dice:** Four to six d6 and two to four d8 per player — the d8s are **thorns**, the wasteland's way of saying *it's complicated*. The GM wants at least eight d6.
- **Paper:** A Survivor Sheet for each player, and index cards for pools and challenges.
- **Time:** Sessions run best at around three hours. Per-session perks reset when a new session begins.
- **Safety:** The wasteland runs dark — slavers, chems, the last taboo. Agree on lines and veils at session zero; the TTRPG Safety Toolkit (bit.ly/ttrpgsafetytoolkit) is the standard kit.
