**Defense rolls.** When trouble comes at you that your own roll didn't invite — the GM spends suspense, a mine clicks — the GM picks the stat that fits the danger and your likely reaction (you can pitch one), and you roll it. **Perfect:** you're clear. **Messy:** the GM lessens it. **Grim:** you take it all. **Disaster:** worse, and you take spark. You don't get a defense roll against the consequences of your own action roll — dodging was already priced in.
