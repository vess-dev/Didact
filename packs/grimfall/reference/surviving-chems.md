Anyone can chem up. A dose is a *trivial* expendable — a single device-level effect whose buff lasts the scene — and the strong stuff always kicks back. One dose, one scene, then the comedown. Chems are finds, purchases, and a *Chemist's* fresh batches (Chapter 4), never a bottomless pocket: a stash is as deep as the fiction says (or a *2d Chem Stash* resource pool, if your table would rather count).

| Chem | While it lasts | The kick |
|---|---|---|
| **Buffout** | **+1d** on feats of raw muscle | mark Brawn when it fades |
| **Psycho** | **+1d** on aggressive actions | take vex (anger) when it fades |
| **Jet** | **+1d** on anything that needs speed; you act first | the crash — +1t for a beat after |
| **Mentats** | **+1d** on puzzling, recalling, calculating | mark Wits when it fades |
| **Med-X** | pick one harm or condition: it adds no thorns while the dose lasts | numbed — +1t on Wits and Presence rolls |
| **Cateye** | darkness never thorns you | bright light dazzles — +1t for a beat whenever it hits |
| **Hydra** | a crippled limb works again, and a long-term limb condition steps down to short-term | it burns the whole time — mark Presence when it fades |
| **Fixer** | cravings go quiet: *Hooked* adds no thorns, and the dose counts as treatment toward kicking it | nausea — +1t on your next roll |
| **Rad-X / RadAway** | see Rads, above | — |

**Addiction.** Ride a chem through a **grim or disaster**, or lean on it session after session, and the GM may hand you a long-term condition — ***Hooked: [chem]***, +1t when you're craving and dry. Clear it like any long-term condition: real treatment, a stretch of clean living, or a story beat that costs you something.
