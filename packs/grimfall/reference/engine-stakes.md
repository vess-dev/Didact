The GM signals how hard the world hits. Unless it's said otherwise, stakes are regular.


- **Low** — an impact move weighs a mark at most. *Barfights and busted knuckles.*
- **Regular** — the default: an impact move weighs one harm.
- **High** — impact moves land double, and a disaster can drop you on the spot.
- **Dire** — death looms: any impact move drops you. If a scene could drop someone outright, everyone hears it before the dice fly.

**How a fight works.** Enemies never roll. A dangerous foe is a **task pool** (Chapter 7): hurt it and the GM rolls its pool; at 0d it's beaten, and one good roll can clear a handful of mooks outright. The world hits back through **impact moves** — on your messy and grim rolls, and whenever the GM spends suspense — and a move you didn't invite gets a **defense roll**.
