> **A SURVIVOR IN TEN LINES — "DUTCH."** *The party: Caravanners and Mercs, definitely not Raiders. The group arc: carve a safe route through the Divide.*
> **Backgrounds:** Caravan Hand *(trade routes · guard rotations · the price of everything)* and Ghoul of the Old World *(how it was before · two centuries of faces · outwaiting anything)*. **Traits:** stubborn and protective, not rash. **Desires:** belonging and justice, not wealth. **Features:** a rasp like a dragged chair · a pre-war wristwatch, still wound · a brahmin-hide duster.
> **Class:** the Everlast — *Toughness*, plus *Ghoulish* to give the heritage teeth. **Stats:** Brawn 3, Agility 1, Wits 2, Presence 2. **Arc:** find out who else is still alive from before. **Bond:** *lowkey doubts* toward the Longshot who keeps winning his caps.
