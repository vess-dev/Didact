When time passes — a month behind settlement walls, a season in a bunker — per-session perks refresh, and each survivor picks **one** downtime action — or **two** different ones if they sold off a haul:

- **Rest** — shake every non-permanent condition (marks and harm already clear with any real rest); the GM may rule that a long-term one lingers.
- **Venture** — odd jobs, courier runs, a solo score. Make a story roll: on a perfect, a minor haul, a rumor, or a new ally; on a messy or worse, it's complicated.
- **Blow off steam** — give a vignette of how you unwind — the bar, the fights, the chapel, the jukebox — and take spark.
- **Pursue a goal** — chase a project with a task pool, rolled each time you pick this: build the still, mod the rifle, retrain a wise or a perk, cook a legend-grade batch.
- **Plan ahead** — write a few words about how you prepare and take a special thread, on top of your usual two, usable only on details from that preparation. It's lost when time passes again.

Meanwhile the GM rolls the **faction pools** (Chapter 9), then picks one and runs it dry. The wasteland moves too.
