Survivors have **four stats** — Brawn, Agility, Wits, and Presence — and they drive every roll and the whole damage track. The seven classes are themed on Vault-Tec's **S.P.E.C.I.A.L.**; that's their identity, the badge on the class card, and good fiction for your vantage (*"all Strength, no Wits"*). It isn't a second stat line — each theme simply leans on one of the four stats.

| Attribute | Class | Mostly rolls |
|---|---|---|
| **S**trength | The Sledge | Brawn |
| **P**erception | The Pathfinder | Wits to spot · Agility to shoot |
| **E**ndurance | The Everlast | Brawn |
| **C**harisma | The Charmer | Presence |
| **I**ntelligence | The Inventor | Wits |
| **A**gility | The Ace | Agility |
| **L**uck | The Longshot | nothing — Luck is fate, working through the Longshot's perks, never a stat |

**Damage rides the stats:** heavy damage or stacked marks to **Brawn or Agility** leave you **bloodied**; to **Wits or Presence**, **rattled**. A second bloodied or a second rattled leaves you **desperate**; once you're desperate, a harm you already carry **drops** you.
