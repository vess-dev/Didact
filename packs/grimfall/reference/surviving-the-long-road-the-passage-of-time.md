**The passage of time.** When the GM declares that time passes, the game jumps ahead — usually a month or a season. Give each stretch three sensory details, and let one be wrong for the old world — *ash-blossom on the wind, geiger crickets, clean snow that shouldn't be trusted.* (Or just track the year on a *3d* campaign timer.) Campaign pools roll, factions move, and downtime opens.
