**Wheels & wings.** A working vehicle is a haul first and vantage after: give it **two style words** (*armored, thirsty · fast, flashy · quiet, fragile*) and let the fiction do the rest. Driving hard is an action roll — Agility to drive, Wits to navigate — and damage lands as conditions on the machine (*4d Radiator Weeps*, a thrown track). A chase is opposed task pools, or one pressure pool closing in. Fuel is a story, not a gauge: when the GM complicates things, the tank is why. The flying machines that matter live in the Armory.
