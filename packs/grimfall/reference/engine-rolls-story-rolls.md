**Story rolls.** For luck and off-screen fact — *is the terminal still powered? does the caravan have Rad-X?* — roll 1d (bad odds), 2d (even), or 3d (good). **Perfect:** ideal. **Messy:** yes, with a catch. **Grim:** it's a problem now. When a story roll stands in for an action, a messy or grim can prompt an impact move; thorns are rare, but allowed.
