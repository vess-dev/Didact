> **GANG CRUCIBLE.** *Every campaign re-skins the raiders weekly — roll or pick a color, a name-shape, and a signature cruelty, and the next gang is nothing like the last:* **Colors:** blood-red handprints — road-sign plate — bleached skulls on poles — neon war paint — chem-vial charms — burned-flag capes. **Names:** the [Animal]s — the [Place] Boys — the [Weapon] Saints — a one-word threat (*Rust, Famine, Wrath*) — a dead man's name — whatever the radio called them first. **Cruelty:** tolls taken in flesh — a trophy from every kill — chem-baptisms for recruits — fire as a signature — they always leave one witness — they never do.
