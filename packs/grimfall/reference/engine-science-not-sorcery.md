The wasteland's wonders run on super-science. Any single effect of it — a jolt from a shock baton, a chem's rush, a stunt of mad science — is a **device**, and a **potent device** is a potent version of the same. **Touchstones** rule everything: a *Shock Baton* shocks and stuns, it doesn't heal — and if a use feels like a reach, it's past the touchstone's edge (the GM can allow it at +1t, or veto it). Effects that carry no risk just work, and a riskless device doesn't count against the Inventor's session — unless it's potent, or it mends someone. A power pool, though, is rolled for every use, risky or not.
