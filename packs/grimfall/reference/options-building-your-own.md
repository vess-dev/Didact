A threat is a tier and a role (Chapter 7); a background is a name and three wises (Chapter 3). The rest need a recipe:

- **Classes.** Build an eighth class as a Core Perk with Growth (at levels 2, 4, and 6, or 3 and 6) plus seven signature perks, measured against Chapter 4. A **per-scene well** of 2–4 buys maneuvers: a critical bonus, a thorn ignored, a narrow potent feat. A **per-session well** of 2–3 buys story beats — a potent feat, an interrupt — and one that only buys off damage or small edges can run to 6. Add at most one always-on rider, and make sure the engine hands its player a decision every scene.
- **Perks.** A compendium-weight perk never grants standing potency outside a narrow domain and never raises a stat. Its pools run 2d–3d, its "always" covers only knowledge and vantage, and every strong trick is once per session, push-priced, or given time.
- **Arcana.** Pick a **function** (*attack — enhance — hinder — influence — protect — restore — reveal — summon — transform — traverse*) and a **form** (see the Schematic Crucible), name it, and give it **three touchstones** and **one Limit that bites** — a pool, a price, or a person who wants it back. Set its tier by Chapter 8's gate; expendables punch one tier up.
