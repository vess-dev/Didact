*every door opens for the right words — or the right price*

**ALSO:** Boss, Broker, Caravaneer, Smooth-Talker

In a world that's forgotten how to trust, you trade in it. You know who to flatter, who to threaten, who to pay, and who to put down. People follow you — or they wish they had.

> **Core Perk — GIFT OF GAB**
> *Growth: At levels 2, 4, and 6, gain +1 Sway per session.*
>
> Anything that can understand you will **always hear you out** once before it comes to blows — unless you struck first. Each session you have **3 Sway**, the currency of a voice people listen to. Spend 1, no roll needed, to: *raise an ally's die result by 1 (a 3 becomes a 4) — calm or provoke someone's vex — talk an ally down from a mark (clear it).*
>
> Or spend 1 **and roll Presence** to: *pull off a potent feat of social influence — interrupt an impact move that hinges on someone's decision (a finger on a trigger, a hand on the alarm) — talk an ally out of being rattled (clear it).* A potent feat takes an approach that shapes it: *charm — threaten — deceive — inspire — barter.* Sway works on others, never on yourself.

> **[Optional] WHAT YOU'RE KNOWN FOR.** *Set a reputation and how you feel about it, and track its spread with a campaign pool:* a name in one town — known across a region — a legend on the radio • for a deal you made — a deal you broke — a town you saved — a town you sold out — a body count — a silver tongue.
