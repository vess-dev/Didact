- **Perks** are the special advantages that set your survivor apart. Each one expands your *vantage* — what you can plausibly attempt.
- **Classes** are bundles of themed perks around a signature engine — nothing more. The class name adds nothing to your vantage on its own; your perks and backgrounds do. Every perk except the Core Perks is open to every survivor; the class you pick just decides your Core Perk and where your story starts.
- **The first perk listed is the Core Perk.** It's your class's engine, and it has **Growth** — it gets stronger as you level: "at levels 2, 4, and 6" or "at levels 3 and 6." Level 7 is the cap.
- **At creation,** take your Core Perk plus one other perk — from your class's list, another class's (never its Core Perk), or the Perk Compendium. You gain a new perk at each level.
- **Push yourself** always costs: after the perk resolves (and any roll it calls for), mark a stat of your choice. Spend those moments wisely.
- **Re-skin everything.** A toughness perk might be power armor, mutated hide, scar tissue, or pure stubbornness. A stealth perk might be a Stealth Boy, a ghoul's stillness, or light feet. Pick the fiction that fits *your* survivor and let the mechanics ride along.
