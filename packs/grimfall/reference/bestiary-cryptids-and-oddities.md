Appalachia and the deep folds of the map keep stranger things. Statted light — a tier, a role, and the one fact that matters. Give any of them a full card the night they matter.

| Creature | Tier · Role | The one fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Grafton Monster** | Elite · Brute | A ton of shoulder and no skull to shoot; can't be tripped, can't be scared. The **Alpha** puts targets through walls. |
| **Mothman** | Elite · Trickster | The **Wise** one can't be fought, only asked — one true, cryptic answer per campaign. The **Dark** strain hunts, and first sight hits the unready with vex. |
| **Sheepsquatch** | Tough · Brute | White fur, foul smell, one gear: straight ahead. Sidestep or be furniture. |
| **Blue Devil** | Tough · Predator | Horned, blue-hided, strictly nocturnal — daylight turns it from monster to rumor. |
| **Ogua** | Tough · Protector | Twenty feet of snapping turtle; withdraws into a shell nothing short of ordnance opens. Wait it out or walk away. |
| **Snallygaster** | Mook · Blaster | Feathered, wrong, and spitting acid that armor doesn't answer. |
| **Honey Beast** | Tough · Swarmer | A bear-shaped bee colony; killing it is a rich harvest and a very bad hour. |
| **Megasloth** | Tough · Brute | Slow as rust, impossible to shove, hide like sod roofing. Walk around it. |
| **Gulper** | Tough · Predator | A salamander that swallows people whole. Rescue is an escape scene, not a search. |
| **Angler** | Tough · Lurker | The bog light is the trap — look at the lantern and your feet start walking. |
| **Punga Creeper** | Mook · Lurker | Vines that hold you for whatever else is coming. The danger is never the vine. |
| **Tick** | Mook · Swarmer | Dog-sized, latches and swells; a gorged one bursts in an irradiated spray. |
| **Mole Miner** | Mook · Protector | Sealed hardsuits full of something no longer minerish; they defend the seams that ruined them. |
| **The Panther** | Tough · Lurker | A big cat bred as a vault experiment's control subject — utterly silent; you hear the keeper's rifle, never the paws. |
