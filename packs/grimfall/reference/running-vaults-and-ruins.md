Build a vault — or any delve — as a **5-point site**: the GM places the Reward first, then everyone spends exploration tokens on the rest (Chapter 6):


- **The Door** *(Entrance)* — getting in is its own story: the terminal, the password, the thing nesting in the gear room.
- **The Experiment** *(Puzzle)* — every vault was testing something; meet it as a problem that can't just be shot.
- **The Twist** *(Hitch)* — what two centuries did to the test. The betrayal, the illusion, the second door.
- **The Confrontation** *(Showdown)* — the fight or reckoning the vault has waited two hundred years to have.
- **What Vault-Tec Left** *(Reward)* — the haul, the truth, and usually a hook to the next site.

> **VAULT CRUCIBLE.** *Roll a thorn (d8) for what they were testing and what it became — or choose — then number the vault.* **Testing:** *isolation — obedience — propaganda — an FEV strain — cryo-sleep — surveillance — a rogue AI overseer — labor worship.* **It became:** *a tomb — a cult — a paradise with a price — a war between levels — something no longer human — a thriving market town — still on schedule, awaiting inspection — waiting for exactly you.*
