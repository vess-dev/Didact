- **Criticals:** more than one 6. Thorns can't cut a critical. Pick a bonus: *greater effect* (drop 1d from the related task pool before rolling it) — *secondary effect* (the effects of a related perfect, too) — *setup* (a benefit to a follow-up roll — usually an assist without risk). If nothing fits, take spark and keep moving.
- **Only roll when it's risky.** Competent survivors don't roll to climb a ladder. No risk, no roll — narrate it. If failure wouldn't be interesting, look at what success *costs* instead.
- **There are no partial successes.** A messy is a full success *and* a full problem. And the story never stalls: if a grim would grind everything to a halt, the GM can keep it moving — you get what you wanted, but in a way that makes things worse or goes sideways — and a roll that turns out to have no effect gives you new information or a setup instead.
- **There are no turns.** The spotlight moves like a camera, not a queue. Act when it makes sense; share the screen. (A few perks grant a **quick action**: a second action taken at once, before the spotlight moves or the world answers.)
