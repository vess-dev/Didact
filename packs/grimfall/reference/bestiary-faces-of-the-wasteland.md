The campaign chairs and vault doors of the old stories, statted light — a tier, a role, and the one fact. Most are conversations that can become fights; a few are fights pretending to be conversations.

| Face | Tier · Role | The one fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Colonel Autumn** | Elite · Tactician | Calm, principled, wrong; while he stands, his troopers' pool can't drop below 1d. Carries the engraved 10mm. |
| **Benny** | Tough · Trickster | All charm and checkered coat — shoots first exactly once, then talks or runs. Once per fight, dumb luck Counters the blow that would have finished him. |
| **Vulpes Inculta** | Elite · Trickster | Never where you look; every time he walks away, the Legion knows more. Kill him early or pay retail. |
| **Kellogg** | Elite · Marksman | A century of cybernetics and no apologies; even dropped, he finishes one last shot. His memories outlive him. |
| **Elder Maxson** | Elite · Tactician | Young, absolute, beloved; his broadcast voice keeps every knight's spine straight. Carries Final Judgment. |
| **Desdemona** | Tough · Tactician | Asks more than she orders; knows a code word that stops lesser synths mid-stride. |
| **DiMA** | Tough · Trickster | Would rather lose an argument than a life — and has memories he chose to forget in a drawer. |
| **Captain Avery** | Tough · Protector | The town's spine; fights like it when the fog comes. May not know what she is. |
| **Lord Ashur** | Boss · Tactician | Ex-Brotherhood in patchwork plate, holding a city up with a vision and a Gatling laser. His fail state is the cure in the crib. |
| **Overseer Barstow** | Elite · Tactician | Two centuries dead and still running the experiment; every protectron in Vault 88 answers her smile. |
| **Dean Domino** | Tough · Trickster | Two hundred years of grudge in a pressed tuxedo; betrays you exactly when it profits him. |
| **Christine** | Elite · Lurker | Circle of Steel, voice taken; from stealth her shots never announce themselves. An ally, if you earn it. |
| **Tobar the Ferryman** | Tough · Trickster | Cheerful, helpful, handy with a bonesaw — and his moves come at high stakes against anyone already hurt. Check your scalp. |
| **Desmond Lockheart** | Elite · Tactician | A pre-war intelligence war still running on two legs; once per fight, he Counters a success out of sheer spite. |
| **Professor Calvert** | Elite · Trickster | A mesmerist bell-jar mind that turns friends on each other — one clean crack in the glass ends everything. |
| **Salt-Upon-Wounds** | Elite · Brute | While his piston fist swings, his warband's pool can't drop below 1d — and it routs the beat he falls. |
| **Dog / God** | Elite · Lurker | One body, two selves; at the fight's turning point the other one answers. Talk to whichever will listen. |
| **The Mechanist** | Elite · Tactician | Every robot in earshot obeys the chair; unseat her and there's just a person who wanted to help. |
| **Overboss Colter** | Elite · Brute | Power armor fed by the arena's grid; the crowd — and the plumbing — are how you beat him. |
| **The Vault Keepers** | Mook–Tough · Protector | Foremen, tinkers, comedians, assistants — two centuries at their posts. Usually a conversation, always a story. |
