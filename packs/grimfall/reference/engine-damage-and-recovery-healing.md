**Healing.** Treatment takes time, tools, and know-how — a doctor's table, a med-kit, steady hands. It nearly always works; the drama is what it costs. Marks are too light to bother treating — they clear themselves. **Harm** clears with treatment, and conditions clear when the fiction says so (treatment, time, or a task pool). Any real rest — a night's camp, a quiet week on the road — clears all marks and harm, and a night **somewhere safe** (behind walls, in a secured camp) also refills stimpaks. In the field, **stimpaks** are the shortcut: every survivor carries a *3d Stimpaks* pool that clears one harm with a breather (Chapter 6).
