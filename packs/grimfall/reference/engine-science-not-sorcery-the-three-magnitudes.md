**The three magnitudes.** A **device** does what one skilled person could do, a new way. A **potent device** bends the rules — group-scale effects, lasting effects, the impossible made merely difficult. A **ritual** is an engineering feat that changes the map: reboot the reactor, launch the rocket, wake the AI, cure the plague. Rituals take three things — a **source** (a schematic, a lab, a mind that can hold the math), **anchors** (two or three concrete requirements the GM and table set: the original blueprints, a fusion core, somebody who was there), and the **invocation**: a task pool worked down over time, usually under fire. Missing anchors make the pool bigger or the whole thing impossible.
