*Dusk. A deathclaw has the caravan pinned behind a dead hauler, and the Ace wants the flare gun in the cab.*

- **Goal and approach.** *"I slide under the trailer and go for the cab — quiet."* Risky, so it's an action roll. The GM calls **Agility** and adds **1 thorn** — the ground is all shale and broken glass. Agility 2, plus 1 spark spent: she rolls **3d + 1t**.
- **The dice: 6, 4, 2 — thorn: 7.** The 6 would be a perfect, but the thorn **cuts** it to a **messy**: she's in the cab, flare gun in hand — *and* the GM makes an impact move. The deathclaw's head snaps around: a *4d The Deathclaw Closes* timer hits the table and rolls at once. Two dice drop. Short fuse now.


- **The Sledge steps up.** *"I put my shoulder into the trailer and shove it into its path."* Nobody else at the table could budge a loaded trailer — so he spends **1 Might**, and the potent feat makes it **reasonable, no thorns**: 3d for Brawn 3 comes up 5, 3, 1 — **messy**. Steel screeches across the deathclaw's line, and instead of striking back now, the GM quietly **banks suspense**. The timer holds at 2d.


- **The flare.** She fires it into the dusk — a **2d story roll** for whether the caravan's hired guns are watching (even odds): a 6, **perfect**. Headlights swing around the ridge.
- **The spend.** The GM spends the banked suspense: the deathclaw vaults the trailer at the Ace — an impact move she didn't invite, so she gets a **defense roll**, Agility again. The Sledge hurls himself in to **assist** (1d, sharing the risk): her 3 and 2, his 4 — **messy**, so the GM lessens it and lets it land on him: a claw-tip **marks Brawn** instead of opening her up. He's laughing about it. He won't be next time.


*That's the whole engine: goal and approach, a stat, thorns for the ugly parts, pools for everything with a fuse, and suspense deciding when the wasteland swings back.*
