Seven classes, one for each letter of **S.P.E.C.I.A.L.** Each is a **Core Perk** — an engine that grows as you level — with seven signature perks gathered around it. The Core Perk is yours alone; **every other perk in this book is open to every survivor.** The lists are neighborhoods, not walls.

| Class | Engine | At the table, you're… | A first perk to try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** · The Sledge | *Might*, fresh every scene | the wrecking ball — you move what shouldn't move, and what you hit stays hit | *Strong Back* |
| **P** · The Pathfinder | a quarry and its *Weak Point die* | the eyes — never blindsided, and the whole crew shoots straighter at what you've read | *Concentrated Fire* |
| **E** · The Everlast | *Grit*, spare marks to burn | the wall — you take the hit, dig deep, and answer back | *Stonewall* |
| **C** · The Charmer | *Sway*, a voice people heed | the voice — you talk first, steady your friends, and turn the room | *Inspirational* |
| **I** · The Inventor | *schematics*, and the *devices* you rig from them | the toolbox — a gadget for every problem, built from the wreckage | *Hacker* |
| **A** · The Ace | *tag skills* and *Cool* | the professional — in, out, and first on the draw | *Quick Draw* |
| **L** · The Longshot | *Luck dice*, rolled in advance | the wildcard — you rewrite the dice, yours and everyone else's | *Mysterious Stranger* |

Pick the class whose engine you want to run every session; pick perks for the survivor you want to be. A Sledge with *Hacker* is still a Sledge — just one who reads the manual before throwing it.
