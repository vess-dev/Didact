**Downtime** is a long break — a month in a settlement, a season behind walls. Per-session perks refresh, and each survivor picks a downtime action — only those who choose to **Rest** shake their lingering conditions (Chapter 6). Meanwhile the GM rolls the faction pools, then picks one and runs it dry. The world moves while you sleep.
