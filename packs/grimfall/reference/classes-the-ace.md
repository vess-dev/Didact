*gone before the dust settles*

**ALSO:** Ghost, Gunslinger, Creeper, Quickdraw

Quick hands, quick feet, quiet steps. You're past the guards and gone before the alarm sounds — or you've put three rounds downrange before anyone else clears leather. You always did keep an ace up your sleeve.

> **Core Perk — THE PROFESSIONAL**
> *Growth: At levels 3 and 6, gain +1 Cool per session and tag +1 skill.*
>
> **Tag three skills** as your trade; take **+1d** when using them.
> **Clean hands:** *blend in* (keep a low profile) — *charm* (persuade with personality) — *investigate* (study, observe, connect the dots) — *negotiate* (bargain and settle terms) — *network* (work contacts and favors) — *parkour* (run, climb, leap).
> **Dirty hands:** *ambush* (plan and strike unseen) — *deception* (lie, disguise, act) — *dismantle* (pick locks, sabotage, disable) — *provoke* (needle, incite, manipulate) — *sleight of hand* (lift, palm, plant) — *stealth* (hide, move unseen).
>
> Each session you have **2 Cool** — nerve under pressure, the plan B in your boot. Spend 1 to pull off a **potent feat within your tagged skills**: the lock that opens like it loves you, the lie that holds up under a spotlight, the vanishing act in an empty hallway.

> **[Optional] RAP SHEET.** *Set a past job, how it ended, and how you feel about it:* *a heist — a hit — a long con — a break-in — a smuggling run — a vault you cracked* • *clean and uncaught — there's a bounty — you took the fall — you left someone behind — they think you're dead* • *I'd do it again — I was set up — never again — I completely forgot I did that.* **CALLING CARD.** *What you leave behind:* an ace of spades — one unspent round, standing on end — a cigarette, still burning — a polite note — the lock, carefully re-locked — nothing; that's the card.
