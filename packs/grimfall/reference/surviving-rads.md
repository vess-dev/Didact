Radiation is **set dressing** until the fiction says otherwise — a crackling Pip-Boy, a warm green glow behind a blast door. When exposure matters, it lands with the usual tools:

- **A hot zone** inflicts **+1t** while you work in it, or a **mark** as you push through (GM's call). A mark from radiation is a **rad mark**, and it lingers like a condition: +1t on every roll with that stat until it clears — and it doesn't clear when you roll the stat, only with RadAway, a real rest, or a perk that sheds it. Mark that stat again and you take harm as usual.
- **A bad dose** is a condition: *rad-sick* (short-term — queasy, weak) or *rad-poisoned* (long-term, until properly treated).
- **A lethal dose** — drinking glowing water, hugging a warhead — is an **urgent condition**: a *4d Lethal Dose* (a warhead's embrace is *2d*), rolled as time passes. At 0d you're dropped; get treatment before it runs dry.
- **Rad-X** is a buff: for a scene, treat radiation hazards as set dressing.
- **RadAway** is treatment: with a breather, clear your rad marks, or treat a rad condition (a lethal one still takes a roll).
- The Everlast's **Lead Belly** lightens all of this a step, **Ghoulish** turns it into food, and Grit simply eats a rad mark like any other.
