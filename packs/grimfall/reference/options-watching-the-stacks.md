A survivor gains only about seven perks by level 7, so a stack that looks broken usually belongs to someone who poured every slot into one trick. A few combinations are worth a GM's eye:

- **Grit and its friends.** *Life Giver* turns most bloodied results into Flesh Wounds, and *Plating* eats physical harm before Grit ever has to — an Everlast in power armor is genuinely hard to hurt. Keep the frame a liability (breach it, EMP it, drain its core, strip it from a captive), and remember that harm costs Grit double: two solid hits empty most of the well.
- **Free pushes.** *Dig deep* makes every push-priced perk cheaper — that's the Everlast's whole trade, and it's working as intended. Grit spent pushing isn't there when the deathclaw lands.
- **Fate's thumb on the scale** — Luck dice, the *Mysterious Stranger*, *Serendipity*, *Dead Man's Burden*, the Remnants frame. If a Longshot seems to shrug off every consequence, cap fate at **one intervention per roll**: a swapped die, a reroll, *or* a helping hand, never two.
- **Adrenaline + Rooted** makes for all-in berserker turns; the 4s-count-as-1s clause keeps it honest.
- **Quick actions** — Blitz, Action Boy / Action Girl, Atomic!, the GRX gland, Grim Reaper's Sprint, the Quantum X-01's vent. However many sources a survivor stacks, they get **one quick action per beat**.
- **Never blindsided.** The Pathfinder's *Keen eye* guarantees a hint, not a reveal: a hint is one shot at intuition, and a wrong guess still springs the trap. Strike the survivors who aren't walking point.
- **Stacked dice.** Dice of a kind don't stack (Chapter 4), but one perk die, a setup, a chem, spark, an assist, and the Weak Point die all can — a crew that lines all of that up on one roll has earned its critical. Answer with stakes, not with thorns.
- **Free mark-clearing** — the Everlast's food-and-rest perks and their arcana cousins. If pushing yourself stops hurting at your table, cap free clears at **one mark per survivor per scene** (a perk that says otherwise, like *Fast Metabolism*, keeps its text).
