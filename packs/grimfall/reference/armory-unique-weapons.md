Named guns and blades are arcana: the name is the touchstone, the history is the permission. One of each, ever — and a few of these are the same gun haunted by different ghosts. A region only needs one of each legend, so shelve the twins a map away. Ordinary guns are just vantage and set dressing; an arcana is the one people tell stories about.
