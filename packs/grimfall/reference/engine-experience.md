**Sessions start warm.** Before each session, look back at the last one together: every player shares their survivor's best moment and takes **spark** for it, thread and per-session perks reset, and the GM takes **suspense** and stitches the moments into the *"Previously, on…"* that opens the night.

After each session, take 1 XP; the GM adds a bonus XP for a standout one (about one session in four). Level up when your XP boxes equal the next level, then clear them; for a slower road, slash each box before filling it. Each level: **a new perk**, and your Core Perk's **Growth** triggers at the listed levels. Cap is **level 7**.
