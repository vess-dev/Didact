*Also in the crates* are the purely priceless — a **Mass Grave Ledger**, the **Vault 69 Archive**, a **G.E.C.K. Bay Schematic**, pre-war **champagne**, a **luxury watch**, sealed **animal and disease samples**, carved **gallery figures**, one family **photograph**, a **Sierra Madre gold bar** (thirty-five pounds of pre-war certainty, heavy as guilt; the vault holds 37, and walking out with all of them is a eulogy — while the Madre's **chips** still feed its vending machines, forever), a **Snow Globe** (twenty caps to anyone; two thousand from the one collector chasing all seven, and the set unlocks a sealed suite), a **Sealed Nuka-Cola Crate** (24 pristine bottles, one glowing blue, and the crate itself buys a favor from the preservation society — there is always a preservation society). They're treasure, not arcana: caps, leverage, and grief by the pound. Price them as hauls (Chapter 6) — a few hundred caps is a minor haul, a few thousand a major one — and let the right buyer make them a story.
