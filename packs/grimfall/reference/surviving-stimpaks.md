Every survivor carries a *3d Stimpaks* resource pool. Given a breather — a moment in cover to catch your breath — jab one into yourself or an ally: clear **one harm**, then roll the pool. It refills after a night somewhere safe or in downtime — or sooner, if you buy a restock (a Caps roll) or loot a stash. A robot's *3d Repair Kit* works the same way. Mid-firefight there's no breather: treating a wound under fire is a risky action roll, and the Inventor's *Field Surgeon* is built for it. Want a meaner or kinder wasteland? Turn the dial in Chapter 10.
