One survivor and a GM (or none): make the party's missing muscle into the world's help. Give a lone PC a **companion** (build one with tricks and flaws, per the Charmer's *Inspirational*), lean harder on montage rolls, and let story rolls answer what a second player would have. Playing truly GM-less, use the crucibles as your oracle: frame the scene, roll the GM crucible for what pushes back, roll story dice for everything uncertain, and spend suspense against yourself honestly — the wasteland doesn't pull punches just because nobody's holding them. Two guardrails keep it honest: on a messy, make an impact move only if you're in a challenge, in a quest's last task pool, or holding 3+ suspense (otherwise take suspense); on a disaster or at dire stakes, take the usual consequences and roll your quest's peril pool too (see Quests, below).

**Side characters.** Want a whole crew without the bookkeeping? Run two or three companions as side characters: no class and no Core Perk, just one perk; stats as usual (1 in each, plus 4 points); backgrounds, but no tracked wises; one trait they are and one they're not, one thing they want and one they don't; and a bond with your survivor only. They don't earn XP, spark, or thread, and they never take marks — anything that would mark them, a push included, is harm instead.
