*the old world left its tools; you make them sing*

**ALSO:** Tinker, Mechanic, Sawbones, Egghead

Pre-war tech, busted robots, raw chems, a sucking chest wound — it's all just a puzzle. While everyone else swings and shoots, you out-think the problem and hand them the solution (or the explosion).

> **Core Perk — SCIENCE!**
> *Growth: At levels 2, 4, and 6, gain +1 device and +1 potent device per session, and learn 1 new schematic.*
>
> You know **4 schematics** (build them with the Schematic Crucible below), kept in your Pip-Boy or a grease-stained notebook. Each session you can rig **4 devices** and **2 potent devices** from parts on hand — jury-rigged weapons, hacks, field medicine, breaching charges, deployable turrets. You roll **Wits**, and the schematic you rig from is the touchstone (Chapter 2, *Science, Not Sorcery*). A device rigged **without risk** needs no roll and doesn't count against the session — unless it's potent, or it mends someone. You learn new schematics by studying salvaged tech or another tinker's notes (this consumes the source).

> **[Optional] SCHEMATIC CRUCIBLE.** *Roll a thorn (d8) for a function and a form, take either word — or just choose — then name it.* **Function:** *shock · burn — cryo · pulse — sonic · smoke — sense · hack — heal · shield — stealth · magnet — drill · launch — jam · lure.* **Form:** *baton · gauntlet — mine · trap — grenade · launcher — turret · drone — rig · harness — injector · dart — beacon · emitter — collar · field.* *(Shock Baton, Pulse Mine, Lure Beacon, Hack Rig.)*
> **CHEM CRUCIBLE.** *Roll a thorn (d8) for an effect and a base — or choose.* **Effect:** *rage — focus — numb — speed — steady — mend — burn — calm.* **Base:** *-jet — -tonic — -shine — -dust — -salve — -mist — -bomb — -draught.* *(Ragejet, Mendsalve, Speeddust.)*
