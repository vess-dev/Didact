**Story rolls & the GM crucible.** Roll to disclaim any decision you'd rather the dice make — 1d bad odds, 2d even, 3d good. When you need the *seed* of an answer, roll a thorn (d8) for each column, take a word from each row you land on, and smash them together:

| d8 | First word | Second word |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | rusted · collapsing | beacon · shortcut |
| 2 | glowing · coded | debt · broadcast |
| 3 | buried · stolen | convoy · water |
| 4 | patient · faithful | machine · turret |
| 5 | hungry · forgotten | signal · bunker |
| 6 | pre-war · desperate | grudge · trade |
| 7 | poisoned · marked | well · door |
| 8 | smiling · singing | uniform · wire |

*What's in the ruin?* — rusted + beacon: a pre-war distress tower, still calling. *Why is the town silent?* — smiling + uniform: someone official arrived first.
