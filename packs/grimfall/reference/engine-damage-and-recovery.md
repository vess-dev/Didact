- **Marks** — light damage to a stat: +1t on your next roll with it, then it clears (rad marks linger — Chapter 6). Marking an already-marked stat inflicts harm instead, and pushing yourself costs a mark. A mark that gets lessened (by a messy defense, say) becomes set dressing.
- **Harm** — the serious ladder:
  - **Bloodied** — pressing physical damage (or stacked marks to Brawn or Agility): +1t on physical rolls.
  - **Rattled** — pressing mental damage (or stacked marks to Wits or Presence): +1t on mental rolls.
  - **Desperate** — a second bloodied, or a second rattled, puts you here: +1t on *everything*. You're running on fumes.
  - **Dropped** — harm that would make you desperate a second time takes you out of the scene: once you're desperate, a bloodied or rattled you already carry drops you (one you don't carry yet just fills its box). Make a story roll (usually 2d): perfect, you're just out until the scene ends; messy, it costs you (a condition — *4d Bleeding Out*, a broken arm, trauma); grim, it's dire — a permanent condition, or the end of your story: **sacrifice** (one last automatic action, then the credits), **retirement** (bow out soon — if you're dropped again first, you die), or another end you choose. Long-term and permanent changes to your survivor — including death — are always yours to call.
- **Vex** — a flash of hot emotion: *fight — flight — freeze — freak out.* You pick how it plays; it lasts a beat or two.
- **Conditions** — specific maladies: **short-term** (*winded — clears after the scene*), **urgent** (a timer to worse: *4d Lethal Dose → dropped*), **long-term** (*Hooked: Jet — needs real treatment*), **permanent** (*lost eye*). +1t when one specifically hinders you; some close doors entirely.
