Everyone feeds the shared picture — say what it looks like, before and after the dice. Use the camera: zoom in on the hands picking the lock, cut away to the raiders taking position. Do what your survivor would do and let the GM map it to the rules; don't lawyer your way into better rolls. There's no turn order, only the **spotlight** — grab it when you have something, pass it when you've held it, open doors for each other and step through the ones opened for you. Failure isn't losing; it's the next scene. Keep secrets from characters, never from players — the table watching the Institute's trap close around your friend *is* the fun.
