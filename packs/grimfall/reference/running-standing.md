The wasteland keeps score. For each faction that matters, write one word for how it sees the party — or one survivor, if they've earned a name of their own. Standing is vantage: it sets the thorns on every dealing with that faction, and what's certain, doubtful, or impossible to ask of them.

| Standing | What it buys |
|---|---|
| **Idolized** | Doors open and reasonable favors are certain. When they hear you're in trouble, they send help. |
| **Accepted** | Fair dealing and fair prices; you're let inside the wire. |
| **Neutral** | The default. They don't know you from a radroach. |
| **Shunned** | Prices double and eyes follow you: +1t on every dealing. |
| **Vilified** | Shot on sight, and somebody's paying: start a *6d They Find You* campaign pool. |

Standing moves a step when you do something that faction will tell stories about — and the same deed usually moves two factions in opposite directions. Out of every faction's earshot, it's just **karma**: what the radio says about you decides whether Talon Company or the Regulators hold your warrant (*Bounty Crews*, Chapter 7).
