One of each. Armor here is vantage, permissions, and a story — the plate keeps you alive, the name makes you known. The heavy suits carry an *Armor* pool: after a physical consequence lands, roll it to lessen the consequence, as a messy defense would. Power armor rides the Sledge's *Power Armor Training*: each unique suit below is that perk's *3d Plating* pool (unless the card says otherwise) plus the listed rider. Untrained, a suit is a loud half-ton liability — you can walk it in a straight line, but delicate work eats thorns, and the Plating pool answers only to hands that know the frame.
