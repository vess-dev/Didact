title: "Settlements Worth Saving"
tags: [gm]
source: running-the-wasteland/settlements-worth-saving
mechanics: [stockpile, community]
---
When the party plants a flag — a fortified diner, a rebuilt water tower — it becomes a **community**, and it grows on a **Stockpile** pool. Trade hauls into it (a minor haul adds +2d, a major +4d, a mythic +8d); when time passes, roll it, and every die that drops pays toward the next **asset**: *minor, 4* (a wall, a clinic, a bar with a name) or *major, 8* (a working reactor, a caravan company, a radio mast that reaches the whole region). Give every asset a face — the barkeep, the militia sergeant, the DJ — and the raiders something to regret. When raiders come, play it out, but don't erase the players' work: damage to an asset costs a die or two of stockpile to fix.
