**The eight moves:**

- **Build Up** *(suspense move)* — a vignette before the storm; every PC adds a detail and takes spark, you take suspense. *The horde's dust cloud on the horizon. How do you spend the last hour?*
- **Clue In** *(suspense move)* — cut away to show the players what their characters can't see; take suspense. *We see the Legion scout snap his spyglass shut and ride east.*
- **Foreshadow** *(suspense move)* — telegraph danger for free; if it goes unaddressed, it lands. *The floor tiles here are strangely clean. A red dot wanders across your chest.*
- **Resolve** *(suspense move)* — hand the party a way out and take suspense. *The Protectron mistakes you for its shift supervisor and waves you all through.*
- **Complicate Things** *(impact move)* — add or worsen a threat. *The alarm starts a 4d Reinforcements pool. The dry brush catches fire.*
- **Counter** *(impact move)* — stop a success or a permission cold; save it for exceptional foes (this is what beats an "always"), and never use it on a defense roll or a critical. A countered perk usually refunds its cost (your call), but it can't be used again right away. *The courser's stealth field eats your perfect shot.*
- **Hit Hard** *(impact move)* — marks, harm, vex, conditions. *The mine goes off — bloodied, and your ears ring.*
- **Lock Down** *(impact move)* — close a door forever; force a stark choice. *The vault seals. The chopper leaves. Him or the chip — pick.*
