Before anyone writes a name, decide together what your crew *is*. Choose **2 concepts** that fit your party and **1 that definitely doesn't**:

*Caravanners — Couriers — Do-Gooders — Mercs — Prospectors — Raiders — Regulators — Scavvers — Settlers — Tribe — Vault Expedition — Wanderers*

Then pick a **group arc** — the next stretch of road, not the destination: *get the water flowing again — find the one who sold us out — carve a safe route through the Divide — make the town too tough to raid.*
