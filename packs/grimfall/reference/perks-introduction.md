Chapter 4 holds each class's seven signature perks; this chapter holds the rest, filed under the class each fits best. Take any of them at creation or level-up exactly like a Chapter 4 perk — every one is open to every class.

**Reading the compendium.** These are smaller perks than the signature seven: a narrow edge, a standing permission, one good trick a session. Perks tagged **repeatable** can be taken again, each take adding what the entry says. Enemies never roll, so a perk that fights for you changes *your* dice, the fiction, or a pool — never an enemy's odds. **Perk dice don't stack** (Chapter 4): a compendium +1d never adds to another perk's +1d on the same roll, a Core Perk's included — pick perks that reach new ground. Where a compendium perk overlaps a signature perk, the signature version is the stronger.
