- **Chapter 2, The Core Engine** — the rules for nightly play: rolls, pools, harm, and healing.
- **Chapter 3, Building a Survivor** — the party, backgrounds, stats, and creation step by step.
- **Chapter 4, The Classes** — seven classes, one for each letter of S.P.E.C.I.A.L., each a Core Perk engine with seven signature perks.
- **Chapter 5, The Perk Compendium** — over two hundred more perks, open to every survivor.
- **Chapter 6, Surviving the Wasteland** — rads, chems, stimpaks, caps, the long road, and downtime.
- **Chapter 7, The Bestiary** — the wasteland's threats, ready to run.
- **Chapter 8, The Armory** — relics, named weapons, and unique armor.
- **Chapter 9, Running the Wasteland** — the GM's kit.
- **Chapter 10, Options & Dials** — optional rules to tune the wasteland to your table, and tools for building your own.
- **The Perk Index** — every perk in the book, alphabetical, with its page.
