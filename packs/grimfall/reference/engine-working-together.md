- **Assist:** Say how you help, roll 1d alongside their roll, and share the risk — the GM's move can hit everyone who leaned in.
- **Protect:** When an ally is about to eat the consequences of their own roll, throw yourself in front: a 1d defense roll. **Perfect,** you both avoid it; **messy,** the GM lightens it for you both or hits only you; **grim,** you both take it.
- **Teamwork:** For a group effort, one rolls and the others assist. The GM picks who — sometimes the best at it, sometimes the worst.
- **Setup:** A success that hands someone the upper hand improves their vantage — often fewer thorns — and gives **+1d**, for one follow-up or the rest of the scene, as the fiction says.
- **Outside help:** A turret you reprogrammed, a friendly settler with a rifle — the GM rolls 1d and folds it in.
- **Interrupt:** Only with a perk that says so. Roll to stop an impact move as it happens: on a perfect it's stopped, on a messy the GM banks the suspense instead, and on a grim the move lands anyway. Whatever the result, you now share the risk.
