Grimfall assumes the classic mix: grim survival on the surface, absurd comedy in the cracks, and old-world sadness underneath. Turn it grimmer or softer with the lethality dial in Chapter 10, or weirder with the **Wild Wasteland** dial in Chapter 9. Set it at session zero, like any flavor of the apocalypse.
