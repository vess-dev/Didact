Four stats, rated **1/poor to 3/great**. Brawn and Agility are physical; Wits and Presence are mental.

| Stat | Governs | On defense |
|---|---|---|
| **Brawn** | power, menace, toughness | you absorb it or overpower it |
| **Agility** | precision, athletics, reflexes | you dodge, dive, or vanish |
| **Wits** | smarts, trickery, reading a situation | you saw it coming |
| **Presence** | influence, willpower, reading people | you keep your nerve |

Stats rarely change. No perk raises one, and a survivor gets at most **one** permanent bump in a lifetime — from a rare relic or the stat progression option (Chapter 10), whatever the source.
