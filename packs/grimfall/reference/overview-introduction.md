*The world ended in fire. The wasteland is what grew back.*

Grimfall is a tabletop roleplaying game about survivors in the irradiated bones of the old world. Deathclaws stalk the ruins. The dungeons are vaults. The miracles are chems, salvage, and dumb luck. Play is fast and conversational — say what you do, roll a handful of six-sided dice, keep the highest, and let d8 **thorns** complicate things — and every survivor is built from **perks** you can re-skin to fit anyone you want to play. The wasteland doesn't care how the world ended; it only cares whether you make it to morning.
