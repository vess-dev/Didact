*see it first, end it first*

**ALSO:** Deadeye, Scout, Spotter, Tracker

Nothing gets past you. You see the shot before you take it, the trap before you trip it, the ambush before it springs. The wasteland is drowning in details — tracks, glints, tells, tripwires — and you read every one.

> **Core Perk — AWARENESS**
> *Growth: At levels 3 and 6, study +1 more foe per session.*
>
> Once per session, when you get a good look at a foe — a creature, a machine, a person, a vehicle — you can **study** it and name its **weak point**: the GM tells you the one it has, or you invent one that fits (an invented one pays out through the die, and only the die). The gap in the power armor's seals. The knee the raider boss favors. That foe is your **quarry** for the rest of the session, and you have a **Weak Point die**, a special d6: whenever you or an ally researches, tracks, or strikes your quarry, add the die to the roll. When it lands a **6**, add a **critical bonus** — the weakness tears wider.
>
> *Keen eye:* You always get at least a **hint** on vigilance — hidden trouble never simply lands on you — and you take **+1d** when scouting, tracking, and defending against the land itself: sandstorms, rockfalls, the ground giving way.

> **[Optional] TELL-TALE WEAKNESS.** *When you name a quarry, describe the tell:* favors one side — flinches at light — sluggish in the cold — armor gap at the joint — reloads on a tic — fixates on one target — heavy, dragging tracks — panics at fire. **WHAT YOU WATCH THROUGH.** *Choose 1:* a cracked rifle scope — pre-war binoculars — one good eye — a Pip-Boy's amber grid — the reflection in a dead screen — nothing; you just look.
