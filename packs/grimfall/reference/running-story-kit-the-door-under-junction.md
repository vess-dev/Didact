*A first session for the Powderkeg. Canyon Junction's well is failing, and the only pre-war purifier for a hundred miles sits behind the vault door the town was built on.*

- **The hook, three ways.** The Council hires you to open the door before the water quits · the NCR lieutenant wants to know what's down there before the Legion does · a Jackal runner sells you a stolen keycard and a bad feeling.
- **Pressure.** *6d The Well Runs Dry* — roll it each dawn, and whenever water is wasted or fought over. *4d The Jackal Boys Come for the Tanks* — roll it whenever the town looks weak.
- **Faces.** **Mayor Odalys Reyes** wants the town to outlive her; she taps her ring on the table when she lies. **Lt. Cass Whitlow** wants one clean win to send up the line; she quotes the regulation she's about to break. **"Mother,"** the door's overseer program, wants a password nobody alive remembers; it answers every question with a satisfaction survey. **Splint,** the Jackals' chem cook, wants out — and knows where the vault's vents surface.
- **The site.** *The Door:* **6d Crack Vault 113** — trait: two centuries of corrosion; moves: Lockout Protocol, Wake the Turrets. *The Experiment:* rationing — every chamber dispenses exactly enough for one fewer person than is standing in it. *The Twist:* the dwellers never died; they sleep in shifts, and the next shift is due. *The Confrontation:* the Overseer's deputy (elite · tactician) and a *4d Security Detail* who believe it's still 2077. *What Vault-Tec Left:* a working purifier core, a major haul — and three hundred sleepers who will need the water too.
- **Fail state.** The well hits 0d: the town turns on itself, and the Garrison declares martial law.
- **The question.** Who gets the water?
