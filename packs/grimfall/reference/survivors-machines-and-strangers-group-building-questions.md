> **[Optional] GROUP-BUILDING QUESTIONS.** *At session zero or around a campfire, ask the player to your left one of these:* What did we scav together that we never told the others about? — When did I save you, and what did it cost me? — What's a rumor about me you half believe? — What did I do in that vault that still embarrasses me? — Which rule of yours have you watched me break? — Whose grave did we dig together? — What did you see me do for a stranger when I thought nobody was looking? — What do you think the wasteland will turn me into, and does it scare you?
