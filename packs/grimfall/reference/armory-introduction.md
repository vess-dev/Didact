These are **arcana** — singular relics of the old world, rated by tier and sorted into three arsenals: **Relics**, **Unique Weapons**, and **Unique Armors**, each broken down into **Minor**, **Major**, and **Mythic**. The name and description are always the touchstones; where a card lists no pool, its permission simply holds. Pools refresh each session unless the card names a **unique reset** — rare ammunition, a price, an act. Single-use finds are **expendable** — marked **×1** — and punch one tier above their weight.

**The tier gate.** Read every card against its badge:


- A **minor** item is a narrow edge: set-dressing vantage, a thorn it ignores, **+1d** in a narrow circumstance, a *2d* pool for a device-level trick, or a once-per-session trick. Minors are never potent.
- A **major** item is a broad edge — +1d whenever it's the right tool, or permissions nobody else has — plus an engine: a **once-per-session potent** use, a *4d* **power pool** for its signature trick, or both. A weapon that's potent on *every* use is mythic-grade, and sits at major only under a harsh limit: heavy ordnance carries a small pool with a unique reset (the ammunition *is* the pool, and it doesn't come back on its own), and a few charge a real price per shot. Nothing refills a unique-reset pool except its own named reset.
- A **mythic** item answers to no cap but the table's judgment: ritual-level deeds and any number of potent tricks, its pools going potent by dropping 1d. It counts as its own ritual anchors; where a card lists what it **needs**, those are the machine's requirements, not a ritual's.
- An **expendable** punches one tier up: a minor ×1 gives one potent effect; a major ×1, one ritual-level effect; a mythic ×1 is a plot device. (A chem-grade one-shot — a single device-level effect — is *trivial*, and belongs with the chems in Chapter 6.)

**Placement is the real rule.** A relic is a story the party can hold — hand out one per session at most, give the strong ones a drawback or an old owner, and remember that a named relic is **one of a kind** (expendables are finds; a crate of them is a haul). When a named weapon changes hands, the region hears about it within the week.
