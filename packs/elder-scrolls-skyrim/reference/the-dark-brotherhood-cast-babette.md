### Babette

6 HP, 14 STR, 16 DEX, 15 WIL, vampiric bite (d8), thrown poison-vial (d6, _blast_)

- A vampire (immune to poison and disease; fire against her is _Enhanced_; sunlight leaves her Deprived): three hundred years old, wearing the body of a small girl — the Sanctuary's master alchemist, gentle in manner and utterly without mercy. Disarming and ancient.
- **Poisoner:** her bite and her vials carry a lingering venom: the first time they wound a creature in a fight, it must pass a STR save or take 1d6 STR, and the venom keeps working for up to three rounds more (see Hazards). She brews the Brotherhood's poisons, and sells the party any of the Crafting chapter's poisons at cost.
