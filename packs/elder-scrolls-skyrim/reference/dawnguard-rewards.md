- **Auriel's Bow** — the sacred elven bow of the Forgotten Vale, claimed in Beat 7, with its **Sunhallowed** and **Bloodcursed** arrows: the very key to the Tyranny of the Sun. See Unique Weapons.
- **Auriel's Shield** — the sun-blessed shield of Auri-El's chantry, claimed in the Forgotten Vale; a companion prize to the Bow for a shield-bearer. See Unique Armors.
- **The Aetherial Crown, Aetherial Shield, or Aetherial Staff** — the prizes of the optional side-quest **Lost to the Ages** (the hunt for the four Aetherium shards and the **Aetherium Forge** at the Ruins of Bthalft; the bow **Zephyr** lies along the way, in Arkngthamz). Run it as a parallel Dwemer-ruin delve and let the party forge **one**: the Crown or the Shield (see Unique Armors), or the Staff (see Relics).
- **Serana, as a companion** — on **either** path, Serana may stay with the party for good after the climax (her stat block above, run as a Companion). At Serana's Trust 5 she gives the party a quiver of **Bloodcursed arrows** (3 uses), refilled from her own veins once per day.
- **Harkon's Sword** — taken from his body on either path. See Unique Weapons.
- **The branch payoffs:**
  - **Dawnguard track:** a suit of **Dawnguard armor** (mail, 2 Armor, _bulky_; or light, 1 Armor), a **Dawnguard crossbow** (d8, _bulky_; the order's signature weapon, refitted by Sorine — her exploding bolts come in 3-use quivers that deal their damage as a _blast_), and the standing of the order. Optionally the **Dawnguard Rune Axe** from Unique Weapons. Faction: **Dawnguard +2 (Sworn)**, **Volkihar −2 (Hunted)**.
  - **Volkihar track:** the **Vampire Lord** power itself (see Playing Unusual PCs, with all the costs of the curse) and a suit of **Vampire Royal Armor** (light armor, 1 Armor) cut for the court. Faction: **Volkihar +2 (Sworn)**, **Dawnguard / Vigil of Stendarr −2 (Hunted)**.
- **Renown:** ending the vampire menace publicly (Dawnguard) is worth a step of **Renown**; becoming a known Vampire Lord is a step of **Infamy** among the living, however the clan is left.
