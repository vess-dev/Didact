Most contract targets are ordinary folk who die to a single clean strike; the drama is in the getting there. The fights come from the Penitus Oculatus, the targets' guards, and the tombs. From the Bestiary: **Draugr** and **Skeleton** for Volunruud; **Bandit** stat lines for bodyguards, sellswords, and city guards; **Vampire** for Babette's own contracts.
