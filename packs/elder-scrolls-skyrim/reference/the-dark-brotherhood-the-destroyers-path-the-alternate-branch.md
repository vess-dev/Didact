Chosen at Beat 2 by killing **Astrid** instead of a captive. This is a short, cold module — not a campaign — and it ends the Brotherhood for good.

1. **The Shack.** The party cuts down Astrid where she stands. The captives may be freed or left; the Black Door behind her never opens for the party.
2. **The Report.** They tell a city guard — or ride straight to the **Penitus Oculatus Outpost at Dragon Bridge** — that they have slain the Brotherhood's leader.
3. **The Commission.** **Commander Maro** (use his block above, as an ally) is delighted. He sends the party alone to the **Falkreath Sanctuary** to finish what they started.
4. **The Massacre.** The party clears the Sanctuary themselves, room by room: **Nazir**, **Gabriella**, **Festus Krex**, **Veezara** (use Nazir's block), and **Arnbjorn** (a **Werewolf** from the Bestiary). (Babette, Cicero, and the Night Mother's coffin are not present — the corpse and her Keeper are elsewhere, and the office of Listener simply goes empty in Tamriel.) Use the family's stat blocks above, all hostile.
5. **The Payment.** Return to Maro for **3,000 Septims** and the Empire's quiet thanks.

**This closes the door.** No Listener, no Sanctuary base, no Shadowmere, no Windshear, no contracts — every join-path reward above is forfeit except the Blade of Woe, which can be lifted from Astrid's body in the shack. What the party earns instead is a clear conscience (or a clear purse) and the distinction of being the ones who ended the Dark Brotherhood in Skyrim. The Warden should let that finality land as heavily as the family's warmth would have.
