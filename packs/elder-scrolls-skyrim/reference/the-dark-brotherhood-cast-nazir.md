### Nazir

9 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 12 WIL, scimitar (d8), throwing knives (d4, _ranged_)

- The dry, unflappable Redguard who hands out the family's contracts and counts its coin. A survivor and a master of light-armored killing; one of the few left standing at the end.
- **Steady Hand:** once per combat, re-roll one of Nazir's failed saves.
