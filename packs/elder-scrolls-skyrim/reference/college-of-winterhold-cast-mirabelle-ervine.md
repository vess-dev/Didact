### Mirabelle Ervine — Master-Wizard, who actually runs the place

10 HP, 1 Armor, 10 STR, 12 DEX, 14 WIL, staff (d6), Frostbite (d6, _blast_)

- A brisk, unsentimental Breton who handles every door, key, ledger, and discipline problem so the Arch-Mage can think. She has no patience and no illusions, least of all about Ancano.
- The party's source of keys, tasks, and blunt truth. She hands over the **Torc of Labyrinthian** and the late Arch-Mage's amulet when the disaster breaks — and the cost of the Eye's first eruption is *her* (see Beat 5).
