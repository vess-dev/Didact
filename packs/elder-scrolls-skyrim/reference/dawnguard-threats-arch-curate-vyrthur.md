### Arch-Curate Vyrthur

14 HP, 1 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, frost magic (d8, _ranged_), drain (d8)

- Gelebor's brother, corrupted into a vampire and twisted by spite — the true author of the Tyranny of the Sun, who wrote a prophecy to spite the god he believes forsook him. The Vale's final foe.
- Immune to poison; fire against him is _Enhanced_; sunlight leaves him Deprived. Once per scene he raises a **shield of frost** (mundane attacks against him _Impaired_ for the round) and wakes the **Falmer** and **Chaurus** frozen in the chapel's ice, or conjures a **Frost Atronach**.
- **Critical Damage**: a lance of black ice freezes the target in place; they lose their next action.
