A war that has slept for an age is stirring. **Lord Harkon**, master of the Volkihar vampire clan, means to enact the **Tyranny of the Sun** — an old elven prophecy that, fulfilled, would blot the sun from the sky forever and free his kind to hunt the day. To do it he needs three things: the **Elder Scrolls** that hold the prophecy, the lost **Auriel's Bow**, and the blood of his own daughter, a **Daughter of Coldharbour**. Against him stands the **Dawnguard**, an order of vampire-hunters scraped back together by the grim zealot **Isran** in the ruin of **Fort Dawnguard**.

Into that gap walks the party. Sent to investigate vampire raids, they break open **Dimhollow Crypt** and free **Serana** — the ancient vampire who carries one of the Scrolls. From there the road forks hard, and the choice is the whole campaign: **ride with the Dawnguard and hunt Harkon down**, or **take Harkon's gift, become a Vampire Lord, and enact the prophecy yourself**. Either way the road runs the same haunted stations — the **Soul Cairn** where Serana's mother is exiled, the **Forgotten Vale** where the Bow is kept — and ends with one master of the night dead on a cathedral floor.
