Skyrim's future is the prize: an independent kingdom of Nords under Ulfric, or a province held inside a battered Empire under Tullius. Jarls will fall, cities will burn, and whichever banner wins, the losing half of Skyrim will remember who carried the other one.

**The hidden hand.** Neither army is the true winner. The **Thalmor** — the High Elf supremacists who forced the Concordat — publicly back the Empire and privately want the war to last as long and bleed as freely as possible. A long civil war keeps Men killing Men, weakens whoever wins, and leaves the Dominion stronger for the next war it actually means to fight. The Warden should let this surface slowly: a captured dispatch, a Thalmor "advisor" too pleased by a stalemate, an embassy dossier (see the Main Quest's *Diplomatic Immunity*) that names Ulfric himself as a Thalmor asset whose rebellion serves their ends whether he knows it or not. Victory for either banner is real — but the elves win something too, and a sharp party will notice.
