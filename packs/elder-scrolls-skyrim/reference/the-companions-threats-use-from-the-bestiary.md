### Use from the Bestiary

- **Giant** (the Arrival hook at Pelagia Farm).
- **Draugr**, **Draugr Overlord** (Dustman's Cairn).
- **Ghost** (the Companion ghosts of Ysgramor's Tomb).
- **Hagraven** (the Glenmoril Coven — the witches themselves).
- **Frostbite Spider** / **Giant Frostbite Spider** (Ysgramor's Tomb).
- **Werewolf** (caged captives loosed by the Silver Hand; NPC Circle wolves).
- **Bandit**, **Wolf** (radiant jobs and wilderness travel between nodes).
