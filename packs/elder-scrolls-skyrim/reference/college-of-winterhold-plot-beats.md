Eight beats. Beats 1–3 establish the College and can be reordered or stretched with side-jobs; Beats 4–8 are the escalating spine and should run in order. Branch points are called out where the players' choices bite.

1. **First Lessons.** At the bridge, the gate-keeper **Faralda** tests each would-be student: cast a spell, or talk your way past with a WIL save. Inside, **Mirabelle Ervine** gives a tour and starter robes; **Tolfdir** lectures in the Hall of the Elements and has the party raise a **Ward** against his (deliberately weak) firebolt. The party meets fellow apprentices — a shy Nord, a proud Dunmer noblewoman, an over-eager Khajiit — any of whom make good recurring ties. *Reward:* admission to the College (**College of Winterhold** becomes Trusted, +1, if it was not already); College robes (clothing, no Armor); a **Spell Tome: Lesser Ward** for the party's caster.

2. **Under Saarthal.** Tolfdir takes the party to the **Saarthal** dig. Recovering artifacts, they find the **Saarthal Amulet** — and the moment it is touched, a hall seals shut around a PC. Escape means wearing the amulet and loosing a spell at the weakened wall. Deeper in, the Psijic monk **Nerien** freezes time to warn them: *"You should not have come here. What you have found will be sought by powers you cannot imagine."* Past him, behind a barrier of light, hums the **Eye of Magnus**, guarded by the draugr champion **Jyrik Gauldurson** (see Threats). The party reports back; the Arch-Mage orders the Eye studied — and that is when **Ancano** starts paying very close attention.

3. **The Library and the Augur.** Two threads, run in either order, that turn "interesting artifact" into "looming catastrophe":
   - **Hitting the Books.** **Urag** reports that his books on Saarthal and the god Magnus were stolen by a washed-out apprentice, Orthorn, who fled to a necromancers' coven at **Fellglow Keep**. The party retrieves them from **the Caller** and her thralls — by force, trade, or persuasion.
   - **Good Intentions.** **Ancano** summons the party to "meet a visitor" — the Psijic **Quaranir**, who freezes time and tells them the Eye must be kept from *anyone's* use, and points them to the **Augur of Dunlain** in the Midden. The Augur delivers the campaign's thesis: the Eye cannot be contained. They need the **Staff of Magnus**.
   - *Reward:* each PC may take one **Skill Book** from the Arcanaeum's spares, with Urag's grudging thanks; a minor Relic from the Augur's chamber; the knowledge of what they must find.

4. **The Synod and the Oculory.** **Mirabelle** reports that the **Synod** — the Imperial mages' order, the College's rivals — have been asking after the Staff and mentioned **Mzulft**. The party travels south to the Dwemer ruin, fights through automatons and a **Falmer** warren, takes the Oculory's focusing crystal from a **Falmer Warmonger** in the deeps, and reaches the dying Synod expedition. With the surly survivor **Paratus**, they work the **Oculory**: set the crystal, play fire and frost across the device, and rotate its bands until it throws a map of Skyrim across the dome, pinpointing the Staff in **Labyrinthian**. *Branch:* help Paratus freely and the Synod owes the party a favor (a later ally, or a discount on tomes); rob or abandon him and the Synod becomes a hostile rival. *The turn:* they return to find disaster has arrived ahead of them — **Ancano** has bound himself to the Eye and sealed it, and himself, inside a barrier of force.

5. **The Eye Awakens.** The point of no return. **Savos Aren** confronts Ancano at the Eye and is killed by its unleashed power before the party's eyes. This is a fixed moment, not a winnable fight: a PC who throws themselves in is flung back for d6, and the Arch-Mage still dies. The Eye's energy lashes out, and **magic anomalies** spill from the Hall into the **town of Winterhold** below. With **Faralda** and the scholar **Arniel Gane**, the party must put them down before they kill the townsfolk — a running fight through the snow that buys the College time. *Branch — the price:* the eruption mortally wounds **Mirabelle Ervine**. By default she lives just long enough to hand the party the **Torc of Labyrinthian** (the key to the ruin) and **Savos Aren's Amulet**, then dies of her injuries while they are away. A merciful Warden may let quick Restoration save her at a cost (a Fatigue, a Scar, a spent Relic); a grim one lets her die at once. Either way, the party leaves for Labyrinthian with the Torc.

6. **Labyrinthian.** The party crosses Hjaalmarch to **Labyrinthian** and descends into the dragon cult's ancient seat. **Visions of a young Savos Aren** and his fellow apprentices replay the day they came here and only he walked out — context, atmosphere, and a quiet warning. The ruin is thick with **Skeletons** and **draugr**, and a magicka-draining curse hangs over the deep halls: every Spell Tome channeled here calls for the duress WIL save, in or out of combat. Partway down, a **Skeletal Dragon** bursts from a buried courtyard.

7. **Morokei.** At the ruin's heart, behind a shield of light fed by two enthralled wizards, waits the dragon priest **Morokei** — "Glorious," four thousand years dead and still draining the magicka of anyone who enters his sanctum (see Threats). *Branch — the Thalmor's last knife:* as the party takes the Staff, the Thalmor agent **Estormo** ambushes them — Ancano sent him to make sure the Staff never reaches the College. Beat him or talk him down; either way, they learn the Thalmor knew everything. *Reward:* the **Staff of Magnus** and the **Morokei** mask. The party returns to find **Tolfdir** holding the line at the College — and, by default, Mirabelle dead.

8. **The Eye of Magnus.** The party climbs back into the **Hall of the Elements**, where **Ancano** stands fused to the Eye behind its force-field, the magicka pouring off him. See **Climax**.
