The folk who keep the place are **hirelings** (Player's Guide → Core Rules → Hirelings; see also Companions): paid NPCs who take Morale saves and walk away when the coin or the welcome stops. They are not a PC's one active Companion. Hire them with Septims and a built home with room.

### The Steward

The single most useful hire. A trusted follower or a hired hand settles in as **Steward** and the steading **runs itself in the party's absence** — exactly the **Steward's House** function in Settlements: the Steward keeps up repairs and hires hands, and once per season a new building can be raised without a PC spending a Downtime Action (the Septims must still be paid). In the fiction the Steward also supplies materials and furnishings and buys in stock, so the party can spend Downtime adventuring instead of hauling logs.

- *Cost:* a one-time fee of **~500 Septims** (or a sworn Companion serving for free), plus a built main hall.
- *On the page:* fills one building slot as the **Steward's House** (Settlements).

### The Carriage Driver

A driver and cart stationed at the steading. Hire one (**~50 Septims** to engage, the Marketplace carriage fare, 5 Septims a seat, per ride thereafter) and the party can travel quickly between the manor, its capital, and the other Hold capitals between sessions — folding the long wilderness legs to and from a remote plot into the Downtime between sessions.

- *On the page:* a service, not a building. Pairs naturally with a **Stable & Paddock** (_petty_).

### The Housecarl

A manor that has grown enough — and a party named **Thane** of its Hold — is assigned a sworn **housecarl** by the Jarl: a warrior bound to guard the house and its lord. Each plot has its own (Lakeview → **Rayya**; Windstad → **Valdimar**; Heljarchen → **Gregor**).

A housecarl is a hireling-grade guard who may serve as one PC's active Companion if that player wishes:

#### Housecarl (sworn guard)

6 HP, 2 Armor, 13 STR, 12 DEX, 11 WIL, steel sword (d8) or steel battleaxe (d10)

- A Nord (or Redguard) warrior sworn to guard the manor and its lord; quarters in the hall and stands watch by night. Wears light armor and carries a shield (**2 Armor**; 1 when the shield is slung for the battleaxe).
- Loyal to the death while the lord lives; will not break Morale while defending the home. Fights among the defenders when a raid comes; to count toward **Defense**, give the housecarl a **Guard Post** or **Barracks** slot (Settlements).
- **Companion option:** if taken as the active Companion, use the standard Companion block (Loyalty starts at **Devoted (4)**, given the oath) and the **Command** action.

> **A Barracks (housecarls)** building slot in Settlements quarters a small standing guard and lets the steading field a defending **detachment without the party present** (+1 Defense). Build it once the manor is grand enough to need it.
