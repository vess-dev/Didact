Skyrim is a province at the breaking point, and the party arrives just as everything comes due:

- **The Civil War.** High King Torygg is dead, slain in a duel by **Ulfric Stormcloak**, who would tear Skyrim free of a crumbling Empire. The Imperial Legion under **General Tullius** holds the cities; the Stormcloaks hold the hearts of the old Nords. Every Hold leans one way or the other, and none can stay neutral for long.
- **The Talos Ban.** The Empire lost a ruinous war with the **Aldmeri Dominion** and signed the **White-Gold Concordat**, outlawing the worship of Talos — a Nord god, a man made divine. The **Thalmor** ride the roads enforcing it, and they have their own reasons to want Skyrim bled white.
- **The Dragon Crisis.** Worst of all, the dragons have returned — beasts out of legend, thought extinct for centuries — and at their head flies **Alduin** the World-Eater, who has come to end the world. The dead stir in their barrows. The Greybeards' summons echoes from the Throat of the World.

Into this walk the player characters: sellswords and mages, thieves and the Hold-born, strangers and natives, every one of them about to matter more than they planned.
