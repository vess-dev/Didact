*A **Dawnguard DLC** frame. Vampires are stealing folk from the roads, and a grim Redguard is rebuilding a fortress of vampire-hunters in a forgotten canyon. Crack open a Nordic crypt and you wake a four-thousand-year-old vampire who carries an Elder Scroll — and a secret her father would unmake the sun to keep. Before the arc is out you must choose: hunt the Volkihar with the Dawnguard, or drink Lord Harkon's blood and become one of the undying.*
