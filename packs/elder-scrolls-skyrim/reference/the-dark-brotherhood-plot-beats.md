Broad strokes; eight to ten sessions on the join-path. The pacing climbs from intimate murders to a national conspiracy, breaks in the middle with the betrayal, and resolves with the party rebuilding what was lost.

1. **The Black Sacrament.** In Windhelm, a grieving orphan boy, **Aventus Aretino**, has performed the forbidden rite — surrounded by candles, a flayed effigy, a dagger, and human remains — praying for the Brotherhood to come. He begs the party to kill the cruel orphanage matron **Grelod the Kind** in Riften. Whether they do it or not, the prayer has already been heard. (A grounded, low-stakes opener that establishes the ritual and the world's cruelty.)
2. **Abducted by Astrid.** The next time the party sleeps, they wake hooded in a derelict shack. **Astrid** is waiting. She tells them they've *stolen a kill* the Brotherhood was owed (Grelod's — or, if they let the matron live, some other death that drew her eye), and a life must be paid for a life. Three captives kneel bound and hooded before them. **THE BRANCH:** kill one to settle the debt and earn passage through the Black Door — or kill Astrid and take the other road. The Black Door's riddle is answered with *"Silence, my brother,"* and it swings open to the Sanctuary.
3. **The Falkreath Sanctuary.** The party descends into the cliffside warren in the pine forest and meets the family: **Nazir** the Redguard contract-broker, the child-shaped vampire **Babette**, the dark conjurer **Gabriella**, the venomous old mage **Festus Krex**, and Astrid's werewolf husband **Arnbjorn**. Each PC is issued a suit of the black-and-red **Shrouded Armor** from the Sanctuary's wardrobe and given their first work. The family also includes the quiet Argonian Shadowscale **Veezara**. The Sanctuary should feel like *home* — that is the trap.
4. **Contracts.** A run of small, varied jobs from Nazir teaches the toolkit of murder: poison, stealth, the staged accident, the open knife. Use these to explore the Holds and let the party develop a *style.* Somewhere in here the unhinged Keeper **Cicero** arrives, hauling a long black coffin and muttering to its occupant — the family is unsettled, Astrid most of all.
5. **The Night Mother Speaks.** Astrid, suspicious of Cicero, asks the party to hide inside the Night Mother's coffin and eavesdrop. Instead the corpse *speaks* — to the listener within. *"Darkness rises when silence dies."* She names the eavesdropper **Listener**, the long-empty office, and bids them seek a nobleman, **Amaund Motierre**, at the ruin of **Volunruud**. Astrid is shaken: power in the Sanctuary has just shifted out of her hands.
6. **The Grand Conspiracy.** At Volunruud, Amaund lays out the contract that will define the family: assassinate **Emperor Titus Mede II**, who is sailing to Skyrim. He hands over a sealed letter and a jeweled token (a noble's down-payment, appraised through a Thieves Guild fence). The plot is built one kill at a time — a wedding (the Emperor's cousin), the killing of **Commander Maro**'s son, framed as a traitor to break the commander of the **Penitus Oculatus**, and the murder of the famous chef the Gourmet to steal his identity and his writ of passage to the Emperor's table.
7. **Cicero's Madness.** Mid-conspiracy, Cicero snaps — he attacks Astrid and flees to the long-shut **Dawnstar Sanctuary**, dragging the family's loyalties apart. Astrid lends the party her undying black mare, **Shadowmere**, to run him down. In Dawnstar they find the wounded Arnbjorn and a cornered Keeper. **Spare or kill Cicero** — the choice colors how the family fractures, and a Listener who heeds the Night Mother stays the blade.
8. **The Betrayal.** The party returns from the false poisoning at Solitude to find the Falkreath Sanctuary aflame, the Black Door broken, and the **Penitus Oculatus** inside putting the family to the sword. Festus, Gabriella, Veezara, Arnbjorn die in the smoke. The party drags out the survivors and learns the unthinkable: **Astrid** sold them all to Commander Maro to save her own family — and it bought the family's destruction. They find her, dying and burned, on the altar of the wreck. She confesses, hands over the **Blade of Woe**, and begs for the mercy of a clean death. *"Hail Sithis."*
9. **The Katariah.** The poisoning was a decoy; the true Emperor is alive aboard his warship, the **Katariah**, anchored off Solitude. (The party may first settle accounts with **Commander Maro** at the docks.) They board her — over the side, through the hold, up through the guards — and reach Titus Mede II. He does not fight. He has known they were coming, and asks only that they consider who *bought* his death and whether that schemer should outlive him. The party makes the kill, escapes, and may close the loop on Amaund Motierre, who waits at the Bannered Mare in Whiterun, before collecting the sealed urn of 20,000 Septims from the Volunruud dead-drop.
10. **The Listener Rebuilds.** What is left of the family — Nazir, Babette, the Night Mother's coffin, perhaps a spared Cicero — gathers in the **Dawnstar Sanctuary**, its old Black Door reopened. The party, now **Listener**, holds the only working chapter of the Dark Brotherhood in Tamriel, a base of their own, and the prayers of the desperate humming in from across the province. The campaign ends not with a death but with a beginning: the family they chose to save, and the long red work ahead.
