*Mead-smoke and wet dog, sword-iron and brotherhood. The Companions are a found family with a shameful inheritance — the warmest faction in Skyrim and the one carrying the heaviest curse. Play the bond first: the sparring, the boasting, the shared cup. Then let the wolf in under it. Every beat should weigh kinship against the cost of the beast.*

**Touchstones:** the Companions questline itself; *Beowulf* and the mead-hall sagas; *The Wolfman*; the werewolf as gift-and-affliction; a brotherhood that closes ranks around a secret it can't speak.

**Lean into:** loyalty and shame in the same breath. The Circle love each other and lie to each other. Aela glories in the wolf; Kodlak grieves it; Skjor would spread it; the twins are caught between. There is no clean side — the Silver Hand are murderers, but they are not *wrong* that the Circle are monsters. Let the party feel both.

**HARD LINE — no Shouts for players.** The Companions are warriors, not Tongues. Word Walls the party finds teach nothing they can wield; describe the dragon-language as ancient noise. Lycanthropy here is the **Werewolf** of *Playing Unusual PCs* — a story transformation taken on by a willing rite, with its Edges and Costs — **not** a power that is "shouted." Keep it in the fiction.
