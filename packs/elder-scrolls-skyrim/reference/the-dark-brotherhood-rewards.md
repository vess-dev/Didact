The frames name the prizes; their rules live in the catalogs. Join-path only — the Destroyer's Path forecloses all of these but the Blade of Woe.

- **Blade of Woe** — Astrid's dagger, handed over in Beat 8 as she dies, or taken from her body. See **Unique Weapons**.
- **Shrouded Armor** — the Brotherhood's black-and-red garb, issued from the Sanctuary wardrobe in Beat 3. See **Unique Armors**.
- **Ancient Shrouded Armor** — the finer garb of the Brotherhood's golden age, found on a dead assassin in Hag's End on an optional contract (Olava's Token leads to it). See **Unique Armors**.
- **Shadowmere** — the Brotherhood's undying black mare, lent by Astrid in Beat 7 and kept thereafter. See **Mounts**.
- **Windshear** — the scimitar lashed to the *Katariah*'s bowsprit. See **Unique Weapons**.
- **20,000 Septims** — the contract's full payment, sealed in an urn at the Volunruud dead-drop after the *Katariah* (Beat 9).
- **Listener Standing & a Sanctuary base** — the campaign's true reward: the party leads the only working chapter of the Dark Brotherhood, holds the reopened **Dawnstar Sanctuary** as a home base, hears the Night Mother's contracts, and counts as **Sworn** with the Brotherhood (what the wider world makes of them is Renown's to track; see Clocks).
