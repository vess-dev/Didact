Pointcrawl the war across the Hold gazetteer (The Holds); these are the nodes that matter to the campaign.

- **Windhelm — Palace of the Kings** (Eastmarch). The black-stone hall of the rebellion; Ulfric and Galmar muster here. Outside it, the **Gray Quarter**, where Dunmer refugees live cramped and despised — the rebellion's conscience and its blind spot. The Stormcloak enlistment node and the Imperial campaign's final battlefield.
- **Solitude — Castle Dour** (Haafingar). The grim Legion fortress under Tullius and Rikke, hard by the **Blue Palace** (Elisif's court). The Imperial enlistment node and the Stormcloak campaign's final battlefield. Above the city broods the **Thalmor Embassy** — Elenwen's poisonous parties, and the dossiers that reveal who really gains from the war.
- **Whiterun — Dragonsreach** (Whiterun Hold). Balgruuf's great hall atop the stepped trade-city; the **hinge** of the whole war (Beat 4). Below it, two feuding families — the rebel-leaning **Gray-Manes** and the Empire-loyal **Battle-Borns** — a city's civil war in miniature.
- **Korvanjund** (a Nordic ruin in the Pale). The barrow of the **Jagged Crown** (Beat 2): a Hall-of-Stories door-puzzle, draugr, and a crowned Overlord.
- **Serpentstone Isle** (in the Sea of Ghosts, northeast of Winterhold). A standing stone on a frozen islet haunted by an **Ice Wraith** — the Stormcloak initiation.
- **Fort Hraggstad** (Haafingar). A bandit-held coastal fort: the Imperial initiation, and later the Stormcloak gateway to Solitude.
- **The contested forts.** **Fort Amol** (Eastmarch, the Imperial gateway to Windhelm), **Fort Sungard** and **Fort Neugrad** (the Reach / Falkreath), **Fort Snowhawk** (Hjaalmarch), **Fort Dunstad** (the Pale), **Fort Greenwall** (the Rift), and the rest — each a fort-skirmish node for the reconquest (Beat 5). Reskin one bandit fort freely into another.
- **Markarth** (the Reach). The City of Stone over its Dwemer bones — *taking the Reach* is the campaign's hardest ground, contested between Legion, Stormcloak, and the ever-present Forsworn.
- **High Hrothgar** (the Throat of the World). Neutral ground; if the optional truce convenes, it convenes here, with Elenwen angling for a chair.
