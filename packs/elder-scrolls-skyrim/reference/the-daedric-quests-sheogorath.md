**Sphere:** madness and creativity.

**Hook.** A filthy, raving beggar named **Dervenin** haunts the streets of **Solitude**, weeping that his master has gone on holiday and won't come home. His master is in the sealed **Pelagius Wing** of the Blue Palace — and Dervenin presses a yellowed *hip bone* on the party and begs them to fetch him.

**The bargain & the twist.** Behind the sealed door, the party falls **into the fractured mind of the long-dead mad Emperor Pelagius III**, where **Sheogorath** himself is enjoying a private dinner inside another man's insanity. The Prince of Madness will only leave (and free Dervenin) if the party becomes Pelagius's *therapist* — using the **Wabbajack** to wrangle the Emperor's personified neuroses into something like health. The twist is pure Sheogorath: there is no betrayal, no soul to sell, only the surreal obligation to fix a dead madman's mind so a god can take a vacation. Refuse, and Sheogorath shrugs and shows you the door — no Wabbajack, and Dervenin stays abandoned; the only "cost" of playing along is your dignity.

**Beats.**

1. **The locked wing.** Get the wing's key (from the palace staff or steward) and step through into Pelagius's mind.
2. **The Wabbajack.** Sheogorath, dining grandly, hands over the staff and sets three trials. Inside Pelagius's mind the staff costs no charges and does whatever the dream needs; its card applies once the party walks out.
3. **Three madnesses.** Shrink the Emperor's monstrous **Anger** and feed his cowering **Confidence**; transform his **Night Terrors** into harmless shapes; win his **Paranoia** by turning a "rival's" bodyguards into wolves. Each is a Wabbajack puzzle, not a slog.
4. **The Prince departs.** Pelagius soothed, Sheogorath leaves; the party walks out of the wing, Wabbajack in hand, Dervenin restored.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Sheogorath** — the Mad God, gracious and lethal-whimsical; not a foe.
- **Dervenin** — the abandoned servant; harmless.
- The "foes" inside the mind are figments — run any needed clash as **Bandits** or wild beasts, and lean on the Wabbajack's transform effect over damage.

**Reward.** **Wabbajack** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. No two strikes do the same thing twice.
