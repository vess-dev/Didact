### Krev the Skinner (Silver Hand captain)

10 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 12 WIL, silver greatsword (d10, _silver_), crossbow (d8, _bulky_)

- The butcher who commands Gallows Rock and skins beasts for trophies; he killed Skjor.
- His greatsword is _silver_ — _Enhanced_ against beast-blooded PCs. He fights from a kennel of caged, half-mad captive werewolves he looses as a _detachment_.
- **Critical Damage**: drives a silver stake home — STR loss he inflicts this round cannot be restored until the wound is treated by a healer.
