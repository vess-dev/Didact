- **The Guild's Luck** (d6, counting down). Starts at 6. Loses a point whenever a Guild job goes wrong, a member is caught or killed, or a session passes without the party moving against Mercer after Beat 4. At 3, the Cistern empties — only Delvin, Vex, Brynjolf, and Tonilia remain; at 1, Maven cuts the Guild loose and the Riften guard raids the Flagon. Returning the Key restores it to 6 at once.
- **Mercer's Head Start** (d4). After Beat 4, ticks each session the party spends on anything but the hunt. At 4, Mercer has the Eyes of the Falmer and is gone from Irkngthand before the party arrives: they must chase him to the coast, a ship, and a last stand on the ice.
