### Ancano — Thalmor agent, the antagonist

**Before the Eye:** run as an elite **Thalmor Justiciar** from the Bestiary (6 HP, 1 Armor, 10 STR, 13 DEX, 14 WIL, elven sword (d8), Destruction bolt (d8, _ranged_); spells against him are _Impaired_).

- A coldly polite Altmer "advisor" who watches everything and answers to the Aldmeri Dominion, not the Arch-Mage. Until Beat 5 he is protected by the rules of hospitality, not by force: striking first paints the party as murderers.
- **Empowered by the Eye:** see **Threats**.
