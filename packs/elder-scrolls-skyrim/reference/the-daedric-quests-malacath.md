**Sphere:** the spurned and ostracized; the sworn oath, the bloody curse; patron of Orcs.

**Hook.** The Orc stronghold of **Largashbur**, in the Rift, is under siege by **giants** — a plague no stronghold should suffer. The wise-woman **Atub** begs the party's aid and reveals the truth: the tribe is **cursed**, abandoned by their god, because their chief is weak.

**The bargain & the twist.** **Malacath**, the spurned Prince and patron of Orcs, will lift the curse — but only through a proof of strength. The ritual (a daedra heart and troll fat) summons his furious voice; he orders **Chief Yamarz** to journey to a giant's sacred grove and slay its champion with his own hands, returning the giant's club as tribute. The twist is Yamarz's cowardice: he will try to bribe the party to do his killing and let him claim the glory — and Malacath despises him for it. Yamarz dies in that grove no matter what (the giant kills him if you won't, or you may end his treachery yourself), and Malacath gladly accepts a worthier order. Here the "victim" is a craven leader, and the moral question is whether you let a coward steal a god's regard or expose him.

**Beats.**

1. **The siege.** Help drive off the giant assault on Largashbur; gather troll fat and a daedra heart for the rite.
2. **The rite.** Atub's ritual; Malacath's wrathful demand; Yamarz's shameful offer.
3. **Fallowstone Cave & the Giant's Grove.** Escort (or be used by) Yamarz to the sacred grove; the giant champion falls; Yamarz does too.
4. **The hammer transformed.** Lay the giant's club on Largashbur's shrine; it becomes **Volendrung**, and Malacath names a new chief.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Atub** — Orc wise-woman; ally, not a fighter.
- **Chief Yamarz** — proud and craven; *run as a* **Forsworn Briarheart** *(8 HP) if he fights, though he is doomed regardless.*
- The **giant champion** of the Grove — run as a **Giant**; **Fallowstone Cave** holds **Bear**s and **Troll**s.

**Reward.** **Volendrung** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. The Hammer of Might, which hurls a struck foe back and drinks its vigor. (Lifting the curse also makes the party **Blood-Kin** to the Orcs: in any stronghold, Orcs who hear of it start at Kind.)
