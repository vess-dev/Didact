### The Skeletal Dragon (Labyrinthian)

Run as the **Skeletal Dragon** variant of the Bestiary's **Dragon** (12 HP, 2 Armor, 16 STR, 13 DEX, 16 WIL, bite (d10), frost breath (d8, _blast_), _detachment_).

- All bone and no soul: it cannot fly, cannot be frightened, and magic against it is _Enhanced_. Scatter its bones when it falls, or it knits itself together again by the next day.
