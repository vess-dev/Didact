**Sphere:** life and light; hatred of the undead.

**Hook.** The party finds **Meridia's Beacon**, a glowing crystal (looted from a corpse, or pressed on them by fate), and a radiant voice commands them to carry it to the **Statue of Meridia** atop **Mount Kilkreath**, near Solitude.

**The bargain & the twist.** Meridia, Lady of Light, is one of the few Princes with a cause a hero can love — she *despises* the undead. A necromancer, **Malkoran**, has defiled her temple, twisting her own light to raise corrupted shades and bind tormented souls. She lifts the party bodily into the air and names them her champion: cleanse the temple, destroy the defiler, reclaim her sword. The "twist" is gentler than her siblings' but real — Meridia is imperious and absolute, brooks no refusal, and will press her crusade against the undead onto the party as a standing demand; her favor is a command, not a gift, and she does not care what it costs *you*. (She will even take a vampire as her champion, the better to set a monster against monsters.)

**Beats.**

1. **The beacon.** Place it at the statue; behold Meridia's blinding true form and take her commission.
2. **The temple of light.** Inside **Kilkreath Temple**, guide Meridia's ray from pedestal to pedestal, burning back the **Corrupted Shades**.
3. **The catacombs.** Press to the depths and wake Malkoran.
4. **Dawnbreaker.** Defeat Malkoran and his shade; take the radiant sword from its pedestal.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Meridia** — a pillar of intolerable light; patron and taskmaster.
- **Malkoran** — a powerful frost-mage necromancer; *run as a* **Necromancer** *(but a boss: 10 HP, and on death he rises once as a "Shade of Malkoran," a* **Ghost** *with his spells).*
- The temple is thick with **Corrupted Shades** — run as **Ghost**s — and reanimated **Draugr**.

**Reward.** **Dawnbreaker** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. The sword of caged daylight; it bursts in holy fire among the undead.
