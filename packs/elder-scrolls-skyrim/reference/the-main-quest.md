*You went to the block in chains, and the headsman's axe was already rising when the sky tore open and a black shape the size of a longhouse came down on Helgen. They say the dragons are gone from the world. They are wrong, and you are one of the few left alive who has seen one. Now the wise are looking at you strangely, the Greybeards are calling your name down the mountain in a voice that shakes the snow loose, and an old order of dragonslayers wants you to do the one thing no living soul has done in an age: kill the World-Eater and make it stay dead.*
