In the Wind District of Whiterun stands **Jorrvaskr**, the overturned hull of a longship that has been a mead hall for nearly five thousand years — home of the **Companions**, warriors-for-hire who keep faith with the band of Ysgramor. They take all comers who can swing a blade. But the inner **Circle** carry a secret: they are **werewolves**, beast-blooded through an old pact with the **Glenmoril Witches** in the name of **Hircine**, Prince of the Hunt. Their Harbinger, old **Kodlak Whitemane**, has come to dread the bargain — when a beast-blooded Companion dies, his soul hunts forever in Hircine's Hunting Grounds rather than feasting in Sovngarde, and Kodlak wants free of it before he dies. He will not get the chance. The **Silver Hand** — fanatic werewolf-hunters — are closing in, and they mean to gut Jorrvaskr and take the shards of Ysgramor's lost axe, **Wuuthrad**, with them.
