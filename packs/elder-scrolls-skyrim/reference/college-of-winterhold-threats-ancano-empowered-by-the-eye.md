### Ancano, empowered by the Eye (the final fight)

14 HP, 2 Armor, 10 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, magicka lash (d10, _blast_)

- **The field:** while the Eye's force-field stands, Ancano **cannot be harmed at all** — every attack and spell against him is wasted. Only the **Staff of Magnus**, turned against the field, drops it.
- Once per round, he draws on the Eye to heal 1d6 HP or to fling a PC back for d6. Spells against him are _Impaired_. The Staff's beam strikes him at its full die, but while he is joined to the Eye it cannot silence him — the lash is the Eye's power, not a spell.
- On taking **Critical Damage**, the stolen power gutters out of him in a shockwave as he dies: d6 _blast_ to everyone nearby, friend and foe.
