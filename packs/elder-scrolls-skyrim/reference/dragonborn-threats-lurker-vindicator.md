### Lurker Vindicator

18 HP, 1 Armor, 16 STR, 8 DEX, 13 WIL, hand swipe (d8+d8), tentacle spray (d8, _blast_, _ranged_)

- A greater **Lurker** that rises to punish the cleansing of an All-Maker Stone (use this or the Bestiary Lurker). Towering, slow, terrible. Fight only one at a time.
- Frost and shock damage against it are _Impaired_; mundane attacks against it are _Impaired_. It regains 1 HP at the start of each round. Its stomp knocks everyone near it flat unless they pass a STR save.
- **Critical Damage**: a gout of acid from its maw eats through the target's armor, destroying it.
