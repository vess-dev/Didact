The campaign resolves at the **Twilight Sepulcher** with the Skeleton Key in the party's hands. The choices:

- **Return the Key (the intended ending).** The party walks the Pilgrim's Path, sets the Key in the Ebonmere, and restores Nocturnal's covenant. Gallus is freed, the Guild's luck floods back, and the party rises to **Guild Master** over a Guild reborn — wealthy, lucky, and theirs. **Thieves Guild: Sworn (+2).**
- **Keep the Key.** A party that walks away with the Skeleton Key keeps the finest lockpick in Tamriel and a sliver of borrowed luck — but Nocturnal's favor never returns. The Guild stays a dying thing, Brynjolf and Karliah turn cold, and there is no Guild Master ceremony. The artifact's own text says it plainly: the Guild's fortune wanes for as long as it is kept.
- **Mercer wins (failure state).** If the party never recovers the Key — if they abandon the hunt after Snow Veil Sanctum, or fall in Irkngthand — Mercer escapes Skyrim with the Eyes of the Falmer and the Key both, and the Thieves Guild quietly dies in the dark beneath Riften, its luck spent. Karliah's exile becomes permanent, and Gallus is never avenged.
