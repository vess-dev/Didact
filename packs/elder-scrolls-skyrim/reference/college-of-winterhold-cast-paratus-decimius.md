### Paratus Decimius — the Synod survivor

6 HP, 1 Armor, 9 STR, 11 DEX, 14 WIL, dagger (d6), Destruction bolt (d8, _ranged_)

- The last living member of the Synod's expedition to Mzulft: arrogant, frightened, and certain the College is beneath him. He will help work the Oculory because he cannot do it alone.
