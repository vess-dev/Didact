The **Black Books** of Apocrypha are tomes of forbidden lore that bodily carry a reader into Hermaeus Mora's realm and, deep within, offer a strange boon. **Write each found Book as a Relic** (charges or uses, and a Recharge where it has one), not as a spell or a Shout. Each is _bulky_ (two slots) and is read in both hands, like a Spell Tome.

Two Black Books drive the plot (*Waking Dreams*, *Epistolary Acumen*); the rest are optional treasures hidden in Solstheim's ruins. A party that hunts them down may keep one or two.

### Black Book: *Waking Dreams* (_bulky_, 1 charge)

- Miraak's own Book, the key to his temple and his tower; reading it is the gate to Apocrypha (Beats 3 and 8).
- Spend the charge to open the way to Apocrypha — for the party, or, in a pinch, to **banish a single foe there for one scene** (it must pass a WIL save to resist; a creature that fails is simply gone until the scene ends).
- **Recharge:** a full Downtime Action spent reading at Miraak's pedestal, or a dragon's soul surrendered to the Book.

### Black Book: *Filament and Filigree* (_bulky_, 1 use)

- A Book hidden in a Solstheim ruin; its reader walks the green chapters of Apocrypha to claim *the Secret of Protection*.
- Read it to gain, for the rest of the scene, +1 Armor (to the cap of 3), and to shrug off the next single source of damage that would harm you. Then the Book closes and is spent.

### Black Book: *The Sallow Regent* (_bulky_, 1 use)

- Found in a Solstheim ruin; its boon is *Seeker of Might*.
- Read it to make your next strike this scene _Enhanced_, and to shrug off one disease or poison afflicting you until the scene ends. Then it is spent.

### Black Book: *Untold Legends* (_bulky_, 1 use)

- A Book kept in Benkongerike, an ice-cave hall held by Rieklings; its boon is the knowledge of the dead.
- Read it to recall a single forgotten thing — a passphrase, a tomb's puzzle-answer, a dead man's secret — as if you had always known it. The Warden simply tells you. Then it is spent. *Knowledge always costs more than it gives.*

> **Reading a Black Book is dangerous.** As with any Spell Tome, a reader under duress or Deprived makes a **WIL save** to avoid ill effect; on a failure the Book ensnares them — they are lost in Apocrypha for a scene (the rest of the party may have to read after them to pull them out), or they take a Fatigue, at the Warden's call. The Books *want* to be read.
