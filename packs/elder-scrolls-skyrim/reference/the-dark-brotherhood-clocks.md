Three simple tracks pace the arc. The Warden ticks them on the fiction, not the calendar.

- **The Tenets** (d6). The Five Tenets forbid betraying the Brotherhood, disobeying the Night Mother, and the like. Tick when the party flagrantly breaks faith — refusing a named contract, harming the coffin, turning on family. At 6, the **Wrath of Sithis** falls: an unseen spectral assassin hunts the offender (use **Astrid's** block, hostile) until the debt is paid in blood. (Reset on a sincere act of atonement at the Night Mother's coffin.)
- **Suspicion** (d4, Beats 4–7). Ticks each time a contract is botched loudly, a witness lives, or a Hold's guards connect a death to the party. At 4, **Commander Maro** finds his opening — bring the betrayal of Beat 8 forward and make the fire harder.
- **The Listener's Standing** (ambient, after Beat 5). Each clean, signature kill that becomes a rumor nudges the party's **Infamy** and, in the right circles, their **standing as Listener** — the desperate begin to whisper the Black Sacrament in their direction. (Killing the clearly monstrous never lowers Renown; murdering the innocent for coin is exactly the sort of deed that earns the **Infamous** rung.)
