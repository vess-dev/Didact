Alduin cannot be killed the way a man or a beast is killed — and not by the party alone. To bring him down the party must wake an ancient secret out of an **Elder Scroll**, win allies among dragons and dead heroes, hunt the World-Eater across the bridge between worlds into the land of the honored dead, and there strike the blow that the heroes of the Merethic dawn could only postpone. If they fail, he feeds on the souls of Sovngarde and then on the world. Along the way the party must also decide what the victory is worth: whether to halt a civil war for a truce, and whether to spare the old dragon who taught mortals the Voice. This is a frame for the whole arc, from the cart at Helgen to the last duel in **Sovngarde**.
