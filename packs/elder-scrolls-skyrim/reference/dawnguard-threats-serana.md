### Serana

8 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 17 WIL, draining bite (d6), Ice Spike (d8, _ranged_)

- The campaign's deep companion: an ancient Volkihar vampire and a powerful necromancer, sardonic and weary and slowly thawing. She walks with the party down **either** path. Run her as a companion (see Companions) — a named ally, hardier than a rolled one — with her Loyalty set by the **Serana's Trust** clock; her trick is necromancy: once per day, she raises a fresh corpse as a thrall (3 HP, its own weapon or d4) that crumbles at the scene's end.
- Her bite drains: the HP it deals are restored to her. Immune to poison and disease; fire against her is _Enhanced_; sunlight leaves her Deprived. She never tires of the cold.
- She can become a **Vampire Lord** as a PC vampire can, but rarely does so unbidden. Only Serana's blood can open the way to the Soul Cairn.
