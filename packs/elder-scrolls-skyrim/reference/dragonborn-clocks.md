- **The Sleepers' Toil** (d6). Ticks each session any of the five outer Stones remains corrupted. At 3, Storn's ward over the Skaal Village begins to fail, and a few villagers walk south in their sleep each night. At 6, the Temple of Miraak is finished: once per session, Miraak may reach through a sleeping PC's dreams (WIL save, or they wake having walked a mile toward the Temple, Deprived).
- **Mora's Regard** (d4). Ticks each time a PC reads a Black Book. At 4, the Prince has marked that reader: Seekers find them in their dreams, and one day Mora will ask for something in return — a seed for a campaign yet to come.
