*A war of cold and conviction. Both sides believe, and both are right about something and wrong about something else. The Stormcloaks have the truer grievance and the uglier prejudice; the Legion has the better discipline and the worse cause to die for. Let no one be a cartoon. The land is white and hard, the forts are stone and ice, and every battle is a handful of desperate people in a courtyard, not a cinematic horde.*

**Lean into:** the breath-fog of a war-camp at dawn, a Talos shrine hidden behind a false wall, a Dunmer in Windhelm's Gray Quarter spat on by the men he's "free" alongside, a Legion auxiliary who is himself a Nord, two great families of one city (the Gray-Manes and the Battle-Borns of Whiterun) ready to murder each other over the war. The kneeling man at the end is a person, and the choice to spare or behead him is the campaign's last true beat.

**A note on the Voice.** This is a soldiers' war, not a hero's saga. **No player wields the Thu'um.** Ulfric's famed Shout (the one that killed Torygg) is his alone, a creature-style ability noted in his block; the party fights with steel, bows, spells, and Relics like everyone else.
