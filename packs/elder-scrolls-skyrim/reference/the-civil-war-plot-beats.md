Six beats, deliberately parallel. The two paths run the same shape — join, prove, the courier-spark, the hinge battle, the long reconquest, the enemy capital — so a Warden runs one campaign whichever banner the party carries. Four to eight sessions.

### Beat 1 — Joining (the muster)

Run the initiation for the chosen side, above. The point of the beat is the *oath* and the first taste of the other ranks: introduce the recruiter who will assign the party's missions (**Galmar** for the Stormcloaks, **Rikke** for the Legion) and let the party meet a common soldier or two so the later dead have faces.

### Beat 2 — The Jagged Crown (both sides)

The first true mission, and it is the *same dungeon* for both armies — a race for a relic (its card is in **Relics**: The Jagged Crown). Long ago the High Kings of Skyrim wore the **Jagged Crown**, a circlet of dragon-bone and teeth; whoever recovers it wears the legitimacy of old Skyrim. It lies in the Nordic ruin of **Korvanjund**.

- The party rides out with a war-band (**Ralof** is along for the Stormcloaks; **Hadvar** for the Legion) and finds the *enemy* faction already at the ruin. Fight through them and into the barrow.
- Inside: a **Hall of Stories** door-puzzle (the claw-door's three rings: Fox, Moth, Dragon), draugr in the dark, and a **Draugr Overlord** crowned and waiting in the crypt.
- Take the Crown. Carry it back to **Ulfric** (Stormcloaks) or **Tullius** (Legion). *This is the last moment a party may switch banners.* **Reward:** the army's gratitude (Ulfric's open thanks; from Tullius only a "Good work, soldier"); the Crown itself goes to the war-leader, not the party.

### Beat 3 — Message to Whiterun (the spark)

Whiterun is the neutral heart of Skyrim, and its Jarl, **Balgruuf the Greater**, has kept his Hold out of the war as long as he can. Both sides need it; the war cannot be won around it. The party carries the demand that ends his neutrality.

- **Stormcloaks:** Carry **Ulfric's war axe** to Balgruuf — by old custom, to leave it is peace, to take it back is war. Balgruuf refuses the rebellion and returns the axe; the party brings it back to Ulfric, and the muster for the assault begins.
- **Imperials:** Carry **Tullius's** message to Balgruuf. The Jarl, pressed, sides with the Empire and offers his own axe to Ulfric as a last gesture of peace; Ulfric rejects it. Warn Balgruuf the storm is coming, then report to the Legion command (**Legate Cipius**) to prepare the city's defense.
- Either way, the courier's errand lights the fuse. **Beat 4 follows immediately.**

### Beat 4 — The Battle for Whiterun (the hinge)

**This is the war's turning point** and the campaign's centerpiece. Whiterun falls to whoever wins here, and with it the strategic heart of the province. Run it as a proper set-piece: a night assault on a walled city, fire on the thatch, soldiers in the streets, the fight climbing district by district to the great hall of **Dragonsreach**.

- **Stormcloak assault.** Orders from **Ulfric**; a briefing with **Galmar** at the Stormcloak camp outside the walls. The party storms the gate, fights through the **Plains** and **Wind** Districts under Imperial arrows, lowers a drawbridge or breaches a barricade, and pushes into Dragonsreach. **Balgruuf** fights to the last of his honor, then yields (he surrenders rather than die). The Stormcloaks install **Vignar Gray-Mane** — an aged ex-Companion and staunch rebel — as the new Jarl. **Reward:** a fine weapon from the quartermaster and the rank of **Ice-Veins**.
- **Imperial defense.** Report to **Cipius**, then **Rikke**. The party holds the besieged city — manning barricades, defending the drawbridge, cutting down the Stormcloak detachment as it pours through the gate until the attack breaks. **Balgruuf** gives a speech of thanks; **Whiterun stays with the Empire**, Balgruuf keeps his seat, and the Legion now owns the center of the map. **Reward:** the rank of **Quaestor** and a fine weapon from the quartermaster.

> **Renown.** Whichever way it goes, the Battle for Whiterun is *public* and sung about. **Renown +1** (one step, the cap per session) — a band that held or took the Jewel of the North is suddenly **Known**, then **Renowned** as the war goes on. Whiterun folk on the losing side will remember the party's banner.

### Beat 5 — Reconquest, Hold by Hold (the long campaign)

With Whiterun decided, the war becomes a grind across the remaining Holds. This is the **Liberation of Skyrim** (Stormcloak) or the **Reunification of Skyrim** (Imperial) — the same engine under two names: the war-leader assigns one contested Hold at a time, and the party takes it, usually by storming its **fort** (the *fort skirmish*: a stat-block courtyard battle, a commander to kill, a garrison to break). **Taking the Reach** is the bloodiest of these — Markarth is bitterly contested, riddled with Forsworn, and changes hands hard.

Run three to five of these as the table's appetite allows; each is a self-contained fort or field mission paying gear, and the first two a rank-step. Use The Holds as the map and let the party's victories *flip the Jarls*:

| Hold (capital) | Early Jarl & lean | Falls to → installs |
|---|---|---|
| **Whiterun** | Balgruuf (neutral→Imperial) | Stormcloaks → **Vignar Gray-Mane** (decided in Beat 4) |
| **Eastmarch** (Windhelm) | **Ulfric** (Stormcloak capital) | Legion → **Brunwulf Free-Winter** (the final assault) |
| **Haafingar** (Solitude) | **Elisif the Fair** (Imperial capital) | does not fall unless the rebellion wins all (the final assault) |
| **The Reach** (Markarth) | **Igmund** (Imperial) | Stormcloaks → **Thongvor Silver-Blood**; contested, Forsworn-ridden |
| **Falkreath** | **Siddgeir** (Imperial) | Stormcloaks → **Dengeir** (his uncle) |
| **Hjaalmarch** (Morthal) | **Idgrod Ravencrone** (Imperial, indifferent) | Stormcloaks → **Sorli the Builder** |
| **The Pale** (Dawnstar) | **Skald the Elder** (Stormcloak) | Legion → **Brina Merilis** |
| **The Rift** (Riften) | **Laila Law-Giver** (Stormcloak) | Legion → **Maven Black-Briar** |
| **Winterhold** | **Korir** (Stormcloak) | Legion → **Kraldar** |

- **Stormcloak path** ends by taking **Haafingar** — the assault on **Fort Hraggstad** opens the road to Solitude.
- **Imperial path** ends by taking **Eastmarch** — the assault on **Fort Amol** opens the road to Windhelm.

Each fall is a public deed in that Hold: **Faction Reputation** with the party's army holds at **Trusted (+1)** until the capital falls; a deposed Jarl's loyalists turn cold while the installed Jarl's people turn warm. A **Bounty** the party carried in a flipped Hold may be quietly forgiven by a grateful new Jarl — or pursued harder by a hostile one.

> **The optional truce.** If the table is also running the Main Quest, the dragon crisis may force a ceasefire (the *Season Unending* talks at High Hrothgar). The war *pauses* — unless the enemy capital is the last city standing. Use it to complicate the map: a Hold or two may change hands at the negotiating table rather than on the field, and the Thalmor's emissary **Elenwen** may insist on a seat, a small, telling sign of who profits from the bloodletting.

### Beat 6 — The Final Assault (the enemy capital)

The war ends in the foe's own throne-room. This is a second great set-piece to mirror Whiterun: a charge through a defended city to a last stand in the great hall, the enemy commanders fought in sequence, and the war-leader's foe brought to his knees.

- **Battle for Solitude (Stormcloak victory).** After Fort Hraggstad, orders from **Galmar**; the party hears **Ulfric's** speech at the gates, then fights up through Solitude to **Castle Dour**. **Legate Rikke** dies covering the retreat; **General Tullius** is beaten into submission. Ulfric offers the party the killing stroke — *behead Tullius yourself, or let Ulfric do it.* Skyrim is independent. **Elisif** is kept on as Jarl of a now-rebel Solitude (a mercy, and a leash). **Reward:** a sword from Ulfric's own armory; the rank of **Stormblade**; Stormcloaks → **Sworn (+2)**.
- **Battle for Windhelm (Imperial victory).** After Fort Amol, **Tullius's** speech; the party charges the gates and fights through the ancient City of Kings to the **Palace of the Kings**. **Galmar Stone-Fist** dies at his Jarl's side; **Ulfric** is forced to his knees. Tullius offers the party the stroke — *behead Ulfric yourself, or let Tullius do it.* The rebellion is broken; the fair-minded **Brunwulf Free-Winter** — a friend to the city's abused Dunmer — is installed as the new Jarl of Eastmarch. **Reward:** an enchanted sword from Tullius; the rank of **Legate**; the party made **Thane of Eastmarch** with property in Windhelm; Legion → **Sworn (+2)**.

> **The last beat is a person.** Whoever kneels — Tullius or Ulfric — let the party look him in the eye. Sparing-and-imprisoning is on the table; so is the headsman. **Renown +1** for ending the war in public; and one quiet scene afterward where a Thalmor agent, or a captured dispatch, makes plain that the Dominion counted this war a victory of its own. The cheering crowd does not know that yet.
