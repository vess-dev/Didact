Three plots are commonly offered across Skyrim. Each sits in a real Hold, answers to a real Jarl, and comes — once the party is named Thane and the manor is built up enough — with a sworn **housecarl**. A Warden may site a fourth elsewhere; the procedure is the same.

| Plot | Hold (capital) | The land | Unique outbuilding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lakeview Manor** | Falkreath (Falkreath) | Wooded land south of Lake Ilinalta, between Falkreath and Riverwood. Timber and game close at hand. | **Apiary** (a bee-yard: honey, and beeswax for candles and mead) |
| **Windstad Manor** | Hjaalmarch (Morthal) | Cold, fog-bound marsh north of Morthal, near the Sea of Ghosts. Bleak, defensible, far from prying eyes. | **Fish hatchery** (penned salmon and slaughterfish roe) |
| **Heljarchen Hall** | The Pale (Dawnstar) | High tundra south of Dawnstar, near the Whiterun border. Bitter winters; a mine and a trade road within reach. | **Grain mill** (a windmill grinding the Hold's wheat) |

### Earning the right to buy

A plot is not sold to strangers. To buy land in a Hold, the party must first be **welcome** there, then pay for it:

- **Standing with the Jarl.** Do the Jarl (or Steward) a service first — clear a nearby ruin or bandit fort, settle a dispute, run an errand of weight. Treat this as a single adventure or a favor cashed in. In **Faction Reputation** terms, the Hold's court should sit at **Trusted (+1)** or better; the Warden may simply judge it from the fiction. (A party already named **Thane** of the Hold qualifies outright.)
- **The price.** The land deed costs **5,000 Septims**, paid to the Steward. This buys the parcel and a starter steading: the cleared ground, a small **on-site quarry** of stone, a **clay deposit**, a **log pile**, and a one-room main hall. On the page, this is the **1/1/1 homestead with one slot (the main hall) filled**.
- **A foothold.** The Steward hands over a **Land Deed** (a _petty_ document — proof of title) and points the party at the site. From here, everything is Downtime.

> **Carriage tie-in.** Each plot sits a hard day or more from its capital. A party that has hired a **carriage driver** (below) can reach the site, the capital, and the other Holds quickly between sessions; without one, factor the travel into wilderness watches per **Wilderness Exploration**.
