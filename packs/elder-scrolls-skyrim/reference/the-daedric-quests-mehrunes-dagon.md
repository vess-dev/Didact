**Sphere:** destruction, change, ambition, revolution.

**Hook.** A courier presses a pamphlet on the party: the Museum of the Mythic Dawn has opened in Dawnstar, admission free. Its keeper, **Silus Vesuius**, is the last scion of the cult that murdered an Emperor, and he has built a shrine to his family's shame. He wants the three scattered shards of his ancestors' relic — Mehrunes' Razor — and will pay finders' gold for each.

**The bargain & the twist.** Silus thinks the Razor is an heirloom to be displayed. The shards reforge only at the **Shrine of Mehrunes Dagon**, and there the Prince's voice fills every skull present: the blade will be made whole only in blood, and the blood Dagon wants is **Silus's own**. Kill the man who hired you on his own family's altar, and the Razor is yours. Spare him, and he flees with the pieces — no prize, only a Prince's contempt. (Silus may beg, offering 500 Septims for his life. Dagon does not care either way.)

**Beats.**

1. **The three shards.** A pommel at **Dead Crone Rock** (a hagraven's perch), a blade-shard vaulted in **Cracked Tusk Keep** (an Orc bandit-chief's hoard), a hilt in a locked chest in **Jorgen's house** in Morthal. Three small delves or heists.
2. **The museum.** Return the pieces; Silus invites the party to the shrine for the "unveiling."
3. **The shrine speaks.** Dagon manifests; the demand is made. The party chooses.
4. **Blood or contempt.** Strike Silus down to claim the Razor — or refuse and watch the chance crumble.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Silus Vesuius** — earnest, deluded collector; non-combatant unless cornered. *Run as a* **Bandit** *if he fights (4 HP, sword d8).*
- **Drascua**, hagraven of Dead Crone Rock — run as a **Hagraven**. Her guard is a small _detachment_ of **Forsworn**.
- **Ghunzul**, chief of Cracked Tusk Keep — run as a **Bandit** detachment leader (the leader variant: 2 Armor, greatsword d10).

**Reward.** **Mehrunes' Razor** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. The black dagger that whispers of ruin; spend its single charge, and the foe it strikes must pass a STR save or die.
