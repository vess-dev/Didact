Background timers the Warden advances on the named triggers. These clocks never tick on session count alone.

- **The Tyranny Clock (d6).** How close Harkon comes to the eternal night. Ticks each time the party hands him an advantage — a Scroll read in his service, the Bow brought to the castle, a Volkihar victory left unanswered. *(On the Volkihar track the party is usually advancing this themselves.)* At 6, if the climax has not resolved, Harkon enacts the prophecy: the sun dims over Skyrim, every vampire is emboldened, and the final assault begins on *his* terms.
- **Serana's Trust (a Loyalty track, 1–5).** Serana begins at **3 (Trusting)**. She rises a step when the party shields her from her father's plans, takes her counsel, or treats her as a person and not a relic; she falls a step when they hand her to Isran's suspicion, threaten her mother, or treat her only as a key. At **5 (Sworn)** she gives the party the **Bloodcursed arrows** and stands at their shoulder in the climax; at **1 (Wary)** she may walk away before the Forgotten Vale, taking her Scroll with her.
