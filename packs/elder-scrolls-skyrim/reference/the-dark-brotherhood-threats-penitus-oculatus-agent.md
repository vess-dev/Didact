### Penitus Oculatus Agent *(use freely)*

5 HP, 2 Armor, 13 STR, 13 DEX, 11 WIL, sword (d8) or crossbow (d8)

- Disciplined Imperial agent-soldiers in dark leather. They sack the Sanctuary in Beat 8 and crew the *Katariah*. Groups of four or more are a _detachment_.
