Riften is a canal-laced wooden trade town on Lake Honrich in the autumn-forest Hold of the Rift, ruled in name by Jarl **Laila Law-Giver** and in truth by **Maven Black-Briar**. It is the most corrupt city in Skyrim, and the Guild is the reason.

- **The Riften Market.** Open stalls in the canal town's heart. Where Brynjolf works the crowd and the campaign begins. Madesi the Argonian jeweler, Brand-Shei the Dunmer trader, and Grelka the armorer keep stalls here.
- **The Ratway.** The flooded sewer-warren beneath Riften, full of skeevers, lowlifes, and the desperate (it is also where the Blades' loremaster Esbern hides, in another tale). Two ways into the Guild: the long crawl through the Ratway, or a secret tomb door east of the Hall of the Dead.
- **The Ragged Flagon.** A dingy bar at the bottom of the Ratway, run by **Vekel the Man**, where the Guild drinks and fences. The fence **Tonilia** works a corner.
- **The Cistern.** The Guild's true home behind the Flagon — a vaulted, water-floored hall of bunks, a strongbox vault, and the Guild Master's quarters. Empty bunks tell the story of the Guild's decline.
- **Mistveil Keep.** Jarl Laila's seat. The party need not enter, but the city guard answers to it, and a high Bounty here makes every job harder.
