### Tolfdir — senior instructor, the party's first teacher

8 HP, 1 Armor, 9 STR, 9 DEX, 15 WIL, staff (d6), Destruction bolt (d8, _ranged_)

- An old, kindly, absent-minded master of Alteration who has taught at the College longer than anyone living. The party's mentor and steadiest ally; he believes the best of everyone, which is both his charm and his blind spot.
- Casts wards and flesh-spells over the party rather than attacking; in a crisis he is calm where others panic. Knows the Alteration shelves of the Arcanaeum by heart. Names the party Arch-Mage at the campaign's end, and means it.
