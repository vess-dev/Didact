Any one of these puts the party on the road to a recruiter. Pick the one that fits the table, or let a background do it.

- **Out of Helgen.** The classic opening (shared with the Main Quest): the party are bound prisoners at Helgen when a dragon levels the town. They flee with **Ralof** (a Stormcloak) or **Hadvar** (an Imperial soldier) — and the man they ran beside is the first to ask them to enlist. (This only sets who *asks first*; the party may still choose the other side.)
- **A background tie.** A **Stormcloak Soldier** or **Imperial Legionnaire** background starts that faction at **+1** (Trusted) and the opposite at **−1** (Distrusted), and points straight at the muster.
- **A Hold under threat.** The party's home or a settlement they've cleared sits in a contested Hold (see *Settlements*). A war-party is coming for the road it stands on; the only protection on offer comes with a banner attached.
- **Coin and grievance.** Both armies pay, both are recruiting, and a sellsword party can be talked into a cause over enough mead — or hired by a Jarl to carry a war-message that drags them in.
