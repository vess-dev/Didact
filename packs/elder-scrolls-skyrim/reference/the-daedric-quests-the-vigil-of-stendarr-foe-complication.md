Stendarr's holy order are the sworn enemies of everything in this module. They hunt Daedra, their cultists, vampires, werebeasts, and the undead — *Abominations* all — and they consider anyone who courts a Daedric Prince or carries a Prince's relic fair game. Their Skyrim chapter-house south of Dawnstar was destroyed by the Volkihar vampires not long ago, so those who remain are few, grieving, and zealous to the point of recklessness. Use a Vigilant as a rival racing the party to a shrine, an accuser who appears the morning after a dark bargain, or an avenger who tracks an Artifact to the party's door.

### Vigilant of Stendarr

6 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 11 DEX, 13 WIL, steel mace (d8), Sun Fire (d6, _ranged_, undead only)

- A grim, robed hunter of the abominable, marked with Stendarr's hammer, who shows no mercy to Daedra, undead, or those who serve them. Patrols in pairs; four or more fight as a _detachment_.
- Against Daedra, the undead, and lycanthropes, a Vigilant's attacks are _Enhanced_. They will not be bargained with by anyone they have seen carrying a Daedric Artifact.
- **Critical Damage**: a burst of holy fire sears the target for an extra d4 damage and, against the undead or Daedra, forces a WIL save or they flee.
