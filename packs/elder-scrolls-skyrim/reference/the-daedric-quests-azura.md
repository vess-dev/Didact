**Sphere:** dusk and dawn; twilight, fate, prophecy, mystery.

**Hook.** At the **Shrine of Azura** south of Winterhold — a great statue raised by Dunmer exiles — the lone priestess **Aranea Ienith** has received a vision from her mistress: a fortress the sea threatens yet has never touched, and an elven mage who can "turn the brightest star black." Her vision points to **Nelacar**, an exiled wizard at Winterhold's Frozen Hearth inn.

**The bargain & the twist.** Azura's Star is a reusable soul gem of legend, but it was **corrupted** by the necromancer **Malyn Varen**, who fed innocents (including a student) into it chasing immortality, and now haunts its crystalline interior. The party recovers the broken Star from the sunken ruin of **Ilinalta's Deep** and faces a clean, defining fork: return it to **Azura** to be **purified** (it will hold only the **white souls** of beasts — the holy, harder path, and Aranea joins as a grateful follower), or side with **Nelacar** to keep it **corrupted** as **The Black Star** (which greedily takes the **black souls** of the sapient — more power, and a taboo crime). Azura's bargain is the rare one that asks the party to choose *purity over power* — and lets them refuse it.

**Beats.**

1. **The vision.** Aranea's cryptic clue sends the party to Nelacar in Winterhold.
2. **The mage's tale.** Coax Malyn Varen's history and the Star's resting place from Nelacar.
3. **Ilinalta's Deep.** Delve the flooded fortress; fight necromancers and undead; recover the broken Star.
4. **The choice — and the duel within.** Side with Aranea or Nelacar, then step *inside* the Star to destroy Malyn Varen's lingering spirit. Emerge with Azura's Star (white) or the Black Star (black).

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Aranea Ienith** — devoted priestess; a **Conjurer**-type follower if you side with Azura.
- **Nelacar** — a guarded, world-weary Altmer; informant, and the other path.
- **Malyn Varen** — a dead master necromancer fought as a spirit inside the Star; *run as a* **Wight** *(no followers may enter with the party).* Two of his surviving **Necromancer** disciples may later hunt the party for revenge.
- **Ilinalta's Deep** is full of **Necromancer**s, **Skeleton**s, and **Draugr**.

**Reward.** Side with Azura → **Azura's Star** (white souls). Side with Nelacar → **The Black Star** (black souls). Both in *Relics*. One ruin, two artifacts, one conscience.
