### Mirmulnir (the Watchtower dragon)

Run as a **Dragon** from the Bestiary: 12 HP, 2 Armor, 16 STR, 13 DEX, 16 WIL, bite (d10), breath (d8, _blast_), _detachment_.

- The first dragon the party fights on the ground (Beat 3), at the Western Watchtower with Irileth and a handful of Whiterun guards (run the guards as a friendly _detachment_ that soaks a breath or two). Breathes fire.
- **Critical Damage**: as Dragon. When it dies, its flesh burns away and the Dragonborn absorbs its soul — narrate the rush of strength and the distant Voice of the Greybeards.
