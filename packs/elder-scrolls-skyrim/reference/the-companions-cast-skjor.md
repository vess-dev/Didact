### Skjor (Circle)

10 HP, 2 Armor, 15 STR, 13 DEX, 12 WIL, Skyforge sword (d10) and shield (counted in Armor); Beast Form claws (d6+d6), bite (d8)

- One-eyed veteran, blunt and battle-glad; gives the beast-blood and pushes the war with the Silver Hand too fast.
- Mundane attacks against his Beast Form are _Impaired_, silver _Enhanced_; immune to disease. **Dies at Gallows Rock (Beat 4).**
- **First Blood**: his attacks are _Enhanced_ in the first round of any combat he begins.
