An island's freedom and a people's secret. If Miraak returns, Solstheim becomes his, and the First Dragonborn walks Tamriel again with the dragons at his call. To stop him, the Skaal must give up the knowledge they have guarded for four thousand years — and their shaman must give up his life. The party wins a victory that costs a friend, and earns the attention of a Prince who does not forget.
