*An old warrior dies afraid of where his soul will go. The mead hall that raised the heroes of Skyrim hides a curse under its floor — the inner Circle run with wolves, sold to a Daedric Prince of the Hunt — and the only people who can break the bargain are the witches who struck it and the hunters who want every werewolf in Skyrim skinned.*
