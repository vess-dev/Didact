*Candle-smoke, canal-rot, and the cold weight of a picked lock. This is not a heist of clean rooftops and clever banter — Skyrim's Guild is broke, mean, and half-feral, and the "luck" everyone leans on is a debt come due. The mood is noir under a fantasy skin: a fallen institution, an old murder no one solved, a friend at the top who turns out to be the knife. Then, halfway through, it turns mythic — a Daedric Prince, a black key, a shrine of living shadow.*

**Lean into:** the descent. The first act is wet stone and small crimes — burning beehives, leaning on debtors, framing a vendor. The second act is the betrayal and the long cold road north after the man who did it. The third act goes underground and underworld at once: the Dwemer dark, the Falmer warrens, and a tomb where standing in the light will kill you. Let the party *feel* the Guild's luck thicken once the Key comes home.

**Touchstones:** *The Usual Suspects* (the partner who was the mastermind all along), *L.A. Confidential* (a corrupt institution rotted from the head), classic guild-of-thieves heist fiction, and the old bargain-with-a-dark-power folktale where the price was always going to come due.
