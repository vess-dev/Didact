### Emperor Titus Mede II (the Target)

8 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 12 DEX, 15 WIL, ceremonial sword (d8 — drawn only in self-defense, which he declines)

- The aging, clear-eyed ruler of the Empire, alone in his cabin aboard the *Katariah*. He does not resist. He has expected the knife, and spends his last words asking the assassin to weigh who *paid* for his death.
- **The Last Request:** the Emperor will not fight. The "combat" here is moral, not martial — the killing is automatic if the party chooses it. His one ask is that they consider turning the blade on the schemer Amaund Motierre instead of, or as well as, himself.
