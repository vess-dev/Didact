Any one of these gets a party to the bridge at Winterhold:

- **The obvious one.** A PC wants to learn magic, or learn *more* magic, and the College is the only school that will have them. They come to apply. A PC with the College Apprentice background is already enrolled, and starts at **College of Winterhold +1**.
- **A tome to translate.** The party recovered a Spell Tome from a barrow that none of them can safely read — it smells of grave-cold, or only shows its letters in moonlight. **Urag gro-Shub**, the College librarian, is the one scholar in Skyrim who might identify it.
- **A missing apprentice.** A merchant or a worried parent in Dawnstar or Windhelm hires the party to find a young mage who left to study at Winterhold and has not written home. (He is fine — for now. He becomes a useful tie inside the College.)
- **Sent by another faction.** A Jarl's court wizard, or the Synod (the Imperial mages' order), wants eyes inside the independent College and pays the party to enroll and report.
- **The cold road.** The party is simply crossing the worst Hold in Skyrim in winter, and the College is the only fire and roof for a day in any direction.
