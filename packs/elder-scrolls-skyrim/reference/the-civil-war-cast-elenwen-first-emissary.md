#### Elenwen, First Emissary

8 HP, 1 Armor, 10 STR, 12 DEX, 16 WIL, bound dagger (d6), Destruction bolt (d8, _ranged_)

- The smiling, ruthless head of the Thalmor in Skyrim, mistress of the embassy above Solitude, who keeps a dossier on everyone who matters — Ulfric included. Rarely fought; mostly a voice at a party and a hand behind the war. If the optional truce convenes, she insists on a seat at the table and savors the deadlock.
