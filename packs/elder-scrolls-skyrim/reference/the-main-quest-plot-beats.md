Broad strokes — the spine of the whole arc, eight to ten or more sessions. Reorder, compress, or stretch as the table pushes. Each beat is a turning point and a branch, not a scripted scene. The **Civil War** can run alongside this whole questline; **Season Unending** (Beat 6 branch) is where the two meet.

1. **Helgen & the Road to Whiterun.** *Goal:* get out alive and warn someone who matters. *Scene:* escape Helgen Keep; reach **Riverwood** (the smith Alvor, Hadvar's uncle, or the mill-owner Gerdur, Ralof's sister, gives the party their first proper supplies and begs them to carry word to the Jarl). At Riverwood the local tale of a stolen **Golden Claw** points up the mountain to **Bleak Falls Barrow** — and Whiterun's court wizard wants something from that same barrow. *Branch:* warn the Jarl first, or detour to the barrow first; tell Ralof's people or Hadvar's people (a first nudge toward a Civil War side).

2. **Bleak Falls Barrow.** *Goal:* recover the **Dragonstone**, an ancient stone tablet the court wizard **Farengar Secret-Fire** needs to map where dragons are buried. *Obstacle:* bandits in the upper ruin, then the awakened dead — the pillar-rotation puzzle (the carved animals match the fallen stones), the spider-choked corridor, and the **Draugr Overlord** who holds the Dragonstone and guards a glowing **Word Wall** the party cannot read. *Scene:* the party touches the Word Wall and *hears* something in the dragon-tongue but takes nothing from it — the first hint that the Voice is real and not theirs. *Branch:* return the Golden Claw to Riverwood (renown, a small reward) or keep it.

3. **Dragon Rising.** *Goal:* deliver the Dragonstone to Whiterun and report the dragon. *Scene:* at **Dragonsreach**, Farengar and a watchful stranger (**Delphine**, in disguise) take the stone — and word comes that a dragon has landed at the **Western Watchtower**. The party rides out with the housecarl **Irileth** and the guard, and kills the dragon **Mirmulnir**. *The turn:* as the dragon dies, its flesh burns away and its strength flows into one of the party — they have **absorbed a dragon's soul**, and everyone present knows what that means. Far up the mountain, the Greybeards' Voice rolls across all of Skyrim, calling that PC's name. *Reward:* **Thane of Whiterun**; the housecarl **Lydia** (a companion — see Rewards) and the right to buy a house in the city.

4. **The Way of the Voice.** *Goal:* answer the Greybeards' summons. *Scene:* the long climb of the **7,000 Steps** above **Ivarstead** (an optional supply errand from Klimmek; a frost troll in the pass, and carved tablets along the way that tell how mortals learned the Voice) to **High Hrothgar**, where **Arngeir** and the silent Greybeards test the Dragonborn. *The Voice, made vivid:* the Greybeards demonstrate the Thu'um and it is plainly a force the party will never command — they meditate on it, the party endures it. Arngeir sends them to recover the **Horn of Jurgen Windcaller** from the barrow of **Ustengrav** as proof of their resolve. *Branch:* the party may treat the Greybeards as the whole answer, or already be wondering who else hunts dragons.

5. **A Blade in the Dark.** *Goal:* find the Horn — and find out who left a note in its place. *Scene:* at **Ustengrav** the Horn is gone, replaced by a message: *meet at the Sleeping Giant Inn, Riverwood.* There the innkeeper drops her disguise — she is **Delphine**, one of the last **Blades**. She returns the Horn and makes the party a darker offer: dragons are *resurrecting* from their burial mounds, and she can predict the next. They travel to the mound at **Kynesgrove** and witness **Alduin** himself tear a dead dragon — **Sahloknir** — back out of the earth (Alduin cannot be touched here; he flies off). The party kills Sahloknir. Delphine, convinced, names the party Dragonborn and her suspicion: the **Thalmor** know something. *Reward:* return the Horn to Arngeir for the Greybeards' full welcome; Delphine's alliance.

6. **Diplomatic Immunity & Alduin's Wall.** *Goal:* learn what the ancients knew about killing Alduin. *Scene:* infiltrate the **Thalmor Embassy** above Solitude (a guest-list party, a smuggled-gear caper, a quiet theft of the ambassador's files) to learn the Thalmor are also hunting an old man — **Esbern**, the last Blades loremaster — in the **Ratway** under Riften. Rescue him, and the reunited Blades lead the party to **Sky Haven Temple**, beyond the **Karthspire** (a Forsworn-held canyon; see Threats). Inside, **Alduin's Wall** — an Akaviri carving — tells how the ancient Nords brought the World-Eater down: not with steel alone, but with a power that could pull a dragon out of the sky and make it *mortal* and *afraid*. They called it **Dragonrend**, and it is lost. *Reward:* the Blades' katana **Dragonbane** is found in the temple. *Major branch — the war:* if Skyrim's civil war is in the party's way (it blocks the trap at Beat 9), the Greybeards can host a truce — **Season Unending** — where the party brokers a ceasefire between Tullius and Ulfric. This is also the doorway into the **Civil War** frame.

7. **The Throat of the World & the Elder Scroll.** *Goal:* recover Dragonrend the only way left — from an **Elder Scroll**. *Scene:* climb past High Hrothgar to the summit and meet **Paarthurnax**, the ancient dragon who leads the Greybeards and once served Alduin before turning against him. He reveals the **Time-Wound**, a scar in time at the summit where the ancient heroes first used Dragonrend against Alduin — and that an **Elder Scroll** read there can let a mind reach across the centuries and *witness* the lost power. Finding the Scroll is its own delve: the College of Winterhold's librarian and the half-mad scholar **Septimus Signus** point the party down through **Alftand** into the vast underground twilight of **Blackreach**, to the **Tower of Mzark**, where a Dwemer orrery yields the **Elder Scroll (Dragon)**. *Reward:* the Elder Scroll; passage through Blackreach (a wonder in itself). *Optional branch — Paarthurnax's life:* the Blades will, sooner or later, demand the party kill Paarthurnax for his ancient crimes. Refusing costs the Blades' support; obeying costs an irreplaceable ally for the finale. Leave the choice with the party (see **Climax**).

8. **Alduin's Bane — the Time-Wound.** *Goal:* learn Dragonrend and see it loosed, once, to break Alduin. *Scene:* the party reads the **Elder Scroll** at the Time-Wound. The world unspools backward: they witness the three ancient heroes — **Gormlaith, Hakon, and Felldir** — wield Dragonrend and drive Alduin off at the dawn of history. The vision *is* the weapon: having seen it, the party (through the Dragonborn, or as a one-time ritual the Warden narrates) can invoke Dragonrend **this once, in this place**. Alduin, drawn by the disturbance in time, descends to stop them — and the party, with Paarthurnax fighting beside them, grounds the World-Eater and beats him bloody. He does not die. He flees, beaten for the first time in an age, north and east toward his secret stronghold. *No player Shout results from this* — Dragonrend is spent with the vision; what the party gains is knowledge of *where Alduin draws his strength*, and that he can be hurt. *Reward:* Paarthurnax as a sworn ally; the certainty that Alduin is mortal.

9. **The Fallen — the Trap at Dragonsreach.** *Goal:* capture a living dragon and wring Alduin's hiding place out of it. *Scene:* the wise reveal that **Dragonsreach** in Whiterun was *built* to hold a dragon — the great wooden yoke on the **Great Porch** was a dragon-trap in the old days. Paarthurnax and Esbern give the party the name of a dragon loyal enough to Alduin to know where he hides: **Odahviing**. The party convinces the Jarl of Whiterun to bait the trap (this is where an unsettled **Civil War** must first be paused via Season Unending). *The set-piece:* the party lures Odahviing to the porch, fights him down to half his strength in the open hall, and **springs the trap** — the ancient yoke slams shut on the dragon's neck. *This is staged entirely in the fiction:* the party's job is to survive the fight and trip the mechanism; no Shout and no player rule is required to close it. *Branch:* interrogate the caged Odahviing. He respects strength; beaten and impressed, he agrees to carry the party to **Skuldafn**, Alduin's mountain temple, which no mortal can otherwise reach. *Reward:* **Odahviing** as a dragon ally (see Rewards & Threats).

10. **The World-Eater's Eyrie — Skuldafn.** *Goal:* reach Alduin's portal and follow him through it. *Scene:* Odahviing flies the party (alone — no hirelings or mounts) to **Skuldafn**, a ruined dragon-priest temple high in the eastern mountains, guarded by a circling **Dragon**, a _detachment_ of **draugr**, and the dragon priest **Nahkriin** who holds the key. *Obstacle:* fight across the courtyard and up the temple steps; the pictograph puzzle on the locked door; the duel with Nahkriin for his staff. *The turn:* Nahkriin's staff powers the **portal arch**; activating it opens the way to **Sovngarde**, the Nordic afterlife where Alduin has fled to feed on the souls of the honored dead. The party steps through. *Reward:* the dragon-priest mask **Nahkriin** (see Rewards — and warn the party that Skuldafn cannot be reached again once they step through; whatever they leave there is lost for good).

11. **Sovngarde & Dragonslayer.** *Goal:* end the World-Eater for good, with allies at the party's side. *Scene:* Sovngarde is a misted golden country, and the mist is Alduin's snare — it swallows the souls of the brave so he can devour them. The party crosses it (a lost soul — a Stormcloak newly fallen in the war — explains the trick), duels the gate-guardian **Tsun** for the right to enter the **Hall of Valor**, and there finds **Ysgramor** and the three ancient heroes — **Gormlaith, Hakon, and Felldir** — who first beat Alduin. They join the party. *Climax:* the heroes' Voices tear away Alduin's protecting mist, the party grounds him a final time, and — with the three heroes fighting at their side — the party lands the killing blow on the World-Eater. See **Climax** below. *Reward:* the world saved; the party walks back into Skyrim as the slayers of the World-Eater.
