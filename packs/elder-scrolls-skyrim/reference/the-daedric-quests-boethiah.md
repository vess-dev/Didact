**Sphere:** plots, deceit, conspiracy, secret murder, treason.

**Hook.** A black book, *Boethiah's Proving*, falls into the party's hands (or a seasoned party simply hears of the **Sacellum of Boethiah**, a mountaintop shrine east of Windhelm where a cult gathers). The **Priestess of Boethiah** offers a path to the Prince's favor.

**The bargain & the twist.** Boethiah, Prince of treachery and "rightful" murder, demands proof that a tongue can wield a lie. The party must lead one of their own **trusted companions** to the shrine's **Pillar of Sacrifice** and kill them there — betrayal made into worship. Then the cult turns on itself in Boethiah's arena (each cultist a rival for the Prince's eye), and the last survivor must hunt down Boethiah's **old, failing champion** at **Knifepoint Ridge** and take his place. The twist is that the entire faith is built on betrayal: there are no innocents here, only conspirators, and the price of the Ebony Mail is the murder of someone who trusted you. A party with no follower to spare — or no stomach for it — simply has no entry, and that is answer enough.

**Beats.**

1. **The Sacellum.** Reach the shrine; the Priestess names the price — a sacrificed friend.
2. **The betrayal.** Lure a trusting follower to the Pillar; the deed opens the way.
3. **The arena.** The cultists fight one another (and the party) for the honor; survive.
4. **Knifepoint Ridge.** Slay the old champion; Boethiah names a new one and the Mail is yours.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Priestess of Boethiah** — zealous cult-leader; *run as a* **Conjurer**.
- The **Sacellum cultists** — a melee of fanatics; run as **Bandit**s (a chaotic, every-side fight).
- **Boethiah's old Champion** at Knifepoint Ridge — *run as a* **Wight** *(12 HP, 3 Armor, ebony greatsword d10)*, with a small _detachment_ of **Bandit**s.

**Reward.** **Ebony Mail** — see its card in *Unique Armors*. Move in unnatural silence inside a shroud of venom.
