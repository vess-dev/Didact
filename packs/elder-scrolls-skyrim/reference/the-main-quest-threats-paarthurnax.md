### Paarthurnax (optional — only if the Blades' ultimatum is obeyed)

18 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, bite (d12), Fire Breath (d10, _blast_), _detachment_

- The party's mentor and greatest dragon ally. Provide this block *only* if the party chooses to kill him for the Blades (see Climax). He does not fight back in earnest against those he counts as kin — but he is an Ancient Dragon's equal if cornered. Killing him forfeits his counsel and weighs on the table; the Blades give no reward (their support resuming is the only "gain").
