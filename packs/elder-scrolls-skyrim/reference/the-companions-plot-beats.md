Six to nine sessions. The spine is fixed at three points — the party is **offered the beast-blood**, **Kodlak dies**, and **his soul is judged at Ysgramor's Tomb** — and open everywhere else. These are signposts, not scripted scenes.

1. **Take Up Arms.** The party reaches Jorrvaskr and speaks with Harbinger Kodlak. He has them **spar with Vilkas** in the yard to take their measure (a friendly bout — first to yield, no Scars). Earn a place as a whelp. Run errands that show the hall's shape: carry Vilkas's sword to **Eorlund Gray-Mane** at the **Skyforge**, deliver a shield to Aela, let **Farkas** show the party their bunks. One radiant job (clear a bandit den, recover a stolen heirloom) proves they're worth blooding.

2. **Proving Honor.** **Skjor** sends the party after a fragment of **Wuuthrad**, with **Farkas** as escort, into **Dustman's Cairn**. Deep in the crypt a lever seals the party behind a gate — and the **Silver Hand** spring their ambush. Farkas **transforms into a werewolf** before the party's eyes, tears the hunters apart, and frees them. *The secret is out:* some Companions are beasts. Fight on through the draugr to the inner chamber, take the **Fragment of Wuuthrad** from the altar (a Word Wall thrums there — ancient noise, nothing the party can use), and carry it home. Kodlak formally inducts the party into the Companions.

3. **The Underforge.** **Skjor** wants a word away from Kodlak's ears. After dark he leads the party beneath the Skyforge to the **Underforge**, where **Aela** waits in beast form by a stone basin. This is the **offering of the beast-blood**: Skjor opens Aela's wolf-vein into the font and invites the party to drink and join the Circle. **This is the campaign's first real fork — see *The Beast-Blood* below.** Whoever drinks blacks out and comes to outside the walls, having run as a wolf in the night, with Aela grinning beside the kill.

4. **The Silver Hand's Den.** Skjor means to strike the Silver Hand while the party's blood is hot — he marches on their stronghold at **Gallows Rock** ahead of the party. By the time the party and Aela catch up, the hunters were ready for him: the party fights through cages of tortured, dying werewolves and the hunter-captain **Krev the Skinner**, and finds **Skjor dead**, hung for skinning. The war with the Silver Hand is now blood for blood.

5. **Blood's Honor.** Kodlak takes the party aside and confesses what eats at him: the Circle's lycanthropy came from a **pact with the Glenmoril Witches**, and a beast-blooded soul belongs to **Hircine's Hunting Grounds**, not Sovngarde. He wants the **cure**, for himself and for any who'll take it — and the cure begins with a **witch's head**. Send the party to the **Glenmoril Coven** in the Falkreath pines to kill a witch and take her head. (A thorough party takes *several* heads — one per Circle member who might want curing later.)

6. **The Fall of Jorrvaskr.** The party returns to find **Jorrvaskr attacked and Kodlak dead** — the Silver Hand struck the hall while the Circle was scattered, killed the old man, and **stole the recovered Fragments of Wuuthrad**. This beat is the hinge: Kodlak's death is not preventable. The hall is in mourning and fury; **Vilkas** and **Aela** are wild with grief.

7. **Purity of Revenge.** With **Vilkas**, hunt the Silver Hand to their den at **Driftshade Refuge** near Dawnstar. Cut through the hunters and their traps, put down the caged beasts, and **recover the stolen Fragments of Wuuthrad**. Vilkas tells the party to bring everything to the Skyforge — it is time to lay the Harbinger to rest.

8. **Glory of the Dead.** At Kodlak's pyre, the party gives every fragment to **Eorlund**, who **reforges Wuuthrad** on the Skyforge. The Circle — **Aela, Farkas, Vilkas**, and the party — carry the axe north to **Ysgramor's Tomb** in Winterhold. Set Wuuthrad in the hands of Ysgramor's statue to open the inner door. Fight through Companion ghosts and skittering frostbite spiders to the **Flame of the Harbinger**, where **Kodlak's ghost** waits, still beast-tangled. Cast a **Glenmoril witch's head** into the Flame: Kodlak's wolf-spirit tears loose as a beast to be put down. Defeat it, and the old man is **freed to Sovngarde**. The Companions name the party **Harbinger**.
