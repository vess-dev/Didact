### Acolyte Priests (Ahzidal · Dukaan · Zahkriisos)

12 HP, 1 Armor, 8 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, life-draining touch (d8), elemental bolt (d8, _blast_)

- Run each as a **Dragon Priest** from the Bestiary — fire for Ahzidal, frost for Dukaan, shock for Zahkriisos.
- Miraak's three masked dragon-priests of Solstheim, each sealed in a ruin of the island. Magic against them is _Impaired_; immune to poison. They keep their distance, cast Destruction bolts and wards, and raise nearby corpses.
- **Critical Damage**: a death-curse strikes the target for 1d6 WIL. Each turns to dust when slain, leaving its mask (see **Unique Armors**).
