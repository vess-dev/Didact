- **The Sacrament.** The party passes through Windhelm and hears of the orphan boy who has been praying for the Brotherhood behind a locked door (Beat 1).
- **A background tie.** A PC with the Dark Brotherhood Assassin background is already family: they start at **Dark Brotherhood +1**, and it is *they* whom Astrid sends to bring the rest of the party to the shack.
- **A stolen kill.** The party kills someone — a bandit, a thug, a cruel master — whose death the Brotherhood had already been paid for. Astrid comes to collect.
