#### Imperial Captain / Stormcloak Officer

8 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 12 WIL, sword (d8) or war axe (d8), shield (counted in Armor)

- The veteran who holds a fort's gate or anchors a battle-line — the named foe at the end of a fort-skirmish (Beat 5). Tougher commanders climb in HP and Armor (to the cap of 3) and die.
