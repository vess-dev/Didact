### Knight-Paladin Gelebor

12 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 15 DEX, 15 WIL, enchanted Falmer-make sword (d8), bound bow (d8, _ranged_)

- The last living Snow Elf, ancient guardian of the chantry of Auri-El in the Forgotten Vale — sorrowful, courteous, the keeper of **Auriel's Bow**. An ally if the party will avenge the Vale.
- His attacks against the undead are _Enhanced_ and shine with sunlight (ignore 1 Armor). He gives up the Bow only once Vyrthur is dead.
