### Storn Crag-Strider (Skaal shaman)

6 HP, 8 STR, 8 DEX, 16 WIL, staff (d6)

- An aged, peaceful man; his power is rite and lore, not the blade. He works the All-Maker rite that grants *the Voice of the Wind* (Beat 4) and, in Beat 7, opens his mind to Hermaeus Mora to spare his people, dying as the Prince drains him. A quest-giver and a sacrifice, not a combatant; do not throw him into a fight.
