A campaign of scholars and secrets, for a party who would rather solve a magical catastrophe than swing a sword at it — though there is plenty of swinging to be done in the deep places. The party joins the last free school of magic in Skyrim, helps unearth a god's left-behind Eye beneath a frozen ruin, and watches a quiet Thalmor "advisor" turn that Eye into a weapon that murders the Arch-Mage and threatens to crack the province open. To stop him they must descend into the worst tomb in Hjaalmarch, take the **Staff of Magnus** from a dragon priest four thousand years dead, and bring it back to the Hall of the Elements for a final duel. The survivor of that duel becomes Arch-Mage.

This frame assumes the party can cast — at least one PC should carry a Spell Tome, and the rest should be the sort who fall in with mages. It runs equally well as the campaign's spine or as a self-contained arc dropped into a wider Skyrim game.
