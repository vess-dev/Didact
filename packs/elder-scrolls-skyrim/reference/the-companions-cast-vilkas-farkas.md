### Vilkas & Farkas (Circle twins)

9 HP, 2 Armor, 15 STR, 13 DEX, 11 WIL, Skyforge greatsword (d12); Beast Form claws (d6+d6), bite (d8)

- Twins and opposites: **Vilkas** thinks and seethes, **Farkas** is broad and kind. Both beast-blooded; both can be cured if they choose.
- Mundane attacks against Beast Form are _Impaired_, silver _Enhanced_; immune to disease. Either can become a follower after the questline.
- **Shield-Brother**: once per day, an ally fighting within arm's reach of a twin re-rolls a failed STR or WIL save to hold their ground.
