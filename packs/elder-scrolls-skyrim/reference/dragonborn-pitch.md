Four thousand years ago a Nord dragon-priest named **Miraak** was the first mortal to bear a dragon's soul. He learned to bend the will of dragons, betrayed his masters, and was dragged into the library-hell of **Apocrypha** by the Daedric Prince he served. He never died. Now his cultists hunt the roads of Skyrim for the "false Dragonborn," and on the island of **Solstheim** his enthralled servants chant in their sleep and haul stone to rebuild his temple. He means to walk back into the world through the corrupted **All-Maker Stones**, and he means to take the Last Dragonborn's soul to do it.

The cultists believe that soul belongs to one of the party. (If your table's Dragonborn is a PC touched by Akatosh, they are right; if the Last Dragonborn is an NPC ally, the cult has simply come for the wrong people — or for the people standing beside the right one. Run the frame the same way.)

To stop Miraak the party must free Solstheim's people, win the trust of the **Skaal**, and follow the **Black Books** into Apocrypha to confront **Hermaeus Mora**, Prince of forbidden knowledge — Miraak's old patron, and a far worse one. Mora will give them the power to face Miraak. He will name a price for it that no decent person should pay, and it will be paid anyway.
