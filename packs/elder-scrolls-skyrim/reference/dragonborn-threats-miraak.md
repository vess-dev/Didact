### Miraak (the First Dragonborn)

18 HP, 3 Armor, 13 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, Miraak's Sword (d8), Miraak's Staff (d8, _blast_)

- He fights at his tower's summit, wreathed in green light, his mask and robes drinking spells before they land. Magic against him is _Impaired_, and the first spell that would harm him each scene simply does not.
- He commands the dragons of Apocrypha: **three are at the summit — Kruziikrel, Relonikiv, and the party's own tamed Sahrotaar — and each time he is brought to 0 HP he devours one to heal to full HP**. Once per combat a foe his sword strikes acts _Impaired_ next round; his staff (3 charges) erupts the ground in tentacles (d8, _blast_; STR save or be **slowed**).
- **Critical Damage**: he draws the target's soul toward his own — 1d6 WIL, and Miraak regains that many HP.
- **The discarding (scripted).** Miraak cannot be finished while a dragon lives to heal him. Once all three are spent and the party has worn him down to 0 HP again, **Hermaeus Mora's tentacles burst up through the floor and kill him outright** — narrate the Prince's strike rather than a clean kill.
