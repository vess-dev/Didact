Five locations anchor the arc; the contracts in Beat 4 may range across any Hold.

- **The Abandoned Shack** *(Hjaalmarch coast)* — a single rotting room, one shuttered window, three kneeling captives, and a closed door behind Astrid. The whole branch happens here. Claustrophobic; the party should feel they cannot leave without a choice.
- **The Falkreath Sanctuary** *(the pine forest)* — the family's home in the cliffs behind a **Black Door** that asks a riddle and punishes the wrong answer. Inside: a central hearth, sleeping alcoves, an alchemy nook (Babette's), a training room, the Night Mother's standing coffin. Warm, lived-in, doomed. Burns in Beat 8.
- **Volunruud** *(the Pale)* — an ancient, half-collapsed Nordic tomb where the conspiracy is hatched and, later, where the blood-money urn is hidden at the dead-drop. Cold stone and old draugr; a place for whispered treason.
- **The Dawnstar Sanctuary** *(the Pale, on the coast)* — a long-sealed older Sanctuary, its Black Door asking *"What is life's greatest illusion?"* (the answer: *"Innocence, my brother."*) Cicero flees here; the family rebuilds here. It begins ruined and ends restored — a visual rhyme for the campaign.
- **The *Katariah*** *(anchored off Solitude)* — the Emperor's great warship, boarded from the freezing water. Decks of Penitus Oculatus guards, a flooded hold, narrow companionways, and at the top a quiet cabin and an old man who will not run. The hidden scimitar **Windshear** is aboard.
