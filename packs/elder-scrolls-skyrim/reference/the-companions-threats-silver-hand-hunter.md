### Silver Hand Hunter

6 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 12 DEX, 9 WIL, silver sword (d8, _silver_) or hunting bow (d6)

- Grim, fur-and-mail werewolf-hunters who lair in forts hung with pelts. Bands of four or more are a _detachment_.
- Their blades are _silver_ — **_Enhanced_ against any beast-blooded PC**, in beast form or out. A band is led by a captain in heavier mail (2 Armor, silver greatsword d10).
