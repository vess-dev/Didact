### Nocturnal — the Daedric Prince

*(not a combatant — a presence)*

- The Daedric Prince of shadow, luck, and the night, patron of all thieves; she manifests in the oath chamber of the Nightingale Hall and again before the **Ebonmere** in the Twilight Sepulcher. Aloof, amused, and entirely transactional — the Nightingales serve her, not the reverse.
- **Not a Fight**: Nocturnal is never an enemy and cannot be harmed. She is the bargain itself: she grants the Nightingale oath (Beat 7) and her blessing (Beat 9), and her favor — the Guild's luck — lives or dies by whether the **Skeleton Key** sits in its shrine.
