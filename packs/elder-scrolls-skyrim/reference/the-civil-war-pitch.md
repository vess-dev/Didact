The **White-Gold Concordat** that ended the Great War bought the Empire peace at a price: the worship of **Talos** — the Nord hero-emperor turned god — was outlawed across the Empire, to satisfy the elves of the **Aldmeri Dominion**. To a devout Nord this is the theft of a god. **Ulfric Stormcloak**, Jarl of Windhelm, made it a war: he challenged High King Torygg to single combat and killed him, and now leads the **Stormcloaks** — the Sons and Daughters of Skyrim — to throw off the Empire, win the province its independence, and worship Talos in the open.

Against him stand **General Tullius** and the **Imperial Legion**, sent from Cyrodiil to crush the rebellion and hold Skyrim together under one law and one crown. To the Legion, Ulfric is a king-killer and an opportunist whose rising weakens the only power that can stand against the elves when the Concordat's truce inevitably breaks.

The party joins one side and fights it to the end. The war moves Hold by Hold — fort skirmishes and contested ground, the hinge **Battle for Whiterun**, then a grinding reconquest, and finally an assault on the enemy's own capital: **Solitude** for the Stormcloaks, **Windhelm** for the Legion. When the dust settles, Jarls have fallen and been replaced, a victor sits the throne, and the common folk sing the party's name (or curse it).
