- **Alduin** — the World-Eater, eldest dragon, firstborn of Akatosh; raises the dead dragons and means to devour the world. The campaign's final foe (stat block in Threats).
- **Paarthurnax** — the ancient dragon who leads the Greybeards from the summit; once Alduin's lieutenant, he rebelled and taught mortals the Voice. Slow, sad, wise. The party's greatest dragon ally — *unless* the Blades force the issue (stat block in Threats, for the optional fight only).
- **Arngeir** — spokesman of the **Greybeards** and the only one of the four who speaks freely (the others' Voices would level the room). Stern, devout, the party's tutor in the Way of the Voice. Hosts the Season Unending truce.
- **Delphine** — one of the last **Blades**; sharp, suspicious, hidden as a Riverwood innkeeper. Tests the party, reforms the order, and later demands Paarthurnax's death.
- **Esbern** — the last Blades loremaster, hiding in the Ratway; the man who reads Alduin's Wall and knows where the World-Eater draws his strength. Frail, brilliant, half-broken by years of running.
- **Odahviing** — a great red dragon, proud and loyal to Alduin until the party out-fights and out-thinks him. Trapped at Dragonsreach, he becomes the party's mount to Skuldafn and a grudging ally (stat block in Threats).
- **Jarl Balgruuf the Greater** — Jarl of Whiterun; tries to keep his Hold out of the war while a dragon burns the countryside. Makes the party Thane; reluctantly lends Dragonsreach for the trap.
- **Ralof / Hadvar** — the Stormcloak and the Imperial soldier who each offer to lead the party out of Helgen. Whichever the party follows seeds the **Civil War** frame.
- **Gormlaith Golden-Hilt, Hakon One-Eye, Felldir the Old** — the three ancient heroes who first beat Alduin, now in the Hall of Valor. They join the party for the last battle, and *their* Voices are Dragonrend in the finale.
- **Tsun** — shield-thane of Shor, guardian of the whale-bone bridge into the Hall of Valor; duels the party for the right to pass (stat block in Threats).
- **General Tullius & Ulfric Stormcloak** — the leaders of the two sides of the civil war; present at Helgen, and across the table at Season Unending. Their war is the backdrop and the one obstacle that can block the trap at Dragonsreach.
