**Sphere:** pestilence, contamination, tasks, the natural order.

**Hook.** A wretched, plague-rotted wanderer — one of the **Afflicted** — directs the party to **Kesh the Clean**, a devotee tending the small **Shrine to Peryite** in the wild Reach, who can let them speak with his Prince.

**The bargain & the twist.** Peryite, the lowest of the Princes — Lord of pestilence and dull order — has a grievance. One of his own monks, **Orchendor**, gathered the plague-touched faithful into the Dwemer ruin of **Bthardamz** and then "lost his way," defying the natural order Peryite embodies. The task: kill Orchendor in the Prince's name. The twist is the awful devotion of it all — the Afflicted *worship* the disease that is rotting them alive, calling their agony a blessing; Orchendor promised them comfort and delivered only deeper misery; and the party is sent to execute him not for that cruelty but simply because he stopped obeying. To brew the incense that summons Peryite, the party must first gather grim reagents (vampire dust, a silver ingot, a flawless ruby, deathbell).

**Beats.**

1. **The incense.** Gather the four reagents across Skyrim; bring them to Kesh.
2. **The Prince's voice.** Inhale the fumes at the shrine; Peryite names Orchendor and his crime (disobedience).
3. **Bthardamz.** Descend the plague-ridden Dwemer ruin — the Afflicted, and Dwarven automatons — to the Arcanex.
4. **The execution.** Slay Orchendor; return to the shrine and breathe the fumes once more to claim the prize.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Kesh the Clean** — Khajiit incense-brewer; ally.
- **Orchendor** — a Bosmer spellsword and apostate; *run as a* **Conjurer** *(but tougher: 8 HP, immune to fire, frost, and shock, and able to turn briefly invisible to reposition).* A real boss.
- The ruin teems with **Afflicted** (run as **Bandits**) and **Dwarven Spider**/**Sphere** automatons.

**Reward.** **Spellbreaker** — see its card in *Unique Armors*. The great ward-shield that turns hostile magic aside.
