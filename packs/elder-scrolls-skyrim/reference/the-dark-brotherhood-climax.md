The Emperor is alone in his cabin and the way out is the way you came, through the cold and the guards. Three shapes the ending can take:

- **The Listener.** Make the kill, brave the freezing water again, and slip away. Settle accounts with Amaund Motierre in Whiterun (his own death cleans the ledger) and claim the urn of Septims at the Volunruud dead-drop. Return to Dawnstar, gather the family that survived, and reopen the Black Door. The Brotherhood lives because the party rebuilt it.
- **The Hollow Crown.** Kill the Emperor but let Maro and Motierre live, or fail to gather the survivors. The contract is fulfilled and the purse is paid, but the family is a handful of ghosts and the party is a Listener with no one left to lead. A bleak, *earned* ending — the Warden describes the empty hearth.
- **The Empty Coffin.** If the party spared the Emperor at his asking, or could not bring themselves to the kill at all, the conspiracy collapses, the Penitus Oculatus closes in, and the last Sanctuary dies with its work undone. The Night Mother goes silent. Sithis is not mocked twice.
