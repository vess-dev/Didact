### Kodlak's Wolf Spirit (at the Flame)

12 HP, 1 Armor, 16 STR, 14 DEX, 8 WIL, spectral claws (d6+d6), bite (d8)

- The beast torn loose from Kodlak's soul when a Glenmoril head burns in the Flame of the Harbinger — a ghostly, savage wolf-shape, all the Hunt and none of the man.
- A creature of the Hunting Grounds; mundane weapons strike it normally here, on hallowed barrow ground. Slaying it frees Kodlak.
- **Critical Damage**: it bears the target to the ground and savages them for 1d6 more STR.
