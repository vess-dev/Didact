- **Miraak** — the First Dragonborn, a Nord dragon-priest of the old cult who turned on his masters and was taken into Apocrypha by Hermaeus Mora four thousand years ago. Proud, cold, and certain he is owed the world. He wears a tentacled mask and robes steeped in Mora's influence, and carries a writhing sword and staff. *Stat block under Threats.*
- **Frea** — Storn's daughter, fighting to free her people; joins the party at the Temple and stays to the end. Brave, blunt, devoted to the All-Maker and her father. Becomes the village's shaman after Storn's death.
- **Storn Crag-Strider** — the Skaal shaman, keeper of the knowledge needed to defeat Miraak and the rite that frees the first stone. He understands from the first that Mora's bargain will cost him his life, and he chooses to pay it so his people are spared. The campaign's heart.
- **Neloth** — Master Wizard of House Telvanni at Tel Mithryn: brilliant, ancient, and serenely self-absorbed. He studies the Stones as fascinating problems rather than tragedies, and alone knows where the second Black Book lies. A reliable, profoundly amoral ally.
- **Hermaeus Mora** — the Demon of Knowledge: a horizon of green tentacles and a thousand eyes, speaking in many overlapping voices. He owns Apocrypha and everything read into it. He uses Miraak, discards Miraak, and would use the party exactly the same way. **Not a fightable stat block** (see below).
- **Lleril Morvayn** — the weary Dunmer Councilor of Raven Rock; a source of rumor, the Earth Stone problem, and lodging.
- **Gjalund Salt-Sage** — master of the *Northern Maiden*; the party's ride to and from Solstheim, and a nervous source of first impressions.
