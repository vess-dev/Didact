#### Stormcloak Soldier

5 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 12 DEX, 10 WIL, war axe (d8) or hunting bow (d6)

- Fur-and-mail rebels, mostly Nords, fighting for Talos and a free Skyrim. Bands of four or more are a _detachment_; a unit fields a standard-bearer and an officer in heavier mail (2 Armor, sword d8).
- On home ground (Eastmarch, the Pale, the Rift, Winterhold) they re-roll their first failed **Morale** save — they believe.
