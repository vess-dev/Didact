### Maven Black-Briar (patron)

5 HP, 9 STR, 10 DEX, 15 WIL, no weapon (she commands those who fight)

- The Black-Briar matriarch who truly rules Riften, mead-rich and venomous, the Guild's patron and paymaster. The party works for her long before they understand they do.
- **Long Reach**: Crossing Maven raises the party's Riften Bounty and can turn the city guard hostile. Pleasing her is worth a step of Thieves Guild Reputation and a quiet word in the Jarl's ear. If the Imperials later take the Rift, she is installed as Jarl — and a Guild Master she favors profits accordingly.
