*Gothic horror in a frozen north. This is not the bright heroism of slaying a dragon; it is candlelit, bloody, and morally gray. The Dawnguard are righteous and half-mad with it; the Volkihar are elegant monsters who genuinely believe they are owed the world. Serana is the heart of the whole thing — a girl out of time, betrayed by her own parents, who must learn to choose her own side. Whichever way the party leans, make them feel the weight of what they're becoming.*

**Touchstones:** classic vampire-castle gothic (Dracula's Carfax and Castle), the cold sublime of a glacier valley, an order of grim hunters in a fallen keep, a daughter caught between a tyrant father and an exiled mother.

**Lean into:** the cold. Snow that muffles the world, a castle of black stone over the Sea of Ghosts, the dead violet twilight of the Soul Cairn, the impossible blue-and-gold of the Forgotten Vale. Under-describe the gore and over-describe the *quiet* — the worst things here happen in candlelight, politely. Let Serana grow on the party. Let Harkon be charming before he is terrible.
