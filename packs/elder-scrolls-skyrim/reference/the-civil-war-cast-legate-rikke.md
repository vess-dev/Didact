#### Legate Rikke

8 HP, 2 Armor, 15 STR, 14 DEX, 13 WIL, Imperial sword (d8) or Imperial bow (d6), Legion shield (counted in Armor)

- Tullius's second and the Legion's recruiter in Skyrim — and a Nord herself, who worships Talos in private and fights for the Empire anyway because she believes a fractured Skyrim cannot survive what is coming. The war's tragedy wears her face: she dies a patriot's death at Solitude if the rebellion wins.
- She assigns the party's missions through the Imperial campaign and stands beside them in every great battle.
