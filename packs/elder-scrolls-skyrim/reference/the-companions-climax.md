The Flame of the Harbinger in Ysgramor's Tomb, Wuuthrad reforged and set in the founder's hands, the Circle at the party's back, and Kodlak's beast-tangled ghost waiting. Cast a Glenmoril witch's head into the Flame and the old man's wolf-spirit tears loose to be put down. Beat it, and Kodlak walks free to Sovngarde at last. Then the party decides what they are:

- **Embrace the wolf.** The party keep the beast-blood (or take it now, at the font, if they held back). The Companions ride on as Hircine's own — fierce, feared, and damned to the Hunting Grounds when they fall. Aela is glad of it. The Ring of Hircine, if found, is theirs to make the wolf limitless.
- **Seek the cure.** With heads enough, the beast-blooded PCs burn the curse out of themselves as they did for Kodlak — mortal again, Sovngarde-bound, the bargain with Hircine broken. The Companions become what Kodlak wanted them to be: warriors, not beasts.
- **A divided hall.** The party need not agree. Some keep the wolf, some take the cure, some never drank — and the Companions go on as a brotherhood that holds together across the difference, the way it always did. Either way, the chair at the head of the table is the party's now, and Jorrvaskr's doors stand open to all comers who can swing a blade.
