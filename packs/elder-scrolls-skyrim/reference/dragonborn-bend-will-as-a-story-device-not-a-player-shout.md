The source quest hinges on the **Bend Will** Shout, by which the hero cleanses the All-Maker Stones, dominates creatures, and tames the dragon Sahrotaar. **The Elder Scrolls 2e has no player Thu'um, and this campaign adds none.** Reframe Bend Will as a *granted, scene-specific power*, never a learned ability the party keeps:

- **As a Skaal rite (recommended).** With the lost words brought back from **Saering's Watch** (Beat 4), **Storn Crag-Strider** works an All-Maker rite that lays a blessing on the party — *the Voice of the Wind* — usable **only to cleanse a corrupted All-Maker Stone**: stand before the stone, speak the words Storn taught, and Miraak's hold shatters (no save; it simply works, and a Lurker rises in answer). It does nothing else, and it fades when the last of the five outer stones is freed.
- **As a one-time gift of the Black Books.** The deeper words — dominating a creature (Beat 5, optional, to turn a freed thrall or beast to the party's side for one scene) and taming **Sahrotaar** (Beat 8) — are **read out of the Black Books** at the moment they are needed. Treat each as a single scripted use granted by the fiction (Mora *wants* the party to reach Miraak), spent the instant it is used. The party never rolls to "use Bend Will," never carries it between scenes, and leaves Apocrypha with no Shout.

If a player asks to keep the power: it is Mora's, not theirs, and it stays in Apocrypha with everything else the Prince owns. The flavor *is* the mechanic.
