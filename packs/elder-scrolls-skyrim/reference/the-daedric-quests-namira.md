**Sphere:** hunger, decay, death, revulsion.

**Hook.** In **Markarth**, the **Hall of the Dead** has been sealed and its keeper, **Brother Verulus**, is frightened: something has been *eating* the corpses. Persuade him to let the party investigate.

**The bargain & the twist.** The trail leads to **Reachcliff Cave** and to **Eola**, a charming cannibal who serves **Namira**, Prince of the repulsive and the devouring. Eola invites the party to clear the cave of its draugr — and then to join her coven's feast. The price of Namira's ring is the meal: the party must lure the kindly, trusting **Brother Verulus** to the shrine and **sacrifice him**, then sit down with the cult and **eat him**. This is a quiet, intimate horror — no army to fight, only a good man led to slaughter by his own faith in you, and a circle of well-dressed Markarth citizens waiting with the cutlery. Walking away leaves the coven hungry and the party clean.

**Beats.**

1. **The defiled hall.** Investigate the Hall of the Dead; follow the desecration to Reachcliff Cave.
2. **Eola's coven.** Clear the cave (draugr); Eola reveals the cult and the feast.
3. **The lure.** Coax Brother Verulus to follow you to the shrine.
4. **The feast.** Sacrifice and consume him at Namira's altar — or refuse.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Brother Verulus** — gentle priest of Arkay; the trusting victim. *Run as a* **Necromancer** *(6 HP) only if somehow provoked.*
- **Eola** and the coven (Markarth folk: Lisbet, Banning, Hogni, and others) — *run as* **Bandit**s if a fight breaks out, e.g. if the party turns on the cult instead.
- **Reachcliff Cave** is full of **Draugr** (and perhaps a **Draugr Overlord**).

**Reward.** **Ring of Namira** — see its card in *Unique Armors*. Feeding on a fresh corpse heals STR and clears Fatigue — at a cost to Renown.
