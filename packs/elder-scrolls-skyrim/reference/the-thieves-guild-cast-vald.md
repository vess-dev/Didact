### Vald (Mercer's guard)

8 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 9 WIL, war axe (d8), crossbow (d8, _bulky_)

- A surly mercenary set to guard Riftweald Manor (Beat 6), in debt to Maven and looking for a way out. Can be fought, bribed, or turned by clearing his debt to Maven.
