*A family drama wearing a horror mask. The Dark Brotherhood is monstrous, and the campaign should never pretend otherwise — these are people who murder strangers for coin and call it worship. But the Sanctuary is also warm: Nazir's dry jokes, Babette's centuries-old patience, Festus's bile, Astrid's fierce loyalty to her "children." The Warden's job is to make the party love the family right up until the fire takes it. Keep the dark tone, keep it tasteful — the dread is in the quiet, the ritual, and the consequences, not in gore for its own sake.*

**Lean into:** the rituals (the Black Sacrament, the Black Door's password, "Sweet Mother, sweet Mother…"), the contrast of a cozy hearth over a charnel purpose, and the slow turn from *taking orders* to *hearing the Void itself.* The reward of the join-path is power and a found family; its cost is everything you do to keep them.

**Hard limits for this frame:** no player Shouts; Cairn balance throughout — Armor never above 3, dice d4–d12. Let the murders happen off the page when the table prefers.
