Any one of these drops a party into Brynjolf's eye-line in the Riften market:

- **The mark.** A PC is a thief, cutpurse, or burglar (the *Thieves Guild Burglar*, *Cutpurse*, or *Nightingale* background) and word of a quiet score in Riften draws them to the canal town. Brynjolf has been watching for new talent. (A Thieves Guild Burglar already belongs to the Guild, and starts at **Thieves Guild +1**.)
- **The debt.** A PC owes coin to the **Black-Briars** or to a Riften loan-shark; Maven offers to wipe the slate clean if they do "a little work" for a friend of hers in the market.
- **The errand.** The party comes to Riften chasing something unrelated — a stolen heirloom, a fence, a rumor — and Brynjolf, sizing them up, offers them a fast hundred Septims to help him fleece a vendor. ("You look like you could use the coin, and I could use a pair of hands.")
- **The exile's letter.** A PC receives a cryptic note from a Dunmer they have never met (Karliah, watching from the shadows), warning that "the Guild you are about to join is not what it was." The note makes sense only much later.
