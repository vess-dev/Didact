### Delvin Mallory

6 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 12 WIL, dagger (d6)

- A grizzled Breton veteran of the Cistern who dispenses the Guild's bread-and-butter jobs and frets over the "curse." Hands out work, gear, and gossip; never the threat.
- **Old Hand**: Knows a fence, a back door, or a name for any city in Skyrim — the Warden may use him to hand the party a lead when they stall.
