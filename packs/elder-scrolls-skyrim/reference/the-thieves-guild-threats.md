For the dungeons, draw on the **Bestiary**. Snow Veil Sanctum (Beat 4) uses **Draugr**, **Draugr Overlord**, and **Frostbite Spider**. The Ratway and the meadery cellar use **Skeever** and **Bandit**. Irkngthand (Beat 8) is a wall of **Dwarven Spider**, **Dwarven Sphere**, and **Dwarven Centurion** automatons backed by **Falmer**, **Falmer Nightprowler**, **Falmer Warmonger**, and **Chaurus**. Goldenglow and Riftweald use **Bandit** (as hired mercenaries). Mercer Frey is the only campaign-specific boss — see the Cast.
