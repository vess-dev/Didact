### Quaranir and the Psijic Order

10 HP, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 16 WIL, no attack of their own (they simply *stop time*)

- Robed monks of the ancient, half-mythical **Psijic Order** of the Isle of Artaeum, who appear and vanish where they please and freeze time around themselves to speak unheard. They watch the Eye from afar and intervene only with warnings — they will not fight the party's battles for them.
- A Psijic can halt time in a scene: all other action stops while they deliver a message, and the party cannot act against them. At the very end they take the Eye away to safekeeping "where no mortal hand can misuse it."
