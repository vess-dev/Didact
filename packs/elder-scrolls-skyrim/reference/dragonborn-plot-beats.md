Broad strokes, six to nine sessions. The spine is: **free the island → earn the Skaal → walk Apocrypha → pay Mora's price → kill Miraak.** The All-Maker Stones (Beat 5) can be taken in any order; everything else is a gate.

1. **The False Dragonborn.** Cultists attack on the Skyrim mainland. The party reads the note, finds the *Northern Maiden*, and sails for Raven Rock. **(Gate: leaving Skyrim.)**
2. **Raven Rock & the Earth Stone.** A weathered Dunmer mining colony of House Redoran under the ash, ruled by Councilor Lleril Morvayn behind the great **Bulwark**. Miraak's name is on every tongue, spoken with fear or with the flat calm of the entranced. At the **Earth Stone** outside the walls, glassy-eyed townsfolk pile stone onto a half-built shrine and cannot be reasoned with. The party meets the Telvanni wizard **Neloth**, who studies the phenomenon with magnificent indifference to the people suffering it. The trail leads inland to the **Temple of Miraak**.
3. **The Temple of Miraak.** A buried dragon-cult temple where enthralled Skaal labor in the dark. Here the party meets **Frea**, a Skaal warrior fighting to reach her people, who joins them. Deep below lies the first **Black Book**, *Waking Dreams*. Reading it pulls the reader bodily into **Apocrypha**, where **Miraak** appears — contemptuous, untouchable, *real* — and casts them back out. From now on, **whenever the party kills a dragon, Miraak's shade may appear and tear its soul away** before the Dragonborn can take it — a recurring beat, not a mechanic, that shows his reach.
4. **The Fate of the Skaal.** Frea leads the party north to the **Skaal Village** on the snowbound north coast, where her father **Storn Crag-Strider**, the village shaman, holds the last people on the island still free of Miraak's spell — barely. Storn explains what must be done: the **All-Maker Stones** that ring the island have been corrupted into the engines of Miraak's return, and they must be cleansed. He sends the party to **Saering's Watch** to face a dragon and recover the lost words needed to do it. **(See "Bend Will, as a Story Device" below — this is where the first cleansing power is granted, *not* learned as a Shout.)**
5. **Cleansing the Stones.** Corrupted stones stand across Solstheim — the **Wind Stone, Beast Stone, Sun Stone, Water Stone,** and the **Earth Stone** at Raven Rock. The sixth, the **Tree Stone** at the Temple's heart, stays in Miraak's grip until he dies (Beat 9). At each, the party uses the granted power to shatter Miraak's hold; the laborers wake confused, and something rises to punish the intrusion — a **Lurker** heaves up out of black water at most stones. Freeing the Beast Stone wins over a tribe of **Rieklings**; freeing the Earth Stone lifts the pall over Raven Rock. Each freed stone leaves a faint blessing on the land (see Rewards). **(Gate: the island must be freed before Mora will deal.)**
6. **The Path of Knowledge.** With the stones free, only the Black Book that taught Miraak to bend dragons can show the way to him — and **Neloth** knows where it is: drowned in the flooded Dwemer ruin of **Nchardak**. The party restores the ruin's ancient steam-engines (a delve of water, clockwork, and Dwarven automatons) to raise the reading-room and claim the second Black Book, *Epistolary Acumen*.
7. **The Gardener of Men — the Price.** Reading *Epistolary Acumen* brings the party before **Hermaeus Mora** himself: a horizon of green tentacles and unblinking eyes. He will grant the final measure of power to face Miraak — but he wants payment in kind: **the secret knowledge the Skaal have guarded for four thousand years**, the very thing that has kept them free of him. Only **Storn** can give it. When the party brings the Black Book to the village, Storn takes it, opens his mind to the Prince so his people will be spared — and **Mora kills him**, draining the secrets and the man together, in front of his daughter. Frea, grieving, tells the party to end Miraak. **(This is the emotional climax. Do not rush it. Storn knows the price from Beat 4 and chooses it.)**
8. **At the Summit of Apocrypha.** The party reads *Waking Dreams* one last time and descends the green chapters of Apocrypha toward Miraak's tower, hunted by **Seekers** and **Lurkers**. At the final reach, a granted word **tames the dragon Sahrotaar** — who serves Miraak, and now turns — and he carries them to the summit.
9. **The Duel.** **Miraak** fights at his tower, healing himself by devouring the dragons he commands. When he is nearly beaten, **Hermaeus Mora reaches up and kills his own champion** — discarding Miraak the instant he is no longer useful — and the soul Miraak spent four thousand years hoarding goes free. See **Climax**.
