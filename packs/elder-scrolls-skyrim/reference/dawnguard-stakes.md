The sun itself. If the Tyranny of the Sun is fulfilled, daylight fails over Skyrim and the Volkihar hunt the living forever; if it is thwarted, a vampire court four thousand years old is broken. Between those ends stands a woman out of time who must choose between her father, her mother, and the strangers who woke her — and a party that may end the campaign as hunters, or as monsters.
