*Candlelight, cold stone, and the hum of something too big to understand. The College is a place of bickering scholars and quiet wonders — a library guarded by an Orc, an oracle in the cellar, a lecture that ends with a firebolt — and the horror, when it comes, is the horror of knowledge in the wrong hands. Keep it cerebral until it isn't.*

**Touchstones:** the College questline itself; *A Wizard of Earthsea* (a school, a pride, a shadow let loose); Lovecraft's *At the Mountains of Madness* (an expedition finds something older than history); *The Name of the Rose* (a library, a murder, a political visitor).

**Lean into:** the snowbound isolation of Winterhold; the townsfolk's hatred of the mages; Urag's books and the Augur's riddles; the Psijics stopping time to deliver a warning; the ghosts of young Savos Aren and his friends replaying their mistake in Labyrinthian. The Thalmor watch who reads what.
