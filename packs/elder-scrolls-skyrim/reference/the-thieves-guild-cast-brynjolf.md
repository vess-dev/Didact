### Brynjolf

6 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 14 DEX, 13 WIL, dagger (d6), short bow (d6, _bulky_)

- The Guild's silver-tongued recruiter, a red-haired Nord who works the Riften market in plain sight. Loyal to the Guild above any one master.
- **Patter**: once per day, when Brynjolf talks rather than fights, a PC backing his play may re-roll a failed WIL save to persuade or distract. An ally, not an obstacle — he turns Nightingale alongside the party in Beat 7.
