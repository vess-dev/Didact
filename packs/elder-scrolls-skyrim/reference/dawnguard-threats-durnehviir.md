### Durnehviir

18 HP, 3 Armor, 16 STR, 12 DEX, 18 WIL, rotting bite (d12), Corrosive Breath (d10, _blast_), _detachment_

- The undead dragon of the Soul Cairn — green-gray, half-skeletal, bound by old bargain to serve the Ideal Masters and patrol the Boneyard. Bars the way to the Blood Scroll.
- A _detachment_, like every dragon: lone attackers are _Impaired_ unless they strike with _blast_. Immune to poison and paralysis; cannot be soul-trapped. His breath rots flesh and armor alike, and ignores Armor. Each round he may raise a **Boneman** or **Mistman** from the soil to fight beside him.
- **Critical Damage**: the corrosive jaws close and tear the target apart; a slain mortal's soul risks being claimed by the Cairn (Warden's call).
