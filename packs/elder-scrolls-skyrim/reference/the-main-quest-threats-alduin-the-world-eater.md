### Alduin, the World-Eater (final boss)

18 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 14 DEX, 18 WIL, bite (d12), breath (d10, _blast_), meteor storm (d8, _blast_), _detachment_ (as in the Bestiary)

- The firstborn of Akatosh, devourer of the world. Fire and frost damage against him are _Impaired_. He breathes both, calls down a storm of burning stones, and can raise a slain dragon to fight beside him.
- **Critical Damage**: he devours the target's soul. They die, and their soul is his — unless he is slain before the fight ends, which sets it free.
- **Beyond reach until Dragonrend.** In the Time-Wound battle (Beat 8) and the Sovngarde finale (Beat 11), Dragonrend is supplied by the Elder Scroll's vision and then by the three ancient heroes; *while they Shout in concert*, Alduin is forced to earth and the party can fight him as a grounded foe. Without that allied power he simply wheels overhead and flies off again, as he does at Helgen and Kynesgrove. **No player Shout is ever required or granted.**
