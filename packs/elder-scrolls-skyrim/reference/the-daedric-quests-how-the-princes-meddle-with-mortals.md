Every quest here follows the same dark grammar:

- **A hook** pulls the party in: a job, a rumor, a found object, a desperate stranger. The Prince rarely shows their hand first.
- **A bargain** is offered, and it always has a **moral twist** — a betrayal, a murder, a humiliation, a soul sold. Taking the artifact means doing the deed. *Refusing is always a valid ending* — the party simply walks away without the prize, and the Warden should never punish a table for keeping its hands clean.
- **A reward**: the Prince's Artifact, already statted on its catalog card. This module gives each quest its frame and points to the card for the prize.

### Running these frames

- **Power level.** These are mid-to-high arcs. A lone party of fresh characters can attempt one, but the Artifacts are load-bearing — treat each as the capstone of a delve, not a starting handout.
- **The Vigil of Stendarr.** Stendarr's holy order hunts Daedra, their cultists, and anyone trafficking in their relics, whom they name *Abominations* and put to the sword without mercy. Their Skyrim chapter-house south of Dawnstar was lately sacked by the Volkihar vampires, so the Vigil is scattered and desperate — which makes its survivors zealous and unpredictable. A Vigilant makes a fine complication on any of these frames: a rival racing the party to the shrine, an accuser the morning after, or an avenger who tracks the Artifact to the party's door. (Stat block below.)
- **Carrying an Artifact draws eyes.** The Prince who lent it is watching; rival cults covet it; the Vigil wants it destroyed. Note who knows the party carries one. A gift from Oblivion is a leash.
- **Cross-reference — Nocturnal.** The seventeenth Skyrim Artifact, the **Skeleton Key**, is won not here but in the Thieves Guild arc (*Trinity Restored* → *Blindsighted* → *Darkness Returns*), where the party kneels to Nocturnal as a Nightingale. See **The Thieves Guild** and the Skeleton Key's card in **Relics**. Do not duplicate it here.
- **Artifact cards.** Every reward already has a stat card. Weapons, shields, masks, and worn artifacts are in **Unique Weapons** and **Unique Armors**; the wondrous oddments (Oghma Infinium, Azura's/Black Star) are in **Relics**. When a frame names a reward, hand the player the card.
