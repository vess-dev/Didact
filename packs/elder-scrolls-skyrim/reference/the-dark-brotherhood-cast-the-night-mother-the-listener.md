### The Night Mother & the Listener

*(not a combatant — a presence)*

The mummified bride of **Sithis**, carried in Cicero's coffin. She does not move or fight. To her chosen **Listener** alone she speaks, naming contracts drawn from the Black Sacrament prayers of the desperate.

- **The Night Mother's Voice:** once a PC is named Listener (Beat 5), that PC may, once per session, commune with the Night Mother's corpse to receive a true answer to a single question about a contract, a target, or a threat to the family — the Warden answers honestly, in her voice.
- Harming the coffin or speaking the wrong word at a Black Door makes the Void recoil: the offender must pass a WIL save or add a Fatigue and take 1d6 WIL.
