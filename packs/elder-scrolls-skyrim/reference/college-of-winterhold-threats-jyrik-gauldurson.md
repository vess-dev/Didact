### Jyrik Gauldurson (Saarthal)

Run as a **Wight** from the Bestiary (12 HP, 3 Armor, 16 STR, 8 DEX, 12 WIL, ancient greatsword (d10)), who also hurls an elemental bolt (d8, _blast_).

- One of the three treacherous sons of the ancient Arch-Mage Gauldur, entombed at Saarthal. He is shielded by the Eye and takes no damage until **Tolfdir severs the link** — give the party a round or two of fighting through summoned **Skeletons** while Tolfdir works; then the shield drops and the real fight begins.
- **Critical Damage**: a death-curse strikes the target for 1d4 WIL.
