### Nahkriin (the gatekeeper of Skuldafn)

Run as a **Dragon Priest** from the Bestiary: 12 HP, 1 Armor, 8 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, life-draining touch (d8), elemental bolt (d8, _blast_).

- The masked priest who holds the staff that powers Alduin's portal at Skuldafn (Beat 10). Magic against it is _Impaired_; immune to poison; it backpedals, throws Destruction bolts, raises nearby draugr, and shields itself with wards.
- **Critical Damage**: a death-curse strikes the target for 1d6 WIL. When he falls he drops the **Nahkriin** mask and the portal-staff.
