The party reads *Waking Dreams* for the last time and walks the green chapters of Apocrypha to Miraak's tower, riding the tamed **Sahrotaar** to the summit. **Miraak** fights as a legend should — drinking spells, devouring his own dragons to heal, certain to the end that the world is his by right. The party cannot finish him while a dragon lives to mend him; they wear him down anyway, and at the last **Hermaeus Mora kills his own champion** — a casual, total betrayal — and the soul Miraak spent four thousand years hoarding is set loose (into the Dragonborn, if one stands among the party).

They have won. Solstheim is free; the Skaal are saved; the First Dragonborn is ash. But **Storn is dead**, killed to buy that victory, and the Prince who killed him is the one who handed the party their power and now considers them *interesting*. Frea takes up her father's place at the well of the Skaal Village. Neloth goes back to his tower as though nothing of consequence has happened. And somewhere in the green dark, Hermaeus Mora watches the party leave with a thousand patient eyes — pleased, and waiting, because everyone who reads his Books comes back.
