Run each as a small keyed site; the College itself is a hub the party returns to between expeditions.

- **The College & the Hall of the Elements (Winterhold).** A free-standing spire above the Sea of Ghosts, reached by one long ruined bridge with a single gate at the far end, kept by **Faralda**. **The Hall of the Elements** is the central domed chamber where lectures are held and, later, where the Eye is brought and the final duel fought. Off it lie the **Arcanaeum** (Urag's library), the Halls of Countenance and Attainment where the mages live, and the **Arch-Mage's Quarters** at the top — locked until the campaign's end, then the party's.
- **The Midden.** Beneath everything, a frozen warren of forgotten experiments, failed conjurations, and the Augur's prison. Cold counts as a hazard here: a watch in the Midden without a fire leaves a PC Deprived.
- **Saarthal (Winterhold).** One of the oldest cities of Men in Skyrim, sacked by the elves in the dawn of history, now a buried barrow of ice-slick halls and draugr in their alcoves. The College has an active dig here. Past a hall that seals shut as a trap lies the chamber of the **Eye of Magnus**.
- **Fellglow Keep (Whiterun Hold).** A ruined fort overrun by necromancers and their thralls, where the stolen books are held by **the Caller**.
- **Mzulft (Eastmarch).** A sprawling Dwemer ruin in the eastern hills, claimed by the **Synod**. Steam-pipes, brass guardians, and a **Falmer** infestation in the lower deeps. At its crown is the **Oculory**, a great Dwarven light-engine the Synod cannot work.
- **Labyrinthian (Hjaalmarch).** An immense, half-buried Nordic city in the snowy hills of southern Hjaalmarch, the seat of an ancient dragon cult and the worst dungeon in the campaign. At its heart, behind a shield of light, waits **Morokei** and the **Staff of Magnus**.
