### Gabriella

7 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 16 WIL, conjured blade (d6), Conjure Familiar (a wolf-spirit, 4 HP, bite d6 — once per combat)

- A cool, courtly Dunmer conjurer and a steady hand on the conspiracy's planning. Dies in the fire of Beat 8.
- **Summoner:** her conjured familiar fights at her side; while it stands, attacks against Gabriella are _Impaired_ as it interposes.
