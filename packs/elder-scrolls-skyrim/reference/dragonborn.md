*A masked killer falls at your feet whispering a name that is not yours — Miraak — and across the Sea of Ghosts an ash-choked island full of sleepwalkers builds his temple from the foundations of the world. To stop the First of your kind, you will trade with the Demon of Knowledge, and the bargain costs a good man his life.*

> **This is Dragonborn DLC content** — Solstheim, the Skaal, House Telvanni, and the realm of Apocrypha. It draws on the Bestiary, the Unique Weapons and Unique Armors catalogs, and the Solstheim entry in The Holds. Run it for a party that has already faced a dragon or two; it pairs naturally with **the Main Quest** but does not require it.
