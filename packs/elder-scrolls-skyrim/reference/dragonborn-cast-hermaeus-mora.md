### Hermaeus Mora — not a stat block

Mora is the realm of Apocrypha itself, not a creature to be fought. He has no HP and takes no damage; he acts by fiat — granting the word to tame Sahrotaar, demanding the Skaal's secrets, killing Storn, killing Miraak. If players insist on attacking him, their blows pass through tentacle and page and water and accomplish nothing, and the eyes only turn to regard them. **He is a force the party bargains with and cannot defeat.** That is the point.
