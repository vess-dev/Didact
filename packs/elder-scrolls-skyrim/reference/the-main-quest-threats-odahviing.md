### Odahviing (the dragon ally)

Run as an **Elder Dragon** from the Bestiary: 16 HP, 3 Armor, 16 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, bite (d10), Fire Breath (d10, _blast_), _detachment_.

- **As an enemy** (the Dragonsreach fight, Beat 9): a proud red dragon loyal to Alduin. Fight him down to roughly half his HP on the Great Porch, then the ancient yoke is sprung in the fiction and the fight ends with his neck pinned — *do not* require a save or a Shout to close the trap; the party's task is to survive and trip the mechanism. He breathes fire, and fire damage against him is _Impaired_.
- **As an ally** (from Beat 10 on): caged and impressed, Odahviing carries the party to Skuldafn (the only way there) and, over its courtyard, can be asked for one strafing pass: a Fire Breath attack (d10, _blast_) across a group of enemies. He will not follow them through the portal — no dragon can. He is an ally, not a controllable PC mount with its own sheet.
