The Guild's survival, and a debt to a Daedric Prince. If Mercer keeps the Key, the Thieves Guild dies in the dark beneath Riften and Nocturnal's favor is gone from Skyrim's thieves for good; if the party returns it, the luck comes back — and the party inherits a Guild, a Prince's regard, and an oath that binds them in life and after death.
