### Urag gro-Shub — the librarian

8 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 11 DEX, 13 WIL, fists (d4), Destruction bolt (d8, _ranged_)

- A massive, glowering Orc who guards the **Arcanaeum** as a mother guards her children, because to him a damaged book is a death in the family. Gruff, fair, and astonishingly learned.
- Identifies strange tomes, names obscure ruins, and points the party at the lore they need at every step — *if* they treat his books with respect. Wreck a book and his Reaction drops two steps. The single best source of information in the campaign.
