### The Jarls (see The Holds)

The pieces the war moves. Each early Jarl and their war-installed rival is listed in the Beat 5 table above; their fuller portraits are in the Hold gazetteer. The campaign's named Jarls in brief:

- **Balgruuf the Greater** (Whiterun) — the neutral man forced to choose; spared or deposed at the hinge (Beat 4).
- **Elisif the Fair** (Solitude) — young Imperial widow of the slain High King; kept on a leash if the rebellion wins.
- **Vignar Gray-Mane** (Whiterun) — aged rebel ex-Companion, installed by a Stormcloak victory.
- **Brunwulf Free-Winter** (Windhelm) — the fair-minded Nord, friend to the Dunmer, installed by an Imperial victory.
- **Maven Black-Briar** (Riften) — the venomous matriarch the Empire openly enthrones if the Rift flips.
- Plus the deposed and installed of the contested Holds (**Igmund**, **Siddgeir**, **Idgrod**, **Skald**, **Korir**, and their rivals) — flip them as the forts fall.
