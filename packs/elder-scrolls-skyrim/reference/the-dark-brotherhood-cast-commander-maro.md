### Commander Maro (Penitus Oculatus — the Betrayer's Tool)

12 HP, 2 Armor, 15 STR, 14 DEX, 13 WIL, Imperial sword (d8), crossbow (d8)

- The grim commander of the Emperor's bodyguard-spies in Skyrim, hunting the Brotherhood to extinction. He cuts the deal with Astrid that burns the Sanctuary — and the party may hunt him down at the Solitude docks before boarding the *Katariah*. (On the **Destroyer's Path** he is instead the party's paymaster.)
- **Bodyguard's Vigil:** Maro never starts a combat unaware; attacks "from stealth" against him are resolved normally, not _Enhanced_.
- On taking **Critical Damage**, he goes down cursing the day he trusted an assassin's word, and his last shout brings every Oculatus agent within earshot.
