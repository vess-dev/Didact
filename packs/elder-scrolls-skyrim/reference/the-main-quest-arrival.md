A cart, four prisoners, bound hands, a cold morning road into **Helgen** in Falkreath Hold. One of the party was caught crossing the border at the wrong moment; the rest were taken with them or with the Stormcloak ambush the Imperials sprang the day before. **General Tullius** and the High Elf **Elenwen** watch from the walls. The list is read. The block is wet. The axe goes up —

— and **Alduin** comes out of the mountains, black against the sky, and Helgen begins to burn. In the chaos the party runs, freed by either the Stormcloak **Ralof** or the Imperial soldier **Hadvar** (whichever they trust; this also seeds the **Civil War** frame), and fights down through Helgen Keep — scavenging their first real gear from a footlocker, past panicked soldiers, a nest of frostbite spiders, and a bear in the escape tunnel — and out into open country, the dragon already gone north. Down the road is **Riverwood**, and beyond it **Whiterun**, where a Jarl will want to hear that a dragon just burned a town off the map.
