### Festus Krex

7 HP, 12 STR, 11 DEX, 17 WIL, Spell Tome: Firebolt (d8, _ranged_), staff (d6)

- A spiteful, brilliant old mage with no patience and a long memory — the family's destruction-magic specialist and architect of the Gourmet ruse. Dies outside the burning Sanctuary in Beat 8.
- **Spellfire:** as a Spell Tome wielder, his Firebolt may be loosed as a _blast_ once per combat against clustered foes (he adds a Fatigue as normal).
