Reskin from the Bestiary where you can: Volkihar raiders are **Vampires** (Lokil, Malkus, and Harkon's court); their kennels hold **Death Hounds** and **Gargoyles**; Dimhollow is full of **Draugr**; the Soul Cairn is thick with **Bonemen**, **Mistmen**, and **Wrathmen**; the Forgotten Vale crawls with **Falmer**, **Chaurus**, and a **Frost Troll** or two. Cross-reference **Auriel's Bow**, **Zephyr**, **Harkon's Sword**, and the **Dawnguard Rune Axe** in Unique Weapons; **Auriel's Shield** and the **Aetherial Crown** and **Aetherial Shield** in Unique Armors; and the **Aetherial Staff** in Relics.
