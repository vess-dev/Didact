### Tsun (the guardian of the bridge)

15 HP, 3 Armor, 18 STR, 14 DEX, 16 WIL, ancient battleaxe (d10), Shout (d8, _blast_)

- Shield-thane of Shor, who guards the whale-bone bridge to the Hall of Valor in Sovngarde (Beat 11). He fights the party not to kill but to **test** — a duel for the right to pass.
- He fights honorably and yields the moment a PC drops to 0 HP or lands a telling blow, then steps aside and salutes. His Shout knocks a struck target back (STR save or prone). Run him as a hard but winnable single-foe duel, not a fight to the death.
