Everything hinges on **Beat 2**, in the abandoned shack:

- **Join the Brotherhood** — the party "pays the debt" by killing one of the three bound captives at Astrid's order. The Black Door opens; they become family. This unlocks the full arc (Beats 3–10) and all its rewards.
- **Destroy the Brotherhood** — the party instead kills **Astrid** where she stands, walks out, and reports to the Empire's **Commander Maro**. He sends them to wipe out the Sanctuary alone. This is the *short* path: one infiltration, one massacre, a purse of Septims, and the door to every reward below slams shut forever. (See **The Destroyer's Path** at the end.)

The Warden should present this honestly and without a thumb on the scale. Both are playable; only one is a campaign.
