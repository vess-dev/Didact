A wheel of corrupted **All-Maker Stones** rings the island; the **Temple of Miraak** sits at its hub. Travel is on foot through ash and snow — or by Riekling boat, once the Beast Stone is freed.

- **Raven Rock.** A weathered House Redoran mining colony on the south coast, ringed by the great **Bulwark** against Red Mountain's ash. Councilor **Lleril Morvayn** rules; the ebony mine that was its lifeblood is thought played out. The **Earth Stone** stands just outside the walls, thick with entranced laborers. Inns, a smith (**Glover Mallory**), a temple, and the party's reachable hub.
- **Skaal Village.** The northernmost settlement in Tamriel, on the snowbound north coast — a Greathall and a clutch of homes around a sacred well. Home of the **Skaal**, a Nord tribe who worship the All-Maker, live in harmony with the land, and alone know the forging of **Stalhrim**. Chief **Fanari Strong-Voice** leads; **Storn Crag-Strider** is shaman, his daughter **Frea** his heir.
- **Tel Mithryn.** A cluster of living giant mushroom-towers on the southeast coast, where **Neloth** of House Telvanni studies Red Mountain and the heart stones from on high. Reached by the levitating shaft inside the great trunk.
- **The All-Maker Stones.** Six standing stones — Earth, Wind, Sun, Beast, Water, and the **Tree Stone** at the heart of the Temple. Each is corrupted into a node of Miraak's spell; each, when cleansed, wakes its thralls and leaves a faint blessing on the land.
- **The Temple of Miraak.** A buried dragon-cult temple at the island's heart, over the Tree Stone, where enthralled Skaal raise Miraak's shrine. Below it: the Black Book *Waking Dreams*, and the first window into Apocrypha.
- **Nchardak.** A vast, flooded Dwemer ruin whose reading-room lies sunk beneath the water until its steam-engines are restored. Holds the Black Book *Epistolary Acumen*. Guarded by Dwarven automatons — spiders, spheres, ballistae, a centurion — with reavers camped on the plaza above.
- **Apocrypha.** The realm of Hermaeus Mora: an endless library under a sick green sky, built of towering shelves, black water, and drifting pages, arranged in numbered Chapters. Nothing here is alive in any honest sense. **Seekers** drift the stacks; **Lurkers** heave up from the black pools. At its summit stands **Miraak's tower**.
