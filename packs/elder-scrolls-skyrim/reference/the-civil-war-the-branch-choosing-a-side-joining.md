This is the spine of the whole campaign, and it should be a real decision, talked through, not a coin-flip. Lay out the stakes honestly:

- **The Stormcloaks** want the Empire gone, Skyrim free, and Talos worshipped openly. They warm to Nords and to anyone who'll fight the Legion, and look hard at elves and Imperials. Their grievance is genuine; their welcome is not extended to everyone (a Dunmer or Altmer PC will feel the chill, and Windhelm's treatment of its refugees is the rebellion's shame).
- **The Imperial Legion** wants the rebellion crushed and the province held together under law, the better to face the Thalmor when the truce breaks. They take any able recruit regardless of blood, prize discipline, and distrust open Talos zealots. Their cause is colder and more pragmatic; the price of "order" is the Concordat that started all this.

**Joining is one short initiation each:**

- **Stormcloaks — Windhelm (Palace of the Kings).** The party speaks with **Ulfric**, who sends them to his war-chief **Galmar Stone-Fist**. Galmar tests every recruit: travel to **Serpentstone Isle** and kill the **Ice Wraith** that haunts the standing stone. Return and take the oath. *Faction Reputation: Stormcloaks → +1 (Trusted); Imperial Legion → −1. Reward: a Stormcloak's kit — fur-and-hide armor (1 Armor) and a hide helmet (+1 Armor).*
- **Imperial Legion — Solitude (Castle Dour).** The party reports to **General Tullius**, who hands them to **Legate Rikke**. Rikke orders them to clear the bandits out of **Fort Hraggstad** on the cold coast. Take the fort, return, swear in. *Faction Reputation: Imperial Legion → +1 (Trusted); Stormcloaks → −1. Reward: a set of Imperial armor (1 Armor) and a Legion shield (+1 Armor).*

> **Once sworn, the door shuts.** You cannot ride for both armies; the Civil War admits no fence-sitting once you take up arms (the opposed pair, *Faction Reputation*). The final chance to switch is after **The Jagged Crown**, below — after that the choice is made.
