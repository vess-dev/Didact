- **The World-Eater's Hunger** (d6). Alduin's growing power as he resurrects the dead. Ticks once each time a *new* dragon attacks a settlement or a named dragon is resurrected (Mirmulnir, Sahloknir, each random dragon the Warden drops on a town), and once whenever the party spends a whole session away from the main quest. At 1–3, dragon attacks are rare and the roads are merely dangerous. At 4–5, dragons appear at burial mounds and over villages; the dead walk more boldly. At 6, the sky is wrong everywhere — dragon attacks are constant background dread until the party reaches Skuldafn. The clock stops mattering once the party steps through the portal.
- **The Blades' Patience** (d4, starting at 1). How hard Delphine and Esbern press for Paarthurnax's death. Ticks the first time the party meets Paarthurnax, again when they recover the Elder Scroll, and again after the Time-Wound. At 4, the Blades issue the ultimatum (see Climax → *The Paarthurnax Question*) and withhold their support until it is answered.
- **The War in the Way** (Civil War standing). Not a die — the *state* of the civil war. While the war is unsettled and Whiterun is contested, the Jarl will not risk Dragonsreach on a dragon-trap (Beat 9 is blocked) until the party brokers **Season Unending** or one side wins outright. Resolve the war, or buy a truce, before the trap.
