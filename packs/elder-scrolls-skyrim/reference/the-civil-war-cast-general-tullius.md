#### General Tullius

10 HP, 3 Armor, 16 STR, 13 DEX, 14 WIL, Imperial sword (d8), Legion shield (counted in Armor)

- The Empire's military governor of Skyrim, sent from Cyrodiil to end the rebellion: a hard, competent soldier with no patience for Nord mysticism and a clear, cold eye on the real enemy — the Thalmor. He thinks Ulfric a useful fool for the elves, and he is not entirely wrong.
- **Veteran command:** any Imperial detachment that can see Tullius re-rolls its first failed **Morale** save.
- On taking **Critical Damage** in Castle Dour, he yields rather than die raving; like Ulfric, his end is the party's to give or stay.
