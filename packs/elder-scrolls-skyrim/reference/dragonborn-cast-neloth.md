### Neloth (Telvanni Master Wizard; ally)

12 HP, 1 Armor, 8 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, staff (d6), Destruction bolt (d8, _blast_)

- Magic against him is _Impaired_. He casts as a Dragon Priest does — wards, Destruction bolts, summoned aid — and is far more dangerous than his manner suggests. If a player provokes him, remember that he has lived for centuries by outliving everyone who tried.
