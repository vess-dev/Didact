**Sphere:** forbidden knowledge, memory, fate, secrets.

**Hook.** In a frozen cave north of Winterhold, a brilliant, half-mad scholar named **Septimus Signus** is bent over a sealed Dwemer lockbox he believes holds the heart of a god. He needs an Elder Scroll to read it, and sends the party to carry a **Blank Lexicon** and an **Attunement Sphere** into the deep places of the earth.

**The bargain & the twist.** The true master of this errand is **Hermaeus Mora**, Prince of forbidden lore, who uses the party (and poor Septimus) to crack open a secret he himself once sealed away. The bargain: Mora's voice demands the party draw **blood from the five kindreds of mer** — Altmer, Bosmer, Dunmer, Orsimer, and the debased Falmer — to open the box. The twist is what becomes of the help: the instant the box yields the **Oghma Infinium**, Mora burns Septimus to ash for having "outlived his usefulness," and offers the party his vacant place as a pawn of Apocrypha. Knowledge has a price, and you watch someone else pay it in front of you.

**Beats.**

1. **The deep descent.** Through the Dwemer ruin of **Alftand** into the vast underground twilight of **Blackreach**.
2. **The Oculory.** In the **Tower of Mzark**, solve the great light-lens to claim an Elder Scroll and a runed Lexicon. (It is the same Scroll the Main Quest needs — there is only one.)
3. **Blood of the mer.** Mora's price — collect blood of the five elven races (from corpses, foes, or willing donors; the Falmer's is the dangerous one).
4. **The box opens.** Septimus is consumed; the Oghma Infinium is yours.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Septimus Signus** — eccentric, gentle, doomed; not a combatant.
- **Hermaeus Mora** — a voice from a churning black abyss; never fought directly here.
- The depths swarm with **Falmer** (and their **Chaurus**), **Dwarven Spider** and **Dwarven Sphere** automatons, and a **Dwarven Centurion** as a deep guardian.

**Reward.** **Oghma Infinium** — see its card in *Relics*. Read it once for a permanent boon, then it vanishes forever.
