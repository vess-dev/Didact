- **The Staff of Magnus** — the campaign's signature prize, and the only thing that can end the final fight. See **Relics**.
- **The Morokei mask** — from the dragon priest in Labyrinthian (Beat 7). See **Unique Armors**.
- **The Archmage's Robes** — awarded with the title at the campaign's end. See **Unique Armors**.
- **The Saarthal Amulet** and the **Gauldur Amulet, Magicka Fragment** (Beat 2), and **Savos Aren's Amulet** (Beat 5). See **Relics**.
- **The Arch-Mage's Quarters** — the top of the College, with its arcane enchanter, alchemy lab, and private library, becomes the party's home base and a place to craft, research, and rest.
- **Spell Tomes and books** — a Spell Tome: Lesser Ward at Beat 1; a Skill Book each and a minor Relic at Beat 3; and the run of the **Arcanaeum** thereafter. As Arch-Mage the party may, at the Warden's discretion, draw one earned tome from the College's locked shelves between arcs — the right place to hand out a Master tome (Lightning Storm, Dragonhide, Mass Paralysis, Bane of the Undead) as a capstone.
- **Standing** — **College of Winterhold** rises to **Sworn (+2)** with the title; Renown shifts with the party's handling of Winterhold (see Climax). Killing Ancano and the dragon priest never lowers Renown.
- **Allies** — **Tolfdir** as a devoted second; the surviving apprentices as companions; and, if well treated, the **Synod** as a distant favor and the town of Winterhold as a neighbor that finally trusts the mages behind its walls.
