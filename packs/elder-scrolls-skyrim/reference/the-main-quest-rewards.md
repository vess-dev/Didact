- **Dragonbane** — the Akaviri katana of the Blades, waiting in **Sky Haven Temple** (Beat 6). The single most useful weapon in this campaign; put it in the party's hands before Skuldafn. See **Unique Weapons**. (A dragon is a _detachment_, so a lone attacker's blows against it are _Impaired_; Dragonbane's _Enhanced_ cancels that, and the blade strikes at its full d8.)
- **The Nahkriin mask** — the dragon-priest mask of Skuldafn's gatekeeper (Beat 10). See **Unique Armors**. *Warn the party:* Skuldafn cannot be reached again once they step through the portal; loot it before they go.
- **Odahviing, dragon-ally** — won at the Dragonsreach trap (Beat 9). A flight to Skuldafn that nothing else can give, and one strafing pass over its courtyard (Fire Breath, d10 _blast_). Run as an ally the Warden voices, never a sheet the party controls.
- **Paarthurnax's counsel** — the ancient dragon's friendship (Beats 7–11), worth more than any item: he fights beside the party in the Time-Wound battle and waits on the summit to greet them home, *unless* the party kills him for the Blades.
- **The three heroes of Sovngarde** — Gormlaith, Hakon, and Felldir join the last battle, and their joined Voices are the thing that makes Alduin mortal. The decisive "ally for the finale."
- **Dragon-slaying renown** — climbing the Renown & Infamy ladder as the party's deeds spread: the soul-absorption and Thaneship (Beat 3) is worth **+1 (Known)**; trapping a dragon on the Great Porch before all Whiterun (Beat 9) rates **+2 (Renowned)**; the World-Eater's death makes the party **+3 (Hero of Skyrim)** — doors open everywhere.
- **Thane of Whiterun & Lydia** — the title and the housecarl (run as the **Sworn Housecarl** in the Player's Guide's Companions chapter; she starts at Loyalty 4), plus the right to buy a house in the city (Beat 3).
- **The Elder Scroll (Dragon)** — recovered from the Tower of Mzark (Beat 7). After it is read at the Time-Wound it is spent of its purpose here, but it is a treasure of immense value and a hook into the **Dawnguard** frame (which needs three such Scrolls).
- **Civil War standing** — brokering **Season Unending** (Beat 6) or winning the war (the **Civil War** frame) shifts the party's standing with one faction (Stormcloaks or Imperial Legion) toward **Sworn**, and clears the one obstacle blocking the Dragonsreach trap.
- **Septims & barrow-loot** — every Nordic tomb and Dwemer ruin in the arc (Bleak Falls, Ustengrav, Alftand, Skuldafn) is worth ordinary treasure: a few hundred Septims, soul gems, the occasional Spell Tome on a Word Wall's altar, Dwarven scrap from the automatons, and a Draugr Deathlord's ancient ebony.
