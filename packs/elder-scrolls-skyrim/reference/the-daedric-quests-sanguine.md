**Sphere:** hedonism, debauchery, revelry, excess.

**Hook.** In any tavern, a jovial stranger named **Sam Guevenne** challenges the party to a drinking contest, staff in hand, grinning too widely. Win, and the world goes black.

**The bargain & the twist.** The party wakes scattered, hungover, and amnesiac — one of them face-down in the **Temple of Dibella** in Markarth, having apparently *redecorated* it. The "quest" is to retrace a night of glorious ruin and find Sam: a stolen goat to return to a furious farmer in **Rorikstead** (a giant named **Grok** is its new owner), a wedding ring bought on credit from **Ysolda** in Whiterun, to be recovered from the **hagraven** Moira, to whom one of the party proposed in her grove. The twist is the whole shape of it — Sam is **Sanguine**, the Prince of Debauchery, who has used the party as the instrument of a glorious mess and finds the cleanup hilarious. There is no betrayal demanded here, only embarrassment; the reward is genuinely, freely given, because to Sanguine the party already *earned* it by the chaos they (don't remember they) caused.

**Beats.**

1. **The morning after.** Talk (or grovel) your way out of the temple; gather the clues left by your own ruined night.
2. **Goat, ring, and a hagraven's heart.** Three comedic errands across the Holds. **Moira** the hagraven (run as a **Hagraven**) will not give the ring back kindly.
3. **The stormed tower.** The trail ends at **Morvunskar**, a fort thick with hostile mages; a portal at its heart opens to **Misty Grove**.
4. **The party.** Sam reveals himself as Sanguine, laughs, and hands over the Rose.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Sam Guevenne / Sanguine** — the Prince in disguise; never fights.
- **Morvunskar's mages** — a warband of **Conjurer** and **Necromancer** types; a real fight.
- **Moira** — a **Hagraven**; **Grok** the giant (run as a **Giant**) is friendly if not provoked.

**Reward.** **Sanguine Rose** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. A staff that tears a rift and summons a Dremora to fight for you.
