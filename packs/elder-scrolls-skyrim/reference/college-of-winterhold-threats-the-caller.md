### The Caller (Fellglow Keep)

Run as a **Necromancer** from the Bestiary (6 HP, 1 Armor, 8 STR, 11 DEX, 14 WIL, bound dagger (d6), elemental bolt (d8, _ranged_)), with a _detachment_ of **Skeletons** and a few vampire thralls. She would rather bargain for her life than die for Urag's books.
