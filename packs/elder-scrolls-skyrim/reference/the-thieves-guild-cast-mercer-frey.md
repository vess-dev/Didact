### Mercer Frey (the traitor)

12 HP, 2 Armor, 13 STR, 15 DEX, 14 WIL, Dwarven sword and dagger (d8+d6)

- Guild Master of the Thieves Guild and the campaign's villain: a Breton who broke the Nightingale oath, murdered Gallus, framed Karliah, and stole the Skeleton Key. Cold, controlled, and utterly without remorse.
- **Stolen Luck**: While he holds the Skeleton Key (Beats 4–8), once per combat Mercer turns invisible and vanishes — the next attack against him is wasted, and he may move away unseen. In the final fight he can, once, force a PC or an ally to pass a WIL save or strike at the nearest *friend*.
- On taking **Critical Damage**, he drops the **Skeleton Key** and the **Eyes of the Falmer**, and the chamber around him begins to flood.
