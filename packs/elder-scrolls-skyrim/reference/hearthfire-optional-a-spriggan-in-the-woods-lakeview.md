For a Lakeview build deep in the pines, the timber-cutting wakes a guardian of the wood. A short, eerie trouble — drive it off, appease it, or learn why it woke.

#### Spriggan

8 HP, 12 STR, 14 DEX, 14 WIL, thorned arms (d8), spore burst (d6, _blast_)

- A glowing green spirit of wood and bark in the shape of a woman, blending into the forest until it strikes.
- Fire damage against it is _Enhanced_. Once per combat it heals itself of all its hurts, and it can call nearby beasts to its aid.
- **Critical Damage**: a cloud of spores fills the target's lungs; they lose 1d4 more STR and cannot speak above a whisper for a day.

> A felled sacred tree, a salted bee-yard, a grave disturbed in the clearing — give the spriggan a *reason*, and the party a way to make peace as well as war.
