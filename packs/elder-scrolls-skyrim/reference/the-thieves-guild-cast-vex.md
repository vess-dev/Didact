### Vex

6 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 15 DEX, 12 WIL, dagger (d6)

- The Guild's best infiltrator, a sharp-tongued Imperial who has never failed a job she took. Runs the "specialist" work alongside Delvin.
- **The Way In**: If consulted before a heist (e.g. Goldenglow), she reveals a hidden entrance: the party need not save to get past the outer guards.
