### Magic Anomalies (Winterhold)

3 HP, 12 STR, 12 DEX, 10 WIL, crackling surge (d8, _blast_)

- Living fragments of the Eye's magicka, flickering blue through the ruined town. Run a pack of four or more as a _detachment_. Weapons strike them normally; spells against them are _Impaired_.
