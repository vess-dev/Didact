Broad strokes — a spine for six to ten sessions. Each beat is a turning point, not a script. Where the two paths diverge, both tracks are given; otherwise the beat is shared.

1. **Dimhollow Crypt (shared).** Isran sends the party east of Morthal to learn why vampires want an old Nordic tomb. Inside, they trail a Volkihar raiding party led by **Lokil**, who is torturing a captured Vigilant for the way down. At the heart of the crypt stands a sealed stone monolith ringed by unlit braziers — a **brazier puzzle** (press the stud on the central pedestal, then push the braziers along their tracks until the violet fire reaches the outer ring). Solving it opens the monolith and frees **Serana**, who has slept here some four thousand years cradling an **Elder Scroll**. She asks only to be taken home. Lokil's vampires attack as the party leaves.
2. **The Long Road Home (shared).** Serana asks to be escorted to **Castle Volkihar**, on an island in the Sea of Ghosts west of Solitude, reached by a ferryman at a ruined dock. Along the way she is wary, then warmer; she lets slip that she is a *Daughter of Coldharbour*, that her mother **Valerica** vanished long ago, and that she would rather not see her father at all. This is the beat to make the party *like* her before the fork.
3. **Bloodline — the Choice (the branch).** In the great hall of Castle Volkihar, **Lord Harkon** receives his daughter coldly and the party warily. He reveals the **Tyranny of the Sun** and offers the party his blood — the **Vampire Lord** gift — to join the Volkihar. **Here the campaign forks.**
   - **Dawnguard track:** Refuse. Harkon casts the party out (or hunts them through the castle); they flee back to **Fort Dawnguard**, where Isran is rebuilding the order — *A New Order* begins.
   - **Volkihar track:** A PC accepts and is remade as a Vampire Lord (see the note above). Harkon welcomes them into the clan and sets them the same hunt — find the Scrolls and the Bow — only now in his service.
4. **Mustering / The Court (the branch, parallel).** Each side gathers its strength and learns it needs a **Moth Priest** to read an Elder Scroll safely.
   - **Dawnguard track — *A New Order*:** Help Isran beat off a vampire assault on the fort, then recruit **Gunmar** (a smith and beast-tamer, found hunting a bear) and **Sorine Jurard** (an inventor of crossbows, found in the wilds). The fort wakes back into an order: a forge, armored war-trolls, and **crossbows** for the party.
   - **Volkihar track — the court:** Harkon's court of vampires (the adviser **Garan Marethi**, the alchemist, the smith) takes the party's measure. Harkon names them his hunters and sends them after the Moth Priest in his name.
5. **The Moth Priest — *Prophet* (the branch, parallel).** A **Moth Priest** named **Dexion Evicus** has come north to read the Scrolls; both sides race for him. The trail runs through **Dragon Bridge** to **Forebears' Holdout**, where a vampire called **Malkus** has already taken him.
   - **Dawnguard track:** Rescue Dexion (kill Malkus) and bring him to the fort. He reads Serana's Scroll: a vision of **Auriel's Bow**, and word that **two more Scrolls** are needed to read the whole prophecy.
   - **Volkihar track:** Isran's hunters got there first — Malkus is dead and they are trying to free the priest. Kill them and **enthrall** Dexion, dragging him back to the castle to read the Scroll for Harkon. The vision is the same; the captive is yours.
6. **The Soul Cairn — *Chasing Echoes* & *Beyond Death* (shared, welcome reversed).** Serana believes her mother **Valerica** fled into the **Soul Cairn** with a second Scroll. From a hidden courtyard beneath Castle Volkihar (reached by fighting through the undercroft and repairing a **moondial** — set the Half, Full, and Crescent crests), Serana's blood opens a portal to that bleak plane. **To cross alive you must be a vampire** — or let part of your soul be trapped (returnable later). In the Soul Cairn the party finds Valerica behind a barrier of three **Keepers**; slay them, follow her into the Boneyard, and face the undead dragon **Durnehviir**. They recover the **Blood Elder Scroll**. *(Welcome differs only in flavor: to the Dawnguard, Valerica is a grudging ally against her husband; to the Volkihar, she is a traitor to the clan — and Serana must choose her mother over her father either way.)*
7. **The Ancestor Glade & the Forgotten Vale — *Unseen Visions* / *Touching the Sky* (shared).** The third Scroll — the **Elder Scroll (Dragon)** — lies in the Tower of Mzark beneath Alftand (the Main Quest, Beat 7), unless the party already carries it. With all three Scrolls, the party seeks the **Ancestor Glade** near Falkreath, harvests bark from a Canticle Tree, and draws the **Ancestor Moths** to sanctify a reader. Reading the three Scrolls together reveals the Bow's resting place: **Darkfall Cave**, and beyond it the hidden **Forgotten Vale**. There the last living Snow Elf, **Knight-Paladin Gelebor**, will give up **Auriel's Bow** only if the party cleanses the Vale's wayshrines and ends his corrupted brother, **Arch-Curate Vyrthur** — the vampire who *authored* the Tyranny of the Sun out of spite after an Initiate's bite made him a vampire and, as he saw it, Auri-El turned away. Kill Vyrthur; claim the **Bow** and **Sunhallowed arrows**.
8. **Kindred Judgment — the Climax (the branch).** All three Scrolls, the Bow, and a Daughter of Coldharbour are now in play. The prophecy can be made or unmade.
   - **Dawnguard track:** Isran leads an assault on **Castle Volkihar**. The party storms the Cathedral and faces Harkon in **Vampire Lord** form. *Deny* him the sun.
   - **Volkihar track:** The party returns to the Cathedral to enact the prophecy at Harkon's altar — and discovers the rite was only ever meant to crown *him*. He demands the Bow; refuse, and he attacks. Either way, **Harkon dies** (the killing blow comes through his altar-ward, pierced by **Auriel's Bow**). See the **Climax** below for resolutions.
