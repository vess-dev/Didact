The party draws the eye of the **Dark Brotherhood**, Tamriel's most feared guild of assassins — worshippers of the Dread Father **Sithis**, bound by old tenets, and all but extinct in this Fourth Era. Abducted and "recruited" by the cell's leader **Astrid**, the party is initiated with a killing, taken into the **Falkreath Sanctuary**, and set to work on contracts. Then the silent corpse of the **Night Mother** speaks — and names one of them **Listener**, the voice through whom Sithis's will is heard. That elevation drags the family into the grandest contract in their history: the murder of **Emperor Titus Mede II** himself. But the family is rotten from within. A betrayer burns the Sanctuary to the ground, and the survivors must finish the Emperor aboard his warship the *Katariah* and rebuild from the ashes — or never finish at all.
