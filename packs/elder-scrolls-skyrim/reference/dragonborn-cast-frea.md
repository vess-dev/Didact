### Frea (Skaal shaman-warrior; companion)

8 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 14 DEX, 13 WIL, Nordic war axe (d8), hunting bow (d6, _bulky_)

- Run her as a companion (see Companions) — a named ally, hardier than a rolled one — whose Loyalty starts at 4 — she needs the party. Her trick: once per day, her knowledge of the All-Maker lets an ally re-roll a failed WIL save against fear, charm, or a mind laid under someone else's will.
- After Storn's death she fights with cold fury: once per combat, one of her attacks is _Enhanced_.
