### Isran

10 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 13 DEX, 15 WIL, Dawnguard warhammer (d10, _bulky_, _two-handed_) or crossbow (d8, _bulky_)

- The founder of the Dawnguard: a scarred, iron-willed Redguard zealot in heavy mail, sworn to end every vampire in Skyrim. An ally on the Dawnguard path, a furious foe on the Volkihar one.
- Against vampires and the undead his attacks are _Enhanced_. He wards himself with **Stendarr's Aura**: once per scene he can loose a burst of holy light, a _blast_ attack (d6) that ignores the Armor of undead and leaves a vampire Deprived for the round.
- **Critical Damage**: the hammer lands with sacred force and breaks what it strikes; an undead target is destroyed outright.
