It is **4E 201**. The dragons, gone from Skyrim for centuries and dismissed as legend, have returned — and at their head wheels **Alduin**, the great black World-Eater, eldest child of Akatosh, who is raising the long-dead dragons from their burial mounds one by one. The old prophecies say he means to devour the world.

The party is caught at the heart of it from the first hour: prisoners in a cart bound for the headsman's block at **Helgen** when Alduin himself burns the town to the ground. They escape into a Skyrim split by civil war and haunted by a thing out of legend. Somewhere along the way — at a watchtower, over a dragon's corpse — it becomes plain that one of them is touched by something old: when a dragon dies near them, its strength does not simply gutter out. The **Greybeards** name it from their mountain. The **Blades**, the last of an ancient order of dragonslayers, name it too, and they have spent their lives waiting for it.
