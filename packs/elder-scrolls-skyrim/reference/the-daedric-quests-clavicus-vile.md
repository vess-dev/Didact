**Sphere:** power and wishes through pacts and bargains; trickery.

**Hook.** Outside **Falkreath**, the smith **Lod** is hunting a stray dog. The dog finds the party first — and *talks.* He is **Barbas**, the immortal hound and other half of **Clavicus Vile**, and he has quarreled with his master and wants the party to broker a reconciliation.

**The bargain & the twist.** Barbas leads the party the long road to **Haemar's Shame**, where Clavicus Vile's shrine sits among vampire cultists. The Prince, bored and cruel, will only take Barbas back if the party first fetches him a toy: the **Rueful Axe**, a great axe Vile once handed a wizard who begged him to cure his daughter's lycanthropy. The twist is the final pact at the shrine — **keep the Rueful Axe** (which means striking Barbas down to be rid of the talking dog's pestering, exactly the petty cruelty Vile hoped to provoke), or **surrender the Axe** to the Prince and walk away with the **Masque of Clavicus Vile** instead, having chosen the harder, kinder thing. Vile profits from your choice either way; that is the whole joke.

**Beats.**

1. **The talking dog.** Meet Barbas; agree (or be pestered into) the long walk to the shrine.
2. **Haemar's Shame.** Reach the shrine through its vampire cultists; speak to Clavicus Vile.
3. **Rimerock Burrow.** Retrieve the Rueful Axe from the frozen lair of the mad conjurer **Sebastian Lort**, who guards it.
4. **The pact.** Back at the shrine — keep the Axe (kill Barbas) or give it up (take the Masque).

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Barbas** — the immortal hound; cannot be killed in the wild (only "dismissed" by the Prince at the shrine); a wry, exasperating companion.
- **Clavicus Vile** — a languid voice from the shrine; never fought.
- **Sebastian Lort** — a grief-mad conjurer guarding the Axe; *run as a* **Conjurer**, with a summoned **Flame Atronach** or **Frost Atronach** at his side. **Haemar's Shame** holds a nest of **Vampire**s and **Death Hound**s.

**Reward.** **Masque of Clavicus Vile** — see its card in *Unique Armors*. The grinning helm that softens a stranger's first Reaction and sweetens every price. *(Keeping the Rueful Axe forecloses the Masque; the Axe has a card of its own in Unique Weapons, but the Masque is the true Daedric prize.)*
