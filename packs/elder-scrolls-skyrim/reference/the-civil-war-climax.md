The enemy capital falls. Two endings, mirror images:

- **A Free Skyrim (Stormcloak).** The Empire is thrown out; Ulfric rules, the Talos shrines reopen, and the Nords have their independence. The party are heroes of the rebellion — **Stormblade**, **Sworn**, **Renowned**. And in the quiet after, the cost: a Skyrim cut off and exhausted, its Dunmer no freer than before, and a Thalmor dossier that named Ulfric their asset all along. The elves wanted the Empire weakened in the north. They got it.
- **A Reunified Skyrim (Imperial).** The rebellion is broken; Ulfric's head rolls or he rots in a cell, Brunwulf rules a kinder Windhelm, and the province is whole under one law. The party are heroes of the Legion — **Legate**, **Sworn**, **Renowned**, **Thane of Eastmarch**. And the cost: the Concordat still stands, Talos is still outlawed, and the elves still smile — for a province held together by the Empire is a province the Empire's masters, the Thalmor, still circle. The war that "ended" only set the table for the next one.

Either way, a man knelt and the party chose whether he died. Either way, somewhere above Solitude, an emissary closes a dossier, satisfied. The Dragon Crisis is still in the sky — and the truest war, the one the elves are actually fighting, has not yet begun.
