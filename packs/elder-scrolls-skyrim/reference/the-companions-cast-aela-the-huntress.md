### Aela the Huntress (Circle)

8 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 16 DEX, 11 WIL, ancient Nord bow (d6) or hunting knife (d6); Beast Form claws (d6+d6), bite (d8)

- Lithe, war-painted, restless; happiest on a hunt and proudest of the wolf.
- In Beast Form she fights as the **Werewolf** of the Bestiary: mundane attacks against her are _Impaired_, silver is _Enhanced_; she is immune to disease. **She refuses the cure.**
- **Huntress's Mark**: once per combat, her next attack on a foe she has already struck is _Enhanced_.
