### Morokei (the heart of Labyrinthian)

Run as a **Dragon Priest** from the Bestiary (12 HP, 1 Armor, 8 STR, 13 DEX, 18 WIL, life-draining touch (d8), elemental bolt (d8, _blast_)).

- **The shield of light:** Morokei cannot be touched until the party kills or breaks the concentration of his **two enthralled wizards** — run each as a **Conjurer** (6 HP, 1 Armor, 8 STR, 11 DEX, 14 WIL, bound sword (d8), elemental bolt (d8, _ranged_)). While the wizards stand, every attack on Morokei is wasted.
- Magic against him is _Impaired_; he keeps his distance, hurls Destruction, throws up wards, and raises the fallen.
- **Critical Damage**: a death-curse strikes the target for 1d6 WIL. He turns to dust when slain, leaving his **mask** and the **Staff of Magnus**.
