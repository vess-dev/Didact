- **Lycanthropy — or its cure.** The campaign's signature prize *and* its central choice. A PC who drinks at the Underforge becomes a **Werewolf** (Beast Blood, Beast Form, the Edges and Costs of Playing Unusual PCs). A beast-blooded PC who later burns a Glenmoril witch's head in the Flame of the Harbinger is **cured** — mortal again, edges and costs gone. The party may end the campaign as wolves, as cured ex-wolves, or as warriors who never drank.
- **Wuuthrad** — Ysgramor's reforged battleaxe, the holy weapon of the Companions; it passes to the Harbinger, which is to say the party. See **Unique Weapons**.
- **Ring of Hircine** — an optional prize, won in the Daedric quest *Ill Met by Moonlight* by sparing the cursed werewolf Sinding; of use only to a PC who keeps the beast-blood rather than seeking the cure. See **Unique Armors**.
- **Skyforge gear.** Membership opens **Eorlund Gray-Mane's** forge: a Skyforge steel weapon on induction, and for the Circle, the Companions' **Wolf Armor** (plate, 3 Armor, _bulky_). Skyforge work is **masterwork** — already tempered, one die step or +1 Armor better than ordinary work (see Crafting), never past the caps.
- **The Shield of Ysgramor** — recovered in Ysgramor's Tomb beside the founder's honored dead. See **Unique Armors**.
- **Harbinger standing.** The party becomes **Harbinger** of the Companions — **Companions +2 (Sworn)** and the chair at the head of Jorrvaskr's table. **Aela, Farkas, and Vilkas** become available as followers (Circle members; use their stat blocks above, run as Companions). Killing the Silver Hand and the monstrous never costs Renown; openly running as a wolf in a settlement can.
