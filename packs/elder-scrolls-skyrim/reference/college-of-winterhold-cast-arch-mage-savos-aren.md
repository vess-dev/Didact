### Arch-Mage Savos Aren — head of the College

12 HP, 2 Armor, 10 STR, 12 DEX, 16 WIL, staff (d6), elemental bolt (d8, _blast_)

- A cautious, secretive Dunmer who has led the College for decades and carries a guilt he never speaks of: as a young apprentice he went into Labyrinthian with his friends and walked out the only survivor. He tolerates Ancano because defying the Thalmor openly could doom the College outright.
- A genuinely powerful mage who nonetheless *loses* — Ancano kills him with the Eye's stolen power at the campaign's turn. His death is the point of no return, not a fight the party can win for him.
