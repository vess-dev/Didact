This frame turns on a single choice, taken at **Beat 3 (Bloodline)** inside Castle Volkihar:

- **The Dawnguard path.** The party refuses Harkon's offer, flees the castle, and returns to Isran to rebuild the order and stop the prophecy. They gather the Scrolls and the Bow to *deny* Harkon the sun, and storm Castle Volkihar at the end.
- **The Volkihar path.** A PC drinks Harkon's blood and accepts the **Vampire Lord** gift (the others may follow, or may grit their teeth and stay for Serana's sake). The party gathers the Scrolls and the Bow to *enact* the prophecy — then, at the climax, turns on Harkon anyway, because the Tyranny of the Sun was only ever meant to crown *him*.

The two paths run the **same four middle stations** (the Moth Priest, the Soul Cairn, the Ancestor Glade, the Forgotten Vale) with the goals inverted and the welcome reversed. Each beat below gives **both tracks** where they differ. Serana walks with the party the whole way down **either** path — she is appalled by neither hunter nor monster, only by her father.

> **A note on the Vampire Lord.** A PC who takes Harkon's gift becomes a **Vampire** and may assume the **Vampire Lord** form exactly as written in Playing Unusual PCs — a Transformed shape taken once per day at the cost of a Fatigue, with claws (d8+d8), hovering flight, a drain touch, +1 Armor, and the power to raise a corpse. It is **not** a Shout and grants no Thu'um. It carries every cost in that chapter: sunlight makes you Deprived, fire against you is _Enhanced_, you hunger, and the Dawnguard and the Vigil of Stendarr hunt you the moment they learn what you are. Put that cost into play.
