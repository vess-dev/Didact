The frames name the prizes; their rules live in the catalogs.

- **Miraak's Sword**, **Miraak's Staff** (see **Unique Weapons**), **Miraak's Mask**, and **Miraak's Robes** (see **Unique Armors**) — the First Dragonborn's panoply, taken from his tower. A trophy that marks its bearer a slayer of legends.
- **A Black Book** — the party may keep one or two found Books as Relics (see above). Mora's gifts always cost more than they give.
- **Stalhrim.** Aiding the Skaal — and recovering the lost Stalhrim source on the island — unlocks **Stalhrim** as a Crafting material, worked only by Skaal hands or those they teach (see Crafting): mined only on Solstheim with an ancient Nordic pickaxe, never smelted, cold to the touch forever. A Stalhrim piece is as strong as any other of its type; its gift is its fiction, its worth (twenty times the Marketplace price), and one quirk: a frost enchantment bound into Stalhrim can be recharged by a night buried in snow instead of a soul gem.
- **The Deathbrand panoply** (the optional side-arc *Deathbrand*) — a treasure-map hunt to four buried chests around Solstheim's coast, and at last to Gyldenhul Barrow, raises the Stalhrim war-gear of the pirate-king **Haknir Death-Brand**: the **Deathbrand Armor** and **Deathbrand Helm** (see **Unique Armors**), and the paired scimitars **Bloodscythe & Soulrender** (see **Unique Weapons**).
- **Trophies of Solstheim's dead** — optional ruin-delves yield the masks **Ahzidal**, **Dukaan**, and **Zahkriisos** (see **Unique Armors**), Ahzidal's rings (see **Relics**) and armor, and **Hoarfrost**, the ice-bitten pickaxe of Kolbjorn Barrow (see **Unique Weapons**).
- **The freed All-Maker Stones.** Each cleansed stone leaves a faint blessing a PC may claim by touching it, in the spirit of a Standing Stone and framed as the All-Maker's gift, never a Shout. Each PC may carry one at a time:
  - **Wind** — once per day, re-roll a failed save against a fall, a gale, or the cold.
  - **Sun** — once per day, one fire attack you make is _Enhanced_.
  - **Beast** — once per day, a natural beast that can see you must pass a WIL save or fight beside you for the rest of the scene.
  - **Earth** — once per day, when an attack would hit you, stone rises to take it: +1 Armor against that attack (to the cap of 3).
  - **Water** — once per day, a short rest restores your HP even where something would deny it.
  - **Tree** (freed only by Miraak's death) — once per day, a short rest among living trees also clears one Fatigue.
- **Neloth as an ally.** Survive the campaign and the Telvanni wizard counts the party useful: a standing contact at Tel Mithryn for arcane lore, enchanting, heart stones, and grudging Black-Book expertise.
- **Renown and Septims.** Breaking Miraak's hold on a whole island is a deed that travels: a step of **Renown**, and the Skaal (**Skaal +2 (Sworn)**) and Raven Rock open their doors. Looted ebony and Stalhrim, Telvanni pay, and barrow-hoards supply the Septims.
