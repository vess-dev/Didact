Rumor reaches the party in any tavern from Riften to Solitude: people are vanishing from the roads at night, and a new order of hunters in **Dayspring Canyon** is paying good Septims for anyone willing to fight the undead. Or a tattered **Vigilant of Stendarr** staggers up with his last breath, warning that the Hall of the Vigilant has been burned and "the ones who did it came for a crypt by Morthal." Either thread leads to **Fort Dawnguard** and Isran, who sends the party straight to **Dimhollow Crypt**.

A PC with the Dawnguard Hunter background is already one of Isran's first recruits, and starts at **Dawnguard +1**. (If a PC is a Vampire Fledgling, Isran will not have them in his fort — but Serana will trust them faster, and Harkon's offer in Beat 3 lands as a homecoming.)
