#### Thalmor Justiciar

6 HP, 1 Armor, 10 STR, 13 DEX, 14 WIL, elven sword (d8), Destruction bolt (d8, _ranged_) (as in the Bestiary)

- A black-and-gold agent of the Aldmeri Dominion: a hunter of Talos-worshippers who turns up at the edges of the war, "advising" the Empire and quietly pleased by every stalemate. Casts Destruction at range and cuts with an enchanted blade.
- **The long war:** the Justiciar is never quite where the party can pin a betrayal on them. A captured dispatch or embassy dossier in their keeping reveals the Dominion's true game — that a Skyrim bled white by its own war is exactly what the elves want.
