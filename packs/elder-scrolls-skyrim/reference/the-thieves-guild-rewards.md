The frames name the prizes; their rules live in the catalogs.

- **Thieves Guild armor** (Beat 2) — the Guild's dark, quiet leathers (light armor, 1 Armor), and a place in the Cistern and the network behind it.
- **Nightingale Blade** (Beat 5) — see **Unique Weapons**.
- **Chillrend** (Beat 6) — the glass sword in its case in Riftweald Manor. See **Unique Weapons**.
- **Nightingale Armor** and **Nightingale Hood** (Beat 7) — see **Unique Armors**.
- **Nightingale Bow** (Beat 8) — Karliah's gift. See **Unique Weapons**.
- **The Skeleton Key** (recovered in Beat 8, **returned** in Beat 9) — see **Relics**. *In this campaign the party uses it on the way out of Irkngthand, then must give it up at the Twilight Sepulcher; keeping it forfeits the restored-luck ending and the Guild Master reward.*
- **An Agent's gift** (Beat 9) — at the Ebonmere, each Nightingale chooses one of Nocturnal's three gifts, exactly as in the Nightingale background: the **Crescent Moon** (Agent of Shadow), the **Half Moon** (Agent of Subterfuge), or the **Full Moon** (Agent of Strife). A PC who already has one may keep it or exchange it for another.
- **Guild Master's Armor** (Beat 10; see **Unique Armors**), the **Amulet of Articulation** (see **Relics**), and a **cut of Riften**: the key to the Guild's tribute chest and a standing share of every score, paid out in Septims each Downtime (a healthy Guild yields a steady purse). The Guild counts the party **Sworn (+2)**.
