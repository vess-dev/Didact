### Kodlak Whitemane (Harbinger)

10 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 15 WIL, Skyforge greatsword (d12); Beast Form claws (d6+d6), bite (d8)

- Old and scarred, calm where younger warriors rage; speaks of honor, the soul, and Sovngarde. Beast-blooded, but he fights it.
- He will not transform if he can help it — the wolf is his shame. In a true crisis the Warden may give him Beast Form for one scene.
- Run him as a mentor, not a combatant. **He is killed by the Silver Hand at Beat 6 regardless of the party's strength** — the loss, not a fight, is the point.
