| # | Frame | In brief |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Main Quest — The Dragon Crisis** | Alduin returns; the party fights through barrow, peak, and Sovngarde to end the World-Eater. |
| 2 | **The Civil War** | Stormcloaks or Legion — take a side and fight for the soul of Skyrim, Hold by Hold. |
| 3 | **The Companions** | Whiterun's warrior band, the secret of the Circle, the wolf, and the price of Wuuthrad. |
| 4 | **The College of Winterhold** | The Eye of Magnus, a Thalmor coup, and the Staff of Magnus beneath Labyrinthian. |
| 5 | **The Thieves Guild** | Riften's shadows, Mercer Frey's betrayal, the Nightingales, and a debt to Nocturnal. |
| 6 | **The Dark Brotherhood** | Sithis, the Night Mother, and a contract to kill an Emperor — or the assassins themselves. |
| 7 | **The Daedric Quests** | A sandbox of the Princes' bargains, each ending in one of the dread Daedric Artifacts. |
| 8 | **Dawnguard** *(DLC)* | Vampire-hunters or the Volkihar court; Serana, Auriel's Bow, and a plot to blot out the sun. |
| 9 | **Dragonborn** *(DLC)* | Solstheim, the ash, and Miraak the First Dragonborn, in the library-realm of Hermaeus Mora. |
| 10 | **Hearthfire** *(DLC)* | Build a homestead, raise its walls and its children, and defend the home you've made. |

Run them in any order. A long Skyrim campaign often opens with a barrow delve or a Civil-War skirmish, lets the Dragon Crisis erupt around the party, and grows into a web of guild oaths, Daedric bargains, and a hard-won home — all of it the players' own.
