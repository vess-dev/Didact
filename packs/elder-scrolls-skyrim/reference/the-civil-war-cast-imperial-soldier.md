#### Imperial Soldier

5 HP, 2 Armor, 13 STR, 12 DEX, 10 WIL, Imperial sword (d8) or Imperial bow (d6), Legion shield (counted in Armor)

- Disciplined Legionnaires and auxiliaries (many of them Nords) in segmented armor. Bands of four or more are a _detachment_; a unit fields an officer (the **Imperial Captain**, below).
- Their discipline tells: a Legion detachment re-rolls a failed **Morale** save once per combat.
