#### Galmar Stone-Fist

10 HP, 2 Armor, 16 STR, 12 DEX, 13 WIL, battleaxe (d10), thrown axe (d6, _ranged_)

- Ulfric's gruff housecarl and war-chief, a grizzled bear of a Nord in a bear's-head helm. He runs the rebellion's musters, tests every recruit, and assigns the party's missions through the campaign.
- Loyal past reason; he dies at Ulfric's side in the Palace of the Kings if the Legion takes Windhelm, and will not be talked into surrender.
