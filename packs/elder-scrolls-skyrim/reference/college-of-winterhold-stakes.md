The **College of Winterhold** is the only place in Skyrim where magic is taught openly. It clings to a spire of rock above the Sea of Ghosts, reached by a single long bridge, on the edge of a town the sea swallowed eighty years ago. The townsfolk blame the College for that disaster — the **Great Collapse** — and the mages have learned to keep their gates shut and their business their own.

Into that uneasy quiet the College has lately taken on a Thalmor "advisor," **Ancano**, foisted on Arch-Mage **Savos Aren** as the price of being left alone. Nobody trusts him. Nobody can be rid of him.

Then a dig at the nearby Nordic ruin of **Saarthal** breaks into a sealed vault and finds a thing that should not exist: a vast humming orb of pure magicka, older than the Nords, older than the elves — the **Eye of Magnus**, a fragment left behind when the god Magnus fled the world at the dawn of creation. No one knows what it does. No one knows how to turn it off. And one of the people standing closest to it is a Thalmor agent who has spent his whole posting waiting for exactly this.

The stakes climb in three steps. First the Eye is a mystery the College wants to study. Then it is a danger the College cannot contain. Then Ancano binds himself to it, kills the Arch-Mage, and begins unmaking the town of Winterhold one anomaly at a time — and only the lost Staff of Magnus can master the Eye before it spreads past the town to the Hold and beyond.
