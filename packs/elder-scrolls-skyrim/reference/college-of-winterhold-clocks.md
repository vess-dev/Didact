- **The Eye's Hunger** (d6). How far the Eye's power has spread since Ancano seized it. Ticks at the start of each session after Beat 5, and whenever the party rests somewhere safe on the road to Labyrinthian. At 2, anomalies stalk the town of Winterhold by night; at 4, the townsfolk flee and the Jarl blames the College; at 6, the Eye's field swallows the whole spire, and the final fight must be fought in a Hall where every Spell Tome forces the duress WIL save.
- **Ancano's Suspicion** (d4). Before Beat 5, ticks each time the party openly pries into Ancano's business or brings him news of the Eye. At 4, he moves early: Beat 5 begins at once, before the party has the Oculory's answer.
