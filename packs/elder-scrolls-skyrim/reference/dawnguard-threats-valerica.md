### Valerica

12 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 17 WIL, Vampiric Drain (d8, _ranged_)

- Serana's mother: a brilliant, frost-cold vampire alchemist and conjurer, self-exiled to the Soul Cairn with a Scroll to thwart her husband. An ally once the Keepers fall; never quite warm.
- Immune to poison; fire against her is _Enhanced_. Once per scene she conjures a **Frost Atronach** (6 HP, 1 Armor, slam d8, as the Spell Tome) to guard her. Within the Soul Cairn she cannot be permanently slain — the plane sustains her.
