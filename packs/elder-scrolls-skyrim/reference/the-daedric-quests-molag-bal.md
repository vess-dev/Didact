**Sphere:** domination, enslavement, brutality; Harvester of Souls.

**Hook.** Outside a long-abandoned house in **Markarth**, a **Vigilant of Stendarr**, **Tyranus**, is investigating Daedric activity and wants a second pair of hands. He is right to be afraid.

**The bargain & the twist.** The door seals behind the party. The Lord of Domination has them now, and his first lesson in helplessness is Tyranus himself: Molag Bal turns the Vigilant against the party (or drives him to despair), and he must be put down inside that house. A rusted, worthless mace waits on the altar — the Prince's promise. To make it the **Mace of Molag Bal**, the party must hunt down an old priest, **Logrolf the Willful**, drag him back to the altar, and **break him** — beat a defenseless man until his proud will shatters and he pledges his soul, then kill him. This is the cruelest frame in the book. A party that refuses keeps only a rusty club and its conscience; that is a perfectly good ending, and the Warden should offer it plainly.

**Beats.**

1. **The trap.** Enter with Tyranus; the door locks; the Prince speaks. Deal with Tyranus.
2. **The errand of cruelty.** Molag Bal sends the party to free Logrolf from a Forsworn cell (a short delve).
3. **The altar.** Bring Logrolf back. Submission, then the killing blow — or walk away.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Vigilant Tyranus** — devout, doomed; combatant. *Run as a* **Vigilant of Stendarr** *(stat block below).*
- **Logrolf the Willful** — an old, stubborn priest of Boethiah; a captive, not a threat. *If he is forced to fight, run as a* **Necromancer** *(6 HP).* The horror is that he cannot win.
- The cell that holds Logrolf is guarded by a small _detachment_ of **Forsworn** (or Bandits, by location).

**Reward.** **Mace of Molag Bal** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. On a hit, a WIL save or have will drained into the mace.
