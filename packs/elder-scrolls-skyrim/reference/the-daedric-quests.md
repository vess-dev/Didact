> *"The Princes do not love you. They are bored, and you are amusing."* — a Vigilant of Stendarr, before the inn burned

The Daedric Princes are the lords of Oblivion — immortal, unbound by the rules of the mortal world, and endlessly curious about those who must live by them. They cannot create, only meddle. So they meddle: they whisper through locked doors, slip pacts into a beggar's babble, leave a wonder on a shrine for whoever will earn it. What they want, always, is to be entertained — and to see what a mortal will do when the price of power is somebody else's soul.

This module is a **sandbox**, not a campaign. It is fifteen self-contained quest frames, one per Daedric Prince, and between them they hand out sixteen Daedric Artifacts (Hircine's frame holds two). Drop them anywhere. A party hears a rumor, finds a beacon, reads a strange pamphlet, or simply wanders past a roadside shrine — and one of the Princes takes an interest. Each frame is built to run in a single session.
