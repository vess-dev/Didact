### Karliah (the wronged Nightingale)

8 HP, 1 Armor, 11 STR, 15 DEX, 13 WIL, **Nightingale Bow** (d6, _bulky_), Nightingale Blade (d8)

- A Dunmer thief and Nightingale, lover of the murdered Gallus, exiled twenty-five years for a crime she did not commit. Patient, grief-worn, and deadly with a bow from the dark. The party's chief ally from Beat 4 onward.
- **Shadow-Shot**: From concealment or dim light her bow attack is _Enhanced_. She fights from the edges of a room, never the middle.
