The Thieves Guild of **Riften** is a shadow of its old self. Jobs go wrong. Marks slip away. Members whisper that the Guild is *cursed* — and they are nearer the truth than they know. Twenty-five years ago Guild Master **Gallus** was murdered, and his killer took his chair: **Mercer Frey**, the man who now runs the Guild from the **Ragged Flagon** in the **Ratway** beneath the city.

What no one living in the Cistern remembers is that the Guild's fortune was never luck at all. It was a *covenant*. The Guild's three best thieves were once the **Nightingales** — sworn agents of **Nocturnal**, Daedric Prince of shadow and luck — who guarded her shrine, the **Twilight Sepulcher**, and in return she let her favor run through the whole Guild. Mercer broke that covenant. He murdered Gallus, framed Gallus's lover **Karliah** for the deed, and crept into the Sepulcher to steal the **Skeleton Key**, the artifact that holds Nocturnal's luck in the world. With the Key gone, the Guild's fortune drains a little more each season, and Mercer grows rich on the side while the Cistern starves.

The party is recruited by **Brynjolf**, proves itself on jobs for the Black-Briar matriarch **Maven**, and is then dragged into the truth: Mercer's betrayal, exposed at **Snow Veil Sanctum** when he stabs them and leaves them for dead. Karliah — innocent all along — pulls them out of the dark, and together they take the Nightingale oath to hunt Mercer down. The chase ends in the Dwemer deep at **Irkngthand**, where Mercer dies clutching the **Eyes of the Falmer**, and the Skeleton Key passes back into the party's hands. It must be returned to the Twilight Sepulcher and Nocturnal's covenant restored — and when it is, the party stands as **Guild Master** over a Guild whose luck has finally turned.
