#### Ulfric Stormcloak

10 HP, 2 Armor, 16 STR, 13 DEX, 16 WIL, ornate war axe (d8), Stormcloak shield (counted in Armor)

- Jarl of Windhelm and leader of the rebellion: charismatic, uncompromising, a true believer in a free Skyrim — and a man whose pride and prejudice the war keeps excusing. A hero to his own, a tyrant-in-waiting to his foes.
- **The Voice (creature ability, not for players):** once per combat, in place of an attack, Ulfric Shouts a foe off their feet — the target makes a **STR save** or is hurled back and loses their next turn. (This is the Shout that killed High King Torygg; no PC may learn it.)
- On taking **Critical Damage** at the campaign's end, he does not die — beaten to his knees, he meets the headsman with a king's composure, and the choice to strike is the party's.
