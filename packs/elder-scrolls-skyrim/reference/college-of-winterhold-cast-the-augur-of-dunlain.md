### The Augur of Dunlain — the oracle in the Midden

A voice and a sphere of blue light; no body to strike.

- Once a brilliant student who reached too far into magic and became a being of pure prophecy, sealed in the freezing Midden. It speaks in riddles but does not lie, and it sees what the living cannot.
- It tells the party plainly: the Eye cannot be contained — only the **Staff of Magnus** can master it. Attacks against it are wasted; it is an oracle, not a monster. (If the Warden wants teeth, a careless or hostile approach blasts the offender for 1d6 WIL: *"You are not ready to know."*)
