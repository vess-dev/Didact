A pointcrawl of five stations, plus the two home bases the branch hangs on. Treat travel between them as Skyrim wilderness travel; the cold is always a threat (see Hazards).

- **Fort Dawnguard (Dayspring Canyon).** A half-ruined keep in a green canyon, reached through a long tunnel that hides it from the world. The Dawnguard home base. Begins crumbling and grows — a forge, a war-troll pen, a crossbow range — as Isran's order rebuilds. Sunlit and safe; a vampire PC is *not* welcome here.
- **Dimhollow Crypt.** A vast, water-flooded Nordic barrow east of Morthal. Draugr in the dark, Volkihar raiders ahead of the party, and at its heart the sealed monolith and the **brazier puzzle** that frees Serana.
- **Castle Volkihar.** A castle of black stone on a fog-bound island in the Sea of Ghosts, reached only by a ferryman. Elegant and rotten — a vampire court above, a flooded undercroft and a moondial **courtyard** below. The Volkihar home base, and the site of the climax. A mortal here walks among polite predators.
- **Forebears' Holdout.** A small cave where the vampire Malkus holds the Moth Priest Dexion. A quick, sharp fight.
- **Ancestor Glade.** A hidden, sun-dappled grove near Falkreath, full of luminous **Ancestor Moths**. No combat — a place of ritual, where the Scrolls are read.
- **The Soul Cairn.** A boundless plane of Oblivion under a dead, bruise-purple sky, where soul-trapped souls drift as glowing wisps and the **Ideal Masters** hoard them. Bone towers, purple lightning, and the undead dragon **Durnehviir** patrolling the Boneyard. The living do not belong here.
- **The Forgotten Vale (via Darkfall Cave).** A breathtaking glacial valley sealed from the world, blue ice and golden chantry-stone, frozen lakes and falls. Falmer and chaurus infest the ruins; **frost giants** wander the ice. Gelebor keeps the first wayshrine at the cave's mouth; five **wayshrines** ring the valley, leading to the **Inner Sanctum** where Vyrthur rules.
