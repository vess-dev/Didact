- **The Silver Hand Clock** (d6). How close the hunters are to gutting Jorrvaskr. Advance it when the party kills Silver Hand in the open (Gallows Rock, ambush sites), when a beast-blooded PC transforms where it can be witnessed and reported, or when the party lingers in Whiterun between barrow-jobs. **The hall falls when this clock fills (never before Kodlak has sent the party to the Coven in Beat 5) or when they return from the Coven, whichever comes first.** It is a pressure gauge on the murder that *will* come, not a way to dodge it.
- **The Harbinger's Hope** (a tally of witches' heads, 0–5). Kodlak's progress toward peace, and the party's stock of cures. Advance it for each **Glenmoril witch's head** the party carries out of the Coven. At the Flame of the Harbinger, **each head spends to cure one soul** — Kodlak's, or a beast-blooded PC's. Fewer heads than willing souls means someone stays a wolf.
