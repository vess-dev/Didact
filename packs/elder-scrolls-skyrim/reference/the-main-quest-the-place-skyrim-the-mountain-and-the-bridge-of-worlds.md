A whole province, plus two stranger places at the end. Key pointcrawl nodes:

- **Helgen.** A walled town in Falkreath Hold, now a smoking ruin. The campaign's spark; the keep's escape tunnel is the party's first dungeon.
- **Riverwood.** A river-mill village under Bleak Falls Barrow. Alvor (Imperial-leaning) and Gerdur (Stormcloak kin) outfit the party; the Sleeping Giant Inn is Delphine's front (Beat 5).
- **Bleak Falls Barrow.** A Nordic tomb above Riverwood: bandits up top, the pillar-puzzle, the webbed spider hall, the Draugr Overlord and the **Dragonstone** in the deep (Beat 2).
- **Whiterun & Dragonsreach.** The Jewel of the North, neutral in the war under **Jarl Balgruuf the Greater**. Dragonsreach is the court, the dragon-trap on the Great Porch (Beat 9), and the seat of Farengar's research. **Jorrvaskr** (the Companions) and the **Skyforge** stand nearby.
- **Western Watchtower.** A ruined tower on Whiterun's tundra; the site of the Mirmulnir fight and the soul-absorption (Beat 3).
- **High Hrothgar & the 7,000 Steps.** The Greybeards' monastery near the summit of the **Throat of the World**, reached by the long stair above **Ivarstead**. Cold, silent, immense (Beat 4, and the truce of Beat 6).
- **The Throat of the World (summit).** Above High Hrothgar: Paarthurnax's perch and the **Time-Wound** (Beats 7–8). The highest place in Skyrim.
- **Ustengrav.** A Nordic barrow in the Hjaalmarch marsh; the Horn that isn't there (Beat 5).
- **The Thalmor Embassy & the Ratway.** The High-Elf legation above Solitude (a social/stealth caper) and the rat-warren under Riften where Esbern hides (Beat 6).
- **The Karthspire & Sky Haven Temple.** A Forsworn-held canyon in the Reach guarding the Akaviri blood-sealed door to **Sky Haven Temple**, home of the Blades and **Alduin's Wall** — and the sword **Dragonbane** (Beat 6).
- **Alftand → Blackreach → the Tower of Mzark.** A frozen Dwemer ruin that drops into Blackreach, an enormous fungal cavern-world beneath Skyrim, lit by glowing motes. The Tower of Mzark holds the **Elder Scroll** (Beat 7). Falmer, chaurus, and Dwarven automatons throughout.
- **Skuldafn.** Alduin's dragon-priest temple, high in the eastern mountains, reachable only on a dragon's back. Draugr, a guardian Dragon, **Nahkriin**, and the portal to Sovngarde (Beat 10).
- **Sovngarde.** The Nordic hall of the honored dead: a misted valley, the **Hall of Valor**, the whale-bone bridge guarded by **Tsun**. Alduin feeds here. The final battlefield (Beat 11).
