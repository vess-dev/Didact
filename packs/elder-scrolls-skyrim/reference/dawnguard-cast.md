The NPCs who matter, with a one-line agenda each. Full stat blocks under **Threats**.

- **Isran** — founder of the Dawnguard. A grim, zealous Redguard who trusts no vampire, not even Serana, and would burn the world clean to be rid of them.
- **Serana** — the ancient Volkihar who carries an Elder Scroll; a Daughter of Coldharbour. Sardonic, lonely, four thousand years out of time. **The party's companion the whole campaign, on either path** (see Rewards). Her arc is choosing her own side against both parents.
- **Lord Harkon** — master of Castle Volkihar and the Volkihar clan. An elegant, ancient Vampire Lord who means to enact the Tyranny of the Sun and rule the eternal night. Charming until he isn't.
- **Valerica** — Serana's mother, an alchemist and conjurer who hid herself (and a Scroll) in the Soul Cairn to keep the prophecy from her husband. Cold, brilliant, estranged from both husband and daughter.
- **Dexion Evicus** — a **Moth Priest** of the Cult of the Ancestor Moth, come north from the White-Gold Tower; the one man in Skyrim trained to read an Elder Scroll. Reading Serana's costs him his sight. A prize both sides seize.
- **Knight-Paladin Gelebor** — the last living Snow Elf, keeper of the Vale's chantry of Auri-El, who guards **Auriel's Bow**.
- **Arch-Curate Vyrthur** — Gelebor's brother, the corrupted Snow Elf who authored the Tyranny of the Sun. The Vale's final foe.
- **Gunmar** *(Dawnguard track)* — a smith and beast-master who arms the order with trolls and steel.
- **Sorine Jurard** *(Dawnguard track)* — a Breton inventor who supplies the order's **crossbows**.
- **Garan Marethi** *(Volkihar track)* — Harkon's Dunmer adviser, who manages the court and (after the climax) the castle.
- **Lokil / Malkus** — lesser Volkihar; the raid-captain in Dimhollow and the vampire holding Dexion. Run as **Vampires** from the Bestiary.
