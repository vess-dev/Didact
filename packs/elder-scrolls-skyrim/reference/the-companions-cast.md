- **Kodlak Whitemane** — Harbinger, weary and wise; wants his soul freed from Hircine before he dies. The heart of the story. *Dies at Beat 6 — this cannot be prevented.*
- **Aela the Huntress** — Circle, and the truest believer in the wolf; glories in the Hunt, will never take the cure. Drives the revenge arc. Potential follower.
- **Skjor** — Circle, the old war-dog; gives the party the beast-blood and dies for his haste against the Silver Hand at Gallows Rock.
- **Vilkas** — Circle, the thoughtful twin; quick to anger, slow to trust, grieves Kodlak hard and leads the hunt to Driftshade. Open to the cure.
- **Farkas** — Circle, the gentle twin; escorts Proving Honor and reveals the wolf to save the party. Loyal to a fault. Open to the cure.
- **Eorlund Gray-Mane** — master smith of the Skyforge; reforges Wuuthrad. Not a fighter, but his work arms the whole hall.
- **The Glenmoril Witches** — hagraven coven who hold the keys to both curse and cure. They struck the pact with Hircine; they are not friends.
- **The Silver Hand** — fanatic werewolf-hunters; murderers who are not entirely wrong. They kill Kodlak and steal Wuuthrad.
- **Krev the Skinner** — the Silver Hand captain at Gallows Rock; flays beasts for trophies.
