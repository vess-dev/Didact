The campaign's combats are courtyard battles and city-storms, not bestiary delves. Run masses of soldiers as _detachments_ (attacks on a detachment by an individual are _Impaired_ unless _blast_; the detachment's attacks on individuals are _Enhanced_ with _blast_). Beyond the Cast's soldiers, draw on the **Bestiary**: **Draugr** and the crowned **Draugr Overlord** for Korvanjund (Beat 2); the **Ice Wraith** for Serpentstone Isle; **Bandit** detachments for the fort the party clears at initiation and for forts not yet garrisoned; **Forsworn**, **Forsworn Briarheart**, and **Hagraven** for the brutal taking of the Reach. A dragon may fall on a battle uninvited (the Dragon Crisis runs alongside this war) — a single **Dragon** as a _detachment_ turns any set-piece into chaos for both armies at once.
