**Sphere:** dreams and nightmares; omens, corruption.

**Hook.** The whole town of **Dawnstar** cannot sleep — a plague of shared nightmares. At the Windpeak Inn, a priest of Mara named **Erandur** says he knows the cause and offers to end it: the **Skull of Corruption**, a relic of Vaermina, drinks the townsfolk's dreams from inside the clifftop ruin of **Nightcaller Temple**.

**The bargain & the twist.** Erandur's secret is that he *was* one of Vaermina's priests — once called Casimir — who fled long ago when his order gassed everyone in the temple (their own brothers included) with a sleeping Miasma to repel an Orc raid. He means to destroy the Skull and atone. But Vaermina's voice tempts the party: the priest will betray you the moment the Skull is in reach; strike first, kill Erandur, and take the Skull for yourselves. The twist is the gut-check — Erandur is almost certainly telling the truth. Kill him on the Prince's word and you gain a Daedric artifact and lose an honest ally. Let him finish the rite and you destroy the Skull forever, gaining a steadfast companion instead.

**Beats.**

1. **Into the temple.** Fight through to a sealed library; find the book *The Dreamstride*.
2. **The Dreamstride.** Drink **Vaermina's Torpor** and walk Casimir's memory of the Miasma — the quest's haunted heart — to open the way forward.
3. **Old comrades.** Two surviving priests, **Veren** and **Thorek**, wait near the Skull, half-mad.
4. **The Skull's altar.** Vaermina speaks. Kill Erandur and take the Skull — or let him shatter it.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Erandur / Casimir** — penitent priest; a follower if spared, a victim if not. *Run as a* **Conjurer** *if it comes to blows (6 HP).*
- **Veren** and **Thorek** — broken Vaermina devotees; run as **Necromancers**.
- Long-sleeping **Orc** raiders may still stir in the depths — run as **Bandits**.

**Reward.** **Skull of Corruption** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. A staff that hurls stolen nightmares as living terror. *(Spare Erandur instead and the Skull is destroyed — the reward is the companion, not the relic.)*
