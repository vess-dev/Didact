Most foes are drawn straight from the **Bestiary** — **Miraak Cultist**, **Seeker**, **Lurker**, **Riekling**, **Ash Spawn**, **Ash Guardian**, **Werebear**, **Netch**, **Ash Hopper**, **Bristleback**, **Bandit** (for the colony's troubles), **Dragon** and **Frost Dragon**, and the Dwarven automatons of Nchardak (**Dwarven Spider**, **Dwarven Sphere**, **Dwarven Ballista**, **Dwarven Centurion**). Solstheim-specific entries follow.

**On dragons.** Every dragon is a _detachment_ (see the Bestiary). The dragon at **Saering's Watch** (Beat 4) and **Krosulhah** (an optional ambush around Beat 6) are ordinary **Dragons**. **Sahrotaar** (Beat 8) is the exception: he is *tamed* by a granted word as a story beat and fights **on the party's side** against Miraak — never roll to control him; the fiction does it.
