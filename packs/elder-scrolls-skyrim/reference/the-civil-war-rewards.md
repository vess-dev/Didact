The war pays in rank, gear, fame, and ground — the four things a soldier wants.

- **Military rank.** Each major mission steps the party's standing in their army. **Stormcloaks:** Unblooded → Ice-Veins → Bone-Breaker → Snow-Hammer → **Stormblade**. **Imperial Legion:** Auxiliary → Quaestor → Praefect → Tribune → **Legate**. Each step comes with a weapon from the quartermaster: a tempered blade (one die step up — see Crafting), or a blade with a minor enchantment (a Relic of 1 or 2 charges).
- **War-gear & Septims.** Beyond the rank-weapons: the initiation kit (Stormcloak fur-and-hide or a set of Imperial armor + shield), looted officer's mail (2 Armor, _bulky_), and a soldier's pay in Septims for each mission (3d6 × the mission's weight, Warden's call).
- **Faction Reputation.** The party's army holds at **Trusted (+1)** from the oath until the final assault makes them **Sworn (+2)** — one meaningful favor per session thereafter (a patrol delayed, a gate opened, a Hold's guard looking away). The enemy army drops to **Hunted (−2)** after Whiterun and attacks the party on sight in its remaining ground.
- **Renown.** Public victories (Whiterun, the capital) each move **Renown +1** (the per-session cap). A party that wins the war for its banner ends **Renowned** or **Hero of Skyrim** — strangers default Kind, doors open everywhere. (Killing soldiers of the enemy *army* in open war does not lower Renown; massacring surrendered men or the innocent will.)
- **A grateful (or installed) Jarl.** The victor's Jarls owe the party. This means **Thaneship** in a won Hold (most readily **Eastmarch/Windhelm** for the Legion, by canon), a housecarl, and a standing welcome in the Jarl's hall — treat the Jarl as a faction at **+1 or +2** under *Faction Reputation*, at the Warden's option. A deposed Jarl's loyalists, of course, will not forget.
- **A Hold to call home.** With a friendly or installed Jarl, the party may buy land and raise a homestead in the won Holds — **Lakeview Manor** (Falkreath), **Windstad Manor** (Hjaalmarch), or **Heljarchen Hall** (the Pale) — and build it up under the **Settlements** rules. War-favor eases the steward's price and the Hold's protection; but a homestead that flew the losing banner finds the new Jarl cold, and a settlement astride a strategic road can be garrisoned or burned by the war itself. A wise party keeps one eye on which way its Hold is leaning.
