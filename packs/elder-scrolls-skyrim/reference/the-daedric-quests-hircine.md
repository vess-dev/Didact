**Sphere:** the Hunt and the chase; progenitor of lycanthropy.

**Hook.** A child has been murdered in **Falkreath**, and a man named **Sinding** sits in the town jail — a werewolf who killed without meaning to, because he stole the **Ring of Hircine**, which Hircine cursed to force the change on him at the worst moments. He begs the party to help him make it right.

**The bargain & the twist.** Sinding stole from Hircine, and the Prince of the Hunt has made him prey. Sinding gives the party the cursed ring before he breaks out of his cell; soon after, a great white stag — the **Aspect of Hircine** — bids them hunt him through **Bloated Man's Grotto**. Here the moral fork is sharp and clean: **hunt Sinding down** and skin him for the Prince (Hircine makes his pelt into the **Savior's Hide**, the reward of the hunter who brought down the quarry), or **spare Sinding** and turn your blades with him on the other hunters Hircine sent (Hircine, amused, lifts the curse from the **Ring of Hircine** and lets you keep it). Murder and mercy each have a reward; the Prince enjoys the chase either way.

**Beats.**

1. **The jail.** Hear Sinding's story; he hands over the cursed Ring and slips away.
2. **The white stag.** In the wilds, the Aspect of Hircine appears and names the hunt: find Sinding in the Grotto.
3. **Bloated Man's Grotto.** Hircine's other hunters are already there, after the same quarry.
4. **The choice.** Kill Sinding (→ Savior's Hide), or side with Sinding against the hunters (→ Ring of Hircine, its curse lifted).

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Sinding** — a haunted, sincere werewolf; *run as a* **Werewolf** *in beast form.* Foe or desperate ally, by the party's choice.
- **Hircine's Hunters** — a band sent to claim Sinding; run as a _detachment_ of **Bandit**s (or Silver Hand, if you prefer hunters of monsters).
- The **Aspect of Hircine** — a spectral stag; voice and omen, not a fight.

**Reward.** Hunt Sinding → **Savior's Hide** (*Unique Armors*). Spare Sinding → **Ring of Hircine** (*Unique Armors*). One quest, two artifacts, one decision.
