### Sahloknir (the Kynesgrove dragon)

Run as a **Dragon**, but resurrected at full vigor by Alduin in front of the party. Alduin himself **cannot be harmed in this scene** — he is established as untouchable until the Time-Wound. He resurrects Sahloknir, mocks the party, and flies off; the party fights Sahloknir alone (Beat 5).
