### Lord Harkon (Vampire Lord)

18 HP, 1 Armor, 16 STR, 14 DEX, 18 WIL, raking claws (d8+d8), Vampiric Drain (d8, _blast_, _ranged_)

- The master of Castle Volkihar in his towering, winged Vampire Lord form — an ancient blood-mage who would unmake the sun. The campaign's final foe on **both** paths.
- Immune to poison; **fire against him is _Enhanced_**; sunlight leaves him Deprived. His drain heals him for the damage dealt. Each round he may **summon a Gargoyle**, **raise the dead**, or **Vampiric Grip** a foe (a STR save or be dragged in and lose its next action). While he hovers, melee from grounded foes is _Impaired_ unless they reach him.
- **The altar-ward**: in the Cathedral, Harkon shields himself with a font of blood-magic — mundane attacks against him are _Impaired_ until the ward is broken. **Auriel's Bow** (or the holy light of Isran's aura) shatters it; then he is vulnerable.
- **Critical Damage**: his paralyzing bite drains d8 WIL and the target loses its next action.
