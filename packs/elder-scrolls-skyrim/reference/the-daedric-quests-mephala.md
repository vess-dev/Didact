**Sphere:** murder, lies, sex and secrets; the Webspinner.

**Hook.** Gossip in Whiterun's Bannered Mare turns to the Jarl's troubled youngest son, **Nelkir**. The boy is sullen and secretive, and when the party speaks with him he confides that a **Whispering Lady** talks to him through a sealed door in the cellars of **Dragonsreach**.

**The bargain & the twist.** The voice is **Mephala**, Prince of secrets and quiet murder, and behind that door lies the **Ebony Blade**. Mephala will give it freely — that is the trap. The Blade is a dull, weak thing until it is **fed on betrayal**: it grows keen only as its wielder murders the people who *trust* them. Mephala's true gift is not the sword but the appetite it breeds. The twist plays out after the quest, every time the party is tempted to sharpen the Blade on a friend, an ally, a trusting stranger. Getting the Blade is easy; the moral cost is paid slowly, in everyone who ever believed in you. (To open the door the party needs the Jarl's key — pickpocketed from **Balgruuf**, or taken from his wizard **Farengar**, whom Nelkir slyly notes "won't be missed.")

**Beats.**

1. **The brooding boy.** Learn of the Whispering Lady from Nelkir.
2. **The door.** Mephala speaks; she wants her Blade carried out into the world.
3. **The key.** Lift it from Balgruuf or Farengar (theft, guile, or worse).
4. **The Blade.** Open the door and take the Ebony Blade — its hunger comes with it.

**Key NPCs & foes.**

- **Nelkir** — a corrupted child; harmless, unsettling.
- **Jarl Balgruuf** and **Farengar Secret-Fire** — obstacles, not foes (unless the party makes them so; Farengar runs as a **Conjurer**, and turning Dragonsreach hostile is a Hold-level catastrophe).
- **Mephala** — a silken voice behind the door.

**Reward.** **Ebony Blade** — see its card in *Unique Weapons*. It heals its wielder, and grows keener with every trusting soul it slays.
