A loose pointcrawl. Whiterun is the hub; the dungeons hang off it.

- **Jorrvaskr (Whiterun, Wind District).** The mead hall — an overturned longship hull, nearly five thousand years old, loud with feasting and brawling. The Companions' home and the party's. Living quarters below; the training yard out back. **Kodlak's** quarters are here; so is the moment of his death.
- **The Skyforge.** The ancient forge on the rock above Jorrvaskr, hotter and older than any other in Skyrim, worked by **Eorlund Gray-Mane**, the finest smith in the Hold. Skyforge Steel comes from here; here Wuuthrad is reforged.
- **The Underforge.** A hidden cave beneath the Skyforge, reached by a concealed door only the Circle knows. A stone basin and a font of beast-blood. Where the wolf is given.
- **Dustman's Cairn.** A Nordic barrow northwest of Whiterun — draugr, a sealing-lever trap, and the **Silver Hand** ambush that exposes the Circle's secret. Holds a Fragment of Wuuthrad.
- **Glenmoril Coven (Falkreath Hold).** A misty cave of hagravens deep in the southern pines, where the witches who struck the Hircine pact still dwell. Source of the curse and the cure. Take heads here.
- **Gallows Rock.** A Silver Hand stronghold — a grim fort hung with werewolf pelts. **Krev the Skinner** commands; **Skjor** dies here.
- **Driftshade Refuge (Winterhold Hold, southeast of Dawnstar).** The Silver Hand den that holds the stolen Wuuthrad fragments after Jorrvaskr falls — traps, caged beasts, and the hunters' last stand.
- **Ysgramor's Tomb (Winterhold).** The frozen resting place of the founder of the Companions, far in the north. Sealed by the statue of Ysgramor, which only the reforged Wuuthrad can open. Within: Companion ghosts, frostbite spiders, and the **Flame of the Harbinger**, where souls are judged and cleansed.
