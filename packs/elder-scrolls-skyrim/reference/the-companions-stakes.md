The party comes up through the ranks, is offered the beast-blood, and inherits a dying man's last wish. Kodlak's murder cannot be prevented — it is the hinge of the story — but everything after is the party's to settle: whether to embrace the wolf or seek the cure, whether the Circle's secret survives, and whether one old Nord's soul reaches Sovngarde or hunts forever under the moons. By the end, the party's own beast-blood is the central question, and the Harbinger's chair is empty for them to fill.
