*A red-haired stranger in the Riften market wants a quiet favor, and one favor becomes a place in the Guild beneath the streets. But the Thieves Guild is dying — its luck has soured, its vault stands empty, and the rot starts at the very top. Follow the coin far enough and you will find a traitor who sold his oath to a Daedric Prince, a wronged woman who has waited twenty-five years to be believed, and a black key that the dark itself wants returned.*
