Raising the manor uses the **Settlements** building rules unchanged. Each structure is **one filled building slot = one Downtime Action + a sum in Septims + gathered materials**. The "materials" are the canon trio — **quarried stone, sawn logs, and clay** — drawn from the on-site quarry, log pile, and clay deposit, plus bought iron fittings, nails, glass, and goods. They are *ordinary inventory and labor, costed in Septims*; they are **not** a new tracked resource. A PC spends a Downtime Action to haul and raise; the Warden names the Septim figure from the fiction (a watchtower is dearer than a chicken coop, per Settlements).

Below is a sensible *order of building* and how each step maps to a **Settlements building slot** and the **Pop / Def / Prosperity** dials. Every effect named here is the Settlements effect — this frame adds only the rough Septim guide and the Hearthfire flavor. Slots and the upkeep rule (filled slots may not exceed **Population × 2** without decay) are tracked exactly as in Settlements.

### Step 1 — the Main Hall (slot already filled)

The starter cottage. With a Downtime Action and materials it is finished out into the proper **Longhouse / Mead Hall** of Settlements — the heart of the steading. Reaction rolls on a first visit shift one step better; a fresh rumor is always available; the party may make a *Strengthening Ties* Downtime Action with the household here.

- *Rough cost:* ~1,000 Septims of finishing (the 5,000 deed bought the frame).
- *Dial:* this is the steading's heart, not a Pop/Def/Prosperity bump on its own.

### Step 2 — the wings (the great house grows)

The main hall throws out wings. Each wing is **one Downtime Action + materials + Septims** and fills **one building slot** unless marked _petty_, taking the named Settlements function. (Raise **Cottages** or a **Livestock Pen** from Step 3 before a second wing, or the upkeep rule bites.)

| Wing built (the fiction) | Settlements slot filled | Effect (per Settlements) | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Smithy / forge wing** | **Smithy** | +1 Prosperity. Unlocks the **Smithing** Downtime Action on-site; the resident smith may temper one piece of party gear per visit. | ~1,500 Septims + ore/ingots |
| **Alchemy laboratory** | **Alchemy Lab** | Unlocks the **Alchemy** Downtime Action on-site; a brew costs half the usual reagents per batch. | ~1,500 Septims + reagents |
| **Enchanter's tower** | **Arcane Enchanter** | Unlocks the **Enchanting** Downtime Action on-site, given a filled soul gem. | ~2,000 Septims + a filled soul gem |
| **Greenhouse** | **Apothecary's Garden** (_petty_ — no slot) | A plot of alchemy reagents: gather a handful of common ingredients each visit without foraging. | ~1,500 Septims of glass + frames |
| **Kitchen / smokehouse** | **Cookfire & Smokehouse** | Raw meat from the bestiary becomes lasting Rations here. | ~800 Septims |
| **Storage / cellar / armory** | **Armory & Forge-rack** | +1 Defense. The militia's gear steps up one tier; a PC in good standing may draw one common weapon per season. (If the party only wants storage, a plain cellar is a _petty_ comfort — see Rewards.) | ~1,500 Septims |
| **Library** | (_petty_ furnishing — no slot) | A reading-room: a safe, quiet place to read **Skill Books**, and shelf-space for found tomes. | ~1,500 Septims |

> Build only what the party uses. **Watch the upkeep rule:** until Population rises, filled slots may not exceed Pop × 2 or one building falls into disrepair each season (Settlements). _petty_ comforts — the greenhouse, the library shelves, a well, a shrine niche — never count against this.

### Step 3 — the land & defenses (when the raids start)

Once there is something worth raiding, build outward. Each is a Settlements slot unless marked _petty_:

| Built (the fiction) | Settlements slot | Effect | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cottages / bunkhouse (beds)** | **Cottages (beds)** | +1 Population. Without beds, new settlers — and adopted children — do not stay. **A child needs a bed.** | ~800 Septims each |
| **Palisade & gate** | **Palisade & Gate** | Defense +1. Required for any Defense above 2. | ~1,500 Septims |
| **Watchtower** | **Watchtower** | +1 Defense (with a palisade); raids spotted one watch early. | ~2,000 Septims |
| **Stable & paddock** | **Stable & Paddock** (_petty_ — no slot) | Quarters mounts; a fresh horse waits when the party sets out. | ~1,000 Septims |
| **Farm plots / livestock pen** | **Farm Plots** / **Livestock Pen** | +1 Prosperity / +1 Population. Free Rations / the steading feeds itself. (Crops fail in **Extreme** weather.) | ~800 Septims each |
| **Unique outbuilding** (apiary / hatchery / mill) | **Trading Post** or **Mill** (Warden's pick by plot) | +1 Prosperity. A standing resource — honey, roe, or flour — that caravans route to. | ~2,000 Septims |

> A grand finished manor is, on the page, simply a homestead with its **eight slots steadily filling** and **Pop / Def / Prosperity climbing**. When the family, the guards, and the trade arrive, raise the dials per Settlements — no separate scorecard.
