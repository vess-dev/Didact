### Dexion Evicus (Moth Priest)

6 HP, 13 STR, 11 DEX, 14 WIL, staff (d6)

- A mild Imperial Moth Priest, trained to read an Elder Scroll without being unmade — and blind from Beat 5 on. A captive, then a guide. He does not fight if he can help it.
- Reading Serana's Scroll in haste (Beat 5) blinds him for good. For the three Scrolls (Beat 7) he talks a PC through the Ancestor Moth rite instead; a reader who skips the rite must pass a WIL save or be blinded and lose all action for the scene. On the Volkihar path he reads while enthralled, his will not his own.
