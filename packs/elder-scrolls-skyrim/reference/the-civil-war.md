*Skyrim is two countries wearing one map. In Windhelm a Jarl who killed his king calls himself a liberator; in Solitude a general from the south calls that murder. Between them lie seven more Holds, and every one of them is choosing — or being made to choose. You will ride for one banner, take a fort in the snow, hold the gates of Whiterun while the city burns around you, and at the end you will stand in the enemy's capital and watch a man kneel. Somewhere above Solitude, behind warded glass, a smiling elf reads the casualty lists and is well pleased.*
