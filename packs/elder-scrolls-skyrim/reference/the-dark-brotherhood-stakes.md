A family, an Emperor, and the party's souls. On the join-path, the Brotherhood either rises from its own ashes with the party as its Listener, or dies in the fire its own leader lit; the Empire loses its Emperor, and Skyrim's war tilts on the murder. On the other path, the party ends the Dark Brotherhood in Skyrim for good.

**The branch is the whole point.** At the second beat, in the abandoned shack, the party chooses: *kill one of the captives and join,* or *kill Astrid and walk out to destroy the Brotherhood for the Empire.* The first choice is this entire campaign. The second is a short, cold demolition that ends everything below. Make the weight of that single knife land.
