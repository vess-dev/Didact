### Soul Cairn Keeper

12 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 14 WIL, spectral greatsword (d10, _bulky_), soul-fire bolt (d8, _ranged_)

- A towering, faceless warden of the Boneyard, one of three who hold Valerica's barrier. Tall as two men, wrapped in tattered death-light.
- Immune to poison; mundane attacks against it are _Impaired_; fire and sunlight against it are _Enhanced_. While even one Keeper stands, the barrier around Valerica holds — drop all three to free her.
- When a Keeper falls it bursts in a gout of cold soul-fire: d6, _blast_, against everything adjacent.
