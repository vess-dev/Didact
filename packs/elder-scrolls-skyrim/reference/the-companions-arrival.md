The party is in or near **Whiterun**. Pick a hook:

- **The giant at the farm.** Out on the tundra, **Aela**, **Farkas**, and the whelp **Ria** are squaring off against a **giant** that has wandered onto Pelagia Farm. They could use a fourth blade. Fight well and Aela tells you where warriors drink.
- **Word of work.** A caravan guard, a tavern-keeper at the Bannered Mare, or a Hold guard mentions that Jorrvaskr "always needs strong arms," and the pay is honest.
- **Faction tie.** A PC with the Companion background already belongs to the hall and starts at **Companions +1**; a Nord Reaver, Housecarl, or Sellsword has heard the old stories and means to earn a seat at that table.

However they arrive, the road ends at the doors of Jorrvaskr, where Kodlak Whitemane is waiting to size them up.
