- **The Long War** (d6). Ticks each session the war drags on without a Hold changing hands, and whenever a truce stalls. At 4, the Thalmor openly "advise": Justiciars ride with the Legion's patrols and hunt Talos-worshippers in every town, whichever side the party serves. At 6, the Dominion has what it wanted — both armies are bled so thin that bandits, Forsworn, and dragons roam the Holds unchecked until the war is ended.
- **Whiterun's Neutrality** (d4). Ticks with each mission the party runs before Beat 3. At 4, the Stormcloaks strike before the Legion can garrison the city: a Legion party finds Whiterun already fallen and runs Beat 4 as the attackers. (A Stormcloak party is the attacker either way — for them the clock only measures how much warning Balgruuf has had.)
