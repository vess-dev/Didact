The hook finds the party anywhere in Skyrim. Two **Miraak Cultists** in bone-and-wood masks walk up out of a crowd or off the road, declare them the "false Dragonborn," and attack without a word of parley (use the Bestiary entry; they travel in pairs). On a corpse is a note — *Kill the false Dragonborn* — and orders pointing to **Solstheim**.

Passage is bought at the **Windhelm docks** from **Gjalund Salt-Sage**, master of the *Northern Maiden*, who is reluctant, overcharges, and has been to the island lately and does not like to talk about it. The crossing of the Sea of Ghosts sets the party down at **Raven Rock**.

A PC with the Skaal Hunter background has come home to find the Stones falling silent, and starts at **Skaal +1**; a Telvanni Mage may already know the way to Tel Mithryn.
