### Cicero (the Keeper)

8 HP, 1 Armor, 12 STR, 16 DEX, 13 WIL, dual daggers (d6+d6)

- The jester-clad Imperial Keeper of the Night Mother's body, devoted past the edge of sanity — by turns giggling, weeping, and lethal. A wildcard whose loyalty is to the corpse, not the cell.
- **Whirling Frenzy:** when reduced to half HP or less, his attacks are _Enhanced_ for the rest of the combat as he laughs and lunges.
- **Spared:** if spared in Beat 7, he later limps back to serve a true Listener faithfully — a strange, capering ally in the rebuilt family.
