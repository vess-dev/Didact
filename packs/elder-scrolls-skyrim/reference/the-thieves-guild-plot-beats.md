Broad strokes. Six to ten sessions. Reorder, compress, or skip as the table pushes — each beat is a turning point, not a script. The hinge of the whole campaign is Beat 4: the betrayal at Snow Veil Sanctum.

1. **A Chance Arrangement.** Brynjolf approaches the party in the Riften market and proposes a small crime: he will draw the crowd's eye while a PC lifts **Madesi's silver ring** from his locked strongbox and plants it on the trader **Brand-Shei**. A clean job (a DEX save under the crowd's nose) earns 100 Septims and an invitation down into the Ratway. A botched one means a Riften Bounty and a harder welcome — but Brynjolf likes nerve either way.
2. **Taking Care of Business.** Down in the **Ragged Flagon**, Brynjolf sets the party loose on three deadbeat debtors — the innkeeper **Keerava**, the pawnbroker **Bersi Honey-Hand**, and the bunkhouse-keeper **Haelga**. Each can be leaned on (a WIL save to intimidate), have a prized possession threatened, or simply beaten in a brawl. Collect all three and the party earns a permanent place in the Cistern and a set of **Thieves Guild armor** each (light armor, 1 Armor — dark, quiet, and a friend in every shadow).
3. **Proving Yourself — the Jobs.** **Mercer Frey** himself sets the party two scores that secretly serve **Maven Black-Briar**. Run them in either order.
   - **Loud and Clear (Goldenglow Estate).** The bee-farm island of **Goldenglow** has stopped paying Maven; its owner Aringoth has hired mercenaries. The party infiltrates (a sewer entrance, if **Vex** is asked), burns *exactly three* of the bee hives — no more, or the reward sours — cracks Aringoth's safe, and lifts the **Bill of Sale**, which proves someone bought the estate to spite Mercer.
   - **Dampened Spirits (Honningbrew Meadery).** Maven wants the rival **Honningbrew Meadery** ruined. With the schemer **Mallus Maccius** the party poisons the brewing vat and the skeever nest below it, fights the mad wizard **Hamelyn** in the cellar, and frames the owner Sabjorn. Spare everyone but Hamelyn and Maven pays in an enchanted blade.
   - The trail of the Goldenglow sale leads (via the fence **Gulum-Ei** in Solitude) to a single, chilling fact: a *woman with a grudge against Mercer* is bankrolling moves against the Guild. Mercer names her — **Karliah**, the thief who murdered Gallus and fled twenty-five years ago — and sends the party to corner her at Snow Veil Sanctum.
4. **The Betrayal — Speaking With Silence (Snow Veil Sanctum).** Mercer joins the party as a companion and leads them into the Nordic barrow of **Snow Veil Sanctum**, past draugr and Karliah's tripwires, to a sealed puzzle-door he alone can open. Beyond it, Karliah ambushes them — but her paralyzing arrow takes *the party*, and in the frozen moment that follows they overhear the truth: **Mercer murdered Gallus.** Mercer runs them through and leaves them for dead in the dark. *(Mechanically: a scripted defeat. The party does not win this fight — Mercer flees regardless. If a Warden prefers, run it as a forced Retreat where Mercer escapes no matter the dice.)* The party wakes to **Karliah** standing over them, the wronged woman revealed innocent at last, holding **Gallus's encoded journal** and asking for their help.
5. **Hard Answers — Reading the Dead Man's Journal.** Karliah sends the party to the mage **Enthir** at the College of Winterhold, and Enthir to **Calcelmo** in Markarth, who alone holds a key to Gallus's cipher (it is written in the Falmer tongue). The party breaks into **Calcelmo's** Dwemer museum and laboratory, makes a rubbing of the **Falmer Translation Guide**, and slips out. Enthir decodes the journal: Mercer was a **Nightingale**, he looted the Guild's vault for years, and he desecrated the **Twilight Sepulcher** — Nocturnal's shrine — stealing the **Skeleton Key**. *(Reward: the* **Nightingale Blade** *can be found or granted in this stretch.)*
6. **The Pursuit — Riftweald Manor.** Back in Riften, Karliah lays the journal before a stunned Guild and **Brynjolf** opens the Cistern vault: it is *empty*. Mercer robbed them all. The party breaks into Mercer's house, **Riftweald Manor**, past his hired bruiser **Vald** and a gauntlet of traps, and recovers his plans — he means one last, impossible heist: the **Eyes of the Falmer**, two flawless gemstones the size of a man's head, in the lost Dwemer city of **Irkngthand**. *(The unique sword* **Chillrend** *waits in a case here.)*
7. **Become a Nightingale — Trinity Restored (Nightingale Hall).** To stop a Nightingale, you must become one. Karliah leads the party and Brynjolf to the hidden **Nightingale Hall** by the Shadow Stone. There each dons a suit of **Nightingale Armor**, and Karliah summons **Nocturnal** herself in the oath chamber at the Hall's heart. The party and Brynjolf swear the Nightingale oath; Nocturnal accepts them as her agents and makes plain the price of the bargain: the **Skeleton Key** must be returned to the Sepulcher, or her luck stays broken forever. Then they ride for Irkngthand.
8. **Blindsighted — Irkngthand.** The party descends into **Irkngthand**, deepest of the Dwemer ruins, through Dwarven automatons, blind **Falmer** war-bands, and their **chaurus** beasts. They reach the great Falmer statue just in time to watch **Mercer** pry the **Eyes of the Falmer** from its sockets — and collapse the ledge beneath them. The final fight is **Mercer Frey**, who turns invisible, twists shadows, and tries to turn Brynjolf against Karliah with stolen Nightingale tricks. Cut him down and take the **Skeleton Key** and the **Eyes** from his body, then escape the chamber as it floods, out through Bronze Water Cave. *(Reward: Karliah gives the* **Nightingale Bow***.)*
9. **Darkness Returns — the Twilight Sepulcher.** The party carries the **Skeleton Key** to the **Twilight Sepulcher** in Falkreath Hold, Nocturnal's true shrine, where the dead Nightingales stand guard. There the spirit of **Gallus** himself waits, a Nightingale in death as in life. The party walks the **Pilgrim's Path** — a trial in which stepping out of shadow into the light is *lethal* — past Nightingale Sentinels who do not know the war is over, and sets the Key back into the **Ebonmere**, the well of shadow that opens on Nocturnal's realm. Nocturnal's luck floods back into the world, Gallus is freed to his rest, and each Nightingale chooses an **Agent's gift** at the Ebonmere (see Rewards).
10. **Under New Management — Guild Master.** With the Key returned and the Guild's fortune restored, the party finishes rebuilding the Guild's reach across the Holds (a handful of jobs from **Delvin Mallory** and **Vex**). In a ceremony in the Cistern, **Brynjolf** names the party **Guild Master**. They receive the **Guild Master's Armor** from the fence **Tonilia**, the key to the Guild's tribute chest, and a standing cut of every score the Guild pulls in Riften and beyond.
