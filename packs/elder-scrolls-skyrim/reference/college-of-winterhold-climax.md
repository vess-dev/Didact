The Hall of the Elements, the Eye blazing, Ancano fused to it behind the force-field.

- **Break the field.** A PC must turn the **Staff of Magnus** against the field — a spent charge, or a full action holding the beam (the Warden's call) — to collapse the barrier and leave Ancano exposed and reeling. Periodically he tries to re-seal it; a held burst of the Staff keeps it down.
- **The duel.** Then it is a fight against the empowered Ancano (see Threats). When he drops, his stolen power detonates: d6 _blast_ to everyone nearby.
- **The aftermath.** **Quaranir** and the Psijics appear and take the Eye away to safekeeping "where no mortal hand can misuse it" — and with **Tolfdir**, the College names the party (or its lead caster) **Arch-Mage of the College of Winterhold**.
- **What becomes of the College.** Depending on how the party treated the Synod (Beat 4), the survivors, and the town of Winterhold during the anomalies (Beat 5), the College ends the campaign either rebuilt and respected — Renown shifts one step better, and the town's Reaction to the mages shifts a step warmer — or isolated and feared, with Jarl Korir and the townsfolk as Wary as ever. The title is the same; the welcome is not.

### Running Notes

- **The Thalmor watch who reads what.** Ancano is the villain, but he is also a reminder that magic is political. The Thalmor's interest in the Eye, the College, and the party's tomes can seed a sequel, or tie into the Civil War and Main Quest arcs.
- **No player Shouts.** The draugr lords use Shout-flavored attacks as creature abilities; the party gains no Thu'um from this campaign. Their power is the Staff, their tomes, and the Arch-Mage's seat.
- **Magic is Cairn magic.** Every spell here is a **Spell Tome** (both hands, read aloud, +1 Fatigue, WIL save under duress) or a **Relic** (charges and a Recharge). The Labyrinthian curse and the Eye's force-field are scene rules laid on top of the game, never changes to it.
- **Let the scholars be scholars.** The best moments here are not the boss fights but the parley with Urag, the Augur's riddle, Paratus at the broken Oculory, and the awful quiet after the Arch-Mage dies. Reward the players who think their way through the College as richly as the ones who fight through Labyrinthian.
