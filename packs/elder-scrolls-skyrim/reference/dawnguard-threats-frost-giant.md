### Frost Giant

14 HP, 2 Armor, 18 STR, 9 DEX, 8 WIL, ice-crusted club (d10, _bulky_), _detachment_

- A pale, hulking giant of the Forgotten Vale's ice fields, frost rimed across its hide. Solitary and territorial; it ignores those who keep their distance.
- A _detachment_. Frost damage against it is _Impaired_. Its club sends a foe flying — a struck target makes a STR save or is knocked prone and loses its next action.
- **Critical Damage**: the blow shatters bone; the target loses d6 STR.
