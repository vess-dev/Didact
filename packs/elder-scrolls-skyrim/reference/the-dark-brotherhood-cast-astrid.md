### Astrid (Sanctuary Leader)

10 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 15 DEX, 14 WIL, **Blade of Woe** (d6; see Unique Weapons), or a poisoned dagger (d6; the first creature it wounds each fight must pass a STR save or take 1d6 STR)

- The fierce, pragmatic Nord who leads the last Sanctuary and loves her family more than the tenets that bind it — which is exactly why she betrays it. Charismatic, controlling, doomed.
- **Vanish:** once per scene, Astrid may drop into shadow and disappear, ending any attack against her this round; she reappears where she likes at the start of the next.
- **In Beat 8** she has no fight left in the burning wreck — she is already dying, and asks only for a clean ending and the chance to pass on the Blade of Woe.
