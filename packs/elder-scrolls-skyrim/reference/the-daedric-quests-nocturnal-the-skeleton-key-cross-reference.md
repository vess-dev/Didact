**Sphere:** night, darkness, mystery; the Ur-dra.

Nocturnal's Skyrim Artifact, the **Skeleton Key** — the unbreakable lockpick that bends fortune itself — is **not won here.** It is taken from the traitor Mercer Frey and carried (and returned) over the course of the **Thieves Guild** arc, when the party is sworn into the Nightingales as Nocturnal's agents. Run that story in **The Thieves Guild**; the Key's card lives in **Relics**. Keeping it withers the Guild's luck until it is returned to the Twilight Sepulcher — a frame in itself, but a thief's, not a Daedra-cultist's.
