Sooner or later a party claims a place. It starts as a ruin they cleared, a crossroads they defended, a homestead they inherited from a dying farmer. The settlement system lets an Overseer track what the place is without turning the game into a spreadsheet.

A settlement has three attributes, each on a 1–5 scale: **Population**, **Defense**, **Industry**. It has up to **eight building slots** by default, with room to grow. _Petty_ buildings (the small amenities — jukeboxes, streetlamps, picnic areas) do not consume a slot. That's the whole model.
