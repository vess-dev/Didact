| Karma | Name | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **+3** | *Messiah* | A name spoken around settler campfires. Children draw the party from memory. Ghouls stop and watch you pass. |
| **+2** | *Honored* | Strangers default to Kind on Reactions. A settler in need will approach the party unprompted. |
| **+1** | *Good* | Small favors offered. A local will volunteer directions, a free meal, a bed. |
| **0** | *Anonymous* | Default. The wasteland has no opinion yet. |
| **−1** | *Shady* | Strangers are Wary on first meeting. Doors close as the party approaches a settlement. |
| **−2** | *Infamous* | Strangers default to Hostile on Reactions. Settlements bar the gate. |
| **−3** | *Hated* | The party is a wasteland boogeyman. Strangers flee on sight. Parents hush children. Bounty hunters pick up the scent. |
