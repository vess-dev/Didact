title: "When to Call a Save"
tags: [saves]
source: overseers-guide/saves#when-to-call-a-save
mechanics: [save]
---
- The action is risky *and* the outcome is genuinely uncertain. If it isn't risky, let it happen. If it isn't uncertain, say what happens.
- The player has described what they're doing and the Overseer has described what's at stake.
- The situation is dynamic — a frozen situation that will never change isn't risky, it's a puzzle.
