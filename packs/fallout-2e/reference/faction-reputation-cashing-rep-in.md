**Liked and above** unlocks small things the Overseer should offer in the fiction: a door that opens, a rumor shared free, a merchant pulling out the good stock, a ride on a caravan wagon, a bed in the barracks.

**Idolized** is a resource the PCs can spend. Once per session, the party may ask the faction for one meaningful favor: a squad of troopers as a distraction, a Vertibird pickup, a sealed door opened, a bounty withdrawn. The party stays Idolized; the faction simply grants no second favor that session while the chits are called in.
