The party — Lena, Wren, and the Ranger — have entered **Subway Station Ossa-7**, a collapsed pre-war metro rumored to hold a stash of fusion cells. Only the Ranger has a flashlight lit. The first room is presented as follows:

### The Concourse

A broad, low-ceilinged concourse open to the **night sky** through a ceiling collapse. **Rubble** is piled near the center. A toppled **vending machine** is rusted shut on the west wall. A set of **turnstiles** runs the width of the room, still operable. A dark **stairwell** descends to the south.

- **Air**: The ceiling collapse admits a cold, damp wind and starlight.
- **Rubble**: Dust and chunks of concrete. A faded poster, half-buried, still shows a clean man and his clean children and the word **VAULT**.
- **Vending Machine**: Jammed shut. Will come open with a crowbar or determined effort. Contains three cans of pre-war beans (still edible, if awful) and a hand-written note that says, *"If you find this, we tried."*
- **Turnstiles**: Clicking as the wind moves them, a steady metallic tick. Any metal object dragged across activates all of them with a loud clang — almost certainly triggering a Ruin Event.
- **Stairwell**: Leads down. Pitch black. Dripping.

**Overseer**: *"You step into a wide concrete concourse. The ceiling's half gone — cold air and starlight coming down in one big column. There's rubble in the middle of the floor, a rusted vending machine on your left, and a row of turnstiles across the whole room. A stairwell drops down into the dark on the other side. It's dripping. What do you do?"*

**Ranger**: *"I'll sweep the rubble with my light, look for anything useful or anything dangerous."*

**Lena**: *"I go for the vending machine. If there's food, I want it."*

**Wren**: *"I'll listen at the stairwell. Don't touch it yet, just listen."*

**Overseer**: *"Ranger, the rubble's mostly concrete chunks and dust. You notice a half-buried pre-war poster — a family, the word VAULT. It's in good enough shape that you could take it if you wanted. Nothing valuable in the rubble itself."*

**Ranger**: *"I take the poster. The kids at the next settlement will like it."*

**Overseer**: *"Lena, the vending machine's jammed shut, rusted through. Your crowbar will open it, but you'll be loud."*

**Lena**: *"How loud?"*

**Overseer**: *"Loud enough that it triggers a roll on the Ruin Events table. Your call."*

**Lena**: *"I do it. There might be Stimpaks in there."*

**Overseer**: *"You pry it open. Three cans of beans and a folded note — the note says, 'If you find this, we tried.' As you get the cans into your pack..."*

> The Overseer rolls on the Ruin Events table: **2 — Sign**.

**Overseer**: *"...Wren, you're at the stairwell. You hear the crowbar scream against the vending machine, and then, over that, you hear — from below, maybe two levels down — a single long, dry, rasping breath. And then nothing."*

**Wren**: *"Ghoul. Probably sleeping."*

**Ranger**: *"Good. Let's leave it sleeping."*

> The party makes its next decision: press on or retreat. The cycle ends.
