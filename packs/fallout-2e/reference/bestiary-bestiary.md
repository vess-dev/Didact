Every major creature, faction combatant, and rogue machine you will find in the Wastes of the old world. Entries are grouped by category.

When an encounter table, vault, or campaign names something that has no entry of its own, use the nearest one. Raiders of any stripe — Khans, Fiends, Jackals, Blood Eagles, slavers — are **Thugs** led by a **Crew Boss**, with **Chem Fiends** or **Psychos** mixed in to taste. Minutemen, caravan guards, vault security, and townsfolk under arms are **Settler Militiamen** under a **Settlement Sheriff**. A Brotherhood Outcast is a **Brotherhood Knight**; a Legion scout is a **Legion Recruit** who knows the ground. A lucid ghoul is a person: give them a settler's stats and a ghoul's face.
