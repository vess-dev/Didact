A pre-war Vault-Tec promotional series: seven matched plastic figurines of the company's cheerful mascot, each themed to one of the old corporate aptitude categories. Collectors in every settlement will pay for them, but keeping one on your person grants a small training benefit — the figurine is said to have been tuned, in pre-war marketing copy, to remind the carrier of the virtue it represents. The effect appears to be real, at least in the post-war world.

A PC may carry up to **one bobblehead** at a time. Carrying a second replaces the first. Each bobblehead is _petty_ (no slot).

Collecting **all seven** is a major campaign achievement. A complete set grants an additional bonus of the Overseer's choosing — traditionally, the owner can consult the full set once per session to ask a direct question about the future, answered honestly in riddle form.
