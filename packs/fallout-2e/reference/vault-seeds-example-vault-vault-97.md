Let's say the region map (see **Wasteland Seeds**) puts a sealed vault in a stretch of **Ruined Suburb**.

Rolling on the tables:

- **Purpose**: *Social experiment*, built by *Vault-Tec*.
- **Construction**: Entrance is *a cliff-face blast door* made of *riveted steel plate*.
- **Ruination**: *Divided*, caused by *competing protocols*.

### What kind of vault is this?

*A sealed Vault-Tec installation, built into a cliff face outside a pre-war suburb. The experiment: split the vault in two from day one, with one half given abundance and one half given scarcity, and see what the two halves became. Two centuries later the original residents are long dead, but their descendants have inherited the experiment and turned it into religion.*

### Denizens, factions, agendas

- **Denizens**: *Practical* but *unimaginative*. The vault's people know how to survive but cannot imagine any other life.
- **Faction A — The Lit**: Descendants of the "abundance" half. Goal *Preservation*, obstacle *Isolation*.
- **Faction B — The Cold**: Descendants of the "scarcity" half. Goal *Redemption*, obstacle *Tradition*.

### Vault Theme

*Vault 97 was Vault-Tec's cruelest test. Two populations, one door, two centuries of controlled want and controlled plenty. The descendants still live by the experiment's rules, though neither side remembers why. The Lit live in the upper levels in relative comfort and will not step below the mid-level. The Cold live in the lower levels in bitter efficiency and are forbidden by sacred law from entering the upper. Between them is the Fracture — the long airlock corridor where the tests were run. Both factions know that somewhere in the Fracture is the original experimental record, and both believe reading it would damn them.*

#### Hooks

- The Lit have placed a bounty of 300 caps for anyone who can bring them "a working water filter from the Fracture." They claim it is all they need. They are lying.
- The Cold have approached the party quietly with an offer: 500 caps if the party retrieves *and destroys* the experimental record. The contact will not come above the third level.
