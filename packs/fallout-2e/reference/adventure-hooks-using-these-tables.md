Roll the four numbers. Sit with them for two minutes. Often the Overseer will see the adventure immediately — who the client is, what the real story is, what the choice at the end should be. When the rolls do not resonate, roll again or pick.

A rolled adventure is a starting point, not a script. If the party resolves the hook in an unexpected way in the first scene, let them. The tables are there to restart the engine, not to constrain the story.
