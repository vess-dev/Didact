- **Rads** fill inventory slots. 10 full slots = 0 HP. Cleared by RadAway, clinic visit, settlement purifier, or a specific chem / perk / relic / background ability. **Not cleared by sleep or rest.**
- **Fatigue** fills inventory slots like Rads. Cleared by a full night's safe sleep. Comes from Super Stimpaks, addiction and alcohol mornings, lost sleep, foul weather, long deprivation, and some relics.
- **Deprived** (missing food, water, sleep): cannot recover HP, Attributes, clear Rads, or clear Fatigue — by any means, chems included (a Stimpak or bandage still stabilizes). Deprived for more than a day = 1 Fatigue per day.
- Every Rad you take, roll 1d20 ≤ **(20 − current Rads)** to resist a **Rad Scar** (several at once: add them all, then one roll per Rad against the new total). Fail → roll 1d20 on the Rad Scars table: 1–10 Minor, 11–20 Major. Three Rad Scars push toward ghoulification.
