See **Playing Unusual PCs → Robot PCs** for the common rules. Additional Sentry Bot traits:

- You do not fit through doors. You do not fit in most pre-war buildings.
- You cannot climb. Uneven ground counts as one step harder for you on the Terrain Difficulty ladder.
- You weigh as much as five humans and will sink in soft mud or flooded basements.
- Most settlements will not let you inside their walls — you camp outside.
- Your tread is loud — you cannot surprise any encounter.
