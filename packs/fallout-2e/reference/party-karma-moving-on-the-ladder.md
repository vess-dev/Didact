The Overseer moves karma at most **one step per session**, and only when the party does something that a bystander could reasonably witness, survive, and tell a story about. Private crimes don't move the needle. Public ones do.

**Karma up:**
- Freeing a slaver's captives
- Giving caps or food to a settler in genuine need, publicly
- Saving a settlement from a raid at real cost
- Honoring a dying stranger's last request
- Sparing an enemy who begged for mercy, where it is seen

**Karma down:**
- Killing an obvious non-combatant
- Looting a body while its family watches
- Breaking a hospitality bond
- Selling someone to slavers
- Poisoning a water source

Combat against ferals, raiders, slavers, and bandits does **not** move karma in either direction. The wasteland agrees those fights were earned.
