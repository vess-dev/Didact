The wasteland kills in small ways first. A fall, a bad breath, a live wire, a winter night. This chapter is the short list. Every rule follows Cairn's pattern: a clear cost, a save when genuinely uncertain, damage to an **Attribute** (not HP) when the harm is environmental. Armor applies only when it would realistically help.

**When a hazard and the fiction disagree, trust the fiction.** These are starting points, not tables to be consulted mid-scene.
