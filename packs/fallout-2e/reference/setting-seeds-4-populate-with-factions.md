At least three factions should have interests in the region, and at least two of them should disagree.

### Faction Generator (d12 twice)

| d12 | Identity | Agenda |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A caravan company (Crimson, Gun Runners, Happy Trails) | Trade monopoly |
| 2 | A Minuteman-style settler militia | Independence and mutual defense |
| 3 | A raider crew flying a spiked banner | Tribute from the highways |
| 4 | A Brotherhood of Steel chapter | Pre-war technology recovery |
| 5 | A desert tribe or canyon clan | Sacred ground and ancestral claim |
| 6 | A Children of Atom cell | A glowing prophecy about a specific date |
| 7 | An Enclave remnant operating in secret | Orders from a pre-war chain of command |
| 8 | A ghoul community keeping their heads down | Survival and recognition as people |
| 9 | A Railroad station, quietly moving synths | Escape routes and cover identities |
| 10 | A super mutant enclave, peaceful or otherwise | Territory and respect |
| 11 | An NCR or Legion frontier element | Expansion of the old flag |
| 12 | A Followers of the Apocalypse clinic | Medicine, knowledge, a safer tomorrow |
