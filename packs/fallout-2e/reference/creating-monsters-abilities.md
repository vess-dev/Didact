A unique action the creature can take in or out of combat.

- If it can be realistically defended against → it's an **Attack** that interacts with HP.
- If it can't be realistically defended → it's an **Ability**.
- Single-PC abilities should usually take effect with no save the first round.
- Group-affecting abilities usually trigger an immediate save.
