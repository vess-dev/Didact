---
- A wasteland region is a place with weather, neighbors, memory, and weirdness. Stock all four.
- The traveler's question is always *"What's out here, and why should I care?"* Answer it twice per watch.
- Leave hooks dangling. A wrecked Vertibird 300 feet off the road should beg to be looted.
- Respect scale. Most of the wasteland is empty. Empty is fine — it's what makes the full rooms matter.
