A broken weapon, armor, or simple gadget — anything that has stopped working — is restored to working order at the appropriate station for **1 Scrap and half its cap value**, plus a Downtime Action, no roll. (Most gear is simply *working* or *broken*; you pay once to fix it, not per point.) Vehicles track damage in Frame HP and are the exception: they are repaired **per point of Frame HP** under the rules in the **Vehicles** chapter — they take longer, and they take more scrap. Relics and named pre-war items have their own rules.
