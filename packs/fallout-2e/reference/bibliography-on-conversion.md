Fallout 2e is not the only conversion of Cairn's chassis to a different setting, and it shouldn't be. Cairn 2e hacks well into any genre where the fiction is king, death is close, and characters are made by their bags and scars. Pick a genre you love; run the same engine; change the tables; find what you find.
