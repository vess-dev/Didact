A **Pip-Boy** is a pre-war wrist-mounted personal computer, issued mostly to Vault-Tec's own vault populations. An intact, still-working unit is one of the rarest items in the wasteland. Functional Pip-Boys cannot be bought casually — the marketplace price is a notional value for a working unit in a merchant's back room, which almost never appears.

A Pip-Boy does **not** grant perks. It is a utility tool:
- Plays and records **holotapes** (lore, audio logs, small programs, occasional functional tools).
- Tracks the wearer's Rad count and HP at a glance.
- Maintains a personal map of terrain the wearer has traveled. While wearing a Pip-Boy, the user cannot become lost on any ground they have walked before.
- Interfaces with most pre-war terminals through a short cable, giving the user basic diagnostic and query access without a hack attempt.
- Most Pip-Boys are designed to clamp onto the wrist for life. Removing one cleanly is a medical procedure.
- A Pip-Boy takes **no inventory slot** while worn (_petty_). When torn off a corpse, it takes 1 slot until donned.

Vault Dwellers almost always start with a functional Pip-Boy. Most other characters do not.
