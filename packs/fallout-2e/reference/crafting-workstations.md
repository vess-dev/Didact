A recipe lists the station it needs. If you don't have the station, you can't attempt the recipe. Most are settlement buildings — see **Overseer's Guide → Settlements** for how each station is built, staffed, and upgraded. A single workshop scrap bench counts as **all** crafting stations for crude backyard work at half yield, at the Overseer's discretion.

| Station | Makes |
|---|---|
| **Cooking Station** | Meals, preserves, pre-war recipes |
| **Chemistry Station** | Chems, Stimpaks, RadAway |
| **Weapons Workbench** | Weapon mods, ammo, repairs |
| **Armor Workbench** | Armor mods, patches, padding, helmets |
| **Power Armor Station** | Power armor mods, frame swaps, fusion-core work |
| **Workshop / Scrap Bench** | Gadgets, mines, traps, generic fixing |
