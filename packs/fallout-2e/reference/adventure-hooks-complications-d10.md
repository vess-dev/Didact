*Every job has something wrong with it. Roll one.*

| d10 | Complication |
|---|---|
| 1 | The client is lying about the target. |
| 2 | A rival party is also after the objective. |
| 3 | The client cannot pay in full up front. |
| 4 | The location is deeper in a hot zone than advertised. |
| 5 | A faction the party has history with is involved. |
| 6 | The target has a sympathetic reason for being where they are. |
| 7 | A clock ticks — the party has less time than they were told. |
| 8 | The objective is not what the client believes it is. |
| 9 | One of the party's companions will be compromised if the job succeeds. |
| 10 | A pre-war relic is involved, and someone *else* wants it. |
