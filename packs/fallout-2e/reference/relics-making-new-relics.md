To make a relic, answer three questions:

1. **What is it?** Name the pre-war device and what it was *for*.
2. **What does it do now?** The effect should solve exactly one kind of problem the party is likely to face — not all of them.
3. **What does it cost?** Charges, a specific recharge, a drawback, attention from NPCs who know what it is.

A good relic is specific, useful, and sooner or later a problem. Don't spill more than one per session; let the party build up a personal collection of strange and specific items over time.
