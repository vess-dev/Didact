At a **Weapons Workbench** with a Downtime Action, **Scrap**, and **caps**, a PC installs **one** mod on a weapon they own. A given weapon holds at most **two** mods. A mod is permanent on that weapon; removing one destroys the mod. A botched install (Overseer's call on a fumbled save when rushing) destroys one component — the scope cracks, the suppressor welds shut — and the PC loses the scrap and caps. The weapon itself survives.

Each mod lists: **Scrap cost / caps cost / downtime**. The downtime figure shows how many Downtime Actions the install consumes — most mods are a single action, but heavy or fussy work (a calibrated receiver, a hardened frame) eats two. A mod beyond a PC's training may require a higher Scrap cost or the aid of a Tinker NPC at the Overseer's discretion.

A mod is keyed to a **weapon type**. A firearm mod won't fit a ripper; a melee mod won't fit a plasma caster. Where fiction genuinely allows overlap (bayonets on a rifle), the Overseer is the final word.

### Firearm Mods (Ballistic Pistols, Rifles, SMGs, Shotguns, Heavy Ballistics)

| Mod | Scrap | Caps | Downtime | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Suppressor** | 2 | 60 | 1 | Shot loses the _loud_ tag — it no longer triggers encounter rolls by itself. |
| **Long Scope** | 2 | 80 | 1 | Long-range shots with this weapon are not Impaired. |
| **Reflex Sight** | 1 | 40 | 1 | First attack each combat ignores the Impaired penalty from bad cover. |
| **Extended Magazine / Drum** | 2 | 60 | 1 | Once per combat, make a second attack with this weapon on your turn. |
| **Reinforced Receiver** | 3 | 120 | 2 | Steps up one damage die (d6 → d8, cap d12). One weapon can only take this mod once. |
| **Bayonet / Underbarrel Blade** | 1 | 30 | 1 | The weapon carries an extra d6 melee attack — roll both in melee, keep the higher. |
| **Bracing Stock** | 1 | 40 | 1 | Counts as a brace for the *Withered Hand* Rad Scar. A heavy weapon with this mod may be fired unbraced without being Impaired on any turn the firer does not move. |
| **Recoil Compensator** | 2 | 60 | 1 | Blast attacks from this weapon no longer hit the firer's allies. |
| **Automatic Receiver** | 3 | 120 | 2 | The weapon gains _blast_ against an adjacent pair of targets once per combat. Semi-only weapons cannot accept this. |
| **Heavy Barrel** | 2 | 80 | 1 | The weapon's first attack each combat is Enhanced, but the weapon becomes _bulky_ if it wasn't already. |
| **Perforating Rounds** | 2 | 100 | 1 | Against Armor 2+ targets, the attack ignores 1 Armor. Does not stack past one. |

### Energy Weapon Mods (Lasers, Plasma, Gauss, Tesla, Pulse)

| Mod | Scrap | Caps | Downtime | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Focus Lens** | 2 | 100 | 1 | Long-range energy shots are not Impaired. |
| **Overcharge Capacitor** | 3 | 140 | 2 | Once per combat, the weapon deals Enhanced damage for one shot but burns **two** charges instead of one. |
| **Photonic Stabilizer** | 2 | 100 | 1 | Energy blasts no longer hit the firer's allies. |
| **Beam Splitter** | 3 | 160 | 2 | Once per combat, gain the _blast_ tag against an adjacent pair of targets. |
| **Shielded Coils** | 2 | 120 | 1 | Seals the coils: rain, mud, and rad storms never Impair this weapon, even when the Overseer rules that foul weather Impairs unshielded energy weapons. |
| **Efficient Reactor** | 3 | 140 | 2 | The weapon's ammo cell provides +2 uses before needing to swap. One weapon can only take this once. |
| **Cryogenic Coil** | 3 | 160 | 2 | On a Critical Damage result, target also loses their next action as they lock up. Incompatible with Overcharge. |
| **EMP Coupler** | 2 | 120 | 1 | The weapon is Enhanced vs. robots and power-armored targets. |
| **Reflex Sight** | 1 | 40 | 1 | First attack each combat ignores the Impaired penalty from bad cover. |
| **Focused Grip** | 1 | 50 | 1 | Counts as a brace for the *Withered Hand* Rad Scar. A heavy weapon with this mod may be fired unbraced without being Impaired on any turn the firer does not move. |

| Mod | Scrap | Caps | Downtime | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Serrated Edge / Spiked Head** | 1 | 30 | 1 | On a natural max damage roll, the target also takes 1 STR damage from the tear of the blade. |
| **Shock Conversion** | 3 | 140 | 2 | On a hit, the target makes a WIL save or loses their next action as the charge locks up their nerves. Once per combat. Requires a fusion cell per scene. |
| **Weighted Head / Pommel** | 2 | 70 | 1 | The weapon's first attack each combat is Enhanced. |
| **Heated Coil** | 2 | 100 | 1 | On a hit, the target makes a DEX save or catches fire — 1d4 STR damage per round until an action is used to smother it. Requires flamer fuel or a fusion cell per scene. |
| **Bladed Handle / Reverse Grip** | 1 | 40 | 1 | Once per combat, when a foe attacks you in melee, you may immediately deal them d4 damage with the handle. |
| **Auto-Axe Conversion** | 3 | 180 | 2 | Melee-only. The weapon gains _blast_ against an adjacent pair of targets once per combat. Incompatible with Bladed Handle. |
| **Reinforced Grip** | 1 | 40 | 1 | Cannot be disarmed; the weapon is not dropped when the PC suffers Critical Damage. |
| **Bleeding Edge** | 2 | 80 | 1 | On a Critical Damage result to a living target, the target also bleeds — 1 STR damage per round until bandaged. |
| **Puncturing Tip** | 2 | 100 | 1 | Against Armor 2+ targets, the attack ignores 1 Armor. Melee only. Does not stack past one. |
