- **A dog** (see Dogmeat's Collar in Relics) has 3 HP, 10 STR, 14 DEX, 4 WIL, bite (d6). It points at danger silently and will not leave the PC who collared it. Loyalty begins at 4.
- **A robot companion** (Mister Handy, Protectron, Eyebot) uses the matching bestiary entry. Loyalty does not apply — instead the robot has a **Directive** the PC must not violate. The first time the PC violates the Directive, the robot warns them. The second time, the robot attempts to enforce it.
- **A pack brahmin** is not a companion, but it will carry 6 slots of gear and stand unreasonably still in combat. It dies the first time something serious hits it.
