Time in Fallout 2e runs on these units, from shortest to longest:

- **Round** (~10 seconds) — one exchange of blows. Combat only.
- **Turn** — a few minutes of focused action. The exploration unit: reading a magazine, searching a terminal, patching armor.
- **Scene** — a continuous stretch of play in one place or situation: a fight, a negotiation, a single ruin. A scene ends when the fiction moves on.
- **Combat** — one combat encounter. "Once per combat" refreshes at the end of the fight regardless of how many scenes it spanned.
- **Watch** — one third of a day (morning / afternoon / night). The wilderness travel unit.
- **Day / week / season / year** — calendar units. Fusion cores last about a week of light wear; settlements take seasons to build.
- **Session** — one evening at the table.
- **Downtime Action** — a block of between-session work. One per PC at a time.

### Rest

- **Short rest** — a few minutes in safety with a drink of clean water. Restores all HP.
- **Long rest** — a full week's rest in a safe place, usually with the help of a doc, a chem, or a pre-war auto-doc. Restores Attribute loss; triggers ghoulification rolls once a PC has three Rad Scars; the checkpoint at which an unattached second companion drifts away.
- **Make Camp** (wilderness) — one watch of camp. Each PC (and their mounts) consumes a Ration; a watch rotation is set.
- **Full night's safe sleep** — clears all Fatigue.
- **Rads do not clear from rest of any kind.** They require RadAway, a clinic, a purifier, or a specific chem / perk / relic / background ability. See **Rads** above.
