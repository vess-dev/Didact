When a vehicle is reduced to 0 Frame HP in a single impact, the **Crash** rule applies:

- The driver rolls on the **Scars table** at HP Lost **2d6** — the wheel and the windshield are right there.
- Every passenger rolls on the Scars table at HP Lost **1d6** (Overseer's call, based on whether they were belted, braced, or in the back).
- The vehicle itself is a wreck. It may be stripped for parts (**2d6 Scrap** plus **2d6 × 10 caps** of loose salvage, plus the fuel source if intact).

A vehicle worn down to 0 Frame HP over several hits is *broken, not crashed* — it stops running, the frame holds, and a mechanic can fix it given parts, a workshop, and the downtime to do the work (see **Maintenance** below).
