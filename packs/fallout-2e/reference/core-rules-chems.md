A **Chem** is a one-shot disposable buff from the old world's pharmaceutical industry. Chems:
- Are _petty_ (no slot).
- Do not cause Rads.
- Are consumed on use.
- May be addictive — see the Marketplace.
- Heal nothing while the user is **Deprived**: no HP, Attribute, Rad, or Fatigue recovery, by any means (see **Deprivation & Rads**). A Stimpak or a bandage still stabilizes Critical Damage and Bleeding Out.
