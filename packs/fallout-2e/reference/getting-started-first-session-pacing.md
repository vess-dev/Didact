A good first session has these beats, in rough order. Let the players choose how long each beat takes.

1. **Scene one: arrival.** Put the party in the wasteland with a clear immediate goal. A settlement that just saw smoke on the horizon. A caravan job that went wrong an hour ago. Make the stakes obvious.
2. **First save.** Call a save in the first ten minutes. Teach the system by using it. Make the failure interesting, not fatal.
3. **First moral choice.** Before the first combat, give the party a choice where both options cost. Who they are will show up here.
4. **First combat.** A short fight, two or three rounds, against something the players understand. Raiders, ghouls, a yao guai. Make sure every PC acts. Call Scars when earned.
5. **First Scar.** When a PC drops to exactly 0 HP, pause and let the table read the Scar entry aloud. That is the moment the book teaches itself.
6. **First faction.** End the session with a specific NPC from a specific faction asking the party to do a specific thing next time. The hook for session two lives here.
