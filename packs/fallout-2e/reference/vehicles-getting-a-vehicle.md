Vehicles are not bought at a marketplace counter. A party gets one by:

- Recovering it from a ruin, usually in pieces, and spending a Downtime Action plus a Workshop to get it running.
- Being *given* one by a faction at Liked or better standing, with strings attached — a Brotherhood Vertibird drop-off is conditional on delivering their payload.
- Stealing one from a raider camp or Gunner convoy and not being caught.
- Inheriting one from a dying mechanic, pre-war ghoul, or caravaneer who ran out of road.

The marketplace prices listed under **Transport** (motorbike 600 caps, pre-war car 1500 caps) are for casual offhand fiction — a merchant might have a notional price, but a working vehicle almost never changes hands for caps alone. Expect a favor, a blood debt, or a story alongside the price.
