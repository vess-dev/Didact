> You have set more broken bones than you can count. You have also amputated more limbs than you would like to admit. You carry your tools in a bag that once held your father's. The patient always comes first. Always.

- **Background:** Wasteland Medic
- **Attributes:** STR 9 · DEX 13 · WIL 15
- **HP:** 5
- **Armor:** 1 (sub-dermal armor implant)

**Gear:**
- 3d6 caps
- Rations (3 uses)
- Torch (3 uses)
- **10mm pistol** (d8)
- **Scalpel** (d6)
- Doctor's garb — apron, gloves, clamps (_petty_)
- Sub-Dermal Armor Implant (1 Armor — cancelled by metal armor)
- **Medi-Kit** (3 uses, each heals 1d6 STR as a Stimpak)
- **Stimpak Diffuser** (2 uses)
- 2 Stimpaks

**Where You Learned to Cut:** *The New Vegas Medical Clinic.* You worked the counter for the implant surgeon and took your wages in surgery: a Sub-Dermal Armor Implant, pre-war polymer mesh stitched under the skin of your chest. 1 Armor. Wearing metal armor over it disrupts the mesh and cancels the bonus.

**What Keeps You Working:** *Stimpak Diffuser.* Two Appalachian aerosol canisters. As an action, pop one: every ally within arm's reach restores 1d6 STR.

**Traits:** Physique *Weathered*, Skin *Sun-darkened*, Hair *Braided*, Face *Heavy-jawed*, Speech *Slow*, Clothing *Patched*, Virtue *Patient*, Vice *Bitter*.

**Bond (Bond 3):** You carry a **Cracked Locket** (_petty_) with a faded photograph of someone you loved. They went into the tunnels ten years ago. You think they're still alive down there.
