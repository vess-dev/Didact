Every rule above exists to add *consequence*, not to turn every scene into a pile of saves. Only call for a hazard save when the fiction has raised the stakes — a trip-and-fall is not a DEX save; a cliff-edge traverse during a firefight is.

Telegraph every hazard clearly before it matters. A player who could not have seen the rad storm on the horizon was not given a fair chance to avoid it. That is the contract.
