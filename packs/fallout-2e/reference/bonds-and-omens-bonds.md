A Bond is a specific, personal item or obligation a character starts with. Every Bond in Fallout 2e answers the question: *"What does this character owe the world, and who is coming to collect?"*

A Bond is not a backstory. It's an anchor. Every Bond suggests at least three things:
- **A place.** Whatever the Bond describes (a map, a letter, a battery cell that won't come out) implies a *where* — a place the party will eventually go.
- **A person or faction.** Whoever left the item, wrote the letter, or installed the thing the character is now stuck with is an NPC or faction the party will eventually meet.
- **A timer.** Something is going to happen because of this Bond, and it is going to happen *soon*, unless the party does something.

When a Bond sits untouched for three sessions, the Overseer should activate it. The NPC comes to collect. The place on the map becomes reachable. The installed cell grows heavier. A Bond should never be a piece of flavor text that goes unread.
