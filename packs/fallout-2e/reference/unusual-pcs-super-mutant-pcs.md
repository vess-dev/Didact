(*Super Mutant background*)

### What a Super Mutant Is
- **Eight feet tall, green-grey, very strong.** When you roll your Attributes, the highest of the three results goes to **STR**.
- **Rad-immune.** The FEV that made you rebuilt your cells. You do not take Rads — not from hot zones, not from dirty water, not from radiation weapons — and you never roll Rad Scars. You cannot ghoulify. RadAway and Rad-X do nothing for you; there is nothing for them to do.
- **Cannot become addicted.** Chems still work on you, harmful ones included, but your metabolism burns them off before they can hook you.
- **Healed by Stimpaks at half rate** — a Stimpak restores 1d4 STR instead of 1d6. Super Stimpaks work normally.

### What a Super Mutant Cannot Do
- **Most pre-war gear is too small.** Worn armor is custom-fitted or improvised. Power armor is not available — no frame is built for your body.
- **Most pre-war weapons are ergonomically wrong.** You can fire heavy weapons without bracing penalty; you cannot use pistol grips made for human hands without breaking them. The Overseer decides what qualifies.
- **You cannot sneak effectively** in most human-sized environments. Corridors, crawlspaces, ventilation shafts — the building was not built for you.

### Social Cost
- Almost no settlement will let you inside. You negotiate at the gate, you sleep in the fields, you trade through an intermediary.
- Brotherhood, NCR, and Enclave patrols treat you as a combat threat on sight.
