title: "Stat Block Format"
tags: [creating, monsters]
source: overseers-guide/creating-monsters#stat-block-format
---
`X HP, X Armor, X STR, X DEX, X WIL, Attack (dX, tags)`

Omit the Armor field when the creature has 0 Armor; otherwise keep the fields in this exact order. Then add up to three bullets:

- A descriptor of appearance, demeanor, or habitat.
- A quirk, tactic, or peculiarity.
- A Critical Damage effect.
