- **Population** — how many people live there. 1 is a handful of farmers; 5 is a town that can field a militia and feed strangers.
- **Defense** — walls, watch, guns, early-warning, fortified terrain. 1 is "we have a gate"; 5 is "we hold off a Brotherhood patrol."
- **Industry** — what the settlement makes that other people want. 1 is "we have clean water"; 5 is "our name is on a trade route."

A new settlement starts at 1/1/1.
