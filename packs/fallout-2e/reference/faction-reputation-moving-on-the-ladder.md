Reputation changes during play when the party does something the faction *notices*. The Overseer shifts the track one step at a time, when a significant action is witnessed, reported, or proven:

**Ways up.** Completing a quest for faction agents. Delivering intel the faction wanted. Sparing a faction member who was caught. Turning over a wanted criminal. Publicly siding with the faction in a dispute.

**Ways down.** Killing a faction member in plain view. Stealing from a faction cache. Siding with a rival in a public contest. Being caught lying about faction business. Destroying a faction-held settlement.

Routine combat with faction patrols doesn't move the needle unless it's witnessed by a survivor or reported back.
