title: "Principles"
tags: [creating, backgrounds]
source: overseers-guide/creating-backgrounds#principles
---
- A Background should make a player excited to roll on it.
- Archetypes are fine. Familiar is fine. Leave room for alternatives.
- A Background should tell the player something about the setting — its factions, threats, and history.
- Every table result should be distinct, so two PCs with the same Background feel different.
