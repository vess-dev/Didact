tags: [detachments]

mechanics: [attack, morale]
---
Take a single combatant stat block and:
- Raise **HP** to match a hardy threat (6–10).
- Raise **STR** to reflect total group numbers (12–16).
- Damage die should match the weapon, but always apply _blast_.
- Add a leader: either one named NPC statted separately or a Morale-save proxy using the leader's WIL.
