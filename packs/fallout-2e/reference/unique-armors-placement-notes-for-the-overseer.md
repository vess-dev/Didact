- A unique armor should tell the party who the wearer *was*. Power armor with a name is a dead man's suit; a costume with a name is a dead serial the wearer refuses to let go of.
- Consider a drawback equal to the bonus. Tesla Armor attracts storms. Ranger armor makes you a target. Power armor needs fusion cores. Every mark of prestige is a target on the wearer's back.
- Do not stack unique armors. One per PC per campaign. Otherwise the story becomes a costume drama.
- When an armor changes hands, note the reason. Armor remembers the last body it stopped.
