When the Overseer needs to drop a named unique weapon and doesn't want to decide which, roll here. Unique weapons are legendary and should usually be placed deliberately — but if the dice are the easiest way to hand one to a villain, a vault champion, or a caravan's lost cargo, these tables are fair. Re-roll any duplicate the party has already found, or let the second find be a worn copy with a scar of its own.
