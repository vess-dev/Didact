Every character has three:

- **Strength (STR)** — raw power, endurance, resistance to radiation, poison, and heavy weather. Used to kick doors, shoulder aside debris, haul a wounded friend, or shrug off a rad storm.
- **Dexterity (DEX)** — reflexes, aim, balance, stealth, and fine motor control. Used to dodge, shoot, climb rusted scaffolding, pick a pocket, or disarm a mine.
- **Willpower (WIL)** — nerve, charisma, tech savvy, sanity under pressure. Used to talk down a raider boss, crack a terminal, resist a cult's conditioning, or hold the line when the mutants charge.

These are not descriptions of your whole character. A low-STR courier isn't *weak*; she just has worse odds when the risk is muscle. She can still try anything.
