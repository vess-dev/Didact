A settlement's entire record can be written on one line of a session notebook:

> *Five-Corners — Pop 3 / Def 2 / Ind 2 — slots: walls, clinic, trading post, brahmin pen, (four open, two before upkeep bites — filled slots beyond Pop × 2 = 6 go dark seasonally)*

That's the whole game. When the party upgrades a slot, erase and rewrite.
