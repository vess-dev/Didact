See **Playing Unusual PCs → Robot PCs** for the common rules. Additional Robobrain traits:

- **Your brain is vulnerable.** The glass dome is armored and sealed, but not invulnerable. If you suffer Critical Damage and the narration puts the hit on your dome (Overseer's discretion), the glass cracks and gel begins to leak: you are Deprived until it is repaired. A second such hit while it is cracked shatters the dome, and that kills you.
- **You dream.** Every night, the brain dreams memories from Before. The player may ask the Overseer once per session what the most recent dream was about. The Overseer answers honestly and may tie it to something in play.
