- Ten slots total: about 4 carried at hand, the other 6 in the Backpack. A Backpack provides 6 of the 10 slots; it does not add slots past the cap of 10 (only a background can).
- _Petty_ = no slot. _Bulky_ = 2 slots.
- A Rad or Fatigue takes a slot like an item.
- **Scrap** is the crafting resource. Every 5 Scrap = 1 slot. Gained from wrecks, scavving, settlement scrap heaps. Required to craft, mod, and repair. See **Crafting**.
