- Attacks **automatically hit**. Roll the weapon die, subtract Armor, apply to HP.
- **First round**: each PC makes a DEX save to act; failure = no turn.
- **Impaired** = d4 damage regardless of weapon. **Enhanced** = d12. Both at once cancel out.
- **Blast** = rolls against each target in the area.
- **Two weapons** = roll both, keep higher.
- **Overflow damage** spills into STR. After taking STR damage, roll a STR save with the *new* score — fail = **Critical Damage**, crawling and dying in an hour without aid.
- **Detachments** (groups of similar fighters) take Impaired attacks from individuals (Blast excepted) and deal Enhanced Blast damage.
- **Retreat** requires a DEX save and a direction.
- **Heavy weapons** must be braced or the attack is Impaired.
- **Power armor** untrained = every DEX save auto-fails.
