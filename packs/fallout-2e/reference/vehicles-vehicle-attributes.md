A vehicle has four numbers:

- **Frame HP** — how much damage the chassis absorbs before it's a wreck. Restored by a mechanic and parts, not by rest.
- **Armor** — subtracted from damage to the frame before HP is touched. Passengers inside are shielded by half the vehicle's Armor, rounded down, in addition to their own (up to the usual cap of 3).
- **Capacity** — passengers and cargo slots. A rider counts as one passenger; a mounted weapon gunner counts as one passenger plus the weapon's slots.
- **Fuel** — what it burns and how far a full tank goes.

A vehicle also has a **Speed Tier**: **Slow**, **Standard**, or **Fast**. **Slow** vehicles use the normal watch costs. **Standard** vehicles remove **one** watch of travel penalty (from Path, Terrain, or Weather) per fuel use spent on the journey. **Fast** vehicles remove **two**. A journey's base watch cost cannot be reduced below the distance's base cost — speed erases penalties, not the road itself. Weather, hot-zone, and storm effects on the driver and passengers still apply regardless of speed.
