HP represents luck, skill, toughness, and the narrow miss. It is *not* a pool of meat. Starting HP is rolled once at character creation (1d6) and recovers fast.

- A few minutes of rest and a sip of purified water restores all HP.
- HP doesn't reflect injury — Scars and Attribute loss do.
