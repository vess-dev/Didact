### Housing & Infrastructure

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Beds (barracks)** | +1 Population cap. Without enough beds, new settlers do not stay. |
| **Private Cottages** | +1 Population, costs a full slot but settlers are more loyal; population cannot drop below 1 from a single raid. |
| **Recruitment Radio Beacon** | +1 Population per season. Broadcasts a pre-war signal. Faction patrols (good and bad) take notice. |
