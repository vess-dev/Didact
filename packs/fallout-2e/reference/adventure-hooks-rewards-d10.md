*Base pay plus a bonus. Base pay for an ordinary job is **2d6 × 25 caps** for the whole party — more from a rich client, less from a desperate one. Roll one bonus.*

| d10 | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Caps**: the base pay again. Straight pay. |
| 2 | **Gear**: a specific marketplace item worth ~200 caps the client has spare. |
| 3 | **Ammo**: 3d4 boxes of a useful ammo type. |
| 4 | **A Perk Magazine** (Overseer's pick, scene-appropriate). |
| 5 | **A relic** (Overseer's pick from **Relic Roll Tables → Common Relics**). |
| 6 | **Faction reputation** up one step with a friendly faction. |
| 7 | **Settlement standing** — a specific settlement owes the party a favor. |
| 8 | **A map** to a further adventure the Overseer has been holding onto. |
| 9 | **A contact** — a new NPC who now owes the party their life. |
| 10 | **A relic and a complication** — the pay is generous, and the party picks up a secondary hook. |
