### Light

- Flashlights, torches, and helmet lamps light about 40 ft clearly, with dim shapes beyond.
- A torch has **3 uses** before it burns out entirely.
- A flashlight or lantern can be relit indefinitely, but it runs on a separate fusion cell or oil flask (**6 uses**).
- A character without light may panic until remedied.

### Doors

- Many doors in the ruins are locked, stuck, collapsed, or sealed with bulkheads. Force them with crowbar, cutting torch, explosives, or raw strength.
- The party's marching order decides who gets hit first by whatever's on the other side.
- A careful character can listen, sniff, or peek and glean what's beyond.

### Traps

- A cautious player should always be given a chance to see a trap. An unwary character has a **2-in-6** chance of triggering one, or it triggers by fiction if the fiction demands it.
- Traps are found by searching.
- Trap damage usually hits an **Attribute**, not HP. Armor reduces damage only if it would realistically help (plate won't stop poison gas).
