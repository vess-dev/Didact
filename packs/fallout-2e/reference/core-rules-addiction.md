Chems are disposable in the fiction but not free in the body. Every chem with the *Addictive* tag (see **Marketplace**) may hook a user who takes it carelessly.

- **Every dose** of an addictive chem forces a **STR save**. A failed save means the PC is **addicted** to that chem.
- Repeated doses of the same chem in a single session tempt fate — the Overseer may rule that a second dose in the same session automatically causes addiction, no save.
- An **addicted** PC gains **1 Fatigue** each morning until they take another dose of that chem. A dose clears that addiction's Fatigue, but not the addiction itself: the next morning without a dose brings the Fatigue back.
- Withdrawal is ugly but survivable. A PC who refuses doses for a full week recovers on their own at the Overseer's discretion, or they may take the **Cure an Addiction** Downtime Action.

A PC may be addicted to more than one chem at a time. Stacked addictions stack Fatigue — two addictions is 2 Fatigue every morning, three is 3, and so on. That is usually how PCs die.
