- **Ruin Events** (d6): 1 Encounter / 2 Sign / 3 Environment / 4 Loss / 5 Exhaustion / 6 Quiet.
- **Wasteland Events** (d6): 1 Encounter / 2 Sign / 3 Environment / 4 Loss / 5 Exhaustion / 6 Discovery.

Roll Ruin Events after long dwells, hurried moves, loud actions, or new zones. Roll Wasteland Events once per watch of travel.
