(*Mister Handy, Mister Gutsy, Protectron, Assaultron, Sentry Bot, Securitron, Eyebot, Robobrain*)

A robot PC is a pre-war chassis that survived the bombs. Its specific abilities come from its background; the rules below apply to *all* robot PCs. Built-in parts — chassis, weapon arms, lamps — take inventory slots like any item (one each; a _bulky_ chassis two, a _petty_ part none) and can never be dropped to make room; an Eyebot's take none.

### What a Robot Does Not Need
- **No food.** You do not eat. You do not carry rations.
- **No water.** You do not drink. You cannot be Deprived from thirst.
- **No sleep.** You do not sleep, and you are never Deprived for lack of it. In its place you run an hour of diagnostics in a safe place, once per day: that clears all your Fatigue (overheated servos, fouled joints, a sagging cell) the way a night's safe sleep does for flesh. You recover HP on a short rest like anyone — no water needed.
- **No breath.** You are immune to inhaled gas and smoke.

### What a Robot Cannot Use
- **No chems.** Stimpaks, Med-X, Buffout, Mentats, Jet, RadAway — none of them affect you. You cannot become addicted. You also cannot benefit.
- **No medical clinics.** Doctors do not fix you; mechanics do. A **Tinker**, a **Brotherhood Initiate**, a **Rust Devil**, or any mechanic at a Workshop restores 1d6 STR per repair — their background says how often; for anyone else it is a Downtime Action and 1 Scrap. A long rest restores your Attributes as it does anyone's, but only at a Workshop or with such a mechanic on hand.

### What Harms a Robot Differently
- **No Rads.** You do not take Rads from any source, and you never roll for Rad Scars.
- **EMP, pulse, and Tesla weapons are Enhanced against you.** Your background may clarify further.
- **Water** does not kill you, but full submersion fries you in 1d6 rounds unless your chassis is sealed.
- **Fire and electrocution** work as the Hazards chapter describes, with one difference: electrocution ignores any Armor below 3 on a robot chassis.

### Social Cost
- Most settlements will let a robot inside but will watch you. Some factions (Brotherhood, Enclave) will try to acquire or dismantle you.
- A robot PC cannot pass as human. You can hide behind coats and scarves, but only briefly.
