title: "4. Inventory"
tags: [character, creation]
source: players-guide/character-creation#4-inventory
---
- Ten slots total. You can comfortably carry about four of them at hand; the rest need a pack, a vehicle, a brahmin, or a hireling.
- Each PC starts with a **Backpack** holding 6 slots of items, Rads, or Fatigue.
- Most items take 1 slot. _Petty_ items take none. _Bulky_ items take 2.
- A small pouch of caps (under 100) is _petty_.
- A full inventory (10 slots) reduces HP to 0. You cannot exceed 10 slots unless your background says otherwise (two results grant an eleventh; an Eyebot has only six).
