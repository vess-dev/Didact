Fallout 2e's "perks" are pre-war skill books — paper magazines that survived the war in attics, vault libraries, locker rooms, and the back rooms of dead shops. A PC who finds a magazine, reads it for a few minutes in a safe place, and absorbs its contents gains the perk. See **Perk Magazines** in the Overseer's Guide for the full list of series and issues. Short version:

- A magazine takes **one inventory slot** while carried.
- Reading a magazine takes **one turn** in a safe, quiet space. Cannot be done in combat or while Deprived.
- Once read, the magazine is **consumed** — only one character ever benefits from a given copy.
- A second character can learn the same perk only by finding a different copy of the same issue.
- Each PC has **5 Perk Slots** flat, regardless of background. When reading a new magazine with all slots full, the reader must *swap*: drop one existing perk to make room. The old perk is gone until a new copy of its magazine is found and re-read.
- A PC may refuse to read a magazine and carry or gift it instead. Only actual reading consumes the copy.
