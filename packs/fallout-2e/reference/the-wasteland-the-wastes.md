**The Wastes** surround and divide the known settlements. They are not one place. They are everywhere-between: the highways, the farmland going back to dirt, the pine forests grown up through shopping malls, the open salt flats, the craters, the badlands.

Wastelanders far from any town are fiercely independent and believe themselves subject only to the rules of the ground under their feet. Their peoples, creatures, and weather have kept the cities at bay for centuries. At the core of any given wild region lies some profound ruin — a collapsed city, a crashed aircraft carrier, a reactor still glowing — which is where the region is at its most dangerous and most valuable. Most call them **Hearts**.

The Wastes are full of things strange and terrible: feral hounds bigger than a man, mole rats, yao guai, deathclaws, super mutants, radroaches the size of a dog, chem-twisted things with no names, and worse. Most are hostile. A few will parley with travelers who show respect for the ground. All of them know better than to cross the **Wild Folk** — what the old-timers call the remnant that lives out there still: feral ghouls, children of cults, hermit synths, things that used to be people.

The Wastes are not empty of *people*, either. Those who live out there learn the rules of the ground fast. Those who ignore the rules go missing, and are found later without their shoes.
