tags: [quick, reference]
source: players-guide/quick-reference#perk-magazines
---
- 5 Perk Slots, flat, regardless of background.
- One magazine = one turn of safe reading = one perk.
- Each physical copy is consumed on reading — only one PC ever benefits.
- When all 5 slots are full, reading a new magazine swaps one out.
