Fallout 2e assumes the party is mostly human. Most backgrounds are. But the wasteland is two hundred years of pre-war experiments, escaped vault-dwellers, smuggled synths, and worse. A player may roll or choose a background that is *not human*, and the rules for that PC live here.

Every unusual PC keeps Cairn's core chassis — three attributes, HP, Scars, saves, inventory slots, fiction-first resolution. What changes is what the body is made of, what the body needs, and how the rest of the world reacts.
