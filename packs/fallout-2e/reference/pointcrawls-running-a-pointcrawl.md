title: "Running a Pointcrawl"
tags: [pointcrawls]
source: overseers-guide/pointcrawls#running-a-pointcrawl
---
- The party always knows what points they can currently see on the map, and roughly how long it takes to get to each adjacent point.
- They don't know what's at a point until they reach it or ask around.
- Each movement along a path is resolved by the Wasteland Exploration rules (Path + Terrain + Weather penalties).
- When a party reaches a new point, describe it briefly and let them decide whether to engage.
