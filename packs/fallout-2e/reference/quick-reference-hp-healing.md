- HP is luck and near-misses, not meat. Restored by a **short rest** in safety.
- **Attribute loss** is the real wound. Restored by a **long rest** (a week's rest), a doc, a chem, or an auto-doc. A Stimpak patches STR fast.
- **STR 0** = dead. **DEX 0** = paralyzed. **WIL 0** = broken mind.
