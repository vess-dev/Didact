> "Vault" here is shorthand. It covers any dangerous enclosed location: vaults, subways, pre-war office towers, raider camps, underground labs, hospitals, hollowed-out aircraft carriers, whatever.

### Basics

- Exploration runs in **Turns**, **Actions**, and consequences.
- On a turn, a character can move about 40 ft (the reach of a flashlight) and take one **Action**. They may use the action to move up to three times that distance — but fast movement increases the chance of a **Ruin Events** roll.
- The Overseer describes any obvious information about a room and its dangers **for free**. Rushing, making noise, or entering a new zone raises the odds of springing a trap or triggering an event.

### Exploration Cycle

1. Overseer describes surroundings and immediate threats. Players declare what they're doing.
2. Overseer resolves all actions simultaneously. The Die of Fate can break ties.
3. Players mark resource loss, new conditions (Rads, Deprivation, item use). When relevant, Overseer rolls on the Ruin Events table.

### Ruin Events

Roll 1d6 when the party:
- Spends more than one exploration cycle in a single room,
- Moves hurriedly through a room,
- Enters a new area, level, or zone,
- Makes a loud disturbance (gunshots, breaking a door, a detonation).

| d6 | | |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Encounter** | Roll on the encounter table. Possibly hostile — check Reactions. |
| 2 | **Sign** | A clue, spoor, scorch mark, old corpse, fresh footprint, or abandoned nest. |
| 3 | **Environment** | Ceiling collapses, a water pipe bursts, a generator backfires, a radiation leak spreads. |
| 4 | **Loss** | A torch blows out, a program crashes, a battery dies, a door seals itself. Resolve before moving on. |
| 5 | **Exhaustion** | The party must rest (triggering another roll on this table), add a Fatigue, or consume a Ration. |
| 6 | **Quiet** | Safe. For now. |

### Actions

Non-passive: searching for traps, forcing a door, listening, defusing a mine, fighting, running, playing a holotape into a terminal, reading a magazine, resting. Some take multiple turns. Loud actions may trigger encounters.

#### Searching

- A character can spend a turn searching **one** object or area thoroughly, revealing hidden loot, traps, caches, or passages.
- Big rooms and complex ruins take several turns to sweep.
- Searching first is safer — but it costs time, and time costs Rations (and, in a hot ruin, Rads).

#### Resting

- Spend a turn resting: restore all HP.
- Requires a light source and a safe location.
- Rest does **not** clear Fatigue — there is no safe way to Make Camp inside a ruin — and it does **not** clear Rads. Rads only clear through RadAway, a medical clinic, a settlement's Industrial Water Purifier, or a specific chem, perk, relic, or background ability.
