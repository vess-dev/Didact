When the Overseer needs to hand out a named piece of armor, costume, or headgear and doesn't want to pick one, roll here. Like unique weapons, these are *one-of-a-kind* — re-roll any duplicate the party has already found, or let a second sighting be a copy stitched together by a settlement tinker who saw the original once and never forgot.
