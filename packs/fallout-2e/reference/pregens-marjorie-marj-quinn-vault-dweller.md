> You walked out through the blast door a week ago. The sun still hurts your eyes at noon. Your jumpsuit is already torn. You have never killed anyone. You intend to make it home.

- **Background:** Vault Dweller
- **Attributes:** STR 11 · DEX 13 · WIL 14
- **HP:** 4
- **Armor:** 0 (vault jumpsuit, _petty_)

**Gear:**
- 3d6 caps (roll at the table)
- Rations (3 uses)
- Flashlight + fusion cell (6 uses)
- **10mm pistol** (d8), ammo abstracted
- Vault jumpsuit (_petty_)
- Pip-Boy 3000 (_petty_, functional)
- Printed Vault Handbook (_petty_) — cheerful, wrong about everything outside
- Vault Door Key (_petty_) — it won't open the door again
- Water-Purification Tablets (3 uses)

**Why You Left the Vault:** *Reclamation Day.* The door opened on a schedule set before the war. No warning, just the gear-wheel rolling back and half the vault walking out beside you. You kept the **Vault Door Key** (_petty_) — it won't open the door again, no matter what you try — and the **Water-Purification Tablets** (3 uses) every dweller got at the door.

**What Your G.O.A.T. Said:** *Vault Loyalty Inspector.* Your job was reading everyone's file, and you read every file the vault kept. Once per session, you can declare you read about something before — a building layout, a pre-war protocol, a passcode format — and it turns out to be true. The Overseer decides the scope.

A pre-war courier in a long coat met you at the blast door and asked for you by name. They did not say whose name. You have caught sight of them twice since.

**Traits (roll/pick):** Physique *Wiry*, Skin *Paper-pale*, Hair *Vault-regulation*, Face *Youthful*, Speech *Pre-war formal*, Clothing *Vault jumpsuit*, Virtue *Hopeful*, Vice *Twitchy*.

**Bond (Bond 7):** You carry half of a **Vault-Tec Master Key** (_petty_). They say joined with its twin, it opens any door in any vault.

**Omen:** *(Rolls the party Omen — youngest at the table.)*
