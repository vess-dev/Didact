Named, one-of-a-kind weapons from the old world and the new. Unlike Relics, these are unique *weapons* — each carries a story, a name, and mechanical weight. There should be only one of each in your campaign.

Place them deliberately: in the hands of a feared NPC, at the bottom of a dangerous vault, inside the collection of a wealthy lunatic, or as the reward for a long-running quest. When a unique weapon changes hands, it changes the world a little.

Each entry lists base damage, any tags, a short description, and — where relevant — a charge limit or drawback. Some entries are pure flavor — a weapon with a famous name is already mechanically special because the whole wasteland reacts to the name. Don't feel obliged to bolt a crunchy rule onto every one.
