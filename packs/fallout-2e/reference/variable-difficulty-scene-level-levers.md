tags: [variable, difficulty]

---
- **Number of threats.** The most powerful lever. Two ghouls is a fight. Six is a problem. Twelve is a retreat.
- **Environment.** Light, cover, footing, verticality. A raider on a catwalk is a different opponent than the same raider in a parking lot.
- **Time pressure.** A bomb's timer, a faction closing in, a collapsing floor — all harder than the same situation with no clock.
- **Knowledge.** An informed party is a prepared party. The more they know about a fight, the easier it should feel.
