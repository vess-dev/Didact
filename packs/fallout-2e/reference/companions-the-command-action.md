title: "The Command Action"
tags: [companions]
source: players-guide/companions#the-command-action
mechanics: [command]
---
Once per combat, the PC whose companion it is may spend their own action to direct the companion. The companion takes a clean action — attack, drag an ally to cover, read a terminal, hurl a grenade — with Enhanced damage or a re-rolled save, as the fiction fits.

Without a Command action, companions act on their own initiative under the Overseer's control. They will defend themselves and the party, but they will not execute a clever plan unless told to.
