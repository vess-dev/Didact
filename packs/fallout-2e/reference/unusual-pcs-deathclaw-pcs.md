(*Deathclaw background*)

### What a Deathclaw Is
- **Ten feet tall, armored, clawed, faster than anything the wasteland has made.** Max DEX is **16** — a higher roll at character creation is lowered to the cap.
- **Thick hide** — 2 Armor natural, cannot be removed, does not stack with worn armor.
- **Claws cannot be disarmed.** You are always armed. You cannot sheathe or drop them.

### What a Deathclaw Cannot Do
- **No pre-war weapons.** Your fingers are too large and too clawed for most triggers. You may use heavy weapons braced against a wall or the ground. A handful of unique weapons (Overseer's call) have been modified for deathclaw hands.
- **No Stimpaks.** Your biology is too different. You heal from rest and from eating **a large amount of fresh meat** — that restores 1d6 STR once per day.
- **No armor stacking.** Worn armor does not stack with your natural hide.

### What the Wasteland Does
- No settlement will let you inside its walls. You negotiate at the gate, you sleep in the fields, you trade through an intermediary.
- Your Scar rolls never use the **Severed** or **Bleeding Out** entries — re-roll. You were built to survive things that would kill a person.
