See **Playing Unusual PCs → Robot PCs** for the common rules. Additional Eyebot traits:

- You are small. You have only **6 inventory slots**, not 10 — your built-in parts take none of them, and you drop to 0 HP when all six are full. You cannot carry _bulky_ items. You fit through any gap a basketball would — vents, broken windows, holes in a fence.
- Max STR is **8** (you cannot lift much, or take much). Max DEX is **16** (your thrusters are quick, but a sphere was never built for fine work). A higher roll at character creation is lowered to the cap.
- You float. Tough terrain never slows you, and pressure plates don't trigger under you.
