### Materials
- Paper, notebook, pencil, eraser.
- At least one d6 and one d20 — a fistful of d6s (six or more) is ideal for the map step.

### Overview

1. History (purpose, construction, ruination).
2. Denizens and factions.
3. Theme.
4. Map.
5. Stock the rooms.
