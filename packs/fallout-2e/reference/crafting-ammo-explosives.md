At a Weapons Workbench with scrap. One Downtime Action makes up to three batches of a single line, if the crafter can pay for each — the caps are powder, primers, and casings:

| Item | Scrap | Caps | Yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Box of small-caliber rounds** (_petty_, 3 uses) | 1 | 5 | 1 box |
| **Box of magnum / rifle rounds** (_petty_, 3 uses) | 1 | 8 | 1 box |
| **Box of shotgun shells** (_petty_, 3 uses) | 1 | 6 | 1 box |
| **Frag grenade** (d10, _blast_, 1 use) | 2 | 10 | 1 |
| **Pipe bomb** (d6, _blast_, 1 use) | 1 | 5 | 1 |
| **Molotov cocktail** (d6, _blast_, 1 use) | — | 5 | 1 |
| **Frag mine** (d10, _blast_, proximity, 1 use) | 2 | 15 | 1 |

Energy ammo (fusion cells, microfusion, EM slugs) can only be *repacked* at a Weapons Workbench with a donor cell — half cost, half yield. Fresh pre-war manufacture is beyond any wasteland shop.
