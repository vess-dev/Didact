### Defense

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Walls & Gate** | +1 Defense. Required for any Defense above 2. |
| **Watchtowers** | +1 Defense if paired with Walls. Settlers post rotating lookouts; raids are detected one watch early. |
| **Guard Post** | +1 Defense. A named settler with a gun and a shift schedule. Militia upgrades one weapon tier. |
| **Turret Line** (pipe, laser, or heavy) | +1 Defense. Requires Power Generator. Laser and heavy variants cost an extra Downtime Action to install. |
| **Armory** | +1 Defense. Militia upgrades one weapon tier, and the party may draw one free common weapon (Overseer's pick from the Marketplace) once per visit (limit 1 per PC per season). |
