> The crew is dead or on your tail. You stole the wrong thing, or the right thing at the wrong time, and walked out with both. You are trying not to be a raider anymore. The crew is making it hard.

- **Background:** Raider
- **Attributes:** STR 14 · DEX 12 · WIL 9
- **HP:** 5
- **Armor:** 1 (spiked leather)

**Gear:**
- 3d6 caps
- Rations (3 uses)
- Torch (3 uses)
- **Sawed-off double-barrel** (d8, _blast_ at close range)
- **Nail-board** (d8, _bulky_)
- Spiked leather (1 Armor)
- Skull paint (_petty_)
- **Two Sticks of Dynamite** (d10, _blast_, 1 use each, from the job that broke you)

**How It Fell Apart:** *The Bomb.* You were going to hit a settlement. You stole the explosives and ran. Take the warrant from your old crew.

**What Makes You Dangerous:** *The Growl.* Once per combat, shout something so brutal that a single opponent who can hear and understand you makes a WIL save or loses their next turn. Robots, ferals, and beasts don't care.

**Traits:** Physique *Brawny*, Skin *Burn-scarred*, Hair *Matted*, Face *Broken-nosed*, Speech *Rasping*, Clothing *Leather-and-chain*, Virtue *Brave*, Vice *Cruel* (working on it).

**Bond (Bond 9):** You owe a debt to a local boss. You carry their **Brass Token** (_petty_). It's protection — and it's a leash.
