A settlement will be attacked. Expect one raid per season for a Defense 1 settlement, one per year for Defense 5. A raid is a combat scene (Overseer-designed: raider warband, super mutant hunters, Gunner contract, something worse). When it matters, rate a raid from 1 to 5 on the same scale as Defense — a handful of thugs is a 1, a Gunner company with a Vertibird is a 5. Outcome:

- **Party wins**: no attribute loss, and the Overseer may raise Defense by one.
- **Party loses or absent**: Population −1 and a building is destroyed (Overseer's pick, usually the one nearest the breach). A critically bad loss may wipe the settlement entirely.

A settlement Defense 3+ with at least one militia building can repel a raid rated 1 *without* the party present, if the Overseer judges the settlement has the numbers and the warning.
