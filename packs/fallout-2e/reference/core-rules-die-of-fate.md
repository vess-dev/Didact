source: players-guide/core-rules#die-of-fate
mechanics: [fate]
---
When a situation's outcome is genuinely uncertain and no save applies — weather, a random encounter, whether a merchant has the part — roll 1d6. **4+** leans toward the party; **3 or less** against.
