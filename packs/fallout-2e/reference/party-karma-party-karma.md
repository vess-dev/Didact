Faction reputation covers how the Brotherhood or the NCR sees the party. **Karma** covers what the wasteland as a whole — strangers, settlers, ghosts, children — remembers about them. It is a single number, kept for the party (not each PC), ranging from **−3 to +3**. It starts at **0**.

Karma is lower-stakes than faction rep. Faction rep opens and closes doors; karma changes how the world *feels*.
