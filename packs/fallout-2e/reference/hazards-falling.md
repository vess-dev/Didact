A short tumble is scraped knees. A real fall hurts.

- Every full **10 ft** you fall rolls **1d6 STR** damage. Under 10 ft, no save and no damage.
- Make a **DEX save** to land right. On a pass, **halve** the total damage roll (round down).
- Landing on something soft (water, mud, a pile of coats) rolls one fewer d6 (minimum 1d6). Landing on something hard (rebar, rocks, a rooftop skylight) adds 1d6.
