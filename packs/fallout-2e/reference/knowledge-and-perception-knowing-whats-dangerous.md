The Overseer owes the party fair warning on everything that can kill them. The warning is in the fiction:
- The feral ghoul has a particular sound before it charges.
- The minefield has a certain dust pattern.
- The deathclaw leaves a scent no one who has smelled it ever forgets.

If the fiction contains the warning, and the player pays attention, the character lives. If the player does not pay attention, the character dies. That is the whole contract.
