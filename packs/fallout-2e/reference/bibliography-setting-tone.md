The setting text is a generic post-atomic wasteland, a flavor space long occupied by post-apocalyptic fiction and film. Some things that informed the tone of *this specific* hack:

- **A Canticle for Leibowitz** (Walter M. Miller Jr.) — the long, slow cycle of knowledge lost and found.
- **The Road** (Cormac McCarthy) — the grammar of a dead world you have to cross on foot.
- **Riddley Walker** (Russell Hoban) — a wasteland language that's half prayer, half misremembered science.
- **Roadside Picnic** (Strugatsky brothers) — the zone as a place where the old world's leavings have gone strange.
- **Mad Max** (the whole series) — vehicles, leather, road codes, silence.
- **A Boy and His Dog** (Harlan Ellison / L.Q. Jones) — wasteland tropes before they were tropes.
- **Tank Girl** — chem cowboys, broadcast punks, vault-as-experiment gags.
- **The Book of Eli** — the wasteland as a theology of walking.
