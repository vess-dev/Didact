Wastelanders will travel with the party for caps. A hireling is *paid help* — a gunhand, a caravaneer, a medic, a tinker — and walks away the day the pay stops. To make a hireling, roll 3d6 for each Attribute, 1d6 HP, kit them out with gear appropriate to their trade, and roll some traits. Or use a Background and roll for it the normal way. See **Marketplace** for daily rates.

A party may hire as many hirelings as it can afford to pay. Hirelings take Morale saves normally — a well-paid gunhand will flee a deathclaw. They do not develop, do not have Loyalty, do not speak for the story. For an NPC who does, see **Companions**.
