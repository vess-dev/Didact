Creature venoms (cazador, nightstalker, radscorpion) work as each creature's Bestiary entry says. For a venom with no listed rule, the victim makes a STR save each round for 1d6 rounds, taking **1d6 STR** damage on each failure. **Antivenom** (see Marketplace) cures any of them.

Inhaled toxins — pre-war gas, chem smoke, hot rad cloud — deal **1 WIL** per round of exposure; a WIL save avoids any hallucinatory effects. Gas masks and sealed armor halve or negate inhaled harm.
