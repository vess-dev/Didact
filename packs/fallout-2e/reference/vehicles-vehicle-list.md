Each entry uses the stat line: **Frame HP / Armor / Capacity / Fuel / Speed Tier.**

### Ground

#### Pack Brahmin
*6 HP, 0 Armor, 1 rider or 6 slots cargo, feed, Slow.*
- Cannot be driven, only led. Never runs out of fuel. Dies easily.

#### Brahmin Wagon
*6 HP, 0 Armor, 4 passengers + 8 slots, feed, Slow.*
- The caravaneer's standard. Needs a brahmin or a horse to pull.

#### Riding Horse
*5 HP, 0 Armor, 1 rider + 4 slots, feed, Standard.*
- Rare. Rarer than a rifle. Reacts badly to deathclaws.

#### Motorbike (retrofit)
*6 HP, 1 Armor, 1 rider + 1 pillion + 3 slots, gasoline, Fast.*
- Loud. Every raider for a mile will hear it.

#### Raider Dune Buggy (jury-rigged)
*8 HP, 1 Armor, 2 passengers + 4 slots, gasoline or flamer fuel, Fast.*
- Held together with wire. On a natural 20 DEX save, a component falls off.

#### Pre-war Corvega (running wreck)
*10 HP, 2 Armor, 4 passengers + 6 slots, fusion core, Fast.*
- Atomic engine. A shot that reduces the Frame to 0 HP ruptures the reactor — it leaks 1 Rad to every passenger per hour until the car is abandoned or repaired.

#### The Highwayman (iconic pre-war sedan)
*12 HP, 2 Armor, 4 passengers + 8 slots, fusion core, Fast.*
- A cleaner, rarer, more dangerous Corvega. The marquee pre-war civilian car. Worth a campaign-level quest to get running.

#### Pre-war Truck / Flatbed
*14 HP, 2 Armor, 2 in cab + 6 in back + 20 slots, gasoline, Standard.*
- A mobile outpost. The party may camp in the back instead of paying the Make Camp action's Ration cost.

#### Pre-war APC
*18 HP, 3 Armor, 6 passengers + 10 slots + 1 mounted turret, fusion core, Standard.*
- Salvaged military hardware. The turret is usually a .50 MG or a mounted minigun. Raiders want it. The Brotherhood wants it more.

### Airborne

#### Vertibird (Brotherhood or Enclave)
*20 HP, 3 Armor, 6 passengers + 12 slots + door-gunner position, pre-war aircraft fuel, Fast airborne.*
- Flying. Ignores terrain penalties entirely. Requires a trained pilot (usually NPC). A crashed Vertibird is a common adventure hook.

#### Zeppelin (raider or caravaneer salvage job)
*24 HP, 2 Armor, 8 passengers + 30 slots + two mounted weapon positions, flamer fuel + gasoline, Slow airborne.*
- A pre-war hot-air or hydrogen-lifted scavenge build. Cannot hover precisely — lands at a drift, needs a long run to take off. Ignores ground terrain penalties but is helpless in storms. A hit to the envelope deflates the craft over 1d6 rounds.

#### Brotherhood Airship (Prydwen-class flagship)
*60 HP, 3 Armor, 30+ passengers + settlement-scale cargo + multiple mounted guns + Vertibird dock, fusion core (1 per week of flight), Standard airborne.*
- A **mobile faction base**, not a vehicle the party owns. The party may be flown aboard as allies, assigned a bunk, and carried across a region by it — but they do not drive.
- Boarding or sabotaging one is a campaign-arc event. A Prydwen-class airship on fire is the end of a chapter.
