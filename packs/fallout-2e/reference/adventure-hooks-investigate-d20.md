| d20 | Hook |
|---|---|
| 1 | A settlement's water has turned red overnight. The mayor wants to know why before the people do. |
| 2 | A caravan vanished on a safe stretch of road. No bodies, no wreckage. |
| 3 | A pre-war radio tower started broadcasting again last night. Unknown signal. |
| 4 | Three ghoul elders in three different settlements all died at the same moment. |
| 5 | Children in a farming town are all drawing the same unfamiliar symbol. |
| 6 | A Brotherhood patrol went into a ruin and sent back a distress call on an old frequency, then silence. |
| 7 | A named pre-war figure has been sighted alive — impossible. The settlement wants verification. |
| 8 | A specific raider gang has not raided in a month; the settlements are nervous, not relieved. |
| 9 | A synth in a settlement has been behaving oddly. The settlement does not know they're a synth. |
| 10 | A pre-war vault's terminal sent a data burst to the settlement's radio. Nothing since. |
| 11 | A settler woke up remembering events they could not possibly have lived. |
| 12 | The local deathclaw population has begun moving together in formation. |
| 13 | A Minuteman's old war journal has entries no one remembers writing. |
| 14 | Three pre-war corpses appeared in a dry well overnight, dressed for the Before. |
| 15 | A named settlement leader has not spoken in a week and will not explain why. |
| 16 | An old holotape recording has begun playing on every radio in the region at the same hour. |
| 17 | A specific farmer's mutfruit is bearing fruit that causes nightmares. |
| 18 | A pre-war underground test facility has started venting steam through a collapsed grate. |
| 19 | The party's own rumor network reports seeing them somewhere they never went. |
| 20 | Something none of the senior NPCs will speak about. Only the children talk. Quietly. |
