title: "Which Issue? (d12 within a series)"
tags: [roll-tables, perk, magazines]
source: roll-tables/perk-magazines#which-issue-d12-within-a-series
---
For most series, roll **d12** and pick the issue with the matching number, re-rolling any result beyond the last issue (Unstoppables! has 11 issues; Grognak, the Massachusetts Surgical Journal, and the Wasteland Survival Guide have 9). **Guns and Bullets**, with 16 issues, is the only series that needs a **d20**; re-roll 17–20.

Alternatively, the Overseer may simply pick an issue that fits the room, the NPC, or the moment.
