title: "Death, Departure, Recovery"
tags: [companions]
source: players-guide/companions#death-departure-recovery
mechanics: [command, loyalty]
---
- A companion who takes damage to STR makes a Critical Damage save like a PC. On a fail, they die in one hour without aid — a PC may spend a Stimpak or a bandage, or take a Scar of their own, to save them.
- A companion at Loyalty 1 who sees the party do something irredeemable will leave at the next town. They may return later, in a story.
- A dead companion cannot be replaced by another until the PC has one long rest and a scene to mourn.
