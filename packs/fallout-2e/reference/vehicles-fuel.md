Fuel is the real scarcity. A tank is abstracted as a number of **Fuel Uses**; each use covers roughly a day's travel.

| Fuel Type | Source | Uses per Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gasoline** (pre-war petrol) | Tapped tanks, sealed drums, pre-war gas stations | 2 per drum | Vanishingly rare; most tanks are dry. One of the few real chokepoints on wasteland travel. |
| **Fusion core** | Power armor supply, pre-war facilities | 7 per core | Drives nuclear-powered pre-war cars for a week per core. |
| **Flamer fuel** | Fuel cans | 3 per can | Runs small engines and retrofit vehicles. |
| **Animal feed** | Brahmin pen, grass, whatever | unlimited | Biological vehicles. Counts as a Ration. |
| **Pre-war aircraft fuel** | Crashed Vertibird reservoirs | 1 per tank | A Vertibird's one-way ticket home. |

When a vehicle's fuel runs out, it stops moving until a new unit is loaded. A driver who runs out in a hot zone is having a much worse day.
