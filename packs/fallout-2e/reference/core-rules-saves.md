- To avoid a bad outcome from a risky action, roll 1d20. If the result is **equal to or under** the relevant Attribute, you succeed.
- **Natural 1** always succeeds. **Natural 20** always fails.
- When two sides oppose each other, whichever side is more at risk rolls.
- When several characters act together, the one with the worst relevant Attribute saves for the group.
