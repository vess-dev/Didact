### Signal & Command

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Observation Post** | Raids are detected a full day early. The settlement may prepare — a prepared settlement automatically repels any raid rated below its Defense. |
| **Faction Liaison Office** | Choose a friendly faction. The settlement counts as one reputation step better with that faction, and one step worse with the opposed faction. |
| **Holotape Archive** | A library of recovered holotapes. A PC spending a Downtime Action here may attempt a **Research** action without needing to find a new Source first. |
| **Training Yard** | +1 Defense. Militia drill weekly. Any PC who spends a Downtime Action here may pick up a single signature combat technique (a named maneuver tied to a specific weapon type) from the resident sergeant. |
| **Memorial** | _petty_ building. A list of settlers lost, carved in metal or stone. Settlers hold together after a raid — population loss from a critically bad raid is halved, rounded up. |
