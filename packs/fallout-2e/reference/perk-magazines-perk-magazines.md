Perk Magazines are **Fallout 2e's version of character perks**. They are physical pre-war paper magazines — skill primers, gun-nut gazettes, surgical journals, comic serials with educational sidebars, Vault-Tec pamphlets, corporate training manuals — that have somehow survived two centuries in attics, lockers, vault libraries, and the back rooms of looted shops.

A magazine is a physical object. Whoever picks it up, claims it; whoever claims it, reads it, and gains the perk. That is the entire system.
