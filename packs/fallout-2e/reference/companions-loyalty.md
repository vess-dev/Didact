Loyalty runs from **1 (Wary)** to **5 (True)**. It changes when the party's decisions match or clash with the companion's values.

| Loyalty | What It Means |
|---|---|
| 1 — Wary | Follows for now. Will leave at the next clean opportunity. |
| 2 — Tested | Stays, but complains. Will not run into obvious death for the party. |
| 3 — Trusting | The default. Acts as a reliable ally. |
| 4 — Devoted | Puts themselves in harm's way for a PC. Will not leave without a direct order. |
| 5 — True | Will die for the party, and mean it. Once per session, the companion may take a hit meant for the PC — redirect the damage to the companion instead. |

The Overseer raises or lowers Loyalty at their discretion — usually after a significant choice, a kept promise, a broken one, a shared Scar, or a long silence.
