When a Bond and an Omen point at the same place, you have a campaign. A PC's inherited journal leads to a town where the Omen is manifesting. A PC's dying courier's letter is addressed to a woman who is hunting the thing the Omen promised. The Overseer should look for these alignments and exploit them ruthlessly. That is the craft.
