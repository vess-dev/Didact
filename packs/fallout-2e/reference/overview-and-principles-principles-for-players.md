### Agency
- STR, DEX, WIL are tools, not identities. A low-DEX dweller can still shoot a raider in the face — it's just riskier.
- Ask what *you* would do with this inventory, this situation, this wasteland.
- Be resourceful. A broken mirror, a Nuka-Cola bottle, a length of fence wire — everything is a solution to something.

### Teamwork
- Don't run ahead of the group without checking in.
- Agree on goals before the first bullet flies.
- The wastes kill loners quick.

### Curiosity
- Listen carefully to room descriptions — the Overseer is usually telling you what will save your life.
- Ask questions. There's no check for "noticing." If you think to look, you see it.
- There is no single right way through a ruin.

### Talking
- Treat every NPC like a real person with needs, fears, and a favorite meal. Most of them would rather trade than fight.
- Charisma isn't a stat here — it's what you actually say at the table.

### Caution
- A firefight is a choice, rarely the best one. Stack the deck: flank, ambush, negotiate, run.
- A retreat is a victory if everyone's still breathing.

### Planning
- Scout. Ask around. Spend a cap on a drunk's rumor. Find the back door.
- The wastes reward preparation and punish improvisation.

### Ambition
- Have a reason to be out here. Caps, a relic, a kidnapped cousin, revenge on a slaver chief. Something specific.
- Earn your reputation — the wasteland doesn't grant it.
