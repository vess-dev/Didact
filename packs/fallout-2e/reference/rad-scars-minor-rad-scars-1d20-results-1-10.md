| d20 | Scar |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Patchy Hair Loss.** Cosmetic, but strangers stare: −1 to first-meeting Reaction rolls. |
| 2 | **Radiation Burns.** A stretch of skin is permanently raw and pink. −1 max HP. |
| 3 | **Cracked Teeth.** Chronic pain. −1 max WIL. |
| 4 | **Rotgut.** One day each week, you are Deprived unless you drink purified water. |
| 5 | **Shakes.** When Deprived, ranged attacks are Impaired. |
| 6 | **Tumored.** A visible soft growth has appeared somewhere on your body (Overseer picks where). It does not hurt. It makes fitted armor sit wrong — any body armor you wear provides 1 less Armor (minimum 0) until it has been re-cut around the growth at an Armor Workbench (one Craft action and 1 Scrap per suit). Strangers notice. |
| 7 | **Milky Eye.** One eye clouds permanently. −1 max DEX. |
| 8 | **Radio Hum.** Within 40 ft of a live pre-war terminal or robot, you always know it is there. |
| 9 | **Leathered Skin.** You may re-roll a failed STR save against environmental damage (heat, cold, rad burn) once per scene. You look visibly changed. |
| 10 | **Dry Mouth.** You can no longer taste food. You gain no benefit from cooked Meals (see Crafting). |
