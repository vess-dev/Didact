- **Running the Game** (Matt Colville) — general refereeing craft, especially on running factions.
- **West Marches** (Ben Robbins) — the logic of a prep-light sandbox.
- **Return of the Lazy Dungeon Master** (Sly Flourish) — lightweight session prep.
- **Goblin Punch** (Arnold K., blog) — weird dungeons and weirder loot.
- **Bastionland** (Chris McDowall, blog) — how rules teach play.
