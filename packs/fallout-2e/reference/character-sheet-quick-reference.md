- Saves: roll 1d20, equal to or under the Attribute. Natural 1 succeeds; natural 20 fails.
- Attacks auto-hit. Roll weapon die, subtract Armor, apply to HP.
- Impaired = d4 damage. Enhanced = d12. Blast = each target in the area.
- HP to exactly 0 = roll on Scars table using HP Lost.
- Overflow spills into STR; then STR save at the new score to avoid Critical Damage.
- STR 0 = dead. DEX 0 = paralyzed. WIL 0 = broken mind.
- Panicked = 0 HP, cannot act first round, all attacks Impaired until a WIL save on your turn shakes it.
- Heavy weapons must be braced or the attack is Impaired.
- Full inventory (10 slots) = 0 HP.
