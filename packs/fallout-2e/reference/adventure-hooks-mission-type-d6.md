---
| d6 | Type |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Rescue** — somebody is missing, captured, or stuck. |
| 2 | **Retrieve** — a specific object needs to be recovered. |
| 3 | **Investigate** — something is wrong, and the client wants to know what. |
| 4 | **Escort** — a person or cargo needs to get somewhere alive. |
| 5 | **Negotiate** — two parties are about to fight and the client wants peace (or a favorable deal). |
| 6 | **Sabotage** — something needs to stop working, or someone needs to stop being a problem. |
