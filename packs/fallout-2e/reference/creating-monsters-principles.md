- Use **Critical Damage** to express the creature's essential horror or strangeness.
- Flavor matters more than numbers. Players remember *the protectron that still thinks it's 2077 and won't stop calling them "customers"* more than *a d6 combat robot*.
- Adjust any stat to make the encounter fun.
