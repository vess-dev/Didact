### Crafting & Workshops

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Weapons Workbench** | +1 Industry. Unlocks the **Craft** Downtime Action for ammo, explosives, and weapon mods on-site — see the **Firearm**, **Energy**, and **Melee & Unarmed** mod tables in Player's Guide → Crafting. |
| **Armor Workbench** | Unlocks the **Craft** Downtime Action for light and medium armor mods on-site — see the **Light & Medium Armor Mods** table in Player's Guide → Crafting. Broken armor can also be repaired here (see **Crafting → Repairs**). |
| **Power Armor Station** | A T-shaped frame stand. Power armor may be unstowed, rebuilt, or swapped between suits here. Required to install **Power Armor Mods** (see Player's Guide → Crafting). Requires Power Generator. |
| **Chemistry Station** | Unlocks the **Craft** Downtime Action for chems on-site. A chem cook working here pays half the usual reagent cost per batch. |
| **Cooking Station** | Raw meat from the bestiary becomes edible Rations. A Cooking Station attached to a Farm Plot makes one Ration free to every settler per day. |
