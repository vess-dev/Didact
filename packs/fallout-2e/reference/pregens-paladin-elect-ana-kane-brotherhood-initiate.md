> You swore an oath. Pre-war technology is the only thing standing between the ashes and the next extinction. You believe this. Or you did. Your codex is missing a page.

- **Background:** Brotherhood Initiate
- **Attributes:** STR 14 · DEX 11 · WIL 13
- **HP:** 5
- **Armor:** 1 (Brotherhood field fatigues)

**Gear:**
- 3d6 caps
- Rations (3 uses)
- Flashlight + fusion cell (6 uses)
- **Laser pistol** (d8, fusion cell)
- Brotherhood-issue combat knife (d6)
- Brotherhood field fatigues (1 Armor)
- Brotherhood codex (_petty_)
- **Field Repairs** — with your tools, once per day on a short rest (or as a Downtime Action), restore 1d6 STR to a robot
- **Power Armor trained** (no DEX penalty in a fueled suit — see Core Rules → Power Armor)

**Why You're Alone:** *Doubt.* You read a record you weren't cleared to read — something the Order did that you cannot square with your oath. Take the **Copied Page** (_petty_) — you haven't decided what to do with it.

**What the Order's Knowledge Gave You:** *Targeting HUD.* A power armor helmet's targeting lens, rewired to clip onto any goggles. While worn, ranged attacks at long range are not Impaired. It burns one use of a fusion cell per day.

**Traits:** Physique *Stout*, Skin *Pitted*, Hair *Shaved*, Face *Scarred cheek*, Speech *Clipped*, Clothing *Pre-war uniform*, Virtue *Disciplined*, Vice *Vain*.

**Bond (Bond 2):** You were mailed a **Pre-war Compass** (_petty_) that always points toward something deep in the Glow. You don't know what. Take 15 caps.
