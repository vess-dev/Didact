A short list of sins that kill Fallout 2e games faster than any other:

- **Railroading.** If the players refuse the hook, let them refuse. Pick it up later or drop it. The wasteland has more hooks than the Overseer can prep.
- **Revenge killing.** If a player talks the warlord down, the warlord stays down. Don't resurrect a boss because you wanted the fight.
- **Gotcha traps.** Every lethal trap must be telegraphed in the fiction. A minefield is described before it is crossed. A hot zone reads on a Geiger counter. No player should die to something they were not told about.
- **Rules lawyering.** Fallout 2e is a fiction-first game. When a rule and the fiction disagree, the fiction usually wins. Cite the rule as support, not as an override.
- **The single-winner ending.** Most Fallout DLC ends ambiguously. Let your campaigns do the same. A party that walks out without the prize can still have walked out having done something.
- **Punishing failure with boredom.** A failed save should make the scene *more interesting*, not less. Fail-forward is always on the table.
