### Watches

- A day is split into three **watches**: morning, afternoon, night.
- Each character takes *one* Wilderness Action per watch.
- If the party splits, each group runs its own clock.

### Points

- Destinations on a map are **points**: a settlement, a ruin, a crater, a bridge, a caravan stop.
- One or more watches are needed to travel between points, depending on path, terrain, weather, and party condition.
- The party knows roughly what the journey will cost but not the specifics.

### Travel Time

Travel is counted in watches. Three watches = one day. Most parties camp the third watch, so you can say "days" as shorthand.

To determine travel time, combine penalties from Path, Terrain, Weather, and Distance tables. For very vast stretches (the Glowing Sea, the Big Dead), add up to +2 watches. Mounts, local guides, or maps can reduce the penalties.

### Path Difficulty

| Path | Penalty | Lost |
|---|---|---|
| Old highway | None | None |
| Beaten trail | +1 watch | 2-in-6 |
| Open wastes | +2 watches | 3-in-6 |

| Distance | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Short | +1 watch |
| Medium | +2 watches |
| Long | +3 watches |
