A companion is simpler than a full PC:

- **HP** 1d6 (rolled once when recruited)
- **STR / DEX / WIL**: 3d6 each, swap any two
- **1 Armor** unless their background says otherwise
- **One weapon** appropriate to who they were
- **One special trick** — one thing they reliably do that a PC can't, drawn from their background (a ghoul reads old technical manuals at a glance; a brahmin has a keen nose for water; a Mr. Handy can fly six inches off the ground).
- **Loyalty** — start at 3 on a 1-to-5 scale.
