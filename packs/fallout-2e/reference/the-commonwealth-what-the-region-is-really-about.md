Every campaign, regardless of setting, is about *one thing*. When players and Overseer can all name it in one sentence, the campaign is healthy. When they can't, pick one and commit. Examples:

- *"Can anything of the old world be saved without becoming the old world again?"*
- *"Who deserves to live when there isn't enough for everyone?"*
- *"What is owed to the dead by the people walking over their graves?"*
- *"How do you become a parent in a place that eats parents?"*

Once you have the question, the region writes itself.
