id: hazards-drowning
title: "Drowning"
tags: [hazards]
source: players-guide/hazards#drowning
mechanics: [drown]
---
A PC holds their breath for three rounds. Every round after, a **STR save** — on a fail, take **1d6 STR** damage. Repeat every round until the PC reaches air or is pulled out. STR restores with rest like any Attribute.

Heavy armor, a full pack, or unconsciousness shortens the grace rounds. A PC in power armor with a dead core climbs out slowly or drowns.
