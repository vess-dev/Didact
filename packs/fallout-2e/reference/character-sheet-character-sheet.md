A character fits on a scrap of paper. Keep the following written down; the rest of the details will fill in as play goes.

---

Write down your **name, age, gender, and background**.

Then your three **Attributes**: **STR** (body, endurance, radiation), **DEX** (reflex, aim, stealth), and **WIL** (nerve, charisma, tech). Each has a current score and a max; write both. Below them, write your **HP** (current and max) and your **Armor**.

Write down your **caps** and your **Scrap**.

Write down your five **Perk Slots** — numbered 1 through 5 — filled in as you read magazines, empty until you do.

Write down your ten **Inventory Slots** — numbered 1 through 10. A Rad or a Fatigue fills a slot like an item. The first four slots are at-hand; the rest need a pack, vehicle, brahmin, or hireling. A backpack provides six of the ten slots.

Write down your **character traits**: physique, skin, hair, face, speech, clothing, virtue, vice. These are color, not mechanics.

Write down your **Bond** (always) and your **Omen** (only if you were the youngest at the table).

Write down your **Scars** as you earn them, and your **Rad Scars** separately. You will not earn many of either before the wasteland takes you, but leave room.

Write down any **addictions** you pick up in play.

Leave a **Notes** section at the bottom for the rumors, favors, debts, and names the Overseer drops on you.
