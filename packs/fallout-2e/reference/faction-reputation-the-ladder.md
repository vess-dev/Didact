Each major faction in a campaign has a reputation track for the party (not each PC). Reputation runs on a five-step ladder:

| Step | Name | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| −2 | **Vilified** | The faction shoots on sight. Wanted posters exist. |
| −1 | **Hostile** | The faction distrusts the party. Patrols are aggressive. Merchants refuse or charge double. |
| 0 | **Neutral** | The default. No opinion either way. |
| +1 | **Liked** | Patrols wave. Merchants give a small discount (10%). The party can walk into most faction holdings armed. |
| +2 | **Idolized** | The party is legend within the faction. Merchants sell at cost. Senior faction members will extend one meaningful favor per session. A PC with a matching background may wear faction colors openly. |

Start at Neutral. A PC whose background ties them to a faction stands a step apart from the rest of the party — see **Background Implications** below.
