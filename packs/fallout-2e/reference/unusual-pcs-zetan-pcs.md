(*Zetan background*)

### What a Zetan Is
- **An alien.** Grey skin, three fingers, wrong eyes, a language of high-pitched screeches and wails.
- **Fragile but quick.** Max STR is **12**, max DEX is **16** — a higher roll at character creation is lowered to the cap.
- **Cannot eat most wasteland food** without discomfort. Your ration kit is custom — water, pre-war plant matter, specific foraged mushrooms.

### Alien Gear
- Your **Alien disintegration pistol** (d10, ranged, ignores Armor; a target killed by it is disintegrated to ash) recharges from a small internal cell. A full day of sunlight exposure restores four shots. Do not lose it — it has no replacement on this planet.
- Your **Translation Device** lets you understand any spoken language, but you still speak your own in high-pitched screeches and wails.
- Your ship is somewhere, probably broken.

### Social Cost
- First-meeting Reactions in settlements shift **one step worse** — your ship-cloth wrap hides you at a distance, not up close.
- Certain pre-war cults (Hubologists, specific pre-war alien-watchers) react **one step better** on recognition.
