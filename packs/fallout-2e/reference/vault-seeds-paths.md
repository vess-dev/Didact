- **Standard** — obvious connectors. Solid line.
- **Hidden** — not on any map. Discovered at a cost. Dashed line.
- **Conditional** — requires tools, keys, climbing gear, or a map or guide. Crossed line.

Starting from the entrance, draw 1–4 paths per room. Create loops, branching halls, dead ends, shortcuts. Add intersections.
