- **A table of three to six players** — pick from the pregens, any combo. No two of the same background.
- **A one-shot** — hand each player a pregen, a printed character sheet, and the quick reference. You are ready to play in five minutes.
- **A player who wants to tweak** — let them re-roll any *one* table result, swap the Bond, or swap a starting gear item for an equivalent-slot piece.
- **A player who wants a different background entirely** — send them to Character Creation and roll a full PC. The pregens are a convenience, not a cap.

Each pregen carries a seed of a story — a key to a door that won't open, a flag decal still smoldering at the edges, a journal in a dead father's voice. An Overseer can use those seeds as campaign hooks if the players lean into them.
