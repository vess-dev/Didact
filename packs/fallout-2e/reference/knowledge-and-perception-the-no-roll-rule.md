---
If the character is standing in the room, the room is described to them for free.
- A blood trail on the floor is seen as soon as the character enters a room with a blood trail on the floor.
- An open terminal is seen.
- A pressure plate under the rug is *not* seen (by default) — but any player who asks *"is there anything under this rug?"* is told the answer honestly.

The rule is: **the game describes what the character would see if they were really there.** It does not require saves to operate.
