- **Cairn 2e** (Yochai Gal) — the whole chassis: 3-stat saves, slot inventory, automatic-hit combat, HP-as-luck, Scars, Fatigue, detachments. The game text at cairnrpg.com is the reference.
- **Into the Odd** (Chris McDowall) — the foundational engine Cairn builds on. Dice as consequence, not resolution.
- **Electric Bastionland** (Chris McDowall) — the principle that character is defined by equipment and predicament.
- **Knave** (Ben Milton) — slot inventory as a player-facing resource.
- **The Black Hack** (David Black) — lean OSR combat.
- **The Mothership RPG** (Tuesday Knight Games) — stress, panic, horror in a survival frame; light cross-pollination in the Panic rules and in the feel of vault exploration.
