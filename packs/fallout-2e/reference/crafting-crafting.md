Most wasteland crafting happens between sessions as a **Downtime Action** at a **Workshop** — a settlement building, a pre-war facility, a Brotherhood scribe's bench, a Railroad safe-house basement. You don't track parts in ounces and screws. You track **Scrap**, **caps**, a station, and a downtime clock.

The rule of thumb: if you have the station, the scrap, the caps, and the time, you succeed. Rolls only come in when the job is ambitious or rushed.
