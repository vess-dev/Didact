Some factions move in pairs. Going up with one moves you *down* with its opposite:

- **NCR ↔ Legion**
- **Brotherhood of Steel ↔ Enclave**
- **Railroad ↔ Institute**
- **Minutemen ↔ Raiders (as a bloc)**

When the party gains a step with one side of a pair and the other side hears of it, the Overseer moves the matching opposite down a step. A party cannot be Idolized by both sides at once. They can be Liked on both sides if they stay quiet — but any Idolized step forces a choice.
