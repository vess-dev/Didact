Power armor is its own category. All power armor is **3 Armor**, _bulky_ (2 slots, worn or doffed), **loud**, and requires a **fusion core** to operate. A core runs the suit for roughly a week of ordinary wear or a single day of sustained combat, then must be replaced.

**Who can wear it.** See **Core Rules → Power Armor** for the training requirement. Brotherhood Initiate, Brotherhood Outcast, and Enclave Defector start trained; any other PC can earn it with a Downtime Action under a qualified instructor. Robot PCs never wear power armor — their chassis is their armor.

**What it does.**
- 3 Armor against all physical and energy damage.
- Built-in lamp, HUD, environmental seals. Immune to gas, smoke, and most airborne toxins. Halves Rads from ambient exposure while sealed and powered.
- The wearer cannot sneak, cannot sprint further than a normal walk, cannot climb, cannot swim. In combat, power armor servos act as a brace — the wearer never suffers the Impaired penalty for firing an unbraced heavy weapon.
- A running fusion core discharges its dregs into anything the wearer strikes with an unarmed attack: such attacks are treated as d6 damage at minimum, even without Iron Fist.
- A dead fusion core leaves the wearer essentially trapped in a metal coffin — they may open the back plate and climb out as a full turn of difficult effort.
