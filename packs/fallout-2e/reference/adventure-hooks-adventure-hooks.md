When the party is between campaigns, or when a session needs a shape fast, roll here. This chapter is the "mission of the week" generator. Each hook is four rolls:

1. **Roll 1d6 — Mission Type** (what the party is asked to do).
2. **Roll 1d20 — Specific Hook** (who's asking, and why).
3. **Roll 1d10 — Complication** (what's wrong with the job).
4. **Roll 1d10 — Reward** (what the client offers).

Combine and run. Expand as needed.
