- **Be clear about what is about to happen.** Is that fusion core stable, or does it look like it'll blow at the next bump? Say so.
- **Keep restating the space.** Where are the PCs, where are the exits, what do their senses tell them? Every few turns, refresh.
- **Obvious threats first.** Describe the thing that will kill them, then the memorable furniture, then the rest.
- **Don't tell players how they feel.** Describe what's in front of them. Let them react.
- **Foreshadow.** Odors, sounds, temperature, and hints of light should tell players what's a few rooms ahead.
- **Keep them oriented in the Ruin Cycle.** Remind them who has already moved, whose turn is next, and what one more turn costs.
- **Exploration costs turns.** Too long in one spot, loud actions, or entering a new zone triggers a roll on the Ruin Events table. Players should know the price before they pay it.
