Named, one-of-a-kind armors, costumes, and headgear from the old world and the new. Unlike ordinary marketplace armor, each of these has a name, a history, and a reason the wearer is still alive. There should be only one of each in your campaign.

Place them deliberately: on the back of a legend, locked in a collector's safe, or under a coffin lid a caravan has been burying and reburying for a century. When someone pulls a unique armor out of a crate, the whole region hears about it within a week.

Each entry lists the base armor value (or _petty_ for cosmetic pieces), a short description, and — where relevant — a mechanical effect or drawback. Remember, as with unique weapons: *sometimes the flavor is the mechanic*. An armor that makes the wearer *look like Abraham Lincoln* does not need a stat line to matter in play.
