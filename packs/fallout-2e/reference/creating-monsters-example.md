- **Appearance**: *Hunched* + *Carapace*
- **Traits**: *Moves in a straight line* + *Weak spot*
- **Attack**: *Slams* + *Grapples*
- **Abilities**: *Disables* + *Hearing*

*A carapaced, chitin-shelled thing the size of a tractor. It moves only in straight lines and has a soft underbelly. It slams into anything it sees, pinning victims against walls. The reverberation of its charge drowns out all sound around it.*

This creature is physically tough with strong Armor, but it is slow and brittle on its belly. It attacks by charging, impacting multiple targets.

### Charge Beetle

6 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 6 DEX, 4 WIL, slam (d10, _blast_)

- Thick-plated insectoid predators that attack by charging straight through their prey. The drumming of their shells deafens anything nearby.
- **Critical Damage**: Target is pinned against the nearest wall by the beetle's charge, taking an additional 1 STR per round until freed (STR save to escape).
