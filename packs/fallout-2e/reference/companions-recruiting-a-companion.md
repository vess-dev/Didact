Companions are not bought. A PC may recruit one by:

- Saving their life in a way the companion cannot repay.
- Fulfilling a request the companion has been trying to get done for years.
- Offering something concrete the companion wants — a cure, a home, a name cleared.
- Finding them at a crossroads and making them a promise the Overseer thinks they'll keep.

Common companions: a ghoul with a pre-war grudge, a freed slave, an off-duty Brotherhood scribe, a stray mongrel, a talking Mr. Handy with a bad accent, a synth who has no idea what they are, a courier with one more letter to deliver.
