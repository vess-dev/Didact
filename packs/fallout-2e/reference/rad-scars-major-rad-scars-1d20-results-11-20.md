| d20 | Scar |
|---|---|
| 11 | **Glowing Marrow.** Once per session, when you would roll on the Scars table, you may take 1 Rad to instead treat that roll as a Close Shave (row 1). |
| 12 | **Rad-Clever.** You detect hot zones at twice normal range without a Geiger counter. |
| 13 | **Withered Hand.** Your grip is weakened on one side. Any two-handed weapon you wield — rifle, super sledge, heavy weapon — is **Impaired unless braced** against a wall, the ground, a bipod, or power-armor servos. |
| 14 | **Pale Eyes.** You cannot see clearly in direct sunlight. Ranged attacks at midday outdoors are Impaired. |
| 15 | **Scabbed-Over Ear.** You are deaf on one side. You cannot detect threats approaching from that direction by sound alone. |
| 16 | **Mutfruit Smell.** Bloatflies, bloodbugs, and mirelurk hatchlings track you at short range. |
| 17 | **Thinned Blood.** The first Scar you roll in any session gains +1 HP Lost on the lookup (maximum 12). |
| 18 | **Radiant.** You glow faintly in pitch dark. Stealth in darkness is impossible. Allies within 20 ft gain a soft light. |
| 19 | **Pre-war Memory.** Once per session the Overseer tells you something true about the pre-war world you could not possibly know. |
| 20 | **Ghoul-Kissed.** Counts as **two** Rad Scars toward ghoulification — a PC who rolls this while already carrying any other Rad Scar reaches the three-Scar threshold and rolls for ghoulification at the next long rest. +1 max HP, −1 max WIL. |
