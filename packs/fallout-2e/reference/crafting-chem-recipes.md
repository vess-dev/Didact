A PC with the **Chem Cook** background, another background or perk that says it can brew, or one working under a chem-cook NPC, may prepare chems at a Chemistry Station. Each recipe is a Downtime Action and costs caps worth of reagents. Yield is 1d4 doses per attempt (roll once per recipe). Where a background or perk sets its own terms — a different yield, no station, half cost — those terms replace the ones here.

| Chem | Reagent Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Stimpak** | 2d6 × 10 caps | Heals 1d6 STR. |
| **Super Stimpak** | 2d6 × 25 caps | Full STR. Adds 1 Fatigue on use. |
| **RadAway** | 2d6 × 10 caps | Clears 1 Rad. Non-addictive. |
| **Rad-X** | 2d6 × 5 caps | Immunity to next Rad exposure. Non-addictive. |
| **Med-X** | 2d6 × 10 caps | Ignore a single Scar's onset for one scene. Addictive. |
| **Psycho** | 2d6 × 12 caps | Enhanced damage one combat. Addictive. |
| **Buffout** | 2d6 × 10 caps | Immune to Critical Damage for one scene. Addictive. |
| **Mentats** | 2d6 × 12 caps | Cannot fail WIL saves against deception or confusion for one scene. Addictive. |
| **Jet** | 2d6 × 8 caps | Extra action this round. Addictive. |
| **Turbo** | 2d6 × 12 caps | Automatically pass the first-round DEX save to act this combat. Addictive. |
| **Day Tripper** | 2d6 × 8 caps | Reaction rolls shift one step better for one scene. Addictive. |
| **Calmex** | 2d6 × 10 caps | First attack from stealth this scene is Enhanced. Addictive. |
| **Steady** | 2d6 × 6 caps | Ranged attacks are not Impaired for one scene. Addictive. |
| **Overdrive** | 2d6 × 12 caps | For one combat, targets pushed past 0 HP automatically fail their Critical Damage save. One dose per session. Addictive. |
| **Cateye** | 2d6 × 6 caps | See in darkness for one scene. Non-addictive. |
| **Hydra** | 2d6 × 12 caps | Mends one Broken or Crippled Scar over a single night's rest. Addictive. |
| **Ultrajet** | 2d6 × 15 caps | Extra action this round and the next. Addictive; its addiction save counts as STR −2. |
| **Addictol / Fixer** | 2d6 × 16 caps | Cures one addiction immediately. Non-addictive. |
| **Antivenom** | 2d6 × 6 caps | Cures creature venom. |
| **Healing powder** | 2d6 × 2 caps | Heals 1d4 STR; ranged attacks Impaired for one scene. No station needed — a mortar and the right weeds. |

A botched batch (Overseer's call) produces **one** dose of something unpleasant: the Overseer picks a chem at random, and that dose counts as addictive even if the chem normally isn't.
