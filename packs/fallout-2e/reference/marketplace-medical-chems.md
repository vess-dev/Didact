All chems are _petty_ (no slot), consumed on use, and do not cause Rads.

### Medical

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stimpak (heals 1d6 STR, 1 use) | 50 | The wasteland's universal painkiller |
| Super Stimpak (heals full STR, 1 use) | 120 | Adds 1 Fatigue after use |
| RadAway (clears 1 Rad, 1 use) | 40 | Non-addictive |
| Rad-X (immune to next Rad exposure, 1 use) | 30 | Non-addictive |
| Bandages (3 uses) | 25 | Stabilizes a dying ally |
| Doctor's bag (3 uses) | 60 | Used during a week's rest, one use mends a Broken Scar — the "when mended" roll is made without a clinic visit |
| Antivenom (1 use) | 30 | Cures cazador/nightstalker/radscorpion poison |
| Healing powder (1 use) | 10 | Tribal remedy. Heals 1d4 STR. Blurs vision — ranged attacks Impaired for one scene |
| Addictol / Fixer (1 use) | 80 | Cures one addiction immediately. Non-addictive. |
| Medical clinic visit | 80 | Full STR restoration, one Scar treatment, and a rad flush that clears all Rads |

### Alcohol

Alcohol is cheap, common, and briefly comforting. Each drink heals 1 HP but adds 1 Fatigue the next morning.
