> You held a pre-war checkpoint for two centuries. Nobody came to relieve you. You walked away looking for reinforcements. You are still, technically, on duty.

- **Background:** Mister Gutsy
- **Attributes:** STR 14 · DEX 12 · WIL 11
- **HP:** 4
- **Armor:** 2 (combat chassis plating, _bulky_, built-in)

**Gear:**
- 3d6 caps (confiscated from enemy combatants per subparagraph 7)
- No rations (you do not eat)
- Built-in spotlight
- **Plasma gun arm** (d10, microfusion cell)
- **Buzzsaw arm** (d8)
- Combat chassis plating (2 Armor, _bulky_, built-in)
- **Ammo Crate** (_bulky_, counts as 3 uses of any ballistic ammo you claim)
- American flag decal (_petty_, faded, slightly on fire at the edges)
- *See Playing Unusual PCs → Robot PCs for baseline rules.*

**Your Last Mission:** *Checkpoint Duty.* You held a pre-war checkpoint on a highway for two centuries. The checkpoint is still technically yours. You came out looking for reinforcements.

**What Military Hardware They Gave You:** *Targeting Assist.* Your first ranged attack in any combat is Enhanced.

**Traits:** Pre-war military-styled. Cheerful sergeant voice-module. Cannot lie by design.

**Bond (Bond 1):** A dead uncle (in *human* terms — a retired general who left this unit with a sealed package) left you a **Gold-Plated Bar** (400 caps, heavy, cold). The note that came with it said, *"Spend it and they will find you."*
