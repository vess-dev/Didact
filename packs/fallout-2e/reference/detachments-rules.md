- A detachment has its own HP, STR, DEX, WIL, and attack, like any creature.
- When a detachment takes **Critical Damage**, it is routed or significantly weakened. The Overseer decides which.
- When a detachment reaches **0 STR**, it is destroyed.
- Attacks on a detachment by individuals are **Impaired** unless the attack has the _blast_ tag.
- Attacks on individuals by a detachment are **Enhanced** and deal _blast_ damage.
