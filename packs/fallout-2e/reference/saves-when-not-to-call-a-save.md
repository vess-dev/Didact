title: "When Not to Call a Save"
tags: [saves]
source: overseers-guide/saves#when-not-to-call-a-save
mechanics: [save]
---
- To notice something obvious.
- To perform a skill that matches the character's background without pressure.
- To let a player try again at the same thing (if the first save failed, the outcome stands).
- To add drama to a foregone conclusion.
