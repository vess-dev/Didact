At a Cooking Station with ingredients, a PC spends a Downtime Action (or 10 minutes of a rest in camp, if the Overseer allows) to turn raw materials into usable meals. Meals are _petty_.

| Meal | Ingredients | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Brahmin Steak** | 1 raw brahmin steak | Full HP, clears 1 Fatigue. |
| **Radstag Stew** | 1 radstag meat + foraged herbs | Full HP. For one watch, cannot become Deprived from hunger or cold. |
| **Mirelurk Cakes** | 2 mirelurk meat | Full HP, clears 1 Rad. |
| **Yao Guai Roast** | 1 yao guai meat | Full HP. For one watch, you cannot fail a STR save from physical exertion. |
| **Squirrel on a Stick** | Any small-game meat | One meal. |
| **Mole Rat Chunks** | 1 mole rat meat | One meal. Cheap. |
| **Purified Water** | 1 dirty water + fuel | One use of purified water. Boiling kills the rot, not the Rads — water drawn inside a hot zone stays dirty. |
