Roll **1d50** or just pick. Entries 1–40 are mostly **human** backgrounds, with two ghouls (the Ghoul and the Pre-war Celebrity), a super mutant, and a synth among them. Entries 41–50 are non-human and non-organic — eight robot chassis, a Zetan, and a talking deathclaw. Every non-human background follows the extra rules in **Playing Unusual PCs**. Any background can be taken by a player who prefers to choose rather than roll. The Overseer may veto a background that doesn't fit the tone of the campaign.
