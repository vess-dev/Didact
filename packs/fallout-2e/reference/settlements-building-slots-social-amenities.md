### Social & Amenities

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Clinic** | Settlers heal Attributes in three days instead of seven. Stimpaks available for half price. A visiting PC may roll a single **Cure an Addiction** attempt here for free. |
| **Bar / Saloon** | Settler spirits lift; the rumor mill runs. Reaction rolls on first visit shift one step better. The party may always hear a fresh rumor here, Overseer's pick. |
| **General Store** | +1 Industry. Merchant restocks when the party visits. Party may sell one unused loot item at full market price once per session. |
| **Trading Post** | +1 Industry. Full caravan turnaround — caravans route through this settlement automatically. Pairs with Brahmin Pen for compound effect. |
| **Jukebox / Radio Set** | _petty_ building. A pre-war radio that plays Diamond City Radio, Galaxy News Radio, or whatever the Overseer decides. Settlement detachments automatically pass their first Morale save per raid. |
| **Shrine / Meeting Hall** | The settlement's culture hardens. The town automatically passes WIL saves against panic during raids. A PC may use this as the target of a Downtime *Strengthening Ties* action with the whole settlement. |
| **Schoolhouse** | +1 Population over the next season. Children grow up literate enough to read a terminal. |
| **Picnic Area / Gardens** | _petty_ building. Settler spirits lift, and a night's rest here counts as a full night's safe sleep (clears Fatigue) even when the settlement is under threat. |
