---
Roll 1d20. **Equal to or under** your Attribute succeeds. **Natural 1** always succeeds; **natural 20** always fails. When two sides clash, whichever is at greater risk rolls.

- **STR** — power, endurance, radiation resistance
- **DEX** — aim, reflex, stealth
- **WIL** — nerve, charisma, tech, sanity
