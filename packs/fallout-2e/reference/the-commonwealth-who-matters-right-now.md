Pick three factions. Any three. Give each one:
- A **home** (a place on the map they hold).
- A **hand** (a specific person the party will meet first).
- A **want** (one specific thing the faction wants *this season*).
- A **rival** (another faction whose want is incompatible).

That's a campaign. The rest is details.
