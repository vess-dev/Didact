Holotapes are **data cartridges** — pre-war audio logs, recorded journals, corporate training videos, security camera footage, the occasional jingle, the occasional voice you recognize. They are not perks and they do not grant mechanical benefits. They carry the voice of the pre-war dead into the ruin.

- A holotape takes one inventory slot unless marked _petty_.
- Playing a holotape requires a Pip-Boy, a terminal, or a power-armor HUD.
- Every holotape is lore — an NPC's last words, a corporation's training reel, the audio of the moment the bombs fell, a coded message between lovers, the minutes of a pre-war board meeting nobody ever heard.
- Some holotapes contain **passcodes, keycodes, or map data** that unlock a specific door or reveal a specific location. Treat these as information, not as a permanent character upgrade.

Overseers should use holotapes to deliver flavor and mystery, and to reward players who take the time to listen.
