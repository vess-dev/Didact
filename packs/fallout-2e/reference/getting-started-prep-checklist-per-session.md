Fallout 2e rewards light prep. Before a session, have these ready:

- **One settlement** the party might visit — name, one building, one named NPC, one rumor.
- **One ruin** the party might enter — name, one reason to go in, one memorable room, one danger, one hidden thing.
- **One faction** that is pushing on the party this session — what they want, who's carrying it.
- **One clock** ticking toward something the party would want to prevent.
- **One card up your sleeve** — a named NPC, a strange relic, a weather event — you can drop in if the session goes quiet.

Do not pre-plan the beats of the session. The party's choices fill in the rest.
