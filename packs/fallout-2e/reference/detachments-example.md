**Raider Warband (Detachment)**
- 8 HP, 1 Armor, 13 STR, 12 DEX, 9 WIL, mixed pipe weapons (d8, _blast_)
- Led by **Scrap**, a Crew Boss with 14 WIL and a combat rifle. Morale saves use Scrap's WIL.
- If Scrap falls, the warband saves Morale immediately with its own 9 WIL.
