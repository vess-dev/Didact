3 deficient, 6 weak, 10 average, 14 noteworthy, 18 legendary.

- **STR**: Health, physical power, toxin resistance. The ability to *take* a hit.
- **DEX**: Start at 10. Raise for speed, agility, finesse; lower for slow or clumsy.
- **WIL**: Influence, presence, spirit, defense against mind-affecting effects.
