Between sessions, characters can do research, train, mend fences, or build things. A PC is limited to one **Downtime Action** at a time. Not available in recovery or unsafe conditions.

### Milestones

For multi-step activities, the Overseer sets 1–5 **Milestones**. Each represents a non-interactive block of work. Different approaches may have different milestones. The Overseer can add or remove milestones as play develops.

### Costs

Each milestone has a **Cost**:
- **Caps**: direct payment.
- **Resources**: specific items or trade goods.
- **Reputation**: trading on your standing with a faction.
- **Loss**: a unique sacrifice — a finger, a relic, a secret, a friend.

A character unable to pay may try to work around a cost with skills, force of will, or a favor. The Overseer states the risk, the PC makes a WIL save, and the cost is reduced or avoided on a success.
