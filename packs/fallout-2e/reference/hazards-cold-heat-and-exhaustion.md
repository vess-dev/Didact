Exposure to **Cold** or **Hot** weather without shelter makes a PC **Deprived** after a watch. Each additional watch without shelter adds **1 Fatigue**. A PC caught without shelter in **Extreme** weather (blizzard, firestorm, rad storm) is Deprived immediately. Travelling through foul weather has its own costs — Fatigue, or Rads when the storm is radioactive; see **Procedures → Weather**.

Frostbite, heatstroke, and dust-lung are narrative. The Overseer may apply a Scar from the table if exposure gets severe.
