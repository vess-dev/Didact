A PC on fire takes **1d4 STR** damage per round until it's put out. Putting it out takes your action — drop and roll, jump in water, smother it with a blanket.

A PC inside a sustained flame (flamethrower, burning building, crashed Vertibird) makes a **DEX save** each round they stay — on a fail, they catch fire.

Armor does *not* apply to fire damage unless it's rated against it (a hazmat suit, the Asbestos Lining mod).
