Combat in Fallout 2e is fast, brutal, and usually resolved in fewer than three rounds. It has four characteristics that set it apart:

- **Side Initiative**: Except for the first round (DEX save to act), both sides take turns as a whole, and each side's actions resolve simultaneously.
- **Attacks Automatically Hit**: Combatants deal damage automatically once they've committed to an attack — no to-hit roll. When more than one attacker targets the same foe, only the single highest damage die applies.
- **HP and Scars**: HP is luck, skill, grit, and the near-miss. It comes back fast. Damage to exactly 0 HP yields a Scar from the table; damage beyond HP spills into STR.
- **Critical Damage**: Once a character's STR is reduced, they make a STR save at the new (lower) score. On a fail, they are down.
