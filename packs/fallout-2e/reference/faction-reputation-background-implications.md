A PC whose background ties them to a faction in good standing — a Brotherhood Initiate still under orders, an NCR Trooper on leave, a Minuteman — begins one step higher than the party's default with that faction, and one step lower with its opposite. A PC whose background makes them a deserter, exile, defector, or runaway — most Legion Warriors, a Brotherhood Outcast, an Enclave Defector, a Gunner who walked, a Synth Runaway — begins one step *lower* with the faction they left; the background's first table usually says which they are. Either way the faction knows their face, and they lose a step faster than their companions when the party acts against it. A Brotherhood Initiate whose party slaughters a patrol is not Neutral — she is personally Hostile.
