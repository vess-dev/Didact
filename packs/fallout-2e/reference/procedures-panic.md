Surrounded, cornered, pitch dark, face-to-face with a deathclaw — any of these may trigger *panic*. The Overseer calls a WIL save. On a fail:

- You are **Panicked**: 0 HP, cannot act in the first round of combat, and all your attacks are Impaired.
- A Panicked PC must make a WIL save on their turn as an action to shake it off.
