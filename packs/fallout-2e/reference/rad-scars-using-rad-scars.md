A Rad Scar should always be visible in fiction before its mechanical effect matters. When the Overseer hands a player one, they describe what the PC notices in the next mirror they pass, or what the next stranger flinches at.

A PC who has cleared all their free slots of Rads but has multiple Rad Scars is no longer truly safe from radiation — they are walking around with the memory of it on their skin.
