- **HP**: Restored by a **short rest** in safety.
- **Attribute loss**: Restored by a **long rest** (a week's rest), usually with the help of a doc, a chem, or a pre-war auto-doc. A Stimpak can restore a little STR in the field.
- **Scars**: Persistent, rolled from the table in combat.
- Some healing is free (water and rest). Anything faster or more specialized — bandages, a Stimpak, surgery, a cybernetic graft, RadAway — costs caps.

See **Time Units & Rest** below for the full list of rest types and what each one clears.
