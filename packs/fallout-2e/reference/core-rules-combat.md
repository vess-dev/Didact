### Rounds

- A round is about ten seconds. Both sides take turns acting, and the results of a side resolve simultaneously.
- **First round only**: each PC must pass a DEX save to act. Failure means losing the turn (surprise, fumbling the weapon, freezing up). Items, abilities, or fiction may bypass this.
- Enemies then act. First round ends. Subsequent rounds begin with PCs and proceed normally.

### Actions

On their turn a character may move up to about 40 feet *and* take one action: attack, reload, shove, use a chem, shout orders, break cover, play a holotape into a nearby device, etc. Declare before rolling. The Overseer calls saves where needed.

### Attacking & Damage

- The attacker rolls their weapon die, subtracts the target's Armor, and applies the rest to HP. **Attacks in combat automatically hit.**
- If several attackers target the same foe, roll all their damage dice and keep the single highest result.
- If damage would reduce a PC's HP to *exactly* 0, roll on the **Scars** table.

### Attack Modifiers

- **Impaired**: firing from bad cover, blind, bound, dazed. Roll **1d4** for damage regardless of weapon. Unarmed always does d4.
- **Enhanced**: helpless target, perfect flank, daring stunt that pays off. Roll **1d12** instead of the normal damage die. Enhanced never makes an attack worse — an attacker whose own dice are already better (d12, or d10+d10) keeps them.
- **Enhanced does not stack** — an attack is Enhanced at most once; multiple sources of Enhanced on the same attack give no extra benefit (it is still a single d12). An attack that is both Impaired and Enhanced is neither: roll the weapon's normal die.
- **Blast**: grenades, flamers, minigun sprays, shotgun at close range. Rolls separately against each target in the area. If the number of targets is unclear, roll the weapon die itself to determine how many are hit.
- **Two weapons**: roll both damage dice, keep the higher (e.g., d6+d6).

### Critical Damage

- Damage beyond HP spills into **STR**.
- After taking damage to STR, the target immediately makes a STR save using the *new* STR score. Fail = **Critical Damage**.
- A PC with Critical Damage can only crawl and gasp. Bandages or a Stimpak stabilize them; untreated, they die within an hour.
- NPCs and monsters failing a Critical Damage save are dead at the Overseer's discretion. Some enemies have special triggers on their victim failing a Critical Damage save (see Bestiary).

### Attribute Loss

- Damage taken *outside* combat (traps, radiation bursts, hunger, failed saves) is usually applied to an Attribute directly — typically STR.
- **STR 0** = dead.
- **DEX 0** = paralyzed, helpless.
- **WIL 0** = broken mind, catatonic or hallucinating.
- DEX and WIL loss can be restored with long rest or expert care.

### Character Death

When a character dies, the player either makes a new one or takes over a hireling. They rejoin the party immediately to keep the game moving.

### Detachments

A large group of similar fighters — a raider gang, a squad of super mutants, a feral horde — is a single **Detachment**.

- A Detachment taking Critical Damage is routed or broken.
- At 0 STR, it is destroyed.
- Attacks on Detachments by individuals are **Impaired** (unless Blast).
- Attacks on individuals by Detachments are **Enhanced** and deal **Blast** damage.

### Retreat

Breaking off from a fight requires a DEX save and a direction to run toward. No save, no escape.

### Ranged Attacks

- Ranged weapons can hit anyone near enough to see clearly. At long range (across a canyon, through a storefront window), attacks are Impaired.
- **Ammunition is not tracked** unless a weapon's description says otherwise, or the Overseer decides resupply is part of the fiction. When it is tracked, **1 ammo use = 1 use of the weapon**: one attack, whether that is a single crank-charged musket shot or an SMG burst that sprays half a corridor.

### Heavy Weapons

- **Heavy weapons must be braced** — against a wall, the ground, a bipod, a mounted rig, or power armor servos. A heavy weapon fired unbraced, or while moving, is **Impaired** (d4) regardless of its normal die.
- The *Heavy Gunner* perk and the Gunner background's *Heavy Weapons Cert* ignore this penalty. So does a super mutant, and so does a trained power armor wearer in a fueled suit.

### Called Shots

- A player may declare a specific target — a gun, a collar, a fuel tank, a kneecap. The attack is **Impaired** (d4 damage). On a hit the target takes the damage *and* the narrative effect (disarmed, freed, spilled, crippled). The Overseer is the final word on whether a called shot is possible in the fiction.

### Power Armor

- Power armor is its own category: **3 Armor**, _bulky_, **loud**, fusion-core-powered.
- **Wearing power armor without training means every DEX save automatically fails** — the servos fight the wearer and every step is a tripping hazard. The following backgrounds start trained: **Brotherhood Initiate**, **Brotherhood Outcast**, **Enclave Defector**. Any other PC can earn training through a Downtime Action under a qualified instructor. (Robot PCs never wear power armor — their chassis is their armor.)
- A trained wearer in a fueled suit suffers no DEX penalty. See **Marketplace → Armor — Heavy** for the full list of power armor models and the rules for cores, combat posture, and exiting a dead suit.
