A settlement in good standing with the party acts as a permanent **+1 step** of local reputation in a region, and may count as a friendly faction at the Overseer's option under **Faction Reputation**. A settlement the party abandons or betrays becomes a grudge-holder and can appear as an NPC antagonist later.
