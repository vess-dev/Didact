**The Glow** is the name for what lies beneath and between the ruins: the subways, the bunker networks, the pre-war vault levels, the hot zones, the caves carved out by old mining and filled by old monsters. It is a realm without sun.

The things that survive down there have adapted — blind ghouls, luminescent mole rats, deaf mutants with skin like boiled leather, worms that swim through concrete. Entrances to the Glow are called **Gates**: vault doors, subway tunnels, storm drains, cracked bunker hatches, reactor access shafts. Most Gates are locked, guarded, or hidden. When one opens, whatever was behind it comes out.

Only the brave and the foolish enter the Glow. Few come back. The few who do whisper of living machinery, of dead-eyed corporate ghosts still punching time cards in a flooded lobby, of things that watch from behind the walls and wait for sleep. But the pull is the same as it ever was: working fusion cores, intact Stimpaks, pre-war weapons in factory wrap, relics that do the impossible. People will always go down.
