The Commonwealth of Ashes is a deliberately unfixed region — close enough to California, DC, the Mojave, or the Commonwealth that any Fallout game is legible here, far enough that the Overseer can steal freely. It does not have a map you need to memorize. It has:

- **A small farming belt** — three or four Minuteman-style settlements growing enough mutfruit, tato, and razorgrain to trade, plus one brahmin ranch.
- **A pre-war city** in the middle distance, mostly collapsed — call it New Boston, the Capital, or just "the ruin." A Diamond City–style settlement lives inside a defensible pre-war landmark at its center.
- **A Vault-Tec vault** under the city, sealed since 2077, running a social experiment that has had two centuries to go wrong. The survivors inside are no longer quite human.
- **A glowing crater** at the city's edge — a Glowing Sea in miniature, left by a tactical warhead the Enclave called down to finish the city after the bombs. Children of Atom pilgrims walk toward it.
- **A mountain range** to one side where a pre-war Anchorage-era military installation remains under the snow, still patrolled by half-functional sentry bots and the occasional Brotherhood scouting party.
- **A long highway** — call it Route 1, Highway 95, whatever fits — broken, draped with kudzu, walked by caravans who know which overpasses the raiders have taken.
- **A major caravan hub** where the highway crosses a river, with a Crimson Caravan–style trading post and a Followers of the Apocalypse clinic sharing the same stockade wall.
