A **companion** is an NPC who has chosen — or been chosen — to travel with the party for reasons beyond caps. A hireling works for pay, walks away when the pay stops, and is handled under **Hirelings** in the Core Rules. A companion stays because the story made them stay.

Each PC may have **one active companion** at a time. A second potential companion will travel, fight, camp, and share loot, but they will not develop — they'll drift away by the next long rest unless the first companion leaves first. A party may travel with any number of hirelings *alongside* their companions, but only companions have Loyalty and the Command action.
