---
A detachment breaks when:
- It loses its leader and the leader's WIL was carrying it.
- It takes Critical Damage and the Overseer rules it routed rather than weakened.
- Half its number is down and it fails the Morale save (narrative judgment — the Overseer decides what "half" means for an abstract unit).

A broken detachment scatters, surrenders, or routs. Individuals may be left behind — use them as story hooks, not loose ends.
