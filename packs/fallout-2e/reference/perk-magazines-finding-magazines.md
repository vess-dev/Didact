Magazines are common in ruins, vaults, pre-war libraries, settlement bookshops, pre-war newsstands, and the personal effects of dead wastelanders who were trying to improve themselves. Overseers should:

- Place at least one magazine in every second or third significant location the party explores.
- Hide the rarest (marked _rare_) in vaults, locked rooms, and named NPC possessions.
- Let players find duplicates sometimes — a magazine the party already has one character trained on is still valuable: trade it, sell it, or hand it to another PC.
- Treat magazines as loot that tells a story. A copy of *Grognak the Barbarian Issue 4* on a raider boss's desk says something about the raider boss.
