These rules exist to make unusual PCs *distinct* at the table, not to punish the player for choosing one. A ghoul PC, a synth PC, and a human PC should all have the same moment-to-moment agency — they just have different edges and different costs. If a rule here is making a player's life miserable without producing interesting story, ignore it.

The Overseer's job is to put every unusual PC's edge *and* their cost on the table equally often. A synth who is never hunted by the Institute is just a human; a deathclaw who is always locked out of settlements is just a monster. Use the rules as the starting texture and let the fiction turn the knob.
