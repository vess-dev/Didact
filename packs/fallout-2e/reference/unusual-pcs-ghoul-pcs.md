(*Ghoul and Pre-war Celebrity backgrounds; also a PC who ghoulified through the Rad Scar system*)

### What a Ghoul Is
- **Two centuries of radiation and still walking.** You remember the Before. Or you try to.
- **Scar-proof, not rad-proof.** A ghoul PC never rolls for Rad Scars, but Rads still fill their slots as normal. Some Ghoul background results extend this to environmental Rads.
- **Feral risk.** A ghoul who spends a full day at six or more Rads without clearing any rolls 1d20 at the next watch. On a 20, the player loses the character to ferality (see Core Rules → Rad Scars & Ghoulification).
- **No aging.** A ghoul PC does not die of old age. They remember what food tasted like, badly.

### How Other Wastelanders React
- Most settlements will let a ghoul inside but charge more, seat them apart, or post a guard. A few (Underworld, the Slog, Goodneighbor-type enclaves) are ghoul-run and treat non-ghouls the same way.
- **Feral ghouls** do not attack ghoul PCs first unless provoked — ferals go for smoothskins, not their own kind. The Ghoul background's *Ghoul-Sign* result goes further.
