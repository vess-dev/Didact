```
     +---------------(drovers' trail, 2W, hidden)---------------+
     |                                                           |
[Dim Hollow] --(highway, 1W)-- [Overpass] --(highway, 1W)-- [Five-Corners]
     |                             |                             |
 (trail, 2W)                  (wastes, 3W)                  (trail, 2W)
     |                             |                             |
 [Vault 97]                  [Runoff Pool]               [Radio Station]
                                                                 |
                                                            (wastes, 3W)
                                                                 |
                                                          [Old Purifier]
```

- **Dim Hollow**: Farming town. Mother Cupper. Population frightened of the radio.
- **Overpass**: Collapsed highway. Raiders here extort caravans — the fast road between the two towns runs straight through their toll.
- **Drovers' trail** *(hidden path)*: Skirts the Overpass. As long as the highway, with a chance of getting lost, and no raiders. Not on the players' map until a local in Dim Hollow or Five-Corners shows them.
- **Five-Corners**: Trade post. Hub for the valley.
- **Vault 97**: Sealed Vault-Tec facility. See Vault & Ruin Seeds.
- **Runoff Pool**: Clean for one hour a day at noon. Ora the prospector knows this.
- **Radio Station**: Source of the Operator-7 broadcasts.
- **Old Purifier**: Pre-war water plant, locked down, strange lights at night.

Paths marked "highway" (old highway) cannot be lost; "trail" (beaten trail) is 2-in-6 lost; "wastes" (open wastes) is 3-in-6. Each watch count already includes the path's distance and type (every path here is a Short one); add terrain and weather penalties on top.
