### 1. Through Scars

The Scars table on the Core Rules page is mostly a record of what broke you, but nearly every entry also gives you something back — a chance at a higher maximum HP or a higher Attribute. A character who has survived three Scars is a mechanically different character from one who has taken none.

### 2. Through Training

A character can learn a specific technique during Downtime by finding a **Master** and paying a **Cost**. See *Downtime Actions* in the Procedures chapter. Each successful training produces a concrete, mechanical change — a new attack trick with one weapon (Enhanced on a named condition), a new way to resist a specific effect, or a new option in a specific situation.

Training should always:
- Come from an experience in play.
- Cost something specific (caps, time, a favor, a relic, a piece of the character's past).
- Produce a small, precise mechanical upgrade (not a general +1 to everything).

### 3. Through Acquisition

Every item a character keeps is a form of growth. A PC with a Targeting Visor is a different character than one without. A PC who has befriended a trained mongrel dog has a new ability — the dog. Every relic, every Perk Magazine read, every permanent ally is an upgrade that required a story.
