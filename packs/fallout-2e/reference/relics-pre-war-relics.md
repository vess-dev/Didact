Relics are unique pre-war items — things that still work, or work differently. Using one costs nothing by itself — no Fatigue, no Rads — unless its entry says otherwise. Most have limited uses or charges with a specific **Recharge** condition described in the item entry.

Relics are not loot in the usual sense. A relic is a story the players can hold in their hands. A relic is a decision. Distribute them one per session at most, and let the party fight for each one.
