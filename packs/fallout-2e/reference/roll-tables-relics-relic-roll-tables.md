When the party cracks a pre-war cache and the Overseer needs to decide what's inside, roll here. Relics are scarce — distribute at most one per session under normal circumstances. If the same relic comes up twice, re-roll or let it be a second copy in a different location. Armors and weapons have their own tables; these are the pre-war *things*.
