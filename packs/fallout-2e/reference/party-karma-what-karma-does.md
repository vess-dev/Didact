Karma modifies **Reaction rolls with strangers** by its value. A +2 party with an Honored reputation rolls 2d6+2 on Reaction; a −2 Infamous party rolls 2d6−2. The defaults named on the ladder (Kind, Wary, Hostile) are what a stranger shows when the Overseer doesn't bother to roll. Karma does not touch faction encounters — factions have their own ladder.

At high or low karma, the Overseer is encouraged to **introduce named NPCs who respond to the rumor**: a bounty hunter contract at −2 or lower, a wandering pilgrim who seeks the party at +2 or higher. Karma should produce faces at the table, not just dice modifiers.
