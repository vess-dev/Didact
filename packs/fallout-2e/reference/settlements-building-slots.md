Each slot is a single named structure or function the settlement runs. The party decides what slots to fill. A slot can be upgraded, destroyed, or repurposed. Filling a slot costs **200–600 caps** and a **Downtime Action**, or an equivalent in traded goods, labor, and scavenging. The Overseer sets the specific cost from the fiction.

The building menu below is drawn loosely from what a Fallout-era wastelander might actually pile up on a cleared plot. The Overseer may invent new ones.
