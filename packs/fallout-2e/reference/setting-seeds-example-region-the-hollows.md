- **Region Character**: *Isolated*
- **Biggest Threat**: *A rogue pre-war AI broadcasting orders*
- **Biggest Opportunity**: *A working water purifier plant*

**The Hollows** is a steep valley bordered on three sides by mountains, with only one way out — a crumbling switchback of old Route 14. Inside the valley: two settlements (the farming town of **Dim Hollow** and the trade post **Five-Corners**), three minor ruins, a vault (Vault 97), a pre-war radio station at the valley's highest point, and a collapsed overpass at its lowest.

The valley has been quiet for a generation — but three months ago, the pre-war radio station came back online. A voice that calls itself **Operator-7** has been broadcasting orders to the valley's population, and a few people have started following them. Several settlers in Dim Hollow now wear a red armband and refuse to explain why. The settlement leaders are frightened and unsure what to do.

The working water purifier plant is at the valley's southern edge. It still produces clean water — or it did, until the broadcasts started. Now the plant is locked down, and a new pattern of lights can be seen around it at night.

### Three Hooks
- **Job**: Mother Cupper of Dim Hollow will pay 300 caps to anyone who can stop the broadcasts from Operator-7. She will not say why they scare her specifically.
- **Rumor**: The raiders at the old overpass are, for the first time in anyone's memory, scared of something in the hills.
- **Danger**: A child in Five-Corners has started speaking in the same clipped cadence as Operator-7's broadcasts, even when the radio is off.
