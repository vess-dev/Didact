A PC knows what their Background would know without asking. The Ranger knows how to read sign. The Medic knows the difference between a bullet wound and a knife wound. The Hacker knows the difference between a Vault-Tec terminal and a RobCo one.

When the Overseer is in doubt whether a character's Background covers a specific piece of knowledge, they can:
- Say "yes" by default, and move on.
- Ask the player to make a short case for why their character would know it.
- Roll the **Die of Fate** when it is a true toss-up — the higher the result, the more they might reasonably know.
