- A passenger firing a personal weapon from a moving vehicle is **Impaired**. The shot is not Impaired if the vehicle is stopped or the passenger is a gunner in a proper firing position.
- A vehicle with a **mounted weapon** (minigun, .50 MG, missile launcher, mounted laser) may be fired by a dedicated gunner without the Impaired penalty. The gunner is exposed to do it, though: incoming attacks against the gunner are made normally (not Impaired).
- **Ramming** a target deals the vehicle's Frame HP / 4 (round up) as damage to the target, and the same back to the vehicle. Against a creature larger than the vehicle (a deathclaw matriarch, a mirelurk queen), ramming is a bad idea.
