At a **Workshop / Scrap Bench**, a PC with a Tinker background (or in apprenticeship) may build small pre-war-style gadgets. Each is a Downtime Action, a Scrap cost, and a cap cost.

| Gadget | Scrap | Caps | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Homemade Geiger Counter** | 2 | 50 | Detects hot zones at 40 ft. |
| **Trap Kit** (3 uses) | 1 | 30 | Places a snare or tripline; the target makes a DEX save or takes 1d6 STR damage. |
| **Radio Jammer** | 3 | 70 | Blocks a single nearby radio transmission for one scene. |
| **Signal Beacon** | 2 | 50 | Calls a known ally to the party's current point within a day. |
| **Fusion Cell Recharger** | 4 | 120 | Once per session, recover 1 use on a spent fusion or microfusion cell. |
| **Pipe Pistol** (d6) | 1 | 5 | A crude but working firearm. |
| **Pipe Rifle** (d6, _bulky_) | 2 | 5 | A crude but working long-arm. |
| **Frequency Whistle** | 1 | 20 | A pre-war dog whistle. One trained animal in earshot comes to the user once per scene. |
| **Salvaged Pip-Link** | 3 | 100 | A strapped-on handheld that mimics one Pip-Boy utility function of the user's choice (light, map, rad meter). Fragile — breaks on a Scar roll. |
| **Tripwire Camera** | 2 | 60 | Fires a flashbulb and logs a crude print-still of whoever crossed the line. 5 uses. |
| **Shoulder Lamp Rig** | 1 | 40 | A helmet-less lantern harness. Frees a hand. Fusion-cell or oil-flask powered. |
| **Tinker's Multitool** | 2 | 80 | _petty_. Re-roll a failed DEX save involving tools or tinkering, once per scene. Breaks on a natural 20 on the re-rolled die. |
| **Improvised Smoke Pot** (3 uses) | 1 | 25 | One scene of heavy smoke in a room-sized area. All ranged attacks through the smoke are Impaired. |
| **Pop-Up Turret Kit** (1 use) | 5 | 300 | A deployable pipe turret: 4 HP, fires d6 at anything that crosses its cone for one combat, then burns out. Takes 2 Downtime Actions to assemble. |
| **Scrap Drone** (1 scene per fusion cell) | 6 | 400 | A crude flying eyebot. No combat ability. Scouts a room at the Overseer's discretion, up to 400 ft line of sight, and relays basic shapes. Fragile — 2 HP, 0 Armor. |
| **Workshop Auto-Doc Bay** (installed, not portable) | 8 | 600 | Installed only at a settlement with a Clinic. Once per week, a PC may use it for a Stimpak's worth of STR restoration without paying the Stimpak cost. Requires power. |
| **Mounted Floodlight** (installed) | 2 | 80 | Lights a building or watch tower. The settlement gains +1 Defense against raids at night. Requires power. |
