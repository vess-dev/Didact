A live wire, a ruptured fusion core, a Tesla arc: **1d6 STR** damage and a **DEX save**, dropping whatever's in the PC's hand on a fail.

Metal armor does *not* help — it conducts. Rubber soles, Insulated Weave, and a grounded power armor frame do.
