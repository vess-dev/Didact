Explosions already have the _blast_ tag — see **Core Rules → Attack Modifiers**.

A PC outside the blast radius but close enough to be shaken makes a **DEX save** — on a fail, they are knocked prone and their next attack is **Impaired** from the ringing. A wall, a hard corner, or a ditch lets a PC ignore the first near-miss per scene.
