A character's Background is the most important piece of who they are. It tells you where they're from, what they did for a living, what they believe, and what's in their bag. In Fallout 2e, a Background is both your starting loadout and a lens for interpreting dice rolls — a Desert Ranger doesn't need to make a WIL save to face down a feral dog, and a Wasteland Medic knows without rolling that the wound in front of her is going to kill in six hours. Your Background is the story you bring to the table.
