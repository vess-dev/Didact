> You were alive before the bombs. You remember the taste of unrusted water. The smoothskins flinch when they see you. That's fine. You've had two hundred years to stop caring.

- **Background:** Ghoul
- **Attributes:** STR 12 · DEX 12 · WIL 13
- **HP:** 6
- **Armor:** 1 (heavy duster)

**Gear:**
- 3d6 caps
- Rations (3 uses)
- Lantern + fusion cell (6 uses)
- **Pre-war revolver** (d8)
- Heavy duster (1 Armor)
- Rad-soaked bandages across the face (_petty_)
- *See Playing Unusual PCs → Ghoul PCs for baseline rules (no Rad Scars, feral risk, no aging).*

**How You're Turning:** *Old Memory.* You remember the world before the bombs in painful detail — prices, streets, who owned which building, what the curfew sirens meant. Once per session, describe a detail of the pre-war world that bears on the current situation, and the Overseer confirms it or adjusts it.

**What the Long Years Left You:** *Ghoul-Sign.* Ferals take you for one of their own even in a fight: they never attack you, provoked or not. Anyone who stays at your side and moves slowly can walk through a feral pack with you.

**Traits:** Physique *Hunched*, Skin *Radiation-mottled*, Hair *Patchy*, Face *Gaunt*, Speech *Whispering*, Clothing *Rad-scorched*, Virtue *Steady*, Vice *Lying* (at her age, it's habit).

**Bond (Bond 6):** You inherited a **Journal** from your father. It writes itself at night — in his handwriting, in his voice, describing your day. It is never wrong.
