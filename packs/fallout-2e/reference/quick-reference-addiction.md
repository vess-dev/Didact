- Every dose of an addictive chem = STR save or **addicted**.
- A second dose in the same session can be automatic addiction at the Overseer's call — no save.
- Addicted = +1 Fatigue every morning until you take another dose. Cure: Addictol / Fixer, or the **Cure an Addiction** Downtime — 50 caps + clinic, or a week's rest + a companion; then a WIL save.
