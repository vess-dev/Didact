When an Overseer needs to populate an encounter fast, these tables give a creature appropriate to the situation. Roll on the matching threat level — or roll Die of Fate first to decide *which* threat level is appropriate given party strength, terrain, and narrative pressure.

Each table is a d20 list. Where a creature is typically a detachment (raiders, feral ghouls, mole rats), assume a detachment unless the Overseer decides otherwise.
