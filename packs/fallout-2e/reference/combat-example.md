> Two days along the old interstate, the party has reached the outer fence of **Dim Hollow**, a settlement rumored to be hoarding Stimpaks. Morning fog is starting to burn off, and through the mist they see three figures at the gate — one with a rifle, two with pipes.

**Overseer**: *"The gate guards see you. The one in the middle — woman with a hunting rifle and a denim coat — raises her weapon. The other two fan out to either side. She shouts for you to stop where you are. Roll DEX to see if any of you can act before they do."*

> Lena, Wren, and the Ranger all roll DEX saves. Lena and the Ranger pass. Wren fumbles and loses her first turn.

**Ranger**: *"I don't want to start this fight. I yell back, 'We're not raiders, we came about the Stimpak shipment from the clinic — is Mother Cupper in?'"*

**Lena**: *"And I keep my hands visible."*

**Overseer**: *"The guard hesitates. I'll roll 2d6 for her reaction — she's not obviously hostile anymore."*

> The roll comes up 9. Kind.

**Overseer**: *"She lowers the rifle about halfway. 'Mother Cupper doesn't do business at the gate. If you came about the clinic, you can wait here and I'll send word. But one of you tries to cross that fence, and I'll drop you.'"*

**Wren** *(back in the action)*: *"I do not cross the fence."*

> The fight doesn't happen. This is the most common outcome in Fallout 2e — when players and Overseers are both playing the fiction, most combat is avoided or ended before damage is dealt.

**Overseer**: *"You wait. Mother Cupper comes. You trade. Two Stimpaks for eighty caps and a promise to deliver a message to the next town over. That's the scene."*

> But sometimes it goes the other way.

**Overseer**: *"Different story this time. You're pinned down in a subway tunnel. Three feral ghouls are shambling fast from the far end. Torchlight, echoes, no cover worth mentioning. Roll DEX to see who can act before they reach you."*

> Everyone passes except Wren. On the party turn, the Ranger and Lena both fire on the leading ghoul. The Ranger rolls a d8 (service rifle, 6 damage), Lena rolls a d6 (pipe pistol, 4 damage). They keep the higher — 6 damage. The ghoul has 5 HP, so its HP drops to 0 with 1 damage left over. The remaining 1 is subtracted from its STR (12 → 11). It makes a STR save at the new value. It rolls an 8. It's still on its feet.

**Overseer**: *"You both shoot. Lena, your pipe pistol round clips its shoulder and spins it — the Ranger's rifle round takes it square in the chest, but the thing keeps coming. It's on you next turn."*

> On its turn, the lead ghoul reaches Wren, who still hasn't acted. It attacks with claws (d6+d6). The Overseer rolls 5 and 2, keeping the 5. Wren has 4 HP and no armor. The 5 damage empties her 4 HP with 1 to spare — past 0, not exactly on it — so this is not a Scar. The 1 point beyond her HP spills into STR: that is attribute loss. Wren's STR was 10, and is now 9. She makes a Critical Damage save at the new STR: rolls a 9. Still in the fight.

**Overseer**: *"The ghoul's claws rake across your vest. It's a near thing. You are not dead. You feel something pull in your side that shouldn't have pulled. You're hurt, but you can still fight."*

**Wren**: *"I'm going to put my pipe pistol in its mouth and pull the trigger."*

> This is an Enhanced attack — point-blank, barely conscious target, no dodge. d12 instead of d6. Wren rolls an 11. The ghoul has 11 STR — 11 damage drops it to 0 STR. It falls.

**Overseer**: *"The pistol goes off. The ghoul's head snaps back. It hits the tile hard. The other two are still coming."*

> And on it goes. Combat ends when one side is wiped, surrenders, or flees.
