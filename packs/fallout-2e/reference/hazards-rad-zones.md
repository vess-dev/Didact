Ambient rad zones add **1 Rad per watch** of travel. Hot zones — glowing craters, ruptured reactors, weapon-test sites — add **1 Rad per scene** on top of that.

Protective gear halves or negates rad exposure: a sealed hazmat suit negates ambient Rads; a radiation suit, sealed power armor, or a Lead Lining halves them. When gear *halves* Rads, the wearer ignores every second Rad of that kind. A dose of Rad-X negates the next Rad from any source. See **Core Rules → Rad Scars & Ghoulification** for the Rad Scar save each new Rad forces.
