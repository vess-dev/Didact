At an **Armor Workbench** (light/medium) or a **Power Armor Station** (heavy), a PC installs **one** mod on a suit of armor they own. An armor suit holds at most **two** mods. A power armor frame counts as a separate armor suit from the chassis plates — it may hold two additional mods specific to the frame.

### Light & Medium Armor Mods

| Mod | Scrap | Caps | Downtime | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Padded Lining** | 1 | 50 | 1 | +1 max HP while worn. |
| **Pocketed** | 1 | 30 | 1 | One ordinary item stowed in the pockets counts as _petty_. |
| **Lead Lining** | 2 | 80 | 1 | Halve Rads gained from ambient radiation while worn. Does not affect direct rad weapons. |
| **Reinforced Plating** | 3 | 120 | 2 | +1 Armor against ballistic damage only (up to the cap of 3). |
| **Insulated Weave** | 2 | 80 | 1 | +1 Armor against energy damage only (up to the cap of 3). |
| **Muffled** | 1 | 40 | 1 | Armor loses the _loud_ tag, if it had one. The wearer can attempt stealth normally in this armor. |
| **Deep Pocket Webbing** | 2 | 80 | 1 | Treat one _bulky_ item worn on the harness as a single slot instead of two. One use per suit. |
| **Biometric Seals** | 2 | 100 | 1 | The suit resists gas and airborne toxins. The wearer ignores the first dose of any inhaled poison or gas per scene. |
| **Shock Absorber** | 2 | 80 | 1 | When the wearer falls, they automatically pass the DEX save — damage is halved. When they crash a vehicle, halve the HP Lost lookup on the Scars table. |
| **Asbestos Lining** | 2 | 90 | 1 | The wearer takes no damage from incidental fire (torches, embers, a brief burst). In a sustained flame (flamethrower, burning building), they still suffer but make a DEX save each round to avoid catching fire. |

### Power Armor Mods

Power armor mods require a **Power Armor Station** and at least one **spare fusion core** on the bench. Power armor can carry two plate-slot mods *and* two frame-slot mods (four total), but the installation time is real — every power armor mod consumes **2 Downtime Actions** unless listed otherwise.

| Mod | Scrap | Caps | Downtime | Slot | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tesla Coils** | 4 | 250 | 2 | Plate | Enemies striking the wearer in melee take 1d4 electric damage (ignores Armor). |
| **Kinetic Dynamo** | 3 | 200 | 2 | Plate | The suit's core drains at half speed during walking and light combat. |
| **Reactive Plating** | 4 | 280 | 2 | Plate | Once per combat, the wearer may ignore one incoming hit entirely — it sparks off the reactive plate. |
| **Jet Pack Assembly** | 5 | 400 | 3 | Plate | The suit may make one 40-ft vertical leap or horizontal gap crossing per scene. Each use drains the suit's fusion core; after several uses in a day the core is spent and must be swapped. |
| **Optimized Servos** | 3 | 200 | 2 | Frame | The wearer ignores the sprint and climb restrictions of power armor. |
| **Stealth Boy Rig** | 4 | 350 | 3 | Frame | Once per scene, the suit fades from sight for one action. Each use drains the suit's fusion core; after several uses in a day the core is spent and must be swapped. |
| **Targeting HUD** | 3 | 200 | 2 | Frame | The wearer is never Impaired at long range. |
| **Medical Injector Array** | 4 | 300 | 2 | Frame | Once per scene, the suit automatically administers a Stimpak from internal stores. Holds 3 Stimpak doses; refilled at a Chemistry Station. |
| **Hydraulic Bracers** | 3 | 200 | 2 | Frame | The wearer's unarmed attacks step up one damage die (d4 → d6, cap d12). |
| **Blood Cleanser** | 4 | 280 | 2 | Frame | The suit filters Rads from the wearer's blood. While worn, the suit's filtration clears 1 Rad at the end of each long rest. |
| **Calibrated Shocks** | 2 | 150 | 2 | Frame | The servos carry heavier loads: up to two ordinary items strapped to the frame count as _petty_ while the suit is worn. |
