Every Fallout game has set a different tone. Show the dial to your players and let them put a pin in it.

| Setting | Reference | What It Feels Like |
|---|---|---|
| **Bleak** | Fallout, Fallout 3 | Cold, heavy, melancholy. Every settlement is a graveyard with a cookfire. Violence is ugly. Survival is the story. |
| **Pulpy** | New Vegas, Fallout 4 | Noir voice-over, leather dusters, named raider bosses, a radio DJ narrating the party's legend. Violence is cinematic. |
| **Weird** | Fallout 2 (late), Fallout 76 | Super mutants making conversation, aliens, mothmen, pre-war TV hosts, a ghoul running for mayor. Violence is absurd. |
| **Horror** | Point Lookout, Far Harbor, Dunwich | Swamp dread, eldritch whispers, things in the fog that should not be there. Violence is a relief from the worse thing that was about to happen. |

A campaign can shift the dial over its length. Commit to one setting *per session*.
