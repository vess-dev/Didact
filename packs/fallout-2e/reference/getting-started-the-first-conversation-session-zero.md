Before the first session, the Overseer and the players spend half an hour — no dice, no characters yet — talking about the game they're about to play. This is the single most useful half hour of prep you will ever do.

**Cover these five things:**

1. **Tone.** Is this a bleak wasteland out of *The Road*, a pulpy one out of *Mad Max*, a weird one out of *Fallout 2*, or a horror story told in the fog? Pick one — see **The Tone Dial** below. Revisit if the game drifts.
2. **Safety.** Name the topics the table does not want in play. Slavery, child harm, body horror, sexual content — whatever is on the list, it stays off the screen. Agree on a signal (the X-card, a gesture, a word) a player can use when a scene is getting to them. Honor it instantly and without question.
3. **Stakes.** Death is possible in Fallout 2e. Make sure the table wants that. If someone doesn't, lean on Scars and moral consequence instead of PC deaths.
4. **Pacing.** Are you running one-shots, a season, or a campaign? What's the session length? How often?
5. **Reach.** Is this a Fallout-3-style regional adventure, a New-Vegas-style sandbox, or a Fallout-4-style settlement-focused game? Pick one. It'll shape the first session.
