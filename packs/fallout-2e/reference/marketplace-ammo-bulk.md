Ammo is usually abstracted, but when the Overseer wants to make scarcity matter: **1 ammo use = 1 use of the weapon** — one attack, whatever that looks like in the fiction. A laser musket spends 1 use per shot; an SMG spends 1 use to hose a burst across a room. A weapon that lists an ammo type spends one use of that ammo per attack, unless its description says otherwise:
