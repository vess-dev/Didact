- **Terrain**: Pre-war Highway
- **Feature**: A collapsed overpass
- **Inhabitants**: A raider warband + a lone prospector
- **Rumor**: *The water is clean for one hour a day.*

The stretch of Route 14 between the farming town of **Dim Hollow** and the crater of **Hexington** is a long pre-war highway draped with kudzu and cracked concrete. At its center, a collapsed overpass forms a makeshift bridge down into an old toll plaza. A raider warband has taken over the plaza and extorts passing caravans. An old prospector named **Ora** lives in the overpass supports and claims that at noon each day, the runoff pool down the wash from the bridge runs clean for an hour — clean enough to drink without a single Rad. The raiders do not know this. Ora will tell anyone who isn't a raider, but only if they look her in the eyes.

### Hooks
- A settlement has offered 200 caps for a safe passage agreement with the raiders.
- Ora says she has seen something in the runoff pool that shouldn't be there. She asks someone to come back with her at midnight and look.
- A caravan is missing its lead brahmin, and the tracks lead under the overpass.
