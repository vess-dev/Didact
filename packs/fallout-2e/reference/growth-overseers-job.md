tags: [growth]
source: overseers-guide/growth#overseers-job
---
Offer growth opportunities constantly, woven into the world:
- A trainer who knows a specific technique the party has seen used against them.
- A relic in a ruin that specifically matters to a specific PC.
- A hireling who will teach if treated well.
- A faction that will grant a permanent boon in exchange for loyalty.

Every session should contain at least one chance for a character to come out the other side with something they did not walk in with — a Scar, a new trick, a new friend, a new enemy. A character who has gained nothing in a session has wasted the session.
