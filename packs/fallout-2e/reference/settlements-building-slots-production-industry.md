### Production & Industry

| Building | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Farm Plots** (mutfruit, tato, corn, razorgrain) | +1 Industry. Rations are free for the party in good standing. Failed crops on an **Extreme** weather week. |
| **Brahmin Pen** | +1 Population. Caravans stop here for free. Settlement feeds itself. |
| **Water Pump** (hand-cranked) | +1 Industry at 1 Population. Provides unlimited dirty water; add a purifier to make it clean. |
| **Industrial Water Purifier** (RobCo or pre-war water chip) | +1 Industry. Clears 1 Rad per day from anyone who drinks here. Produces trade-grade purified water as a resource. |
| **Scrap Heap** | +1 Industry. Passive trickle: settlers deliver 1d6 Scrap each visit. |
| **Junk Fence** (trader outpost) | A broker who buys salvage at 60% value with no questions asked. Stacks with a Trading Post. |
| **Power Generator** (small, medium, or fusion-core) | Powers one (small), three (medium), or six (core) electric buildings per week. Required for turrets, lit signs, workshops, and most advanced crafting. |
| **Lit Signs & Streetlamps** | _petty_ building. Requires power. Reaction rolls in the settlement shift one step better on first visit; against night raids, the settlement gains +1 Defense. |
