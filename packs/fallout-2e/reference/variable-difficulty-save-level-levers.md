tags: [variable, difficulty]
source: overseers-guide/variable-difficulty#save-level-levers
---
- **What's at stake.** You won't normally make a save harder — the roll is always against the same attribute — so raise the cost of failure instead. A STR save to hold a collapsing beam is the same roll whether the failure is a bruise or a dead friend. Make it the dead friend.
- **Risk of multiple saves.** Forcing a save every round is a form of difficulty escalation.
