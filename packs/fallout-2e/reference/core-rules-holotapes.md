Holotapes are separate from magazines. They are pre-war **data cartridges** — audio logs, recorded journals, corporate training reels, occasional passcodes and map data. They deliver lore and clues, not perks and not mechanical bonuses. Most are simply the voices of the pre-war dead. See **Marketplace → Holotapes (Lore Items)**.
