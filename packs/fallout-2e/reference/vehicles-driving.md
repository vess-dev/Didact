- **Normal driving** on a road or known route does not call for a roll. A working vehicle just works.
- **Under pressure** — a chase, a swerve, a collapsed overpass, an incoming missile — the driver makes a **DEX save**. On a fail, the vehicle takes damage (Overseer's call: 1d6 for a graze, 1d12 for a real crash), loses a turn of forward motion, or both.
- **Driving in a rad storm, through a hot zone, or across broken terrain** uses a **STR save** instead. The rad-shielded passenger cabin protects against the weather; the chassis does not.
- **Driving while Deprived**: The driver cannot avoid hazards — any DEX save to swerve or brake automatically fails.
