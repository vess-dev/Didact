tags: [setting, seeds]
source: overseers-guide/setting-seeds#3-place-points-of-interest
---
Drop about a dozen points on your map. At least:
- **2 settlements** (one larger, one smaller)
- **2 ruins** (one obvious, one hidden)
- **1 vault or sealed facility**
- **1 wasteland encounter site** (a landmark where weird things happen)
- **1 caravan hub or crossroads**
- **2–3 hex-filler oddities** (a crashed Vertibird, a standing statue, a lake that glows at night)
