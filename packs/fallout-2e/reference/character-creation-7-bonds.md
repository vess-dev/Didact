Roll 1d20 on the Bonds tables below. The list is split into two halves so each fits cleanly on the page.

### Bonds — 1 to 10

| d20 | Bond |
|---|---|
| 1 | A dead uncle left you a **Gold-Plated Bar** (400 caps, heavy, cold). The note that came with it said, *"Spend it and they will find you."* |
| 2 | You were mailed a **Pre-war Compass** (_petty_) that always points toward something deep in the Glow. You don't know what. Take 15 caps. |
| 3 | You carry a **Cracked Locket** (_petty_) with a faded photograph of someone you loved. They went into the tunnels ten years ago. You think they're still alive down there. |
| 4 | You found a **Reflective Shard** (_petty_) in the sand. Hold it to sunlight and you see a place you've never been — a cold corridor lit in green. Sometimes something looks back. |
| 5 | You pulled a trader out of a canyon flood once. She gave you a **Dog Whistle**. Blow it near water, and a scarred mongrel will find you. Once. |
| 6 | You inherited a **Journal** from your father. It writes itself at night — in his handwriting, in his voice, describing your day. It is never wrong. |
| 7 | You carry half of a **Vault-Tec Master Key** (_petty_). They say joined with its twin, it opens any door in any vault. |
| 8 | You received a **Letter** (_petty_) claiming you are the heir of one of the pre-war families that built the Enclave. It names a meeting place, three months out, deep in the ashlands. |
| 9 | You owe a debt to a local boss. You carry their **Brass Token** (_petty_). It's protection — and it's a leash. |
| 10 | You were dosed with an experimental pre-war nanite culture during a botched lab raid. It talks to you in your head in a clipped corporate voice and wants to be taken to its parent facility. It occupies one slot but clears 1 Rad each day. It wants you alive — for now. |

### Bonds — 11 to 20

| d20 | Bond |
|---|---|
| 11 | A drunk at the crossroads told you a ruined facility holds a working fusion core the size of a fist. He drew you **A Wrinkled Map** (_petty_). It's marked with a red circle. |
| 12 | You met a dying courier in a ditch. They asked you to deliver a **Sealed Envelope** (_petty_) to a woman known only as **The Listener**, somewhere in the southern glowstates. |
| 13 | You once left a wounded yao guai in a gully. You still see it, sometimes, in the corner of your eye — a trick of post-traumatic wiring, your settlement's medic said. You cannot become Panicked when alone. |
| 14 | You promised a child in your settlement you'd bring back something no one had ever seen. Take a **Bottle-Cap Charm** (_petty_) they pressed into your hand. |
| 15 | A pre-war auto-doc mistook you for a different patient and installed a **Lead Battery Cell** in your chest. It grows heavier by one slot each month. Until it is removed by someone who knows what they're doing, you cannot truly die — the cell is keeping your heart beating. |
| 16 | You pulled a **Rifle Bullet** (_petty_) from a mass grave and wear it on a cord. It's still warm. Held against a fresh wound, it keeps the wearer from bleeding out for one extra hour beyond any Scar's normal timer. Once per session. You can feel it watching you. |
| 17 | The **Minutemen** did your family a service and left you a dried **Blue Petal** (_petty_) as payment. When it turns black, the favor is called in. |
| 18 | A wandering musician stayed with your family one winter and left behind **A Small Guitar**. Something rattles inside that isn't a tuning peg. |
| 19 | A white crow landed on your windowsill in a dream and dropped a **Bone Splinter** (_petty_). You woke with it in your hand. It smells faintly of sulfur. Lucky, maybe. |
| 20 | You were in the wrong place when a pre-war experiment on perception-altering chemistry leaked. Glass nearby you cracks when you are under stress. The pieces, when you pick them up, sometimes show you a frame of something that hasn't happened yet. |
