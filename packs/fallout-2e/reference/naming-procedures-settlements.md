Two-word settlements work best: **[descriptor] + [noun]**.

### Descriptor (d10)

| d10 | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Dim |
| 2 | Long |
| 3 | Red |
| 4 | Old |
| 5 | Last |
| 6 | Cold |
| 7 | Far |
| 8 | Broken |
| 9 | Lower |
| 10 | Still |

### Noun (d10)

| d10 | Noun |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hollow |
| 2 | Crossing |
| 3 | Watch |
| 4 | Yard |
| 5 | Springs |
| 6 | Field |
| 7 | Ford |
| 8 | Flats |
| 9 | Fork |
| 10 | End |

Examples: **Dim Hollow**, **Last Watch**, **Old Ford**, **Cold Flats**, **Red Springs**.

Alternatively: take a surviving pre-war place-name and corrupt it.
- *Hexington* (from "Lexington")
- *Conquord* (from "Concord")
- *Pitt* (from "Pittsburgh")
- *Jay-Cee* (from "JC" on a road sign)
