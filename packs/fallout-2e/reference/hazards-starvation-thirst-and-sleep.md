These are already covered under **Deprivation** in Core Rules. A Deprived PC cannot recover HP or Attributes, or clear Rads or Fatigue, until the lack is remedied. A PC Deprived for more than a day takes **1 Fatigue per day** until they rest, drink, or eat.
