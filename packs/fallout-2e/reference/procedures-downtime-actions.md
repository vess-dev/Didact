### Research

Investigate a question — a lost fact, a hidden location, an NPC's whereabouts. You need a **Source** — a person, place, or archive that might know. If you don't have one, your first downtime finds one (if at all).

Examples:
- "Where is the **Vault 87 access code**?"
- "Who in **Junktown** knows how to disarm a tripwire bomb?"
- "Where can I find a working **auto-doc**?"

### Sources
- **Rue Sixpenny**, a fixer in Junktown. Your relationship is good, but she doesn't forgive being used in raider business.
- A **Ghoul Archivist** in the old library. You helped him once. He still remembers the words to a song that used to play before the war.
- The **Broadcast Tribe**, a nomadic band who have crossed nearly every ruin on the map. They owe you a favor from the rad storm last summer.

### Training

Improve a skill or weapon through a teacher (a **Master**) and work. Describe what you want to learn and who you're learning from. The Overseer sets milestones and costs.

Examples:
- **One-Handed Brace**: With one hand free, once per combat one attack with your one-handed weapon is Enhanced. A veteran sniper in the Rangers will teach anyone who beats her in a footrace.
- **Field Surgery**: Given supplies, make a **Homemade Stimpak** (heals 1d6 STR) as a Downtime Action. A wasteland medic in the settlement owed the party her life.
- **Radsense**: When you take Supply within reach of clean water, step the Supply die up one (1d4 → 1d6, cap 1d12). A desert ranger will train anyone willing to spend a week drinking from an irradiated river.

### Strengthening Ties

Build a connection with a faction or NPC. Name the target, name the intent (trust, membership, alliance, courtship, forgiveness), and the Overseer defines milestones and costs. With each milestone the relationship changes.

Examples:
- A PC returns from a ruin with a stowaway — a tiny, eyeless creature barely out of the egg. They decide to raise it in secret.
- A PC befriends a Brotherhood Paladin in play. Impressed, they ask what it would take to join.
- An agent of a local faction dies under the party's watch. The faction now wants restitution — a run of favors, paid in blood or caps.

### Scavenge a Ruin

Return to a specific pre-war ruin the party has already mapped, with a specific target: a particular vending machine, a specific office floor, a known terminal, a known body. A scavenging run is *not* an adventure — the party has already done the dangerous work, and this is the cleanup.

Pay a **Downtime Action** and roll on the **Loot Roll Tables** in the Marketplace: *What's in the Cache?* (d100), or straight on *Random Weapon*, *Random Gear*, or *Chem Stash* when the target makes the answer obvious. Risk is one roll on the Ruin Events table. If the event forces a save and the PC fails, it costs a Scar or a Rad.

*Not* a substitute for a full expedition. A ruin that has been scavenged twice is empty.

### Craft

Build chems, meals, weapon mods, ammo, or gadgets at a workstation. See the **Crafting** chapter for the station, cost, and effect of each recipe. A craft action is one Downtime Action per recipe.

### Cure an Addiction

A PC carrying an addiction (see **Core Rules → Addiction**) may spend a Downtime Action and either **50 caps** at a clinic, or **a full week in a safe place** with a companion willing to sit with them. On a WIL save, the addiction is broken and the morning Fatigue stops. On a failure, the addiction deepens — they cannot try to break this particular addiction again until their next Downtime.
