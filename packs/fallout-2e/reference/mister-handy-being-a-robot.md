See **Playing Unusual PCs → Robot PCs** for the common rules (no food, no chems, EMP vulnerability, submersion, repair via Tinker). This chassis is a baseline Mr. Handy unit — a single central thruster, three arms (circular saw, flamer, pincer), a cheerful pre-war voice module. You hover half a foot off the ground: pressure-plate traps don't trigger under you, and you cannot climb or swim.
