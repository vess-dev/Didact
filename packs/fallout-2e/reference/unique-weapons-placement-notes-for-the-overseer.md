- A unique weapon should answer a story question, not replace one. Let the party earn it.
- Consider giving a unique weapon a **drawback** in addition to its bonus — the more powerful it is, the more it costs to carry. Attention from its old owner, a curse, a vulnerability, a loud reputation.
- When a PC loses a unique weapon, note who took it. It will come back.
- Do not give more than one unique weapon per character per campaign. The story is in the scarcity.
- Flavor counts as mechanics. A weapon with a famous name is already doing work at the table — it changes NPC reactions, starts arguments, gets asked about, gets hunted. You do not have to bolt a bonus onto every one.
