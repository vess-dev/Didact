Names should be short, distinct, and easy to say three times in a row.

- **One-syllable wasteland**: Ash, Dix, Rook, Cass, Tam, Pike, Dust.
- **Pre-war-fragment**: Halloran → Hal, Johannes → Jo, Margaret → Mag, Preston → Pres.
- **Epithet**: Mother Cupper, Uncle Barney, Old Longfellow, Big Johnny.
- **Title + short name**: Colonel Hsu, Doctor Amari, Sister Mary, Elder Reed, Overseer Dane.
- **Faction rank + name**: Paladin Danse, Ranger Boone, Scribe Pell, Knight-Captain Li, Frumentarius Vulpes.
- **Vault-suit nickname**: Blue, Yellow, 111, 101 — a ghoul or synth who's forgotten their old name often picks one up from the color of the jumpsuit they crawled out in.
- **Ghoul holdover**: a pre-war first name kept as a banner of who they used to be — Desmond, Daisy, Carol, Kent, Raul.

Avoid names that rhyme or start with the same letter as another named NPC in the session.
