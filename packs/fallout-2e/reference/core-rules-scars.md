When damage would reduce HP to exactly 0, look up the **HP lost in that attack** on the table. Example: from 4 HP to 0 after taking 4 damage → row 4. When an ability says you never get a row, or lets you re-roll one, roll 1d12 for the row instead, rolling again on any row you never get.

### Scars Table

| HP Lost | Result |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Close Shave.** A round clipped something. Roll 1d6 for where: 1 neck, 2 hand, 3 eye, 4 chest, 5 leg, 6 ear. Then roll 1d6 — if higher than max HP, replace. |
| **2** | **Concussed.** Your helmet rang like a gong. Describe how you shake it off. Roll 1d6; if higher than max HP, replace. |
| **3** | **Flung Clear.** A blast tossed you through the rubble. You're winded, bruised, and Deprived until you rest a few hours. Then roll 1d6 and add it to max HP. |
| **4** | **Broken.** Roll 1d6: 1–2 leg (crushed under debris), 3–4 arm (wrong angle), 5 rib (a raider's rebar), 6 jaw (a ghoul's fist). When mended, roll 2d6; if higher than max HP, replace. |
| **5** | **Rad-Necrotic Wound.** Something radioactive got into the cut and it's not healing clean. When it finally clears — RadAway, auto-doc, a pre-war antibiotic — roll 2d6; if higher than max HP, replace. |
| **6** | **Scrambled.** A nearby mini nuke, a plasma burst, a chem overdose — your head is still rattling. Roll 1d6: 1–2 STR, 3–4 DEX, 5–6 WIL. Roll 3d6; if higher than current value of that attribute, replace. |
| **7** | **Crippled.** A round to the knee, a super sledge to the hip, or a bear trap that wouldn't let go. You cannot walk unassisted until you get serious medical help. After full recovery, roll 3d6; if higher than max DEX, replace. |
| **8** | **Burst Eardrums.** A frag grenade too close, or the shriek of a Tesla arc. Deaf until extraordinary aid. Make a WIL save; on a success, max WIL +1d4. |
| **9** | **Shell-Shocked.** Something in your mind comes loose — the sound of an incoming missile, the last radio broadcast from the old world, whatever it was. Roll 3d6; if higher than max WIL, replace. |
| **10** | **Severed.** You lose a limb or an eye (Overseer's choice). A prosthetic or a cybernetic graft in a big settlement can restore some function. Make a WIL save; on a success, max WIL +1d6. |
| **11** | **Bleeding Out.** Gut shot. Deprived, immobile. You die in one hour without aid — a Stimpak, a bandage, an auto-doc. On recovery, roll 2d6 and replace your max HP with the result. |
| **12** | **Doomed.** Death breathed on your neck in a pre-war bunker nobody's walked out of. If you fail your next Critical Damage save, you die horribly. If you pass, roll 3d6; if higher than max HP, replace. |
