- **Fusion cell** — lasers, power fists, rippers, some melee
- **Microfusion cell** — plasma, pulse
- **Fusion core** — power armor, gatling laser, heavy energy
- **2mm electromagnetic** — gauss weapons
- **Electron charge pack** — Tesla
- **Alien power cell** — alien blaster only
- **Flamer fuel** — flamer, heavy incinerator, shishkebab
- **5mm belt** — minigun
- **.50 BMG** — anti-materiel rifle, .50 MG
- **.308** — hunting rifle, sniper rifle
- **5.56** — service rifle, marksman carbine, assault rifle
- **7.62** — battle rifle, handmade rifle
- **10mm / .44 / .357** — as named
