tags: [variable, difficulty]

---
- **HP**, **Armor**, and **Damage Die** are the main dials. A creature with 2 Armor is substantially harder than the same creature with 1.
- **Critical Damage**. A harsh Critical Damage effect makes any fight terrifying even if the creature is statistically easy.
- **Abilities.** A single signature ability changes how the fight feels without changing the math.
- **Leader.** Putting a single stronger NPC among weaker ones doubles the difficulty of the encounter.
