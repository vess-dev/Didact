If you're the youngest, roll 1d20 on the Omens tables below and read the result aloud. The list is split into two halves so each fits cleanly on the page.

### Omens — 1 to 10

| d20 | Omen |
|---|---|
| 1 | The river that feeds three settlements has turned black and sour. An old prospector blames a ruin the kids have started exploring. |
| 2 | Rad storms have arrived a full season early. Lightning patterns etched into metal roofs look almost like a map. |
| 3 | A pre-war hospital's automated PA system came back online last week. Every morning at 0600 it announces a surgery scheduled for Dr. Halsey in Operating Room 3. Anyone who answers the announcement does not come back out. |
| 4 | Caravan guards in the south say lights in the sky are taking brahmin out of the fields. A Zetan scout party is operating in the region. Nobody wants to say the word *alien* out loud. |
| 5 | The birds have left the eastern wastes. Hunters report a tall, thin grey figure standing on ridges at dusk, watching. It does not move when shot at. |
| 6 | A pre-war Nuka-Cola vending machine in a ruined subway has begun dispensing Nuka-Cola Quantum again. Fresh. Nobody has found the mechanism inside. Pilgrims are already gathering. |
| 7 | A constant distant hum is keeping a whole town awake. Tinkers say the frequency matches an Institute relay signal. It's getting louder each week. It is also coming closer. |
| 8 | The oldest mutfruit tree in a farming settlement has started weeping a red sap that tests positive for pre-war motor oil. The settlement's brewer is bottling it. Nobody has asked the brewer what she's doing with it. |
| 9 | The moon is going red. A pre-war reactor core beneath the nearest city has begun to bleed. The Brotherhood of Steel has already sent a scout. |
| 10 | A rogue pre-war satellite in decaying orbit has begun painting a green laser grid across the night sky. People say they can read the grid. Some of them say they can *answer* it. |

### Omens — 11 to 20

| d20 | Omen |
|---|---|
| 11 | A night-blooming flower nobody has cataloged is sprouting in the glowing craters. Pilgrims from the Children of Atom are harvesting the blooms. They dream afterward, and they do not wake alone. |
| 12 | Brahmin in a dozen settlements panic at moonrise. A pre-war Anchorage-era army howls from inside a sealed mountain base each full moon — a loop of a thousand voices that should not be running after two hundred years. |
| 13 | Giant ants are boiling out of a pre-war subway station in numbers no nest should hold. The settlement downslope has begun to evacuate. Someone is *feeding* the nest. |
| 14 | Hunters who kill a white-furred yao guai are found dead at home a week later. A Legion frumentarius has been sighted in the hills, wearing a yao guai pelt as a cloak. |
| 15 | Laughter is coming out of the storm drains of the capital ruin. At night it turns to weeping. A pre-war AI running the city's sewer system has been broadcasting the same recorded child's voice for two centuries. Something else is starting to answer it. |
| 16 | A pre-war Vault-Tec emergency satellite thought dead since 2077 has started broadcasting on Channel 16. The broadcast is a single word — *RETURN* — repeated in thirty languages, every hour on the hour. |
| 17 | An old apple tree at the center of a settlement withered overnight. When they cut the trunk open, a functioning Institute synth component was inside — still warm, still ticking. Nobody knows how long it had been growing there. |
| 18 | A pre-war park statue is leaking a red fluid from its base. An Enclave surveyor tested it and left the same night without his pack. No child has been born in that settlement in a year. The fluid smells like FEV. |
| 19 | Wildlife in the region has gone either aggressive or silent. A deathclaw matriarch has moved into a ruined Vault-Tec research site and begun calling the smaller beasts to her. No one who has gone up the hill has come back. |
| 20 | Every child in the border settlements has started waking at 0317 each night repeating the same sentence: *"A fire is coming, and it will take everything."* The settlement medics blame a leaking Institute broadcast. The mothers do not. |
