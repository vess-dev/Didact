The implied setting of Fallout 2e is called **the Commonwealth of Ashes** — or just **the Ashes**. It is a generic post-atomic wasteland two centuries after the war that ended the old world. No specific continent, no specific president, no specific vault-door number. Most play will happen in a small region of ruins, settlements, and wildlands. Pick a spot on a map and drop a crater on it.

The old world is called **the Before**. Almost nobody alive remembers it — a few ghouls, a few machines, a few sleepers thawed out of the ice. Everyone has opinions about it.

Settlements cluster around clean water and defensible ground — a river bend, a half-collapsed interchange, the stone foundations of an old strip mall, a hollowed-out high school. They're within a day's walk of each other along any reasonably safe road. Most run on farming, salvage, brewing, or some pre-war industry they've resurrected — a still, a brahmin ranch, a bullet press, a trade post. A local militia or a hired gun usually keeps the worst of the Wastes off.

Old highways between big towns still exist; maintained roads are rare and controlled. Caravans move between settlements every few weeks, sometimes hiring **Couriers** or **Gunners** for protection. **Caravaneers** run the whole game from the back of a brahmin.

Even the smallest town might see a scholar now and then — a **Hacker** breaking terminals in the ruin down the road, a **Tinker** looking for pre-war wiring, a **Prospector** who claims to have mapped a fusion core field. A **Wasteland Medic** is never far — the settlements demand one. A **Chem Cook** gets called when somebody in charge has a problem they don't want to talk about in daylight. Musicians, news-runners, and storytellers tag along with these travelers for the reflected glory, and occasionally for the caps.

Those who enter a ruin without a guide are foolish. Those who hire a **Scavver** or a **Desert Ranger** with local knowledge tend to come back. Those pushing deeper into the Glow — the irradiated dark beneath the region — seek out a **Ghoul** or a **Synth Runaway**, whose strange biology gives them an edge ordinary flesh doesn't have. On the border, the **Brotherhood Initiates** try to claw back pre-war tech for safekeeping, while **Enclave Defectors** dodge the hunters sent after them — or go looking for the thing they all ran from.
