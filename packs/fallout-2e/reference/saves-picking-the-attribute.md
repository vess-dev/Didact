- **STR** — the body. Force, endurance, poison, radiation, hauling, grappling, surviving direct harm.
- **DEX** — the reflexes. Shooting, dodging, climbing, stealth, balance, fine motor work.
- **WIL** — the mind and heart. Persuasion, deception, interrogation, holding one's nerve, resisting mental influence, operating technology under pressure.

When two might apply, pick the one the player's stated *approach* invokes. A character who throws herself off a collapsing catwalk rolls DEX; a character who grabs a rail and holds on rolls STR.
