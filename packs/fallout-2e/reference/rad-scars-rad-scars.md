Every Rad a PC takes risks a **Rad Scar** — a permanent marker of radiation damage. Roll 1d20 against a target of **20 minus the PC's current Rad count** (counting the new Rad). Equal or under: no Scar. Over: gain one. Several Rads at once: add them all, then roll once per Rad against the new total.

The target shrinks as Rads pile up:

| Current Rads | Resist on 1d20 ≤ |
|:-:|:-:|
| 1 | 19 |
| 2 | 18 |
| 3 | 17 |
| 5 | 15 |
| 8 | 12 |
| 10 | 10 |

When a Scar triggers, roll **1d20** on the combined table below to determine which one. **1–10** yields a **Minor** Rad Scar; **11–20** yields a **Major** Rad Scar. Write the result on the character sheet.

Rad Scars are cosmetic, mechanical, and permanent. Three of them together pushes a PC toward ghoulification — see **Rad Scars & Ghoulification** in Core Rules.
