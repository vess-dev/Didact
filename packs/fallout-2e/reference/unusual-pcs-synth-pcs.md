(*Synth Runaway background*)

### What a Synth Is
- **An Institute-manufactured human-form android.** Most synths have human attributes. You bleed — or you don't, depending on your model.
- **You do not age.** You do not recall being born.
- **You eat, drink, sleep, and bleed.** A Gen-3 body was built to pass, and it suffers hunger, thirst, exhaustion, Rads, chems, and addiction exactly as a human does — unless a result on your background's tables says otherwise.
- **You may or may not have memories.** Gen-3 synths often have implanted lives; Gen-2 have none.

### The Institute Risk
- The Institute tracks escaped Gen-3 synths. Once per campaign, the Overseer should introduce a **Courser** (see Bestiary) who has been sent to recover you.
- **Institute Recall Codes** exist. A synth PC may be subject to a verbal recall phrase once per campaign — on a failed WIL save, the phrase briefly overrides them. The Overseer picks the exact moment.
- The **Railroad** (see Faction Reputation) wants to protect you. The **Brotherhood of Steel** wants to destroy you.

### Passing as Human
- A synth PC can pass as human without a close examination. A deep medical scan, a bleeding wound, or a surprised reflex may expose them.
- Synth-specific detection tech exists and appears in some campaigns (see *Far Harbor*).
